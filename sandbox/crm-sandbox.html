Document-Centric CRM

We're reimagining the CRM experience with these key improvements:

  • Document-Centric CLM: Create and manage SOWs, invoices, and agreements as the center of your workflow
  • CRM + CLM Hybrid: Link documents to accounts and contacts for complete relationship visibility
  • Account-Based Management: Track both pursuing and active customer accounts in one place
  • Enhanced Activity Tracking: Tasks, calls, emails, and meetings as first-class objects
  • Related Lists: See connections between accounts, contacts, documents, and agreements at a glance
  • Modern UI/UX: Tab-based layouts, quick actions, and responsive design
