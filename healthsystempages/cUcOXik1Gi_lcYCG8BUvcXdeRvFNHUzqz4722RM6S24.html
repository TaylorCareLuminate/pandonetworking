Nov 25, 2024
A patient at Compass Behavioral Health in Alexandria, Louisiana, was accused of attempted first-degree murder after allegedly disarming and shooting a Rapides Parish Sheriff's Office deputy. The incident occurred on the evening of Thursday, November 21, 2024, when deputies responded to a disturbance call at the facility on Lake Charles Highway. According to the Sheriff's Office, the 22-year-old male patient, identified as Dontrail Rubin, was reportedly fighting with employees before fighting with officers. During the altercation, Rubin allegedly took a female deputy's sidearm and shot her in the upper thigh. Another deputy on the scene then shot Rubin in the torso. The wounded deputy was transported to a hospital, treated, and subsequently released. Rubin also received hospital treatment. He was held under a $610,000 bail and faced charges including attempted first-degree murder, disarming an officer, and aggravated resistance with force or violence. The incident triggered an automatic investigative response procedure for officer-involved shootings within the Rapides Parish Sheriff's Office. Records indicated Rubin had prior charges from June 2024 in St. Landry Parish for possession with intent to sell or distribute and monetary instrument abuse.
Jun 01, 2023
Natchitoches Regional Medical Center (NRMC) in Natchitoches, Louisiana, which is located in the north-central part of the state near Alexandria, announced a partnership with Compass Health on June 1, 2023. Under this collaboration, Compass Health assumed management and daily operational oversight of NRMC's inpatient behavioral health unit, known as Insights, and its day treatment program, Reflections. These programs are dedicated to providing comprehensive psychiatric care for patients. The partnership aims to leverage a dedicated team of experienced behavioral healthcare professionals, including psychiatrists and psychiatric nurse practitioners, to offer an integrative and holistic approach to diagnose and treat a wide range of behavioral health disorders. The goal is to enhance both the inpatient and day treatment behavioral health programs at NRMC to better serve the community's needs, ensuring accessible and high-quality psychiatric services close to patients' homes.
Feb 28, 2023
Compass Health, headquartered in Crowley, Louisiana, expanded its services by opening a new behavioral health center in Lafayette, Louisiana, as reported on February 28, 2023. The new facility is a 7,600-square-foot psychiatric outpatient center designed to increase access to mental healthcare in the region. It features six group rooms intended for intensive outpatient therapy sessions and also houses a mental health clinic for individual appointments. Leadership at the Compass Behavioral Center includes David Dawes, MD, as the psychiatric medical director, Charles Bramlet, MD, as the associate medical director, Kate Spears, psychiatric nurse practitioner, and Tara Charpentier, a social worker. According to Mark Cullen, founder and chair of Compass Health, the opening of this clinic was a key step in treating psychiatric symptoms earlier and in a less restrictive setting, with the potential to provide an additional 8,000 mental health appointments annually and potentially reduce the need for inpatient psychiatric beds.
Compass Health recently announced an organizational restructuring initiative prompted by financial challenges, including inflationary pressures and what the organization describes as deficiencies in funding for community behavioral health services. This reorganization is intended to ensure the long-term sustainability of Compass Health and its ability to continue meeting client needs. As part of this restructuring, Compass Health has transitioned several service lines and programs, including expanding some crisis and intensive programs deemed financially sustainable. However, the reorganization also involves consolidating some outpatient services, which will result in the closure of select facilities. Additionally, the healthcare integration program will be affected. While the specific locations of facility closures are not detailed in the announcement, Compass Health's goal is to minimize the impact on client care, anticipating that many affected clients can be transitioned to other locations, programs, or utilize telehealth services. The reorganization is also expected to impact approximately 6% of Compass Health's workforce, with efforts being made to identify alternative roles within the organization for affected team members. Compass Health acknowledges the difficulty of these changes, particularly given the high demand for mental healthcare, and emphasizes the need for sustainable funding for community behavioral health in Washington state.
