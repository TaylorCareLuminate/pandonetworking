Jun 06, 2025
St. Luke's University Health Network received the In Safe Hands Award from the Hospital and Healthsystem Association of Pennsylvania (HAP). This award recognizes hospitals and health systems for innovation and commitment to patient care. St. Luke's was honored for applying data-driven strategies to identify at-risk patients and enhance care outcomes, specifically through a real-time predictive model embedded in the electronic health record. This model continuously analyzes clinical data to detect early signs of patient deterioration, triggering tiered alerts and coordinated responses, which led to a 14.6 percent reduction in inpatient cardiac arrests, higher survival-to-discharge rates, fewer unanticipated transfers, and over $2.8 million in annualized financial gains. Jun 03, 2025
DeSales University and St. Luke's University Health Network have announced a partnership to launch the SLUHN/DSU Nurse Anesthesia Program. This program is designed as a Doctor of Nursing Practice (BSN-DNP) Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetist (CRNA) track for experienced nurses looking to advance their careers. DeSales University will be the conducting institution for the academic portion, while St. Luke's University Health Network will serve as the exclusive clinical affiliate, providing comprehensive general surgical and subspecialty clinical training and practicum experiences. The program spans 12 semesters and includes 39 courses, with a blend of online and in-person classes for anesthesia-related coursework. Students will complete a significant clinical rotation of at least 2,000 hours in the operating room, gaining experience with patients of all ages and acuity levels across various specialties such as obstetrics, pediatrics, trauma, and cardiac care. This collaboration aims to address the growing need for skilled anesthesia providers and strengthen the local healthcare workforce. [6]
