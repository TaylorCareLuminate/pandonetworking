Recent News

Jun 12, 2025
Adventist Health, headquartered in Roseville, CA, has announced that Kerry Heinrich, JD, will serve as the organization's next chief executive officer. The announcement was made this week by Board Chair John Freedman. Heinrich brings extensive experience to the role, having most recently served as the CEO for over seven years at the Seventh-day Adventist Loma Linda University Medical Center, Children's Hospital, and Behavioral Medicine Center. His tenure at Loma Linda included overseeing the construction of a new 16-story hospital and a nine-story Children's Hospital tower. Prior to his CEO role at Loma Linda, Heinrich served as legal counsel for that organization for more than 20 years. He has also been recognized for his leadership, being named among Becker's Hospital Review's “135 Nonprofit Hospital and Health System CEOs to Know” in 2017. Additionally, Heinrich previously served for seven years on the board of Adventist Health and was actively involved in the development of the system's 2030 strategic vision. He holds a bachelor's degree in history with a minor in business from Walla Walla University and a juris doctor degree from the University of Oregon's School of Law. Heinrich will be taking over from Scott Reiner, who announced his resignation in July to establish a family foundation focused on global health and well-being. The leadership transition is anticipated to be finalized by the end of the current year. [3]
Jun 05, 2025
Kerry L. Heinrich, president and CEO of Adventist Health, which is headquartered in Roseville, CA, recently spoke to students and community members at Walla Walla University in Washington. He addressed an introduction to healthcare administration class and later a colloquium lecture series sponsored by the university's School of Business on June 5, 2025. Heinrich shared insights on leading a large healthcare organization in a challenging and rapidly changing environment, emphasizing his commitment to Adventist Health's mission of inspiring health, wholeness, and hope. He also discussed his career path and encouraged students to remain open to God's purpose for their lives, highlighting diverse career opportunities within healthcare beyond clinical roles. According to Brendan Collins, Adventist Health Office of Mission program manager and class instructor, Heinrich's observations were inspiring, and his engagement with students was well received.
Jun 03, 2025
Adventist Health Glendale, which is part of the Adventist Health system headquartered in Roseville, CA, announced on June 3, 2025, that it has earned its 21st consecutive “A” grade for Hospital Safety from The Leapfrog Group. This achievement is significant as only a small percentage of hospitals nationwide have accomplished this milestone. The Leapfrog Hospital Safety Grade is a peer-reviewed, transparent rating system that focuses on preventing medical errors and patient harm. Earning 21 consecutive 'A' grades demonstrates Adventist Health Glendale's consistent dedication to maintaining high standards of patient safety. The hospital highlighted that this rating underscores its commitment to being a safe, caring, and reliable provider of expert-level care, and they are the only hospital in their service area with an active 'A' grade from Leapfrog in the Spring 2025 cycle.
Apr 30, 2025
Adventist Health, headquartered in Roseville, California, has been recognized as one of Becker's Hospital Review's 150 top places to work in healthcare for 2025. This recognition highlights the faith-based, nonprofit health system's commitment to delivering compassionate, whole-person care across its numerous locations on the West Coast and in Hawai'i. Adventist Health is noted for fostering a supportive environment grounded in spiritual values and community commitment, where employees are encouraged to find personal purpose in their work. The system provides comprehensive employee well-being programs, including medical benefits, mental health resources like SyncTALK, chaplaincy services, and affordable healthcare plans, along with prioritizing career development through internal promotions, tuition assistance, leadership residencies, and educational partnerships. The organization has demonstrated strong employee retention, with a decrease in turnover and a significant percentage of leadership positions filled internally, and has also received recognition from Forbes and Newsweek as a top employer, blending compassionate care, employee support, and civic engagement.
Apr 23, 2025
Adventist HealthCare, based in Gaithersburg, Maryland (a separate entity from Adventist Health with headquarters in Roseville, CA), has implemented AI-driven software called KATE AI in its emergency departments to improve patient safety by helping clinicians identify high-risk conditions such as sepsis and heart attack. [2, 11] The tool analyzes patient symptoms against medical records and provides recommendations for the appropriate level of care. [2, 11] Emergency teams are utilizing KATE AI to verify care plans for patients at higher risk of serious conditions, and while there was initial concern about it slowing down workflows, emergency teams have found the opposite to be true, according to Seleem Choudhury, DNP, COO of Adventist HealthCare Shady Grove Medical Center. [2, 11] The technology is designed to support teams in recognizing 100 high-risk presentations and also aims to improve health equity by identifying and eliminating potential biases in triage.
