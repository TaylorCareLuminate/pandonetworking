Jun 10, 2025
University Hospitals Harrington Heart & Vascular Institute is the first facility in Ohio to utilize the FDA-approved Flyrcado™ (flurpiridaz F 18) injection for PET MPI cardiac stress tests in patients with known or suspected coronary artery disease (CAD). This novel F 18 radiopharmaceutical enhances the ability of physicians to detect CAD and blockages in heart arteries that can lead to heart attacks. Flyrcado is an advanced PET myocardial perfusion imaging agent that precisely assesses heart function under stress. It offers improved image quality compared to traditional SPECT imaging, particularly for patients who may be more challenging to image. A significant advantage of Flyrcado is its longer half-life, which allows for centralized manufacturing and wider distribution, increasing the accessibility of advanced cardiac imaging. The extended decay time also provides clinicians with the flexibility to perform rescans during the same appointment if necessary to improve image quality. [4, 9, 16, 17]
