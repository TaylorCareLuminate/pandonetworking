Recent News

Jun 13, 2025
New insights into the genetic factors influencing the survival and growth of brain tumors, specifically gliomas including glioblastoma, have been published in the journal Nature Communications by a research team from the Keck School of Medicine of USC and City of Hope. This preclinical study utilized advanced technology, including DNA and RNA sequencing and a cutting-edge method called spatial transcriptomics, to analyze key genes within brain tumor cells and their surrounding microenvironment. The findings reveal the complex genetics at play, particularly the role of extrachromosomal DNA (ecDNA) in driving rapid cancer cell proliferation and contributing to genetic instability within tumors. This heterogeneity within a single tumor makes treatment challenging, but the researchers suggest these tools can help understand the tumor-environment relationship in new ways, potentially paving the way for more personalized and targeted therapies. Gabriel Zada, MD, a professor of neurological surgery and physiology and neuroscience and director of the USC Brain Tumor Center, highlighted that these tools could inform personalized medicine approaches, potentially involving delivering different treatments to target distinct cell populations within a tumor. The study provides foundational knowledge aimed at improving precision medicine for brain cancer patients. [1, 2, 3, 4]
Jun 10, 2025
The Keck Medicine of USC corporate communications team was recognized for national public relations excellence, receiving a Silver Anvil Award during the Public Relations Society of America (PRSA) 2025 Anvil Awards ceremony held in May in New York. The team won in the Multicultural Public Relations category for their "Hearing Aids Save Lives" media campaign. This campaign effectively promoted a study by Keck Medicine otolaryngologist Dr. Janet Choi, which suggested that hearing aid use could extend the lives of individuals with hearing loss by 24%. The multi-pronged media strategy for this campaign resulted in 652 media placements and reached an audience of 142 million, marking the second time in recent years that Keck Medicine has been honored at the annual PRSA awards.
Jun 10, 2025
In recognition of the Juneteenth holiday, Keck Medicine of USC has organized several community events. These celebrations, hosted by Keck Medicine's B.L.A.C.K. Med employee resource group, are taking place across multiple Keck Medicine locations, including the Health Sciences Campus, USC Arcadia Hospital, and USC Verdugo Hills Hospital. These events commemorate the emancipation of enslaved Black people in the United States and are part of Keck Medicine's broader efforts in community relations and fostering inclusion and belonging within the health system.
Jun 04, 2025
A new study led by the USC Schaeffer Center, part of Keck Medicine of USC, revealed that spending by California hospitals on supplemental payments to contracted medical groups in key specialties has steadily increased over the past two decades. The findings, published in the journal Health Affairs, indicate that hospitals across the country utilize these stipends to help address staffing needs. The study highlights that despite concerns about rising hospital labor costs, these stipends have received limited attention, partly due to a lack of comprehensive data on their usage. By leveraging California's specific hospital financial reporting requirements, researchers were able to track this spending within the state over time.
Jun 04, 2025
Through longstanding advocacy and outreach efforts by the USC Brain Tumor Center, which is part of Keck Medicine of USC, the City of Los Angeles, Los Angeles County, and the State of California have officially proclaimed May 2025 as Brain Tumor Awareness Month. Assemblymember Mike Fong successfully presented a motion in the California State Assembly on May 1st to declare this, emphasizing the importance of public awareness, advocacy, support for research, and education regarding the impact of brain tumors. On May 13th, the Los Angeles County Board of Supervisors unanimously approved a motion honoring the resilience of individuals with brain tumors and the work of healthcare professionals and researchers, noting that Los Angeles County has the highest number of brain tumor diagnoses and treatments in California and commending the USC Brain Tumor Center's contributions. The City of Los Angeles also issued a formal declaration on May 30th, with Councilmember Adrin Nazarian presenting the proclamation to the USC Brain Tumor Center team.

Chris Allen

Chief Financial Officer

Chris Allen serves as the Chief Financial Officer at Keck Medicine of USC. He officially began this role on August 31, 2023, having previously served as interim CFO for Keck Medicine and CFO for Keck Medical Center of USC. Allen holds a Bachelor of Arts degree in business administration and finance from the University of Georgia and a certificate in direct costing and contribution reporting from the University of Wisconsin School of Business. He has over 20 years of finance leadership experience, primarily in healthcare. His tenure at Keck Medicine of USC began in 2016 as the executive administrator for financial planning and decision support. Before joining Keck Medicine of USC, Allen held CFO positions at Adventist Health Glendale Medical Center and served as interim CFO at the University of California Irvine Medical Center.
