Jun 16, 2025
Ochsner LSU Health – Spring Lake is hosting a summer health fair on Friday, June 20, 2025, from 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. at the Spring Lake Clinic located at 8445 Line Avenue in Shreveport. The event aims to provide the community with easy access to essential health services and screenings, encouraging individuals to be informed about their health metrics. Ochsner LSU Health staff will be on-site offering a variety of health checks and services, such as blood pressure measurements, A1C testing for diabetes monitoring, screenings using a diabetic eye camera to detect diabetic retinopathy, mammograms, various cancer screenings, cervical self-swabs for HPV detection, assistance with scheduling colonoscopies, and sign-ups for Cologuard®, a noninvasive colon cancer screening method. The fair will also include enhanced wellness visits specifically for Medicare patients. While many of the services offered will be free of charge, some will be billed to attendees' insurance plans. The event will also feature giveaways throughout the day, and attendees will have the opportunity to schedule follow-up appointments for further care. [10]
Jun 03, 2025
LSU Health Shreveport (LSUHS) has received continued accreditation with no citations from the Accreditation Council for Graduate Medical Education (ACGME). The ACGME is an independent organization responsible for setting and monitoring professional education standards to ensure physicians are prepared to deliver safe and high-quality medical care. They also oversee the accreditation of residency and fellowship programs across the United States. This continued accreditation signifies that LSU Health Shreveport's graduate medical education programs meet the rigorous standards set by the ACGME.

LSU Health Shreveport celebrated its largest commencement in the school's history, with 242 graduates receiving degrees from the Schools of Applied Health Professions, Graduate Studies, and Medicine. This event highlights the academic mission of the partnership between Ochsner Health and LSU Health Shreveport, focusing on training the next generation of healthcare professionals for North Louisiana and beyond. The commencement is a significant milestone for the institution and the graduating students who will enter various healthcare fields. [9, 27]
May 02, 2025
LSU Health Shreveport, in partnership with the LSU Health Sciences Foundation, hosted the inaugural Rasberry Lecture on Thursday, May 1, 2025. The featured visiting lecturer was Michael A. Curran, PhD, a Professor of Immunology from MD Anderson Cancer Center, who spoke on cancer immunotherapy. This lecture series was established through the generosity of W. Clinton “Bubba” Rasberry and his family via the Rasberry Family Endowed Lecture Series, created in 2024. The series aims to bring renowned biomedical leaders to LSU Health Shreveport to share the latest advancements in medical knowledge, science, and technology with the community, benefiting faculty, students, and ultimately patient care at affiliated hospitals like Ochsner LSU Health Shreveport. May 01, 2025
Ochsner LSU Health - Monroe Medical Center received an 'A' Hospital Safety Grade from The Leapfrog Group, an independent national nonprofit watchdog focused on patient safety. This is the first time the Monroe facility has achieved this rating, and it is currently the only hospital in northeast Louisiana to hold this grade. The recognition highlights the medical center's commitment to reducing preventable medical errors, accidents, injuries, and infections, reflecting the dedication of its medical staff and care teams to upholding high standards of quality and safety.
