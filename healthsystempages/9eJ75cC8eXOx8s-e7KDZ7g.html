Recent News

Jun 16, 2025
The Allegheny Health Network West Penn Burn Center recently hosted its annual pediatric summer camp for young burn survivors, Camp KOALA (Kids Overcoming, Adapting, Learning and Achieving). The five-day immersive camp, held at Camp Kon-O-Kwee Spencer in Fombell from June 7-11, provides a safe and supportive environment for nearly 30 adolescents who have experienced burn injuries. The camp allows the children to engage in typical summer camp activities and connect with others who have similar experiences, fostering healing both mentally and physically. The initiative is a significant program offered by AHN's Burn Center to support the recovery and well-being of young burn survivors.
Jun 16, 2025
Allegheny Health Network Schools of Nursing has partnered with Slippery Rock University (SRU) to offer a new 1+2+1 nursing degree pathway program. This innovative program is designed to help address the critical shortage of registered nurses in Pennsylvania. Through this pathway, students will complete their first year of general education courses at SRU, their second and third years earning their RN at a partner institution like AHN Schools of Nursing, and their final year completing their Bachelor of Science in Nursing degree back at SRU or online. This collaboration aims to provide an efficient and cost-effective route for aspiring nurses to enter the workforce.
Jun 15, 2025
A unique family trio of neurosurgeons, Drs. Donald, Alexander, and Benjamin Whiting, are all practicing at Allegheny Health Network's Neuroscience Institute. Dr. Donald Whiting, the father, is a senior clinical executive at AHN and is known for his work in Deep Brain Stimulation surgery for movement disorders. His son, Alexander, serves as the director of epilepsy surgery within the network, while his other son, Benjamin, specializes in spine surgery. The article highlights their shared profession and dedication to patient care within the same health system, noting their challenging work and commitment to their family life.
Jun 12, 2025
Allegheny Health Network is planning a $6.2 million investment to expand inpatient services at two of its hospitals: Allegheny Valley Hospital in Natrona Heights and Forbes Hospital in Monroeville. At Allegheny Valley Hospital, the inpatient rehabilitation unit will nearly double in size. Forbes Hospital will gain 20 new medical-surgical beds. These expansions are intended to address the increasing needs of patients in AHN's eastern service region and improve patient flow, particularly in busy areas like the emergency department. Construction at Allegheny Valley Hospital is expected to begin in July 2025 and be completed by the end of the year, resulting in a consolidated unit with 29 inpatient rehabilitation beds. The expansion at Forbes Hospital involves converting its former inpatient rehab unit to accommodate the additional medical-surgical beds, aiming to reduce wait times and enhance the overall patient experience.
Jun 10, 2025
Allegheny Health Network and health insurance provider Cigna are facing a potential contract split that could result in thousands of Cigna commercially insured members losing in-network access to AHN providers starting July 1, 2025. AHN patients who use Cigna were notified of the impasse in negotiations for a new contract. Approximately 20,000 Cigna members have utilized an AHN provider within the past year. This dispute specifically impacts commercially insured members and does not affect Medicare or Medicare Advantage members, as Cigna sold its Medicare-related business to Health Care Service Corp. in March 2025. This is reportedly the second contract dispute between AHN and Cigna in the last two years.

Brian Devine

Chief Financial Officer

Brian Devine is the Chief Financial Officer for Allegheny Health Network (AHN), a role he assumed in January 2024. In this position, he is responsible for overseeing AHN's financial performance, including accounting, revenue cycle operations, budget planning, and capital investments. Before becoming CFO at AHN, Devine served as CFO and Senior Vice President of Finance & Commercialization for enGen, a subsidiary of Highmark Health. His history with Highmark Health and its subsidiaries is extensive, including roles such as Vice President of Enterprise Financial Strategy, Planning and Analysis, Director of Transformation, and Vice President of Financial Operations and Clinical Transformation. He also previously served as Vice President of Finance for AHN from 2016 to 2018. Earlier in his career, Devine worked as a Manager of Operational Finance at RTI International Metals and started as an Audit Associate at PricewaterhouseCoopers. Academically, Brian Devine holds a Bachelor's degree in Accounting and Management Information Systems from Pennsylvania State University and an MBA in Finance and Strategic Management from the University of Pittsburgh.

Angela Costa, RN, BSN, MPM

Chief Nurse Executive

Angela Costa, RN, BSN, MPM, serves as the Chief Nurse Executive for Allegheny Health Network (AHN), a role she has held since early 2023, after serving as interim CNE. With over 30 years of her nursing career spent at AHN, starting at Allegheny General Hospital, she has held various roles including supervisor, manager, director, and VP positions at different AHN hospitals. Ms. Costa holds a Bachelor of Science in Nursing from Duquesne University and a Master of Public Policy and Management from Carnegie Mellon University. Her clinical background encompasses trauma, critical care, and neurology/stroke. As Chief Nurse Executive, she is the advocate for AHN's 6,000 nurses and contributes to the executive leadership team. She has spearheaded initiatives focused on nurse recruitment and retention, improving the quality of nursing care, and developing flexible work arrangements like the "Work Your Way" program, which introduced a mobile internal nursing team and digital nursing roles. Additionally, Costa is committed to preventing workplace violence and promoting nurse well-being. She is actively involved in several organizations and boards, including the Allegheny Credit Union board, the Hospital and Health System Association of Pennsylvania's Nurse Ambassador Advocacy Initiative, the American Organization for Nursing Leadership, Partner for Work, and Palladium's board of directors.
