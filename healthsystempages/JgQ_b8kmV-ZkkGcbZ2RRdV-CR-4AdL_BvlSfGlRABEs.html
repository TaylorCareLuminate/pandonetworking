Recent News

May 28, 2025
Dr. Lynn Fitzgibbons has joined the Cottage Health Board of Directors. This appointment is a recent development in the leadership and governance of Cottage Health. Further details about Dr. Fitzgibbons' background and the impact of her appointment on the board's strategic direction are available in the source article.
May 23, 2025
Cottage Health highlighted its available healthcare options for the Memorial Day weekend, including technology-based services. Among the options mentioned is Cottage Virtual Care, which provides 24/7 access to healthcare providers for minor illnesses and injuries, allowing patients to receive care from their homes. This service underscores Cottage Health's utilization of telehealth technology to expand access to care beyond traditional in-person visits.
May 17, 2025
Cottage Health has significantly increased its down payment assistance program for employees, doubling the amount offered up to a maximum of $500,000. This initiative is aimed at addressing the challenges employees face with high housing costs in the Santa Barbara area and is a strategy to attract and retain talent. Eligible employees contributing at least 5% of the down payment can receive up to 40% of the home's price. This expansion of employee benefits is also linked to Cottage Health's broader efforts to provide employee housing, including two projects announced in September 2024 that will add 204 housing units in Santa Barbara and Goleta by 2029. The program demonstrates Cottage Health's focus on workforce support and community relations by helping employees reside in the communities they serve. [16]
May 09, 2025
Ron Werft, President Emeritus of Cottage Health, was honored with the American Hospital Association's (AHA) 2025 Board of Trustees Award. This national recognition highlights his substantial contributions to the hospital field and the AHA. Werft, who retired on April 7, 2025, after a 38-year career with Cottage Health, including 25 years as President and CEO, was recognized for his leadership in expanding access to care, developing new facilities like the Santa Barbara Cottage Hospital and Goleta Valley Cottage Hospital, and establishing key initiatives such as the Cottage Center for Population Health and the Cottage Health Research Institute. His efforts also included championing partnerships for workforce development and addressing housing needs for healthcare professionals. This award is a significant recognition of his impact both locally and nationally. [8, 18]
May 08, 2025
Cottage Health announced on May 8, 2025, that specialized heart surgery is now available closer to home. This indicates an expansion of services in the area of cardiac care, allowing patients to receive advanced treatment locally within the Cottage Health system. [5]

Lisa Moore

Executive Vice President and Chief Operating Officer

Lisa Moore is the Executive Vice President and Chief Operating Officer at Cottage Health. She leads the operations of Santa Barbara Cottage Hospital, including Cottage Rehabilitation Hospital and Cottage Children's Medical Center, Goleta Valley Cottage Hospital, Santa Ynez Valley Cottage Hospital, and Pacific Diagnostic Laboratories. Moore joined Cottage Health in 1991 as an administrative fellow. She served as Vice President for Clinical Services since 2000, providing administrative leadership for many of the hospitals' procedural areas. Her accomplishments in this role included the development and growth of regional programs for Cottage Health, and she also served as the lead executive for government relations. She is a graduate of St. Olaf College and earned her Master's degree in Hospital and Health Care Administration from the University of Minnesota. She completed her residency at St. Mary's Hospital in London. Moore was selected as a Fellow for the American Hospital Association Next Generation Leadership Innovation Program in 2020 and was a Fellow of the California Healthcare Leadership College in 2005.

Kristin Tufvesson

Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer

Kristin Tufvesson is the Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer at Cottage Health. She started her career at Santa Barbara Cottage Hospital in 1991 and has held various significant roles within Cottage Health for over thirty years, including interim senior vice president/CFO, vice president, finance and controller, director of financial services, and director of financial planning and decision support. She holds a bachelor's degree in business administration from Minnesota State University in Mankato and a master's degree in business administration from the University of Phoenix.

Stacy Bratcher

Katy Bazylewicz

CMO and Vice President, Chief Marketing Officer and Vice President for Population Health

Katy Bazylewicz is the Chief Marketing Officer and Vice President for Population Health at Cottage Health, a position she has held since joining the organization as Vice President of Marketing in July 2014. She possesses 25 years of marketing expertise, with responsibilities at Cottage Health encompassing strategic marketing, public and media relations, community relations, and communications across all hospitals and affiliates. She is also an Advisory Board member for YouthWell, an organization focused on youth mental health.
