Jun 12, 2025
The Cook Children's Healthy Homes Asthma Program is making a significant impact by helping families in its eight-county service area identify and reduce asthma triggers within their homes. Established in 2016, the program's goal is to manage environmental factors like dust, dander, and mold that exacerbate asthma symptoms, thereby preventing the need for emergency care. The program works by having Community Health Workers connect with families whose children have had frequent asthma-related emergency or urgent care visits. These workers provide personalized support over three months, including conducting home assessments, assisting with obtaining necessary supplies and services, and facilitating referrals to social and medical support systems. The Healthy Homes program has demonstrated remarkable success, with data showing an 85% reduction in emergency room visits for enrolled families in the year following their participation, highlighting its effectiveness in improving children's health outcomes.
Jun 10, 2025
Cook Children's Health Care System in Fort Worth, Texas, is conducting a national search for Pediatric Pulmonologists. The health system is seeking individuals interested in general pediatric pulmonology or sleep medicine to join their established department of eight pulmonologists and two sleep physicians. This opportunity involves providing inpatient and consultative services, working within units such as a 16-bed technology-dependent unit and an accredited Cystic Fibrosis center that cares for nearly 200 patients. The integrated system includes a 477-bed quaternary care pediatric hospital, a physician network, and multiple primary and specialty care offices across North and West Texas. The position offers opportunities for teaching and clinical research, with support available through a campus IRB, grant writing assistance, research nurses, and a dedicated biostatistician. Qualified candidates may also be eligible for a fellow's retention program.
Jun 10, 2025
Cook Children's Health Care System is also seeking a Child Psychiatrist to join the Jane and John Justin Institute for Mind Health and the Department of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry in Fort Worth, TX. This national search aims to fill a position within a collaborative clinical initiative located in the Institute for Mind Health, which opened in October 2023. The Institute provides a central location for the care of children with nervous system disorders, housing various specialties including Neurology, Neurosurgery, Psychology, and Psychiatry. The role is primarily focused on outpatient clinical practice, with opportunities for strong interdisciplinary collaboration with related specialties within the Institute. Candidates, including 2025/2026 fellows, are encouraged to apply and can explore developing subspecialty interests in caring for the psychiatric needs of children and adolescents with comorbid neurologic conditions. Clinical research and teaching opportunities are also available.
Jun 09, 2025
During a recent Regional Child Health Summit, Cook Children's Health Care System presented analytics related to unintentional injuries among children in their service area. According to Cook Children's analytics, from 2021 to 2023, the health system treated 23 patients for firearm-related injuries and 44 for injuries from other gun types (such as BB guns or airsoft guns). The data also revealed that approximately one in five children in their service area live in a home where a gun is not always safely stored. Furthermore, analytics from 2021 to 2023 showed that Cook Children's Medical Centers in Fort Worth and Prosper treated 224 drowning-related injuries. The summit also highlighted the prevalence of Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs) based on survey data, indicating that about 18% of children in the service area had two or more ACEs, with difficulty covering basic needs and caregiver divorce being the most common. Cook Children's analytics are clearly being utilized to understand significant child health issues and inform community health efforts. [9]
Jun 02, 2025
Cook Children's Center for Community Health was scheduled to host the second session of their Regional Child Health Summits on June 3, 2025. The summit, starting at 9 a.m. CST, aimed to provide updates on data specifically related to the oral health, mental health, injury prevention, and caregiver support of children within their eight-county service area, which includes Collin, Denton, Grayson, Hood, Johnson, Parker, Tarrant, and Wise counties. This session was a continuation of focusing on priorities identified in their first formal assessment and strengthening efforts in areas like asthma, caregiver support, healthy lifestyles, injury prevention, mental health, and oral health. The data presented is derived from Cook Children's comprehensive Community Health Needs Assessment (CHNA), which is conducted every three years to understand the evolving health needs of children aged 0-17 in their service area. The summit was also expected to highlight the prevalence and impact of Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs) and examine the importance of oral health as a critical part of a child's overall well-being, emphasizing education and awareness.
