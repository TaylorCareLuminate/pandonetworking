Recent News

Jun 05, 2025
Blue Mountain Hospital District has announced the recipients of its 2025 scholarships. The district awarded scholarships to six local students: Fallan Giffin, Adeline Northway, Morgan Majors, Olivia Thunell, Madalynn Grassl, and Elena Ciochetti. These scholarships are intended to support students from the area who are pursuing further education and careers in healthcare fields, including radiological sciences, exercise science/chiropractic care, nursing, speech-language pathology, anthropology/sociology (for counseling), and biology (for cardiothoracic surgery). The hospital district expressed pride in supporting these students and highlighted their dedication and passion for healthcare.
May 07, 2025
Misty Robertson has been named the new Chief Executive Officer for the Blue Mountain Hospital District in John Day, OR, following an extensive search. Robertson previously served as the Chief Operating Officer and Chief Nursing Officer at St. Luke's Nampa and Fruitland Medical Centers in Idaho. Her background includes leadership roles in emergency and critical care, as well as chief nursing officer positions at critical access and larger hospitals. Robertson is noted for her focus on improving quality, safety, and the patient experience, areas often intertwined with technology adoption and effective use. She holds a Doctor of Nursing Practice in Leadership and a Master of Science in Health Services Administration. Her official start date is scheduled for June 25, 2025.
Apr 29, 2025
Blue Mountain Hospital in John Day was nearing the conclusion of its search for a new CEO, with the hospital's board of directors scheduling a special meeting for April 30, 2025, to potentially offer the position to one of three finalists. The search for a new chief executive commenced following the resignation of Cam Marlowe, who stepped down effective December 1, 2024, for health reasons. Margie Molitor assumed the role of interim CEO on December 2, marking her second time serving in this capacity for the hospital. The rigorous selection process involved reviewing over 74 applications, which were subsequently narrowed down to 13 candidates for initial interviews, and ultimately, three finalists were selected for final interviews. The board prioritized candidates with prior CEO experience, experience in critical access hospitals, a connection or interest in Eastern Oregon, and strong educational backgrounds with advanced degrees. Apr 28, 2025
According to community benefits reporting information from the Oregon Health Authority, Blue Mountain Hospital utilized 13.5% of its total operating revenue in 2024 for community benefits. These benefits include covering Medicaid shortfalls, funding other community programs, and providing a charity care program that offers full medical cost coverage for eligible patients who cannot afford to pay. Hospital Chief Financial Officer Mike Ellis noted that public and non-profit hospitals are required to have such charity care policies, which are need-based and require applicants to provide financial information for eligibility determination based on factors like the national poverty scale, income, and resources.
Apr 02, 2025
To address a persistent nursing shortage, Blue Mountain Hospital is launching an in-house nursing education program through a partnership with Joyce University of Nursing and Health Sciences. This distance-learning program will allow local residents to pursue a nursing degree without leaving the John Day area, a strategy aimed at 'growing their own nurses' as recruiting medical professionals to a remote rural community is challenging. The hospital is also in final discussions with Western Governors University for a four-year distance-learning program. The Oregon Board of Nursing recently approved Western Governors University's plan to proceed with the program at the hospital. The program with Joyce University is expected to begin classes for its first cohort in the fall, with significant community interest already noted.

Mike Ellis is the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) at Blue Mountain Hospital District. He is involved with the hospital's community benefits program and charity care policies.

Deborah Morris

Chief Nursing Officer

Deborah Morris, the Chief Nursing Officer at Blue Mountain Hospital District, has a professional background that includes extensive experience in various nursing roles. She has worked as a Critical Care nurse, a Charge nurse in Critical Care, an Emergency Department nurse and Unit Educator, and an Infusion nurse. Her experience also extends to clinical education, having served as a Clinical Education Program Coordinator for a regional hospital and an American Heart Training Center Coordinator with certifications including BLS, ACLS, and PALS instructor. Morris has also been a member of the Blue Mountain Hospital District board during the periods of 2017-2020 and from 2024 onwards. She is currently involved in initiatives to establish a local nursing education program to help address the nursing shortage in the area.
