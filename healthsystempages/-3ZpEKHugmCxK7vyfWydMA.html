Recent News

Jun 30, 2025
MUSC Health's contract with Humana Medicare Advantage and Humana's Healthy Horizons Medicaid insurance is set to expire on June 30, 2025. MUSC Health has indicated that they have been working with Humana for the past two years to reach a resolution regarding how claims are reviewed and paid by Humana. Despite these efforts, MUSC Health remains concerned about the number of denials for necessary treatment plans ordered by their physicians. These denials are viewed as causing unnecessary delays in patient treatment and creating anxiety for patients. MUSC Health is advising patients covered by these Humana plans that they will need to switch plans or find an in-network provider if they wish to continue receiving care within the MUSC Health network after the expiration date. Patients with a Humana Medicare Supplemental plan or TriCare are not impacted by this contract termination. MUSC Health emphasizes its commitment to providing exceptional care and believes that health care decisions are best made between a doctor and a patient. They have provided information and resources for patients to explore other insurance options that have contracts with MUSC Health for 2025.

Jun 16, 2025
The Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC) College of Medicine has received a historic and transformational anonymous donation of $50 million. This is the largest single gift in MUSC's 200-year history. The substantial funds are intended to accelerate MUSC's mission of improving health outcomes for South Carolinians and addressing critical health challenges facing the state, such as challenging health outcomes and a rapidly aging population. The donation will support the College of Medicine's commitment to innovative medical education, allowing for the expansion of training opportunities to help address the significant shortage of healthcare providers in South Carolina. Additionally, the gift will enable MUSC to attract, train, and retain talented physicians, renowned faculty, and researchers, which is expected to positively impact the lives of students, patients, and their families. Part of this vision includes supporting a new home for the College of Medicine, currently under construction, which will serve as a hub for collaboration, discovery, and innovation in medical education and research. Jun 13, 2025
MUSC Health has integrated AI Agents to automate revenue cycle tasks, resulting in significant improvements in operational efficiency and revenue potential. By deploying these digital workers, which can automate complex, multi-step tasks and adapt, MUSC Health has seen pre-visit completion rates increase by 88%, from 25% to 47%. Additionally, no-show rates have been nearly halved, dropping from 14% to 8% through the use of targeted appointment reminders. Time-of-service copay collections have also risen to 52%, an increase from the previous 44%. This initiative is part of a broader strategy to leverage technology to address challenges like a high support staff-to-provider ratio, workforce attrition, and open revenue cycle positions, shifting the focus from hiring more staff to utilizing technology to enhance capabilities. The AI Agents handle tasks including eligibility checks, insurance verification, copay collection, and appointment reminders across the MUSC Health enterprise. This approach is aimed at freeing up staff to concentrate on patient care and strategic initiatives, thereby increasing revenue.
