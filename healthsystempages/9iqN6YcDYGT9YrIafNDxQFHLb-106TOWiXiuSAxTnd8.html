Recent News

Jun 02, 2025
In the ongoing litigation case of Daniel H. Golden, as Litigation Trustee of the QHC v. Community Health Systems, Inc. et al., a Notice of Withdrawal of certain Pro Hac Vice Counsel was filed by the CHSI Defendants on June 2, 2025. This filing is part of the larger adversary proceeding case 21-51190 related to the Quorum Health Corporation bankruptcy case (20-10766). The litigation was initiated on October 25, 2021, by the Litigation Trustee and Wilmington Savings Fund Society against Community Health Systems, Inc. and related entities and officers, as well as Credit Suisse Securities (USA), LLC. The complaint asserts various claims, including intentional and constructive fraudulent transfers and breach of contract, stemming from Quorum's 2016 spin-off from Community Health Systems.
May 26, 2025
Quorum Health utilizes Brightly Software's TheWorxHub, a computerized maintenance management system (CMMS), to improve productivity in their biomedical and facilities maintenance departments. Quorum Health needed an updated system as their previous one for managing biomedical assets was only compatible with Internet Explorer, which was losing support. They sought a system that could handle work orders for both biomedical programs and facilities maintenance and be accessible via a mobile app to keep technicians in the field. TheWorxHub was selected by a vote within Quorum Health. Since implementing TheWorxHub, Quorum Health has been able to provide technicians with more time in the field by allowing real-time updates from mobile devices, streamline work order maintenance, simplify reporting with intuitive systems, and gain real-time visibility into preventive maintenance. The implementation included training and support from the Brightly team.
May 14, 2025
Quorum Health, headquartered in Brentwood, TN, has been selected by Modern Healthcare as one of the 2025 Best Places to Work in Healthcare. This award program recognizes outstanding employers in the healthcare industry nationwide based on an assessment process that includes an extensive employee survey. Chris Harrison, CEO of Quorum Health, stated that this recognition is a testament to the organizational transformation journey and the evolution of their culture to align with values of integrity, accountability, collaboration, and community. Quorum Health will learn its specific ranking on the Best Places list at the 2025 Best Places to Work in Healthcare Awards Gala on October 8th in Nashville, TN. [7]
Apr 24, 2025
A lawsuit has been filed in Ector County District Court by an Odessa couple against Odessa Regional Medical Center, Quorum Health Corporation, and a doctor of osteopathic medicine. The lawsuit alleges that negligence on the part of the hospital staff and doctor led to their baby suffering irreversible brain damage at birth on June 29, 2023. According to the lawsuit, the mother presented to the hospital with severe pain during contractions at 36.5 weeks pregnant and despite being told she would be scheduled for a C-section, pain medications were not alleviating her pain. The lawsuit further alleges that the hospital "intentionally and in bad faith or negligently breached its duty" by failing to preserve the fetal heart tracing, which the plaintiffs claim the hospital knew or should have known would be material and relevant to a potential claim. Odessa Regional Medical Center has a policy of not commenting on pending litigation. [12]
Mar 31, 2025
Bay Area Hospital has extended its negotiation talks with Quorum Health. This follows a previous report that the hospital defaulted on a $47 million loan after not meeting its promised profit margin. Bay Area Hospital had announced plans in December to partner with Quorum Health as a strategy for financial recovery from the impact of COVID-19. According to a press release, the partnership negotiations have been extended past the original target date of March 31 for a definitive agreement. Hospital President and CEO Brian Moore emphasized the importance of getting the partnership right despite the urgency of their financial situation. [3]
