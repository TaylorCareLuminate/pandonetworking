Recent News

Jun 13, 2025
The CHOC Research Institute recently held its 2025 strategy retreat, marking a significant development as Rady Children's Hospital-San Diego was included for the first time. This inclusion signals a new phase under the combined Rady Children's Health umbrella. The retreat served as a platform for leaders and collaborators to evaluate progress, share updates, and align on future priorities in pediatric research. Dr. Terry Sanger, CHOC's chief scientific officer, highlighted the potential of this expanding collaboration to make the combined entity a leading destination for families seeking answers and hope for their children. Key areas of focus for research include brain health, rare disease treatment, improvement science, and data science, with community-engaged research also being a vital component. The integration of Rady Children's Hospital is expected to broaden the possibilities for future research endeavors.
Jun 10, 2025
Rady Children's Hospital Foundation shared a patient story titled "Guada's Story: A Long Journey Ahead." The story features Guada, who was born prematurely at 30 weeks along with her twin sister in Mexico City. The narrative highlights the beginning of her medical journey, implying significant care and challenges from early in her life. Patient stories are a key part of community relations, illustrating the impact of the hospital's services on individual children and families.
May 20, 2025
During the Pediatric & Lifespan Data Science Conference, John Henderson, Chief Information Officer of Rady Children's Health, highlighted the ongoing work to develop an enterprise data platform. This platform is intended to accelerate data science and AI applications and research within the health system. The conference itself brought together experts to discuss challenges and advancements in pediatric and lifespan research, including the role of artificial intelligence in clinical care and data-driven decision-making for diagnoses and treatment. May 19, 2025
Rady Children's Institute for Genomic Medicine (RCIGM) announced that Alexion, AstraZeneca Rare Disease has become the first Platinum member of the BeginNGS Consortium. This collaboration aims to accelerate the adoption of newborn screening through whole genome sequencing to identify babies at risk for hundreds of genetic diseases early on and recommend effective therapies. The BeginNGS technology platform has shown promising results, reducing false positives by 97 percent and diagnosing genetic diseases earlier, potentially benefiting a significant number of infants. This partnership builds on an existing nine-year collaboration focused on rare disease diagnostics. [29]
May 12, 2025
Blue Shield of California and Children's Hospital of Orange County (CHOC), which is now part of Rady Children's Health following a recent merger, have reached a new agreement. This new contract ensures that Blue Shield members will continue to have in-network access to all CHOC facilities and providers. The agreement covers CHOC Hospital in Orange, CHOC at Mission Hospital in Mission Viejo, and the network of primary and specialty care provided by CHOC Specialists. Blue Shield emphasized their commitment as a nonprofit health plan to ensuring Californians have access to quality, affordable healthcare, highlighting the vital role children's hospitals play. Rady Children's Health, through Kim Milstien, president, CHOC Region, expressed pleasure in continuing the long-standing partnership with Blue Shield, ensuring families have uninterrupted access to pediatric care.
