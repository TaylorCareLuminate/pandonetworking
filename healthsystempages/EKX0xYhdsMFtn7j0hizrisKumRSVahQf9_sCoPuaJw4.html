Recent News

Mar 27, 2025
College Health Enterprises has acquired and is now operating College Medical Center Phoenix (CMCP) in Phoenix, Arizona. Formerly St. Luke's Behavioral Health Center under Steward Healthcare, CMCP is focused on providing behavioral health services to voluntary and court-ordered patients. College Health Enterprises plans to increase staffing and expand services at CMCP to include pediatric, adolescent, adult, and senior adult populations. This acquisition marks an expansion of College Health Enterprises' operations into Arizona, adding to their existing hospitals in California. [7]
Dec 06, 2024
St. Luke's Behavioral Health Center in Phoenix was approved to reopen by the Arizona Department of Health Services (ADHS) following necessary repairs. College Health Enterprises was approved to be the new manager and operator of the facility. The hospital had been shut down in early August 2024 by the state health department due to several problems, including a malfunctioning air conditioning system. The previous operator, Steward Health Care, filed for bankruptcy in May 2024. ADHS lifted the suspension order in accordance with a settlement agreement, allowing College Health Enterprises to open one unit with 26 beds specifically for adults needing inpatient care or those with court-ordered treatment. As part of the agreement, Steward Healthcare is required to transfer ownership of the facility by January 31, 2025. College Health Enterprises took over as the interim manager/operator in October 2024. College Health Enterprises, founded in 1973, is described as a prominent independent, privately-owned hospital and healthcare services company specializing in behavioral healthcare in Southern California. They expressed anticipation for providing behavioral healthcare services to the Phoenix community and highlighted the hospital's commitment to the community and providing exceptional care. [5, 8, 9]

Jan 07, 2024
A psychiatric hospital in Cerritos, College Hospital-Cerritos, which is an affiliate of Santa Fe Springs-based College Health Enterprises, is seeking the dismissal of most allegations in a "patient dumping" lawsuit. The lawsuit was filed in May 2019 by a special education teacher with a bipolar disorder, identified as Jane Doe, who alleges she was discharged in 2017 with little ability to care for herself and was subsequently sexually assaulted twice while homeless. The lawsuit claims the hospital failed to protect her and abandoned her before her condition stabilized by discharging her to the street. The hospital's lawyers dispute this account, stating the patient refused assistance with transportation and left on her own volition after a due process hearing. They argue the lawsuit's claims for dependent adult abuse and neglect, professional negligence, unlawful business practices, and intentional infliction of emotional distress should be dismissed based on the statute of limitations and legal immunities. The hospital also contends that the plaintiff cannot recover punitive damages. [6]
Jan 07, 2024
College Hospital-Cerritos, a psychiatric hospital operated by College Health Enterprises, is seeking the dismissal of most allegations in a lawsuit filed in May 2019. The lawsuit, brought by a former patient identified as Jane Doe, alleges "patient dumping," claiming she was discharged in 2017 with insufficient ability to care for herself despite having bipolar disorder. The plaintiff's suit in Norwalk Superior Court alleges the hospital failed to protect her from health and safety hazards and abandoned her before her condition stabilized. Hospital lawyers, however, dispute the plaintiff's account, stating she objected to extending her hold and declined post-discharge care options, leaving on her own volition. They are seeking to dismiss claims including dependent adult abuse and neglect, professional negligence, unlawful business practices, and intentional infliction of emotional distress, citing the statute of limitations and legal immunities. The hospital also argues the plaintiff cannot recover punitive damages. The lawsuit claims that while a patient, staff failed to obtain an accurate history and provide adequate treatment and post-discharge care, and that her mother was told she would not be discharged before her arrival but saw her daughter leaving the hospital alone.
