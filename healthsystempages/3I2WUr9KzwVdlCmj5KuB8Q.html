Recent News

Jun 16, 2025
Ochsner Orthopedics & Sports Medicine Institute has opened a new walk-in clinic. This clinic is located at Ochsner Health Center – Elmwood. It provides services from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Mondays through Fridays, and from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Saturdays and Sundays, offering accessible orthopedic and sports medicine care.
Jun 16, 2025
Ochsner Health has announced a new three-year agreement with Latent, formalizing their collaboration which began in November 2023. This partnership focuses on leveraging AI-driven clinical tools to streamline prior authorizations for specialty, infusion, and retail pharmacy, as well as for medical benefits. The AI platform from Latent has demonstrated significant impact, reducing prior authorization review times at Ochsner to an average of four to five minutes, which is 75% faster than industry benchmarks. This efficiency gain is aimed at accelerating time-to-therapy for patients and improving the morale of the pharmacy team by reducing administrative burden. Ochsner has designated Latent as its enterprise technology partner for medication access, planning to scale the platform across its pharmacy network for various workflows, including appeals and real-time eligibility audits, and to jointly develop new product features for future applications. Both organizations emphasize a shared vision for enhancing patient outcomes and optimizing workflows through technology.
Jun 10, 2025
Ochsner Eat Fit has relaunched its mobile app with enhanced features and a new design. The app aims to help users find nearby restaurant partners that offer dishes meeting the Eat Fit nutritional standards, which were developed by Ochsner Health's registered dietitians. The app also provides detailed nutrition information, recipe ideas, cooking videos, and curated grocery guides to support healthy eating habits both when dining out and at home.
Jun 10, 2025
According to an article in Becker's Hospital Review, Ochsner Health is leveraging ambient AI technology, specifically from DeepScribe, to help recruit and retain physicians. Dr. Jason Hill, Innovation Officer at Ochsner Health, highlighted ambient listening as a "Cinderella story" in healthcare AI, noting its high desirability among providers. While a clear financial ROI can be challenging to quantify initially, the technology offers both short-term benefits like improved documentation capture for revenue cycle management and long-term advantages in reducing physician burnout, increasing productivity, and enhancing recruitment and retention efforts in a competitive landscape. Ochsner has adopted DeepScribe's technology across its 46-hospital system, rolling it out to 500 providers, and values the vendor's co-development approach.
Jun 05, 2025
Abdul Khan, MD, has been appointed as the new chief executive officer of Ochsner River Region. In this capacity, Dr. Khan will be responsible for overseeing Ochsner facilities and the healthcare services provided in the areas of Kenner, Luling, Destrehan, and LaPlace. This includes leadership of Ochsner Medical Center – Kenner, Ochsner Medical Complex - River Parishes, and St. Charles Parish Hospital.
