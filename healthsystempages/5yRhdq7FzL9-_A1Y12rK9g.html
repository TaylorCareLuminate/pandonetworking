Jun 09, 2025
Cone Health has announced the appointment of Shannon Scaturro, CRNA, FACHE, as its new Executive Vice President and Chief Operating Officer. Scaturro will be responsible for leading operational teams across the entire health system. He takes over the role from Bernie Sherry, who was named interim Chief Executive Officer on June 1st following the retirement of Dr. Mary Jo Cagle. Scaturro is an experienced senior executive with a strong background in leading operations for large integrated healthcare systems. Prior to joining Cone Health, he served as the system Chief Operating Officer for the University of South Alabama Health System, where he also previously held the position of CEO of University Hospital. His prior experience also includes senior leadership roles in operations and surgical services at Ascension St. Vincent's Hospital and Ascension Providence Hospital. Scaturro holds an Executive Master of Science in Healthcare Administration from the University of Alabama at Birmingham and a Master of Science in Nursing, Anesthesia from the University of Tennessee. He is also a Fellow with the American College of Healthcare Executives. Cone Health is a nonprofit healthcare system based in Greensboro, NC, with approximately 13,000 employees and more than 150 locations, including five hospitals. [1, 2, 3, 4]
Jun 02, 2025
Dr. Matthew Tsuei, a general surgeon and president of Central Carolina Surgery in Greensboro, discussed how Cone Health transformed its operating room access and efficiency using AI. As the former chair of Cone Health's surgical governance committee, Dr. Tsuei played a key role in implementing changes to OR coordination across the five-hospital system. The changes involved modernizing scheduling and governance by moving from manual processes to a system providing real-time, transparent access to scheduling data. Cone Health partnered with LeanTaaS to implement iQueue for Operating Rooms, which helped right-size block allocations, increase open time, and streamline block adjustments. This data-driven approach allowed for identifying underutilized time and reallocating it effectively. The results have been substantial, including saving over 220 labor hours per day systemwide, nurses gaining back an average of 65 minutes per shift, and a 98% reduction in vendor coordination calls. Improved staffed room utilization and increased access for urgent cases were also achieved without adding new ORs. [27]

Cone Health has been honored with two Bronze Awards in the 46th Annual Telly Awards. These awards recognize outstanding video and television content across various platforms. The two videos specifically recognized were part of Cone Health's "Get Screened" campaign, which aimed to increase awareness and encourage early detection of colon and prostate cancers. According to Cone Health, this campaign successfully increased the number of completed at-home colorectal cancer tests by 25%. The health system was also a finalist in a third category. The Telly Awards celebrate excellence in storytelling and video production and received over 13,000 global entries this year.

May 15, 2025
For the fifth consecutive time, Cone Health has earned the prestigious 2025-26 Great Place to Work Certification™. This recognition is significant as it is based entirely on feedback from the health system's own team members regarding their work experience. According to Michelle Adamolekun, Cone Health Chief People & Culture Officer, this certification reflects the dedication and compassionate nature of their staff and the strength of their workplace culture. Vi-Anne Antrum, DNP, RN

Chief Nursing Officer

Dr. Vi-Anne Antrum, DNP, RN, is the System Chief Nursing Officer and a Senior Vice President at Cone Health. She is a seasoned executive with over 25 years of nursing and operations experience. She also completed Harvard University's managing healthcare delivery executive education program. She is a Fellow of the American College of Healthcare Executives (ACHE) and serves as Regent for North Carolina. She received the ACHE Distinguished Service Award in 2016. Dr. Antrum is also a member of the American Organization for Nursing Leadership (AONL) System Chief Nurse Executive Committee and the North Carolina Healthcare Association Workforce Advisory Council. She has been recognized as a 2022 Triad Nurse of Distinction and an NC Great 100 Nurse in 2021, and received the Triad Business Journal Outstanding Woman in Business Award in 2023. She previously held executive nursing roles at Great Lakes Health System, including Vice President of Patient Care Services and Chief Nursing Officer.
