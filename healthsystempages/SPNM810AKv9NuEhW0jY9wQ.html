Recent News

Jun 12, 2025
Nemours Children's Health has named Mark Twite, MD, as the Chief of Cardiac Anesthesia and Co-Director of the Nemours Children's Cardiac Center in the Delaware Valley. This appointment was announced on June 12, 2025. Dr. Twite is described as an acclaimed clinician. His new roles indicate a significant leadership addition to the cardiac care team at Nemours Children's Health, specifically within their facilities in the Delaware Valley region.
Jun 11, 2025
Baptist Health Jacksonville is ending its long-standing pediatric services partnership with UF Health, and Nemours Children's Health is set to take over pediatric care at Baptist's Wolfson Children's Hospital in the first quarter of 2026. This transition expands Nemours' existing three-decade relationship with Baptist Health. The move is expected to enhance the team of pediatric physicians at Wolfson Children's and improve care continuity for families in the Jacksonville area. While the articles primarily focus on the operational and strategic aspects of this partnership change, the transfer of pediatric services between two large health systems will inherently involve significant IT integration, including potential electronic health record system adjustments, data migration, and ensuring seamless access to patient information and technology platforms for clinicians and staff transitioning to Nemours. Nemours stated that continuity of care for patients and families is their top priority during this transition.
Jun 10, 2025
Wolfson Children's Hospital in Jacksonville, Florida, which is part of Baptist Health, announced on June 10, 2025, that it will end its decades-long clinical collaboration with UF Health and the College of Medicine – Jacksonville. Instead, Wolfson Children's Hospital will be partnering with Nemours Children's Health. This change is not immediate and is set to take place in the first quarter of 2026. UF Health expressed being "surprised and disheartened" by this decision. Wolfson Children's Hospital stated that Nemours Children's Health's expertise and scale will enhance their ability to recruit and retain healthcare providers and expand their team of pediatric physician specialists who will practice at Wolfson.
May 07, 2025
A researcher from Nemours Children's Health played a significant role in shaping national recommendations for screening children with injuries for trauma. Children who experience injuries are at a higher risk than average for developing mental health difficulties, including post-traumatic stress disorder and depression. This new study and the resulting recommendations aim to improve the identification and support of these vulnerable children to address their mental and emotional well-being alongside their physical recovery. May 06, 2025
A survey conducted by Nemours Children's Health revealed that teachers are observing the nation's youngest children falling behind in literacy skills. The survey highlights concerns that this lag in reading development is not only impacting academic progress but also has potential implications for children's lifelong health. The findings suggest a link between early literacy challenges and future health outcomes, emphasizing the importance of addressing foundational reading skills.

Executive Leadership

R. Lawrence Moss, MD, FACS, FAAP

President and Chief Executive Officer

With more than 25 years as an academic surgeon and physician executive, Dr. Moss became increasingly concerned by how America's health care system narrowly defines health and consistently incentivizes complexity and volume of medical services over outcomes. He joined Nemours Children's to focus the next phase of his career on transforming the definition of children's health and fundamentally changing the financial incentives determining their care.

Gina Altieri, CPA

Executive Vice President, Chief Communications Officer

Gina Altieri, CPA, is the Executive Vice President and Enterprise Chief Communications Officer at Nemours Children's Health. She has been a key member of the Nemours leadership team for over a decade. In her role, she has been instrumental in developing and implementing the system's digital and consumer-centric strategy, including overseeing the launch and expansion of a mobile app for patients with chronic conditions and helping to launch the Center for Health Delivery Innovation. She was promoted to her current position just before the COVID-19 pandemic and managed the implementation of virtual rounding and telehealth tools during that time. Altieri also previously held the title of senior vice president, corporate services at Nemours. She was recognized as a winner in the Health Care Communicator of the Year category of Ragan & PR Daily's Communicator of the Year Awards. Prior to her role at Nemours, Gina Altieri was President of Phenix7 Mktg. Inc., where she worked as a marketing strategist and innovator for over 25 years.

Mary M. Lee, MD, FAAP

Executive Vice President and Enterprise Chief Scientific Officer, Physician-in-Chief, Delaware Valley

Mary M. Lee, MD, FAAP, is the Executive Vice President and Enterprise Chief Scientific Officer, and Physician-in-Chief, Delaware Valley at Nemours Children's Health. She is also a Professor of Pediatrics at Sidney Kimmel School of Medicine, Jefferson University. Dr. Lee is an internationally recognized physician-scientist with expertise in developmental reproductive endocrinology. Her research interests include the biology and regulation of Mullerian inhibiting substance, sexual differentiation and intersex disorders, testicular differentiation and development, the effects of environmental toxins on reproductive development, and the gonadal toxicity of chemotherapy and radiation for childhood malignancies. Her research has been funded by the NIH, the Hood Foundation, and the March of Dimes. She has a significant history of funded and published research and is nationally recognized for her work in endocrinology. Prior to joining Nemours, Dr. Lee served as Physician-in-Chief, and Professor and Stoddard Chair of Pediatrics at UMass Memorial Children's Medical Center and University of Massachusetts Medical School. She also served as the pediatric lead for the UMass CTSA. Dr. Lee earned her MD from the SUNY Buffalo School of Medicine and Biomedical Sciences and her AB from Harvard College. She completed her Pediatric residency at the Children's Hospital of Buffalo and a fellowship in Pediatric Endocrinology and Metabolism at the Children's Hospital of Philadelphia. She also completed a research fellowship at Massachusetts General Hospital. Dr. Lee has held leadership roles in numerous professional organizations, including serving as the immediate past-president of the Pediatric Endocrine Society and a past-president of the American Society of Andrology. She has also chaired several study sections at the National Institutes of Health (NIH) and served on the Scientific Advisory Council for the National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences at NIH. She has been recognized among the Best Doctors of America and Top Pediatricians in America.
