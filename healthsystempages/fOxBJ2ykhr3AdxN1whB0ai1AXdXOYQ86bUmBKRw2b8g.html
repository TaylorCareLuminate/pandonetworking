Executive Leadership

Patrick Falvey

System Chief Operating Officer

Patrick Falvey is the System Chief Operating Officer at Baptist Health (KY). He was appointed to this role in 2018. Prior to joining Baptist Health, he spent 26 years at Advocate Aurora Health in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, where he held numerous operational executive positions, including Executive Vice President and Chief Transformation Officer. His background includes extensive experience in operations, quality improvement, patient safety, disease management, care management, clinical research, service quality, information technology, strategy, and healthcare measurement. During his time at Aurora, facilities under his leadership achieved high marks in hospital quality and productivity.

Rick Carrico

Chief Financial Officer

Rick Carrico is the Chief Financial Officer at Baptist Health in Kentucky. He has an extensive background in finance and leadership, with nearly 40 years of healthcare-related experience. Prior to joining Baptist Health, he served as executive vice president and CFO for WakeMed Health and Hospitals in Raleigh, North Carolina. His previous roles include vice president and CFO of Greater Hudson Valley Health System in Middletown, New York, executive vice president and CFO of Springstone LLC, associate CFO at Norton Healthcare, and vice president of internal audit at Kindred Healthcare. He began his healthcare career in the internal audit department at Humana. Carrico holds an MBA from the Mendoza College of Business at the University of Notre Dame and a Bachelor's degree in Accounting from Bellarmine University. He is also a certified public accountant.

Janet M. Norton

Secretary, Vice President, Chief Legal & Regulatory Affairs Officer

Janet M. Norton serves as the Secretary, Vice President, Chief Legal & Regulatory Affairs Officer at Baptist Health System in Kentucky and Indiana. In this role, she provides legal counsel and support to the Baptist Health Board of Directors and 30 affiliated corporations. Prior to her current position, she held roles at Humana as General Counsel and Senior Counsel, where her work focused on legal matters for health insurance companies and HMOs across multiple states. She also has experience as a Staff Attorney at the Kentucky Court of Appeals, gaining a strong background in research and legal advice. Janet Norton has been with Baptist Health for a significant period, noted as a 29-year employee who has served as general counsel since 1999. She holds a Juris Doctor from the University of Kentucky J. David Rosenberg College of Law and a Bachelor's degree in American Social, Economics, and Political Institutions from the University of Kentucky. In 2017, she was appointed as a co-interim leader for Baptist Health. She is also listed as the Secretary on the Board of Managers for Baptist Health.

Marea Aspillaga

Compliance Officer, Chief Compliance Officer

Marea Aspillaga is the Chief Compliance Officer for Baptist Health System in Kentucky and Indiana. She took on this role on January 1, 2020, following the retirement of Donald R. Riggs. Aspillaga has over 20 years of experience in management and compliance within both private and employed professional practices. Before her current position, she was the system director of compliance and privacy for Baptist Health for over eight years, also managing the internal audit program. She earned an MBA in healthcare administration from Indiana Wesleyan University and a bachelor's degree in business administration/finance from the University of Louisville. Her professional certifications include MBA, CIA, CHC, CPC, COC, and CPMA. She is also involved with the local AAPC chapters in Lexington and Louisville, Ky.

Brian Chaney, MD

Medical Director

He graduated from the University of Louisville School of Medicine in 1999 or 2000. Michael Erickson

Chief Information Security Officer

Nilesh Desai

Chief Pharmacy Officer

Nilesh Desai is the Chief Pharmacy Officer at Baptist Health (KY & IN), a position he assumed in March 2020. In this role, he is responsible for pharmacy operations across Baptist Health's system, which includes multiple hospitals and retail pharmacies. Before joining Baptist Health, Desai spent 21 years at Hackensack University Medical Center/Hackensack Meridian Health in New Jersey. His roles there included Administrator of Pharmacy and Clinical Operations, where he oversaw inpatient, outpatient, and retail pharmacies, as well as having responsibility for OB/GYN and maternal-fetal medicine physician faculty practices. Desai has also been involved in pharmacy education, serving as a program preceptor for the PGY1 Pharmacy Residency program and a clinical adjunct professor with the Ernest Mario School of Pharmacy and Massachusetts School of Pharmacy. His educational background includes a Bachelor of Science in Pharmacy and an MBA in healthcare. He is also a registered pharmacist (RPh) and a Certified Professional in Patient Safety (CPPS). Throughout his career, Desai has focused on transforming pharmacy operations through centralization, automation, and optimization of processes. He has led initiatives to centralize pharmacy services, including establishing a central distribution center and expanding specialty pharmacy services. He has also been involved in implementing technology solutions like Epic and other automation systems. Desai has experience managing challenges such as drug shortages and has been recognized for his leadership in navigating these issues. He is a member of the Council of Pharmacy Executives and Suppliers (CPES).

Tricia Julian

Chief Information Officer

Tricia Julian is the Chief Information Officer at Baptist Health System KY & IN. She is responsible for leading and developing IT structures across the system, including overseeing the implementation and refinement of their electronic health record (EHR) system. Julian joined Baptist Health in 2011 as Ambulatory IT Director, where she built a team and led the implementation of Allscripts practice management and EHR systems in employed physician practices. She also formed a governance committee with physician representation to guide IT direction. As Executive Director, she helped lead the EHR implementation in Baptist Health's hospitals and ambulatory locations, managing vendor relationships, staying on budget and schedule, and overseeing training for thousands of employees. Prior to joining Baptist Health, Julian held leadership roles in EHR implementations at four different healthcare organizations dating back to 2004. She served as Chief Operating Officer and Director of Operations for The Corvallis Clinic in Oregon from 2007 to 2011. She also served as Director of Information Technology for the Welborn Clinic in Indiana and held managerial roles at the Arnett Clinic in Indiana. Julian's exposure to IT began in a banking career with Bank One, where she was selected from operations to serve on a multidisciplinary group focused on technology implementations and data migration during a period of growth and acquisitions. Julian earned a bachelor's degree from Purdue University and an MBA with a healthcare specialization from Indiana Wesleyan University. She has experience with various EHR systems, including NextGen, Allscripts, and Epic.
