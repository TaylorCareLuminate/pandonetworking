Jun 02, 2025
Highland District Hospital, which is part of the Ohio State Health Network and headquartered in Hillsboro, Ohio, has announced the addition of Dr. Sandeep Kapur to provide full-time pulmonology medical services. Dr. Kapur, a board-certified pulmonologist and critical care physician, will begin offering care five days a week in the hospital's Outpatient Specialty Services Clinic starting in June 2025. This addition aims to significantly expand access to specialized respiratory care for the region, allowing patients to receive treatment for a range of pulmonary and respiratory conditions locally, such as COPD, asthma, lung cancer, and sleep apnea. May 26, 2025
Highland District Hospital in Hillsboro, Ohio, is a listed member of the Ohio State Health Network. The Ohio State Health Network works to support its member healthcare organizations by providing benefits such as cost savings, learning opportunities, and valuable resources. These resources include access to Information Technology Contracts, Internal Team Expertise, and the opportunity to participate in Sector Meetings and Roundtable Discussions. The network also supports members in extending The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center's instance of Epic through its Community Connect program and offers access to the Ohio State's TeleConsult Network.
Jan 02, 2025
The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center and The Ohio State University Comprehensive Cancer Center – Arthur G. James Cancer Hospital and Richard J. Solove Research Institute (OSUCCC – James) announced they reached a new multi-year network participation contract with Anthem Blue Cross and Blue Shield. [8] This agreement, effective January 1, 2025, ensures that Anthem employer-sponsored health plan members continue to have in-network access to care at Ohio State. [8] Dr. John J. Warner, CEO of The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center, expressed pleasure in reaching the agreement before the December 31 deadline, allowing continued care for commercially insured Anthem patients. [8] Patients who had canceled or rescheduled appointments due to the uncertainty were encouraged to reschedule online or by phone. [8] However, an agreement was not reached regarding the Anthem Medicare Advantage network for 2025, meaning Ohio State will be out-of-network for Anthem Medicare Advantage members starting January 1. [8] These patients will need to switch to another Medicare Advantage plan during the open enrollment period to continue receiving in-network care at Ohio State. [8] Both organizations stated their commitment to working together to serve the community and maintaining access to high-quality care. [8]
Jan 02, 2025
The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center and The James Cancer Hospital and Solove Research Institute announced on January 2, 2025, that they had reached a new multi-year network participation contract with Anthem Blue Cross and Blue Shield. This agreement is effective starting January 1, 2025, and ensures that patients with Anthem employer-sponsored health plans will retain in-network access to care at Ohio State facilities. However, an agreement was not reached regarding the Anthem Medicare Advantage network, meaning Ohio State Wexner Medical Center and The James are out-of-network for Anthem Medicare Advantage members as of January 1, 2025. Patients with commercial plans who had canceled or rescheduled appointments due to the uncertainty were encouraged to re-schedule. Both parties expressed satisfaction with reaching an agreement for commercial plans, allowing continuity of care for thousands of patients. [6, 15]
Dec 31, 2024
Leading up to the December 31, 2024 deadline, negotiations between Ohio State University's Wexner Medical Center and Anthem Blue Cross and Blue Shield were described as strained, putting approximately 135,000 insured patients at risk of losing in-network access. The dispute centered on reimbursement rates and payment practices. Ohio State accused Anthem of underpaying claims and not keeping pace with rising healthcare costs, stating their costs had risen 45% in the last five years while Anthem enjoyed significant profits. Anthem countered that Ohio State was seeking price increases exceeding three times the inflation rate, which they claimed would significantly increase healthcare costs in Central Ohio. The negotiations went down to the wire, with both sides having taken aggressive stances publicly before the eventual agreement for commercial plans was reached just before the contract expired. [8, 9, 10]

Warner leads the vision, strategy and operating performance of The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center's research, education and patient care mission across its hospitals, outpatient care centers, nationally ranked College of Medicine and research institutes.
