Recent News

Jun 16, 2025
An art workshop held at the University of Nebraska Medical Center's (UNMC) Munroe-Meyer Institute (MMI), a close partner of Nebraska Medicine, is helping participants with communication disorders. The workshop, which is themed each week with conversational topics, encourages individuals to improve their communication skills in a relaxed setting. Participants reported becoming more comfortable and engaging in easier conversation as the program progressed. The program, called Creative Arts and Aging, involves about a dozen participants with various cognitive communication disorders. In addition to individual artwork, the participants also collaborated on a painting that will be displayed at MMI. For individuals like Jon Reid, who has primary progressive aphasia, the workshop provided a valuable opportunity to work on his communication alongside his wife, with their daughter facilitating the program. The experience was described as creating an increased opportunity for communication and being wonderfully pleasing for attendees.

Jun 13, 2025
A historic hospital built by the nation's first Native doctor, Dr. Susan La Flesche Picotte, in Walthill, Nebraska, is being restored and will again provide medical care to Nebraskans. An all-volunteer group successfully raised $6 million for the restoration of the hospital, which was originally built in 1913 on the Omaha reservation. The restored building will be known as the Dr. Susan La Flesche Picotte Center and will serve members of the tribe and nonmembers in Nebraska's poorest county. A private dedication event for the restored hospital was scheduled, with a public open house to follow on June 22. The project leaders, including tribe members, doctors, community members, and La Flesche's descendants, anticipate medical care to be available by the end of the summer. The Nebraska Medical Association was involved in raising awareness and funds for the project, highlighting the significance of Dr. La Flesche Picotte's legacy.

Anna Cramer

Chief People Officer | Chief Legal & Compliance Officer

Anna Cramer serves as the Chief People Officer and Chief Legal & Compliance Officer at Nebraska Medicine. She oversees various departments including the People Team, Legal, Compliance, Community Engagement, Government Affairs, Risk Management, and Privacy. Cramer's legal journey at Nebraska Medicine began in 2012 as Counsel, supporting corporate legal functions. She later held roles as Interim General Counsel and Deputy General Counsel before being appointed as Chief Legal & Compliance Officer and General Counsel in June 2018. She has served in her current dual leadership role since November 2023. Before her time in healthcare, Cramer worked as legal and regulatory counsel in industries such as pharmacy, health insurance, real estate, and franchising. She also served as Regulatory Counsel at Home Instead, Inc., specializing in legal and policy matters for the home care sector, and taught graduate and undergraduate business law and ethics courses at the College of Saint Mary. Anna Cramer earned her Juris Doctor from the University of Nebraska College of Law and holds Bachelor of Science and Bachelor of Arts degrees from the University of Nebraska at Lincoln. She is a board member for Saving Grace Perishable Food Rescue and is involved in professional organizations including the American Health Lawyers Association, Nebraska Bar Association, Association of Corporate Counsel, and the Rotary Club of Omaha. She has been recognized as one of Becker's Healthcare's 99 Chief People Officers to Know.

Harris Frankel

Chief Medical & External Affairs Officer

Harris A. Frankel, MD, is the Chief Medical & External Affairs Officer at Nebraska Medicine. He is a native of Omaha, Nebraska, with over three decades of leadership experience in physician practice, hospital, and health system settings. Dr. Frankel is also an Associate Professor in the Department of Neurological Sciences at the University of Nebraska Medical Center (UNMC) and maintains an active patient practice in Neurology. He earned his medical degree from the University of Nebraska College of Medicine in 1986, completed an internship in general internal medicine at Creighton University Medical Center in 1987, and finished his neurology residency at the University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center at Dallas in 1990, serving as chief resident. Prior to joining UNMC, he was in private practice for nearly 21 years. In his role as Chief Medical Officer, Dr. Frankel works to represent the interests of Nebraska Medicine's medical staff and has been involved in initiatives to improve quality, patient safety, and outcomes. He also oversees accreditation/regulatory compliance, privacy, internal audit, and risk management departments. In his external affairs capacity, he is involved in developing strategic relationships and philanthropic efforts. Dr. Frankel is board certified in Neurology and is a member of several professional organizations, including the American Academy of Neurology, American Medical Association, Nebraska Medical Association (NMA), and Metropolitan Omaha Medical Society (MOMS), having served as past president of both the NMA and MOMS.

Micah Beachy

VP & Chief Clinical Quality Officer

Micah Beachy, DO, FACP, SFHM, is the Vice President and Chief Clinical Quality Officer at Nebraska Medicine and an Associate Professor in the Department of Internal Medicine, Section of Hospital Medicine at the University of Nebraska Medical Center (UNMC). He earned his medical degree from Des Moines University College of Osteopathic Medicine in Iowa and completed his internal medicine residency at UNMC. After residency, he became an academic hospitalist and core faculty at Nebraska Medicine, holding leadership positions such as unit-based medical director and medical director for the Clinical Effectiveness program before becoming chief quality officer in 2020. Dr. Beachy is also involved in professional organizations including the American College of Physicians (ACP), Society of Hospital Medicine (SHM), American Medical Association (AMA), Nebraska Medical Association (NMA), and the Metro Omaha Medical Society (MOMS). He was recently selected as the governor for the Nebraska chapter of the ACP. He also holds a BS from Eastern Mennonite University and an AA from Hutchinson Community College.

Steven J. Lisco

Chief Academic Officer

Steven J. Lisco, MD, serves as the Chief Academic Officer for Nebraska Medicine and Senior Associate Dean for Clinical Affairs at the University of Nebraska Medical Center (UNMC) College of Medicine. He also holds the title of Myrna Newland Endowed Professor and Chair of the Department of Anesthesiology at UNMC. Dr. Lisco earned his undergraduate degree in biology from Grinnell College and his medical degree from Loyola University-Stritch School of Medicine. He completed residency training in internal medicine at the University of Michigan Medical Center, followed by an anesthesiology residency at Brigham and Women's Hospital, and a critical care medicine fellowship at Massachusetts General Hospital. He joined the UNMC faculty in 2012 as a professor and chair of Anesthesiology. Before his time at UNMC, Dr. Lisco was a faculty member at the University of Cincinnati College of Medicine, where he established and directed the anesthesiology critical care services and non-OR anesthesia. He also previously held faculty appointments at Mt. Sinai School of Medicine and Harvard Medical School. Dr. Lisco specializes in critical care medicine and thoracic and vascular anesthesia and remains active as a clinician. He is also a clinician scientist with numerous publications. During his tenure as chair of Anesthesiology from 2012 to 2023, the department significantly grew in size and research funding. He is a fellow of the American College of Critical Care Medicine, the American College of Chest Physicians, and the American College of Physicians.
