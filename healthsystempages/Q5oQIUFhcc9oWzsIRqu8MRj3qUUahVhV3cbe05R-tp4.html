Atlantic Health System is currently integrating genomics into its electronic health record (EHR) system to enhance precision medicine. This initiative aims to provide physicians with advanced tools for faster diagnosis, personalized treatment plans based on patients' unique genetic profiles, and improved outcomes at the point of care. The integration is expected to reduce guesswork in prescribing decisions and minimize adverse drug reactions while enhancing treatment efficacy. The health system has been expanding its team of genetic counselors since 2013 and is working towards making genomic testing available to physicians later this year, pending the necessary agreement processes. They are also tracking key performance indicators to validate the technology's value in terms of both patient outcomes and cost savings. [4]

Jun 06, 2025
Morristown Mayor Tim Dougherty has commented on the departure of outgoing Atlantic Health CEO Brian Gragnolati and welcomed his successor, Saad Ehtisham. Gragnolati's decade-long tenure significantly impacted Morristown, notably through a landmark 2015 settlement where Morristown Medical Center agreed to pay property taxes as a non-profit. This $15.5 million agreement was crucial for the town's finances, helping it manage through the pandemic without layoffs or major tax increases and influencing state legislation for similar contributions from non-profit hospitals statewide. As Gragnolati prepares to retire next month, the focus shifts to the proposed $1 billion hospital expansion, a project initiated during his leadership that has sparked community debate regarding potential benefits like enhanced medical care versus concerns over increased traffic and decreased home values. This expansion project will now be a key responsibility for the incoming CEO, Saad Ehtisham, whose appointment was announced recently. Mayor Dougherty expressed confidence in Ehtisham, citing his strong track record and community-focused approach as ideal for building upon Gragnolati's legacy and ensuring Atlantic Health System's continued role as a pillar of health and wellness. While conceptual plans for the expansion have been shared, a formal application has not yet been submitted. [4]
