Stephanie Johnson

Vice President, Quality and Risk Management

Stephanie Johnson serves as the Vice President, Quality and Risk Management at St. Peter's Health Partners. She is a member of the leadership team at St. Peter's Health Partners, which is part of Trinity Health New York.

Michelle Mazzacco

Executive VP, Continuing Care

Michelle T. Mazzacco is the Executive Vice President of the Continuing Care Network at St. Peter's Health Partners (SPHP), a position she has held since 2023, after serving as Interim EVP since November 2021. She has a long history with both The Eddy and SPHP networks, spanning over 37 years. Mazzacco is recognized as a significant contributor to the healthcare services field and a strong advocate for the advancement of community-based care services. Prior to her roles as EVP, she served as regional vice president of SPHP's Community Services Division starting in 1994. This division included several key services such as Eddy Visiting Nurse and Rehab Association, Eddy SeniorCare, and The Community Hospice. Her work has involved leadership, strategic guidance, and direction for all Continuing Care services, as well as integrating these services within the SPHP healthcare system and the wider community. She also previously held the role of vice president and executive director of the Community Services Division. Mazzacco began her career with The Eddy in 1985 as an executive secretary. She was supported by the organization in pursuing higher education, earning both a bachelor's degree and an MBA. She has been involved in initiatives aimed at addressing healthcare staffing shortages and is the board chair of the Home Care Association of New York State (HCANYS).

Nicholas Joseph Montalto Jr, MD

Chief Medical Officer, Acute Care, SPHP

Nicholas Joseph Montalto Jr, MD is the Chief Medical Officer, Acute Care, SPHP at St. Peter's Health Partners, a position he has held since January 2024. Prior to this role, he served as a surgeon at Ellis Medicine starting in January 2013, specializing in complex benign gynecologic conditions and robotic surgery. During his tenure at Ellis Medicine, he also held several leadership positions, including Chief Medical Officer and Medical Director of Surgical Services. Dr. Montalto's background also includes experience in fertility care and complex gynecologic surgery at CNY Fertility Centers and Albany Gynecology & Fertility. He is an Obstetrician Gynecologist with over 25 years of experience and is board certified in Obstetrics & Gynecology. He earned his MD from St. George's University School of Medicine and holds a Bachelor's degree in English from Villanova University, and began his career with research at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center.

Dr. Amine Hila

CMO Acute Care, St. Joseph's Health. and CCO, Trinity Health New York

Dr. Amine Hila serves as the Chief Medical Officer for Acute Care at St. Joseph's Health and as the Chief Clinical Officer for Trinity Health New York at St. Peter's Health Partners. He assumed these roles from Dr. Michael Trevisani in January 2025. In his position, Dr. Hila is responsible for leading the Acute Care medical and clinical teams at St. Joseph's Health and is involved in the implementation of strategic and operating plans, as well as service line development. St. Joseph's Health and St. Peter's Health Partners are part of Trinity Health. Dr. Hila brings extensive clinical and leadership experience to his current roles. In addition to his leadership roles, Dr. Hila is a board-certified Gastroenterologist and practiced in Binghamton for 18 years. He attended medical school at Faculté de Médecine de Tunis in Tunisia. He completed his internship and residency in Internal Medicine at The Graduate Hospital in Philadelphia, PA, and a combined clinical and research fellowship in Gastroenterology at the Medical University of South Carolina in Charleston, SC. Dr. Hila is affiliated with UHS Binghamton General Hospital. He has also been a Clinical Associate Professor of Internal Medicine at SUNY Upstate Medical University.

Guha Bala

Board of Directors

Guha Bala is a successful technology executive and venture capitalist who was named to the St. Peter's Health Partners (SPHP) Board of Directors with a three-year term beginning January 1, 2019. He is the president of Velan Ventures Inc., an innovation investment company focused on video games and game-related technology, and Velan Studios Inc., a game studio. Previously, Guha co-founded Vicarious Visions with his brother, Karthik, in 1991. Vicarious Visions became an industry-leading game developer, known for contributing to popular franchises like Tony Hawk's Pro Skater, Guitar Hero, Skylanders, and Crash Bandicoot. The company was sold to Activision-Blizzard in 2005, and the brothers led the business as senior vice presidents of Activision Publishing for 11 years. Vicarious Visions generated over $4 billion in sales and sold 45 million units of software. After 25 years, the Bala brothers transitioned out of Vicarious Visions in 2016 to become entrepreneurs again, founding Velan Studios and Velan Ventures. Guha Bala was honored as Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute's “Entrepreneur of the Year” in 2013 and enjoys mentoring start-up ventures. He holds a bachelor's degree in chemistry from Harvard College and a Master of Business Administration from the MIT Sloan School of Management. Guha Bala has also declared membership interest in St. Peter's Health Partners, Northeast Health and affiliates, and Rubin Dialysis Centers. He is also listed as a member of the Board of Trustees at Emma Willard School.
