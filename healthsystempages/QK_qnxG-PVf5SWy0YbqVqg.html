Recent News

Jun 11, 2025
The Louisiana Department of Health, Office of Public Health, announced it will hold a public hearing. The purpose of this hearing is to receive input from the public regarding the 2025 Louisiana Preventive Health and Health Services Block Grant. This grant is administered by the agency, and public feedback is a crucial part of the planning and implementation process for how these federal funds are utilized to support public health initiatives across the state.
Jun 11, 2025
With summer temperatures rising, the Louisiana Department of Health issued guidance to help residents prevent heat-related illness. The department emphasized the importance of residents, workers, and employers being aware of the symptoms of heat illness and the necessary treatments to prevent serious conditions like heat stroke or death. This advisory serves as a timely reminder during warmer months when the risk of heat-related health issues increases significantly.
Jun 10, 2025
The Louisiana Department of Health submitted an initial State Plan Amendment (SPA) for the Medicaid Purchase Plan, identified as SPA 25-0004-A. This submittal is part of the required process for making changes to the state's Medicaid program as outlined in compliance with Act 212 of the 2013 Regular Session of the Louisiana Legislature. The amendment is currently pending, and the department has made the initial submittal and related redlined state plan pages publicly available for review.
Jun 10, 2025
Louisiana Healthcare Connections, a Medicaid managed care organization operating in Louisiana, informed providers of updates to their policies and procedures. These updates were shared with providers as part of the ongoing process to communicate changes that may affect their practices and the services provided to Medicaid recipients. While the specific details of all the policies and procedures are not provided in the summary, such updates are often aligned with or necessitated by directives and informational bulletins issued by the Louisiana Department of Health.
Jun 09, 2025
Louisiana Healthcare Connections notified healthcare providers about updates to the 2025 Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) and Physician-Administered Drug Reimbursement. This notification specifically references Louisiana Department of Health Informational Bulletin 25-17, indicating that these changes stem from official guidance issued by the state health department. The updates, made effective on June 9, 2025, are crucial for providers to ensure accurate billing and reimbursement for medical services and administered drugs under the Medicaid program.

Bruce D. Greenstein

Secretary

As secretary of the Louisiana Department of Health, Bruce D. Greenstein leads the state's largest agency with a budget of $19.8 billion and a team responsible for delivering services to millions of Louisianans. [2, 9] He has a deep understanding of healthcare systems, from Medicaid to post-acute care. [6] He was formerly the Health Secretary for Bobby Jindal in Louisiana, and before that he was running Microsoft's worldwide health and human services business division. He has run operations in behavioral health and spent time in venture capital and private equity and is on the boards of a publicly traded health care company, a health technology company, and two private equity companies addressing the home health market. [6] Bruce has spent the last 30 years in healthcare – split between the public and private sectors. [6] Today, he runs strategy and innovation at the nation's largest post-acute care company. [6]

Surgeon General

Practicing family medicine physician in Richland Parish for more than 30 years. Served three terms in Congress, representing Louisiana's 5th Congressional District. [5, 7] Before earning his Medical Doctor degree from LSU Health Shreveport, he practiced veterinary medicine for 10 years. [5] The Surgeon General is co-equal with the Secretary within the Department's organizational chart, and is charged with crafting health policy, including healthcare workforce development; advocating for wellness and disease prevention, and coordinating with other state agencies and institutions to improve health outcomes in Louisiana.
