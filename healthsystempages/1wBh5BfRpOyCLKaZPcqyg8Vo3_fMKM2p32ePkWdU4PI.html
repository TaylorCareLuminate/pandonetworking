Jun 02, 2025
The Self Regional Healthcare Foundation announced the recipients of the 2025 Mary Ella Ruff Scholarship and the Charlotte Blackwell Scholarship. These annual scholarships are awarded to local individuals pursuing careers in healthcare. The Mary Ella Ruff Scholarship honors a beloved nurse, and the Charlotte Blackwell Memorial Nursing Scholarship is in memory of a young ICU nurse. Mar 31, 2025
Piedmont Technical College broke ground on the $16.1 million Self Regional Healthcare Center for Health Sciences. This new facility, located on the college's Lex Walters Campus-Greenwood, is designed to provide high-quality training for future healthcare professionals in realistic settings. The two-story, 27,000 square-foot building will feature 10 simulation rooms, six skills labs with collaborative teaching spaces, a catheterization and echocardiography lab, classrooms, and computer labs. Self Regional Healthcare is contributing significantly to the project by purchasing and donating $1.3 million worth of advanced equipment. This equipment includes advanced patient simulators, an Anatomage Table with digitized human cadavers for medical learning, hospital beds and tables, infusion pumps, mobile computing workstations, and ultrasound systems. The substantial donation led to the naming of the building. The project is largely funded by an $11 million grant from the U.S. Department of Commerce’s Economic Development Administration and state funding. The center is expected to be completed in July 2026 and will replace the college's existing healthcare facilities.
