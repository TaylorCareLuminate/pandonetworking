Recent News

Jun 10, 2025
Bronstein, Gewirtz & Grossman, LLC has initiated an investigation into potential claims against Acadia Healthcare Company, Inc. (NASDAQ: ACHC) on behalf of investors. The investigation is focused on whether Acadia Healthcare and certain of its officers and/or directors have engaged in corporate wrongdoing. Investors who acquired Acadia Healthcare securities before February 28, 2020, and still hold them, are encouraged to contact the firm for more information and to potentially assist with the investigation. The firm is a nationally recognized entity that represents investors in securities fraud class actions and shareholder derivative suits, working on a contingency fee basis. This means they only seek reimbursement for out-of-pocket expenses and attorneys' fees, typically as a percentage of the total recovery, if they are successful. The announcement was made from New York, NY.
May 12, 2025
Acadia Healthcare reported its first-quarter 2025 financial results on May 12, 2025. The report highlighted continued progress on the company's growth strategy, specifically mentioning the addition of 378 newly licensed beds during the first quarter. This total includes 90 beds added to existing facilities and 288 beds from newly constructed facilities. As part of this expansion, Acadia commenced operations at two new facilities: a de novo facility in North Port, Florida, and a joint venture hospital in West Bloomfield, Michigan, in partnership with Henry Ford Health. The company also expanded its network of comprehensive treatment centers (CTCs) by adding seven new locations, bringing their total to 170 CTCs across 33 states. Acadia stated its expectation to add between 800 and 1,000 total beds during the full year 2025. [5, 12, 17, 18]
May 08, 2025
Acadia Healthcare announced its participation in the upcoming BofA Securities Health Care Conference. The company stated that it would be presenting at the conference, providing updates and insights into its operations and financial performance. Further details about the time and webcast of the presentation were available on the investor relations section of Acadia Healthcare's website. [2]
Apr 22, 2025
Acadia Healthcare's leadership highlighted their ongoing investments in technology as part of their commitment to quality and safety. They specifically mentioned the use of cloud-based performance management software in 100% of their acute facilities. This software allows for systematic collection and analysis of performance data to identify and address even minor deficiencies. Additionally, they noted investments in patient monitoring systems as part of measures designed to meet the specific needs of patients and staff at each facility. These technology investments are part of hundreds of millions of dollars invested annually in talent, technology, clinical protocols, and training to deliver safe, quality care.
Apr 22, 2025
Acadia Healthcare is facing renewed scrutiny over alleged abuse at its now-closed Timberline Knolls Residential Treatment Center in Lemont, Illinois. A New York Times investigation reported alleged instances of patient suicide and rape at the facility. Acadia closed Timberline Knolls on February 13, 2025, six months after a lawsuit was filed in August 2024 alleging sexual assault and rape of a patient by a staff member. Acadia stated the closure was due to changes in the landscape for the specialized programs offered there since its acquisition 13 years prior and called the New York Times story inaccurate and a conflation of historical incidents.
