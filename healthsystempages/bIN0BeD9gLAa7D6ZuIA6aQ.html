Recent News

Jun 17, 2025
A group of U.S. lawmakers sent a letter to the acting Secretary of the U.S. Department of Labor, Julie Su, requesting the department's support for Steward Health Care's approximately 30,000 employees and its retirees. This action by lawmakers highlights the significant human impact of Steward's financial crisis and bankruptcy. While not directly about carrier or payer disputes, the well-being of Steward's workforce is intrinsically linked to the system's operational stability and its ability to continue providing care that is reimbursed by payers.
Jun 14, 2025
Steward Health Care secured a commitment for $225 million in debtor-in-possession financing from a group of lenders on June 10, 2025. This funding was intended to help keep the health system's hospitals and its physician group, Stewardship Health, operational while it goes through the bankruptcy process and asset auctions. The U.S. Bankruptcy Judge Christopher Lopez approved this financing on June 14, 2025. This was a critical step for Steward as it faced liquidity issues and aimed to fund its operations during the Chapter 11 proceedings. [3]
Jun 13, 2025
Steward Health Care System LLC has opposed requests from the Justice Department's bankruptcy monitor and a group of doctors to convert its Chapter 11 case to a trustee-supervised liquidation. The company argued in a court objection filed on Thursday, June 13, 2025, in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas that these parties have not demonstrated a “substantial or continuing loss” to the estate. Steward contends that its proposed liquidation plan, along with a settlement agreement between Steward, a group of lenders, and the official committee of unsecured creditors, is the most effective way to maximize the value of the estate. They also assert that this plan is the only viable method to achieve a recovery for unsecured creditors and provide necessary funding for administrative expenses.
Jun 10, 2025
Recent reports discussing the impact of private equity in healthcare highlighted Steward Health Care's bankruptcy as a significant example. The reports noted that Steward, formerly owned by private equity firm Cerberus Capital Management, filed for bankruptcy in May 2024, putting all 31 of its U.S. hospitals up for sale to address its substantial $9 billion debt. This situation underscores the financial distress that impacts Steward's ability to operate and fulfill its obligations, including those to and from carriers and payers. The ongoing sale of assets is a direct consequence of this financial distress, influencing future payer contracts and relationships for the affected hospitals under new ownership.
Jun 01, 2025
Optum, a subsidiary of UnitedHealth Group and a major player in the healthcare market, reportedly pulled out of an agreement to purchase Steward Health Care's physician network, known as Stewardship Health, in June 2025. This development is significant as the potential sale to a large health insurer affiliate was seen as a key part of Steward's strategy to address its financial woes prior to and during bankruptcy. The failure of this deal with a major carrier has implications for Steward's restructuring plans and the future of its physician network, potentially affecting existing or future contracts and relationships with various payers.

Executive Leadership

Ralph de la Torre, MD

Chairman and Chief Executive Officer

Orchestrated the 2010 buyout that created Steward Health Care, becoming chairman, CEO, and principal stockholder. Formerly CEO of Caritas Christi Health Care and former cardiac surgery chief at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center. Voted one of the 25 most powerful physician executives in the United States by Modern Healthcare magazine. Graduated from Duke University (B.S.E.), and from a joint program between Harvard Medical School (M.D.) and the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (M.S.).

Justine Carr, MD

Chief Medical Officer and Senior Vice President of Quality and Safety

Formerly Chief Medical Officer of Caritas Christi Health Care. She is a hematologist and formerly held assistant professor positions in Medicine, Surgery and Pathology at Harvard Medical School. Received her medical degree at Columbia College of Physicians and Surgeons and trained at New York Presbyterian Hospital, Massachusetts General Hospital and Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center. Chairs the National Committee on Vital and Health Statistics, an 18-member public advisory board to the Secretary of Health and Human Services on health data strategy and implementation of HIPAA privacy and administrative simplification.

Brian Carty

Chief Marketing Officer

Nationally recognized as a preeminent authority on marketing in service companies. He has served as president of three advertising agencies, including Hill Holliday in Boston.

Mark Girard, MD

President of Steward Health Care Network

A practicing interventional radiologist and was formerly President and CEO of Commonwealth Radiology Associates, and the Medical-Staff President at North Shore Medical Center. Prior to being named President of Steward Health Care Network, Dr. Girard served as Vice President of Specialty Care and Vice President of Network Imaging Services for Caritas Christi Health Care Network. He is an active member of the American College of Health Care Executives and the American College of Physician Executives. He also holds an M.B.A. from Salem State College Graduate School of Business.

Sr. Marie Puleo, MFIC

Senior Vice President

Recently acted as interim President of Carney Hospital. Previously she served as the Senior Vice President for Mission at Caritas Christi Health Care. Prior to joining Caritas Christi, Sr. Marie was the Director of the Franciscan Adult School at St. Anthony Shrine in Boston. She is the co-founder of the National Catholic AIDS Network and served as the Director of Seton Manor, a residential facility for people living with HIV. Started her career in the accounting department of Steward's St. Anne's Hospital in Fall River. She holds a master's degree from Providence College.

Joshua Putter

Chief Operating Officer

Brings over 22 years of hospital management experience, including oversight of a 13-hospital system in Florida as a Division President of Health Management Associates, as well as serving as CEO, Chief Operating Officer and Executive Director for hospitals in Florida, Oklahoma and Texas. Mr. Putter received his B.S. from Texas A&M, and his MBA/MHA from the University of Houston.

David Colarusso

Chief Information Officer

David Colarusso is the Chief Information Officer at Steward Health Care. He has over 25 years of experience in health IT and joined Steward in 2012, serving as deputy CIO before becoming the lead. At Steward, he was responsible for standardizing the EHR platform and overseeing acquisitions and onboarding of new hospitals. He has 17 years of experience working for EHR vendors. Prior to his work in health IT, David Colarusso had a diverse background. He taught high school physics and astronomy for six years and co-founded a software business. He is also an attorney and worked as a public defender. Additionally, he is a data scientist and has been involved in legal innovation and technology, directing the Legal Innovation & Technology Lab at Suffolk University Law School. He holds a BA from Cornell University and an MEd from Harvard Graduate School of Education, and a JD from Boston University Law School.
