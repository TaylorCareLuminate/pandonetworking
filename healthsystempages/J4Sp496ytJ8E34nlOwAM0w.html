Recent News

Jun 03, 2025
UnityPoint Health is pursuing a tentative acquisition of MercyOne Siouxland Medical Center in Sioux City, Iowa. The two health systems announced signing a letter of intent for the acquisition, which they hope to finalize in the summer of 2025. While the primary focus of this news is the merger and expansion of UnityPoint Health's footprint in Iowa, such a significant change in ownership and integration into a larger system like UnityPoint (which is part of Trinity Health, the parent company of MercyOne) will inevitably involve substantial IT and technology integration efforts to align systems and operations.
May 31, 2025
MercyOne is transitioning to a new electronic health record and billing system, with the change taking effect on May 31, 2025. In preparation for this significant update, MercyOne has temporarily paused its online scheduling feature from May 16 through May 31. During this two-week period, patients who need to schedule appointments are instructed to call the specific clinic location directly. This system-wide change is a major operational update aimed at improving health records and billing processes across MercyOne facilities.
May 27, 2025
MercyOne is transitioning to a new anesthesia services provider, Vituity, effective May 31, ending a long-standing partnership with Medical Center Anesthesiologists (MCA). According to MercyOne, this change was due to unresolved financial and operational disagreements with MCA, including a request for significantly increased financial support and MCA's unwillingness to use downtime procedures for charting during a transition to new technology. MercyOne is also in the process of moving to Epic TogetherCare on May 31, as part of a systemwide EHR standardization effort by its parent company, Trinity Health. MercyOne stated that the transition to Vituity is scheduled to coincide with the end of the MCA contract to ensure no disruption to patient care and expressed hope of retaining current anesthesia providers within the new model. May 22, 2025
MercyOne Central Iowa has been awarded over $125,000 by Variety – the Children's Charity to enhance care and services for children. A significant portion of this funding, $116,275, is designated for MercyOne Children's Hospital to purchase three GE Panda infant warmers and renovate three labor and delivery suites to accommodate this new equipment. These infant warmers are equipped with specialized features crucial for the care of the hospital's smallest patients. Additionally, an $8,895 grant was provided to MercyOne House of Mercy to install new Americans with Disabilities Act accessible picnic tables at its outdoor playground. This funding will help MercyOne facilities in Central Iowa, including those serving the Ankeny area, continue to invest in vital equipment and improved facilities for pediatric care and supportive services.
May 20, 2025
MercyOne released a statement regarding its separation from Medical Center Anesthesiologists (MCA), a long-time anesthesia partner. The split reportedly stemmed from MercyOne's transition to a new electronic medical record (EHR) system, identified as the TogetherCare/Epic Go Live system. According to the President-Elect of MCA, while they were aware of the transition to Epic, they identified issues with the system and proposed temporary solutions. MercyOne, however, disagreed with these proposed solutions, leading to the decision to part ways. MercyOne's statement emphasized their commitment to providing exceptional healthcare despite the change in their relationship with MCA.
