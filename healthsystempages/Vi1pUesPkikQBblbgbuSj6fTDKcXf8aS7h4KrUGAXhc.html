Jun 17, 2025
Dragon Medical One (DMO) and Dragon Medical Advisor (DMA), voice recognition and coding tools, are being made available to housestaff at Olive View-UCLA Medical Center starting June 17, 2025. These tools are part of Project Monarch from the Los Angeles County Department of Health Services (DHS) and are intended to assist with clinical charting in the ORCHID electronic health record system. DMO allows dictation via microphone or mobile app, while DMA provides real-time documentation suggestions. Training is required for housestaff to gain access to these tools. Olive View-UCLA Medical Center is one of the four hospitals operated by the LA County Department of Health Services. The implementation of such voice recognition technology aims to improve efficiency and accuracy in medical documentation for the hospital's medical staff.
Jun 16, 2025
The LA County Department of Public Health has addressed concerns regarding the spread of the new Nimbus COVID subvariant in California, confirming a slight rise in positive surveillance tests within Los Angeles County. In light of this, the department has reiterated that current COVID-19 vaccine recommendations for individuals aged 6 months and older remain actively in effect and that insurance coverage for these vaccines is still available. Furthermore, the department voiced apprehension that recent policy changes implemented by the Trump administration at the federal level could potentially jeopardize access to and affordability of vaccines, cautioning that such actions may undermine public and medical provider confidence in established vaccine schedules. Jun 16, 2025
The Los Angeles County Department of Public Health has issued advisories warning the public against water contact activities at several county beaches due to elevated bacterial levels. Recent water quality testing revealed that bacterial concentrations at locations including portions of Topanga Canyon Beach, Redondo Beach Pier, Ramirez Creek at Paradise Cove, Santa Monica Pier, Castlerock Storm Drain at Topanga County Beach, Mothers Beach in Marina Del Rey, Inner Cabrillo Beach in San Pedro, and near the Bel Air Bay Club at Will Rogers State Beach exceeded state health safety standards. These warnings are a measure to protect community health by advising beachgoers to avoid swimming, surfing, or playing in the ocean at these specific sites until bacterial levels return to acceptable limits. Jun 15, 2025
In response to concerns about patients avoiding in-person clinic visits due to immigration enforcement activities, the Los Angeles County Department of Health Services (DHS) has published resources for remote care. These resources include phone numbers and portals that allow county clinic patients to access a nurse advice line and request medication drop-offs. The department is encouraging patients who prefer not to visit clinics in person to contact their clinics and convert their appointments to virtual or phone visits. This highlights the department's efforts to leverage technology and remote services to ensure continued access to care for vulnerable populations during challenging circumstances.
Jun 15, 2025
Reports indicate that encounters between suspected immigration agents and health clinics in the Los Angeles area have led to heightened fear among patients, resulting in an increase in canceled medical appointments. While the Los Angeles County Department of Health Services has stated that its county health facilities have not been entered by immigration agents, the department acknowledges that the perceived threat of immigration enforcement near healthcare sites erodes public trust and negatively impacts community health. In response to these concerns and to ensure continued access to care, the department has highlighted and published resources for remote health services, including nurse advice lines and options for virtual appointments, encouraging patients who feel unsafe visiting clinics in person to utilize these alternatives.
