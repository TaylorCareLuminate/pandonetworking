Jun 01, 2025
DRH Health is once again presenting The Territory Classic, an All Pro Tour (APT) event, for the third consecutive year in Duncan, Oklahoma. The 72-hole tournament is scheduled to take place from June 4th to 7th, 2025, at The Territory Golf and Country Club. This event is the third on the APT's 2025 season schedule. The anticipated prize purse for the tournament is $150,000, with the winner guaranteed to receive $30,000. The schedule for the week includes Pro-Am events on June 2nd and 3rd, a Ladies Sip N' Chip event, and a Welcome Party. The tournament rounds will begin on June 4th and conclude on June 7th with a trophy presentation. Attendance is free for the public. Apr 25, 2025
DRH Health released information regarding a data security incident that occurred at their third-party vendor, Nationwide Recovery Services, Inc. The incident, which may have impacted the security of certain individuals' personal information, took place between July 5 and July 11, 2024, when an unknown actor gained unauthorized access to systems on NRS's network and copied files. DRH Health was notified by NRS on February 14, 2025, that the incident may have affected the security of personal information related to certain DRH patients. Following this notification, DRH Health conducted an extensive review to identify affected individuals and gather necessary address information, completing this process on March 28, 2025. The third-party vendor was unable to conclusively determine the specific information involved for each individual, but it may have included names, addresses, Social Security numbers, birth dates, financial account information, and medical-related information. DRH Health is offering impacted individuals free credit monitoring, credit reports, and credit score services for 12 months, as well as proactive fraud assistance. [7, 9, 20]

Feb 10, 2025
DRH Health President and CEO Jay Johnson provided a community address highlighting recent activities and future plans. He discussed ongoing physician recruitment efforts, noting successful recruitment with new providers joining DRH Health, including an OB/GYN and an Orthopedics and Sports Medicine physician. Johnson also mentioned educational partnerships with the University of Oklahoma and Murray State, with approximately 30 graduates expected to join DRH Health in May 2025. Regarding expansions, plans are in place for the construction of a new Duncan Allergy Ear, Nose and Throat Clinic and a new orthopedic building. Significant progress has been made on the east building of Jefferson County Hospital, with a grand opening anticipated in the coming months for the new emergency room, front entry, lab, and pharmacy. A new clinic in Walters is also planned. Johnson introduced the 'Pathways to a Healthier You' program, a community health initiative focusing on areas identified through a community survey, including healthy lifestyle, behavioral health, cancer, and safety. He also announced the upcoming retirement of the Chief Financial Officer in 2026 and the search for candidates for that position. [9]

Executive Leadership

Jay R. Johnson

President/CEO, FACHE

Jay R. Johnson is the President & CEO at DRH Health. Under his leadership, DRH Health has grown and expanded its services and clinics. He has also held leadership positions at Mercy and Stillwater Medical Center. Johnson holds a Master of Health Administration from Virginia Commonwealth University and a Bachelor of Business Administration from Cameron University. [5, 10]

Ken Miller

Vice President & CFO

Ken Miller is the Vice President & CFO at DRH Health. Prior to this role, he was named System Chief Financial Officer at Memorial Health System of East Texas in 2013 and had over 13 years of financial healthcare experience at that time. His previous positions included serving as Division CFO with RegionalCare Hospital Partners and as CFO at Paris Regional Medical Center, The Medical Center of Mesquite, and Sandhills Regional Medical Center.

Roger L. Neal

VP/Chief Operating Officer

Roger L. Neal is the Vice-President & Chief Operating Officer at DRH Health (Duncan Regional Hospital, Inc.). He has an extensive background in healthcare technology and management. His career in technology began in 1994 at Jackson County Memorial Hospital, where he rose to become the Director of Information Systems by 1996. In 2000, he earned a Master of Science in Telecommunications Management from Oklahoma State University and served as the Director of Information Technology for the Resident Shows Division at Cirque du Soleil. Since joining Duncan Regional Hospital in 2001, he has been instrumental in leading technology initiatives, contributing to the hospital's recognition as a leading technology institution nationally and multiple appearances on the Hospital & Health Networks Most Wired list. He currently oversees various departments including Auxiliary Services, Information Technology, Telecommunications, Pharmacy Services, and more, and also serves as the Chief Information Officer for the Cancer Centers of Southwest Oklahoma LLC and MSO Healthcare of Oklahoma LLC. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Psychology from the University of Oklahoma and an Associate degree in Pre-Professional Studies from Western Oklahoma State College.
