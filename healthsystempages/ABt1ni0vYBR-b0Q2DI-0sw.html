Jun 11, 2025
Roper St. Francis Healthcare has officially broken ground on its transformative future Roper Hospital campus in North Charleston. The $1.2 billion project is planned on a 27-acre site and is designed to reshape healthcare delivery in the Lowcountry by combining advanced technology, resilience planning, and a patient-centered approach. [4, 5, 12, 15] The future facility is envisioned as a model of 21st-century healthcare, built to withstand environmental challenges while improving access and enhancing the experience for patients and visitors. [4, 5] Upon full buildout, the 805,000-square-foot hospital building will include 328 inpatient beds, a 47-bay emergency department, 44 critical care beds, 18 operating rooms, a comprehensive imaging department, and a 12-bay hemodialysis unit. [4, 5] The layout integrates procedure rooms from different disciplines on the same floor for efficient interdepartmental care. [4, 5] The project is expected to generate approximately 3,600 jobs and contribute $2.5 billion to economic growth. [3, 11] The new campus is slated for completion in 2029. [5, 11, 12, 15] This development is part of the Roper St. Francis Healthcare 2030 vision and systemwide master plan. [3, 9, 12] To support the project, the Roper St. Francis Foundation has launched Imagine: The Campaign for Roper St. Francis Healthcare, a $100 million fundraising initiative running through 2030. Jun 11, 2025
Roper St. Francis Healthcare broke ground on its new $1.2 billion Roper Hospital campus in North Charleston, South Carolina. This transformative project, spanning 27 acres, is a significant step in the health system's 2030 strategic plan. The new campus is designed to modernize technology and expand Roper's presence to better serve the growing population across the Tri-County area. Heralded as one of the most advanced hospital construction projects in the South, the facility will integrate advanced technology and resilience planning with a patient-centered approach. The hospital is expected to be completed in 2029 and will feature inpatient beds, an emergency department, critical care units, operating theatres, a comprehensive imaging department, and a haemodialysis unit. The design includes strategically placing procedure rooms from various disciplines on the same floor to enhance interdepartmental collaboration. The project is being managed by Barton Malow Builders and Edifice Construction, with design by E4H Environments for Health Architecture and Skidmore, Owings & Merrill (SOM). This move will replace the current Roper Hospital campus in downtown Charleston. [11, 14, 15, 17, 23, 26, 28]

The Roper St. Francis Foundation welcomed Anthony Burchard as its new president on April 21, 2025. Burchard's appointment is expected to lead the Foundation's efforts in fundraising and philanthropy to support the healthcare system's initiatives and strategic goals. The Foundation plays a crucial role in securing resources that enable Roper St. Francis Healthcare to expand services, invest in technology, and enhance patient care for the Lowcountry community.
Mar 05, 2025
The Roper St. Francis Foundation officially launched its $100 million fundraising initiative, Imagine: The Campaign for Roper St. Francis Healthcare, on February 22, 2025, with the campaign extending through 2030. The launch event, held at the Charleston Gaillard Center, brought together over 550 donors and supporters. A major highlight was the announcement of a historic $20 million lead gift from the Medical Society of South Carolina, marking the largest donation in the Foundation's history and the second-largest to a Lowcountry healthcare system. The campaign aims to significantly invest in enhancing care across various specialties, create a Community Health Center of Excellence, and build hospitals and clinical care systems.
