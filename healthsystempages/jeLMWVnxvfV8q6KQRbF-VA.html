Recent News

Jun 13, 2025
In observance of World Blood Donor Day on June 14, Aspirus Health, in conjunction with regional blood centers, is highlighting the critical and ongoing need for blood donations. According to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, a person in the United States requires blood every two seconds. This underscores the constant demand for blood products and the importance of voluntary donations to ensure an adequate supply for patients in need.
Jun 12, 2025
Access TeleCare is working to bring life-saving stroke care closer to home by empowering hospitals like Aspirus Tomahawk and other hospitals nationwide. This initiative focuses on utilizing telemedicine to provide timely and specialized stroke treatment, which is crucial given that stroke is a leading cause of disability and death. For rural communities, where access to specialized care may be limited, telestroke technology is particularly vital in ensuring patients receive prompt intervention, as every minute lost can result in the loss of 1.9 million neurons.
Jun 11, 2025
June is recognized as Men's Health Month, serving as an important reminder that consistent, small actions can lead to significant and lasting improvements in overall well-being. Aspirus Health emphasizes that one of the most effective steps men can take for their health and happiness is to establish and maintain a close relationship with a primary care provider. Regular check-ups and open communication with a doctor can help in the early detection and management of potential health issues.
Jun 11, 2025
Aspirus Health is implementing upcoming security enhancements for the MyAspirus patient portal, with new measures taking effect on June 11, 2025. To bolster account security, users will be required to verify their identity using two-factor authentication when logging in from new or unsaved devices. This change aims to provide an extra layer of protection for patient health information accessible through the portal. The MyAspirus app offers patients 24/7 access to their medical records, appointment scheduling, prescription refills, and billing information, and this security update is part of Aspirus's commitment to safeguarding patient data in the digital environment.
Jun 09, 2025
Community Health Improvement Week was observed from June 9 through 13, highlighting the essential work of public health professionals and their partners. This week recognizes their dedication to building healthier communities through collaborative efforts and preventative strategies. Aspirus Health actively participates in this endeavor, emphasizing their commitment to working with others to improve community health outcomes and implement initiatives focused on prevention.
