Recent News

Jun 13, 2025
Providence, the Renton, Washington-based non-profit health system, announced a restructuring this week that will result in the reduction of 600 full-time-equivalent positions. The organization stated that these difficult but necessary steps are part of a comprehensive approach to achieving financial sustainability. The affected roles are primarily in non-clinical, administrative functions, although some patient-care roles are also impacted. Providence is providing transitional resources to impacted employees and noted that there are over 5,000 open roles across the system where affected individuals may be shifted where possible. The health system cited proposed cuts to Medicare and Medicaid, ongoing payment denials and delays from insurers, and higher labor and supply costs as necessitating the cuts. This move follows previous steps taken by Providence, including downsizing its executive team and freezing nonclinical hiring earlier in the year, as the system has faced significant operating losses in recent years, including a $644 million operating loss in 2024 and a $244 million operating loss in the first quarter of 2025. Jun 05, 2025
California Attorney General Rob Bonta filed a motion on June 5, 2025, to enforce a stipulation and order requiring Providence St. Joseph Hospital to comply with the state's Emergency Services Law (ESL), particularly concerning emergency abortion care. This action comes after Providence reportedly indicated an intent to modify its prior stipulation and assert exceptions to its obligations under the ESL. Attorney General Bonta emphasized that the terms of the court order are clear and require full compliance, ensuring patients can access life-saving health services, including emergency abortion care, without exception. The motion is a response to concerns that Providence is attempting to find loopholes to avoid its duty to patients, referencing a previous lawsuit filed in September 2024 regarding the hospital's alleged refusal to provide emergency abortion care to a patient experiencing an obstetric emergency.
Jun 05, 2025
Legislation aimed at ensuring safe hospital staffing and improving patient care advanced in the California Senate on June 5, 2025. While this news is reported by SEIU 121RN, a nurses' union that represents healthcare professionals at various hospitals including some within the Providence St. Joseph Health system, it is relevant as it directly impacts the operational requirements and potentially the labor relations of hospitals like those run by Providence in California. The advancement of such legislation signifies a push for stricter regulations on nurse-to-patient ratios and working conditions, which could have significant implications for staffing models and costs within the Providence network in the state.
Jun 04, 2025
Providence Saint John's Health Center in Los Angeles unveiled its first mobile Hands-Only CPR Kiosk on June 4, 2025. This initiative is a partnership with the American Heart Association and aims to teach life-saving CPR skills to the public. The mobile kiosk is designed to travel to various locations across Los Angeles, making CPR training more accessible to the community. This effort highlights Providence's commitment to community health education and improving outcomes for cardiac emergencies by empowering more people with the knowledge and skills to perform Hands-Only CPR.
Jun 02, 2025
Providence was recognized as a System of Change by Practice Greenhealth for the fifth consecutive year, receiving a total of 51 Environmental Excellence Awards across 32 of its hospitals in 2025. This annual awards program acknowledges the nation's leading organizations dedicated to environmental sustainability in healthcare. Five Providence hospitals, including Swedish Edmonds, Providence St. Patrick Hospital, Swedish Cherry Hill, Swedish First Hill, and Swedish Issaquah, received the high honor of a Top 25 Environmental Excellence Award. Additionally, two hospitals, Providence Mission Hospital and Providence Santa Rosa Memorial Hospital, received awards for the first time. Providence also earned Circles of Excellence, Greenhealth Emerald, and Greenhealth Partner for Change awards. The health system highlighted that these environmental stewardship efforts not only contribute to a healthier planet but also result in significant operational cost savings.
