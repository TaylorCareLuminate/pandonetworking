Executive Leadership

David Banks

President and CEO

David Banks is the President and CEO of AdventHealth, assuming the role effective April 3, 2025. He has a history with AdventHealth spanning over three decades. Prior to his appointment as CEO, Banks served as the group CEO for the Primary Health Division and the Multi-State Division, overseeing 22 campuses across eight states and three regional partnerships. For eight years, he also held the position of AdventHealth's chief strategy officer, where he was involved in the development and execution of the organization's 10-year business plan, Vision 2030. His operational expertise is rooted in earlier administrative roles at several facilities within the Central Florida Division. Banks holds a master's degree in nonprofit administration from the University of Maryland and a bachelor's degree from Pacific Union College. He has also been involved in community initiatives, including serving on the executive board for the American Heart Association of Central Florida, as chairman for Community Vision, and on the board of the YMCA of Central Florida.

Gary F. Thurber

AdventHealth Board Chair

Gary F. Thurber is the Board Chair at AdventHealth and has a background in pastoral leadership within the Seventh-day Adventist Church. He currently serves as the president for the Mid-America Union Conference of Seventh-day Adventists, which covers a nine-state area in the Midwest. Thurber was ordained in 1987 and has pastored in various conferences, including Kansas-Nebraska, Florida, and Michigan. He has also held presidential roles in the Rocky Mountain, Indiana, and Northern New England conferences. Before his current role, he was the executive secretary of the Lake Union Conference. Thurber holds a bachelor's degree from Southern Adventist University and a master's degree in religion from Andrews University, where he is also enrolled in the PhD leadership program. He previously served as AdventHealth Board Chair from 2017 to 2019 and was re-elected in December 2023. He also serves on the board of other healthcare and higher education institutions and chairs the board of Union College.

Brian Adams

Executive Vice President, President/CEO for the Central Florida Division

Brian Adams serves as the Executive Vice President, President/CEO for the Central Florida Division at AdventHealth. In this role, he is responsible for the strategy, planning, development, implementation, and operational oversight of AdventHealth's care network across Orange, Seminole and Osceola counties, which includes 17 hospitals and emergency departments and over 32,000 team members. Before leading the Central Florida Division, Adams held leadership positions at AdventHealth in the Tampa Bay and Polk County communities. His previous roles include serving as senior vice president of new markets for the AdventHealth Central Florida Division – South Region and president/CEO for AdventHealth Tampa, where he oversaw a $100 million expansion project. He also served as president/CEO for AdventHealth Wesley Chapel and CEO of AdventHealth's operations in Polk County, where he was market CEO for the newly acquired Heart of Florida Regional Medical Center and Lake Wales Medical Center. During his tenure in Polk County, AdventHealth also formed an affiliation with Bond Clinic. Adams has been with AdventHealth for 20 years. Adams holds a Master's in Business Administration degree from the University of Central Florida and a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration from Union College in Lincoln, Nebraska. He is originally from Oshawa, Ontario, Canada. Adams serves on the Board of Lift Orlando, a community development organization. Under his leadership, AdventHealth's Central Florida Division has achieved straight "A" patient safety grades from The Leapfrog Group, and AdventHealth Orlando has been ranked as the No. 1 hospital in the metro area by U.S. News & World Report for 14 years. He has also overseen multi-million-dollar development projects and expanded recruitment efforts in local high schools and invested in Florida nursing schools.

Olesea Azevedo

Senior Executive Vice President, Chief Culture and Transformation Officer

Olesea Azevedo is the Senior Executive Vice President, Chief Culture and Transformation Officer at AdventHealth. She has a strong background in Human Resources, having started her career as an HR Generalist and progressing through various leadership roles. Executive Vice President, Chief Legal Officer

Michael Cacciatore, MD

Executive Vice President, Chief Clinical Officer

Michael Cacciatore, MD, is the Executive Vice President and Chief Clinical Officer for AdventHealth. He assumed this role on March 4, 2024, and has been with AdventHealth for 17 years. In his current position, he provides leadership for safety and quality programs and the clinical performance of the company. Before this role, Dr. Cacciatore served as Chief Clinical Officer for AdventHealth's West Florida Division from February 2022 to 2024, where he oversaw the clinical agenda and advanced strategies to improve clinical outcomes. Previously, he was the Chief Medical Officer for AdventHealth Medical Group (AHMG) in Central Florida, where he focused on enhancing team culture and positioning physicians as leaders. Under his leadership, the first AHMG Wellness Committee was created, and the first Chief Wellness Officer was appointed. Dr. Cacciatore also served as Medical Director of the AdventHealth Obstetrics Hospitalists team in Central Florida and was a clinical faculty member in the Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology for the AdventHealth Family Practice Residency Program. Dr. Cacciatore is a board-certified Obstetrician Gynecologist with over 30 years of experience in the medical field. He completed his undergraduate and medical degrees, as well as his medical residency, at the University of South Florida.

Trish Celano

Senior Vice President, Associate Chief Clinical Officer, Chief Nurse Executive

Trish Celano, MSN, RN, is the Senior Vice President, Associate Chief Clinical Officer, and Chief Nurse Executive at AdventHealth. She has over 30 years of experience in nursing and patient care. Celano's career path to nursing was unconventional, starting in the insurance industry where she developed an interest in healthcare. She earned a Bachelor's degree in Nursing from the University of Delaware and a Master's degree in Nursing Management from the University of Central Florida (UCF). She is a proud alumna of the UCF College of Nursing. Throughout her career at AdventHealth (formerly Florida Hospital), Celano has held various leadership roles. She was the first Chief Nursing Officer at AdventHealth for Children, where she played a key role in the hospital receiving Magnet designation for nursing excellence. She also served as Senior Vice President and Chief Nursing Officer for AdventHealth Orlando and as Senior Vice President and Regional Chief Clinical Officer for AdventHealth's Central Florida Division – North Region. In her current role, which she assumed in 2019, Celano supports 52 hospitals across nine states and is responsible for advancing the clinical strategy for the health system. She is actively involved in professional nursing organizations and has served as the president of the Central Florida Organization of Nurse Executives. Celano is recognized for her compassionate leadership, focus on whole-person care, and efforts to support and develop nurses. She has been instrumental in initiatives focused on workforce retention and professional development for nurses within AdventHealth.
