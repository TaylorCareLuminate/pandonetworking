Recent News

Jun 14, 2025
Billings Clinic is reportedly attempting to address what it terms "unsustainable" financial losses. According to a memo obtained by KTVQ-television and reported by Sheridan Media, Montana's largest hospital is seeking to implement financial cuts exceeding $4.5 million per month. These cost-saving measures are expected to include salary reductions, suspension of company contributions to employee retirement plans, a hiring freeze, and a decrease in contract labor. This effort to mitigate financial challenges comes after the resignation of former CEO Dr. Scott Ellner and other executives less than a year prior and follows financial strain experienced during the COVID-19 pandemic.
Jun 10, 2025
Billings Clinic held a Virtual Trauma Education session on Tuesday, June 10, 2025, from 9:00 AM to 10:00 AM Mountain Time. The session was conducted via TEAMS. The presentation was titled 'Trauma in Tandem: A Case Study on Geriatric Trauma' and was presented by Kat Raftery, PA. The listing for this event is available on the Virtual Trauma Education page of the Billings Clinic website, which also provides information on viewing recordings of past virtual trauma education sessions.
Jun 06, 2025
Billings Clinic has opened a new 21-bed surgical intensive care unit (SICU) and a regional operations center, marking an expansion of their critical care capacity. This new SICU is the first dedicated surgical intensive care unit in Montana and is designed to provide continuous monitoring and specialized treatment for critically ill surgical patients. The expansion was made possible through a donation of over $10 million from the Helmsley Charitable Trust. The new regional operations center, also funded by the donation, is equipped with monitors to allow staff to communicate with providers, manage patient traffic, and coordinate with rural hospitals. Both additions aim to enhance regional care and allow patients with severe injuries to remain closer to home for treatment. The SICU is slated to open later in the summer.
May 23, 2025
Billings Clinic received a $200,000 grant from PacificSource to acquire new state-of-the-art robotic surgery equipment. This equipment, specifically a digital C-arm with Cone Beam CT capabilities, will be paired with robotic bronchoscopy equipment. The technology provides real-time tridimensional imaging, enhancing the precision for evaluating smaller and more complex lesions during lung biopsies. This is expected to lead to safer, earlier, and more accurate diagnoses for lung cancer and pulmonology patients. The advanced imaging also allows for precise targeting and sampling, potentially leading to faster time to treatment. The new equipment is anticipated to be installed and ready for clinical use by the summer of 2025. [9, 11, 19]
May 01, 2025
Billings Clinic announced that it earned an 'A' Hospital Safety Grade from The Leapfrog Group for the spring 2025 Он. This recognition is a national acknowledgment of Billings Clinic's commitment to patient safety and efforts to protect patients from preventable harm and errors. The Leapfrog Group is an independent national nonprofit watchdog focused on patient safety, and they issue grades to hospitals based on over 30 measures of performance in preventing patient harm and medical errors. This is an increase from their 'B' grade in the spring of 2022, highlighting the organization's focus on continuous improvement in patient care and safety. [5]

Executive Leadership

Clint Seger, MD

Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

Board-certified family medicine physician. With Billings Clinic for 16 years, serving first as a family medicine physician and hospitalist, then serving as Billings Clinic's Regional Chief Medical Officer before being named as the CEO of Billings Clinic and then the Chief Physician Executive of the combined health system.

Erin Rains, DO

Chief Medical Officer (CMO), Hospital

Erin Rains, DO, is the Chief Medical Officer (CMO), Hospital at Billings Clinic, also holding the title of Chief of Hospital & Acute Care. She earned her Doctor of Osteopathy (D.O.) degree from the Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine and holds a Bachelor of Science (B.S.) degree in Human Nutrition from Virginia Polytechnic Institute and State University. Dr. Rains is board certified in Internal Medicine and has experience in pulmonary and critical care medicine. Prior to joining Billings Clinic in 2017, she worked as an Academic Hospitalist for Main Line Health and as a Pulmonary & Critical Care Fellow at Lankenau Medical Center.

Dania Block, MSN, RN, NEA-BC

Chief Nursing Officer

Dania Block, MSN, RN, NEA-BC, is the Chief Nursing Officer at Billings Clinic. She has over 24 years of experience in nursing and joined Billings Clinic in March 2004. Her career includes work in critical access hospitals, rural care, acute care, and leadership roles. At Billings Clinic, she has focused on improving nursing practice, enhancing nursing-sensitive quality indicators, fostering professional development, and championing the Magnet program. She was previously the Interim Chief Nursing Officer and a Director for Med/Surg. Dania Block is described as a nursing advocate who focuses on creating an environment of trust and transparency, is accessible, and willing to assist her teams directly. She is a proud Montanan, raised in Northeast Montana, and has raised two daughters in Billings.

Ryan Schwanke, MD

Chief Medical Officer (CMO), Clinic

Jim Duncan

President Emeritus, Billings Clinic Foundation

Jim Duncan served as the President of the Billings Clinic Foundation for 30 years and is now the President Emeritus. During his tenure, the Foundation's assets grew significantly from $3 million to over $160 million, supporting various critical programs and initiatives at Billings Clinic. He was integral to shaping and expanding healthcare in Montana. Notably, he was involved in the Foundation's efforts that led to breaking a $30 million fundraising goal for Montana's first Level One Trauma Center, acquiring new technology, and purchasing an airplane for the MedFlight program. Prior to his work at the Foundation, a source mentions he came from ZooMontana. He was also listed as the Billings Clinic Chief Communications Officer and Foundation President in a 2023 financial filing. Jim Duncan was scheduled to retire in July 2025. He also previously served as the Past President on the Governing Board of Beartooth Billings Clinic.
