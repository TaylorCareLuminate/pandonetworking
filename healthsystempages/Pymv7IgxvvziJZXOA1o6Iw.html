Jun 10, 2025
Seventy-four high school students participating in the Fairfax County Health Department's Public Health Youth Ambassadors Program (PHYAP) spent a day at the Inova Fairfax medical campus last week. The students, from Justice and Mount Vernon high schools, had the opportunity to interact with healthcare professionals and explore various career paths within the medical and healthcare fields. This visit aimed to provide insight into the healthcare industry for the young ambassadors.
May 30, 2025
Inova Fairfax Medical Campus hosted the "Transforming Maternal Health: Innovations in Cardiac, Critical, and Mental Care" conference. This event was designed to advance the skills and knowledge of healthcare providers in managing complex maternal health conditions, including cardiac complications, obstetric emergencies, and severe mental health disorders during pregnancy. The conference highlighted evidence-based strategies and best practices for improving maternal care, integrating patient and family perspectives, and promoting interdisciplinary collaboration with the goal of reducing maternal morbidity and mortality, addressing health disparities, and ensuring safer outcomes for pregnant women and their families. The program included unique high-fidelity simulation labs to reinforce topics covered in lectures, with cases based on actual obstetric critical care events. Topics covered in the simulations included Placenta Accreta Spectrum Management and Hypertensive Emergencies in Pregnancy.
May 27, 2025
Inova Schar Cancer has joined a national initiative called the ADvanced Analysis for Precision cancer Therapy (ADAPT) Program, funded by the Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health (ARPA-H). This program aims to transform cancer treatment decision-making through a groundbreaking national effort.
