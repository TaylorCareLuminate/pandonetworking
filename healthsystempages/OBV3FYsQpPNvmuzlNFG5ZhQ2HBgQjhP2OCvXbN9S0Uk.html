Recent News

Jun 11, 2025
Winchester, Va.-based Valley Health has announced the expansion of its leadership team with the promotion of Chris Rucker to chief administrative officer. In this new role, Rucker will be responsible for overseeing key administrative functions within the health system, including human resources, legal, compliance, business development, and importantly, IT. This appointment signifies a change in the leadership structure directly impacting the information technology department at Valley Health. Previously, Rucker served as the chief strategy officer and chief of staff for the system. Prior to that, he led Valley Regional Enterprises, which is the system's subsidiary for ambulatory and post-acute services. This move centralizes the oversight of IT under a new chief administrative officer as part of the health system's broader leadership changes which also included the promotion of Tonya Smith, PharmD, to COO, overseeing operations for all six hospitals, ambulatory sites, and post-acute services. [4]
Jun 07, 2025
Valley Health has announced the development of two new healthcare facilities: Valley Health Primary Care - Greenwood in Winchester, VA, and a new campus in Ranson, WV. These projects broke ground in December 2024 and are expected to be completed by the fall of 2025. The Winchester facility, located off Senseny Road near the Greenwood Fire Department, will be a 19,000 sq ft building with 18 exam rooms, offering comprehensive primary care and an on-site outpatient lab. The Ranson facility, an 18,000 sq ft building off Route 9, will replace the current Valley Health Internal Medicine | Ranson practice, providing more space, a dedicated outpatient lab, and new specialty services for the first time in Jefferson County, WV. The existing Valley Health Urgent Care in Ranson will remain at its current location. These new facilities are intended to improve resident access to high-quality medical care closer to home. [8, 7]
Jun 06, 2025
Valley Health reaffirmed its commitment to fostering a safe, supportive, and secure environment in recognition of Hospitals Against Violence Day. This initiative highlights the health system's dedication to patient and staff safety and aligns with broader efforts in the healthcare industry to address and prevent violence in healthcare settings. The recognition underscores the importance of creating a secure environment for everyone within the Valley Health System.

Jeffrey Feit, MD, MSHA

Valley Health Population Health and Community Health Officer

Jeffrey Feit, MD, MSHA, is the Chief Population and Community Health Officer and Chief Medical Information Officer at Valley Health System. He became part of the administrative team at Valley Health in 2011 or 2012, following six years as vice president of Medical Affairs at Page Memorial Hospital. His tenure at Page Memorial Hospital spanned from 2000 to 2012, where he held roles including Chief Medical Officer. Dr. Feit has a background in family medicine, having practiced in Luray and Fairfax, Virginia, and served on the faculty of Family Medicine departments at Virginia Commonwealth University and the University of Virginia. His career began as a physician at Inova Health System from 1998 to 2000. Dr. Feit completed his medical degree and a family medicine residency at the University of Virginia School of Medicine. He also holds a Master of Science in Health Care Administration from Virginia Commonwealth University and a Bachelor of Arts in International Affairs and Public Policy from Princeton University. He is recognized as an experienced physician leader with expertise in population health, community health, and clinical informatics.

Elizabeth Savage, MSOD

Chief Human Resources Officer

Elizabeth Savage, MSOD, is the Chief Human Resources Officer at Valley Health System, a position she has held since 2010. She also serves as Senior Vice President and Vice President of Community Health and Wellness. With over 25 years of experience in human resources and organizational development, Savage has worked in both for-profit and not-for-profit organizations. Prior to joining Valley Health, she was the Senior Vice President of Human Resources at Bon Secours Charity Health System. Her background also includes leadership roles at GE Capital, Norbert Technologies, and Hyperion Software. Savage earned a Master of Science in Organization Development from American University/National Training Laboratory and a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration/MIS from the University at Albany, SUNY. She is also certified in Lean and Myers-Briggs Type Indicator. She is a member of the Society of Human Resource Management and the American Society for Healthcare Human Resource Administration. Additionally, she serves on the Discovery Museum Board in Winchester and is involved in community efforts to create healthcare career paths.
