Recent News

Jun 10, 2025
Ascension, a Catholic nonprofit health system headquartered in St. Louis, is reportedly in advanced discussions to acquire AmSurg, a prominent operator of ambulatory surgery centers (ASCs). AmSurg currently manages over 250 facilities across the United States. This potential acquisition signifies a strategic move by Ascension to bolster its outpatient care capabilities. The healthcare industry is increasingly shifting towards more cost-effective outpatient procedures, and this acquisition aligns with that trend, following similar strategies seen with other major healthcare systems like Tenet Healthcare. Acquiring AmSurg would significantly expand Ascension's footprint in the ASC market and enhance its competitive standing against other large ASC operators.
Jun 06, 2025
St. Louis-based Ascension is demonstrating financial recovery and is actively looking into acquisitions after a period of divesting assets and restructuring its operations. The health system, which reported an operating loss for the nine months ending March 31, 2025, has significantly improved its liquidity, partly by collecting accounts receivable that were impacted by the May 2024 ransomware attack and the earlier Change Healthcare cyberattack. Ascension's recurring operating performance, patient volumes, and revenue have shown measurable growth. This improved financial standing is allowing Ascension to reinvest in crucial areas including clinical infrastructure, digital innovation, and expanding access to care in the communities it serves. This strategic financial management supports their potential re-entry into acquisition activities.
Jun 05, 2025
Ascension has released a policy spotlight titled "Medicaid Deserves a Brighter Future Through Effective, Sustainable Reforms." The article highlights Ascension's perspective on the importance of Medicaid in providing healthcare access, particularly for vulnerable populations. It discusses potential reforms that could strengthen the program and ensure its long-term viability, aligning with Ascension's mission to serve all, especially those in need, across the 16 states and Washington, D.C. where it operates.
Jun 05, 2025
Ascension's utilization of digital nudge technology to address gaps in preventive care has been highlighted in a feature in NEJM Evidence. This indicates Ascension is employing technology to improve patient outcomes by encouraging them to receive necessary preventive services. The article likely details how Ascension is leveraging digital tools and behavioral science principles to remind and motivate patients to schedule appointments, get screenings, or adhere to treatment plans, ultimately aiming to proactively manage health and reduce the need for more intensive interventions.
Jun 03, 2025
Ascension has announced the appointment of Rob Rose, DNP, RN, NEA-BC, as their new Senior Vice President of Nursing and Chief Nursing Officer. This leadership change aims to support Ascension's mission and operational goals within their nursing teams. Rose's background and experience in nursing leadership are expected to contribute to the organization's ongoing efforts in patient care and clinical excellence across its network.

Executive Leadership

Tom VanOsdol

MS, MA, FACHE. Began serving on July 1 (as of March 31, 2022), replacing Timm Glover. Had served as Senior Vice President, Ascension, and Ministry Market Executive, Ascension Florida and Gulf Coast, since 2017.

Peter M. Leibold

Executive Vice President and Chief Advocacy Officer

Peter M. Leibold is the Executive Vice President and Chief Advocacy Officer at Ascension, a role he took on in 2014. In this position, he is responsible for leading Ascension's advocacy and government affairs strategy with a focus on transforming healthcare and ensuring access for vulnerable populations. Prior to joining Ascension, Leibold was the Executive Vice President and Chief Executive Officer of the American Health Lawyers Association (AHLA) for fifteen years. His background also includes serving as General Counsel to the Catholic Health Association of the United States (CHA) from 1996 to 1999 and working at the law firm of Bryan Cave. Earlier in his career, he held multiple roles, including chief of staff, for U.S. Senator John C. Danforth and clerked for a judge on the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit. Leibold is a graduate of Haverford College and Yale Law School.

Christine Kocot McCoy

Executive Vice President and General Counsel

Christine Kocot McCoy is the Executive Vice President and General Counsel at Ascension. In this role, she is responsible for providing legal counsel to the organization, its Board of Directors, and executive management, as well as leading legal services for Ascension and its subsidiary organizations. Her responsibilities include managing affiliation initiatives, supporting business development, and handling outside legal relationships. Before becoming Executive Vice President and General Counsel, she served as Senior Vice President and General Counsel, and earlier as Deputy General Counsel at Ascension. She joined Ascension in 2004. Prior to joining Ascension, McCoy was a partner at the law firm Armstrong Teasdale, LLP, where she focused on medical malpractice and employment litigation. She represented hospitals and physicians in malpractice cases in Missouri and Illinois state and federal courts, and also handled employment and labor matters. Before her time at Armstrong Teasdale, she served as an Assistant Attorney General acting as Associate General Counsel for the Missouri Department of Labor, Division of Labor Standards. McCoy holds a bachelor's degree in business from Eastern Illinois University and a Juris Doctorate from Saint Louis University School of Law. She is a licensed member of the Missouri and Illinois bar associations. She has been recognized for her legal work, including being named St. Louis Business Journal's 2021 General Counsel of the Year and one of the area's "Most Influential Business Women" in 2007. She has also been elected to the board of directors of FM Global.

Eric S. Engler

Executive Vice President and Chief of Staff

Eric S. Engler is the Executive Vice President and Chief of Staff at Ascension. In this role, he works with the President and CEO to align the activities of various Ascension leadership teams and the Board of Directors. He also oversees the day-to-day operations and strategic evolution of Ascension's Mission-inspired Transformation program and leads the Ascension Transformation Office. Prior to his current position, Engler served as Senior Vice President and Chief Strategy Officer for Ascension, where he was responsible for developing the organization's strategy. He joined Ascension in 2001. Before joining Ascension, Engler held financial planning roles at Reuters, plc, and Bridge Information Systems, Inc. He also served as a Transaction Services Director for PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP, leading merger and acquisition transactions, and was an Equity Research Associate with Morgan Stanley & Co., Inc. He began his career in the Assurance and Business Advisory Services practice of Price Waterhouse. Engler holds a bachelor's degree in Accountancy from the University of Illinois at Champaign/Urbana and a Master of Business Administration from the University of Chicago Booth Graduate School of Business. He is also a graduate of the Ascension Leadership Academy and serves on the Board of Trustees for Saint Louis University.

Richard Fogel

Executive Vice President and Chief Clinical Officer

Dr. Richard Fogel is the Executive Vice President and Chief Clinical Officer at Ascension. He assumed this role in August 2022, joining the Ascension Leadership Team. Prior to this appointment, he was the Chief Clinical Officer for Clinical & Network Services at Ascension since 2019, responsible for clinical performance across the national health ministry. His work has included leading quality and safety initiatives, developing physician alignment strategies and service lines, and building a system-wide clinical research infrastructure. Dr. Fogel also headed Ascension's clinical response to the COVID-19 pandemic. A practicing cardiologist and electrophysiologist, he previously served as Chief Clinical Officer for Ascension Indiana. His past leadership roles in Indiana include Chief Executive Officer of St. Vincent Medical Group and Chair of the Office of the Chief Medical Officer at St. Vincent. He earned his bachelor's and medical degrees from Brown University and completed his postgraduate medical training, including an electrophysiology fellowship, at Boston University Medical Center and Ascension St. Vincent Indianapolis Hospital. Dr. Fogel is scheduled to transition to the role of Chief Health Outcomes Officer on January 1, 2025, before retiring on June 30, 2025.
