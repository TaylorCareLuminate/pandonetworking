Oct 16, 2024
The closure of St. Luke's Behavioral Health Center in Phoenix has caused significant challenges for families seeking care for loved ones with serious mental illnesses due to a severe lack of hospital beds in the valley. The state health department shut down St. Luke's in August after an investigation revealed safety violations. The closure resulted in the transfer of nearly 100 patients to other facilities, including Phoenix Medical Psychiatric Hospital. Families are finding it difficult to secure court-ordered treatment, with limited options available unless a patient becomes violent or severely disconnected from reality. While the state health department stated they had not seen an indication of a lack of access to behavioral health beds within a hospital setting, families and advocates argue that the system, already at a breaking point, is now severely impacted by the loss of St. Luke's capacity. Steward Health Care, the parent company of St. Luke's, had filed for bankruptcy in May, and although Honor Health took over operations at other Steward hospitals in Arizona, the future of the behavioral health center remains uncertain.
