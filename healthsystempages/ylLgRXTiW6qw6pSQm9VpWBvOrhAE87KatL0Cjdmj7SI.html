Recent News

Jun 05, 2025
Marshfield Medical Centers in Beaver Dam, Eau Claire, Marshfield, Minocqua, Rice Lake and Weston have been recognized as “Birthing-Friendly” hospitals by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). This designation acknowledges the health system's commitment to providing safe, high-quality maternity care. The "Birthing-Friendly" recognition is part of CMS's Maternity Care Action Plan, aimed at improving maternal health outcomes nationally. Hospitals receiving this honor participate in perinatal quality improvement collaboratives and implement evidence-based practices for maternal safety. In addition to the CMS designation, Marshfield Medical Centers in Eau Claire and Marshfield were named High Performing in Maternity Care (Uncomplicated Pregnancy) by U.S. News & World Report, based on outcomes like low C-section rates, breastfeeding support, and access to midwife-led care. The Eau Claire team also received Advanced Certification in Perinatal Care from The Joint Commission, recognizing their integrated, evidence-based care for both uncomplicated and high-risk pregnancies. These accolades highlight the dedication and excellence of the women's health teams across Marshfield Clinic Health System.
Jun 05, 2025
Marshfield Clinic Health System (MCHS) was presented with an award for excellence in quality improvement leadership by the Wisconsin Collaborative for Healthcare Quality (WCHQ) at WCHQ's annual Statewide Quality Improvement Event in Madison. The award was presented by Dr. Susan Schneider, chief medical officer, Aspirus Clinics, and accepted by Kori Krueger, MD, chief quality officer, and Tammy Simon, vice president of the Institute for Quality, Innovation & Patient Safety on behalf of MCHS. WCHQ recognizes member organizations annually for superior clinical performance and patient outcomes, but this specific award honors exceptional quality improvement, active engagement with WCHQ's work, and mentorship of other organizations. MCHS was recognized for its notable improvement across many WCHQ measures and its willingness to share its knowledge with other organizations to help them improve patient care quality, demonstrating a commitment to ensuring access to high-quality care for all Wisconsin citizens.
Apr 09, 2025
Sanford Health is implementing layoffs impacting approximately 96 leadership and administrative positions as part of the ongoing integration with Marshfield Clinic Health System. This move is aimed at realigning and consolidating roles as Marshfield Clinic is incorporated into the Sanford structure. While specific positions were not disclosed, Sanford stated that no patient-facing roles would be affected by these changes. The affected employees are being offered severance packages, counseling, financial, and career services, and are encouraged to apply for other open positions within the larger Sanford system. The integration process also involves developing a unified operating model for the combined health plans, Sanford Health Plan and Security Health Plan, which will include the addition of around 20 new positions within the health plan structure to enhance value for members. Apr 03, 2025
Following the completion of the merger in January, Sanford Health CEO Bill Gassen discussed the integration with Marshfield Clinic Health System. He highlighted the planned investment of $500 million over the next five years for improvements at Marshfield Clinic facilities. A significant technological undertaking of this integration is the migration of Marshfield Clinic's hospitals and other facilities onto Epic's electronic health record (EHR) system, which is the platform used by Sanford Health. Gassen anticipates that this comprehensive transition of EHR systems will take approximately two years to complete, with planning and design work currently underway. He also noted the benefits already being seen through the synergies between the two health plans, Sanford Health Plan and Marshfield's Security Health Plan. [33]
Feb 14, 2025
Leaders from the recently merged Sanford Health and Marshfield Clinic Health System are discussing the potential of artificial intelligence (AI) in healthcare. Sanford Health CEO Bill Gassen expressed his anticipation for advancements in AI, believing they will enable clinicians to dedicate more of their time and focus to direct patient care. While the article itself requires a premium membership to access the full details, the headline and snippets indicate that AI's promise in improving clinical workflows and efficiency is a topic of focus for the leadership of the combined health system. Executive Leadership

Kathy Bressler

Chief Operating Officer, Marshfield Clinic Health System

Kathy Bressler is the Chief Operating Officer at Marshfield Clinic Health System. She has an extensive background in healthcare administration and nursing, with over 42 years of experience in the field. Prior to her role at Marshfield Clinic Health System, where she started in July 2022, Bressler held positions at CHI Health and CHI Franciscan Health, including SVP COO/CNO and COO/CNO-President of St. Clare Hospital. She began her career as a nurse manager at St. Joseph Medical Center in Tacoma in 1990. Bressler holds a Master of Nursing degree with a concentration in Community and Leadership from the University of Washington Tacoma. She is recognized for her skills in strategic planning, relationship building, and fiscal responsibility, with a track record of developing standard operating procedures and managing complex healthcare operations. Her experience also includes managing various services such as emergency services, medical-surgical services, ambulatory care, burn treatment, and comprehensive orthopedic and gastrointestinal services.

Melissa Breen

Executive Vice President of System Transformation and Chief of Staff, Marshfield Clinic Healthcare System

Brings together multiple administrative groups to operationally and strategically create an environment that enables Marshfield Clinic Healthcare System to transform healthcare for Wisconsin. Helped transform the Marshfield Clinic from a multi-specialty clinic to a healthcare system with 11 hospitals. Led the development of the first System HEDI Office. Coordinates and implements leadership prerogatives across a multi-faceted organization. Oversees the integration of the system's external affairs function. Has 25 years of healthcare experience and has been with Marshfield Clinic since 2016. Prior to joining Marshfield Clinic, she was the Vice President of Membership and Certification of the Medical Group Management Association. Melissa is Fellow of the Carol Emmott Foundation and a member of The Equity Collaborative.
