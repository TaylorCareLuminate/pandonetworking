Apr 03, 2025
Southern Tennessee Regional Health System (STRHS) Pulaski's 2024 Community Benefit Report, published in April 2025, detailed significant investments in healthcare technology totaling nearly $1.4 million in capital improvements during the 2024 calendar year. These investments included the acquisition of a Mako robotic surgery device, new EKG machines, and a Hana Orthopedic Surgery Table. The report highlights that these technological advancements are part of the hospital's ongoing efforts to meet the growing healthcare needs of its community by continually investing in its facilities and healthcare technology. The report also noted that STRHS Pulaski added 34 providers in 2024 and donated more than $18.3 million in health services to those in need, demonstrating a commitment to ensuring access to care. Mar 03, 2025
Lifepoint Health and Ascension Saint Thomas are expanding their Highpoint Health joint venture in Middle Tennessee to include Southern Tennessee Regional Health System (STRHS) – Winchester and Southern Tennessee Regional Health System (STRHS) – Sewanee. These two facilities, previously legacy Lifepoint Health sites, are located in Southeastern Tennessee and will now join the Highpoint Health System joint venture, which is jointly owned and operated by Lifepoint Health and Ascension Saint Thomas. The expansion aims to enhance healthcare access for the communities served by bringing together the clinical expertise and resources of both organizations. The Winchester and Sewanee hospitals and sites of care will be majority-owned and operated by Lifepoint Health and co-branded with Ascension Saint Thomas. The current leadership team is expected to remain in place, with no significant changes anticipated for employees. Moving forward, the facilities will be renamed Highpoint Health – Winchester and Highpoint Health – Sewanee. [2, 3, 4, 22]

Southern Tennessee Regional Health System (STRHS) – Winchester and Southern Tennessee Regional Health System (STRHS) – Sewanee, which were legacy Lifepoint Health facilities, have joined the Highpoint Health joint venture partnership between Lifepoint Health and Ascension Saint Thomas. This expansion into the Highpoint Health system, which is jointly owned and operated by Lifepoint Health and Ascension Saint Thomas, is intended to enhance healthcare access for the communities served. The Winchester and Sewanee hospitals will be majority-owned and operated by Lifepoint Health and co-branded with Ascension Saint Thomas. The current leadership team is expected to remain in place, with no significant changes anticipated for employees. The facilities will adopt the names Highpoint Health – Winchester and Highpoint Health – Sewanee, aiming to grow and strengthen specialty services as part of the Highpoint Health network. Dec 12, 2024
Southern Tennessee Regional Health System Pulaski, part of the STRHS network, announced their 2024 Core Value Award Winners. These awards recognize employees who exemplify the hospital's Core Values: Champion Patient Care, Do the Right Thing, Embrace Individuality, Act with Kindness, and Make a Difference Together.
