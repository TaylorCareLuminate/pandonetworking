Recent News

May 07, 2025
The PAM Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Dover, located in Dover, Delaware, has filed for an expansion to its existing facility, as reported on May 7, 2025. The proposed expansion will add 13,084 square feet to the hospital, comprising two distinct additions of 10,062 and 3,022 square feet. The plans for the expansion also include the addition of a therapy garden, 64 parking spaces along with associated circulation improvements, and a stormwater management pond. The application for this expansion was scheduled to be presented to the Dover Planning Commission during their meeting on May 19, 2025.
Apr 30, 2025
An article in Modern Healthcare discussed the challenges with remote patient monitoring (RPM) in the home health sector, mentioning Pam Health at Home's experiences. Peggy Dunaway, patient care coordinator for Pam Health at Home in Buda, Texas, noted difficulties with patients using digital blood pressure devices and reluctance to share internet passwords for heart rate monitors due to security concerns. Despite these challenges, Dunaway indicated that the technology provided valuable patient information and that Pam Health at Home is considering trying RPM again. The article highlights broader issues in the industry regarding reimbursement, connectivity, and patient acceptance of RPM technology.
Apr 17, 2025
PAM Health has entered into a multi-year exclusive agreement with Nuo Therapeutics to utilize their Aurix System for chronic wound healing across all of PAM Health's long-term acute care hospitals, inpatient rehabilitation hospitals, and outpatient and wound care clinics. The Aurix System is an FDA-cleared autologous platelet-rich plasma (PRP) therapy. This collaboration aims to provide patients across the PAM Health network access to this innovative treatment to accelerate wound healing, reduce complications, and improve outcomes. PAM Health highlighted that integrating Aurix PRP aligns with their strategy to offer comprehensive, cost-effective care, especially for patients at risk of limb loss and hospital readmission. Nuo Therapeutics noted that PAM Health's clinical reach across 23 states will significantly expand access to the Aurix System. [18]
Mar 28, 2025
News from Texas indicates that a significant health insurer, Blue Cross Blue Shield of Texas, and a large hospital network, Southwestern Health Resources, were in contract negotiations with a deadline of April 1st. Failure to reach an agreement could result in 25 North Texas hospitals going out-of-network for some Blue Cross Blue Shield of Texas policyholders. This situation illustrates the potential for disputes between healthcare providers and large payers in Texas that can impact patient access to care, reflecting the broader tensions in payer-provider relationships mentioned in other reports, although this specific negotiation does not directly involve PAM Health.
Feb 19, 2025
PAM Health announced plans to build a 42-bed physical medicine and rehabilitation hospital in Carmel, Indiana. This new facility will be PAM Health's third in Indiana, supplementing the services provided at their hospitals in New Albany and Clarksville. The hospital is expected to create approximately 80 new jobs for medical professionals and support staff. This expansion is part of PAM Health's ongoing commitment to expanding access to high-quality rehabilitation services in the Indianapolis area, providing comprehensive inpatient rehabilitation, including physical, occupational, and speech therapy, for patients recovering from various debilitating conditions such as stroke, brain and spinal cord injuries, amputations, and orthopedic issues. Executive Leadership

Anthony F. Misitano

Founder, Chairman & Chief Executive Officer

Anthony F. Misitano is the Founder, Chairman, and Chief Executive Officer at PAM Health, a healthcare provider specializing in rehabilitation. With over 30 years of executive experience in the healthcare sector, he is responsible for PAM Health's strategic direction, development, and overall operations across its long-term acute care, rehabilitation, and behavioral health hospitals. Before establishing PAM Health in 2006, Misitano founded and served as Chairman, President, and CEO of Trans Healthcare, Inc., which operated a large network of specialty facilities. He also held the position of President and CEO at Continental Medical Systems, Inc., and was a Senior Vice President at Horizon Health Care. Misitano is an alumnus of Penn State University, holding both a bachelor's and a master's degree with a focus on healthcare administration and public administration. He has been recognized as a top CEO in the U.S. hospital sector and is involved in community service and various boards.

Misitano began her career at PAM Health in 2019 as Associate General Counsel and has since held various positions, including Division President.
