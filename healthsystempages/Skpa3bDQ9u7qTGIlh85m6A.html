Recent News

Jun 11, 2025
Maimonides Health celebrated a significant milestone, marking 20 years of cancer care excellence as Brooklyn's first comprehensive clinic. The event, titled "Survivors Take Center Stage," honored cancer survivors and highlighted the health system's long-standing commitment to providing comprehensive cancer care to the Brooklyn community. This celebration underscored two decades of dedicated service, advancements in treatment, and support for patients battling cancer in the borough.
Jun 10, 2025
Maimonides Health officials held their annual volunteer luncheon on June 5, honoring the contributions of 1,600 volunteers who collectively provided 200,000 hours of service. Maimonides' volunteer department is recognized as one of the largest in New York. Nearly 300 current volunteers were celebrated at the event held in The New Ballroom in Borough Park. Key speakers acknowledged the vital role of volunteers in patient care and advocacy. A special recognition was given to Erminia Rivera, Vice Chair of the Maimonides Board of Trustees and a notable community leader, with the naming of the Erminia Rivera Breast Imaging Center of Excellence. Longtime volunteer Mordechai Meisles was also honored for his service, particularly during the COVID-19 pandemic. The luncheon brought together leadership from 150 community, school, and city organizations that partner with the Maimonides Volunteer Department. [18]
Jun 07, 2025
Maimonides Health is connected to an event called "Airway: Doctors' Tales From Inside the Hospital" which took place on June 7, 2025, at the Brooklyn Public Library. This open-mic storytelling event for doctors was co-founded in 2015 by Maimonides emergency physicians Mert Erogul, MD, and Joshua Schiller, MD. The event features doctors sharing their experiences as physicians and has become a staple at academic assemblies and hospitals nationwide over the past decade. Dr. Mert Erogul, an emergency doctor at Maimonides Medical Center and co-founder of Airway Stories, was a participant. The event also included a performance by guitarist and composer Roberto Granados. Airway aims to highlight the power of stories in providing perspective and context to medical work, with Dr. Erogul encouraging residents and others to read widely outside the profession. Maimonides Health is described as Brooklyn's largest healthcare system, serving over 320,000 patients annually through its hospitals, physicians, and community-based practices. [2]
Jun 05, 2025
MyLaurel, a Midtown-based startup providing at-home health care, announced on June 5, 2025, that it has raised $12 million in funding. This funding round was led by Deerfield Management and GV (formerly Google Ventures). The announcement highlighted MyLaurel's partnership with Borough Park-based Maimonides Health. This collaboration allows MyLaurel to provide home-based acute care services to certain Maimonides patients, specifically those over 65 and individuals with conditions like chronic obstructive pulmonary disease and pneumonia. The program utilizes remote telemedicine for physician consultations and check-ups, and MyLaurel sends clinicians and nurse practitioners to patients' homes. The service provides phone, video, and in-person consultations for up to a month after a patient's intake. Maimonides Health reportedly plans to expand this offering to more patients in the future. The partnership aims to improve patient health, reduce unnecessary emergency room visits, and help hospitals free up beds. MyLaurel has been partnering with Maimonides Health for some time and sees the ability to help manage hospital capacity as a key reason for its own growth. [4, 6]
May 30, 2025
Maimonides Health has launched a new Smoking Cessation Program aimed at helping patients overcome tobacco and nicotine dependence. The program was established with support from the TD Charitable Foundation. This initiative is part of Maimonides' efforts to enhance community health by providing resources and support for patients to quit smoking, addressing a significant public health issue. [12, 19]

Kenneth D. Gibbs

President and CEO

Has served as CEO of Maimonides Medical Center (MMC) since January 2016. In March 2022, he also took on the role of President of Maimonides Health, the Brooklyn-based health system that includes both MMC and Maimonides Midwood Community Hospital (MMCH) as well as a broad network of ambulatory services. [1] Joined the Board of Trustees at Maimonides Medical Center in 2013 and was Chair from May 2014 through December 2015. [1] Under his leadership, MMC has continued to build upon its legacy of clinical excellence and innovation, achieving designation as an Advanced Comprehensive Stroke Center and establishing Brooklyn's only Pediatric Trauma Center and Comprehensive Spine Center. [1] Has overseen the development of several significant capital and strategic initiatives, including the formation of a relationship with MMCH in 2018, leading up to an affiliation agreement in 2021, and the issuance of $140 million in bonds in 2020 for modernization. [1] Chairs the Board of Healthfirst and serves on the Board of the Greater New York Hospital Association and the Healthcare Association of New York State. [1] Before Maimonides, was President of the Municipal Securities Group of Jefferies, LLC, CEO of DEPFA First Albany Securities, LLC, and held leadership positions at First Albany Capital, Inc. [1]

David I. Cohen

EVP, Population Health & Academic Affairs

David I. Cohen, M.D., M.Sc., is the Executive Vice President for Population Health & Academic Affairs at Maimonides Medical Center. He holds a B.A. and M.D. from the University of Pennsylvania and an M.Sc. in Epidemiology and Health from McGill University, where he was a Robert Wood Johnson Clinical Scholar. Dr. Cohen's career includes significant roles at other major healthcare institutions. He served as Director of Ambulatory Care and Associate Dean for Graduate Medical Education at Mount Sinai Medical Center in New York, Vice President for Medical Operations for the New York City Health and Hospitals Corporation, Deputy Dean for Clinical Affairs at the City University of New York Sophie Davis Medical School, and Medical Director at Bellevue Hospital Center before joining Maimonides. He is also an internist with over 50 years of experience. Dr. Cohen serves on several boards, including Housing Works, the Brooklyn Health Home, Healthix, and the New York eHealth Collaborative.

Laurie Weinstein

EVP, Legal Affairs & General Counsel

Laurie E. Weinstein is the Executive Vice President, Legal Affairs & General Counsel at Maimonides Health. She has an extensive background in the healthcare industry, having held various executive legal positions representing large multi-specialty medical groups and integrated health care delivery systems. Weinstein began her career in private practice after graduating from Suffolk University Law School. She then served as assistant general counsel at the Massachusetts Department of Mental Health and later as associate counsel for the University of Massachusetts. Her experience also includes roles as deputy general counsel at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center and as managing counsel and acting general counsel at The Permanente Medical Group. She joined Maimonides Medical Center in October 2020.

Robert Press

EVP, Medical Affairs

Robert Press is the Executive Vice President, Medical Affairs at Maimonides Health. He has over twenty years of experience in medical affairs. Prior to joining Maimonides, he served as the Chief Operating Officer at NYU Langone Medical Center from January 2015 to June 2018. He has held his current role at Maimonides Medical Center since May 2019. Dr. Press holds an MD and a PhD from New York University, and completed a fellowship in Infectious Diseases at Montefiore Medical Center, and residencies in Medicine at NYU Medical Center and Beth Israel Hospital. He has experience leading teams focused on quality and patient safety, utilizing data analysis to improve care, and fostering a compassionate and equitable care environment.
