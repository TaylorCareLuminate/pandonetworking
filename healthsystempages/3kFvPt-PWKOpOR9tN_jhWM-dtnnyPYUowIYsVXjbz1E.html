Recent News

Jun 11, 2025
Northern Nevada Health System participated in the third annual Interprofessional Care TTX 2025 at the University of Nevada, Reno. This high-impact simulation exercise, held on May 22, 2025, brought together over 150 students, faculty, and professionals from various health agencies, including NNHS, Renown Health, REMSA, Northern Nevada Public Health, and the Washoe County School District. The event utilized a simulated 6.9-magnitude earthquake scenario to challenge participants in coordinating responses to a mass casualty event. The training focused on operational coordination, lifesaving treatment during a crisis, and strengthening community bonds and emergency preparedness across the region. [26]
May 20, 2025
Northern Nevada Health System is among the supporters of The Discovery's newest featured exhibition, "Super Human: Power of Technology." This exhibition, running through October 12, 2025, explores how technology is transforming human capabilities through interactive displays, including testing reflexes, exploring bionic eyes, controlling robotic limbs with gestures, and experimenting with night vision and camouflage. The exhibition aims to inspire future engineers by showcasing innovations that help overcome physical limits. NNHS's support of this exhibition indicates an involvement in promoting technology within the community. [18, 29]
May 07, 2025
Sierra Medical Center, part of Northern Nevada Health System, has been named the Medical Health Services Company of the Year at the 18th Annual NCET Tech Awards. This award recognizes companies and individuals who have significantly contributed to the growth and prestige of Northern Nevada's technology community. The NCET Tech Awards celebrate innovation and technology use in the region. [3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 25]
Mar 26, 2025
Northern Nevada Health System (NNHS), including Northern Nevada Medical Center and Sierra Medical Center, announced a new partnership with greenUP!, a non-profit focused on environmental education and sustainable business practices. This collaboration introduces greenUP!'s Green Box To-Go (GBTG) program in the hospitals' cafeterias. The GBTG program aims to reduce single-use dining boxes by encouraging the use of reusable takeout containers. Staff and the public can check out these containers and return them to any of 18 participating local restaurants in Reno, Sparks, and Carson City. This initiative is part of NNHS's ongoing sustainability efforts to minimize food and dining waste.
Feb 05, 2025
Northern Nevada Health System announced the opening of Northwest Specialty Hospital, an extension of Northern Nevada Medical Center, located at 6225 Sharlands Avenue in northwest Reno. This new facility is designed to provide acute rehabilitation and adult behavioral health services, addressing a significant need in the community. The specialty hospital features 48 adult behavioral health beds and a 22-bed acute rehabilitation unit equipped with a full-scale therapy gym and a transitional apartment to help patients prepare for returning home. The opening of this facility allows NNHS to move and expand these services previously offered at Northern Nevada Medical Center, increasing access to care in the rapidly growing northwest Reno area. Executive Leadership

Chris Loftus

Interim Chief Executive Officer

Chris Loftus is the Interim Chief Executive Officer at Northern Nevada Sierra Medical Center, part of Northern Nevada Health System. He has extensive experience within The Valley Health System, having previously served as CEO for Desert Springs Hospital and COO at both Summerlin and Henderson hospitals. As COO, his responsibilities included day-to-day hospital operations and strategy implementation. Before focusing on healthcare administration, Loftus was a professional soccer player. He earned a master's degree in health service administration from the University of Evansville and a bachelor's degree in psychology and pre-med from Duke University. He currently serves as Chair of the American Heart Association Las Vegas chapter.
