Jun 12, 2025
An article discussing hospitals urging Congress to make attacks on healthcare workers a federal crime references Northeast Georgia Health System's use of technology to protect staff. According to a related article from January 7, 2025, Northeast Georgia Health System implemented a system with technology firm Kontakt.Io that provides employees with badges equipped with a small button. When pressed, this button sends a silent alarm to alert security and other staff members that an employee requires assistance due to aggressive behavior from a patient or visitor. Chris Paravate, Chief Information Officer at Northeast Georgia Health System, was noted in the January article discussing the system's focus on technology and training to improve safety and that the badges were tested in the trauma center at NGMC Gainesville before expanding to other hospitals within the system. Employee feedback on this technology has reportedly been very positive, with staff appreciating the organization's investment in their safety. [6]
Jun 11, 2025
Northeast Georgia Medical Center (NGMC) Gainesville's nuclear cardiology services unit has achieved re-accreditation by the Intersocietal Accreditation Commission (IAC). This marks a continuation of their accreditation which began 17 years ago. According to Vedang Bhavsar, MD, medical director of Echo/Vascular and Nuclear Cardiology Imaging for NGHS, IAC accreditation is considered the 'gold standard' and confirms that their cardiac imaging departments meet stringent standards and extensive guidelines for providing high-quality patient care. The re-accreditation acknowledges the efforts of the technologists, staff, and physicians. The nuclear cardiology services at NGMC Gainesville, now located in the new Green Tower, include nuclear stress tests, SPECT scans for 3D heart imaging to diagnose cardiovascular conditions, and cardiac PET scans to identify blocked blood flow. These imaging techniques assist cardiologists at Georgia Heart Institute in making accurate diagnoses and developing treatment plans. [3]

Jun 03, 2025
This week, Northeast Georgia Health System (NGHS) and Lifepoint Rehabilitation celebrated the opening of Northeast Georgia Rehabilitation Institute, a new free-standing, 40-bed inpatient rehabilitation hospital located on Limestone Parkway in Gainesville. This 61,000-square-foot facility significantly increases the patient capacity compared to the previous 24-bed unit at Northeast Georgia Medical Center (NGMC) Gainesville. The institute offers a wide range of services delivered by a multidisciplinary team, including physical medicine oversight, occupational, physical, and speech therapies, as well as internal medicine and medical/surgical consultations. The facility is equipped with advanced rehabilitation technology, such as a Smart Car, a Senaptec Sensory Station, and a virtual reality platform for neuro rehabilitation, to support customized therapy plans. It also features large therapy gyms, a micro-apartment for practicing daily living activities, a secured brain-injury unit, and an on-site pharmacy. This opening is a result of a joint venture partnership between NGHS and Lifepoint Rehabilitation, with Lifepoint Rehabilitation managing the day-to-day operations. All inpatient rehabilitation services previously offered at NGMC Gainesville are transitioning to this new institute. The partnership aims to expand access to crucial inpatient rehabilitation services for patients in the region, enhancing the patient experience and broadening the types of therapies available.
