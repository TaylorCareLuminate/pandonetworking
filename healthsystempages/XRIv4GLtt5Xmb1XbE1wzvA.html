Recent News

Jun 10, 2025
According to an update from the Washington State Nurses Association (WSNA), contract negotiations with Astria Toppenish Hospital are ongoing. The negotiation team held their seventh bargaining session on June 5, 2025. Key issues being discussed include wages and break relief for nurses. The union has stated that management's wage offers are not considered adequate to address the high turnover rate and recruitment and retention challenges at the hospital, especially when compared to the Eastern Washington market and facilities like Prosser Memorial Hospital. The WSNA is proposing competitive wages and increased differentials and premiums. Discussions also heavily involve ensuring nurses receive adequate meal and rest breaks without compromising patient care or increasing the burden on other staff. Additionally, the union has proposed the establishment of a Safety and Workplace Violence Committee to monitor and address related issues. The negotiation team emphasized that more work is needed to reach an agreement.
Apr 14, 2025
Astria Sunnyside Hospital, part of Astria Health, received a five-star rating for Spinal Fusion procedures in both 2024 and 2025 from Healthgrades. This recognition is based on Healthgrades' evaluation of risk-adjusted mortality and complication rates for common conditions and procedures across approximately 4,500 hospitals nationwide. According to Astria Health's President and CEO, Brian Gibbons, this five-star rating reflects the exceptional skill, commitment to excellence, patient safety, and positive outcomes achieved by the hospital's neurosurgeons. The award signifies that Astria Sunnyside Hospital's performance in Spinal Fusion is statistically significantly better than expected, placing it among the top hospitals in the nation for this procedure and demonstrating Astria Health's commitment to providing high-quality care. Mar 13, 2025
Astria Health expanded access to life-saving Naloxone at its Sunnyside and Toppenish Hospitals, as well as at five of its health centers. This initiative is aimed at combating the opioid crisis and increasing the availability of the overdose reversal medication within the communities served by Astria Health. Expanding access to Naloxone is a key public health strategy to reduce opioid overdose deaths and demonstrates Astria Health's commitment to community health and safety. This expansion makes the crucial medication more readily available to individuals who may be at risk of opioid overdose or who may be in a position to assist someone experiencing an overdose. [3]
