Recent News

Jun 10, 2025
Baptist Health and Wolfson Children's Hospital in Jacksonville announced on Monday, June 10, 2025, that they will be ending their decades-long clinical collaboration with UF Health. Wolfson Children's Hospital, which is part of Baptist Health and is the only children's hospital in North Florida, stated that clinical services previously provided by UF Health at Wolfson Children's Hospital will now be provided by Nemours Children's Health. Despite this change in clinical partners, Baptist Health/Wolfson Children's Hospital intends to continue serving as the primary pediatric teaching facility for the University of Florida College of Medicine. UF Health expressed surprise and disappointment regarding this unilateral decision by Baptist Health/Wolfson Children's Hospital. Both organizations have indicated that the transition is expected to take place in the first quarter of 2026. Baptist Health clarified that the employment of team members at Wolfson Children's Hospital is not affected by this change, as the impacted providers are employed by UF Health, not Wolfson Children's Hospital.
Jun 04, 2025
Healthcare IT Leaders, a healthcare consulting and IT services firm, announced an agreement with Baptist Health to provide comprehensive managed services for Baptist Health's Oracle Health clinical application. This multiyear agreement involves Healthcare IT Leaders offering 24/7 monitoring, support, and maintenance of the Oracle Health applications. This partnership aims to allow Baptist Health's internal IT team to concentrate on clinical alignment and strategic priorities while ensuring system reliability and continuous improvement. The services will be supported by Healthcare IT Leaders' Center of Excellence. Clyde Williams, CIO at Baptist Health, stated that this partnership ensures critical applications supporting patient care teams are monitored and maintained with high expertise and responsiveness. Ben Hilmes, CEO of Healthcare IT Leaders, added that this managed services model brings agility, transparency, and measurable value to Baptist Health's IT operations.
Jun 03, 2025
Baptist Physician Partners, LLC, which is part of Baptist Health, has earned URAC accreditation for its Clinically Integrated Network. This accreditation, announced on June 3, 2025, signifies that Baptist Physician Partners meets rigorous national standards for quality care, patient satisfaction, and health outcomes. URAC is an independent non-profit organization that promotes healthcare quality by accrediting organizations involved in medical care services. Achieving this accreditation demonstrates Baptist Health's commitment to improving the coordination and quality of care provided through its network of physicians.
May 28, 2025
Baptist Health, headquartered in Jacksonville, FL, announced the appointment of new board members and chairs for the health system's Board of Directors, as well as for the boards of Baptist Medical Center Clay and Baptist Physician Partners (BPP) and its ACO subsidiary. These appointments are aimed at leveraging local expertise and leadership to guide the health system. Michael A. Mayo, DHA, FACHE, president and CEO at Baptist Health, emphasized that the diverse perspectives and dedication of the board members are crucial for the long-term direction of the health system, enhancing access to exceptional care, and ensuring preparedness for the future health needs of the region. The newly appointed board members are set to serve three-year terms. Baptist Health operates as a locally governed, faith-based, not-for-profit health care system in Northeast Florida, with its boards comprising local leaders and Baptist Health professionals, including physicians.
