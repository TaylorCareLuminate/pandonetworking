Recent News

Jun 15, 2025
Nurses and technical staff at UPHS-Marquette, represented by the Michigan Nurses Association (MNA), are engaged in contract negotiations with Lifepoint Health and held a community rally on June 16th. According to a news report, the unions have grievances regarding Lifepoint's alleged actions that have negatively impacted them, including attempting to implement an inferior health insurance plan for RNs in the fall and imposing changes that reduced care options. The report also states that Lifepoint changed the insurance for Marquette Ancillary Staff and Technologists (MAST) members to a plan with less coverage and higher costs without proper notification. Negotiations for the RNs' contract, which was extended to July 1st, resumed on June 17th. MAST bargaining began on March 19th and is set to resume on June 23rd. Union representatives stated that Lifepoint executives should prioritize the needs of healthcare professionals and patients in the Upper Peninsula over shareholder profits, expressing concern that proposals for safer staffing have not been addressed.
Jun 05, 2025
Frye Regional Medical Center, which is a Duke LifePoint hospital, is now offering low-dose computed tomography (LDCT) screening for the detection of lung cancer. This screening is available to patients who meet specific eligibility criteria.
Jun 03, 2025
This week, Northeast Georgia Health System (NGHS) and Lifepoint Rehabilitation, a business unit of Lifepoint Health, celebrated the opening of the Northeast Georgia Rehabilitation Institute. This new free-standing, 40-bed inpatient rehabilitation hospital is located in Gainesville on Limestone Parkway. The facility is the result of a joint venture partnership between NGHS and Lifepoint Rehabilitation, with Lifepoint Rehabilitation managing the day-to-day operations. The 61,000-square-foot hospital significantly increases patient capacity compared to the existing 24-bed unit at Northeast Georgia Medical Center (NGMC) Gainesville, and all services previously provided at NGMC are transitioning to the new institute. The diverse clinical team at the institute will offer physical medicine oversight, occupational, physical, and speech therapies, internal medicine, and medical/surgical consultations, supported by advanced rehabilitation technology. This opening is seen as a significant step forward for specialty care in the region, allowing for the care of patients with more complex medical needs in a space designed for recovery.
