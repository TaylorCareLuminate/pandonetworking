Recent News

Jun 17, 2025
Starting in January 2025, LifeBridge Health providers will no longer be accepting certain Medicare Advantage plans from Aetna and Humana. According to an announcement on the Maryland Medicare Options website, patients with current Aetna HMO Advantage Plans (SmartFit Plan) or Humana PPO or HMO Advantage Plans (Gold Plus / Choice Plans) will need to change their Medicare Advantage plan to continue accessing LifeBridge Health providers in 2025. The deadline for those with Humana plans to make a change for coverage to start on January 1, 2025, is December 7, 2024. For those with Aetna HMO plans, the deadline is December 31, 2024. LifeBridge Health has partnered with independent licensed Medicare brokers in Maryland to assist affected patients in understanding their 2025 plan options and enrolling in a new plan.
Jun 16, 2025
David Krajewski, Executive Vice President and CFO of LifeBridge Health, discussed the health system's use of payer scorecards to evaluate insurers. LifeBridge Health utilizes these scorecards to track performance metrics such as denial rates, response times, and administrative burdens, comparing payers against their competitors. Krajewski stated that LifeBridge Health scorecards their Medicare and Medicare Advantage plans and is willing to cancel contracts with those that exhibit poor behavior. He also noted the importance of health systems ensuring they provide cost-effective services, such as total knee replacements, to gain leverage in negotiations with payers. This systematic tracking and willingness to terminate contracts highlight LifeBridge Health's proactive approach to managing payer relationships and addressing potential disputes over performance and costs.

May 02, 2025
LifeBridge Health celebrated the completion of a $61.6 million transformation of Grace Medical Center in West Baltimore. This five-year modernization project of the former Bon Secours Hospital is a significant expansion and community investment. The final phase includes the opening of state-of-the-art adult and pediatric behavioral health clinics and a new facility entrance with expanded parking and green space, all designed to better serve the West Baltimore community. A ribbon-cutting ceremony was held on April 29, followed by a community open house. Since acquiring the facility in 2019, LifeBridge Health has also modernized the emergency department, upgraded the dialysis unit, and expanded outpatient clinic areas based on community feedback. This project represents the majority of LifeBridge Health's $85 million investment in the West Baltimore community related to the acquisition. The medical facility remained operational throughout the renovations. [17, 6, 13]
