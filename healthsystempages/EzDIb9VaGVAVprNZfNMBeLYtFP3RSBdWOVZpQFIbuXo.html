Recent News

May 30, 2025
Baptist Health System in San Antonio recently celebrated the graduation of the first cohort from its Project SEARCH program. This initiative is designed to provide young adults with autism and related developmental disorders with valuable job and life skills through a nine-month internship at Baptist Medical Center Downtown. Seven students received certificates after gaining experience in various hospital departments, including sterilizing medical supplies, customer service, radiology, pharmacy, food and nutrition, and human resources. The program is a collaboration with Edgewood Independent School District and Texas A&M University-San Antonio. Project SEARCH aims to help these individuals build skills to pursue future employment opportunities, and Baptist Health System is already planning to hire one of the interns. Officials highlighted that the program not only imparts job skills but also significantly boosts participants' confidence and self-esteem, demonstrating the hospital system's commitment to community inclusivity and workforce development for individuals with disabilities.
Apr 18, 2025
OBHG (Obstetric Hospitalist Group) has announced a new partnership with the recently opened Westover Hills Baptist Hospital in San Antonio, TX. This collaboration aims to provide full obstetric emergency department (OBED) coverage at the new hospital, with a target implementation by December 2025. The partnership will support community physicians, standardize care, and help the hospital be competitive in the local market. Westover Hills Baptist Hospital, located in a growing area of San Antonio, anticipates handling around 350 deliveries in its first year. This is OBHG's 14th partnership with Tenet Healthcare, the parent company of Baptist Health System, their 5th with the Baptist Health System specifically, and their 47th program in Texas.
Apr 07, 2025
Envision Healthcare and its affiliates are implementing layoffs impacting 120 employees who have been providing services at Baptist Health System in San Antonio. These layoffs are scheduled to become effective on May 31, 2025, and affect staff offering hospital medicine and clinical support services through Envision Physician Services, Questcare Hospitalists, and Questcare Matrix. The reason cited for these job cuts is the conclusion of the medical staffing group's contract with Baptist Health System. According to a WARN notice filed with the Texas Workforce Commission and reported by local news outlets, Baptist Health System is transitioning to a single, albeit unidentified, physician provider network and has indicated an intention to retain as many of the affected employees as possible to continue working at their current facilities following the transition.
Apr 07, 2025
Envision Healthcare and its affiliates are laying off approximately 120 employees who provide hospital medicine and clinical support services at Baptist Health System facilities in San Antonio. The layoffs are effective May 31, 2025, and stem from the conclusion of Envision's contract with Baptist Health System. According to Envision's Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification (WARN) notice filed with the Texas Workforce Commission, Baptist is transitioning to a single physician-provider network. Envision stated that while they valued the partnership, they are transitioning services due to Baptist's decision. The affected employees include physicians, nurse practitioners, practice coordinators, and clinical operations specialists. Baptist Health System and the incoming provider group have expressed their intention to retain as many of these affected employees as possible at their current facilities. [6, 9, 11, 16, 26]
Mar 26, 2025
St. Luke's Baptist Hospital in San Antonio is undergoing an $18 million renovation and expansion of its Emergency Department (ED). The project, which began in the spring of 2025, will modernize 5,500 square feet of space to help reduce patient wait times, enhance stroke patient treatment, and meet the increasing demand for emergency care in the Medical Center area. The renovations at the busy ED, which sees over 1,600 patients monthly, will include adding two more EMS bays, three additional ED beds, eight more Medical-Surgical patient beds, and new "Right Care, Right Place" rooms. A more spacious and comfortable waiting area is also part of the plan. The enhancements are expected to be completed in May 2026 and represent Baptist Health System's commitment to the Medical Center community. [8, 15]

Charles Handley

Group Chief Financial Officer

Charles Handley is the Group Chief Financial Officer at Baptist Health System in San Antonio, Texas. Before this role, he was the Market CFO for the El Paso and Rio Grande Market of Tenet Healthcare in Dallas. His responsibilities at Tenet included serving as CFO for The Hospitals of Providence Memorial Campus, Sierra Campus, and Providence Children's Hospital in El Paso, Texas. He joined Providence in 2007 and, as of 2018, had been with Tenet for over 21 years. Earlier in his career, he held the position of CFO at Park Plaza Hospital/Plaza Specialty Hospital in Houston.

Stephanie Talley

Group Chief Human Resources Officer

Stephanie Talley serves as the Group Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO) at Baptist Health System in Texas, with responsibility for HR leadership at hospitals in San Antonio, New Braunfels, the Rio Grande Valley, and Nacogdoches. She also holds the position of Chief HR Officer at Northeast Baptist Hospital. Talley's tenure with Tenet Healthcare, the parent company of Baptist Health System, began in 1993, during which she has taken on various leadership roles. Her educational background includes a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from Pfeiffer University and a Master of Business Administration degree from the University of Phoenix. Becker's Hospital Review recognized her expertise by including her in their list of "70 African American Leaders in Healthcare to Know" in both 2019 and 2020.

Group Chief Compliance Officer

Matt Stone

Baptist Health System Group CEO

Matthew Stone is the Group CEO of Baptist Health System and Texas Tenet Group, with a career at Baptist Health System spanning nearly 15 years. His background is in accounting and finance, and he previously held positions as Chief Financial Officer and later President at Northeast Baptist Hospital.
