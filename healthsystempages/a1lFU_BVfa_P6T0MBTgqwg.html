Jun 13, 2025
DHR Health hosted a special graduation ceremony on June 9th to honor three students whose families have been impacted by severe health issues requiring advanced medical care at the hospital. The ceremony celebrated Robert Adame, Kaylynn Adame, and Jose Manuel Acosta Jr. in the Discharge Lounge at DHR Health's Main Hospital. Robert and Kaylynn's mother, Bobbie Adame, has been hospitalized and on Extracorporeal Membrane Oxygenation (ECMO), an advanced form of life support, for the past month, preventing her from attending her children's official graduations. This special event allowed her to witness their significant milestone. Jose Manuel Acosta Jr., a former ECMO patient who is still recovering, also missed his high school graduation and was able to receive his diploma and recognition at this event. Dr. Andrew Phillips, DHR Health intensivist and medical director of the ECMO program, emceed the ceremony, emphasizing the importance of not missing such cherished life moments. A special congressional recognition was presented by Victor Garza from Congressman Vicente Gonzalez's office, and Bobbie Adame delivered a heartfelt speech expressing gratitude for the care and support from the DHR Health team. This event highlights DHR Health's commitment to patient-centered care and supporting families beyond medical treatment. The DHR Health ECMO program, launched in February 2023, is the only one in the Rio Grande Valley, providing critical life support for patients with severe heart or lung failure and contributing to DHR Health's Level I Trauma Center designation.

DHR Health has been awarded a $2.8 million grant from CPRIT (Cancer Prevention and Research Institute of Texas) to launch a Drug Delivery and Nanomedicine Core. Dr. Daniel Tamez

Executive Vice President

Dr. Daniel Tamez, FACHE, is an Executive Vice President at DHR Health with over 15 years of combined direct clinical services and healthcare leadership experience. He holds a Doctor of Science (Sc.D) degree in Health/Health Care Administration/Management from A.T. Still University of Health Sciences. Additionally, he completed a Postgraduate Degree PGY 1 in the Hospice and Palliative Medicine Program at Harvard Medical School and earned a Master of Public Health degree in Health/Health Care Administration/Management from Texas A&M University. His educational background also includes a Master of Science in Medicine and a Bachelor of Science in Biology and Chemistry with a minor in Spanish from The University of Texas-Pan American. Dr. Tamez's work experience includes roles as a Biosecurity and Environmental Health Research Specialist at Texas A&M University, a Physician Associate at Tavarez Medical Center, and an Internal Medicine/Geriatrics Physician Associate-Certified at WellMed Medical Management. He also worked as a Physician Associate at Norma Iglesias and Associates Day and Night Clinic and gained experience in Palliative/Hospice Medicine. Prior to his current role, he served as a Wound Care Consultant at Wound Management LLC and as Vice President of Clinical Medicine at DHR Health. He is also an Assistant Clinical Professor of Medicine and Adjunct Professor of Physician Assistant Studies at The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley. Dr. Tamez is a Fellow of the American College of Healthcare Executives, a Board Certified Wound Specialist, and Board Certified in Palliative/Hospice Palliative Medicine. In 2020, he was appointed to the Texas Health and Human Service Executive Counsel in Healthcare Policy.

Lisa R. Trevino

Vice President Of Research And Development

Lisa R. Trevino, PhD, is the Vice President for Research and Development at DHR Health. She earned her doctorate in Molecular, Cellular and Developmental Biology from the University of California at Santa Cruz, and holds a Master of Science in Molecular Biotechnology from the University of Texas San Antonio. Dr. Trevino's scientific training and career include experience at St. Jude Children's Research Hospital and the Human Genome Sequencing Center at Baylor College of Medicine. Her research has focused on the genetics and genomics of both adult and pediatric malignancies. Dr. Trevino joined DHR Health in 2014 and has been instrumental in establishing and expanding the research portfolio, which now includes over 215 ongoing projects across various medical fields. Her areas of expertise include cancer genetics, genomics, and pharmacogenomics. She is also passionate about community engagement in health literacy and increasing access to advanced clinical care. Prior to her role at DHR Health, she was a Project Manager/Staff Scientist at the Human Genome Sequencing Center at Baylor College of Medicine from 2010 to 2014 and a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at St Jude Children's Research Hospital from 2006 to 2009. Dr. Trevino is also a professional member of SACNAS (Society for Advancement of Chicanos/Hispanics and Native Americans in Science) and has been involved since the early 1990s. She is bilingual, fluent in both English and Spanish.

Maritza R. Padilla

VP Rehabilitation Services

Maritza R. Padilla is the Vice President of Rehabilitation Services at DHR Health. Her work experience at DHR Health (formerly Doctors Hospital at Renaissance Health System) began in 2009 as an RN. Veronica Villarreal

Chief Ambulatory Officer
