Jun 05, 2025
BayCare Health System and Northwestern Medicine have announced a strategic collaboration aimed at enhancing healthcare in West Central Florida. [20] This partnership will focus on expanding access to advanced medical treatments, boosting clinical research efforts, and improving training and educational opportunities for current and future physicians in the region. [20] As part of this collaboration, BayCare is designating a 36-acre portion of Tampa Bay Park as the BayCare Academic Health and Research Corridor. [20] This corridor will serve as a central location to consolidate and expand BayCare's academic and research initiatives, including those undertaken with Northwestern Medicine. [20] The acquisition of this property significantly increased BayCare's land holdings in west Tampa. [20] The collaboration is intended to accelerate BayCare's growth as an academic health system by leveraging the expertise of Northwestern Medicine, which includes the Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine. [20] BayCare emphasized that this strategic alliance will not alter its existing ownership, leadership structure, or the composition of its volunteer board of trustees. [20] The initial phase of the collaboration will concentrate on developing educational and professional development programs for clinicians and advancing clinical research projects. [20]
May 30, 2025
BayCare Health System has agreed to pay $800,000 to the Department of Health and Human Services' Office for Civil Rights (OCR) to settle potential HIPAA violations. The settlement stems from a 2018 complaint where a former nonclinical staff member of a physician's practice, with access to BayCare's electronic medical records for shared patients, allegedly accessed a patient's ePHI without permission. The individual reportedly had photographs of printed medical records and a video of someone scrolling through the patient's electronic chart. The potential violations investigated by the OCR included failing to implement proper policies and procedures for authorizing access to ePHI, not adequately reducing risks and vulnerabilities to ePHI, and failing to regularly review information system activity logs. In addition to the financial settlement, BayCare will undertake a two-year corrective action plan monitored by the OCR. This plan requires BayCare to conduct a thorough risk analysis, develop a risk management plan, revise HIPAA policies and procedures, and train staff with ePHI access on these policies. [16, 21]
May 12, 2025
BayCare Health System is expanding its nurse well-being program across all 16 of its acute-care hospitals in 2025. This program, which aims to address nurse stress and improve workplace culture, will be implemented in over 160 inpatient units. The expansion follows a successful three-year pilot program conducted at select units at St. Joseph's, St. Anthony's, and Winter Haven hospitals. The pilot demonstrated significant improvements in reducing nurse burnout (34% reduction), increasing positive perceptions of leadership (82% increase), improving perceived staffing levels for quality care (34% improvement), and increasing meaningful conversations around patient care (26% increase). The systemwide rollout began in April with children's and women's units and continues in May, coinciding with Mental Health Awareness Month. The program, titled 'Nurse Well-Being: Building Peer and Leadership Support,' utilizes the Stress First Aid (SFA) program to promote peer and leadership support. BayCare also recently received the Well-Being Excellence™ designation from the American Nurses Creding Center (ANCC) on May 1st, the first in Florida to earn this recognition, further underscoring their commitment to team member well-being. May 06, 2025
BayCare Health System achieved a significant financial milestone in 2024, boosting its operating margin to 9.6%, a notable increase from 6% in 2023. According to BayCare's Executive Vice President and CFO Janice Polo, this strong performance was driven by a combination of strategic cost management initiatives and collaborative leadership across the health system. Additionally, the margin improvement was credited to an increase in regulatory funds from the state, which assisted in covering Medicaid losses, and a higher percentage of charity costs being covered. BayCare also saw an increase in its Medicare wage index. The system's operating margin initiatives, first implemented in 2023, involve bringing leadership together to identify opportunities in revenue and cost management, with a focus in 2024 on reducing premium labor, resulting in substantial savings that were partially reinvested in the team. Sowmya Viswanathan, MD

Chief Physician Executive

Sowmya Viswanathan, MD, MBA, MHCM, FACP, is the Chief Physician Executive at BayCare, a position she assumed on January 1, 2023. She initially joined BayCare in June 2022 as the Vice President and Chief Medical Officer for St. Joseph's, St. Joseph's Children's, and St. Joseph's Women's hospitals in Tampa. Dr. Viswanathan is a board-certified internal medicine physician with over two decades of experience in quality care, patient safety, and patient satisfaction. Prior to her time at BayCare, she served as the Group Chief Medical Officer for Tenet Health in Massachusetts, Tennessee, and South Carolina. Her extensive background also includes leadership roles at Dartmouth Health's Accountable Care Organization, Harvard Pilgrim Health Care & Network Health, and UMass Memorial Medical Center. She holds a medical degree from Madurai Medical College in India, completed her residency at the Boston University Residency Program, and earned a Master's in Healthcare Management (MHCM) from the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, where she has also been an instructor and program director. Additionally, she holds a Master's in Business Administration (MBA) from the University of Massachusetts.

Patricia Shucoski

Chief Nurse Executive

Patricia “Trish” Shucoski, DNP, MSN, RN, NEA-BC, is the Chief Nurse Executive for BayCare Health System, where she oversees nursing practice, programs, and education across its 16 hospitals and manages over 11,000 nurses. She assumed this role in December, succeeding Lisa Johnson upon her retirement. Prior to becoming Chief Nurse Executive, Shucoski served as BayCare's vice president of customer access and operational excellence. Before joining BayCare in June 2023, Shucoski was the senior vice president of performance excellence and system integration at Jefferson Health in Philadelphia since 2019. Her experience also includes roles at Cooper University Hospital, where she was the vice president of patient care services for four years and executive director of hospital-based services for three years. As a registered nurse in New Jersey and Pennsylvania, she worked at Cancer Treatment Centers of America, where she held the position of chief nurse, and at Hahnemann University Hospital/Tenet Healthcare. Shucoski holds a Doctor of Nursing Practice in healthcare systems leadership from Chamberlain University, a Master of Science in nursing, leadership/nursing management from Drexel University, and a bachelor's degree in nursing from Villanova University. She also earned associate degrees from the State University of New York at Farmingdale in allied health and science/nursing and from Nassau Community College in science. Shucoski has nearly 20 years of experience as a healthcare leader and is a Florida licensed nurse.

Keri Eisenbeis

Chief of Staff and Chief Communications Officer

Keri Eisenbeis serves as the Chief of Staff and Chief Communications and Marketing Officer at BayCare Health System. She joined BayCare 16 years ago as the director of government relations, child advocacy, and ambulatory care for St. Joseph's Children's Hospital in Tampa. She has held roles of increasing responsibility, including Director of Government Relations (2011-2017), Director of Government Relations and Community Outreach (2017-2018), and Vice President of Government and Community Relations (2018-2022). She assumed the role of Chief of Staff upon Stephanie Conners' arrival as BayCare President and CEO in 2022. In May 2025, her role expanded to include oversight of marketing efforts, adding "Marketing" to her title. Eisenbeis's responsibilities include supporting the BayCare President and CEO, leading external affairs with a focus on government relations, strategic communications, community benefit, and broader community partnerships, and overseeing the health system's research administration. She is also the Vice Chair of Human Resources for Metropolitan Ministries. She holds a Bachelor of Arts in international relations from Emory University (1991-1995), where she was involved in student government and was a member of Phi Beta Kappa, and completed post-graduate studies at the University of St. Andrews in Scotland (1995-1996) as a Bobby Jones Scholar. She also earned a Master of Legal Studies from Washington University in St. Louis School of Law (2016-2018). Her hometown is Wamego, Kansas.

Jennifer Touse

Chief Legal Officer

Jennifer Touse is the Chief Legal Officer at BayCare Health System. She began her tenure at BayCare in 2011 as a contract coordinator, joining shortly after the establishment of the in-house Legal Services Department. Prior to becoming Chief Legal Officer, Touse held several roles within the department, including associate counsel, associate counsel of special projects and strategic initiatives, manager, and department director. She served as interim Chief Legal Officer starting in September 2021. Touse is an alumna of the University of Florida, where she completed a double major in psychology and business management, and earned her law degree from the University of Florida Levin College of Law in 2011. Originally from Chattanooga, Tennessee, she relocated to Florida for her college education. She was named to the Tampa Bay Business Journal's 2024 40 Under 40 list.

C. Todd Jones

Chief Ambulatory Services Officer

C. Todd Jones is the Chief Ambulatory Services Officer at BayCare, a role he assumed on February 6, 2023. In this position, he is responsible for overseeing various ambulatory services including laboratory, imaging, ambulatory surgery, telehealth, urgent care, and BayCare HomeCare. Before his current role, Jones served as BayCare's vice president of ambulatory experience and operations for five years. His accomplishments in this role included expanding care sites, facilitating the expansion of COVID-19 testing, and negotiating a joint venture for an Ambulatory Surgery Center. Prior to his tenure at BayCare, Jones had a career spanning over two decades at Baptist Health System in central Kentucky, where he held several leadership positions, including president of Baptist Health in Richmond, Ky. He holds a bachelor's degree in health science and a master's degree in health administration from the University of Kentucky.
