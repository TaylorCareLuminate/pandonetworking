UFCW 3000 is holding a Kaiser Permanente Contract Act Team Meeting on Tuesday, June 17, 2025, at 6:00 pm at the Renton King County Library in Renton, Washington. Jun 13, 2025
Ambient AI technology deployed at The Permanente Medical Group, part of Kaiser Permanente, has saved nearly 16,000 hours in documentation time over a 15-month period (October 2023 to December 2024). Approximately 7,260 physicians utilized this technology, which transcribes medical visits and generates drafts of EHR notes, across about 2.5 million patient encounters. According to Vincent Liu, MD, chief data officer of the Permanente Medical Group, the technology successfully alleviates physician workloads and supports face-to-face interactions with patients. [4]
Jun 10, 2025
A document dated June 10, 2025, provides several updates related to union negotiations with Kaiser Permanente. It mentions that the new contract expiry date for UFCW and Kaiser employees has been set for October 1, 2025, aligning with the National Alliance Agreement. The document also refers to the ratification of mid-contract wage increases for all UFCW 3000 members on April 19, 2024, with details on retroactive pay processing in July 2024. Additionally, it briefly touches upon resumed contract talks between Kaiser Permanente and NUHW on January 29, stating that while a tentative agreement was reached on Educational Leave, several non-economic and economic issues remained unresolved as of that date. The document emphasizes the ongoing nature of bargaining with UFCW, addressing key employee concerns like minimum wage increases, ratification bonuses, staffing reforms, and higher earning potential.
Jun 06, 2025
Oakland, Calif.-based Kaiser Permanente laid off 38 employees across multiple locations in California. These affected positions were primarily business function-related, according to a statement from a Kaiser spokesperson in late April. While the layoffs have an earlier effective date, the reporting of this news occurred within the past week.
