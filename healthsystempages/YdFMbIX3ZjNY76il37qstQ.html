Jun 12, 2025
A lab at the University of Nevada, Reno School of Medicine (UNR Med), led by Professor Paul Brett, is significantly contributing to global efforts to combat melioidosis, a tropical infectious disease. The lab's work focuses on immune therapeutics, diagnostics, and vaccines for the disease, which causes substantial morbidity and mortality in tropical regions. In collaboration with researchers at Mahidol University in Bangkok, Thailand, and fellow UNR Med researcher Mary Burtnick, Brett's lab has co-developed a rapid diagnostic test for melioidosis that has been licensed by the Thai FDA and can provide results in as little as 15 minutes. Additionally, the lab is developing a vaccine that has shown excellent protection in animal models and is moving towards human clinical trials with support from a $29 million contract from the Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA). This pioneering work underscores the importance of cross-disciplinary and international collaborations in addressing global infectious diseases and highlights research efforts connected to the University of Nevada, Reno School of Medicine, which has an affiliation agreement with Renown Health. Jun 09, 2025
CGT Global, a company focused on cell and gene therapy access, has announced its support for the Conrad Breast Center at Renown South Meadows Medical Center in Reno, Nevada. This support includes a financial contribution that will result in the Center's Pharmacy Office being named "CGT Global." The contribution, made on behalf of CGT Global, its clients, and industry partners, aims to expand access to life-changing treatments and reflects a shared vision with Renown Health to bring next-generation care to Nevada. The Conrad Breast Center, which recently opened, offers a wide range of comprehensive services including imaging, genetic counseling, surgical and medical oncology, radiation oncology, infusion therapy, wellness medicine, and clinical research. Renown Health's Pennington Cancer Institute has been recognized among the top 100 cancer programs in the U.S. by Becker's Hospital Review, and this partnership continues the effort to deliver cutting-edge therapies to local communities and support personalized medicine in the fight against cancer. Jun 09, 2025
Seventeen first-year medical students from the University of Nevada, Reno School of Medicine (UNR Med) participated in a week-long immersive experience at the Betty Ford Center in Rancho Mirage, California. This program, supported by the E.L. Wiegand Foundation and in collaboration with the Hazelden Betty Ford Foundation, is a continuation of UNR Med's commitment to providing in-depth medical education, particularly concerning substance use and behavioral health. The experience aimed to provide students with firsthand insight into the lives of individuals in recovery and the complexities of treating addiction, shifting their perspectives to view addiction as a disease rather than a series of conscious choices. Students reflected on how the immersion redefined their understanding of patient care, emphasizing the importance of empathy, meeting patients in their vulnerable moments, and approaching care without judgment. This initiative is part of UNR Med's broader educational efforts, which are connected to Renown Health through an affiliation agreement. [6, 9]
Jun 06, 2025
Renown Health participated in the American Hospital Association's Hospitals Against Violence (HAV) initiative, specifically supporting #HAVhope Friday, an annual day dedicated to raising awareness of workplace violence in healthcare and addressing all forms of violence within their health system and the community. Renown Health emphasized its Zero Tolerance for Violence policy, stating that the health and safety of everyone on their property is a top priority. They highlighted their collaboration with local law enforcement agencies like the Washoe County Sheriff's Office and the Reno Police Department, with whom they maintain a close relationship and alert about violent incidents. Renown leadership stressed that their caregivers deserve to work in a safe, dignified, and respectful environment and called on the community to help keep healthcare settings safe spaces. Acts of violence, including physical assault, sexual assault, verbal or written threats, anger-related incidents, arson, sabotage, stalking, vandalism, and throwing objects, are subject to removal and criminal charges or prosecution.
Jun 06, 2025
Services have been suspended at the Renown Crisis Care Center. This decision was made after thoughtful consideration and a review of current circumstances, following the termination of an American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) grant that had been funding the center. The Renown Crisis Care Center had been providing short-term, 23-hour behavioral health crisis intervention services. The opening of this center was previously highlighted as a significant step in strengthening Renown's mental health infrastructure and expanding resources and access to behavioral health care in the community.

Executive Leadership

Brian Erling, MD, MBA

President & CEO

Dr. Brian Erling stepped into his role as president and CEO of Renown Health two years ago, infusing a people-first approach into the organization's mission, vision and values. He also implemented a new strategic plan to serve 1 million patients, led a restructuring initiative for more efficient operations, and set organizational benchmarks for top quartile performance in quality, finance, strategy, growth, and people. Dr. Erling also reinvested in the community via an affiliation with the University of Nevada, Reno School of Medicine to build Nevada's first nonprofit academic health system. Prior, Dr. Erling held leadership positions at Centura Health, including CMO, senior vice president and chief clinical officer. Most recently, he was president and CEO of Penrose-St. Francis Health Services. An emergency medicine physician by training, he also previously served as president of the Alliance Group and was a founding partner of an independent emergency medicine group. [6]

Anna Loomis, MBA

Chief Financial Officer

Ms. Loomis oversees all aspects of financial management for Renown Health including budgeting, financial planning, revenue cycle management, supply chain and strategic investments. With over 20 years in finance leadership and strategy, Ms. Loomis is leading several transformative efforts to strengthen the organization and build partnerships. [7]

Charles Podesta

Chief Information Officer

Charles (Chuck) Podesta is the Chief Information Officer at Renown Health. He is a seasoned executive with over 37 years of experience in information technology, with 26 of those years as a Chief Information Officer in healthcare organizations. Prior to joining Renown Health in July 2021, Podesta served as Interim Chief Information Officer for UCONN Health's John Dempsey Medical Center, School of Medicine and Clinical Research IT. His extensive background includes CIO roles at various healthcare systems across the country, such as UCI Health/UC Irvine School of Medicine and the University of California Health System, University of Vermont Health Network/Fletcher Allen Health, Steward Health/Caritas Christi Healthcare, Berkshire Health, Baystate Health, and UMASS Medical Center. Podesta specializes in developing and implementing strategic technology plans, optimizing electronic medical records systems, streamlining governance, and aligning IT with medical informatics needs. He is a graduate of the Massachusetts College of Liberal Arts and an active member of professional organizations including CHIME, HIMSS, and the Society for Information Management (SIM). He is also an avid marathon runner for various charities.

Justin Coran

Chief Analytics Officer

Justin Coran is the Chief Analytics Officer at Renown Health, having joined the organization in 2023. He is an executive, data scientist, and applied medical sociologist with over two decades of experience in healthcare reporting systems, advanced analytic product development, and healthcare quality. In his role at Renown Health, he is responsible for the strategy and vision for data engineering, data analytics, and data science, leading a team of nearly 60 professionals. His work includes developing healthcare models, managing data infrastructure, and overseeing AI/ML applications. Before his time at Renown Health, Coran served as the Chief Data Science and Analytics Officer at NOMS Healthcare and held various roles, including Manager and Sr. Data Scientist, at University Hospitals, where he helped establish the data science department. He earned a Doctoral degree in medical sociology and a Master of Public Health degree in hospital administration and epidemiology from the University of Florida. He also holds an adjunct professor position at the University of Nevada, Reno School of Medicine. Coran has been recognized nationally as a leader in data science.

Sandeep Randhawa, MS

Chief People Officer

Mr. Randhawa leads the Human Resources function and continues to develop the purpose-driven culture that supports the personal needs and well-being of Renown's more than 7,000 exceptional physicians and associates. He oversees talent management programs focused on hiring, training, and managing the performance of the workforce.
