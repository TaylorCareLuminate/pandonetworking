Jun 17, 2025
UAB Health System is currently in a contract dispute with United Healthcare, a situation highlighted in recent news. In articles discussing this dispute, Huntsville Hospital Health System is mentioned as another regional healthcare provider that has experienced similar contract conflicts with UnitedHealthcare. This indicates that while Huntsville Hospital Health System is not currently in a *new* dispute in the past week, their previous disagreement and subsequent resolution with UnitedHealthcare is still relevant in the context of ongoing payer-provider negotiations in the state. The UAB dispute could potentially impact over 500,000 Alabamians relying on United insurance, and UAB has cited concerns regarding United's claims denials and payment practices, issues that can be common in such disputes and were likely factors in Huntsville Hospital Health System's previous negotiations with the same carrier. Jun 03, 2025
Huntsville Hospital has received a failing 'F' grade for patient safety from the Leapfrog Group, a national non-profit organization. This grade is a significant drop from previous assessments. The hospital has challenged this finding, stating that the grade is based on incomplete information because the hospital chose not to complete the voluntary 353-page survey. According to the Leapfrog report, Huntsville Hospital scored in the highest tier ("Better Than Average") only once for not leaving objects in a patient's body after surgery. The hospital was rated "Average" in 12 categories and "Worse Than Average" in 16 categories. The hospital was not scored in three categories because it declined to participate in providing data for those areas. Thrive Alabama's CEO, Mary Elizabeth Marr, commented on the failing grade, noting that safety hazards can include less obvious issues like ensuring a clean environment and avoiding clutter in hallways. Despite this grade, Huntsville Hospital highlights its other recognitions, including being ranked by U.S. News as the second-best hospital in Alabama, being fully accredited by the Joint Commission, and being recognized by Forbes and Newsweek as a top workplace. [10, 12]
Jun 02, 2025
Huntsville Hospital and Madison Hospital, part of Huntsville Hospital Health System, are pushing back against the 'F' grades they received in the latest patient safety scores from The Leapfrog Group. The hospitals argue that the scores are not accurate because they did not report data to Leapfrog's biannual safety grade survey. According to the hospitals, Leapfrog chose to publish a rating with incomplete information, which they feel unfairly represents their safety and quality performance. They stated that while they would prefer staff focus on patient care, they will utilize significant resources in the future to complete Leapfrog's extensive survey to avoid such misleading scores. Leapfrog, however, maintains that a good grade is possible without their survey data and that the scores also incorporate measures from CMS, suggesting the hospitals may be performing poorly on some of those measures.
