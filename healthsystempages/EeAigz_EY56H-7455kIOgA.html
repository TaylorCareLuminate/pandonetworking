Recent News

Jun 16, 2025
Logan Health Rehabilitation in Polson has moved to a new location at 103 Suite D, Whitewater Place. The relocation, which occurred on June 1, 2025, provides patients with an expanded space that includes both private and open treatment areas, as well as a larger gym. This move aims to enhance the therapy services available in Lake County and allow for the potential growth of additional services, such as adding a full-time physical therapist and offering occupational therapy in the coming year. An open house is planned for late summer or early fall to introduce the public to the new facility and staff. This Polson location is one of eleven rehabilitation service sites operated by Logan Health in Northwest Montana.
Jun 16, 2025
Carrie Thompson, a nurse practitioner at Logan Health, is exploring the use of genetics to inform treatment options for patients. Her work has led to the establishment of a high-risk cancer screening and surveillance program at Logan Health, which currently monitors approximately 350 patients whose genetic profiles indicate a higher risk. Thompson emphasizes that genetic testing can benefit a wider population by identifying the underlying causes of conditions and tailoring treatment through precision medicine to prevent future health issues. The program recently received a five-year National Institutes of Health grant for its population screening process and launched a new app called PREMMplus, a simple screening tool available to hospital patients to help identify risks for certain cancers.
