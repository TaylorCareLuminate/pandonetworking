Recent News

Jun 17, 2025
SSM Health utilized an AI-based tool, iQueue for Infusion Centers, to effectively manage operational complexities and fluctuating patient volumes experienced at four of its cancer centers during the COVID-19 pandemic. This implementation led to notable improvements in efficiency and patient care. Specifically, SSM Health saw an 18% increase in patient volume without the need to add additional chairs, a significant reduction in wait times including a 44% decrease in infusion wait time and a 25% decrease in drug wait time, and a 30% increase in the utilization of infusion chairs during afternoon hours. These results were discussed in a webinar featuring presenters from SSM Health, detailing the health system's approach to leveraging artificial intelligence for operational agility.
Jun 12, 2025
U.S. Senate Democratic Whip Dick Durbin (D-IL) recently spoke with SSM Health CEO Laura Kaiser regarding the potential impacts of President Trump's and congressional Republicans' proposed "One Big Beautiful Bill Act" on Medicaid, hospitals, and patients. According to Senator Durbin, this Republican bill would significantly cut Medicaid coverage to fund tax breaks for billionaires. The bill is projected to result in 16 million Americans losing health insurance coverage, including hundreds of thousands across the states where SSM Health operates (Illinois, Missouri, Wisconsin, and Oklahoma). Durbin stated that President Trump and congressional Republicans are targeting healthcare to provide tax cuts for the wealthy, which he described as cruel and impactful to hospitals and patients alike, regardless of their state's political leaning. The proposed cuts include $800 billion from Medicaid and $300 billion from the Affordable Care Act (ACA). The bill would also halt provider taxes used by states like Illinois to secure federal matching funds for Medicaid, potentially harming patient access in both urban and rural hospitals. [5]

Jun 06, 2025
On June 6, which was the ninth annual Hospitals Against Violence (#HAVhope) Friday, SSM Health's Amy Wilson, chief nurse executive, and Todd Miller, vice president of security, discussed the health system's collaborative efforts to combat workplace violence. They highlighted how cooperation between clinical and security teams through simulations and de-escalation scenarios is transforming the safety culture at SSM Health. They noted a concerning trend of external violence increasingly impacting healthcare settings, which were once considered sacred spaces. Despite this challenge, their de-escalation training program has shown positive results, with 93% of care and security teams reporting feeling more capable of handling violent situations. The discussion emphasized the importance of not only de-escalation techniques but also strategies for staying safe when de-escalation is not effective. This initiative reflects the broader efforts within the healthcare industry to address the growing issue of workplace violence and protect healthcare workers, patients, and visitors. [6, 8]

Laura S. Kaiser

President & CEO

As President and Chief Executive Officer, Laura S. Kaiser leads SSM Health, a $10.5 billion Catholic, not-for-profit, integrated health system serving communities across the Midwest. [6] Headquartered in St. Louis, SSM Health includes hospitals, physician offices, outpatient and virtual care services, senior care facilities, comprehensive home care and hospice services, a pharmacy benefit company, a health insurance company and an accountable care organization. [6] She joined SSM Health in May 2017, bringing decades of experience in health care strategy, value-based care, improving clinical quality, fueling innovation, driving performance and growth, creating partnerships and leading change while fostering a healthy and inclusive culture. [6] Ms. Kaiser previously served as Executive Vice President and Chief Operating Officer for Intermountain Health in Salt Lake City, Utah, and held senior leadership roles at Ascension Health. [6] She earned a Bachelor of Science degree from the University of Missouri, and Master of Business Administration and Master of Healthcare Administration degrees from Saint Louis University. [6] Ms. Kaiser is a Fellow of the American College of Healthcare Executives and has been named one of the “100 Most Influential People in Healthcare” by Modern Healthcare magazine. [6]
