May 30, 2025
Blanchard Valley Health System is now offering radiofrequency ablation (RFA) as a new treatment option for spine tumors. This minimally invasive procedure uses targeted radiofrequency waves to remove or reduce the size of both benign and malignant spinal tumors. According to Dr. Ogden, a neurosurgeon with Neurosurgical Associates of Northwest Ohio, a division of BVHS, RFA is a valuable tool that provides patients with effective yet less invasive treatment alternatives. The procedure can be used on its own or in conjunction with other treatments like surgery, chemotherapy, or radiation therapy, and is often performed in an outpatient setting with a short recovery time. Kelly Shroll

Chief Operating Officer, Ambulatory Services

Kelly Shroll, the Chief Operating Officer of Ambulatory Services at Blanchard Valley Health System, has a career in healthcare spanning over 30 years, with 20 years at BVHS. She began her career as a team leader in nutrition services at the Toledo Hospital and joined BVHS in 1996 as a clinical nutrition manager. Shroll holds a bachelor of science degree in allied health professions from The Ohio State University and a Master of Business Administration with a concentration in healthcare management from the University of Findlay. Prior to her current role, she held various managerial positions at BVHS, including director of corporate quality & patient safety and director of support services. She has also been involved in community leadership, serving on the YMCA board and the Community Foundation board.

Eric Proctor

Chief People Officer

Eric Proctor is the Chief People Officer at Blanchard Valley Health System (BVHS), a role he began in January 2023. In this position, he is responsible for the talent strategy for over 3,300 associates, as well as overseeing corporate public relations, marketing, and the Blanchard Valley Health Foundation. Before joining BVHS, Proctor served as the Vice President of People & Culture Business Transformation at Novant Health from June 2020 to January 2023. At Novant Health, his responsibilities included organizational development and engagement, compensation and benefits, human resources information systems, and talent communications. He was involved in Novant Health being recognized as the 2021 Most Admired Employer Brand in healthcare and the people and culture department being listed on HRM America's 2022 Most Innovative HR Teams. Prior to his work in healthcare, Proctor was the Director of Strategic Communications and Recruitment at Wake Forest University from August 2012 to June 2020. He also held the position of Assistant Vice President of Compensation & Benefits Communication Administration at BB&T Corporation (now Truist) from December 2007 to August 2012. Eric Proctor holds a Master of Science Degree in Integrated Marketing Communications from the West Virginia University Reed College of Media and a Bachelor of Arts in English (Professional Writing) and Journalism from Gardner-Webb University. He is also a Prosci-certified change management practitioner and was recognized by HRO Today as a 2022 HR Superstar. He is a co-host of the BVHS podcast "Health Connect," where he discusses BVHS culture, career opportunities, and stories of the organization's people.
