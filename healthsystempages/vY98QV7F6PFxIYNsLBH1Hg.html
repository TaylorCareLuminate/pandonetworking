The Pennsylvania Department of Human Services (DHS) is scheduled to hold a listening session on June 16, 2025, as part of its "Food is Medicine Stakeholder Engagement Series." Jun 12, 2025
The Pennsylvania Department of Human Services (DHS) updated its website on June 12, 2025, by adding Medical Assistance Bulletin number 01-25-38. This bulletin pertains to Statewide Preferred Drug List (PDL) Updates for Pharmacy Services. These bulletins are important for healthcare providers and stakeholders as they outline changes and updates to the Medical Assistance program's policies and procedures, specifically regarding pharmaceutical services and the list of preferred drugs. The update provides guidance on which medications are preferred within the state's Medicaid program.
Jun 10, 2025
A federal budget bill currently under consideration could potentially cause over 300,000 Pennsylvanians to lose their Medicaid coverage and significantly impact rural hospitals, according to a report from June 10, 2025. The state Department of Human Services (DHS), which manages the Medicaid program in Pennsylvania covering over 3 million people, estimates the substantial loss of coverage under the proposed federal cuts of nearly $800 billion over the next decade. Val Arkoosh, the secretary of the DHS, voiced concerns that the bill would not only harm those losing coverage but also lead to overcrowded emergency departments, increased health insurance costs, and devastating effects on economies and health systems in rural areas. The bill also includes new work requirements for certain Medicaid recipients and more frequent eligibility verification.
Jun 10, 2025
On June 10, 2025, the Pennsylvania Department of Human Services (DHS) updated its website to add the Remittance Advice Dates for July 2025. This information is vital for healthcare providers who receive payments through the state's Medical Assistance program, allowing them to anticipate when they will receive their electronic remittance advice detailing claim payments and adjustments for the upcoming month. Keeping providers informed about payment schedules is a key function of the department's administrative operations.
Jun 09, 2025
The Pennsylvania Department of Human Services (DHS) released its proposed 2025-2026 state plan for the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP), which was published in the Pennsylvania Bulletin on Saturday, June 7, 2025, and reported on June 9, 2025. The proposed plan outlines changes to the program, including a projected decrease in the overall budget available for LIHEAP benefits from $168.8 million to $160.5 million. However, the cash component budget is expected to increase, as is the estimated number of households to be served. Notably, the minimum cash benefit is proposed to decrease from $300 to $200, while the maximum cash and crisis benefits remain at $1,000. The LIHEAP season start and end dates are expected to remain the same. Public comments and testimony from hearings will be used to finalize the plan.

Executive Leadership

Valerie A. Arkoosh, MD, MPH

Secretary of Human Services

Valerie A. Arkoosh, MD, MPH, was appointed to lead the Pennsylvania Department of Human Services by Governor Josh Shapiro on January 17, 2023. [6] Drawing on her background as a physician, she oversaw a multi-pronged, integrated response to the opioid overdose epidemic, including issuing a standing order in 2015 to enable participating pharmacies in Montgomery County to dispense the overdose antidote naloxone at the request of any member of the public. [6] As a physician and public health professional, Secretary Arkoosh was at the center of Montgomery County's efforts to combat the COVID-19 pandemic, leading a data- and science-driven approach to the unprecedented challenge. [6]

Andrew Barnes

Executive Deputy Secretary

Andrew Barnes is the Department of Human Services' Executive Deputy Secretary. [6] Previously, he served as the Executive Deputy Secretary in the Governor's Office of Policy and Planning and was responsible for the overall progress and development of relevant public safety, policing, veterans affairs and emergency management policy priorities. [6] He has served within the Commonwealth since 2004 in varying public service capacities, including Director of Victim Services within the Office of Victim Advocate and Legislative Liaison for the Pennsylvania Department of Corrections. [6]

Stephanie Shell

Deputy Secretary for the Office of Administration

The Office of Administration's (OA) mission is to create partnerships to deliver quality service to customers through collaboration, consultation, and supports. Services provided by OA include administrative appeals/hearings, administrative services, equal opportunity in the Department of Human Services' (DHS) programs, financial operations, fraud and abuse recoveries in medical assistance and third-party recoveries, procurement and contract management. OA ensures that DHS provides prompt access to its public records while abiding by exemptions and protections in the Right-To-Know Law to safeguard certain information, including confidential personal information of department clients and other third persons. OA also reviews out-of-state travel and has oversite for agency corporate cards.
