Recent News

Jun 12, 2025
Pediatric patients in the emergency room and inpatient unit at Franciscan Health Crown Point received a donation of 724 new stuffed animals, 292 pediatric blankets, and 59 baby blankets from The Eva Martin Project. This project honors the memory of three-year-old Eva Martin and aims to bring comfort to children in hospitals. The donated items each included a tag with the organization's logo and a QR code linking to a personal message, and the tags contained wildflower seeds intended for planting. Rick and Rebekah Martin founded the Michigan-based nonprofit after losing their daughter Eva in a swimming pool accident. They coordinate fundraisers twice a year to provide these comforting items. According to health care providers at Franciscan Health Crown Point, these donations help bring a sense of normalcy and peace to young patients and their families during potentially stressful medical situations.
Jun 06, 2025
Virginia Mason Franciscan Health is laying off 116 employees in their Virtual Health Services starting in July. This is part of a larger impact to around 200 employees as the health system realigns resources and improves operational efficiency. The changes include transitioning several virtual services and administrative functions due to significant financial pressures, including rising labor and supply costs, increasing claim denials, and underpayment from government programs. These challenges are reportedly intensified in Washington state due to new taxes and reduced reimbursement, particularly capping payments for care to state and school employees, costing the system an estimated $30 million extra annually. The impacted employees include cardiac monitor techs who remotely monitor patients' vital information. While this news relates to Virginia Mason Franciscan Health, it is part of the larger Franciscan system.
Jun 05, 2025
A lawsuit filed against Franciscan Alliance, the parent of Franciscan Health, by Indianapolis orthopedic surgeon Dr. Greg Hardin, showed recent activity on June 5, 2025. The lawsuit, filed in Indiana Commercial Court in June 2024, alleges breach of fiduciary duties, dereliction and waste, intentional interference, and breach of contract. Dr. Hardin claims Franciscan Alliance harmed his practice by directing patients away, prematurely terminating his office lease (effective December 31, 2023), and failing to meet financial obligations on two joint medical ventures where he and Franciscan Alliance were 50-50 partners: South Indy MRI & Rehab Services LLC and South Emerson Surgery Center LLC. The complaint states that Franciscan also suspended his hospital privileges and removed him and his colleague from the medical records system and online physician roster. According to the lawsuit, this conduct led to a nearly 80% decrease in patient volumes and revenues for the surgery center venture, making both joint ventures insolvent. The docket activity on June 5, 2025, indicates a telephone conference was held, and a Zoom Settlement Conference has been set for July 16, 2025. [3, 7, 12]
Jun 05, 2025
Case number 19-CA-363102, filed with the National Labor Relations Board (NLRB) against Virginia Mason Franciscan Health Rehabilitation Hospital, showed recent docket activity on June 5, 2025. This case, which was originally filed on April 2, 2025, is currently open. The allegations in the case include 8(a)(5) Repudiation/Modification of Contract (Sec 8(d)/Unilateral Changes), 8(a)(3) Changes in Terms and Conditions of Employment, 8(a)(1) Coercive Statements (Threats, Promises of Benefits, etc.), and 8(a)(1) Coercive Actions (Surveillance, etc.). The location associated with this case is Tacoma, WA, and it is assigned to NLRB Region 19 in Seattle, Washington. [5, 21]
May 27, 2025
Franciscan Health WorkingWell, a program providing comprehensive occupational health services to businesses, celebrated its 20th anniversary on May 27, 2025. The program was initially launched in June 2005 as a regional initiative and has since expanded to include 18 offices across Indiana and the southern Chicago suburbs. Franciscan WorkingWell focuses on providing an efficient continuum of care, improved case management, a unified team of healthcare professionals, and customized healthcare solutions for companies. The program offers a range of services including physicals, drug and alcohol screening, vaccinations, injury treatment and prevention programs, and fitness for duty evaluations. Tim Ross, director of employer solutions at Franciscan Health, highlighted the program's growth, adaptation, and evolution over the past two decades to meet the changing needs of businesses and industry regarding employee health and well-being. Many WorkingWell locations also offer Franciscan ExpressCare services for common illnesses and minor urgent care injuries. [11]
