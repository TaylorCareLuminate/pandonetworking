May 23, 2025
Nuvance Health is seeking a remote Cardiovascular Quality Data Coordinator for its Danbury Hospital Quality Data department. The position requires experience in various cardiovascular data registries such as NCDR Cath/PCI, LAAO, EP Device, STS/TVT Registries, STS ACS Registry, VQI Registries, or GWTG Stroke, Heart Failure or CAD Registries, with experience in New York State DOH PCIRS being a plus. The role involves supporting Quality Data Specialists in all registry functions, including data collection, entry, and submission, as well as reviewing and analyzing quality and outcomes reports. Key responsibilities include conducting in-depth chart reviews, ensuring accurate and timely data abstraction and submission, assisting in data analysis before submissions, tracking adverse events and metric outliers, participating in interrater reliability (IRR's), and demonstrating proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite. The coordinator will also identify opportunities for improvement, prepare presentations, ensure deadlines are met for active databases, identify data trends, and collaborate with IT and physician experts in the ongoing development and implementation of registries. This job posting indicates Nuvance Health's ongoing efforts in detailed data collection and analysis within its cardiovascular services.

May 07, 2025
Northwell Health and Nuvance Health have officially completed their merger, creating a large integrated regional health system spanning New York and Connecticut. This merger, finalized after receiving all necessary regulatory approvals including the final one from the Connecticut Office of Health Strategy, results in a combined entity with 28 hospitals and over 1,000 care sites. As part of the agreement, Northwell has committed to investing at least $1 billion into Nuvance's hospitals over the next five years. While primarily a strategic and financial move driven in part by Nuvance's financial challenges, the integration is expected to include investments in a unified electronic health records (EHR) system and improvements in cybersecurity across the combined network. The merger aims to enhance care access, attract talent, and facilitate investment in medical advancements and innovation across the region.
May 06, 2025
Nuvance Health's AVP of Data and Analytics, in a discussion about transforming healthcare data, highlighted the organization's approach to integrating AI. According to the AVP, Nuvance Health has a two-tiered AI strategy. The first involves partnering with external vendors to incorporate AI into clinical systems, recognizing the need for rigorous oversight and regulatory approvals for these applications. The second tier focuses on building internal AI capabilities, starting with identifying specific business problems and testing initial solutions to gain experience. A key aspect of their data strategy involves leveraging the Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources (FHIR) standard to embed capabilities into their AI platforms, aiming to improve data trustworthiness. The organization is working towards developing a multimodal cloud environment to support various AI tools with the goal of empowering teams and driving measurable improvements in outcomes. The AVP also reflected on the health system's data transformation since the 2019 merger and the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic in highlighting existing data gaps and the need for near real-time data access.

Executive Leadership

John M. Murphy

President and Chief Executive Officer

The Global Health Program (GHP) was created under the leadership of Dr. John Murphy, the CEO of Nuvance Health.

Michelle Robertson

COO

Michelle Robertson, RN, BSN, MBA, joined Nuvance Health as Chief Operating Officer in July 2022, bringing with her decades of healthcare leadership experience. Prior to her role at Nuvance Health, she held senior leadership positions at Ascension Health, including serving as COO and Chief Nursing Officer of Ascension Saint Thomas. Her experience at Ascension Saint Thomas encompassed oversight of 10 acute care facilities, behavioral health, imaging, urgent care, sleep, and rehab services. Before her time at Ascension Saint Thomas, Robertson was CEO of the Seton Family of Hospitals and then served as system COO for Ascension Texas. She also held various executive leadership roles at Seton Healthcare Family. Robertson's educational background includes a Master of Business Administration degree with a specialization in Healthcare Administration from the University of Colorado and a Bachelor of Science in Nursing degree from the University of Arizona.

Dan DeBarba

CFO

Dan DeBarba is the Chief Financial Officer at Nuvance Health, joining in September 2022. He has decades of financial leadership experience in healthcare organizations. Prior to Nuvance Health, he was the Executive Vice President and CFO of Catholic Health on Long Island from 2016 to 2022. Before that, he served as President of Danbury Hospital and New Milford Hospital as part of the Western Connecticut Health Network from 2014 to 2016, and was CEO of Norwalk Hospital from 2007 to 2014, having previously been the Vice President and CFO there starting in 2007. DeBarba also held roles at Saint Mary's Hospital in Waterbury as Vice President and CFO, and was corporate director of financial planning at Yale New Haven Health System. He began his career as a certified public accountant at Ernst & Young and later worked at PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC). He holds a Bachelor of Arts in English from the University of Connecticut and a Master of Science in Accounting from the University of Hartford.

Geoff Hook

CIO, SVP

Geoffry G. (Geoff) Hook is the SVP and CIO at Nuvance Health. He was appointed to this role in 2020, following the merger of Western Connecticut Health Network (WCHN) and Health Quest, where he had previously held the same position at WCHN. Hook specializes in healthcare information systems. His prior experience includes serving as interim CIO and chief digital officer for Nuvance Health. Before the merger, he was the SVP and CIO at Western Connecticut Health Network starting in 2018. From 2014 to 2018, he was the VP, Business and Revenue Systems at MaineHealth. He also held Director roles at Adventist Health System from 2012 to 2014 and Florida Hospital from 2012 to 2012. At Florida Hospital, he also served as Senior Manager, Business Applications (2009-2012) and Product Lead and Senior Project Manager (2007-2009), starting there as a Performance Improvement Manager in 2002. Hook holds an MBA from Webster University and a BBA in Information Technology from Andrews University. He was recognized in the 2022 BostonCIO ORBIE Awards.
