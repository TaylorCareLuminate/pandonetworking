May 30, 2025
Dr. Hays Todd, a child and adolescent psychiatrist with AltaPointe Health, provided insights on managing children's screen time to promote positive mental health habits. He emphasized that there is no universal 'right' amount of screen time and encouraged parents to consider what activities screen time is replacing, such as social interaction, exercise, or homework completion. Dr. Todd acknowledged the role technology plays in modern life, offering educational tools, social connection, and convenience, but stressed the importance of balance. His tips included establishing 'no-phone zones' during family meals and for parents to model healthy screen habits themselves. He also advised starting conversations about screen use early and explaining the reasoning behind rules.

Feb 17, 2025
AltaPointe Health has a specialized substance use treatment program tailored for women in recovery, known as the Special Women's Program. Recognizing that women face unique challenges in battling addiction and seeking recovery, this program provides a safe, women-only environment. The program complements AltaPointe's medically-managed withdrawal unit at the Behavioral Health Crisis Center in Mobile, which opened in September 2024. After detoxification, patients can transition to outpatient care like the Special Women's Program or residential treatment.
Jan 30, 2025
AltaPointe Health's Behavioral Health Crisis Center in Mobile, which opened in May 2021, has reportedly saved the community over $50 million. These savings come from diverting psychiatric hospitalizations ($20 million), jail diversions ($5.6 million), ambulance diversions ($3.3 million), petition diversions ($21 million), and emergency room diversions ($4 million). Beyond the financial impact, the center is credited with preventing 2,250 suicides and 2,092 overdoses. The center expanded its services in September 2024 to include a six-bed medically managed withdrawal unit for individuals beginning substance use recovery. This center plays a crucial role in reducing the strain on emergency services, law enforcement, and hospitals by offering immediate care for those in mental health crises. [25, 34]

Sandra Parker, MD

Vice President and Chief Medical Officer

Dr. Sandra Parker is the Vice President and Chief Medical Officer at AltaPointe Health. She graduated from the University of Alabama in 1982 with an honors degree in biology. Dr. Parker attended medical school and completed residency training in psychiatry at the University of Alabama School of Medicine in Birmingham. She completed her fellowship in child/adolescent psychiatry at the University of Arkansas for the Health Sciences in Little Rock. Dr. Parker is dual certified by the American Board of Psychiatry and Neurology in adult psychiatry and child/adolescent psychiatry. Before becoming Chair of the USA College of Medicine Department of Psychiatry in 2021, she served as vice chair and associate professor for the department starting in 2009. She also held various faculty and adjunct faculty positions at the USA College of Medicine beginning in 2001. Throughout her career, Dr. Parker has received numerous honors and awards. She has served as president of the Alabama Psychiatric Physicians Association and past president of the Mobile Bay Psychiatric Association. In 2007, she was named an Alabama Health Care Hero by the Alabama Hospital Association. She was appointed by Governor Bob Riley to serve on the Alabama Interagency Autism Coordinating Council. Her major psychiatric interests include autism-related disorders, healthcare disparities, and telepsychiatry.
