Recent News

Jun 15, 2025
Kentucky's Lung Cancer Control Program has achieved a #1 national ranking, a success significantly contributed to by St. Elizabeth Healthcare's Lung Cancer Screening Program. St. Elizabeth Healthcare has performed over 50,000 lung cancer screenings since 2013, a milestone that has led to the identification of over 500 cases of stage 1 lung cancer with a high cure rate. The program's success is attributed to a dedicated multidisciplinary team, including nurse navigators, and pioneering initiatives aimed at reaching high-risk and underserved populations through efforts like the Appalachian and Firefighter initiatives. This achievement highlights St. Elizabeth's commitment to early detection and improving lung cancer outcomes in the state.
Jun 11, 2025
St. Elizabeth Healthcare's Edgewood Hospital has been recognized by Newsweek as one of America's Best Hospitals for Specialized Care 2025. This notable achievement highlights the hospital's excellence across six key specialties: Cancer Care, Cardiac Care, Neurological Care, Orthopedic Care, Endocrine Care, and Pulmonary Care. This recognition is based on a comprehensive evaluation that includes recommendations from medical experts, patient experience surveys, and hospital quality metrics. Earlier in 2025, St. Elizabeth Edgewood was also listed among the top 175 hospitals in the U.S. by Newsweek, further solidifying its reputation for providing high-quality, specialized medical services to the community.
Jun 10, 2025
St. Elizabeth Healthcare held a Safe Sitter Class at their Edgewood, KY location on June 10, 2025. This program is designed for adolescents aged 11-13, providing them with essential skills for handling emergencies while caring for younger children. The curriculum includes crucial topics such as safety and security measures, child development, age-appropriate activities, and the fundamental business aspects of babysitting. Although the class held on this date is now full and registration is closed, St. Elizabeth Healthcare regularly offers these classes as part of their commitment to community education and safety.
