Recent News

Jun 17, 2025
Baylor Scott & White Health has been recognized as being named No. 2 in the Nation. This significant ranking was reported by D CEO - D Magazine as part of their Dallas-Fort Worth Healthcare News coverage. This achievement reflects the health system's standing among healthcare providers nationally. [3]
Jun 12, 2025
Within the last year, Baylor Scott & White Medical Center-Grapevine earned the status of a Comprehensive Stroke Center. This designation signifies the highest level of specialized stroke care available. According to Matthew Olivolo, Baylor Scott & White Health's manager of North Texas media relations, the Grapevine location offers advanced imaging, has neurovascular specialists, and is equipped to handle multiple types of strokes and complex cases. This highlights the hospital's capability in providing comprehensive and advanced treatment for stroke patients in the region. [6]
Jun 10, 2025
Baylor Scott & White Medical Center in Buda, part of the Baylor Scott & White Health system, has several recent updates. Dr. Jordan Handcox, an orthopedic surgeon specializing in hip and knee replacements with a focus on health equity, is working to improve access to joint care for underrepresented populations at the Buda location. There are also hopes to bring robotic surgery technology to this hospital. Additionally, the hospital recently added a mobile PET/CT scanner, available every Friday, to help reduce travel and wait times for patients in southern Travis and Hays counties. This allows for enhanced diagnostic capabilities closer to home for patients in that area. [2]
Jun 07, 2025
Baylor Scott & White Health is offering free skin cancer screenings at their Sports Medicine & Orthopedic Institute in Waxahachie, Texas. The screenings are scheduled for June 7, 2025. These events provide an opportunity for individuals to undergo a non-invasive skin examination by a Baylor Scott & White Health physician. Early detection is emphasized as a key factor in the successful treatment of skin cancer. The screenings are designed to be quick, lasting approximately 10 minutes per person, and are conducted in a private setting to ensure patient confidentiality and comfort. Participants are advised to wear clothing that allows easy access to the skin they wish to have examined. [48, 49]
Jun 02, 2025
Dr. Melanie Raffoul of Baylor Scott & White discussed the increasing integration of artificial intelligence in healthcare during a CBS News Texas Eye on Health segment. She highlighted AI's role in streamlining patient access and navigation, describing it as a "digital front door" to health systems. Dr. Raffoul acknowledged the need for caution but emphasized that AI is becoming a permanent fixture in healthcare and is already positively impacting the process of guiding patients to appropriate care.
