Southern Illinois Healthcare (SIH) is actively seeking a Chief Health Informatics Executive (CHIE). This executive role is intended to be a key leader in leveraging technology to improve patient safety, quality of care, clinician engagement, and operational performance within the health system. The CHIE will be the primary link between IT and the clinical staff, responsible for developing an enterprise-wide informatics strategy. This strategy includes optimizing the Epic platform to enhance its Gold Star status and promoting the thoughtful adoption of technology tools that support clinical workflows. A significant focus of this position is to improve clinician wellness by identifying and implementing technologies that decrease cognitive burden, such as the continued deployment of ambient listening tools and exploring emerging AI and digital solutions. The CHIE will work closely with physicians, nurses, and advanced practice providers to encourage technology adoption, improve digital literacy, and ensure that clinical perspectives are central to the design and implementation of health IT solutions. May 09, 2025
Southern Illinois Healthcare (SIH) has announced the launch of PocketHealth, a platform that provides patients with secure online access to their diagnostic imaging and reports. This technology eliminates the need for patients to travel to the hospital to pick up physical CD-ROMs of their medical images. Patients can view and share their images securely on any device and utilize PocketHealth's Report Reader function, which helps define complex medical terms found in radiology reports. The platform also benefits physicians and other healthcare providers by offering instant electronic access to view imaging in full diagnostic quality or import images into their systems without requiring additional software or account setup. SIH is reportedly the first hospital in Illinois to adopt PocketHealth, aiming to improve patient and provider satisfaction by streamlining the image-sharing process and reducing costs associated with burning CD-ROMs and administrative time. According to Marcia Matthias, SIH Corporate Director of Health Information, this initiative puts patients at the center of their healthcare journey and makes image-sharing more timely, ultimately improving access to important health information. Rishi Nayyar, Co-Founder and CEO of PocketHealth, highlighted that the platform empowers patients with more control and visibility over their health information.
