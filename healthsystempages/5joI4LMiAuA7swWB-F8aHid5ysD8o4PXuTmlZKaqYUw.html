May 09, 2025
Ponce Health Sciences University (PHSU) announced the launch of its new Orthopedic Surgery Residency Program, an accredited five-year initiative that includes clinical rotations at recognized hospitals, including Hospital Menonita in Cayey. [15] This program aims to provide comprehensive training in orthopedic surgery and musculoskeletal health. [15] Dr. Olga Rodríguez de Arzola, Dean of the PHSU School of Medicine, stated that this program represents a concrete investment in expanding professional development opportunities in specialized medicine and addressing the critical need to retain physicians on the island. [15] The program, led by Dr. Rafael Fernández Soltero, focuses on healthcare leadership, community impact, patient-centered care, research innovation, and continuous medical education. [15] Dr. Fernández Soltero mentioned that the residency prepares orthopedic surgeons with clinical and research excellence and is committed to strengthening the local healthcare system with a vision of increased access to specialized orthopedic care and social responsibility. [15] New residents are scheduled to begin training in July 2025. [15]
Feb 12, 2025
El Sistema de Salud Menonita inauguró la primera unidad especializada en 'stroke' en el sur de Puerto Rico, ubicada en el Hospital Menonita Ponce. Esta unidad está dedicada a proporcionar tratamiento y manejo a pacientes que sufren accidentes cerebrovasculares, una condición que es la principal causa de incapacidad en Puerto Rico y la quinta de mortalidad. La unidad busca ofrecer cuidado inmediato y atención integral y personalizada, dado que la atención médica oportuna es crucial para mejorar los resultados de los pacientes de stroke. Aunque ubicada en Ponce, esta unidad especializada forma parte del Sistema de Salud Menonita, demostrando la expansión y mejora de los servicios especializados disponibles para los pacientes en la región que abarca el sistema, incluyendo aquellos en el área de Cayey.
Oct 29, 2024
Sistema de Salud Menonita announced that Centro Médico Menonita Cayey received accreditation from the Accreditation Council for Graduate Medical Education (ACGME) for its Internal Medicine residency program. This follows the institution's accreditation as a Teaching Hospital by the same agency in late 2023. This accreditation is seen as a significant achievement, reinforcing the hospital's commitment to excellence in medical training and patient care. It also marks the beginning of a new phase for the hospital system focused on educating new medical talent, with residency programs expected to commence in July 2025. The Internal Medicine program is planned to eventually accommodate 24 residents. This development positions the hospital as a key training center for residents seeking clinical experience and specialized training in a leading institution that provides high-caliber medicine. [13]

Jul 25, 2024
Centro Médico Menonita Cayey received several important accreditations, as announced on July 25, 2024. The institution was accredited by the American College of Cardiology (ACC) as an Expert Center in the Management of Chest Pain, recognizing its proficiency in evaluating, diagnosing, and treating patients with chest pain, including primary percutaneous coronary intervention. Additionally, the American College of Surgeons (ACS) reaccredited the Centro de Excelencia Bariátrica Menonita, noting its consistent clinical quality standards and strict compliance with excellence requirements since 2001, making it the only center in Puerto Rico to maintain this accreditation consistently. The Surgical Review Corporation (SRC) also accredited the hospital as a Center of Excellence in Robotic and Minimally Invasive Surgery. These accreditations were highlighted by Ricardo Hernández-Rivera, executive director of Sistema de Salud Menonita, as reflections of the system's growth, evolution, and provision of world-class services.
