James A. Carter

Senior Vice President/COO

James A. Carter, the Senior Vice President and Chief Operating Officer at Archbold Medical Center, has over 22 years of experience in healthcare. He earned a bachelor's degree in industrial engineering from the Georgia Institute of Technology and holds master's degrees in business administration and health administration from Georgia State University. Before joining Archbold in 2002 as the director of the Ambulatory Care Center, Carter held positions at Piedmont Medical Center in Atlanta, Rockdale Hospital in Conyers, Georgia, and Alamance Regional Medical Center in Burlington, North Carolina. He has served in various leadership capacities at Archbold, including as vice president of Administrative Services before his promotion to COO.

Kristopher Palmer, DO, FACOI, CHCQM-PHYADV

Vice President of Medical Affairs / Chief Medical Officer

Dr. Kristopher Palmer, DO, FACOI, CHCQM-PHYADV is the Vice President of Medical Affairs and Chief Medical Officer at Archbold. He joined Archbold's medical staff as a hospital medicine physician in 2016. Prior to this role, he held several leadership positions at Archbold, including Hospital Medicine Program Director and Director of Archbold's Internal Medicine Residency Program, and most recently, Interim Chief Medical Officer. Dr. Palmer earned a Bachelor of Science degree from Bethany College in West Virginia and a Doctorate of Osteopathic Medicine from the West Virginia School of Osteopathic Medicine. He completed a residency in internal medicine at University Hospitals Richmond Medical Center in Cleveland, Ohio. Before moving to Thomasville, where Archbold is located, he practiced as a hospital medicine physician in Cleveland, Ohio, for nearly six years after his residency. He is board certified in Internal Medicine. He is also the Medical Director of the SouthlandMD Hospital Medicine Program and Assistant Medical Director Inpatient Rehab at Archbold Medical Center.

Amy G. Griffin

Senior Vice President of Patient Care Services/Chief Nursing Officer

Amy G. Griffin, RN, MSN, CENP, is the Senior Vice President of Patient Care Services/Chief Nursing Officer at Archbold Medical Center. She has a 27-year career at Archbold, starting as a nursing student and working her way up to CNO in 2011. Prior to holding the Senior Vice President title, she was the Vice President of Patient Care at Archbold.

W. Kendall Wyatt, MD, RN, EMTP

Vice President of Information Services/Chief Information Officer

W. Kendall Wyatt, MD, RN, EMTP, is the Vice President of Information Services/Chief Information Officer at Archbold. He also holds the title of Chief Medical Information Officer and Interim Vice President of Information Services at Archbold Foundation, part of the Archbold Medical Center system. With a background that uniquely combines medical and technological expertise, he is certified as an MD, RN, and EMTP. Wyatt is passionate about medicine, technology innovation, and education, allowing him to view healthcare technology from diverse perspectives, including those of a physician, nurse, paramedic, user experience designer, and engineer. He is board certified in clinical informatics and Internal Medicine. Prior to his role at Archbold, he served as the associate chief medical officer for informatics at CAMC Health System, where he contributed significantly to COVID-19 response efforts and received an award for his services. He is recognized for his ability to translate problems into solutions to improve healthcare workflows and his skill in explaining complex clinical topics.

E. Vann Middleton

President, Archbold Foundation

E. Vann Middleton serves as the President of the Archbold Foundation at Archbold Medical Center, a role he has held since 2013. In this capacity, he is responsible for overseeing the management of the Medical Center's long-term investable assets to support the operations of John D. Archbold Memorial Hospital and Archbold Health Services. Middleton's educational background includes graduating from The Bolles School and earning a Bachelor of Arts in History and a Bachelor of Business Administration in Economics from Vanderbilt University. His professional career began as a Financial Consultant at Merrill Lynch. Prior to joining Archbold, he held the position of Executive Vice President and Director of Operations at Tall Timbers Research Station & Land Conservatory. Actively involved in the community, Middleton's commitments include serving on the boards of the Thomasville Historical Society, The Fish & Wildlife Foundation of Florida, and the Thomasville – Thomas County Humane Society. He is also an alumnus of Leadership Thomasville and Leadership Georgia programs.

Jonathan Shedlock

Vice President of Revenue Cycle Operations

Jonathan Shedlock is the Vice President of Revenue Cycle Operations at Archbold. Prior to this role, he was the Associate Vice President of Revenue Cycle at Evangelical Community Hospital, where he oversaw all revenue cycle activities. Shedlock has 16 years of experience in the field, including ten years in leadership positions, with a background in both operations and consulting aimed at improving the financial performance of hospitals. Before his time as Associate Vice President, he was a Senior Advisor with Impact Advisors, LLC, and served as the Director of Patient Financial Services at Evangelical Community Hospital from 2020 to 2022. He holds an MBA from California State University, Fresno, and a Bachelor of Science from The Pennsylvania State University. Shedlock is also recognized as a Fellow of the Healthcare Financial Management Association (FHFMA), a Certified Healthcare Financial Professional (CHFP), and a Certified Revenue Cycle Representative (CRCR).
