Recent News

Jun 03, 2025
Tower Health has appointed Brian Rayme as executive vice president and CFO, effective June 2. Rayme previously served as CFO of Johns Hopkins Bayview Medical Center where he was credited with leading an $80 million turnaround. Before that, he was senior director of reimbursement and regulatory reporting for Johns Hopkins Medicine. Mike Eesley, who had been serving as CFO since January 2023 after coming out of retirement in 2021 to be chief transformation officer, has transitioned out of the CFO role but will continue as chief transformation officer. Tower Health President and CEO Michael Stern stated in an internal email that the appointment is part of the health system's three-year strategic plan and the Tower Experience initiative, expressing optimism about the future, including growing patient volume, managing costs, and enhancing the experience for patients and staff.
May 12, 2025
Reading Hospital has launched its first 'Girls on the Run' Berks team on campus, bringing a program focused on empowering young girls through running and positive development. The team, coached by four dedicated nurses from the hospital, meets twice a week on the Reading Hospital campus. The initiative aims to promote health, wellness, and confidence among participants through a fun and engaging curriculum that combines physical activity with life skills lessons. This program represents a community relations effort by Tower Health to support youth development and well-being in the areas it serves.
Apr 30, 2025
The Drexel University College of Medicine at Tower Health campus in West Reading held its annual Transition to the Clinic event for the Class of 2027. This ceremony signifies the students' progression from foundational classroom learning to the practical, hands-on phase of their medical education. During the clinical phase, students will gain experience working directly with patients under supervision at Tower Health – Reading Hospital, applying the knowledge and skills acquired during their first two years of medical school. The event celebrates this significant academic milestone and reinforces the partnership between Drexel University and Tower Health in training future physicians.
Apr 25, 2025
Reading Hospital hosted a 'Take Your Child to Work Day' event, welcoming 120 children of employees to explore the various departments and operations of the hospital. The program provided participating students with a unique opportunity to gain insight into the healthcare environment and learn about the diverse career paths available within a hospital setting. Through behind-the-scenes tours and activities, the event aimed to inspire potential future healthcare professionals and give children a better understanding of their parents' contributions to the community's health.
Apr 10, 2025
A Philadelphia Court granted final approval for a $1.83 million settlement in a class action lawsuit filed against Chestnut Hill Hospital, LLC, Tower Health Medical Group, and other associated entities. The lawsuit alleged violations of Philadelphia's "Ban the Box" law, claiming that the defendants unlawfully asked job applicants about their criminal history during the initial application process. This settlement is notable as a significant resolution under the Philadelphia ordinance and provides compensation to individuals who applied for positions at Chestnut Hill Hospital between October 1, 2017, and September 30, 2020, and were subjected to the allegedly unlawful question.
