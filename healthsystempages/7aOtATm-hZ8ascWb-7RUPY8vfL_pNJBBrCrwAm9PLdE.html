Recent News

May 04, 2025
A recent job posting on Teal, dated May 4, 2025, indicates that Cottonwood Creek Behavioral Hospital in Meridian, ID, part of Oceans Healthcare, is hiring for a Registered Nurse PRN position. The job description highlights that Cottonwood Creek Behavioral Hospital provides specialty behavioral health services in Meridian for individuals with mental health and substance abuse issues. It mentions that the hospital's culture is built around service and that employees contribute to delivering compassionate, quality care. This job posting confirms ongoing operations and staffing needs at the Meridian, ID location under the ownership of Oceans Healthcare following the acquisition of Haven Behavioral Healthcare.
Mar 05, 2025
Comvest Credit Partners announced it provided a $237 million senior secured credit facility to Oceans Healthcare. This financing supports Oceans Healthcare's acquisition of Haven Behavioral Healthcare, Inc. Comvest Credit Partners acted as Administrative Agent and was the sole lender for this financing. This marks the fourth investment by Comvest Credit Partners in Oceans Healthcare since 2016, demonstrating a continued partnership to support Oceans' growth initiatives. Oceans Healthcare, based in Plano, Texas, is a portfolio company of Webster Equity Partners. The credit facility will help facilitate the integration of Haven Behavioral Healthcare's operations and support Oceans Healthcare's continued expansion in providing inpatient and outpatient mental health care treatment programs.
Feb 17, 2025
Ochsner Lafayette General and Oceans Healthcare have broken ground on a new behavioral health hospital in Lafayette, Louisiana, which is expected to be the largest in the region. The facility, named Ochsner Behavioral Health Acadiana, will be a 100-bed hospital offering inpatient services for adolescents, adults, and geriatric patients, as well as outpatient treatment for mental health and co-occurring substance use disorders. Construction is currently underway at 310 Youngsville Highway, with completion anticipated in early 2026 and opening later that year. This project represents a significant investment of approximately $22 million between the two partners and aims to address the pressing need for behavioral health services in the community, particularly for young patients who have limited inpatient options. Jan 14, 2025
Oceans Healthcare announced on January 14, 2025, the promotion of Laura Tarantino to Chief Legal Officer. Tarantino, who has been with Oceans Healthcare for 10 years, previously served as executive vice president, general counsel, and compliance and privacy officer. In her role, she has been crucial in developing systems and processes for compliance with healthcare regulations and mitigating risks across the organization's operations. Her work has been instrumental in facilitating Oceans Healthcare's growth, including managing the legal aspects of facility development, acquisitions, and partnerships such as the recent acquisition of Haven Behavioral Healthcare, which added locations in states like Idaho. In her new role, Tarantino will continue to oversee the legal and regulatory affairs for Oceans Healthcare's expanded network of 48 facilities.
Jan 02, 2025
Oceans Healthcare, a behavioral health provider, announced the acquisition of Haven Behavioral Healthcare, Inc. This acquisition includes seven behavioral health hospitals and outpatient services, expanding Oceans Healthcare's network into five new states: Arizona, Idaho, Ohio, New Mexico, and Pennsylvania. According to Stuart Archer, CEO of Oceans Healthcare, the acquisition aligns with Oceans' mission to expand access to care for at-risk populations in underserved communities, a mission shared by Haven. Oceans Healthcare now operates 48 facilities across nine states, offering a range of services including inpatient and outpatient mental healthcare, intensive outpatient and partial hospitalization programs, a specialized military program, and substance use disorder treatment. The company is backed by private equity firm Webster Equity Partners. This expansion into Idaho is particularly relevant given the user's focus on Oceans Healthcare's headquarters in Meridian, ID.
