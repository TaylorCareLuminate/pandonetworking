Regional Transit Service (RTS) in Genesee County, in partnership with Rochester Regional Health, has launched a new free express bus service to the Rochester Regional Health and University of Rochester Medical Center facilities located in Batavia. [12] This service, which became effective on June 16, provides transportation from the Batavia City Centre to the medical centers throughout the day, from morning to afternoon, on weekdays. Jun 12, 2025
A federal district court in New York has ruled that a lawsuit brought by a whistleblower against Rochester Regional Health and United Memorial Medical Center concerning alleged violations of the False Claims Act can proceed. [11] The plaintiff, Deborah Conrad, a former physician assistant at RRH who was terminated in 2021, claims the hospitals failed to report adverse reactions to the Covid-19 vaccine to the government. [11] She alleges that this failure occurred despite the hospitals receiving government funds, and that they did not meet their obligation to report to the Vaccine Adverse Event Reporting System (VAERS), which serves as a national early warning mechanism for potential vaccine safety issues. [11] Judge John L. Sinatra Jr. of the U.S. District Court for the Western District of New York issued the decision allowing the case to move forward. [11]
Jun 11, 2025
Rochester Regional Health (RRH) has been recognized by Becker's Healthcare as one of only 60 hospitals and health systems nationwide for its excellence in psychiatry and behavioral health programs. [10, 12] This national recognition highlights RRH's unwavering commitment to advancing mental and behavioral healthcare across the communities it serves. [10, 12] The systems on the list were selected based on editorial research and nominations, and inclusion cannot be purchased. [10, 12] RRH operates one of the most comprehensive behavioral health systems in New York State, including 176 inpatient psychiatric and chemical dependency beds, six acute crisis programs, and handles more than 370,000 outpatient visits annually. [12] They also operate 55 school-based clinics across the region and a network of adult and adolescent mental health centers, including the Behavioral Health Access Crisis Center, one of the first behavioral health urgent cares in the state. [12] The system also offers walk-in services, mobile crisis teams in collaboration with law enforcement, and home-based therapy programs, which have helped 93% of youth avoid hospitalization. [10, 12] RRH is equally focused on equity in mental health, offering anti-racist care, Indigenous-focused psychotherapy, and culturally competent treatment, and partners with BIPOC-led agencies while supporting regional behavioral health workforce development to create a more inclusive system of care. Jun 02, 2025
Rochester Regional Health is partnering with the Greater Rochester Chamber of Commerce and the Greater ROC Global program to recruit 60 to 100 international nurses by the end of 2025. This initiative aims to address the nationwide nursing shortage and reduce the health system's reliance on expensive temporary travel nurses. The program focuses on hiring experienced international nurses with the goal of long-term retention, unlike transient staffing models. Interviews with global healthcare professionals are already in progress to fill crucial roles, particularly in emergency departments and medical-surgical nursing. This partnership is seen as a cost-effective strategy to build a diverse and stable nursing workforce, especially benefiting rural hospitals within the RRH system where financial margins are thinner and the impact of high agency nursing costs is more significant. The health system has already placed 16 international nurses in its rural hospitals through this program. The effort is part of RRH's broader strategy implemented in response to post-pandemic workforce challenges to rebuild its core of bedside nursing expertise.
