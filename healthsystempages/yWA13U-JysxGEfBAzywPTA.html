Jun 07, 2025
Registered nurses at Henry Ford Rochester Hospital are planning to strike from June 9th to June 14th due to a breakdown in contract negotiations with the hospital administration. This is believed to be the first labor strike in Henry Ford's 110-year history. The Office and Professional Employees International Union (OPEIU) Local 40, representing the nurses, is seeking higher wages and mandated staffing ratios to address burnout and ensure patient safety, and has filed unfair labor practice claims. Henry Ford Health officials have stated they will keep the hospital open during the strike by bringing in temporary staff and have a plan in place to ensure patient care and safety. The hospital maintains that their offer is fair and competitive and allows for flexible staffing based on patient volumes, disputing the need for mandated ratios. Both the union and the hospital have accused each other of not negotiating in good faith.

Dennis Butts, MBA

Executive Vice President, Chief Strategy & Network Development Officer

Dennis Butts, MBA, is the Executive Vice President, Chief Strategy & Network Development Officer at Henry Ford Health, having started in August 2024. He is a nationally recognized expert in healthcare network design, innovative care delivery, and payment models with over two decades of experience in the healthcare industry. Prior to joining Henry Ford Health, Butts was a Partner and Health Strategy & Innovation Leader at Guidehouse from December 2019 to August 2024. He also served as a Managing Director at Navigant from July 2013 to October 2019, where he led value transformation initiatives and clinical integration. His career began at Pfizer in marketing and sales within the pharmaceutical industry. Butts holds an MBA in Strategic Management and a Certificate in Health System Management from the Weatherhead School of Management at Case Western Reserve University, and a BS in International Business from The University of Akron. He was an Integration Leader for Henry Ford Health's proposed joint venture with Ascension Michigan and is a co-founder of the Pastor Dennis K. Butts Sr. and Jewrusha V. Butts Spirit of Excellence Scholarship Fund. His thought leadership has been featured in publications including Becker's Hospital Review and Fierce Healthcare.

Heather Geisler

Executive Vice President, Chief Brand & Experience Officer

Heather Geisler is the Executive Vice President and Chief Brand & Experience Officer at Henry Ford Health, a role she assumed in 2020. In this position, she is responsible for shaping and enhancing the organization's brand and consumer experience. Before joining Henry Ford Health, Geisler served as the Senior Vice President of Global Brands for Hyatt Hotels Corporation, where she oversaw global marketing, advertising, positioning, and growth for their brands. She is a graduate of The University of Iowa and completed the Punch Sulzberger Fellowship Program at Columbia Graduate School of Journalism.

Steven N. Kalkanis, MD

Executive Vice President, CEO of Henry Ford Hospital and CEO of the Henry Ford Medical Group

Executive Vice President & Chief Development Officer

Eric Wallis

SVP, System Chief Nurse Executive

Eric Wallis, DNP, MSA, RN, NE-BC, FACHE, is the Senior Vice President and System Chief Nursing Officer at Henry Ford Health, a role he assumed in December 2021. With over two decades of experience in nursing and healthcare leadership, Wallis began his career as a bedside nurse, primarily in the cardiac ICU. His career progression includes leadership roles in clinical specialties and hospital operations at various academic medical centers and community hospitals. Before becoming System Chief Nursing Officer, he was the President of Henry Ford West Bloomfield Hospital, joining Henry Ford Health in 2019. His prior experience includes leadership positions at Baylor Scott & White Medical Center and OhioHealth, as well as roles at the Ohio State University Medical Center. Academically, Wallis holds an undergraduate degree from Bowling Green State University, a Master of Science Administration from Central Michigan University, and a Doctor of Nursing Practice from Texas Christian University. He is a fellow of the American College of Healthcare Executives and a certified Nurse Executive. Wallis is recognized for his dedication to fostering a positive culture, enhancing healthcare delivery, and actively working to address nursing workforce challenges. He also contributes to the field through his involvement with organizations like the Michigan Hospital Association and Oakland University, and as adjunct dean of nursing practice at Michigan State University.

Usamah Mossallam

Vice President & Medical Director | HF Health International

Dr. Usamah (Sam) Mossallam is the Vice President and Medical Director for International Initiatives at Henry Ford Health. He graduated from the State University of New York Health Sciences Center at Brooklyn in 1994 and completed a combined internal medicine/emergency medicine residency at the Medical Center of Delaware (Christiana Care Health System), where he was chief resident. He is board certified in both emergency medicine and internal medicine and has over 30 years of experience in the medical field. Dr. Mossallam joined Henry Ford Hospital in 1999, taking on leadership roles in residency programs. He left briefly in 2006 to serve as Chairman of Emergency Medicine and Associate Chief Medical Officer at the International Medical Center in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, before returning to Henry Ford Health System in late 2007. Since his return, he has held positions including service chief/medical director and division head of the Fairlane Emergency Department and Chief Medical Officer – Associate at Henry Ford Hospital, where he developed utilization management programs. In January 2015, he was promoted to his current role, leading Henry Ford Health's international activities, including licensed hospitals in Saudi Arabia and India, a visiting professor program in Dubai, and the international patient division. He also holds an MBA from Oakland University.
