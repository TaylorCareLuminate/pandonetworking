Jun 09, 2025
ECU Health is proposing to establish North Carolina's first Rural Emergency Hospital (REH) in Martin County. The initial location would be within the existing medical facility. As part of this proposal, ECU Health is advocating for a $70 million investment from the State of North Carolina to construct a new Rural Care Center in Martin County. This new center would serve as the future home for the REH and has the potential to offer expanded outpatient services, including preventative care. The REH designation, effective since January 1, 2023, is a CMS initiative designed to address challenges faced by rural hospitals in providing acute inpatient care. The program allows rural hospitals to cease inpatient operations while maintaining a freestanding emergency department and outpatient services, receiving a facility fee and a Medicare outpatient payment add-on. Given that REHs have limited inpatient capabilities, ECU Health is also seeking $150 million to increase inpatient capacity at ECU Health Beaufort Hospital, a campus of ECU Health Medical Center, which was built in 1958 and requires modernization to support the regional care system. [6] ECU Health CEO and Brody School of Medicine Dean Michael Waldrum presented this vision to the Martin County Board of Commissioners on June 4, outlining plans to re-establish essential healthcare services in the area. Jun 06, 2025
ECU Health has put forth a $220 million capital proposal which includes plans to reopen a closed hospital in Williamston, N.C. as the state's first rural emergency hospital and expand its Beaufort campus. The proposal was outlined during a public hearing on June 4th. ECU Health signed a nonbinding proposal with Martin County in mid-May to convert the former Martin General Hospital into a rural emergency hospital. The initial reopening would use the existing facility, but ECU Health plans to seek $70 million in state funding for a permanent facility with space for more outpatient services. Additionally, the non-profit system is requesting $150 million to increase inpatient capacity at ECU Health Beaufort Hospital in Washington, N.C. The Martin County Board of Commissioners is currently reviewing the proposal. [4]

Chief Well-being Officer, ECU Health
