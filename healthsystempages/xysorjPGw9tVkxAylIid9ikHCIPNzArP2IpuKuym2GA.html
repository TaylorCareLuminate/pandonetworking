Recent News

Jun 04, 2025
Wilson Medical Center, a Duke LifePoint hospital, and Barton College announced a strategic partnership that includes a $1 million investment in healthcare education. This initiative aims to address the need for healthcare professionals and enhance educational opportunities. The $1 million investment in Barton College's School of Health Sciences will support a new facility with advanced hospital simulation, interdisciplinary integration, and a focus on rural healthcare. This facility is intended to produce top-tier healthcare professionals and will strengthen existing programs, support faculty development, and expand healthcare education initiatives to meet community needs, including a new family nurse practitioner program. The partnership emphasizes investing in local institutions to build a stronger, healthier region. The first group of Barton College Wilson Medical Fellows will be selected in the 2025-2026 academic year.
Jun 03, 2025
Northeast Georgia Health System (NGHS) and Lifepoint Rehabilitation, a business unit of Lifepoint Health, celebrated the opening of Northeast Georgia Rehabilitation Institute. This is a new free-standing, 40-bed inpatient rehabilitation hospital located in Gainesville on Limestone Parkway. Leaders from both Northeast Georgia Health System and Lifepoint Rehabilitation were present for a ribbon-cutting ceremony to mark the occasion. Lifepoint Health is the parent company of Duke LifePoint Healthcare and is headquartered in Brentwood, TN. This opening represents an expansion of services by a Lifepoint Health entity.
Jun 02, 2025
Frye Regional Medical Center, which is a Duke LifePoint hospital, has announced the expansion of its specialty services with the addition of FryeCare Gastroenterology. The new practice is located at 1771 Tate Blvd. SE, Suite 101, Hickory, NC. Dr. Simon Allport, a board-certified gastroenterologist with over 30 years of experience in diagnosing and treating a wide range of digestive conditions, including pancreatic, liver, and esophageal disorders, is the first provider to join the clinic. Dr. Allport is also skilled in procedures such as esophagogastroduodenoscopy and colonoscopy. This expansion aims to provide compassionate, evidence-based care tailored to individual patients in the Hickory area.
Jun 02, 2025
Rutherford Regional Health System, a facility that is part of the Duke LifePoint Healthcare network, has reached a new agreement with UnitedHealthcare. This agreement follows several months of negotiations between the two entities. The announcement indicates that a resolution has been found, which is significant for patients covered by UnitedHealthcare who receive care at Rutherford Regional Health System. Rutherford Regional Health System is a Duke LifePoint facility, linking this news directly to operations within the Duke LifePoint network.
Jun 02, 2025
UnitedHealthcare and Duke LifePoint have agreed to extend their contract in North Carolina through June 15, 2025. This extension ensures that individuals enrolled in UnitedHealthcare's employer-sponsored commercial plans and Medicare Advantage plans will maintain continuous, uninterrupted in-network access to Duke LifePoint hospitals and physicians while both parties continue to work towards finalizing the terms of a new long-term contract.
