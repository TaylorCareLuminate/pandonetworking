Recent News

Jun 10, 2025
Halifax Health | Brooks Rehabilitation – Center for Inpatient Rehabilitation, located within Halifax Health Medical Center of Daytona Beach, has announced a significant expansion of its facilities. The center has added 12 more private beds to its existing 40-bed unit, enhancing its capacity to provide comprehensive rehabilitation services. This inpatient center holds accreditation from the Commission on Accreditation of Rehabilitation Facilities (CARF) for various programs, including stroke, brain injury, cancer, and comprehensive patients. Notably, the Halifax Health | Brooks Rehabilitation center is one of only three facilities in Florida with a CARF-accredited cancer program. The expansion aims to meet the growing need for high-quality rehabilitation services in Central Florida for individuals recovering from traumatic illnesses or injuries. The center emphasizes providing cutting-edge technology, research-based therapy, and compassionate care through an interdisciplinary team model, with each patient receiving a rigorous therapy schedule, 24/7 nursing care, and an individualized care plan overseen by a board-certified physical medicine and rehabilitation physician. Jun 09, 2025
Halifax Health Hospice is partnering with the City of Flagler Beach for a Veterans & Military Appreciation Care Package Collection drive. This initiative aims to gather much-needed items to create care packages for local veteran organizations and the JAX USO, which supports active-duty military personnel. The collection will run from May through July, with the collected donations being packaged and distributed in August. Community contributions of any size are welcomed and encouraged to show appreciation for veterans and military members. Donation bins are located at various City of Flagler Beach offices, the Fire Department, and the Library. [9]
Jun 02, 2025
Halifax Health is presenting the Volusia-Flagler High School Sports Awards show at the Ocean Center Arena in Daytona Beach. This event is a nine-month program recognizing student-athletes in 26 sports, culminating in a red-carpet awards ceremony. Nominated athletes who RSVP will receive complimentary tickets courtesy of sponsors, including Halifax Health. The event will honor players of the year for each sport, as well as present other awards for accomplishments on and off the field, such as a courage award, coach of the year, team of the year, and boys and girls athlete of the year awards. This sponsorship and presentation highlight Halifax Health's involvement in community relations and recognition of local student achievements. [15]
May 30, 2025
Halifax Health celebrated the successful graduation of their recent Project SEARCH class in Daytona Beach. This program is a year-long initiative designed to provide young adults transitioning from high school with the necessary skills and experience to secure competitive employment. This year's class saw 18 graduates successfully complete the program. The graduation event featured speeches from leaders at both Halifax Health and Volusia County Schools, highlighting the graduates' dedication and perseverance. The partnership between Halifax Health and Volusia County Schools is crucial in offering students the support and resources needed to thrive, creating an environment that encourages growth, learning, and independence. Halifax Health remains dedicated to supporting young people and creating opportunities for their success in the workforce.
May 19, 2025
Halifax Health's Community Relations Coordinator, Anne S. Tracy, R.N., is leading a monthly program at the New Smyrna Beach Regional Library titled "Exercise Your Mind." This program, part of the 'Live Your Best Life' series, aims to help adults understand how to keep their minds sharp for improved overall health. The June session, held on June 5th, provided attendees with tips and information on health and wellness for themselves and their loved ones. The series covers different health topics each month.
