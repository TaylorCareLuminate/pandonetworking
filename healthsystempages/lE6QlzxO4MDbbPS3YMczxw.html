Jun 14, 2025
A bankruptcy judge has approved a plan that could lead to salary increases for top executives at Prospect Medical Holdings, the parent company of Eastern Connecticut Health Network (ECHN), which includes Manchester Memorial Hospital and Rockville General Hospital. This decision comes as Prospect faces bankruptcy proceedings and actively seeks to sell its Connecticut hospitals. A representative for a group of creditors, including the state of Connecticut and union members from Waterbury Hospital (also owned by Prospect), supported the executive incentive plan, arguing it is necessary to maintain the smooth operation of the hospitals while potential buyers are being sought. There are reportedly at least four interested parties in acquiring the Connecticut properties. The judge stated that the incentive amounts were not excessive and were consistent with similar bankruptcy cases. Prospect intends to name a "stalking horse" bidder by the end of June to establish a minimum price for the sale, with auction dates expected to be announced within weeks. Deadlines for sale hearings have been extended to accommodate a fourth potential purchaser's due diligence. While employees hope for a better future under new ownership, they are wary of involvement from private-equity firms like Prospect. Efforts to introduce greater state regulation of private equity firms purchasing Connecticut hospitals did not pass in the recent legislative session, but state officials have pledged increased scrutiny of future deals. Investment banking firm Houlihan Lokey Capital is working with Prospect's landlord, Medical Properties Trust, and other parties to expedite the hospital sales. [20]

May 27, 2025
Bryan Graven, who serves as the CIO and Executive Director of ITS at both Waterbury Health and Eastern CT Health Network (ECHN), was quoted in a Becker's Hospital Review article discussing the anticipated evolution of healthcare IT teams over the next two years. According to Graven, the focus for their teams will be on expanding roles related to automation, data science, and the optimization of workflows through the use of artificial intelligence (AI). He anticipates a decrease in traditional support functions as cloud and self-support systems become more prevalent. The strategic goal is to transition the IT focus from system maintenance towards developing smarter, more scalable solutions aimed at improving both patient care and operational efficiency within the health networks he oversees. May 23, 2025
Yale New Haven Health has stated that acquiring Eastern Connecticut Health Network (ECHN) and Waterbury Hospital for the proposed $435 million is now "impossible" due to ECHN's current owner, Prospect Medical Holdings, filing for bankruptcy. This development is the latest in a series of failed attempts over the past decade by ECHN, which operates Rockville General and Manchester Memorial hospitals, to find stable ownership amidst financial difficulties, high executive salaries, and unsuccessful prospective buyer deals. Prospect's ownership since 2016 has reportedly led to a decline in hospital operations and profitability at Rockville and Manchester hospitals compared to their performance as a nonprofit system. Manchester is also planning to place a lien on Prospect's landlord, Medical Properties Trust, for six delinquent real estate tax bills if payments are not made in the first week of June. [3]
