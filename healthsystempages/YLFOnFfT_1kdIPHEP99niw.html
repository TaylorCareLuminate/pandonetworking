Recent News

Jun 13, 2025
McLaren Greater Lansing has announced the expansion of its Food and Resource Center, which is now open. The center held a ribbon cutting ceremony on May 29th. This expansion aims to enhance the nutritional support provided to patients. Previously serving oncology patients at the Karmanos Cancer Institute, the expanded center will now cater to additional hospital clinics. The new location offers increased space for resources, including canned goods, allowing McLaren Greater Lansing to continue its efforts in ensuring patients receive the necessary nutritional assistance.
Jun 12, 2025
The Barbara Ann Karmanos Cancer Institute's Office of Community Outreach and Engagement is continuing its 2025 Community Conversations on Cancer series throughout June. The focus of the June session is on understanding lung cancer diagnosis and treatment options. This series is part of Karmanos's ongoing efforts to educate the community on various aspects of cancer, providing valuable information on detection, treatment modalities, and related support.
Jun 11, 2025
Staff at the Barbara Ann Karmanos Cancer Institute recently participated in #HAVhope, holding a moment of silence on June 6th. This event was held to raise awareness regarding hospital violence. The #HAVhope initiative aims to highlight the challenges and dangers faced by healthcare workers and promote a safer environment within hospitals and healthcare settings.
Jun 05, 2025
Officials for The Ally Challenge presented by McLaren announced details for The Concert @17, which will feature the band WAR on Friday, August 22, 2025, following the first round of the PGA TOUR Champions event. This concert is part of The Ally Challenge, an event with a significant philanthropic vision that has raised over $8.4 million for charity in southeastern Michigan since 2018. McLaren Health Care is a presenting sponsor of the event, demonstrating its commitment to community support and engagement through this annual tournament and its associated activities.
Jun 05, 2025
Maureen D'Agostino, Vice President of Accreditation and Regulatory CMS Programs and Provider and Facility Enrollment at McLaren Health Care, provided testimony during the Michigan House Insurance and Financial Services Committee hearing. The hearing focused on House Bill (HB) 5350, a bill aimed at protecting access to affordable prescription drugs and healthcare services through the 340B Prescription Drug Pricing Program. D'Agostino highlighted how the savings generated by the 340B program enable McLaren to offer comprehensive care for conditions such as substance use disorder and cancer, emphasizing its importance in expanding access to quality, community-based care. She was accompanied by Deidra Wilson, Vice President of Government Affairs at McLaren. The Michigan Health & Hospital Association continues to advocate for the 340B program and the efforts of Michigan hospitals in utilizing its benefits.
