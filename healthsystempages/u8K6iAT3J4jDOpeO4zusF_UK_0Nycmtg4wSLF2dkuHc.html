Recent News

Jun 12, 2025
Ridgeview was honored with the Healthgrades 2025 Outstanding Patient Experience Award™, an announcement made around June 11 or 12, 2025. This prestigious recognition places Ridgeview among the top 5% of hospitals nationwide for patient experience for the fifth consecutive year, from 2021 through 2025. Additionally, it marks the seventeenth consecutive year, spanning from 2009 to 2025, that Ridgeview has been recognized among the top 15% nationally. The award is based on Healthgrades' analysis of standardized patient surveys that evaluate various aspects of a patient's hospital stay, including communication with medical staff, responsiveness of hospital personnel, and the cleanliness and quietness of the hospital environment. This consistent recognition highlights Ridgeview's sustained commitment to providing a positive patient experience.
Jun 03, 2025
Dr. Purvi Gada has joined Minnesota Oncology and Ridgeview Cancer & Infusion Center in Waconia. Minnesota Oncology provides patients with access to specialists across multiple disciplines, including medical oncology, radiation oncology, surgery, and hematology. They also offer a comprehensive range of support services as part of their "full circle of care," such as social work, patient advocacy, nutrition therapy, financial counseling, educational classes, support groups, nurse navigation, advance care planning, palliative care, and a survivorship program. Ridgeview Medical Center is recognized nationally as a Community Cancer Program, holding accreditation from the Commission on Cancer. Ridgeview partners with various leading cancer care providers and clinics to offer a multidisciplinary approach to diagnose, treat, and support over 500 adult cancer patients annually. Ridgeview is an independent, nonprofit, regional health care system situated about 35 minutes west of Minneapolis.
May 20, 2025
Twenty-three physicians associated with Ridgeview Medical Center have been recognized as 'Rising Stars' by Mpls.St.Paul magazine. This is the eleventh annual listing by the magazine. The 'Rising Stars' danh mục honors exceptional physicians who have been licensed for approximately 10 years or less and are recognized by their peers for providing outstanding care. The recognized physicians span 46 specialties. The selection process for this list is based on peer nominations. The list of recognized physicians includes those from various specialties and associated groups, such as South Valley Anesthesia, Minnesota Urology, Children's Minnesota, Ridgeview's own Orthopedic Surgery and Pulmonology departments, Midwest Radiology, Twin Cities Orthopedics, Kidney Specialists of Minnesota, Minnesota Oncology, Twin Cities Eye Consultants, and Minneapolis Radiation Oncology. May 19, 2025
Ridgeview Hospital in Waconia was mentioned in a medical malpractice lawsuit filed in Hennepin County District Court. According to the lawsuit, a woman initially arrived at Ridgeview Hospital in Waconia with symptoms of a suspected ruptured abscess on her spleen. She was later transferred to Abbott Northwestern Hospital, where a surgery intended to remove her infected spleen allegedly resulted in the removal of her healthy kidney instead. The lawsuit is filed against the surgeon, Allina Health, and Allina Health Surgical Specialists, claiming medical malpractice and battery. Allina Health stated they are reviewing the lawsuit and intend to defend the care provided. [4]
Apr 28, 2025
Effective April 28, 2025, Ridgeview entered into a partnership with Allina Health to enhance and provide expanded outpatient mental health care services. Located at Two Twelve Medical Center in Chaska, the collaboration offers a spectrum of mental health support for adolescents and adults. A key component of these services is the availability of telepsychiatry consultations. This technological integration allows for remote psychiatric support, particularly benefiting Ridgeview's four Emergency Departments by providing specialized crisis management and telepsychiatry access, thereby increasing the accessibility of mental healthcare for patients in need.

Michael Phelps

President and Chief Executive Officer

Michael Phelps was selected as Ridgeview's new President and CEO in 2017. [10, 14] He previously held executive positions including Vice President of Diversified Services (2006-2010), Chief Administrative Officer (2010-2013), and Chief Operating Officer (since 2013). [10, 14] He has also been involved in developing business strategies, expansion projects, clinical partnerships, and leading financial operations.
