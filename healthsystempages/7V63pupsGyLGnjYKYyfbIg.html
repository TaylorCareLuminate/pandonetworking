Recent News

Jun 16, 2025
Universal Health Services (UHS) has launched Hippocratic AI's Generative AI Healthcare Agents. These agents are designed to support clinicians by handling post-discharge follow-up outreach to patients via phone calls. This initiative aims to leverage AI to enhance patient engagement after they leave the hospital. The announcement was made jointly by Hippocratic AI and UHS.
Jun 12, 2025
According to Kim Sassaman, Chief Information Security Officer at Universal Health Services (UHS), the hospital system defends against approximately 7 million cyber threats daily. UHS, based in King of Prussia, PA, employs a team of over 50 cybersecurity professionals and utilizes various technology programs to ward off these attacks. Sassaman highlighted the increasing sophistication of phishing attempts due to large language models like ChatGPT and the resurgence of older social engineering techniques. He emphasized the critical nature of cybersecurity in healthcare due to the potential impact on patient safety.
May 14, 2025
Universal Health Services, Inc. announced that its Board of Directors voted to pay a cash dividend of $0.20 per share on June 16, 2025, to shareholders of record as of June 2, 2025. This is part of the regular dividend payments by the parent company of UHS of Delaware, Inc. [16]
Apr 29, 2025
During Universal Health Services' (UHS) first-quarter earnings call, CFO Steve Filton addressed a dip in behavioral health volumes and uncertainty regarding Medicaid supplemental payments. Despite a 6.7% rise in net revenues year over year to $4.1 billion, same-facility adjusted admissions at behavioral healthcare facilities decreased by 1.6%. Filton attributed this partly to the extra leap year day in 2024 and challenging winter weather. He expressed confidence in the underlying business and reiterated the full-year earnings guidance, noting that labor scarcity issues have largely improved.
Apr 28, 2025
Universal Health Services, Inc. (parent company of UHS of Delaware, Inc.) announced its 2025 first quarter financial results. Similar to previous financial reports, this announcement references the ongoing litigation and claims against the company and its subsidiaries, including those related to Cumberland Hospital for Children and Adolescents in New Kent, Virginia. The financial report highlights that the outcome of this litigation could potentially impact the company's financials.

Thomas Day

Senior Vice President, Finance, Behavioral Health Division
