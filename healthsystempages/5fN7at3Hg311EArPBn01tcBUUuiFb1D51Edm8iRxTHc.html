Recent News

May 08, 2025
Medical Facilities Corporation announced its financial results for the first quarter ended March 31, 2025. The report indicated that facility service revenue remained relatively flat at $81.7 million compared to the same period last year, despite having one less surgical day. Surgical case volumes saw a 2.2% increase. The corporation reported income from operations at $13.0 million and EBITDA at $17.3 million, both showing slight increases year-over-year. A key highlight mentioned was the continued return of capital to shareholders, with $42.3 million from the sale of Black Hills Surgical Hospital proceeds being returned through a substantial issuer bid and normal course issuer bid. The company ended the quarter with a consolidated cash balance of $65.7 million. The full financial statements and management's discussion and analysis were to be filed on SEDAR+ and available on the company's website.

Apr 03, 2025
Rural hospitals in South Dakota, including Brookings Health System, are increasingly questioning the affordability of continuing contracts with Medicare Advantage plans. Hospital leaders, such as Jason Merkley, CEO of Brookings Health System, report ending contracts with major Medicare Advantage companies due to issues with lower reimbursement rates compared to traditional Medicare (76 cents per dollar spent by Medicare Advantage versus 91 cents by traditional Medicare for Brookings in 2023), payment delays, and resistance to authorizing patient care, which they argue jeopardize their finances and ability to provide services. This trend reflects broader challenges faced by healthcare providers in the state regarding reimbursement and administrative burdens from private Medicare Advantage plans, potentially impacting other facilities in South Dakota, although Medical Facilities Corporation's Sioux Falls Specialty Hospital is not specifically named in this article about rural hospitals. Mar 13, 2025
Medical Facilities Corporation reported its financial results for the fourth quarter and full year 2024. The report highlighted a strong year, significantly bolstered by the sale of Black Hills Surgical Hospital. The company's cash position at the end of the year reached $108.5 million. They also returned capital to shareholders by repurchasing $16.6 million in shares and fully repaying their corporate credit facility. Despite a slight decrease in surgical case volumes in Q4 2024 due to factors like physician absences and temporary supply shortages, the company achieved growth in income from operations and adjusted EBITDA. The report also noted that the company's facilities in Arkansas and Sioux Falls were recently recognized as among the top orthopedic hospitals for women by Women's Choice, underscoring their focus on operational efficiency and quality patient care.

David Watson

Chief Financial Officer

David Watson is the Chief Financial Officer at Medical Facilities Corporation. He has 30 years of financial and accounting experience, primarily in multi-site and multi-state U.S. healthcare and institutional operations. Prior to joining Medical Facilities Corporation in June 2019, he served as CFO for Clearway Pain Solutions Institute. From 2011 to 2015, Watson was the Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer for National Surgical Hospitals. His experience includes accounting and financial reporting, mergers and acquisitions, scaling organizations for growth, and navigating capital markets. Watson holds a Bachelor of Arts degree in Economics from the University of Virginia and an MBA in Accounting from Rutgers – The State University of New Jersey.
