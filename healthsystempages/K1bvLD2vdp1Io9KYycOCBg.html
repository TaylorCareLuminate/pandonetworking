Jun 10, 2025
FirstHealth of the Carolinas is pleased to announce that Family Nurse Practitioner Kathy Talbert has joined the care team at FirstHealth Internal Medicine-Hamlet. Talbert, ANCC-FNP, holds a Master of Science in Nursing from Walsh University and a Bachelor of Science in Nursing from Appalachian State University. Jun 09, 2025
FirstHealth of the Carolinas welcomes Jessica Battaglia, PA-C, to FirstHealth Family Medicine – Raeford. Battaglia is a Certified Physician Assistant with several years of experience in both family and urgent care settings. She is particularly passionate about preventative medicine and community-based care. Battaglia earned her Master of Medical Science in Physician Assistant Studies from Methodist University and holds a Bachelor of Science in Chemistry from SUNY Geneseo. Prior to becoming a PA, she served as an EMT and firefighter for about seven years, which instilled in her a deep appreciation for helping others. Battaglia's decision to join FirstHealth Family Medicine – Raeford was driven by her commitment to serving the local community. Her clinical interests include women's health and preventative care, and she believes in building long-term relationships with patients for comprehensive care. FirstHealth Family Medicine – Raeford is located at 313 Teal Drive in Raeford, and appointments can be made by calling (910) 904-2350.
Jun 09, 2025
FirstHealth Pulmonology, in conjunction with Pinehurst Medical Clinic, is pleased to announce the addition of Bradley Icard, D.O., to their team. Dr. Icard is a board-certified specialist in internal medicine, pulmonary medicine, and critical care medicine. He brings extensive experience in lung cancer screening and advanced bronchoscopy, with a strong dedication to improving early detection and patient outcomes. Dr. Icard completed his medical education at Edward Via College of Osteopathic Medicine and his internship, residency, and fellowship at Carilion Clinic-Virginia Tech Carilion School of Medicine. He is a strong advocate for lung cancer screening and has been involved in expanding screening programs. Dr. Icard's vision includes enhancing patient care through the Chest Center of the Carolinas and expanding outreach efforts like the Lung Bus to bring screening services to surrounding communities. He is particularly interested in the future of minimally invasive lung cancer diagnostics. Dr. Icard will be working with the team to further develop a centralized lung screening program and improve the management of incidental pulmonary nodules. To make an appointment with Dr. Icard, call (910) 295-9359.
Jun 02, 2025
FirstHealth Moore Regional Hospital, including its Hoke and Richmond campuses, has been honored with the 2025 Outstanding Patient Experience Award by Healthgrades. This marks the 18th consecutive year that Moore Regional has received this award, demonstrating a consistent commitment to providing exceptional care to patients and their families. The award is based on an analysis of patient surveys from over 3,000 hospitals nationwide, evaluating measures such as staff communication, medication explanations, and hospital cleanliness. FirstHealth leadership, including CEO Mickey Foster and COO Jonathan Davis, expressed pride in their staff, providers, and volunteers for their dedication to patient experience, satisfaction, and safety.
May 29, 2025
For the third consecutive year, FirstHealth of the Carolinas has been recognized as one of the Top 150 Places to Work in Health Care by Becker's Healthcare. This national recognition honors hospitals, health systems, and healthcare companies that prioritize outstanding workplace cultures through comprehensive benefits, inclusive environments, and opportunities for growth. FirstHealth's CEO, Mickey W. Foster, highlighted that this award is a testament to the dedication and compassion of their employees, providers, and volunteers who embody the organization's core purpose of caring for people. Becker's Healthcare noted that top employers like FirstHealth support their teams with resources for a healthy work-life balance, employee engagement, and diversity, reflecting FirstHealth's emphasis on well-being, professional development, and community service.

Executive Leadership

Jenifir J. Bruno, M.D.

Chief Medical Officer

Jenifir Bruno, M.D., was named FirstHealth chief medical officer on December 7, 2020. Dr. Bruno became affiliated with FirstHealth in March 2003 as a hospitalist at Moore Regional Hospital. In July 2008, she joined FirstHealth as assistant medical director for the Hospitalist Program.

Jeffrey A. Casey

Chief Financial Officer

Jeffrey A. Casey has served as the Chief Financial Officer at FirstHealth of the Carolinas since December 2020. He first joined FirstHealth in 1997, holding previous roles including Vice President of Finance and Support Services and Vice President, Finance. Prior to his time at FirstHealth, Casey was the CFO of Columbia Southwest Hospital in Louisville, Kentucky, and the controller of Bluegrass Regional Medical Center in Frankfort, Kentucky. He also served as chief accountant and assistant controller at Southern Hills Hospital in Nashville, Tennessee. Casey holds a Bachelor of Science degree in accounting from Clarion University of Pennsylvania. He is also a graduate of the Moore County Leadership Institute and a member of the North Carolina Healthcare Financial Management Association.

Jonathan S. Davis, MPH, FACHE

Chief Operating Officer and President, Moore Regional

Jonathan S. Davis, MPH, FACHE, is the Chief Operating Officer for FirstHealth of the Carolinas and President of Moore Regional Hospital. He is responsible for the daily oversight of FirstHealth's operations, including Montgomery Memorial Hospital, MRH-Richmond, and MRH-Hoke, and reports to the CEO. With nearly 20 years of healthcare executive experience, Davis previously served as president of Sentara Martha Jefferson Hospital in Charlottesville, Virginia, from 2015 to 2021, where he had strategic and operational responsibilities. His prior roles include president of Methodist Charlton Medical Center in Dallas, Texas, and president/CEO of Via Christi-Mt. Carmel Regional Medical Center in Pittsburg, Kansas. He also has experience with Ascension and CHI-St. Vincent Health System, beginning his career in patient care in the early 1990s. Davis holds a Bachelor of Science from Arkansas State University, a Master of Arts from Northern Arizona University, and a Master of Public Health Administration from Tulane University/Arkansas Medical School. He is a Fellow of the American College of Healthcare Executives.

Bernadette Szczepanski, MBA

Chief People Officer

Bernadette “Berni” Szczepanski, MBA, serves as chief people officer for FirstHealth of the Carolinas. She joined FirstHealth in March 2022.

Dana Casson, CFRE

President, Foundation of FirstHealth

Dana Casson was appointed President of the Foundation of FirstHealth in October 2024. She brings more than 20 years of executive fundraising experience and has held key leadership roles at leading health care organizations.

Amy Graham

Chief Strategy & Innovation Officer

Amy Graham serves as the Chief Strategy & Innovation Officer at FirstHealth of the Carolinas. She earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Business Administration and a Master of Healthcare Administration from the University of North Carolina in Chapel Hill. Graham joined FirstHealth in 1993 and previously held the position of director, business development before becoming chief strategy & innovation officer. She is a member of the American College of Healthcare Executives and actively participates in committees for the North Carolina Hospital Association and the North Carolina Division of Health Service Regulation. In her current role, she is responsible for overseeing the strategy and innovation department, which includes business development, planning, managing professional service transactions, and providing financial, analytics, and performance data for the organization.

Chris Beasley, MBA

Chief Information Officer

Chris Beasley, MBA, is the Vice President, Information Systems/Chief Information Officer (CIO) at FirstHealth of the Carolinas. He joined FirstHealth in 2021. Before his role at FirstHealth, he served as CIO for Houston Healthcare in Warner Robins, Georgia. With over 25 years in the healthcare field, Beasley has also held information systems positions at health systems in Georgia and South Carolina. He earned both his bachelor's degree in business administration, computer-based information systems and his Master of Business Administration from Georgia College and State University. Beasley holds several certifications, including Certified Healthcare Chief Information Officer, Certified Digital Health Executive, Certified Information Systems Security Professional, and is a Fellow in the Healthcare Information and Management Systems Society. He is also a graduate of the Moore County Leadership Institute.
