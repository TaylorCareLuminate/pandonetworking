Recent News

Jun 12, 2025
Health First, based in Rockledge, Florida, has invested over $100 million in implementing a new Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. The system officially went live on June 1, 2025. This significant undertaking aimed to consolidate multiple existing EHR systems into a single unified platform, streamlining clinical workflows and decreasing the organization's reliance on third-party vendors. According to CIO Michael Carr, the project led to the termination of 67 vendor contracts, and the cost of operating the Epic system is expected to be less than the previous suite of software applications. The implementation process involved deep integration with clinical operations, with significant input from nurses, who reported improved access to patient data. The move to Epic was initially announced in 2024. [2]
Jun 10, 2025
An update on the Health First website, dated June 10, 2025, provided information regarding a tragic crane accident that occurred on June 4, 2025, at the construction site of the new Cape Canaveral Hospital. The accident resulted in the deaths of two workers, identified as Isael Martinez and Hector Pozos. Both men were described as dedicated tradesmen working on a concrete column for the project. Health First expressed its deepest condolences to the families and friends of the workers and acknowledged the profound impact of the loss on their loved ones and the entire construction community. Work at the construction site has been paused while authorities conduct their investigation, with Health First fully cooperating and remaining in close coordination with their construction partners who are supporting the affected families and colleagues. Jun 10, 2025
A recent article discusses how private equity-backed healthcare companies, and healthcare providers in general, are navigating evolving tactics by payers. These tactics include delays in contract negotiations, stricter prior authorization requirements, and an increase in claim denials. The article notes that these actions are impacting patient volume and financial performance for provider groups. While the article discusses PE-backed companies primarily, it references 'Health First Health Plans' in a related headline within the same publication, indicating these payer challenges are relevant to the environment in which Health First operates. Providers are reportedly adapting by focusing on operational efficiency, improving claims management, monitoring payer behavior closely, and controlling costs.
Jun 07, 2025
The Health First Foundation has awarded a full scholarship of $8,950 to Michael Gonzalez, a rising senior at Viera High School, enabling him to attend the 2025 Global Youth Leadership Academy in Ireland in July. This scholarship covers all expenses for the program, which focuses on heart-led values, global awareness, and cultural immersion for high school students globally. Paula Just, Health First Chief Administrative Officer, highlighted that this sponsorship aligns with Health First's mission as a local organization committed to investing in the community and developing strong leaders. Gonzalez has a personal connection to Health First, as his grandmother is a retired nurse who worked as a Case Manager for over 15 years at the healthcare system. This initiative by the Health First Foundation demonstrates their commitment to community support and fostering leadership qualities in local youth.

Executive Leadership

Michael Seeley

President, Health First Foundation & Chief Development Officer, Health First system

Michael Seeley joined the Health First Foundation as President in October 2016. He has more than 25 years of experience in building and strengthening comprehensive fund development strategies for both health systems and not-for-profit organizations. As Health First Foundation President, Michael serves as the Chief Development Officer of the Health First system and is responsible for the planning, implementation and coordination of all fundraising activities. His team is also responsible for Community Benefit programs and community partnerships working to support needs identified in the Community Health Needs Assessment. Additionally, he is the Designated Institutional Official for Graduate Medical Education Programs.

Nicholas Romanello

Assistant Secretary

Nicholas Romanello is responsible for handling and coordinating all legal matters for Health First, including all litigation, transactions, contracting, corporate, and regulatory matters, as well as risk management. Prior to joining Health First in 2016, Nicholas served as Chief Legal Officer and General Counsel for the Health Care District of Palm Beach County. He has experience with a wide range of legal and operational issues concerning hospitals and healthcare systems.

Cheyana Fischer

Chief Clinical Officer and Chief Nursing Officer

Cheyana Fischer is the Chief Clinical Officer and Chief Nursing Officer at Health First. She started her nursing career in 2006 at AdventHealth Orlando and joined Health First in 2008 as an emergency department nurse at Holmes Regional Medical Center, specializing in emergency medicine and trauma. Fischer was part of the team that opened Viera Hospital in 2011 and progressed through leadership positions, including director of nursing (2016), Vice President of Nursing (2017), and Hospital Administrator at Viera Hospital (2019), where she was credited with achieving high standards in quality and patient safety. She was named Chief Nursing Officer for Health First's Hospital Division in 2020 and added the role of Chief Operating Officer for the Hospital Division in 2022.

Kent Smith

Health First Board of Trustees' Chair

T. Kent Smith is the Chairman of the Board of Trustees at Health First, a healthcare provider and payor. He also served as Interim CEO in 2023 to lead a financial turnaround. Prior to his role at Health First, Smith was the co-owner, president, and COO of Revolution Technologies for ten years. From 2003 to 2007, he was the president and CEO of DLH, Inc., a public micro-cap focused on the healthcare industry. He also held positions as president of The Honey Baked Ham Company and CEO of the Heavenly Ham Company. Earlier in his career, Smith spent 11 years at Norrell Corporation, a commercial staffing firm. Smith earned his bachelor's degree from Vanderbilt University and his MBA from the University of Virginia. He is currently on the Florida Institute of Technology College of Business Advisory Board and previously served on the Advisory Board of the University of Virginia School of Nursing. He was also an Executive in Residence for Finance at the Georgia State University College of Business.
