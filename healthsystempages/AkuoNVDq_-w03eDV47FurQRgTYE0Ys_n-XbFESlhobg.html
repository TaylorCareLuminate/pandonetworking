Recent News

Jun 02, 2025
Saint Alphonsus Health System, headquartered in Boise, Idaho, which serves a region including parts of Idaho, eastern Oregon, and northern Nevada, has introduced a student loan repayment program for individuals in certain clinical roles. This initiative aims to attract and keep clinicians in positions that are typically challenging to fill across the health system's four hospitals. The program is open to both new hires and current employees who qualify and are pursuing specific careers such as certified registered nurse anesthetists, nurses, surgical technologists, and radiologic technologists. Saint Alphonsus intends to contribute to loan repayments over a three-year employment period for participants. This repayment specifically applies to new debt incurred, not existing debt. According to Brooke Thrasher, chief human resources officer at Saint Alphonsus, this makes Saint Alphonsus the first Trinity Health system to implement such a pilot program. Thrasher highlighted that the program addresses the substantial financial burdens faced by many young adults pursuing healthcare careers, with the goal of alleviating this stress, attracting top talent, and ensuring continued exceptional care for their communities. The program is a collaboration with Clasp, a company focused on bridging the gap between education and employment. [2]
Apr 16, 2025
Saint Alphonsus Medical Center-Ontario has introduced the Altea MR system, which is described as a new cutting-edge MRI technology. This system is intended to provide better imaging capabilities and enhance patient comfort during MRI procedures. The announcement highlights the health system's investment in advanced medical technology to improve diagnostic services and the patient experience in their Ontario, Oregon, facility. The Altea MR system represents an upgrade in imaging technology available to patients in the region served by Saint Alphonsus Medical Center-Ontario. [1, 3]
Jan 22, 2025
Saint Alphonsus Health System confirmed in January 2025 that it holds a contract with Molina Healthcare to provide coverage for dual eligible members (individuals eligible for both Medicare and Medicaid). [4] This confirmation came in the context of Blue Cross of Idaho's contract to serve this population through Idaho Medicaid Plus ending in mid-2025. [4] While Blue Cross of Idaho had partnered with the Idaho Department of Health and Welfare since 2014 to manage the dual-eligible program, the state chose a different direction, leading to Blue Cross of Idaho's contract expiring. [4] The shift to Molina Healthcare as an option for dual enrollees raised questions regarding provider acceptance and how Molina's Medicaid reimbursement rates compare to those of Blue Cross of Idaho. [4] Saint Alphonsus' contract with Molina ensures continued access to care for dual eligible members who choose Molina as their health plan. [4]
Jan 06, 2025
As of January 1, 2025, Saint Alphonsus Health System was no longer part of the Humana Medicare Advantage health care network. This change could result in increased costs for patients covered by Humana Medicare Advantage plans who seek care at Saint Alphonsus hospitals and clinics in locations including Baker City, Ontario, Nampa, and Boise. Both Humana and Saint Alphonsus officials stated their willingness to continue negotiations to reach an agreement that would allow Saint Alphonsus to return to Humana's network. Saint Alphonsus expressed disappointment in the outcome, stating they worked hard to reach a fair agreement covering the true cost of care and did everything possible to protect patient access. Humana indicated they were having good-faith discussions and were open to renewing the contract if it was fair to both sides, while also committing to working with affected members to find new in-network providers.
