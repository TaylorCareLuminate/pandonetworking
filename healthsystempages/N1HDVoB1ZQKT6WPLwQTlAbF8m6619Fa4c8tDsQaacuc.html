Mar 11, 2025
West Suburban Medical Center, part of Resilience Healthcare, is appealing the decision by the Accreditation Council for Graduate Medical Education (ACGME) to revoke its residency program accreditation, which is set to end on June 30. The hospital is working to restore the program, which is the last residency program at the Oak Park hospital. Resilience Healthcare stated in a March 7 press release that they are appealing the decision and reviewing the notification for any errors or inaccurate information. The hospital's CEO, Dr. Manoj Prasad, expressed confidence in defending their record and showcasing the program's strength during the appeals process. Resilience Healthcare also stated that they have invested "millions" into new equipment over the past two years, including computer systems and electronic health records, in addition to dialysis machines and patient monitors. The ACGME has not publicly disclosed the reasons for the revocation, although previous reports cited concerns from residents about staffing shortages, lack of supplies, and management issues impacting patient safety. Resilience Healthcare acquired West Suburban and Weiss Memorial Hospital in 2022 and stated they have since added 200 staff members.
Mar 11, 2025
Crain's Chicago Business reported that West Suburban Medical Center is appealing the ACGME's decision to revoke its residency program accreditation. This loss would result in the termination of the hospital's family residency program, its last remaining one, on June 30. While some residents will graduate, others may need to transfer if the appeal is unsuccessful. Resilience Healthcare, the hospital's parent company, stated they are defending their program and reviewing the ACGME's notification. Resilience Healthcare also accused the former president of the hospital's medical staff of attempting to sabotage the residency program and sent a cease-and-desist letter. Resilience Healthcare claims to have invested millions in new equipment, including dialysis machines, patient monitors, computer systems, and electronic health records, and has onboarded 200 staff members in the past year, reducing open positions. Mar 07, 2025
West Suburban Medical Center, a subsidiary of Resilience Healthcare, announced it will appeal a decision by the Accreditation Council for Graduate Medical Education (ACGME) to terminate the accreditation for its residency program effective June 30, 2025. CEO Dr. Manoj Prasad stated the hospital looks forward to defending its record and showcasing the program's strength during the appeal process, and that they are also reviewing the notification for errors. The current residency program will continue while the appeal is prepared, and support will be provided to residents wishing to transfer. In addition, West Suburban announced the discovery of a year-long effort, spearheaded by the president of the hospital's medical staff, to sabotage operations and move the family medicine residency program to another institution. Resilience's law firm sent a cease-and-desist letter detailing this campaign, which allegedly aimed to create trouble on multiple fronts to force Dr. Prasad to sell West Suburban. The attorneys also reported uncovering efforts to solicit other medical facilities to take over the family medicine residency program sponsored by Resilience, and that Dr. Paul Luning had allegedly been advising faculty to quietly discourage potential residents from joining the program at West Suburban. The letter to Dr. Luning indicated that his actions were considered a clear violation of his fiduciary duties as President of Medical Staff and potentially tortious interference and defamation, reserving the right to file a suit for civil damages. Feb 14, 2025
Elevators at West Suburban Medical Center's River Forest campus buildings, owned by Ramco Healthcare Holdings and leased to Resilience Healthcare, passed inspection on February 11, 2025, for the first time in a year. The inspections had previously failed primarily due to expired fire extinguishers, broken emergency phones, or overdue safety tests. While the buildings are owned by Ramco, whose owner is a business partner of Resilience Healthcare's CEO, Resilience Healthcare leases the property and operates the medical center. The intertwined ownership structure was established in late 2022 when Ramco purchased the real estate and leased it to the newly formed Resilience Healthcare. Despite the recent successful inspection, the article highlights previous infrastructure issues at the campus.
