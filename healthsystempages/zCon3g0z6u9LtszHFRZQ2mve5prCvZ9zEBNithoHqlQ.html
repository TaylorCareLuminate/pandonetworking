Recent News

May 28, 2025
Union Health dedicated new playground equipment at its Child Development Center. This event represents an investment in resources for the center and demonstrates Union Health's commitment to providing support for the children and families they serve in the community.
May 23, 2025
HCA Healthcare, the current owner of Terre Haute Regional Hospital, has indicated that it may consider ending services or closing the hospital if the proposed merger with Union Health is denied by state officials. This statement adds another dimension to the ongoing efforts by Union Health to acquire Terre Haute Regional Hospital under a Certificate of Public Advantage (COPA). The merger has faced significant opposition from federal regulators, including the Federal Trade Commission (FTC), and the Indiana Attorney General, who have raised concerns about the potential for a monopoly, increased healthcare costs, reduced quality of care, and negative impacts on healthcare worker wages. Union Health and Terre Haute Regional Hospital have been pursuing this merger for some time, withdrawing an initial application in November 2024 and filing a new one in February 2025. The FTC has repeatedly urged the Indiana Department of Health to reject the merger, arguing that it poses substantial anti-competitive risks and that claims of the hospital's potential closure without the merger are unlikely based on its financial performance. May 13, 2025
Union Health System, based in Terre Haute, Indiana, has been impacted by a security incident at their electronic health records vendor, Oracle Health/Cerner. The breach, which occurred in January 2025, involved unauthorized access to data hosted in Oracle Health/Cerner's data migration environment. Approximately 262,831 individuals have been affected. The compromised data may include patient names, Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, dates of birth, treating physicians, dates of service, medication information, insurance information, and treatment and/or diagnostic information. Union Health was notified by an unknown party claiming to possess patient data on February 24, 2025, and subsequently verified the information and contacted Oracle Health/Cerner. Oracle Health/Cerner confirmed the cybersecurity event on March 15, 2025, and provided Union Health with a list of affected individuals on March 22, 2025. Union Health is offering affected individuals complimentary credit monitoring services. Union Health has stated that the incident occurred on Oracle Health/Cerner's system and did not involve access to or compromise of any Union Health owned or operated systems, including their live EHR system. At least one federal class-action lawsuit has been filed against Union Health and Oracle Health/Cerner in response to the data breach. [6, 18, 20, 34]

Executive Leadership

Steven M. Holman, FACHE

President & Chief Executive Officer

Steven M. Holman, FACHE, is the President and Chief Executive Officer at Union Health in Terre Haute, Indiana, a role he has held since 2014. He also served as the 2024 Board Chair for the Indiana Hospital Association (IHA). Holman's background in healthcare started in bioengineering as a bio-medical technician, working across different healthcare settings and interacting with physicians and staff. He transitioned into a management role after pursuing business studies and became a CEO of a critical access hospital at age 36. An notable achievement in his career includes serving on the committee that helped establish the critical access hospital system nationwide. Prior to leading Union Health, he was associated with BroMenn Healthcare System.

Jack Hill, FACHE

Chief Operating Officer

Jack Hill, FACHE, is the Chief Operating Officer at Union Health System in Terre Haute, Indiana. He has been in Terre Haute since 2014. Prior to his role at Union Health, Hill served in various capacities in healthcare, including nearly 10 years on active duty in the U.S. Army and 15 years in the U.S. Army Reserves. He holds master's degrees in exercise physiology from Ohio University, physical therapy from Baylor University, and healthcare administration from the University of Alabama at Birmingham. He is also pursuing a doctorate in science in healthcare administration. Hill is involved in the community and is a co-founder and coach of Wabash Valley Crew.

Amy Hock, JD, MSW

Chief Legal Officer

Amy Hock is the Chief Legal Officer at Union Health System, Inc.. She holds a Juris Doctor (JD) from Indiana University and a Master of Social Work (MSW) from New York University. Prior to her role at Union Health, she served as general counsel and vice president at First Financial Bank from 2015 to 2021. Hock also previously served on Union Health's Board of Directors as assistant secretary starting in 2019. She is a native of Terre Haute and is involved in the local community, serving on the boards of the Visiting Nurse Association Home Care and Hospice of the Wabash Valley and Camp Navigate. In 2020, she received the Terre Haute Chamber of Commerce Women's Excellence in Leadership award. She is also a member of the board of directors for First Farmers Financial Corporation.
