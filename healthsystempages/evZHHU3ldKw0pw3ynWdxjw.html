Jun 12, 2025
Wellstar Kennestone Regional Medical Center, located in Marietta, GA, has achieved elite national recognition in cardiothoracic surgery. The hospital earned the highest possible rating, three stars, in four categories evaluated by the Society of Thoracic Surgeons (STS). This places Wellstar Kennestone among the top cardiac surgery programs in the United States and Canada. The STS Star rating system is a highly respected measure of quality in healthcare, based on objective patient outcomes and implemented medical care processes. The recent analysis covered performance from January 2022 to December 2024. Wellstar Kennestone received the three-star rating for isolated coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG), aortic valve replacement (AVR), AVR + CABG, and multiprocedural cardiothoracic surgeries. This is an advancement from their previous two-category three-star rating, solidifying their position as a national leader in the field. [16]
Jun 11, 2025
Wellstar Health System announced on June 11, 2025, that its Digital Care Network is serving as a vital resource for small and rural hospitals. In collaboration with Augusta University's Medical College of Georgia (MCG) Center for Digital Health, this network is enabling residents in rural parts of Georgia to gain faster and more convenient access to essential healthcare resources. The operation of a 'Digital Care Network' inherently involves technology to facilitate remote access and connectivity for healthcare services.

Executive Leadership

Candice L. Saunders, FACHE

President and CEO

Began her career as a critical care nurse, joined Wellstar as president of Kennestone Hospital in May 2007. She was promoted to executive vice president and chief operating officer for Wellstar Health System in 2013. In 2015, she assumed the role of president and CEO. Saunders has built her career around improving community access to vital healthcare needs, implementing new care models and programs, and integrating health delivery processes. She is a champion for delivering world-class healthcare. Under her leadership, Wellstar has been recognized for safety and quality measures, and as a great place to work. She served on the American Hospital Association Board of Trustees and as chair of AHA’s Regional Policy Board for Region 4. She has been recognized by numerous organizations for her leadership and has been named to various prominent lists. Saunders is board certified in healthcare management as a Fellow of the American College of Healthcare Executives and holds master's degrees in business administration and health services administration.
