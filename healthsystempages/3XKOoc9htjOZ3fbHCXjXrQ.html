Jun 13, 2025
Hoag is transforming the future of interventional radiology procedures by becoming the first hospital on the West Coast and the first non-academic hospital in the world to offer MediView's XR90 Augmented Reality (AR) 3D Procedural Navigation System. This innovative “X-ray vision” technology provides clinicians with unprecedented real-time visualization and precision during image-guided procedures. In their first cases, Hoag also achieved two additional global firsts: the use of MediView's AR navigation in a minor procedure room versus a traditional angiography suite or operating room setting, and using the XR90 navigation system for a complex spleen procedure. This breakthrough technology enhances precise execution of minimally invasive, CT- and ultrasound-guided procedures, enabling Hoag's interventional radiologists to clearly visualize internal organs and disease pathology aligned with the patient's anatomy beneath their skin. The clinician's interventional tools can also be tracked in real-time, with a light ray displaying needle trajectory throughout placement and manipulation. The enhanced 3D visualization and tracking capabilities have the potential to make procedures faster while maintaining precision. The technology was funded by Hoag Innovators. [1]

This article from Hoag provides a guide to proactive health measures for men across different age groups, titled "Health Power Moves by Decade." It encourages men to take simple steps for their well-being, advocating that modern strength includes seeking medical advice and getting regular check-ups. The article outlines recommended health practices for men in their 30s, focusing on foundational health checks like blood pressure, cholesterol, BMI, skin, and mental health. For men in their 40s, it suggests knowing key numbers through annual checks for blood pressure, cholesterol, and blood glucose, as well as discussing prostate health and getting eye and hearing exams. For those in their 50s, the focus shifts to detection and protection with recommendations for colonoscopies, heart checks, cancer screenings, and relevant vaccinations. Finally, for men aged 60 and over, the article advises staying strong and sharp through annual wellness visits, cognitive screening, fall risk checks, eye and hearing exams, and necessary vaccines. Jun 12, 2025
Published during Men's Health Month, this article from Hoag aims to reframe the conversation around male fertility, a topic often overlooked despite its significant role in infertility cases. According to the World Health Organization, one in six couples experiences infertility, with men contributing to nearly half of these cases, and the male factor being the sole cause in 20-30%. Hoag emphasizes that male infertility is a medical condition, not a personal failing, and can be an early indicator of underlying health issues. The article advocates for normalizing the discussion, diagnosis, and treatment of male infertility. Hoag offers comprehensive male fertility care, including procedures like microscopic varicocelectomy and vasectomy reversal, sperm retrieval techniques, and hormonal therapies, provided by fellowship-trained urologists specializing in sexual medicine and male fertility.
