Recent News

Jun 12, 2025
Corewell Health in Grand Rapids is now offering a new incisionless brain surgery procedure using focused ultrasound. This technology provides a new treatment option for individuals suffering from essential tremor and tremors associated with Parkinson's disease. The procedure was first performed in Michigan by Corewell Health last month. This non-invasive technique allows for precise targeting of areas in the brain responsible for tremors without the need for incisions, offering a potentially less risky alternative to traditional brain surgery. While not a cure, studies show significant tremor improvement for several years post-procedure, though temporary balance problems can be a side effect. The first patient to receive this treatment at Corewell Health experienced only a few days of brain fog. This represents an expansion of neurological services offered by Corewell Health in West Michigan.
Jun 10, 2025
West Michigan Anesthesia (WMA) provided their perspective on the contract situation with Corewell Health on Tuesday, June 10, 2025. WMA stated that they had made multiple contract proposals over eight months of negotiations, but Corewell Health declined these proposals, leading to the termination of their contract, which is scheduled to end in September. WMA emphasized that as the exclusive anesthesia group at the majority of Corewell Health sites for the past 40 years, their priority has always been to provide safe, high-quality anesthesia care. They reiterated that they made repeated efforts to continue the partnership and offered proposals that would have allowed them to continue caring for patients in the community, but unfortunately, those proposals were not accepted, and Corewell Health ceased negotiations. This statement follows Corewell Health's announcement the previous day regarding surgery reschedulings due to an anesthesiologist shortage and the impending contract termination. [10]
Jun 09, 2025
Corewell Health West Michigan announced on Monday, June 9, 2025, that it is experiencing an anesthesiologist shortage which has forced the rescheduling of some surgeries. According to a statement from Corewell Health, West Michigan Anesthesia is terminating its contract with the hospital in September and is currently not providing sufficient staffing levels. This situation is contributing to the need to reschedule procedures. Corewell Health stated they are actively recruiting and hiring for their anesthesia team to address the issue. The hospital also noted that their teams are working to provide high-quality care during this transition period and they appreciate their patients' understanding. This local issue occurs amidst a nationwide shortage of anesthesiologists, a problem exacerbated by the COVID-19 pandemic which put additional stress on the workforce according to a report by the American Society of Anesthesiologists last year. Jun 05, 2025
An MHA CEO Report from the Michigan Health & Hospital Association (MHA) on June 5, 2025, discusses addressing food and housing insecurity, highlighting its connection to health outcomes. The report mentions that only 20% of health is attributed to medical care, while socioeconomic factors account for 40%. Individuals facing inadequate access to food are at a higher risk for chronic diseases, behavioral health issues, and underutilization of healthcare, potentially leading to increased acute care use in the long term. The report also notes that rising housing costs affect hospital staff, particularly entry-level and non-clinical personnel. While this report tags Corewell Health, it discusses broader issues relevant to many hospitals in Michigan and highlights the MHA's support for related legislative efforts, rather than focusing on a specific recent news event or action taken by Corewell Health in the past week regarding these issues. Jun 04, 2025
Corewell Health, in partnership with Versiti Blood Center of Michigan, is hosting a series of blood drives throughout West Michigan during the month of June 2025. An announcement on June 4, 2025, detailed that there are 26 blood drives scheduled across the region as part of this initiative. Similar blood drives are also scheduled throughout Southeast Michigan and Southwest Michigan in June. These blood drives provide opportunities for community members to donate blood and support the local blood supply. Partnering with Versiti Blood Center of Michigan is a way for Corewell Health to help facilitate blood collection efforts and encourage community participation in blood donation. [2, 3, 8]
