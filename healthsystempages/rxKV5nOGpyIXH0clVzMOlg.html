Recent News

Jun 05, 2025
UCHealth donated $175,000 to Freedom Service Dogs following its annual charity home run derby, Healthy Swings. Freedom Service Dogs is an organization that pairs service dogs with individuals who have physical or intellectual disabilities, significantly enhancing their independence and quality of life. This donation highlights UCHealth's commitment to supporting community organizations and programs that benefit individuals with disabilities and contribute to their well-being and integration into the community.
Jun 05, 2025
UC Health System is currently in ongoing contract negotiations with Blue Shield of California. The current agreement is set to expire on July 9, 2025. If a new contract is not reached by this date, UC Health facilities and professionals, including those at UC Davis Health, will be considered out-of-network for Blue Shield members starting July 10, 2025. This would result in significantly higher out-of-pocket costs for affected patients, or Blue Shield potentially not covering the costs at all. Blue Shield began sending letters to impacted HMO members on May 1, 2025, to inform them of the potential termination and the possibility of being moved to a new primary care provider. UC Health is actively negotiating to reach a new agreement and protect patient access to care. The affected Blue Shield plans include various CalPERS plans, employer-sponsored plans, Covered California marketplace plans, and Medicare Advantage plans administered by Blue Shield of California. Both parties have stated they are continuing to negotiate with the goal of reaching an agreement.
Jun 04, 2025
UCHealth's Burn and Frostbite Center in Aurora is treating three victims of a firebombing terror attack that occurred in Boulder on Sunday. Two of the victims were flown to the Aurora facility due to the severity of their burns. The burn center is one of only around 70 in the United States accredited by the American Burn Association, signifying it provides a high quality of burn care. The nurse manager highlighted the 'all-hands-on-deck' approach when multiple patients arrive and the importance of prompt admission for severely burned individuals. While unable to provide specific patient details due to privacy laws, the report emphasized the center's expertise and accreditation in handling severe burn cases.
May 20, 2025
Estes Park Health has signed agreements to join UCHealth, a move that brings the small critical access hospital closer to becoming part of the larger Aurora-based health system. This partnership is intended to ensure the long-term stability of Estes Park Health. The plan, which requires approval from the Colorado attorney general's office, follows a vote in 2023 where residents overwhelmingly supported the idea of joining a larger nonprofit health system. Upon finalization, Estes Park Health will be rebranded as UCHealth Estes Valley Medical Center, and a new board of directors, primarily composed of local residents, will oversee the hospital. UCHealth plans to offer employment to all Estes Park Health workers and recognize their years of service.
May 12, 2025
UCHealth is extending its partnership with the U.S. Olympic and Paralympic Committee, elevating its role to serve as a national medical center for athletes who live and train in Colorado Springs. This expanded 15-year partnership involves five UCHealth hospitals and numerous clinics in the southern Colorado region providing comprehensive medical care to Team USA athletes. Services will include preventive care, treatment for acute and chronic illnesses, and sports injury management. UCHealth Memorial Hospital Central, as the only Level I trauma center in southern Colorado, will provide a high level of care for critically injured athletes. This collaboration underscores UCHealth's commitment to the athletic community and its role in supporting elite athletes training in the area.
