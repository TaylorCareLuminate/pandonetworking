Jun 16, 2025
St. Mary's Sacred Heart Hospital in Lavonia, formerly Ty Cobb Regional Medical Center, celebrated its 10th anniversary on Friday, June 13, 2025. St. Mary's Health Care System acquired the hospital ten years ago when it was facing financial difficulties and on the verge of closing. A ceremony was held in the hospital's lobby to mark the occasion. Tina Cooper, Vice President of Mission Services for Trinity Health (St. Mary's parent organization), spoke at the event, highlighting the staff's dedication to demonstrating compassion and the love of Christ to patients over the past decade. Trinity President Stonish Pierce also spoke, reading commendations from patients who praised the hospital's care. Pierce recounted how St. Mary's stepped in to save the hospital and keep it open for the community. The acquisition became official on June 1, 2015, with St. Mary's and Trinity Health paying $12.9 million for the 56-bed hospital, its Certificate of Need, grounds, and select related services including Clear Creek OB/GYN, Healthworks, and the Wellness Center. Since the acquisition, Pierce noted that the hospital has continuously worked to improve the services offered to patients. St. Mary's Health Care System also includes the main St. Mary's Hospital in Athens and St. Mary's Good Samaritan Hospital in Greensboro, along with various physician practices, home health/hospice services, a retirement community, and outpatient facilities.
Jun 16, 2025
St. Mary's Sacred Heart Hospital in Lavonia recently celebrated its 10th anniversary as part of the St. Mary's Healthcare system. The hospital marked the occasion with a ceremony on Friday, June 13th, reflecting on the past decade since St. Mary's Healthcare took over the former Ty Cobb Regional Medical Center on June 1, 2015, saving the hospital from financial difficulties. St. Mary's and its parent organization, Trinity Health, acquired the 56-bed hospital, its Certificate of Need, grounds, and several related services for $12.9 million. Hospital officials highlighted the progress made in improving patient services over the past ten years. The acquisition in 2015 was the result of extensive negotiations and allowed St. Mary's Healthcare to assume control without taking on the outstanding debt from the hospital's construction, which was instead absorbed by Franklin County. The celebration acknowledged the efforts of the hospital staff and leadership in continuing to provide healthcare services to the community. [15, 17, 24, 26, 27, 30]

St. Mary's Health Care System announced the recipients of their 2024 annual awards, recognizing three individuals for their exceptional commitment to the system's mission, core values, and dedication to patients, colleagues, and the community. Apr 14, 2025
Dr. Christopher Ward was named Chief Medical Officer (CMO) of St. Mary's Health Care System, effective April 14, 2025. His role includes overseeing the hospitals in Athens, Lavonia, and Greensboro. Dr. Ward brings over 30 years of clinical and leadership experience, having previously served as CMO for several hospitals in the Methodist Healthcare System in San Antonio, Texas. He has focused on improving clinical outcomes, enhancing medical staff collaboration, and empowering teams to maintain high standards of care. Dr. Ward also has extensive experience in emergency medicine, including serving as vice chair of emergency medicine for Steward Good Samaritan Hospital. His stated goal is to improve health outcomes for vulnerable populations through people-first leadership and provide an exceptional patient and family experience by focusing on patient-centered care and enhancing quality. Dr. Ward holds a bachelor's degree from Florida State University, a doctor of medicine degree from the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine, and completed his Emergency Medicine Residency Training Program at Emory University. He is board-certified in emergency medicine and has an MBA from New York University Stern School of Business. [4]
