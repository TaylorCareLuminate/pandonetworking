Recent News

Jun 13, 2025
Blue Cross Blue Shield of Michigan (BCBSM), the state's dominant insurer, is currently engaged in critical reimbursement contract negotiations with Michigan's three largest health systems, including Michigan Medicine, of which UM Health-Sparrow is a part. These talks, highlighted on June 13, 2025, are described as the most consequential in BCBSM's 86-year history, impacting nearly 61% of its total medical spending. BCBSM is seeking to address significant financial losses from the previous year by negotiating the cost of care with large health systems that have gained negotiating power through consolidation, such as Michigan Medicine's acquisition of Sparrow. Hospitals, including those within the UM Health system, argue that they face challenges from what they consider meager reimbursement rates and restrictive prior authorization policies, particularly within Medicare Advantage plans.
Jun 06, 2025
The University of Michigan Health-Sparrow Mobile Health Clinic was scheduled to be at Trinity AME Church, located at 3500 W. Holmes Rd., Lansing, MI 48911, from 1 p.m. to 4 p.m. on Friday, June 6, 2025. The Mobile Health Clinic provides various services to the community, including wellness exams, blood pressure checks, sports physicals, influenza vaccinations (subject to availability), patient education, and information on medical and financial resources. The clinic aims to provide healthcare access to those in the communities who may lack traditional access.
Jun 01, 2025
Effective June 1, 2025, enrollments into the University of Michigan Health Plan's (formerly Physicians Health Plan or PHP) Michigan Care and Michigan Care Advantage health plans ceased. Michigan Medicine, which has majority ownership of the UM Health Plan as part of the 2023 affiliation agreement with Sparrow Health System, announced in late 2024 that these plans would be discontinued entirely after December 31, 2025. The decision was made due to significant financial losses experienced by the health plan over the past few years, as well as challenges posed by the increasingly competitive health insurance landscape and changes to CMS Medicare Advantage plans. This discontinuation affects approximately 64,000 members, including a large number of UM Health-Sparrow employees who utilized these plans, and was a point of discussion during recent labor negotiations with the nurses' union.
Jun 01, 2025
Enrollments into the University of Michigan's Michigan Care and Michigan Care Advantage health plans ceased after 11:59 p.m. on Saturday, May 31, 2025. These plans, which are operated by the UM Health Plan (formerly Physicians Health Plan or PHP), are being discontinued by Michigan Medicine, the parent entity of University of Michigan Health-Sparrow, effective December 31, 2025. This decision, originally announced in late 2024, will require those enrolled in the Michigan Care plans after June 1, 2025 (which is not possible as enrollments have stopped) or those currently enrolled, to select a different health plan during the Open Enrollment period in October 2025 for their 2026 benefits. The discontinuation impacts approximately 64,000 members, including a significant number of UM Health-Sparrow employees who relied on the plan. Michigan Medicine cited financial sustainability and changes in the competitive health insurance landscape as reasons for the discontinuation. Despite the end of new enrollments, healthcare coverage for current members will continue without interruption through December 31, 2025, with all claims for services rendered until that date being processed by the plan. The university stated it will continue to offer other comprehensive health plans to eligible employees and retirees.
