Recent News

Jun 15, 2025
Kaleida Health and its unions, Communications Workers of America (CWA) and 1199SEIU United Healthcare Workers East, have agreed to a second extension of their three-year labor contract. This extension, announced on Friday, June 13th or Saturday, June 14th, runs through Tuesday, June 24th at 11:59 p.m. The previous extension was agreed upon at the end of May. These extensions are in place to cover approximately 8,000 healthcare workers while negotiations for a new long-term agreement continue. A primary focus of the unions during these ongoing talks is the demand for improved staffing levels and enhanced patient care protections. Additionally, workers are seeking a comprehensive package for wages and benefits to aid in the recruitment and retention of experienced staff. The current negotiations cover employees across several Kaleida Health facilities, including Buffalo General Medical Center, Oishei Children's Hospital, Millard Fillmore Suburban Hospital, HighPointe on Michigan, and DeGraff Medical Park. Bargaining sessions are scheduled to continue daily from Monday through Friday.
Jun 10, 2025
Kaleida Health recently convened their Leadership Giving Society members for the 2025 Salute to Excellence event. The purpose of this gathering was to acknowledge and celebrate the significant impact and generous contributions made by these members. This event also served as an opportunity for Kaleida Health to express their gratitude for the support provided by the Leadership Giving Society.
Jun 08, 2025
Buffalo General Medical Center, a Kaleida Health facility, hosted the medical portion of the NHL Scouting Combine this week. Ninety junior and college players underwent various medical assessments including blood pressure, echocardiograms, and orthopedic tests to prepare them for skills and fitness testing. This marks the 11th consecutive year that Buffalo General has served as the location for the medical evaluations ahead of the NHL Draft later this month. The event is a final opportunity for the world's top young hockey prospects to be evaluated by scouts from all 32 NHL teams.
Jun 06, 2025
Towne Health announced a new three-year contract with Kaleida Health to provide valet and shuttle services at three of its key locations in Buffalo, NY: Buffalo General Medical Center, Gates Vascular Institute, and Millard Fillmore Suburban Hospital. This partnership, which began on June 1, 2025, aims to enhance the patient and guest experience by offering seamless arrival and departure services. According to Kelly Corbi, chief operating officer for Kaleida Health, the patient experience starts the moment someone arrives, and they trust Towne Health to handle this crucial first impression. Towne Health plans to utilize advanced parking technology, such as text-to-retrieve and paperless tickets, and has welcomed 27 new associates to their team in Buffalo for these operations. This expansion into Buffalo marks a significant step in Towne Health's growth within the healthcare sector, focusing on delivering exceptional experiences in high-impact environments.
Jun 04, 2025
Kaleida Health highlighted the importance of diversity in healthcare and fostering a workplace where every team member feels valued and supported. In a "Medical Minute" feature, Pam Trevino, Chief Quality and Safety Officer at Oishei Children's Hospital, and Ian Barrett, Chief Human Resources Officer at Kaleida Health, discussed the power of diversity. Shannon Bryant

Chief Diversity and Inclusion Officer

Shannon Bryant, LMHC, was appointed Chief Diversity and Inclusion Officer at Kaleida Health in November 2020. In this role, she is responsible for providing strategic leadership in developing, implementing, and monitoring diversity plans, as well as optimizing organizational culture to be inclusive of all populations served by Kaleida Health. She also works with the system's Employee Resource Groups and Inclusion Council. Before joining Kaleida Health, Bryant was the director of learning, development, and diversity at Person Centered Services, where she was responsible for creating and driving the company's diversity, equity, and inclusion strategy. Prior to that, she served as the director of clinical care coordination at Evergreen Health, overseeing services for the Health Home program. Bryant holds a bachelor's degree in psychology from the State University of New York at Buffalo and a master's degree in health counseling from Canisius College. She is a New York State Licensed Mental Health Counselor, a graduate of Leadership Buffalo, and serves on the boards of the Neighborhood Health Center of WNY and the YMCA Delaware Branch. She was also recognized as a "Top 100 Under 50 Executive and Emerging Leader" by Diversity MBA Magazine in 2024.

Michael P. Hughes

Senior Vice President Chief Administrative Officer

Michael P. Hughes served as the Senior Vice President and Chief Administrative Officer at Kaleida Health for 20 years, having been recruited in 2004. In this role, he was responsible for external affairs, including marketing, communications, and government relations, as well as fundraising, planning, construction, and real estate. He also advised the CEO, executive team, and Board of Directors on policy, strategy, communications, and government relations. Prior to this, his roles at Kaleida Health included chief of staff, senior vice president of marketing, public affairs & business development, and vice president and chief marketing officer. Before joining Kaleida Health, Hughes was the public affairs and marketing manager for Rural/Metro Medical Services, where he managed media, government and community relations, marketing, business development, and contract compliance in upstate New York. He also served as the statewide public information officer for their Disaster Response Team, including during the period following September 11, 2001. Earlier in his career, he was the Director of Communications for Erie County government. Hughes is also a founding partner of Egret Healthcare Ventures, established in 2020, which invests in medical device innovation. He holds a Master of Business Administration degree from Medaille College and a bachelor's in communication from Canisius College. He also completed Harvard Business School's executive program with a focus on healthcare management. Hughes has been recognized as one of Western New York's most influential leaders and has received numerous awards for his work in communications and community service. He has also served on several boards and committees, including the Niagara Frontier Transportation Authority, where he became the youngest Commissioner in its history upon his initial appointment in 2008 and later served as Chair. He is set to join Masiello, Martucci & Associates as a partner in January 2025.

Alyson Spaulding

General Counsel

Alyson Spaulding is the General Counsel at Kaleida Health. She was appointed to this role in March 2015, having previously served as interim general counsel since August 2014. Spaulding has been with Kaleida Health since its inception. In her position as General Counsel, she is responsible for providing primary legal counsel for Kaleida Health and its corporate affiliates. Her oversight also includes Risk Management, Emergency Management, Internal Audit and Corporate Compliance, and Research Management. Before becoming General Counsel, Spaulding was the associate general counsel for Corporate Risk Management at Kaleida Health. In this role, she directed risk management, emergency management, and regulatory affairs, and managed the commercial insurance portfolio and legal services related to professional and general liability. Spaulding is a graduate of the University of Rochester, where she double-majored for her bachelor's degree, and earned her Juris Doctor degree from Syracuse University College of Law, where she was a member of both the Law Review and Moot Court. She was born in Buffalo. In 2020, she was appointed to the New York State Council of Business Leaders in Support of Access to Justice. She has been in the health field for 24 years as of a 2017 article.

Thomas Hull

Senior Vice President, Chief Information Officer

Thomas Hull is the Senior Vice President and Chief Information Officer at Kaleida Health, appointed to the permanent role in January 2022 after serving as interim CIO since February 2021. He is an information technology leader with 30 years of experience. Prior to joining Kaleida Health as Chief Technology Officer, Hull was the CTO for the City University of New York (CUNY). His career includes IT leadership positions in various healthcare, higher education, and academic medical center environments, such as VCU Health, USF Health, Moffitt Cancer Center in Florida, and Pace New York Medical. Hull holds a Bachelor of Arts in computer information science from State University of New York at Potsdam and a Master of Science in computer engineering from Syracuse University. He is also pursuing a Doctorate of Professional Studies in computer science and information technology at Pace University. He has been involved in organizing Kaleida Health's three-year IT strategic plan and the health system's partnership with Infor to move applications to the cloud.

Kenneth Snyder, MD, PhD

Chief Physician Quality Officer

Kenneth V. Snyder, MD, PhD, is the Chief Physician Quality Officer at Kaleida Health. In this role, he oversees physician quality and leads Kaleida Health's Quality & Patient Safety Institute. Prior to this position, Dr. Snyder served as physician advisor, process improvement at Buffalo General Medical Center/Gates Vascular Institute. He is also an Associate Professor of Neurosurgery and an assistant professor of radiology and neurology at the State University of New York at Buffalo (UB). Dr. Snyder joined UB Neurosurgery in 2011 after completing his fellowship training in endovascular neurosurgery at UB and spending six months as a research fellow at the Barrow Neurological Institute. He completed his neurosurgical residency at UB and earned his PhD in biophysics from UB, specializing in mechanoelectric transduction of cellular membranes. He received his medical degree from the State University of New York at Buffalo. His training includes general neurosurgical procedures, with special interests in physiological imaging of the brain, acute stroke management, and endovascular techniques for neurovascular pathology.

Mary Lou Rusin

EVP, Chief Human Resources Officer

Mary Lou Rusin is the EVP, Chief Human Resources Officer at Kaleida Health. She has nearly 30 years of experience in the human resources field. She joined Kaleida Health in 2006 as a corporate recruiter and was later promoted to director of corporate employment and then served as vice president. In October 2019, she was named executive vice president and chief human resources officer for Kaleida Health. Prior to her time at Kaleida Health, she was a human resources director for Clairol. She also owned and operated Klee & Associates, Inc., a distribution company for specialty operating room equipment, and spent 10 years as a general manager for a recruitment firm in Denver, CO. Mary Lou Rusin holds an Ed.D. and is a Registered Nurse. She was also listed as Adjunct Faculty in Graduate Nursing at Daemen University in their 2022-2023 catalog. In a 2012-2013 catalog for Buffalo, she is listed as Mary Lou Rusin, R.N., Ed.D., Chair, Nursing Department, Daemen College.
