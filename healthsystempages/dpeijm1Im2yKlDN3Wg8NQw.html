Oct 04, 2025
In preparation for the influx of visitors for the 2025 NFL Draft in Green Bay, Emplify Health by Bellin is extending urgent care hours. This measure is being taken to prepare for a potential increase in patients during the event, demonstrating Bellin Health's efforts to support the community and visitors during large-scale events.
Jun 16, 2025
Emplify Health by Bellin Green Bay Hospital was recognized with the 2024 Excellence in Tissue Donation Award by UW Organ and Tissue Donation. This award honors hospitals for their significant work in maximizing organ donation, based on metrics such as the true conversion rate of eligible donors. The Excellence in Tissue Donation Award specifically acknowledges partner hospitals that achieve an authorization rate of at least 66% from families regarding tissue donation when approached by care teams. This recognition highlights the collaborative efforts between hospital and tissue bank staff in communicating with patients and families about tissue authorization. Emplify Health's Green Bay hospital was one of 19 hospitals and medical centers recognized by UW Organ and Tissue Donation for their ongoing commitment to saving and improving lives through organ and tissue donation. According to Dr. Scott Rathgaber, CEO of Emplify Health, the care teams go above and beyond to educate and inform patients and families about organ donation, demonstrating extensive work in facilitating organ donation with compassion and efficiency.
Jun 04, 2025
Emplify Health by Bellin and Marquette University have announced the launch of the Resch Mental Health Initiative, a seven-year pilot program designed to address the significant shortage of mental health professionals in Northeast Wisconsin and the Upper Peninsula of Michigan. This initiative is made possible by a substantial gift from The Richard J. Resch Foundation to The Bellin Health Foundation. The program aims to create a fully supported path for 25 students from the region, covering their education through licensure and employment. Participants will receive full tuition and stipend support for an online master's degree in clinical mental health counseling from Marquette University and complete all clinical training components, including paid practicums, internships, and post-graduate fellowships at Emplify Health by Bellin locations. Chris Woleske, Emplify Health Executive Vice President and Bellin Region President, stated that this initiative is a transformative step to ensure communities have access to high-quality mental health care.
Jun 02, 2025
Emplify Health by Bellin has been recognized with a prestigious Environmental Design Research Association's (EDRA) Certificate of Research Excellence (CORE) award. The award was presented on May 29th at the EDRA56 conference in California. This honor acknowledges projects that meet the highest standards in research methodology, innovation, and the practical application of findings. The study conducted by Emplify Health focused on the overall layout, exam rooms, staff work areas, and break rooms within their facilities to improve staff efficiency and patient satisfaction. According to Jen Macdonald, Emplify Health by Bellin Senior Vice President of Specialty Services Lines, the research found that a pod configuration can enhance communication and collaboration among staff while providing a more comfortable and streamlined experience for patients. The outcomes of this study have already informed decisions at the Bellin Health Surgery & Specialty Center and are expected to guide future outpatient facility designs, demonstrating Emplify Health's commitment to evidence-based research and innovation in healthcare.
Jun 02, 2025
Emplify Health by Bellin has been honored with a prestigious Environmental Design Research Association's (EDRA) Certificate of Research Excellence (CORE) award. This award, presented on May 29th at the EDRA56 conference, recognizes projects that demonstrate the highest standards in research methodology, innovation, and the practical application of findings. Bellin's 2023 study focused on comparing linear and pod configurations in orthopedic and total joint service lines at their Titletown and Marinette Clinics. Utilizing observations, surveys, and interviews with staff and patients, the research found that a pod configuration can improve staff communication and collaboration while offering a more comfortable patient experience. The outcomes of this study are being used to inform decisions for future outpatient facilities, including the Bellin Health Surgery & Specialty Center, highlighting Emplify Health's commitment to evidence-based research for advancing healthcare and improving patient care and experience.

Andrea Werner

Chief Population Health Officer

Andrea Werner is the Chief Population Health Officer for Emplify Health (formerly Bellin Health and Gundersen Health System). In her role as Chief Population Health Officer, she is responsible for leading the system's transformation into a population health organization, focusing on creating healthy individuals and thriving communities. She holds a Bachelor of Social Work (BSW) from Marquette University and a Master of Social Work (MSW) from The University of Iowa.
