Jun 06, 2025
Montefiore Nyack Hospital, which is part of the Montefiore Health System, recently honored two physicians as their 2025 Physicians of the Year. Dr. Mathew Lonberg and Dr. Bramdeo Singh were recognized for their long-standing service and commitment to patient care at a reception held on Thursday, June 5, 2025, at the Hilton Pearl River. Dr. Lonberg has been a member of the Montefiore Nyack Hospital Medical Staff since 1988 and is a hematologist and medical oncologist serving as Chief of the Section of The Hematology and Oncology Center at the hospital. Dr. Singh began his medical career as the co-founder of the Hospitalist Program at Putnam Hospital Center and was named medical director of the Sound Physicians Hospitalist Program at Montefiore Nyack Hospital in 2017. He also serves as Chair of the Internal Medicine Sub-Internship Program at Montefiore Nyack and is an Associate Professor at the Albert Einstein School of Medicine. This award acknowledges their significant contributions to patient care and the hospital.
Jun 04, 2025
Montefiore Medical Center experienced an operating loss of 0.2% in the first quarter of 2025, according to its unaudited financial statement released the week prior to June 4th. This loss, amounting to $3.5 million, was primarily driven by increased spending on employee salaries and benefits, which pressured the hospital's budget. [4] Despite a 6% increase in total revenue to $1.4 billion in the first quarter, including a significant boost from government funding through a Medicaid pilot program, expenses also rose by 6%. [4] Wage costs specifically increased by 8% due to hiring more full-time staff to reduce reliance on contract labor, and benefit spending went up by 11% to comply with union contract requirements. [4] The hospital's recent financial performance is similar to the previous year's losses, but potential federal health care cuts could lead to larger deficits, especially as 85% of Montefiore's patients rely on government health plans. May 02, 2025
The Montefiore School Health Program (MSHP) has opened its 34th in-school health clinic at IS 229, which will also serve students from the neighboring PS 274 in the Bronx. This new clinic utilizes a nursing-led model to provide accessible medical, mental, dental, vision, and community health services to approximately 525 students at no cost to their families. The program aims to reduce health-related absenteeism and provide essential care within the school building, offering services ranging from immunizations and physicals to the management of chronic illnesses like asthma and diabetes, as well as mental health support. This expansion is part of Montefiore's ongoing commitment to bringing healthcare directly into the community and supporting the health and academic success of Bronx students, now serving around one in four students across the borough through the MSHP. Loreen Babcock

Senior Vice President & CMO

Loreen Babcock is the Senior Vice President and Chief Marketing Officer at Montefiore Health System. She is an award-winning marketing leader known for applying social science and behavior change models to marketing. Before joining Montefiore, Babcock founded two marketing agencies, LLKFB/Unit7 and Idevoita. She sold LLKFB/Unit7 to Omnicom in 2000 and served as its Chairman and CEO. Prior to founding her own agencies, she held senior leadership positions at advertising agencies including Ogilvy, Saatchi & Saatchi, and Lowe. Babcock has experience building brands across various sectors, including healthcare, technology, financial, and energy, working with companies such as Pfizer, Procter & Gamble, and Johnson & Johnson. She also has prior healthcare marketing experience from her time at Brigham and Women's Hospital and Boston Children's Hospital.

Elizabeth Kaledin

Vice President of Public Relations of Montefiore Health System

Elizabeth Kaledin is the Vice President of Public Relations at Montefiore Health System, a role she began in June 2016. Before joining Montefiore, she had a notable career in journalism, including serving as a CBS News correspondent based in New York from 1996, where she reported on various medical stories. Her experience also includes working for CBS Newspath and as a weekend news anchor and environmental reporter for New York 1 News, which she initially joined at its inception in 1992. Kaledin returned to NY1 in 2007 to co-anchor the "News at Eleven". Earlier in her career, she worked as an anchor, producer, and reporter at television stations in Vermont and California. In 2014, she transitioned into public relations, taking on a role at the Central Park Conservancy. She is a graduate of Vassar College, where she earned a Bachelor of Arts in English in 1984, and also pursued studies at the University of London and Universidad Autonoma De Mexico.

Colleen M. Blye

Executive Vice President & Chief Financial Officer

Colleen M. Blye is the Executive Vice President, Chief Financial Officer, and Chief Business Officer at Montefiore Health System. She joined Montefiore in 2016 as CFO and assumed the additional Chief Business Officer title in 2024. Blye is responsible for the financial strategy, reporting, investments, performance, revenue cycle, and supply chain across Montefiore and Albert Einstein College of Medicine. Prior to her time at Montefiore, she served as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer at Catholic Health Services of Long Island, an integrated healthcare system. Her responsibilities there included treasury management, revenue cycle, financial reporting and planning, third-party contracting, supply chain, accounts payable, payroll, and information technology. Earlier in her career, she was the Executive Vice President for Finance and Integrated Services at Catholic Health Initiatives, a health system with numerous hospitals across multiple states. With over 25 years of leadership experience in large, complex healthcare organizations, Blye is a Certified Public Accountant since 1984 and a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants and the Healthcare Financial Management Association. She holds a Bachelor of Science degree in accounting from the University of Delaware.

Maureen Scanlan

Senior Vice President & Chief Nursing Executive

Maureen Scanlan, MSN, RN, NEA-BC, is the Senior Vice President and Chief Nursing Executive at Montefiore Health System. She has over three decades of experience at Montefiore, beginning her career as a bedside staff nurse in 1992 and progressing through various leadership roles before her appointment as Senior Vice President of Nursing in 2022. Ms. Scanlan provides strategic oversight of nursing across the Montefiore health system and has a background in nursing informatics. She is recognized for implementing data-driven initiatives to improve patient care and outcomes. Her accomplishments include leading efforts to enhance patient safety through evidence-based practices, serving as Principal Investigator on a study focused on reducing patient falls, and coordinating the design of a nursing strategic plan. Under her leadership, Wakefield Hospital achieved Magnet® designation. Ms. Scanlan holds a Bachelor of Science and Master of Science degrees in Nursing from the College of Mount Saint Vincent, where she also served as Adjunct Faculty. She obtained a Post Master's Certificate in Nursing Informatics from Duke University and holds certifications in Nurse Executive Advanced Certification and Nurse Certification in Informatics from the American Nurses Credentialing Center. She also serves as an honorarium lecturer at Teachers College, Columbia University.
