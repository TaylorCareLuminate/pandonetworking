Recent News

Jun 16, 2025
Dr. Alexander Olawaiye, a professor of gynecologic oncology at the University of Pittsburgh and Magee-Womens Hospital of UPMC, presented findings from the Phase 3 ROSELLA trial at the 2025 American Society of Clinical Oncology (ASCO) Annual Meeting. The study investigated the combination of relacorilant and nab-paclitaxel for patients with platinum-resistant ovarian cancer. Results indicated that this combination significantly increased progression-free survival and was well-tolerated, showing no new safety signals. Dr. Olawaiye highlighted that this oral therapy is more convenient for patients, allowing them to spend less time at treatment facilities. The trial demonstrated a 30% reduction in the risk of progression or death compared to nab-paclitaxel alone, regardless of biomarker status, positioning the combination as a potential new standard of care.
Jun 13, 2025
Olivera (Olja) J. Finn, Ph.D., a distinguished professor of immunology and surgery at the University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine and UPMC Hillman Cancer Center, was honored with the 2025 PNC Elsie Hillman Distinguished Scholar Award. This award recognizes her decades of significant research in immunology, particularly her pioneering work on developing a vaccine aimed at preventing several human cancers, including breast cancer. Dr. Finn is credited with identifying a human tumor antigen, MUC1, and demonstrating through preclinical research that immune responses against MUC1 can reject tumors. This discovery has led to clinical trials for a preventative breast cancer vaccine. The award, now in its 14th year, celebrates advancements in cancer research and treatment and individuals supporting these efforts at UPMC Hillman Cancer Center.
Jun 11, 2025
A new study led by University of Pittsburgh researchers, involving the UPMC Sports Medicine Concussion Program, evaluated the effectiveness of the Sport Concussion Office Assessment Tool 6 (SCOAT6) and other assessments for evaluating athletes after a concussion. Published in the journal Sports Medicine, the findings suggest that while some components of the SCOAT6, such as symptoms, the modified Vestibular Ocular Motor Screening (VOMS), and timed tandem gait, were useful in identifying sports-related concussion, other components like brief cognitive tests and balance tests were not effective. The study, involving 134 athletes with recent concussions and 67 healthy controls, highlights the importance of using a multidomain assessment approach that includes symptoms, VOMS, computerized neurocognitive testing, and timed tandem gait for more accurate concussion assessment in clinical settings.
Jun 10, 2025
UPMC Jameson is expanding local behavioral health services in the Lawrence and Mercer County regions with the planned opening of a new 11-bed adult behavioral health inpatient unit. Construction on the unit is expected to begin in June 2025 and is scheduled to open in 2026. This initiative, supported by a $7.5 million grant from Southwest Behavioral Health Management, Inc., is part of UPMC's commitment to addressing the community's need for accessible mental health care, identified in the Community Health Needs Assessment. The new unit at UPMC Jameson will work in partnership with experts from UPMC Western Behavioral Health. This expansion complements the recent opening of an intensive outpatient program (IOP) in New Castle, providing a continuum of mental health care options closer to home for residents.

Leslie C. Davis

President and Chief Executive Officer, UPMC

Timothy R. Billiar, MD

Executive Vice President, UPMC, Chief Scientific Officer

Timothy R. Billiar, MD, is the Executive Vice President and Chief Scientific Officer at UPMC. He also holds the position of George Vance Foster Professor and Chair in the Department of Surgery at the University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine, a role he has held since 1999. Dr. Billiar earned his medical degree from the University of Chicago Pritzker School of Medicine and completed his surgical residency at the University of Minnesota and the University of Pittsburgh. His work focuses on clinical and research advancements, particularly in the areas of immune response to injury and shock, liver disease, and innate immunity. His laboratory is recognized for initially cloning the human inducible nitric oxide synthase gene. He is also a member of the National Academy of Medicine and the National Academy of Sciences.
