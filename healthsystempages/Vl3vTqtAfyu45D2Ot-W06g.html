Jun 12, 2025
During a chamber breakfast held on June 12th, Central Maine Healthcare's CEO and President, Steve Littleson, reportedly did not provide comments regarding the hospital system's ongoing system outage. This indicates that while restoration efforts are underway and updates are being provided through other channels like the temporary website, the CEO chose not to address the situation publicly at this specific event. The system outage stems from a suspected cyber incident that began impacting CMHC's network around June 1st, leading to significant disruptions in IT systems and phone communications. [9]
Jun 11, 2025
Central Maine Healthcare (CMHC) has been actively working on restoring its systems following a suspected cybersecurity issue detected in its computer network around June 1st. According to reports from June 11th and 12th, the healthcare system is making progress in getting its IT applications and hardware back online after staff immediately secured and turned off systems in response to the unusual activity. This disruption had affected network servers and phone systems. CMHC has reported that many phone lines have since been restored, allowing patient calls to be accepted again at Central Maine Medical Center, Bridgton Hospital, Rumford Hospital, and primary care practices. National IT experts are assisting CMHC's internal IT personnel in these restoration efforts, aiming to bring services back online as quickly as possible. The hospitals remain open and are continuing to provide care to patients, though some services may have limited capability. [3]
May 12, 2025
Central Maine Healthcare (CMH) has partnered with Anthem Blue Cross and Blue Shield in Maine to provide expanded access to routine colorectal cancer screenings at Bridgton Hospital. This partnership, which began on May 5, involves Central Maine specialist physicians offering routine colonoscopy services to Anthem members on an expanded weekly schedule. The initiative aims to make these critical preventive screenings more accessible for individuals in rural areas of western Maine and eastern New Hampshire who might otherwise face difficulties accessing care due to their location. Denise McDonough, president of Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield in Maine, highlighted the importance of this partnership in addressing the fact that many people who need colon cancer screenings do not receive them due to lack of access. Prior to this expansion, routine non-hospital-based screenings for a large portion of Maine were primarily available through Portland Gastroenterology. This collaboration is expected to help reduce the backlog of patients needing screenings. [5, 8, 9]
Apr 22, 2025
During a public discussion regarding the proposed acquisition of Central Maine Healthcare by Prime Healthcare Foundation, CMHC CEO Steve Littleson highlighted the health system's significant reliance on government payers, stating that approximately 70% of CMHC's business comes from this source, including MaineCare. This underscores the importance of favorable reimbursement rates from government programs for CMHC's financial health and sustainability. Littleson indicated that CMHC needs external support to keep up with its challenges, including debt and aging infrastructure, which led to the pursuit of an acquisition partner like Prime Healthcare Foundation. The acquisition, if approved, is expected to involve a significant investment from Prime Healthcare Foundation into CMHC facilities over the next five years.
Mar 13, 2025
Central Maine Medical Center (CMMC) received a generous donation of $10,000 from cPort Credit Union, which is preparing to open a new location across the street from the hospital on Main Street in Lewiston. This donation was made to support CMMC's Corporate Partnership Program and demonstrates cPort Credit Union's commitment to community well-being even before its official opening. The credit union expressed excitement about partnering with CMMC and contributing to the strong sense of community in the Lewiston-Auburn area. CMMC expressed gratitude for the contribution, highlighting its impact on their ability to provide vital services and resources to patients and team members.
