Recent News

Jun 11, 2025
Avera Health hosted a Behavioral Health Symposium on Wednesday, June 11, 2025, at the Hilton Garden Inn Downtown in Sioux Falls. The symposium ran from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The event included various sessions and speakers focusing on behavioral health topics. The agenda included an opening remarks session by Thomas Otten, LPC-MH, AVP at Avera, followed by a presentation on "Transforming Trauma: The Path to Hope and Healing" by James Gordon, MD. Other sessions covered topics such as culturally and ethically responsive interventions, and the ethical and mental health issues related to social media and smartphone technology. The symposium was aimed at various professions including Addiction and Prevention Professionals, Counselors/Therapists, Nurses, Physicians, Psychologists, and Social Workers. The event aimed to examine evidence-based strategies to address burnout, secondary traumatic stress, compassion fatigue, moral injury, and vicarious trauma, review the mental health hold process in South Dakota, and discuss the impact of harm reduction and low-threshold addiction treatment. It also intended to cover ethical practices in deploying AI in healthcare and the importance of collaboration among mental health providers.
Jun 05, 2025
As of next year, healthcare services provided by Avera Health physicians in South Dakota will no longer be covered under insurance plans from the Sanford Health Plan. Avera's Chief Medical Officer, Dr. Tad Jacobs, acknowledged that this change could be a source of stress for patients. Rob Bates, Avera's vice president of managed care services, stated that the organization has studied the potential impacts of this decision. Dr. Jacobs added that Avera will work with individuals to help them find appropriate coverage in their local area. According to Bates, these changes are reflective of broader shifts occurring in healthcare coverage across the United States. [8]
Jun 02, 2025
Avera McKennan Hospital & University Health Center in Sioux Falls has received Heart Failure Advanced Certification (HFAC) from DNV. This certification signifies Avera McKennan's dedication to providing high-quality, evidence-based care for patients with heart failure. The certification process evaluates an organization's excellence in the diagnosis and treatment of heart failure, encompassing initial diagnostic services and various therapies. It also includes the implementation of clear metrics to assess program outcomes and a Quality Management System focused on continuous improvement in patient care and safety. According to Dr. Ronald Place, Regional President and CEO of Avera McKennan, this achievement validates the hospital's multidisciplinary approach and clinical excellence in cardiac care, aiming to enhance patient outcomes and strengthen their standing as a leader in the field. [9]
Jun 02, 2025
Avera has offered to donate its former hospital site in Gregory, South Dakota, for the development of a new aquatic center. The demolition of the old hospital, located on the west side of Gregory, was completed in the spring. Tony Timanus, CEO of Avera Gregory Hospital, stated that the donation was considered after recognizing the existing fundraising efforts for a future aquatic center in Gregory. The goal is that this land donation will help make the community's dream closer to reality. The Gregory City Council has accepted Avera's offer, which involves 12 acres valued at $96,000 and is valid for five years to allow for continued fundraising and planning. This follows the opening of the new Avera Gregory Hospital health campus in early 2023, located east of Gregory, which includes a hospital, long-term care center, and clinic. [8, 14]
May 25, 2025
Comvest Credit Partners announced it has provided a senior secured credit facility to support the carve-out of Avel eCare from Avera Health by Aquiline Capital Partners. Avel eCare, based in Sioux Falls, S.D., is described as a national leader and one of the world's largest virtual care providers, delivering two-way video telehealth services to a wide array of healthcare facilities across the country, including health systems, rural hospitals, and long-term care facilities. This investment from Comvest supports Aquiline Capital Partners' acquisition of the Avera eCare telehealth unit, which was initially announced in July 2021. [16]
