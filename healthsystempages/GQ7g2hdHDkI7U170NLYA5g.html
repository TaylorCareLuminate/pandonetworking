Jun 16, 2025
Recent reports, including one on June 16, 2025, mention Tampa General Hospital's settlement of a class action lawsuit related to a 2023 cyberattack. Tampa General Hospital agreed to a $6.8 million settlement to resolve claims stemming from the data breach that affected the protected health information of over 2 million individuals. The lawsuit alleged negligence on the hospital's part for failing to implement adequate cybersecurity measures to prevent unauthorized access to patient data. While Tampa General Hospital denied wrongdoing, the settlement was reached to conclude the litigation and avoid further legal costs and uncertainty.
Jun 11, 2025
Tampa General Hospital is implementing its hurricane preparedness plan for the 2025 season by once again utilizing the AquaFence system. Hospital leaders discussed their strategy during a news conference and tour on Wednesday, June 11, 2025. The AquaFence serves as the hospital's primary defense against storm surge, having successfully protected the facility from flooding during Hurricane Helene previously. This year, the hospital is enhancing its protection by adding a new interior AquaFence to safeguard critical lower-level departments, including the emergency center, surgery services, and the sterile processing department. The AquaFence can reach up to nine feet in height and is bolted into the ground, designed to protect against a 15-foot storm and 140-mile-an-hour winds; the system is strengthened by the pressure of incoming water. In addition to the physical barriers, the hospital ensures a sufficient supply of medical supplies, pharmaceuticals, food, water, and linen are brought in before a storm to care for patients, with plans to receive over 10 semi-truck loads of supplies. Dr. Chang is responsible for designing and implementing strategies to enhance clinical quality, efficiency, and care coordination while reducing costs. He focuses on leveraging technology and strategic partnerships to improve efficiency, patient-centricity, growth, and value creation. A notable achievement under his guidance is the expansion of the Hospital at Home program, which has shown significantly lower readmission rates and higher patient satisfaction compared to traditional inpatient care. Dr. Chang's contributions also include the development of a new platform that integrates real-time situational awareness and advanced workflows to support care teams and automate non-clinical tasks, allowing providers to focus on patient care.
