Recent News

Jun 10, 2025
Nurses at Northwell Health/Huntington Hospital, represented by the New York State Nurses Association (NYSNA), held an informational picket on Tuesday, June 10, 2025. The nurses are advocating for a fair contract that includes safe staffing standards, a plan to retain experienced nurses, and respectful wages and benefits. They have been in negotiations with Northwell Health management for months, with discussions reportedly stalled after failing to meet a March 31 deadline. The nurses are demanding an enforceable staffing grid in their contract, beyond the state's safe staffing law, and claim local hospital administrators have refused to agree to their plans, citing pressure from Northwell Health administration. Safe staffing, particularly in the emergency room, is a major concern for the nurses who are speaking out to demand respect for patients, nurses, and the community. Huntington Hospital stated it respects the nurses' right to picket and that normal hospital operations would continue uninterrupted.
Jun 10, 2025
Northwell Health's annual "Walk to Raise Health" event, which took place on May 18, 2025, across five locations, successfully raised a record-breaking $1.27 million. More than 6,100 participants, including patients, community members, and Northwell employees, joined the walks at Jones Beach, Port Jefferson, Riverhead, Westchester, and Staten Island. The funds raised will support various local hospital programs and services across Northwell Health's network, including initiatives in women's health, cancer care, and pediatric mental health. This year's event marked the third consecutive year of setting new records for both fundraising and attendance, highlighting the strong community support for Northwell Health's mission.
Jun 08, 2025
Dr. Aparna Oltikar, a physician at Danbury Hospital, which is now part of Northwell Health following the merger with Nuvance Health, has been granted a $2 million endowment. This endowment is intended for use at her discretion to advance internal medicine. Dr. Oltikar has a particular interest in serving underserved communities and plans to discuss with community members how best to utilize the funds. She aims to make medical services more mobile and community-based and invest in programs that improve outcomes for patients with complex challenges, emphasizing that factors like immigration status or race should not determine healthcare outcomes. This endowment is the 19th for Nuvance and is notable for recognizing the field of internal medicine. Dr. Oltikar highlighted that while highly technical fields often receive endowments, this one focuses on a crucial, often overlooked area of clinical care.

Jun 06, 2025
A False Claims Act complaint has been filed alleging that Northwell Health improperly billed Medicare, Medicaid, and other federal healthcare programs for inpatient admissions at North Shore University Hospital (NSUH). The complaint claims that NSUH physicians routinely admitted patients as inpatients when emergency department treatment or observation would have been more appropriate for their conditions. Specific diagnoses cited in the complaint include chest pain, pneumonia, abdominal pain, transient ischemic attack, urinary tract infection, sepsis, syncope, and impotence. The complaint also alleges that some patients were wrongly kept for three days as inpatients before being discharged to a skilled nursing facility (SNF) to qualify for Medicare Part A coverage for the SNF stay. Northwell Health plans to file a motion to dismiss the complaint, stating it does not meet the requirement to plead fraud with particularity, although an amended complaint has been filed. Northwell has until June 27 to respond or renew its motion to dismiss.
