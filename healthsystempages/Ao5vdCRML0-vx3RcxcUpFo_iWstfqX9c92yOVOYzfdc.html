Recent News

Jun 12, 2025
Saint Francis Health System announced on Thursday, June 12, 2025, that it has reached an agreement with Dallas-based Integrative Emergency Services (IES) to provide physicians and advanced practice providers for its six emergency rooms. This transition is expected to be implemented in approximately 90 days. IES is described as a physician-led organization with a strong reputation for clinical excellence and operational efficiency, handling over 2 million patient visits annually. IES leadership has already commenced meetings with the current Saint Francis emergency room physicians and providers to facilitate the transition process. Barry Steichen, Saint Francis Health System Executive Vice President and Chief Operating Officer, stated that this partnership is part of Saint Francis' commitment to providing high-quality emergency care in an evolving healthcare landscape. Saint Francis previously partnered with US Acute Care Solutions (USACS) for these services and thanked them for their 17 years of collaboration. IES Chief Medical Officer Dr. John S. Garrett expressed IES's commitment to partnering with hospitals and care teams to deliver exceptional patient care. Saint Francis operates emergency rooms at its main hospital (Adult and Pediatric ERs), Saint Francis Hospital South, Saint Francis Glenpool, Saint Francis Hospital Muskogee, and Saint Francis Hospital Vinita.
Jun 11, 2025
Saint Francis Health System and the Oklahoma State University (OSU) Medical Authority are partnering to establish a Level I Trauma Center in Tulsa. This collaboration is made possible through recently passed state legislation, House Bill 2784 and House Bill 2793, which create a formal public-private partnership for the development of a Level I Trauma Center in Eastern Oklahoma. According to the American Trauma Society, a Level I Trauma Center provides comprehensive care for all aspects of injury, from prevention through rehabilitation. This new center is expected to significantly enhance access to the highest level of emergency care for residents in Eastern Oklahoma, potentially reducing the need to transport critically injured patients to Oklahoma City or other distant locations. Officials emphasized that having a Level I Trauma Center in Tulsa will mean faster, more coordinated emergency response, which is crucial when dealing with life-threatening injuries. Saint Francis has been working towards establishing a Level I Trauma Center for over a decade and is currently in the final stages of preparing for adult Level I Trauma verification by the American College of Surgeons, with a survey anticipated this year. Following the adult verification, they will pursue pediatric Level I Trauma verification. The partnership with OSU Medical Authority will also support the training of future physicians. Leaders from both Saint Francis and the state highlighted the importance of this development for patient safety and access to care closer to home.
Jun 02, 2025
An article published on June 2, 2025, highlights the dedication of longtime employees at Saint Francis Health System in Tulsa, Oklahoma, and their reasons for their loyalty to the organization. Two married couples, who have worked for the health system for many years, shared their positive experiences, emphasizing that Saint Francis takes care of its employees, which in turn enables them to provide better care for patients. One employee, who has been with the hospital for 42 years, described Saint Francis as the "Cadillac of the city" and praised its strong relationship with the community and its reputation as a respected healthcare system. The employees also mentioned the opportunities for growth and education provided by the system and the availability of mental health support for staff who witness traumatic situations. This piece reflects positively on the health system's internal culture and its perceived standing within the community, touching on aspects of community relations through the lens of employee satisfaction and the system's role in the area.
May 30, 2025
Warren Clinic, part of Saint Francis Health System, is expanding its services with a new location coming soon to Claremore, Oklahoma. The new clinic, located at 1340 S. Lynn Riggs Blvd., is expected to open in late summer 2025. This expansion aims to provide increased access to primary and specialty care for the growing community in Rogers County, marking a significant step in Saint Francis Health System's mission to deliver high-quality, compassionate care closer to where patients live. Dr. Brent Dennis, Chief Medical Officer of Warren Clinic, stated that bringing the trusted name of Warren Clinic to Claremore is a meaningful opportunity to serve more patients with the health system's defined level of excellence and personalized care. The new clinic will offer comprehensive, team-based care supported by the full resources of Saint Francis Health System, including hospital services, advanced diagnostics, and specialty care. Leadership from Warren Clinic expressed enthusiasm about becoming part of the Claremore community and building strong relationships with patients and families.
May 15, 2025
Saint Francis Health System is making significant progress on three major construction projects across eastern Oklahoma, which are set to reshape healthcare in the region and create nearly 700 permanent jobs. These projects include a $70 million expansion at Laureate Psychiatric Clinic and Hospital, adding 60 new acute psychiatric beds, a crisis center, renovated administrative spaces, and an expanded kitchen and dining area, with completion expected by the end of 2026. Saint Francis Hospital South is undergoing a $258 million expansion that will add 283,600 square feet of new and 51,000 square feet of renovated clinical space, including a new 120-bed patient tower, expanded surgery services with eight operating rooms, consolidated pre- and post-anesthesia units, and expanded inpatient physical medicine and rehabilitation services, scheduled for completion in 2026. Additionally, Saint Francis Hospital Muskogee is benefiting from a $275 million expansion that will add a new seven-story patient bed tower with 158 new patient beds, featuring an expanded 30-bed Intensive Care Unit and a 32-bed Progressive Care Unit, along with additional patient and visitor parking, slated for completion later in 2025. These projects underscore Saint Francis Health System's commitment to enhancing healthcare facilities and services in the communities it serves.
