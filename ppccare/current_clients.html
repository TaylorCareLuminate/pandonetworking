How overrides work:
  • Default — no override; the org follows its normal Active/Inactive status in Bullhorn.
  • Force Include — the org appears on all listing pages even if Inactive in Bullhorn.
  • Force Exclude — the org is hidden from all listing pages even if Active in Bullhorn.

Full override control (Default / Force Include / Force Exclude) requires the org to be on the KARE list. Orgs in the customer list but not on KARE can still be Force Excluded directly from Section 2 below.

Combine organizations for reporting

Choose the organization name to keep, then select one or more historical aliases. Question Summary will show their combined task history, responses, and notes under the kept name. No tasks or source organizations are deleted.
