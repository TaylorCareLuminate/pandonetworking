When to use this Use this when the numbers don't add up — e.g. you submitted 90 messages but only saw 23 show up for customer review. The orange scanner below only looks at messages that are still approved and not deleted, so it will show "no orphaned messages" even when most of your batch was auto-deleted (superseded duplicates, HeyReach duplicate auto-delete, exclusions, etc.). This tool queries every connect_queue record for a BDR — deleted or not — in a time window, and sorts each one into exactly what happened to it.
Defaults to the last 24 hours for the selected BDR. Widen the range if you're not sure exactly when you submitted.

Fix Records After BDR Email Change
When to use this After changing a BDR's primary email in Email Controls, historical records (connect queue, activity, HeyReach data, etc.) still reference the old address. This tool scans those collections and rewrites the email field so the records stay linked to the correct BDR. Routing/config collections are also re-synced.

Updates linkedin_accounts, linkedin_email_associations, and harvest pool collections — fixes the "LinkedIn Email Not Configured" warning and restores correct HeyReach routing for the new address.

What this page does
  • Scans connect_queue for messages that were approved/pushed within the date range you choose.
  • Flags any whose BDR had no LinkedIn email association at the time (or still doesn't), meaning they may not have been routed to HeyReach correctly.
  • Shows you the full message text and contact info so you can manually re-send or take action.
  • To fix the root cause: set up LinkedIn emails in Email Controls →
