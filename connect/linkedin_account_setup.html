LinkedIn Account Setup

Tee up a BDR to connect their LinkedIn account to HeyReach. Choose the company and BDR, enter their LinkedIn 2FA authenticator key (saved with the invite), then send them the link. They sign in on a public page; their username and password go straight to HeyReach and are never stored by us. If LinkedIn emails or texts them a verification code, the page asks for it and relays it to HeyReach.
