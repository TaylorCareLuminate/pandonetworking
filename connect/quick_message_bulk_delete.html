Quick Message Bulk Delete

Select one or more BDRs and remove every contact from their Quick Message campaigns, regardless of age. For the age-based, one-BDR-at-a-time version, use Quick Message Cleanup.

Each contact is stopped in HeyReach and purged from the campaign's underlying list (same method as the automated cleanup). HeyReach has no API to remove a contact from the campaign itself, so previously-messaged contacts still show in the campaign's lead history as "Finished", and re-adding them to the same campaign will not send a new message. This cannot be undone.
