Message Hypothesis Lab

Find what actually drives success in a company / BDR / hypothesis group's outreach: compare the 50 best and 50 worst messages, let AI propose hypotheses, test every hypothesis against every message with a cheap classifier, and see which ones hold up statistically—always controlling for the prospect's LinkedIn connection count.

5-step pipeline
How it works: (1) Pull outreach for the scope you pick, with titles, messages, and outcomes · (2) Rank & pull the 50 most successful (scheduled > replied > connected) and 50 least successful (not connected, most LinkedIn connections) · (3) A strong model looks at both groups in one prompt and proposes testable hypotheses · (4) A cheap model scans every message in scope to tag which hypotheses each one follows · (5) Each hypothesis is tested with a connection-count–controlled statistical test (Mantel–Haenszel), then you can sweep the winning principles across in-process (not-yet-sent) messages.
