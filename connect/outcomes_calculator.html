What the client pays for month 1
At-risk launch month is off. Turn it on to guarantee the first month against the projected meetings.
How it works: the month-1 target defaults to the projected direct meetings above. Under Keep going until target, nothing is due until the target is booked — outreach continues past month-end if needed, then the standard month-1 volume fee is invoiced. Under Pay pro-rated, the month closes on schedule and the client pays the month-1 fee × (meetings delivered ÷ target), capped at 100%. From month 2 onward, every month is billed on volume.
