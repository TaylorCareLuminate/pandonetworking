Leader Finder

Look up a company, pull its director+ / VP+ leaders, and rank them by whose career best matches the prospects you are reaching out to — the leader whose background is closest to your prospects and most recent is the best person to send from.

1 Look up a company

Uses the same company employee scan as Quick Outreach → Company. People found are saved to the selected BDR's Prospect Contacts, and the company is added to that BDR's organizations if it isn't there already. Work histories are then read from the shared LinkedIn profile cache and only fetched from LinkedIn for people who aren't cached yet.

2 Describe the prospects you are reaching out to

Every role in each leader's history is scored against this profile. A role scores higher the more closely it matches, and its score is then discounted by how long ago it ended — a current or recent matching role beats an older one.
