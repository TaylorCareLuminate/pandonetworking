Step 1 Load the Contacts Database

Pick which database to scan, then load it. This is free — no AI is involved yet.

Step 2 Free Keyword Pre-Filter (optional, big cost saver)

Narrow the pool with plain keyword matching before any AI runs — this costs nothing. Comma-separated keywords; a contact passes if any include-keyword appears in their title or headline. Leave blank to send everyone to the AI.

Step 3 AI Screening — Describe Who You're Looking For

The AI reads each contact's title, company, headline (and optionally their experience/about text) and decides match / no match with a short reason. Contacts are sent in batches; the estimate below updates live as you change the model, filters, detail level, or limit.
