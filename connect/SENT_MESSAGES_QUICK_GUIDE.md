# Sent Messages - Quick Reference Guide

## 🚀 Quick Start

**Access**: Navigate to **Admin** → **Sent Messages** in the header

**What It Does**: Shows all your sent connection requests and messages, plus who replied or accepted

## 📊 At A Glance

### Summary Cards (Top of Page)
```
┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┐
│ Messages Sent   │ Connection      │ Connections     │ Replies         │
│                 │ Requests        │ Accepted        │ Received        │
└─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┘
```

### Filter Tabs
- **All** - Everything you've sent
- **Messages** - Direct messages only
- **Connections** - Connection requests only  
- **With Replies** - Contacts who responded
- **Accepted** - Connections that accepted

## 🎯 Common Tasks

### See Who Hasn't Responded
1. Select **All** filter
2. Look for yellow **Pending** badges
3. These contacts haven't replied yet

### Check Your Response Rate
```
Response Rate = (Replies Received / Messages Sent) × 100
```
Get numbers from summary cards at top

### Export Your Activity
1. Click **Export CSV** button
2. Open in Excel/Google Sheets
3. Analyze trends, sort by date, etc.

### Change Time Period
Use the **Time Range** dropdown:
- Last 7 days
- Last 14 days  
- Last 30 days (default)
- Last 60 days
- Last 90 days

## 📋 Table Columns Explained

| Column | What It Shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Contact** | Name, title, company + LinkedIn link |
| **Type** | Message or Connection Request |
| **Date Sent** | When you sent it |
| **Message** | Preview (hover for full text) |
| **Status** | Pending / Replied / Accepted |
| **Last Activity** | When contact last engaged |

## 💡 Pro Tips

### 1. Follow Up Strategy
**Wait 3-5 days** before following up on pending messages
- Too soon: Seems pushy
- Too late: They forgot about you

### 2. Best Times to Review
- **Monday morning**: Plan week's follow-ups
- **Friday afternoon**: Review week's results
- **Month-end**: Calculate monthly metrics

### 3. Use the Data
**High reply rate?** Your messages are working - keep it up!
**Low reply rate?** Try different message approaches
**No replies?** Review message templates with team

### 4. Connection Request Tips
Look at your **Accepted** filter:
- What did those messages have in common?
- What titles/companies respond best?
- Use patterns for future requests

### 5. Quick Follow-Ups
Click LinkedIn icon → Opens profile → Send follow-up
All in under 10 seconds!

## 🎓 Understanding the Data

### Q: Why don't I see a message I just sent?
**A**: Webhooks take 1-2 minutes to process. Click Refresh.

### Q: What counts as a "reply"?
**A**: Any message the contact sends back to you

### Q: Does "Accepted" mean they replied?
**A**: No - they accepted your connection but may not have messaged yet

### Q: Can I see messages others sent?
**A**: No - you only see your own sent items

### Q: Why is the status still "Pending" if they replied?
**A**: Click Refresh - the reply webhook may be processing

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Problem: Page is blank
- Try expanding time range to 90 days
- Click Refresh button
- Check your internet connection
- Reload page (F5)

### Problem: Numbers seem wrong
- Click Refresh
- Verify time range setting
- Check if you're using correct account
- Clear browser cache

### Problem: Can't export CSV
- Check popup blocker
- Try different browser
- Ensure JavaScript is enabled
- Contact support if persists
