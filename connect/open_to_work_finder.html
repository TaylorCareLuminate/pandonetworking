Open to Work Finder

Scans every contact in prospect_contacts marked "Open to Work" on LinkedIn, uses AI to narrow the list to reputable executives & B2B sales professionals, and lets you save the qualified list as a workspace you can pull from going forward.

Step 1 Scan Database for "Open to Work" Contacts

Reads the openToWork flag already stored on every prospect_contacts document (set by your existing LinkedIn scraping pipelines) — no live re-scraping, so this is fast and free. It scans system-wide, across every BDR and workspace.

Open to Work Candidates
Step 2 AI Qualification — Executives & B2B Sales Professionals

For each candidate, the AI reviews their job title, current company, work history and education (already on file) against your criteria below and returns a keep/exclude decision with a short reason. Candidates are processed in small batches, so cost scales with how many are sent — use the limit field to control spend on a first pass.

Reasoning effort is capped low automatically so the model spends its budget on the JSON answer, not hidden "thinking".
Step 3 Save Selected Contacts to a Workspace

Copies each selected contact into the target workspace (originals stay exactly where they are — nothing is removed from the BDR or workspace that currently owns them). Re-running this scan later and saving into the same workspace automatically skips anyone already copied in.
