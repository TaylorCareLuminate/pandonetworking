Shared contacts

Connection rate is accepted ÷ sent. Reply rate is replied ÷ accepted. Scheduling rate is meetings ÷ replied. A contact is shared when both BDRs pushed a connection request to the same LinkedIn profile in the selected window. Outcomes count whenever they happened for that BDR, including after the window.
