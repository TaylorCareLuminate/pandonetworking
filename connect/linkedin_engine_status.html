LinkedIn Engine

The sending engine that replaces HeyReach, one LinkedIn account at a time. It starts switched off and in dry-run: dry-run does everything except actually send. Nothing is sent until the engine is on, dry-run is off, and an account has been moved to the engine below. The STOP button halts all sending immediately.

LinkedIn accounts

Moving an account: link it through the Setup page → verify identity → rehearse (the engine plans and checks everything, HeyReach keeps sending) → drain (new messages go to the engine; HeyReach finishes what it has) → go live. Rolling back is only possible when nothing is in flight.
