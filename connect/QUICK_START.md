# Quick Start: Upload Your LinkedIn Messages

## 🚀 Get Started in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Get Your LinkedIn Data
1. Go to [LinkedIn Settings](https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/download-my-data)
2. Select **"Messages"**
3. Click **"Request archive"**
4. Wait for email (can take up to 48 hours)
5. Download and extract the ZIP file

### Step 2: Upload
1. Open [Manage My LinkedIn Data](https://healthluminate.com/connect/manage_my_linkedin_data.html)
2. Drag and drop `messages.csv` (or click to browse)
3. Wait for processing to complete

### Step 3: View Your Messages
1. Go to [HeyReach Inbox](https://healthluminate.com/crm/heyreach_inbox.html)
2. Your uploaded messages are now integrated!
3. You can now see messages beyond the 3-month HeyReach limit

## 📊 What You'll Get
- ✅ **All your LinkedIn messages** (complete history)
- ✅ **Merged with HeyReach data** (seamless integration)
- ✅ **Searchable and filterable** (same inbox interface)
- ✅ **Easy to delete** (if you change your mind)

## 🔒 Privacy & Security
- Your data is stored securely in Firebase
- Only you can see your uploaded messages
- You can delete your data at any time
- Data is tagged so you know what's user-uploaded vs. API-synced

## 💡 Tips
- Upload your most recent export to get the most complete history
- You can upload multiple times (new data is merged)
- Check the processing logs if anything seems wrong
- Larger files (>10MB) may take a few minutes to process
