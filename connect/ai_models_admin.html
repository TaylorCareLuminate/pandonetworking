How this is used
The catalog is stored in Firestore at ai_model_catalog/catalog. Pages load it through js/ai-model-catalog.js and rebuild their model dropdowns from it, grouped by cost tier. The Railway backend's /api/ai-models/list endpoint (meeting-date scan, company research) also reads this catalog. Every AI tool across Connect and CRM pulls its default model from one of the four job columns above: Scans (filters, classifications, cleanup & conversation/meeting scanners), Research (Organization Finder, LinkedIn URL Finder, web-search company summaries & Outcomes Calculator personalization), Writing (Generate Messages, Full Pipeline, reply & meeting-alert drafts, Human-like rewrites, Quick Text / Bulk AI edits, follow-up sequences, SOP Builder, Connection Requests), and Checking (Opus Review everywhere, Final AI Review rules, Hypothesis Target Rules, Rescue Untagged Contacts, Message Hypothesis Lab, About Me provenance lookup, pre-send safety checks). With a single model ticked it is always used; with several ticked they rotate randomly; with none ticked, each page keeps its own built-in default. If the catalog can't be loaded, pages fall back to the built-in list, so a bad save can never take the dropdowns down completely.
