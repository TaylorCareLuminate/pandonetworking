How this is used
The catalog is stored in Firestore at ai_model_catalog/catalog. Pages load it through js/ai-model-catalog.js and rebuild their model dropdowns from it, grouped by cost tier. The Railway backend's /api/ai-models/list endpoint (meeting-date scan, company research) also reads this catalog. The model marked Default is what the meeting-date scanner uses when no model is picked. If the catalog can't be loaded, pages fall back to the built-in list, so a bad save can never take the dropdowns down completely.
