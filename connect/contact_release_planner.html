Contact Release Planner

Pick a hypothesis group and BDR(s) to see (1) how many contacts you're actually working with — unique people vs. contacts the rules dropped — (2) what each organization in the group produced, and (3) because some customers don't want too much outreach to any one organization at a time and Full Pipeline can cap contacts per organization in a rolling 30 days, how many waiting contacts can get messages right now and the earliest date each remaining contact can be messaged as older messages age out of the window. Nothing is created or changed from this page — it is read-only planning. To act on it, run Full Pipeline with “ONLY use already-scanned contacts without a message” and the same 30-Day Organization Limit.
