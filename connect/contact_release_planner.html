Contact Release Planner

Some customers don't want too much outreach to any one organization at a time, so Full Pipeline can cap how many contacts per organization get a message in a rolling 30 days. That leaves a lot of good contacts found and saved but waiting. Pick a hypothesis group and BDR(s), set the per-organization limit, and see how many contacts are already found, how many can get messages right now, and the earliest date each remaining contact can be messaged as older messages age out of the window. Nothing is created or changed from this page — it is read-only planning. To act on it, run Full Pipeline with “ONLY use already-scanned contacts without a message” and the same 30-Day Organization Limit.
