Post Ideas

Write ten complete LinkedIn posts for a BDR (2 about the company, 4 built on current industry news, 4 reacting to posts their network is sharing), then choose which ones get a picture. Pictures follow the company's image style guide, and the writing follows its writing style guide (both on About Me, at the company level). Finished posts show up on the BDR's Repost Ideas page, where they choose one and a time. Nothing is published from this page.
