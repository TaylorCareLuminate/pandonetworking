Reply Response Times

Measures how long it takes BDRs to reply after a prospect messages, counted in Eastern Time business hours (Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM ET). Example: a prospect replies overnight and we answer by noon ET next day = 4 hours. Uses the same two-way conversation data as Company Conversations.

Business hours = Mon–Fri 8:00 AM–5:00 PM America/New_York. Nights and weekends do not count. Clock starts at the first unanswered prospect message in a streak and stops when we send our reply.
