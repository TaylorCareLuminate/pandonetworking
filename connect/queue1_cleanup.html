Queue 1 Cleanup

Finds and (with confirmation) soft-deletes two kinds of dead-weight in connect_queue's Queue 1: duplicates — pending messages for a contact who already has an approved/queued message from the same account within the duplicate window — and stale, never-reviewed messages older than the age threshold. Nothing is deleted until you review the list and click "Delete Selected." Deletes are soft (deleted:true), so they disappear from every page's counts immediately but remain recoverable in Firestore.

Automated Cleanup

This same scan-and-delete logic now also runs automatically every Tuesday & Thursday at 2:30 AM Mountain Time on the server, across all active BDRs, using the default settings (75-day duplicate window, 40-day stale threshold, Harvest Questions not exempted). You don't need to run this page manually anymore for routine upkeep — it's here for spot-checks, custom windows, or a single BDR. You can also trigger the exact automated job on demand below.
