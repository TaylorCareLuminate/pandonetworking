Test Insert Times: All BDRs

Pre-flight check for "Draft Follow-Up Sequences". For every active BDR this pulls the saved calendar link and runs the same availability lookup the scan uses (starts 4 days out, searches 45 days, keeps slots fully inside 8am to 5pm for the prospect, then takes the first 4 open days). Nothing is drafted, saved, or sent.

How to read this. Working found the full 4 open days. Few times works, but fewer than 4 open days exist in the next 45 days. No times the link loaded but nothing falls inside 8am to 5pm. Error the lookup failed (bad link, unsupported scheduler, timeout). No link no calendar link saved; the scan will ask the prospect for times instead. The "EA settings" column matters too: without them the scan holds back the executive assistant steps for that BDR.
