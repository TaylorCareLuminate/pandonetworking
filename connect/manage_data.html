Manage Data

One place for everything that builds, finds, organizes, cleans and tracks prospect data. Pick what you're working in at the top once (a BDR, a Workspace, a Snapshot or the Unassigned Pool) — every function on the left then acts on that. The groups on the left are in the order the work normally happens.

How the work flows

Organizations become contacts when you scan them; contacts become connections when a request is accepted. Each group below answers one question.

The words used everywhere

BDR
A person running outreach. Organizations and contacts tagged with their email feed their campaigns.
Workspace
A named research session (e.g. "Rehab clinics – West Coast") holding organizations and contacts that aren't assigned to a BDR yet. Can be tied to a client and marked as a reusable Source pool 📚.
Snapshot
A frozen, read-only copy of a workspace. "Open in workspace" makes it editable again.
Unassigned Pool
Organizations saved to nobody yet; move them to a BDR or workspace when ready.
Batch
One import / move / group event. Many functions act on a whole batch (clean it, move it, delete it, reconcile it).
Hypothesis Group
A research tag on contacts so results can be compared across messaging hypotheses.
DNC
Do-Not-Contact organizations. Sweeps find and remove matching orgs and their contacts.
Excluded
Contacts flagged out of campaigns but kept; they can be browsed, downloaded and re-included.

    Upload a contact list, match its columns to our fields, and save the people into a workspace — optionally tagging them to a hypothesis group in the same step. Each row needs a LinkedIn profile URL; people already in the workspace (same URL) are never duplicated.
