The prospect-data pipeline

Every tool that creates, finds, organizes, cleans, or tracks prospect data lives on this page. The work flows in five stages: decide which organizations to target, find who works there, put each contact where it belongs, remove what shouldn't be there, and then track who connects. Pick a stage on the left, or start from a task below.

Five stages, left to right

Each stage card lists the tools that belong to it and the data they read or write. Click a tool to open it here. A green dot in the sidebar means a tool is already loaded and will keep its state while you switch.

Where the data lives

All tools read and write the same Firestore collections, which is why work done in one shows up in the next. Organizations become contacts when scanned; contacts become connections when a request is accepted.

Shared vocabulary

Every tool opens with the same selector at the top. Choose the same BDR / Workspace in each tool as you move through the stages — each tool is a complete app and keeps its own selection.

BDR
A person running outreach. Organizations and contacts tagged with their email are "theirs" and feed their campaigns.
Workspace
A named research session (e.g. "Rehab clinics – West Coast") that holds organizations and contacts without assigning them to a BDR yet. Can be tied to a client and marked as a reusable Source pool 📚.
Snapshot
A frozen, read-only copy of a workspace's organizations + contacts. "Open in Workspace" turns it back into something editable.
Unassigned Pool
Organizations saved to nobody in particular; move them to a BDR or workspace when ready.
Batch
An import / move / group event. Many tools let you act on a whole batch at once (clean it, move it, delete it, reconcile it).
Hypothesis Group
A research tag applied to contacts so results can be compared across messaging hypotheses.
DNC
Do-Not-Contact list of organizations. Sweeps find and remove matching orgs and their contacts.
Excluded
Contacts flagged out of campaigns but kept in the database. They can be browsed, downloaded and re-included.
