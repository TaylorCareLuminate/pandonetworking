How Smart Search Works

  • First, it builds a duplicate-check baseline from everything you selected above — no point paying to find a contact you already have somewhere.
  • Then it generates a few concrete search strategies (keyword search, or organization search → employees) from your description.
  • Each strategy runs a small test batch only, scored by AI against your description, checked against the duplicate baseline, and vetoed by any selected hypothesis group's Target Contact / Exclude Rules when they're filled in.
  • Strategies that clearly match and return enough net-new (non-duplicate, non-excluded) results auto-scale to a full run. Strategies that don't pause for your review — cost never runs ahead of demonstrated, non-duplicate relevance.
