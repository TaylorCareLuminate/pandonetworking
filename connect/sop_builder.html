SOP Builder

Paste the transcript of a team call or training. Claude Opus finds every "how to" that was taught, links each step to the real PandoConnect pages, and files it in the How-To Library. Anyone can then find it from the search bar on the Admin Guide.

1. Add the transcript

Paste text, or load one or more files (.txt, .md, .vtt, .srt). Zoom, Teams, Meet and Fireflies exports all work. Timestamps are cleaned up for you.

Opus is the default because it follows long transcripts and "only use what was said" rules the best. Models come from AI Models Admin.
Nothing is saved until you review the drafts and click Save.

2. Review the drafts

Preview each one, fix anything that's off, then save. Steps marked needs confirmation are places where the speaker wasn't clear, so check those with the team.
