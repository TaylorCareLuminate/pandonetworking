Total – contacts in this group assigned to the BDR
Outreached – contacts that already have a connection request (in queue or ever pushed)
Available – contacts with no connection request yet
In Queue – messages currently in Queue 1 (pending admin review in fast_connect_review.html) that are matched to a hypothesis-group contact. Queue 2 (customer review) is not included, so this lines up with the "Queue 1" number on index_admin.html.
Queue 1 Total (Live) – every Queue 1 message for these BDRs, straight from connect_queue (same query/logic as index_admin.html's "Queue 1" column) — regardless of whether it's matched to a contact or group
Not in a Group – Queue 1 messages that are not covered by any hypothesis group (either an unclassified contact, or no matching contact record at all). Click the stat, or the ⚠ badge on a BDR, for details.
Submitted – pushed to HeyReach this month · ✓ confirmed = HeyReach actually dispatched to LinkedIn · ⏳ in queue = submitted but still waiting in HeyReach to be sent
Unclassified – contacts assigned to the BDR but not in any hypothesis group
