Review Reply Prompts

The prompts and message templates behind the AI-drafted follow-ups on the Review Replies pages (Scan for Follow-up Opportunities and Draft Follow-Up Sequences). Edit them here and the next scan picks up your changes.

Changes apply to the next scan you run. Drafts that already exist are not rewritten. Use Preview to see a prompt filled in with sample data, and Restore Default to go back to the built-in text.
Placeholders: words in double curly braces, like {{bdr_name}}, are filled in automatically when a scan runs. Click a placeholder chip under any prompt to insert it where your cursor is. Each prompt lists the placeholders it can use. A placeholder that doesn't exist for that prompt is left as-is in the draft, so a typo will be easy to spot.
