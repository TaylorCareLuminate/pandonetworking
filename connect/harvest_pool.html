Hypothesis groups in the harvest pool

Each hypothesis group is split into Pando Executive Networking Connections (connections our outreach generated) and Previous Connections (people already connected before our outreach, not generated by Pando / HealthLuminate). Generated connections are counted when they are in the harvest pool; previous connections are counted once they have a hypothesis group, whether or not they are in the harvest pool.

Harvest outreach

Harvest-tagged rows in connect_queue (weekly review, harvest questions, etc.). Pipeline rate is internal workflow only (approved, sent, or pushed)—not whether the prospect answered. Reply rate uses HeyReach reply webhooks after the message went out; slot rate uses LinkedIn message slots scheduled on or after that send time.
