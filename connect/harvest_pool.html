Harvest pool

Your harvest pool is the set of LinkedIn connections who accepted your outreach and have aged into the follow-up window (usually 30+ days). The chart below shows how that pool has grown day by day (computed nightly on our servers; admins can run an immediate recalculation from the Account section). At the bottom you can see harvest outreach volume and how often those messages move forward successfully.
