What the plan looks like

The next days at the usual numbers, after holidays and make-up, and inside the daily, weekly and any-seven-days limits. “Shortfall” is volume that could not be placed anywhere without breaking a limit.

What the engine has learned

Each hypothesis group, client and the company as a whole has its own send windows and weekday numbers. A window is prime (most of the volume), testing (the 20% share), or trickle (10% kept going). Statuses change only after enough sends and two reviews in a row. Pin a row to freeze it; reset to start learning again.
