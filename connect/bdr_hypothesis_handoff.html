BDR Hypothesis Hand-off

Take contacts from one BDR's hypothesis group who have connected with or replied to that BDR, and hand them to a second BDR (often at another company) for a Full AI Generate message built from a different hypothesis group. The messages land in the second BDR's queue for review. Nothing is ever sent from this page. Use steps 1 to 5 to hand contacts off by hand (you then run Full AI Generate yourself), or set up a nightly rule below to do all of it every night and have the messages waiting for your approval in Fast Connect Review.

1 Who they connected or replied to (BDR 1 and hypothesis group 1)

Only contacts that are members of this group are considered.

2 What counts (trigger)

Applies to the "connected" part only. Looking further back loads more events and takes longer.
Anyone already booked or who declined is skipped by default.

3 Who gets the message (BDR 2 and hypothesis group 2)

Must be an active BDR. The contacts are copied into this BDR's prospect list.
Full AI Generate writes the message using this group's instructions and variations. The contacts are added to it so results are tracked under this group.

4 Preview the contacts

Pick both BDRs and both groups, then preview. Contacts BDR 2 is already connected to, has queued, already has in their prospect list, excluded, or that are at a Do Not Contact organization are removed automatically.

5 Hand off and generate

Sets how many rows are ticked after you preview. You can tick or untick individual rows.
This copies the ticked contacts into BDR 2's prospect list as one batch, tags them with hypothesis group 2 and adds them to that group. Then open Full AI Generate (set up for you) and run it. Its messages go to BDR 2's queue, where you review them in Fast Connect Review as usual.

Nightly rules

Every night (about 2:00 AM Mountain) each enabled rule finds contacts in hypothesis group 1 who newly connected or replied to BDR 1, copies them into BDR 2's list and group 2, writes each message with the same Full AI pipeline Generate Messages uses, and runs the Opus review. Nothing is sent. The messages wait in Fast Connect Review for you to approve. A contact that fails is retried on later nights (up to 3 tries).
