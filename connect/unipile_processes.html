Unipile Processes

Processes that run each day through Unipile, using a BDR's own LinkedIn account. Each kind of process is set up, scheduled and reviewed here, side by side. Everything that goes out on LinkedIn is sent by the LinkedIn engine, so its limits always apply (for example, never more than 20 comments a day per account), along with its do-not-contact list and its pauses.

For the Post comments process: it drafts a short comment in the BDR's voice on recent posts by their target contacts, and grades the comment and the post. Good drafts wait here for an admin, then (depending on the BDR's setting) for the BDR. Very interesting posts are also saved as repost ideas and feed the BDR's post ideas.

Who it looks for

Every run searches LinkedIn for posts from the last 24 hours that contain one of the phrases below. The AI then reads the post and the person's profile. Someone qualifies only if they are really looking for work, are a senior sales or growth leader (VP, head of or chief of sales, revenue or growth; a CEO or founder is worth a try) in a company that sells to businesses, and score at least the fit score below. The decision is made by rules in code from the AI's answers; nothing the AI or the post says is ever copied into a message.

What it does

LinkedIn has no feed of notifications that a program can read, so each run reads the profiles of this BDR's connections (the same list as My Leads), a few at a time, and remembers what it sees: the birthday (month and day only, never the year) and the current job. On the day, it sends a short greeting you chose below. When someone has started a new job or role, it sends a congratulations, and it updates their title and company in our records (My Leads) to match LinkedIn. Only 1st-degree connections are greeted, never someone on the do-not-contact list, and never more than one greeting to a person in a day. Every message is one of the fixed wordings below; nothing is written by AI.
