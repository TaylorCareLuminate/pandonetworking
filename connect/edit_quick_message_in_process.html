Applies to every loaded editable message (not just the ones on screen or checked). Add one rule per variation — e.g. Oct 14 → Oct 21 and October 14 → October 21. All rules are applied in a single pass, so one rule’s output is never re-matched by another. Tip: include suffixes in the search text (October 14th) or you may end up with “October 21th” — check the preview below.
