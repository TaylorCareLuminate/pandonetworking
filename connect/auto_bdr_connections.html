Auto BDR Connections

Hands-off connection messages between BDRs. A set number of days after a contact connects with one BDR, a second BDR sends them a connection message that mentions the first. An AI reviewer checks every message, and anything that doesn't pass is not sent.

Nightly automation

On
Master switch for the nightly run on Railway (about 1:30 AM Mountain). Turn off to pause every rule at once. Individual rules also have their own switch.
  1. Finds contacts who accepted BDR 1's connection request at least N days ago (and not longer ago than the catch-up window).
  2. Skips anyone BDR 2 is already connected to, has queued, excluded, or that sits at a Do Not Contact organization, plus anyone who declined or was deeply engaged with BDR 1.
  3. Writes BDR 2's message, runs hard rule checks, then an AI review (correct name, correct colleague, nothing invented). Failed drafts are rewritten with the reviewer's feedback; if none pass, nothing is sent.
  4. Passing messages are queued as approved. The regular Connect Queue push then sends them into BDR 2's connect campaign.
