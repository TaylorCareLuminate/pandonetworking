How to Download Your LinkedIn Messages

  1. Sign in to LinkedIn on a desktop browser
  2. Click your profile picture and choose Settings & Privacy
  3. In the left menu, select Data privacy
  4. Look for "Get a copy of your data"
  5. Choose "Messages" (or select specific data)
  6. Click "Request archive" (may need to re-enter password)
  7. Wait for email with download link (can take up to 48 hours)
  8. Download the ZIP file and extract it
  9. Upload the messages.csv file below

CSV Format Requirements

✨ Smart Field Mapping: Your CSV columns don't need exact names! After upload, you'll map your columns to these required fields:

  • First Name - Required
  • Last Name - Required
  • Title - Optional (job title/position)
  • Company - Required
  • Company Domain - Required (e.g., company.com)
  • Location - Required (city, state, or country)
  • Category - Required (e.g., Healthcare IT, Vendors, etc.)
  • LinkedIn URL - Required (full LinkedIn profile URL)
  • Notes - Optional

The system will automatically detect and suggest field mappings!
