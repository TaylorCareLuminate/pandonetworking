Full Pipeline
One page that runs the entire flow: organization URL research → Apify employee contact scan (with cost tracking) → target-rule filtering → organization news (fetched once per org) → Full AI Generate → Opus Review → Final AI Reviews. Finished messages land in Fast Connect Review exactly like today.
1 Client & Hypothesis Group

Pick the client/customer first, then one of their hypothesis groups. The group's organizations (managed in Prospect Organizations Admin → Hypothesis Group Organizations), Target Contact / Exclude Rules, Message Build Instructions, and Message Review Instructions all come from this group.

2 BDRs & Messages to Generate

Only BDRs linked to the selected hypothesis group are shown (link BDRs to groups in Connect Hypothesis Documentation). Check each BDR who needs messages and set how many. Organizations from the group are scanned one at a time and their contacts assigned to the current BDR until that BDR's count is met, then the pipeline moves to the next BDR.

6 Cost Shut-Down Rates

The pipeline stops automatically when contact-search spend gets out of line with messages produced. Of the target cost per message, roughly $0.03 goes to AI (build + reviews) and $0.02 to post scrapes, so the rest is the Apify contact-search budget. When the average Apify cost per generated message exceeds that budget, the run shuts down — that usually means the Step 4 scan rules are finding too few usable contacts and need adjusting.

Default $0.20 per generated message.
