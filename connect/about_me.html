About Me Connect — What to Write

How your profile statements are used when generating LinkedIn connection messages

The AI compares your profile statements against a prospect's LinkedIn profile and recent posts to find genuine commonalities, then writes a short, personal connection message on your behalf. The quality of that message depends entirely on how well your statements reflect the real you — not a job description.

Write statements that sound like you talking
  • Be specific — name the actual industry, cause, or challenge you care about
  • Include hobbies, interests, and personal passions (not just work)
  • Mention where you grew up, went to school, or lived if relevant
  • Share what genuinely drives you professionally
  • Include past companies or career moves that shaped you
Avoid statements that are generic or salesy
  • Don't copy your LinkedIn headline or job description
  • Avoid vague phrases like "passionate about healthcare"
  • Don't pitch your product or company benefits
  • Skip buzzwords — "results-driven", "strategic", "synergy"
  • Don't list responsibilities — share experiences and perspectives

AI Instructions for About Me Messages

🤖 Guide the AI when building About Me Connect messages. These instructions tell the AI exactly how to behave when generating personalized "About Me" connection messages in Generate Messages. The more specific you are, the better the output. The AI reads your I Statements above, compares them to the prospect's LinkedIn profile and recent posts, finds personal commonalities, then writes a connection request using these instructions as its creative brief.

How should the message feel? (e.g., "Warm and collegial — like a fellow healthcare professional reaching out, never salesy or scripted")

Which types of personal connections should the AI lead with? (e.g., "Prioritize shared alma mater first, then industry passion; avoid leading with just geography")

How long and focused should messages be? (e.g., "Under 250 characters. One strong commonality only — do not list multiple connections at once")

What should the AI NEVER say? (e.g., "Never mention family, religion, or politics. Avoid the phrase 'reaching out to connect' — it sounds generic")

How should the message open and close? (e.g., "Lead with a genuine observation about their work. End with a light ask to connect — never ask to schedule a call in the opener")

Any other specific guidance for the AI when crafting About Me Connect messages (e.g., formatting rules, personas to channel, dos and don'ts)
