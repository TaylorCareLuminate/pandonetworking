Target Contact / Exclude Rules

For a hypothesis group, review the titles of contacts that were excluded in Fast Connect Review alongside contacts that were successfully sent connection requests. The AI model you choose below analyzes the most recent 250 titles from each bucket and proposes the targeting logic — edit it and save it as the group's Target Contact / Exclude Rules. Then sweep every remaining, not-yet-contacted prospect in the group against those rules and drop or delete the ones that don't fit.

This tool is restricted to Pando Executive Networking admins.
