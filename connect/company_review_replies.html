Scan for Follow-up Opportunities

Filters conversations by category and drafts a follow-up automatically — the logic is fixed, not a freeform prompt

Scanning conversations for: —
How this works — one outcome per conversation, based on its categories:
  1. Prospect replied, we haven't followed up (Follow-Up = "No Follow Up") → drafts a message that introduces the BDR a bit more and tries to find a time to meet.
  2. We followed up, they went quiet, but they'd shown interest in meeting (Response = "Response Willing to Meet") → drafts the executive-assistant nudge on the BDR's behalf, offering times.
  3. We followed up, they went quiet, and they never showed interest → left alone for a harvest campaign — no message is drafted.
  4. Scheduled / Declined to Meet / Qualified Wrong Contact → always excluded.

Contacts who accepted a connection but never replied are handled separately by the Follow-Up Schedule Filter at the bottom of this page — not by this scan.
