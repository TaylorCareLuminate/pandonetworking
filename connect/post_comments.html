Post Comments

Finds recent LinkedIn posts by a BDR's target contacts, sorts them, drafts a short comment in the BDR's voice (from their About Me), and grades both the comment and the post. Good drafts wait here for an admin. Depending on the BDR's setting they then go to the BDR for a second look, and approved comments are posted by the LinkedIn engine, never more than 20 a day per LinkedIn account. Very interesting posts are also saved as repost ideas, and they feed the BDR's own post ideas.

Processes

A process reads the posts of a number of contacts from one or more of a BDR's hypothesis groups, on a schedule or when you press Run now. Contacts are the BDR's leads that were assigned to the group on My Connections; the ones read longest ago go first, so the whole group is covered over time.
