Proxy 1 · CheckApiKey (via Railway)

GET /proxy/heyreach/auth/checkapikey → HeyReach /auth/CheckApiKey. Does not change content.

Direct 2 · GetLead (browser → HeyReach)

POST /api/public/lead/GetLead — requires the optional override key (not Railway env). May hit CORS; prefer approach 3.

Proxy 3 · GetLead via Railway

Same idea through /proxy/heyreach/leads/getlead (avoids CORS if direct fails).

Proxy 4 · AddLeadsToCampaignV2 — customVariables.content
