How to read this. With / without compares messages that contain the phrase to messages that don't. Effect is the difference in percentage points (pp) after adjusting for the prospect's LinkedIn connection count, so it can differ from the raw gap between the two rates. Green / red means the effect is statistically significant (p < 0.05); grey means we can't tell it apart from chance. Because thousands of phrases are tested, a few will look significant by luck — phrases that are used often and across many clients are the most trustworthy. Phrases are correlations, not guarantees: they can stand in for other things (who we targeted, what the message was about).
