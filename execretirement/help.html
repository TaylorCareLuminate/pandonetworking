Key Concept

The system follows a hierarchical structure: Process Definition → Team Members → Advisors → Clients → New Clients. Each level builds upon the previous one to create a comprehensive tracking system.

Initial Setup Process

Before creating new clients, several foundational elements must be configured. This setup ensures smooth operation and proper tracking throughout the new client lifecycle.

1
Define New Client Process
The new client process defines all the steps, sub-steps, and checkpoints that new clients will follow from creation to completion.
What gets configured:
  • Process Steps: Major milestones (e.g., "Initial Review", "Client Meeting", "Document Submission")
  • Sub-Steps: Detailed tasks within each major step
  • Checklist Items: Required approvals or validations
  • Dependencies: Which steps must be completed before others can begin
  • Assignments: Which team members are responsible for each step
Configure Process
2
Set Up Team Members
Team members are the internal staff who will be assigned to work on various new client steps and tasks.
Team member information includes:
  • Name and contact information
  • Team/Department assignment
  • Role and responsibilities
Manage Team
3
Register Advisors
Advisors are external partners who bring new client opportunities to your organization. They must be registered before creating new clients.
Advisor information includes:
  • Personal details (name, contact information)
  • Agency/Firm affiliation
  • License information and credentials
  • Territory or specialization areas
  • Commission and fee arrangements
Manage Advisors
4
Create Client Records
Client records represent potential customers that advisors want to create new clients for. Each client can have multiple new clients over time.
Client information includes:
  • Company/Firm name and contact details
  • Primary contact person
  • Industry and company size
  • Current retirement plan details (if any)
  • Associated advisor relationship
Manage Clients
Important Setup Order

These setup steps should be completed in order. You cannot create new clients without first having advisors and clients defined, and the process definition determines how new clients will be tracked.

Proposal Workflow

Once the foundational setup is complete, you can begin creating and tracking new clients through their entire lifecycle.

1
Create New Client
Start by creating a new client record with all the essential details about the potential retirement plan.
Required information:
  • Advisor: Select from registered advisors
  • Client: Select from clients associated with the advisor
  • Plan Details: Type, size, assets, participants
  • Financial Information: Fees, assets under management
  • Timeline: Proposed effective date, submission deadlines
Create New Client
2
Automatic Process Initiation
Once created, the new client automatically enters the first step of your defined process and gets assigned to the appropriate team member.
What happens automatically:
  • New client gets assigned a unique ID
  • Process status is set to the first defined step
  • Initial team member assignment occurs
  • New client appears on relevant worklists
3
Progress Through Steps
Team members work on their assigned tasks and advance the new client through each step of the defined process.
Step progression features:
  • Validate requirements before advancing
  • Add notes and documentation
  • Handle approvals and sign-offs
  • Track time spent on each step
  • Automatic notifications to next assignee
View Worklist
4
Monitor and Report
Track new client progress, identify bottlenecks, and generate reports on new client performance and team productivity.
Monitoring capabilities:
  • Real-time status dashboard
  • Team member workload views
  • Process timeline and history
  • Performance metrics and analytics

Key Features

Process Automation
New clients automatically follow your defined process with proper assignments, dependencies, and validations at each step.
Real-Time Visibility
See exactly where each new client stands in the process, who's working on it, and what's needed to move forward.
Task Management
Team members get personalized worklists showing their assigned tasks with all the context they need to complete them.
Quality Control
Built-in validations ensure required information is complete and dependencies are met before advancing steps.
Complete Audit Trail
Every action, note, and status change is tracked with timestamps and user information for complete accountability.
Analytics & Reporting
Generate insights on proposal performance, team productivity, and process efficiency to drive continuous improvement.

Tips & Best Practices

Team Management
  • Keep team member information up to date, especially email addresses for notifications
  • Assign process steps to specific roles rather than individuals when possible
  • Use the worklist feature to balance workload across team members
Process Design
  • Start with a simple process and add complexity as needed
  • Use dependencies to ensure steps happen in the right order
  • Include validation checkpoints for critical information
  • Document requirements clearly for each step
Proposal Management
  • Enter complete information when creating proposals to avoid delays later
  • Use consistent naming conventions for easy searching
  • Add detailed notes at each step to maintain context
  • Review and update client information regularly

Troubleshooting

?
Proposal Won't Advance
If you can't advance a proposal to the next step, check these common issues:
  • Ensure all required fields are completed
  • Check that all dependencies for the next step are met
  • Verify you have permission to advance this proposal
  • Look for any validation errors in the process timeline
?
Missing Tasks in Worklist
If expected tasks don't appear in your worklist:
  • Check that your team member record is properly configured
  • Verify the process step is assigned to you or your role
  • Ensure the proposal is in an active status
  • Try refreshing the worklist or adjusting filters
?
Can't Create Proposal
If you're unable to create a new proposal:
  • Ensure the advisor is properly registered and active
  • Verify the client exists and is associated with the advisor
  • Check that you have the necessary permissions
  • Make sure all required fields are filled in
Need Additional Help?

If you're still experiencing issues after checking these troubleshooting steps, please contact your system administrator or the IT support team for assistance.
