Emerging Market Study

Autonomous Coding Solutions

Identified Data and Sources Cost: $500 (provider organizations) $2,400 (HIT software and consulting firms)

Autonomous coding solutions offer a solution to one of the greatest challenges of US healthcare—dollars meant for patient care instead go to administrative and billing costs.

Pando Executive Networking has pioneered advanced AI-driven search tools and algorithms, complemented by meticulous human expert reviews, to accurately identify healthcare IT solutions in use. As part of this innovative research technique, we found the following reported benefits for autonomous coding solutions:

  • Reduced manual labor: Health systems like Henry Ford Health and Mass General Brigham saw up to a 70% reduction in manual coding tasks.
  • Accelerated processing time: Systems like RapidClaims and Nym drastically sped up claims processing, with Nym coding charts in around 5 seconds and ApolloMD reducing coding turnaround to about 3 hours.
  • Reduced denial rates: CodaMetrix users, including Henry Ford Health and Mass General Brigham, reported denial reductions of 60-70%.
  • Decreased administrative burden: Systems like AKASA, RapidClaims, and MediMobile significantly reduced repetitive administrative tasks, freeing up staff to focus on patient care or more complex billing issues.
  • Revenue growth: Bon Secours Mercy Health realized up to 19% revenue growth using XpertDox’s AI-enabled medical coding.
  • Revenue recovery and leakage reduction: Centra Health (SparkChange) identified an additional $200M in revenue; Semantic Health helped Boston Children's Hospital identify over $2M in missed revenue; Arintra provided Mercyhealth with a 5.1% revenue uplift.
  • Cost savings: Mass General Brigham, Henry Ford Health, and others reduced coding costs by up to 60%, and Cedars-Sinai reduced the number of required FTE coders significantly.
  • Improved cash flow: CodaMetrix implementation accelerated cash collection by up to 5 weeks and improved cash collections by as many as 47 days (Henry Ford Health).
  • Enhanced job satisfaction: Coders at Henry Ford Health and Inova reported improved job satisfaction due to reduced repetitive tasks and increased opportunities for skill development.
  • Reduced burnout: AI automation, such as RapidClaims at CommonSpirit Health, reduced staff burnout by handling routine coding and administrative tasks.
  • Improved analytics and business intelligence: Solutions like XpertDox provided healthcare systems with real-time analytics to optimize revenue cycles and operational efficiency.

It should be noted that Pando Executive Networking did not interview live customers for these insights, but rather gathered insights from across case studies, news stories and other public reporting. We leave to organizations such as KLAS Research or Elion to interview clients and report on client feedback.

Inpatient Focus and Methodology

While nearly all of these companies have sold to and implemented their solutions in outpatient clinics and other care settings, this analysis only searched across inpatient health systems and hospitals. Success in an inpatient setting is a critical indicator of solution maturity.

This study included over 120,000 AI-powered web searches to identify 62 customers across 17 vendors (three vendors had no identified customers). This methodology obviously does not capture every contracted or live client, but it does provide a powerful insight into the traction of each vendor. Over 70 different variations of web searches were conducted for every health system or hospital in the country to identify the Autonomous coding solutions in place, making this report and data set a complete census of all public information about US hospital autonomous coding solutions.

Solventum 360 Encompass Autonomous Coding

The spin-off of the long-time coding powerhouse, 3M, Solventum already boasts enormous market share with their traditional 360 Encompass coding solution. Because of the large 360 Encompass install base, and because Solventum’s autonomous coding is being incorporated into 360 Encompass, it was difficult to identify customers truly using autonomous coding. A conservative approach was taken, only including organizations with evidence of true autonomous coding. Below are some of the success stories reported in public articles, press releases and interviews:

  • One federal healthcare agency implemented Solventum’s computer-assisted coding tools across its network, supporting automated coding workflows and improving administrative efficiency.
  • A health system used Solventum’s prioritization and ROI features to increase clinical documentation improvement (CDI) impact and provide individualized CDI scorecards to leadership.
  • One provider organization was able to move toward direct-to-bill without increasing FTEs, thanks to automated workflows and real-time clinical intelligence reporting.
  • A major hospital reported saving multiple full-time equivalents (FTEs) and improving billing turnaround time through Solventum’s autonomous coding solutions.
  • A customer using the Solventum cloud implementation noted 85% or more reduction in IT burden, along with stable or improved application performance post-divestiture.
  • Health systems reported that Solventum’s cloud application management enabled IT teams to focus on strategic initiatives by offloading infrastructure and process management to Solventum.
  • Providers noted that Solventum’s integrated solutions for coding, CDI, and auditing boosted coding accuracy, supported compliance, and reduced documentation burden for clinicians.
  • A revenue cycle leader role at one organization highlighted Solventum’s tools as essential to optimizing professional revenue cycle implementation, efficiency, and accuracy.

  • A customer was able to expand coding automation to new specialties after success in one area, indicating strong cross-specialty adaptability.
  • One provider reduced expenses and freed coders to focus on complex cases, improving both efficiency and compliance.

Nym

Nym is well established with a wide variety of proof points, with multiple very large health system customers and a smaller hospital and children’s hospital. Customers reported the following successes:

  • One provider reported a denial rate of less than 1% out of over 27,000 claims coded by Nym during a two-month review, indicating exceptionally high coding accuracy.
  • A customer reported that Nym’s autonomous coding solution codes charts in approximately 5 seconds, dramatically reducing the time from discharge to coding.
  • Nym’s Clinical Language Understanding (CLU) engine was reported to achieve 98% coding accuracy, helping reduce human intervention and improve coding compliance.
  • One health system implemented Nym across all of its EDs and was able to automate coding for over 60% of ED visits, while maintaining internal accuracy standards.
  • A hospital implemented Nym to automate emergency department billing, enabling faster, more accurate billing, reducing administrative burdens, and improving payment cycles.
  • A revenue cycle department requested a multi-year agreement with Nym due to its ability to process charts autonomously and accurately, with plans to expand across service lines.
  • A customer described Nym’s implementation as scalable, with benefits including lower denial rates, greater revenue cycle efficiency, and enhanced analytical transparency.
  • One organization credited Nym with enabling clinicians and staff to focus more on patient care, due to administrative efficiency gains from coding automation.
