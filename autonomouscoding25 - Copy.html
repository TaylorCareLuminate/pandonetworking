Emerging Market Study: Autonomous Coding Solutions

Report Details and Data Cost: $500 (provider organizations) $2,400 (HIT software and consulting firms)

Autonomous coding solutions offer a solution to one of the greatest challenges of US healthcare—dollars meant for patient care instead go to administrative and billing costs.

HealthLuminate has pioneered advanced AI-driven search tools and algorithms, complemented by meticulous human expert reviews, to accurately identify healthcare IT solutions in use. As part of this innovative research technique, we found the following reported benefits for autonomous coding solutions:

  • Reduced manual labor: Health systems like Henry Ford Health and Mass General Brigham saw up to a 70% reduction in manual coding tasks.
  • Accelerated processing time: Systems like RapidClaims and Nym drastically sped up claims processing, with Nym coding charts in around 5 seconds and ApolloMD reducing coding turnaround to about 3 hours.
  • Reduced denial rates: CodaMetrix users, including Henry Ford Health and Mass General Brigham, reported denial reductions of 60-70%.
  • Decreased administrative burden: Systems like AKASA, RapidClaims, and MediMobile significantly reduced repetitive administrative tasks, freeing up staff to focus on patient care or more complex billing issues.
  • Revenue growth: Bon Secours Mercy Health realized up to 19% revenue growth using XpertDox’s AI-enabled medical coding.
  • Revenue recovery and leakage reduction: Centra Health (SparkChange) identified an additional $200M in revenue; Semantic Health helped Boston Children's Hospital identify over $2M in missed revenue; Arintra provided Mercyhealth with a 5.1% revenue uplift.
  • Cost savings: Mass General Brigham, Henry Ford Health, and others reduced coding costs by up to 60%, and Cedars-Sinai reduced the number of required FTE coders significantly.
  • Improved cash flow: CodaMetrix implementation accelerated cash collection by up to 5 weeks and improved cash collections by as many as 47 days (Henry Ford Health).
  • Enhanced job satisfaction: Coders at Henry Ford Health and Inova reported improved job satisfaction due to reduced repetitive tasks and increased opportunities for skill development.
  • Reduced burnout: AI automation, such as RapidClaims at CommonSpirit Health, reduced staff burnout by handling routine coding and administrative tasks.
  • Improved analytics and business intelligence: Solutions like XpertDox provided healthcare systems with real-time analytics to optimize revenue cycles and operational efficiency.

It should be noted that HealthLuminate did not interview live customers for these insights, but rather gathered insights from across case studies, news stories and other public reporting. We leave to organizations like KLAS Research or Elion to interview clients and report on client feedback.

Inpatient Focus and Methodology

While nearly all of these companies have sold to and implemented their solutions in outpatient clinics and other care settings, this analysis only searched across inpatient health systems and hospitals. Success in an inpatient setting is a critical indicator of solution maturity.

This study included over 120,000 AI-powered web searches to identify 62 customers across 17 vendors (three vendors had no identified customers). Over 70 different variations of web searches were conducted for every health system or hospital in the country to identify the Autonomous coding solutions in place, making this report and data set a complete census of all public information about US hospital autonomous coding solutions.

Vendor Summaries

Solventum 360 Encompass Autonomous Coding

The spin-off of the long-time coding powerhouse, 3M, Solventum already boasts enormous market share with their traditional 360 Encompass coding solution. Because of the large 360 Encompass install base, and because Solventum's autonomous coding is being incorporated into 360 Encompass, it was difficult to identify customers truly using autonomous coding. A conservative approach was taken, only including organizations with evidence of true autonomous coding.

While Solventum customers reported the following success stories:

  • One federal healthcare agency implemented Solventum's computer-assisted coding tools across its network, supporting automated coding workflows and improving administrative efficiency.
  • A health system used Solventum's prioritization and ROI features to increase clinical documentation improvement (CDI) impact and provide individualized CDI scorecards to leadership.
