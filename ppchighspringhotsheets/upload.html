Upload Instructions

Upload your CSV file with Bullhorn interaction data. The system expects the following columns:

  • owner - The person this interaction should be documented under
  • Name (or name) - Contact name
  • email - Contact email address
  • dateLastComment - Date of the last activity

Note: If there's already a Bullhorn record for this client and day, the system will skip adding a duplicate.
