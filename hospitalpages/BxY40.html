Parkview DeKalb Hospital

Welcome to Parkview DeKalb Hospital, located at 1316 E Seventh St in Auburn, IN. We are committed to providing exceptional care with a wide range of services, including a 24/7 emergency department, advanced diagnostic imaging, specialty clinics, and comprehensive inpatient and outpatient services. Our state-of-the-art facility features a surgery center with robotic capabilities, a sleep disorders lab, and rehabilitation services. Partnered with DeKalb County for EMS, we ensure the health and well-being of our community with dedication and compassion. Experience the resources of the Parkview Health system right here in Auburn!

Tasha Eicher

President

Tasha Eicher joined Parkview Health in 2019 when DeKalb Health joined the system. In 2020, she was mentioned as the President of Parkview DeKalb Hospital. She also serves in community organizations including St. Martin's Healthcare Clinic, United Way of DeKalb County, Community Foundation of DeKalb County, Big Brothers Big Sisters, and the Hicksville School Foundation. She holds associate degrees in business and health information management, and bachelor's and master's degrees in business administration.
