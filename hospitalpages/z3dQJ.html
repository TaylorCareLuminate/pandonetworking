Pike County Memorial Hospital

Pike County Memorial Hospital, located in Louisiana, Missouri, is dedicated to providing quality healthcare to our community. As a critical access hospital, we offer a range of services, including emergency care, radiology, and laboratory services, all delivered with a patient-centered approach. Our team of dedicated professionals is committed to ensuring the comfort and well-being of you and your loved ones. We continually strive to enhance our services through advanced medicine and technology, ensuring access to better healthcare right here in our region. For exceptional care close to home, choose Pike County Memorial Hospital.
