Everyone in HealthCare Should Care

Just because we don't take blood pressure or administer medications does not mean that we don't work to always hold ourselves to the standards that everyone in healthcare should be. Our focus is on creating efficiencies and improvements to healthcare.

We are passionate about revolutionizing medicine through AI, technology and human caring.

HealthLuminate is part of CareLuminate Inc. that has a mission to revolutionize healthcare through transparency into the quality of care.

Our C.O.D.E. of Values:

  • Commitment to Better Healthcare: Too often, healthcare is a business without a commitment to patients. We understand the vulnerability of healthcare. We are committed to ensuring our every effort truly improves healthcare.
  • Obsessed With Excellence: Our research and services have real impact on the care given to patients. We have to be right, every time, for every patient and every community.
  • Dedicated to Truth and Veracity: No matter how hard it is to face, the truth is always the best place to start. We will always share what we have found to be the truth, no matter the cost.
  • Enthusiasm for Transparency: Transparency changes everything in healthcare for the better. We are working to unravel years of sheltered decisions that hurt both the affordability and the quality of healthcare. Sunlight is the best disinfectant.
