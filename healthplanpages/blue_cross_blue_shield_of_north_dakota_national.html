State Leadership

Dan Conrad

President and CEO

Previously served as CLO, providing strategic and legal expertise. Progressively took on operational and executive responsibility before becoming CEO on Jan 1, 2020. Native North Dakotan with more than 20 years of varied experience. Joined BCBSND in July 2013 after spending time as in-house counsel at two large public companies and started his career as a trial attorney with the U.S. Justice Department. [1, 6]

Amber Blomberg

Executive Director of the BCBSND Caring Foundation

Promoted to this role in Sept 2023. Joined Blue in 2012 and previously held the position of Community Partnerships and Caring Foundation manager for over a decade. Involved in the management and growth of the Foundation's efforts such as grant and scholarship programs, employee engagement initiatives, charitable giving, and community resources. Graduate of Concordia College with a bachelor's degree in marketing and business management. [4]

Kristi Huber

Senior Vice President and Chief Marketing Officer

Hired in April 2021, began in the role on June 1, 2021. Previously President & CEO of United Way of Cass-Clay. Worked in the insurance industry as a broker before joining United Way. Served in various executive level roles providing expertise in strategic leadership, relationship development, and served as a brand and community ambassador and spokesperson. Responsible for providing executive leadership and direction for strategic marketing and sales plans. Earned her bachelor's degree in Business and English at Concordia College. [5]

Krystal Wheeler Dennis

Vice President of Pharmacy Operations

Promoted to this role in March 2024. Oversees pharmacy operations for BCBSND and provides key leadership, direction and vision for the pharmacy division. Joined the BCBSND team in 2018 as director of pharmacy consulting after nearly a decade of working in long-term care and pharmacy retail. Most recently served as the director of pharmacy operations. Graduated with a Doctor of Pharmacy degree from Creighton University. [8]
