Lisa Buckley

General Manager for the Midwest, Cigna Healthcare

Lisa Buckley was named Cigna Healthcare General Manager for its group employer business in the Midwest in February 2024. She is responsible for driving market growth for client employers with under 3,000 employees and ensuring the local market strategy, product, and network offerings meet customer and client needs. She also works with network leaders and medical executives on community outreach. Lisa brings over 30 years of experience building strategic client relationships and managing high-performing sales teams with Cigna Healthcare, having previously served as the Existing Business Sales Manager for the Midwest Middle Market segment and held roles in National Accounts. She resides in Chicago and is based at Cigna Healthcare's Chicago office. [3]
