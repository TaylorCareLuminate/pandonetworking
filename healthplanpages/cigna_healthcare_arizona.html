State Leadership

Edward Kim

President of Cigna Medical Group, President of Arizona Health Plans and GM of Arizona Health Plans

Has been the President and General Manager of Arizona Health Plans at Cigna Corp. since November 2013. Serves as the President of Cigna Medical Group. Senior health care executive with over 25 years of experience in complex service delivery markets, including managed care, physician practice management and health care consulting. Prior to joining Cigna in 2011, he served as the President of Molina Medical Group in California and subsequently expanded his role to drive national expansion of direct delivery of medical services as corporate vice president at Molina Healthcare. Serves on the board of directors for the Boys and Girls Club Metro Phoenix and the Partners in Care Foundation.

Pete Chuchro

General Manager for Arizona Market (Group Employer Business)

Named to this role in December 2023. Responsible for driving market growth for client employers with under 3,000 employees in Arizona. Has been with Cigna Healthcare for nearly two decades. Most recently served as the Director of Risk Management and Underwriting for the Arizona market. Veteran and served as an Army Ranger Team Leader. Resides in Peoria, Arizona and is based at Cigna Healthcare's Scottsdale office.

Jeff Holt

President and General Manager of Cigna Medical Group

Named to this role in June 2022. Leads the medical group through its next chapter of growth and innovation in Arizona. Has 20 years of experience leading large integrated health care organizations with roles in strategy and business development, operations, clinical quality and patient and provider experience. Most recently served as chief operating officer at a hospital within Robert Wood Johnson Barnabas Health. Prior to this, he served in various executive leadership positions at hospitals within Kaleida Health and Meridian Health.
