2025-04-22
Molina Healthcare of California celebrated the opening of its first Northern California One Stop Help Center in Sacramento on April 22, 2025. The center offers various services including health care enrollment support, utility assistance, housing resources, and more, in partnership with local organizations.
2025-03-17
Molina Healthcare of California hosted a grand opening and ribbon-cutting ceremony for its new One Stop Help Center in Chula Vista on March 17, 2025. This center is the third community support location opened by Molina in Southern California in the past year, offering various services in partnership with local organizations.
