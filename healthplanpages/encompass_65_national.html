Michael W. Cropp, M.D.

President and Chief Executive Officer

Dr. Cropp is the president and chief executive officer at Independent Health. Under his leadership, the organization delivers products and services that improve the health of almost 375,000 customers. [2] He is a board-certified family physician with over 25 years of experience. [2] He joined Independent Health in 1996 as executive vice president and chief medical officer and was appointed president and CEO in 2004.
