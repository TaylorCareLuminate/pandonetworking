Recent News

2025-07-01
Health New England has committed $185,000 to fund health equity mini-grants in Western and Central Massachusetts. These grants, up to $10,000 per organization, are available to eligible 501(c)(3) nonprofits addressing health or healthcare-related needs in Berkshire, Franklin, Hampden, Hampshire, and/or Worcester counties. The grants aim to support organizations focusing on social determinants of health such as built environment, education, employment, housing, and social environment. The application deadline is August 4th, and more information can be found on the Health New England website. [3]
2025-01-14
Baystate Health announced in January 2025 that it would not be selling its insurance arm, Health New England, to Point32Health as previously planned. The $165 million sale had received regulatory approval but was called off after further analysis by both parties. Health New England is set to continue operating as usual under Baystate Health ownership, with no immediate changes expected for members' coverage, benefits, or provider networks. Baystate Health plans to evaluate the strategic positioning and performance of Health New England in the coming months to inform future decisions. [1, 2]
