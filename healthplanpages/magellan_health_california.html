Recent News

2025-06-22
Magellan Health provides comprehensive behavioral health services and mental health treatment coverage in California. Through partnerships, individuals with Magellan insurance can access a network of qualified mental health providers, treatment facilities, and specialized programs for various mental health conditions and substance use disorders, designed to meet state and federal parity laws.
2025-05-29
Magellan Health Insurance is accepted at various treatment centers in California for drug and alcohol rehab. Magellan's coverage for substance use disorders and co-occurring mental health disorders supports evidence-based treatments, including medication-assisted treatment, and aims to help individuals recover. Coverage details depend on individual plans, including commercial, Medicaid, Medicare, and Medi-Cal populations.
2025-02-06
Human Affairs International of California (HAI-CA), a subsidiary of Magellan Healthcare Inc., has earned a full three-year Managed Behavioral Healthcare Organization (MBHO) Accreditation from The National Committee for Quality Assurance (NCQA) for its Commercial, Exchange, Medicaid and Medicare product lines. This accreditation recognizes HAI-CA's compliance with approximately 60 standards for quality in managing behavioral health and substance use disorders.
2025-01-10
Magellan Health Opens 24-hour Crisis Line for Individuals Impacted by Wildfires in California. The complimentary service, available via a toll-free number, offers confidential consultation and resources to help people cope with the emotional impact of natural disasters and provides guidance to other available support.

State Leadership

Dr. Andrew Sassani

Corporate Vice President and Chief Medical Officer for California Behavioral Health Services

Recruited to Magellan in early 2012. Prior to Magellan, held various leadership roles in direct-care services including department chair and hospital vice chief of staff in southern California. In 2017, received a faculty appointment as associate professor of psychiatry at a University of California teaching hospital. Residency trained at Harvard, and is board certified by the American Board of Psychiatry and Neurology.
