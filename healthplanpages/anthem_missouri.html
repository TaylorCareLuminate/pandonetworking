2025-06-10

2025-06-02
Contract negotiations between MU Health Care and Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield remained far apart as of early June 2025, with nearly 100,000 patients covered by Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield being out of network with MU Health Care since the end of March.
2025-05-07
The contract dispute between MU Health Care and Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield, which resulted in MU Health Care being out-of-network for many Anthem members since April 1, 2025, also impacted University of Missouri students with Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield insurance plans.
2025-04-01
As of April 1, 2025, MU Health Care became out of network with Anthem's commercial plans, impacting patients with employer-sponsored, ACA marketplace, and other Blue Cross Blue Shield plans, potentially leading to higher out-of-pocket costs. This change followed months of unsuccessful negotiations regarding healthcare pricing.
2025-02-12
University of Missouri Health Care notified patients that effective April 1, 2025, their participation in Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield Medicare Advantage plans would terminate, meaning patients would no longer have in-network access to MU Health Care services, doctors, and facilities through these plans. Negotiations for commercial plans were also ongoing.
