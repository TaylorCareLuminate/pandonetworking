Gary D. St. Hilaire

Chairman and Chief Executive Officer

Mr. St. Hilaire is Chairman and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Horizon Blue Cross Blue Shield of New Jersey, the region's leading health solutions company. [4] He has nearly 35 years of experience as a health care executive, entrepreneur and financial leader with experience in strategy, start-up operations, and business turnarounds. [4] Before joining Horizon BCBSNJ, he led the transformation of Capital BlueCross into a nationally diversified family of companies. [4] Prior to Capital BlueCross, Mr. St. Hilaire co-founded the health care technology company Choicelinx, achieving success as an industry innovator. [4] He also served in executive leadership roles with Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Massachusetts, where he led a $200 million turnaround, and Healthsource, Inc., a publicly-traded managed care company. [4] In addition to his role as President and CEO of Horizon BCBSNJ, Gary serves as the chair of the National Institute for Health Care Management (NIHCM) and the NIHCM Foundation, and serves on the boards of directors of the Blue Cross and Blue Shield Association and of America's Health Insurance Plans (AHIP), BCS Financial, Ernest Health, and the New Jersey Performing Arts Center. [4] Gary D. st. Hilaire is also listed as President and Chief Executive Officer. [5]
