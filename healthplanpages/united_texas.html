Recent News

2025-06-01
UnitedHealthcare Community Plan of Texas announced a new Medicaid billing process for Dual Special Needs Plan (D-SNP) members, effective September 1, 2025. This change is mandated by Texas House Bill 1, and UnitedHealthcare Community Plan of Texas will now handle billing directly for Medicaid-covered services and medication for these members.
2025-06-01
UnitedHealthcare Community Plan of Texas announced a new Medicaid billing process effective Sept. 1, 2025, for Dual Special Needs Plan (D-SNP) members, mandated by Texas House Bill 1. This change means providers will bill UnitedHealthcare Community Plan of Texas directly for Medicaid-covered services and medication, instead of the Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC).
2025-02-19
UnitedHealth Group provided an update on a Texas Hospital Matter, stating that UnitedHealthcare approved the outpatient surgery and post-surgical overnight observation stay on December 27, 2024. The company's inquiry to the hospital was due to an erroneous order for a separate inpatient stay request, which the hospital acknowledged. The patient received all necessary care and was not responsible for the erroneous bill.
2025-02-19

Their inquiry to the hospital was due to an erroneous separate inpatient stay request. They stated their medical director called the hospital for clarification regarding this request and did not ask to speak with the physician in surgery or ask her to leave surgery.

Scott Flannery

CEO, UnitedHealthcare, Employer & Individual in Texas and Oklahoma

Serves as chief executive officer of UnitedHealthcare's (UHC) Employer & Individual operations in Texas and Oklahoma. His role expanded to cover the remainder of Texas markets in November 2022. He previously served as health plan CEO from 2011-13 and returned in April 2017 after being UnitedHealthcare's central region growth officer and the sales national practice lead for UMR. He has held other sales leadership roles since joining the company in 1999, including vice president of sales and account management and senior account executive. Before joining UnitedHealthcare, he held various positions with Prudential Healthcare and started his healthcare career at The New England. He serves on the board of directors for the Collin County Business Alliance and is a member of the Junior League of Dallas Advisory Board. He earned a Bachelor of Arts in economics and business administration from Ursinus College.
