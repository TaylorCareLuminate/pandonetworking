Thomas Halloran

President and CEO, EVP & CFO

President and CEO of Fidelis Care, which is part of the Centene family of health plans. Served as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of Fidelis Care for more than two years, with responsibility for overseeing provider operations, finance, IT, strategic planning, and product development. Joined Fidelis Care as Chief Financial Officer in 2007. Prior to joining Fidelis Care, spent 20 years providing financial, strategic, and operational consulting services. Was a founding partner at Huron Consulting Group and a partner at Andersen LLP in New York City. Received a Bachelor's Degree in Business Administration from Iona College and is a certified public accountant.
