State Leadership

Margaret Anderson

President, Health Alliance Plan

Named permanent president in May 2025. Joined HAP in 2018 as senior vice president, chief sales and marketing officer. Held leadership roles at Blue Shield of California, WellCare Health Plans, and HealthNow New York.

Michael Genord, M.D.

President and Chief Executive Officer, HAP; Executive Vice President, Henry Ford Health

Became president and CEO of HAP in 2020. Previously HAP's senior vice president and chief medical officer. Only physician/CEO of a health benefits company in Michigan. Also deals with concerns of individuals who often cannot afford insurance and suffer from chronic health problems as president and CEO of HAP Empowered Health Plan, Inc.

Sarah Musser

HAP CareSource Market President, Michigan

Oversees program operations and initiatives for HAP CareSource across Michigan, including building strategic partnerships that help achieve better health outcomes. Over a decade of experience in health care management and finance. Previously held leadership roles with Cardinal Health, Battelle, and EY.
