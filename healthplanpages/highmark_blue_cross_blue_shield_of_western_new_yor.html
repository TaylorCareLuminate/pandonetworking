State Leadership

Jessica Cox

President, Highmark Western and Northeastern New York

Succeeded Michael Edbauer as Market President in July 2024. Seasoned healthcare executive with over two decades of experience in the New York market, including 23 years at Excellus BlueCross BlueShield in various leadership roles across commercial sales, provider relations, and corporate development. Previously served as senior vice president for corporate development and strategy and chief risk officer and regional president for the Southern Tier Region for Excellus. Began career as a medical technologist. Resident of Western New York. Holds a bachelor of science from Upstate Medical University, an associate of science from Alfred State College, and completed an executive education program from the University of Chicago Booth School of Business Executive Education.

Michael Edbauer

Senior Vice President of Markets (formerly Market President for Highmark Western and Northeastern New York)

Promoted to Senior Vice President of Markets, in charge of provider partnership strategy across New York and Highmark's multi-state footprint. Previously served as market president for Highmark Western and Northeastern New York. Was in charge locally of HealthNow's transition to Highmark over the last three years.

Sheila L. Brown

Board of Directors, Highmark Western and Northeastern New York

Elected to the Board of Directors in March 2024. Visionary leader, three-time author, and CEO of Vision Multi-Media and owner of WUFO 1080 AM radio. First African-American female radio owner in Buffalo. Also CEO and President of the WUFO Black Radio History Collective.

Dave Anderson

Board of Directors, Highmark Western and Northeastern New York (formerly CEO of HealthNow New York)

Former CEO of HealthNow New York, continued in the role through the end of 2021 and joined the Board of Directors of Highmark Western and Northeastern New York in 2022. Led HealthNow during the affiliation with Highmark.

Julie Snyder

Overseeing New York-based community affairs and charitable giving (formerly SVP and Chief Marketing Officer at HealthNow)

Formerly HealthNow's senior vice president and chief marketing officer, stepped into a new role overseeing New York-based community affairs and charitable giving after the affiliation.
