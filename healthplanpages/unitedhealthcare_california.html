Recent News

2025-06-12
UnitedHealthcare Benefits Plan of California, an underwriter for individual and family health plans and part of UnitedHealth Group, continues to operate and innovate, offering PPO and EPO plans. The company, founded in 1974, has introduced various digital tools over the years and has been accredited by the National Committee for Quality Assurance (NCQA).
2025-06-01
UnitedHealthcare has updated its Prescription Drug Lists for California plans, including Standard Tier 4 PPO, Advantage Tier 3, Large Group Access 1-3 Tier, and Large Group Access 1-4 Tier plans, with various effective dates in 2025.
2025-05-20
UnitedHealthcare updated its mailing address for submitting formal contract notices. The announcement was made in its May 2025 Network News and provided an address in Minnetonka, MN, for notices via certified or overnight mail, emphasizing it is not for claims. The article also mentioned other California-specific updates like the Medical Board of California's transition to paperless renewals.
2025-05-13
UnitedHealth Group withdrew its financial outlook for 2025. An office building in Santa Ana, California, was shown in a file photo accompanying the article, which also discussed the killing of a UnitedHealthcare CEO and related events impacting the company.
2025-05-07
Shareholders have sued UnitedHealth, claiming the company misled them by not adjusting financial projections following changes to corporate practices after the killing of a top executive. The lawsuit mentions the involvement of a California pension fund in a separate complaint regarding the company's practices.
2025-05-01
UnitedHealthcare Community Plan highlights five benefits of Dual Health Plans available in California, such as monthly credits for everyday needs, dental coverage, UCard for purchasing healthy food and OTC products, virtual medical visits, and help with utility bills.
2025-03-28
The California Public Employees' Retirement System (CalPERS) has accused UnitedHealth Group of securities fraud in an amended class action lawsuit. The lawsuit, filed in Minnesota, alleges that UnitedHealth misled investors about its Medicare Advantage billing practices, including 'upcoding,' and that executives engaged in insider trading.
2025-03-18
UnitedHealthcare announced rate updates for the third quarter of 2025 in California, including a 1.8% statewide increase for HMO plans and a 3.5% statewide increase for PPO plans. The company is also working with regulators regarding the effective date and coverage requirements for California Senate Bill 729, which mandates treatment for fertility and infertility services.
2025-02-05
UnitedHealthcare agreed to a $2.5 million settlement in California to resolve a class action lawsuit alleging unauthorized telemarketing calls in violation of federal law. The settlement impacts over 12,000 individuals who may have received settlement notices via email or mail with instructions on how to claim a payment by April 15, 2025.
2025-01-07
UnitedHealthcare announced support measures for healthcare professionals in Los Angeles and Ventura counties affected by the January wildfires. These measures include extending prior authorization and timely claim filing deadlines and allowing care from alternative settings.
