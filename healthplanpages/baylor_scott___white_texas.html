Recent News

March 20, 2025
The Communities Connected – Hill Country group, part of the Baylor Scott & White Central Texas Foundation, donated over $27,000 in 2024 to support the needs of Baylor Scott & White Medical Center – Marble Falls. The funds were specifically allocated to purchase a stretcher for the emergency department, designed with enhanced safety and comfort features for both patients and staff.
March 17, 2025
Baylor Scott & White Texas Spine & Joint Hospital reported a data security incident where an unauthorized party accessed a company email account. This breach potentially compromised sensitive patient information, including names, dates of birth, medical information, and billing and claims information. Notification letters were sent to affected individuals.
June 27, 2025
Baylor Scott & White Health is set to open its new medical center in Frisco, Texas, at PGA Parkway in the summer of 2025. The facility is a 340,000-square-foot, 84-bed hospital offering a range of services including cardiovascular care, emergency care, women's health, surgery, lab, and imaging. The campus will also feature communal green spaces and over two miles of trails. Construction on the project began in 2023 and the opening is anticipated in July 2025.
June 25, 2025
Baylor Scott & White Health's medical center in McKinney is planning a vertical expansion set to begin in the fall of 2025. The project is expected to add up to 84,000 square feet by its completion, with plans to add a seventh and eighth floor to the hospital to meet the increasing demand for hospital services in the rapidly growing McKinney area.
January 24, 2025
Baylor Scott & White Health has implemented ambient listening technology in its facilities across Texas. This technology utilizes artificial intelligence to record and transcribe conversations between physicians and patients to automatically generate clinical documentation, although its adoption has raised some concerns regarding patient privacy.
February 20, 2025
Baylor Scott & White Medical Center – Waxahachie has been recognized with the prestigious Magnet with Distinction® designation for nursing excellence by the American Nurses Credentialing Center (ANCC). This places the hospital among a select group of top-performing Magnet® organizations globally, acknowledging its dedication to quality nursing practice.
