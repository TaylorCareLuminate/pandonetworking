State Leadership

Gregory A. Adams

Chairman & CEO

Is the chair and chief executive officer of Kaiser Foundation Health Plan, Inc. and Hospitals. A nationally recognized leader and a champion of health care transformation, improving access, and pushing for better health outcomes. Has over 30 years of leadership experience as a senior health care executive and has played an integral role in leading the transformation and improvement of patient care outcomes at Kaiser Permanente. Prior to his current role, Adams served as executive vice president and group president, with direct responsibility for health plan and hospital operations in all 8 Kaiser Permanente regions. He was also regional president of Kaiser Permanente for Northern California.

Janet Liang

Executive Vice President, Group President of California, and COO of National Care Delivery Operations

Is executive vice president, group president of California, and COO of national care delivery operations for Kaiser Foundation Health Plan, Inc. and Hospitals. Throughout her career, Liang has championed the adoption of new technologies and evidence-based practice to improve the lives of members. Her 30-year career has included numerous president and executive roles in Hawaii and Washington for Group Health Cooperative. In 2022, Liang was named one of the Top 25 Women Leaders in Health Care by Modern Healthcare magazine. Liang has also been recognized as a Game Changer by the Silicon Valley Leadership Group and repeatedly honored by the San Francisco Business Times as one of the most influential women in Bay Area business.
