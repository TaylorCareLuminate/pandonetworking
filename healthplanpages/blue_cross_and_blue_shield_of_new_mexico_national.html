Recent News

2025-06-25
Blue Cross and Blue Shield of New Mexico is celebrating its 85th anniversary, marking over eight decades of commitment to improving the health and well-being of New Mexicans through various programs and services. The company traces its history back to 1940 and has grown to be one of the state's largest health benefits providers. In recent years, BCBSNM has focused on addressing social and economic factors influencing health, including expanding the Care Van® mobile health program and opening the Blue Door Neighborhood Center in Albuquerque in August 2024. They have also made significant community investments, awarding over $800,000 in grants to nonprofits in 2025 and contributing substantially to nursing and physician workforce development programs in 2023 and 2024.
