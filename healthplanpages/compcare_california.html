Recent News

2025-05-27
Anthem Blue Cross of California has expanded its behavioral health network by 10,000 providers in the past two years, adding partnerships with Rula, Headway and Octave to connect members with in-network mental health providers.
2025-05-13
Anthem Blue Cross and Scripps Health have agreed to an extension of their contract through September 2026, restoring in-network access for Anthem members to Scripps Health doctors and hospitals in San Diego.
2025-04-04
Anthem Blue Cross and Aspire Health, a medical group in Monterey, have reached a contract agreement, returning Aspire Health to the Anthem Select HMO network effective March 1, 2025, for CalPERS members.
2025-02-26
Anthem Blue Cross Foundation, the philanthropic arm of Anthem Blue Cross of California, announced a $300,000 inaugural impact investment loan to Burlingame-based Psyched Services to support mental health services in California schools.
2025-02-24
Anthem Blue Cross recognized three California-based health care providers: Torrance Memorial IPA, Seoul Medical Group, and Korean American Medical Group, in its inaugural Care Provider Recognition Program.
