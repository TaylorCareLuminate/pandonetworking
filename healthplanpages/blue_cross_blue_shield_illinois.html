Recent News

2025-04-29
Illinois residents with certain Blue Cross Blue Shield HMO Illinois plans will see a change effective July 1, 2025, as Advocate Physician Partners, including Advocate Medical Group, will no longer be part of this network. Blue Cross Blue Shield of Illinois stated they were informed by Advocate Physician Partners of their departure. This change does not affect Advocate Health hospitals or other BCBSIL plans like Advantage HMO, BlueCare Direct, Medicare Advantage, and Community MMAI. Members affected were advised to select a new medical group by May 31, 2025, or one would be assigned.
2025-04-25
Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Illinois (BCBSIL) experienced unauthorized activity on its Blue Access for Members portal between November 8, 2024, and March 5, 2025. Discovered on February 11, 2025, the incident may have resulted in protected health information and personally identifiable information of 6,903 members being viewed. While BCBSIL stated there is no evidence of misuse, they are offering affected members a year of free identity monitoring services.
