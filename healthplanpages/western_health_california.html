Recent News

2025-06-17
CalPERS announced changes to health plans for 2026, noting that Western Health Advantage MyCare Select will be exiting all counties and will no longer be part of the CalPERS health plan offerings.
2025-02-03
Western Health Advantage (WHA) announced that Garry Maisel, the company's founding Chief Executive Officer and President, will retire effective December 31, 2025. Maisel led the organization for almost 30 years, focusing on providing access to quality local health care and serving as a community leader. A transition plan is in place, with Dr. Khuram Arif taking over as President and later CEO.
2025-01-23
Sacramento Republic FC and founding partner Western Health Advantage are continuing their 11-year partnership with a renewed focus on community health and well-being, specifically promoting heart health and mental wellbeing. They will continue to use the 988 National Suicide and Crisis Lifeline patch on team uniforms to raise awareness and encourage conversations around mental health.

State Leadership

Garry Maisel

Chief Executive Officer

Garry Maisel has been with Western Health Advantage (WHA) since its inception in 1996, serving as President and CEO. He led the company from its startup to current annual revenues exceeding $1 billion. Maisel is recognized for his commitment to improving healthcare access and affordability and developing innovative programs. He is also actively involved in the Sacramento and North Bay Regions community, serving on numerous non-profit boards. He holds a degree in finance and economics from California State University Sacramento and has over 34 years of experience in healthcare plus 10 years in banking and finance. Garry Maisel is set to retire at the end of 2025.

Khuram Arif, M.D., MBA

President

As of July 1, 2024, Dr. Khuram Arif serves as the President of Western Health Advantage. He is a board-certified pediatrician with 19 years of practice experience. Dr. Arif has a decade of experience in physician leadership guiding medical groups in the transition from volume to value-based care. Prior to his role as President, he served as Chief Medical Officer at Woodland Clinic and Medical Director Managed Care at Mercy Medical Group. Upon Garry Maisel's retirement at the end of 2025, Dr. Arif will also assume the role of CEO.

Rick Heron

Chief Experience Officer

Rick Heron joined Western Health Advantage's senior management team in 2005. He has held roles as Director of Community Relations and Chief Marketing and Brand Officer before becoming Chief Experience Officer. He is responsible for coordinating the organization's external affairs, including advertising, marketing, communications, branding, charitable outreach, public affairs, and member services. His background also includes experience with the San Francisco Giants, Sacramento Kings, and AOL INC.

Jeff Cinciarelli

Chief Sales Officer (CSO)

Jeff Cinciarelli is a Sacramento-based health insurance industry veteran with over 25 years of experience. He joined Western Health Advantage as Chief Sales Officer after serving as Area President for Sacramento with Arthur J. Gallagher & Co. He also held the role of Vice President of Commercial Products and Labor & Trust with HealthNet, Inc., where he led the Central Valley's sales effort.

Rebecca Downing

Chief Legal Officer

Rebecca Downing provides legal and compliance leadership for Western Health Advantage. She is responsible for the company's Legal, Compliance, and Privacy and Information Security programs. Her background includes significant experience working in state government and the private sector with a focus on health policy, compliance, and law, including involvement in health care reform efforts related to the Affordable Care Act.

Dr. Kiran Biring

Chief Pharmacy Officer

Dr. Kiran Biring is a healthcare leader with nearly 20 years of experience across multiple sectors in the healthcare industry. Since joining Western Health Advantage in 2021, she has provided strategic and operational leadership for the Pharmacy and Medical Management Department, with a background spanning the public health sector, hospital systems, long-term care, pharmacy benefit management (PBM), and national health plans.
