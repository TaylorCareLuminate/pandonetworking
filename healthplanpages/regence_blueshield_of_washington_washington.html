State Leadership

Michael Cole

President, Washington and Oregon markets

Named president of Regence BlueShield's Washington market, while maintaining his Oregon market leadership position in January 2025. [3, 7, 8, 9] Oversees strategy and growth for Regence BlueShield in Washington, which serves over 1.6 million people. [8, 9] Took over from Claire Verity upon her retirement. [3, 8, 9] Joined Regence in 2023. [3, 7, 8, 9] Previously served as president of Aetna's North Atlantic region, overseeing 11 states and 6 million members. [3, 8, 9] Brings over 20 years of executive healthcare leadership experience across payer, payment integrity, and healthcare diagnostics. [8, 9] Longtime board member at Erie Family Health Centers and on the board of Trillium Family Services. [8, 9]

Stacy Crim

Vice President, Washington market

Took on an expanded role as vice president, Washington market, adding leadership of account management in the state to her current duties in February 2025. [2] Served as vice president of small business in Washington since 2022. [2] Served as director of sales and account management for Regence's 51-99 segment since January 2019. [6] Has worked in account management and strategic planning in the healthcare industry for 25 years and has lived in Washington for over two decades. [2] Holds a master's degree of health administration from the University of Washington and a bachelor's degree from Winthrop University. [2]

Stephanie Walker

Vice President for the Washington State Health Care Authority (HCA) and public sector

Took on this role in February 2025, adding oversight of all public-sector accounts to her current duties overseeing the HCA business. [2] Served as vice president of key accounts in Washington since 2022. [2]

Claire Verity

Former President, Regence BlueShield (Washington)

Retired in January 2025 after a three-decade career in the healthcare industry, including four years as president of Regence BlueShield. [3, 8, 9] Served as president of Regence BlueShield as of March 2022. [6]
