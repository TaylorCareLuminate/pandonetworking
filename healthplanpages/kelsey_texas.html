Recent News

May 6, 2025
Harris County Sheriff's Office deputies fatally shot a man who was allegedly trespassing with a knife in the parking lot of a Kelsey-Seybold Clinic in north Houston. Authorities responded to a call about a suspicious person in a vehicle who refused to leave when asked by clinic security. The incident is under investigation.
May 23, 2025
Kelsey-Seybold is urging the City of Conroe, Texas, to allow its $24 million medical facility project to move forward despite a city-wide building moratorium. The company indicated it might have to withdraw the project if unable to proceed, impacting its ability to serve the Conroe area where it currently leases space and plans to build a new clinic.
May 21, 2025
Dunaway, a civil engineering firm, highlighted its decade-long partnership with Kelsey-Seybold Clinic in a news article. The article details Dunaway's role in providing civil engineering services for numerous Kelsey-Seybold expansion and renovation projects across the Greater Houston area as Kelsey-Seybold works towards its goal of having a clinic within 15 minutes of every Houston resident.
June 11, 2025
Kelsey-Seybold is reportedly seeking to build a new medical clinic in east Pearland, Texas. The company is pursuing rezoning approvals for 35 acres of land along Pearland Parkway for the proposed clinic, aiming to expand its reach to serve communities in eastern Pearland, Friendswood, and other nearby areas.
