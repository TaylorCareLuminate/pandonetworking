2025-06-30
Sharp HealthCare announced a workforce reduction on June 30, 2025. This cut represents about 1.5% of Sharp's workforce and includes some executive and management positions, primarily in non-clinical areas such as IT, human resources, and marketing. The company cited challenging economic conditions and rising costs as reasons for the layoffs.

Sharp Healthcare has threatened to file a lawsuit against Palomar Health for allegedly breaching an exclusivity arrangement established last year, after Palomar recently accepted a loan from UC San Diego Health. The arrangement between Sharp and Palomar Health was formalized through a Letter of Intent to work towards a joint affiliation, where Sharp would expand its network into North County and Palomar's patients would have access to Sharp's specialized services. This news was reported on March 27, 2025.
