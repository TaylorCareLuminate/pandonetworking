Recent News

2025-02-10
Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Kansas (BCBSKS) is investing $2.5 million in Wichita State University, with $1.8 million dedicated as the lead gift to the Wichita Biomedical Campus and the remaining $700,000 allocated to create two endowed nursing scholarships in the WSU College of Health Professions. This investment aims to support the training of future health care workers and enhance access to medical services in Kansas.

State Leadership

Matt All

President and CEO

Matt All is the President and CEO of Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Kansas (BCBSKS). [2, 4] He joined BCBSKS in 2006 as its General Counsel and became the eighth President and CEO in April 2018. [2] He brings nearly three decades of experience in leadership and service. [2] Prior to joining BCBSKS, All spent several years in private legal practice before serving in state government in Kansas as Assistant Insurance Commissioner and Chief Counsel to Governor Kathleen Sebelius. [2] He led the company’s preparation for the Affordable Care Act and navigated the company and its members through navigating the health care system during the COVID-19 pandemic. [2] In addition to his role as President/CEO, All serves in leadership positions for the company’s subsidiaries, including Chairman of Advance Insurance Company of Kansas, Chairman/CEO of BlueCross BlueShield Kansas Solutions, and President of the Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Kansas Foundation. [2]

Treena Mason

Executive Vice President, Chief Sales Officer

Treena Mason is the Executive Vice President and Chief Sales Officer of Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Kansas (BCBSKS). [8] She has served in this role since 2023. [8] Mason joined Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Kansas in 1997 as a Group Consultant. [8] She was promoted to Regional Manager in 2003, Chief Marketing Officer and Vice President of Group Sales in 2013, and Senior Vice President, Sales and Operations in mid-2020. [8] She is a graduate of the company's Blue University Leadership Quest program. [8] Mason plays a pivotal role in driving company growth, building brand value and enhancing relationships. [8] She previously served on the boards for PrairieStar Health in Hutchinson, Kansas, and Heartland Community Health Center in Lawrence, Kansas, both Federally Qualified Health Centers; and Apostrophe Health. [8] Mason currently serves as a director on the board of the Wichita Chamber of Commerce and is on the Dean’s Advisory Board for Wichita State University’s Barton School of Business. [8]

Staci Berger

Vice President, Network Solutions

Staci Berger joined BCBSKS in July (2024) as the new Vice President of Network Solutions. [7] In this role, Staci will be instrumental in developing and executing innovative network programs and creating high-value network products and solutions. [7] Berger brings more than 20 years of experience in the health care industry, having previously held significant roles at UnitedHealthcare and Humana. [7] A native Kansan, Staci is deeply passionate about serving members and has a strong background in optimizing health care operations and network processes. [7]
