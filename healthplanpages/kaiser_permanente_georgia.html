2025-05-13
Kaiser Permanente is expanding health care access in Coweta County, Georgia, with the construction of a new 36,000-square-foot Fischer Crossings Medical Center. The facility, scheduled to open in fall 2026, will offer a range of primary and specialty care services, imaging, lab work, and pharmacy access. This expansion coincides with Kaiser Permanente's 40th anniversary serving the Atlanta area. [4]
2025-02-27
Kaiser Permanente updated information regarding its participation in the Georgia Families (Medicaid) program, noting that more than 21,000 patients are assigned through contracts with three Care Management Organizations. The update also detailed their efforts in social health screening and connecting members to community resources.

Corwin N. Harper

President, Kaiser Foundation Health Plan of Georgia

Corwin N. Harper was named president of Kaiser Foundation Health Plan of Georgia, effective January 21, 2025. [3, 4] He brings 39 years of health care and hospital leadership experience, including 25 years at Kaiser Permanente in Northern California where he held various senior vice president, medical group administrator, and area manager roles, and served as director of hospital operations with The Permanente Medical Group. [3, 4] Most recently, he was CEO of Ochsner LSU Health North Louisiana and earlier served as regional CEO and chief growth officer for Ochsner Health. [3, 4]

Pamela Shipley

Regional President

Pamela Shipley was appointed as the new regional president for Kaiser Permanente's Georgia operation, as of January 10, 2022. [2] She came from Sharecare, where she was chief operating officer. [2] Prior to Sharecare, Shipley worked for 12 years at Centene Corp., where she served in various roles, including senior vice president with oversight of over 15 markets, chief executive officer in three different markets (including Georgia), and executive leader for the enterprise's digital strategy. [2]
