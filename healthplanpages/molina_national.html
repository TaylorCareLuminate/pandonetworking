2025-05-22
Molina Healthcare of Iowa has several news and updates relevant to providers. These include information on their Provider Advisory Council, changes to clinical information requests for authorizations effective October 1, 2024, a Maternal Health Provider Incentive Program ending June 30, 2025, and encouragement for providers to use Availity Essentials for prior authorization requests. Additionally, updated 2025 provider materials are available.
2025-04-24
Molina reported mixed results for the first quarter of 2025, with earnings and revenue beating analyst expectations, but a higher-than-expected medical loss ratio, particularly in Affordable Care Act plans. The increase in medical costs was attributed to factors including utilization of long-term supports and services, expensive drugs, behavioral health, seasonal illnesses, lower-than-expected risk scores for ACA plans, and higher medical costs among new members from the ConnectiCare acquisition. Despite this, Molina reiterated its guidance for 2025, expecting 8% earnings growth and $42 billion in premium revenue.
2025-03-07
Attorney General Ken Paxton announced that Texas has secured $40 million following a Medicaid fraud investigation into Molina Healthcare of Texas, Inc. and Molina Healthcare, Inc. The settlement resolves allegations that Molina failed to timely assess Medicaid beneficiaries for required services under the STAR+PLUS program and concealed its non-compliance. The lawsuit was brought by a whistleblower.
2025-03-03
Molina Healthcare is facing challenges due to rising healthcare costs and potential changes to Medicaid funding. The company's medical loss ratio in the fourth quarter surged past 90%, higher than expected, meaning over 90 cents of every premium dollar was spent on care costs. This negatively impacted investor confidence, with shares dropping after the earnings release. Uncertainty surrounding potential Medicaid cuts by the Republican-led Congress also poses a risk, as about 80% of Molina's business comes from Medicaid.
2025-02-05
Molina Healthcare completed its acquisition of ConnectiCare Holding Company on February 1, 2025. This acquisition adds an established government-sponsored health plan and a new state to Molina's portfolio. ConnectiCare served approximately 140,000 members across the Affordable Care Act marketplace, Medicare, and certain commercial products. The purchase price was reported as $350 million. Molina intends to fund the purchase with cash on hand.
