Recent News

May 9, 2025
The Cigna Group's Safe Swimming with Sickle Cell program is expanding to Memphis, Tennessee, in August 2025. The program, which started in Atlanta in 2024, aims to help children with sickle cell disease learn to swim with appropriate precautions.
June 30, 2025
Cigna is in contract negotiations with TriStar Health System and Parkridge Health System in Tennessee, which could potentially lead to them leaving the Cigna Open Access Plus network. Letters were scheduled to be mailed to impacted members on June 30, 2025. Additionally, an extension agreement was signed with Ballad Health through July 16, 2025, for the LocalPlus and Cigna Open Access Plus networks.
June 30, 2025
The Cigna Group Foundation announced grants to 24 nonprofits as part of its initiative to address the youth mental health crisis. The list of grantees includes Communities in Schools of Appalachian Highlands (Tennessee) and STARS Nashville (Tennessee).
July 8, 2025
The Cigna Group Foundation announced a new round of grants totaling $3 million aimed at improving the mental health of military veterans, including support for Tennessee Homeless Solutions to assist veteran homeowners facing foreclosure.
July 2, 2025
The Sixth Circuit Court of Appeals upheld a lower court's dismissal of an unjust enrichment lawsuit filed against Cigna by two Tennessee hospitals, Saint Francis Hospital and Saint Francis Hospital-Bartlett. The hospitals alleged Cigna underpaid for out-of-network emergency services.

State Leadership

Tony Doyle

General Manager for MidSouth

Promoted to General Manager for Cigna Healthcare's group employer business in the MidSouth region (comprised of Tennessee, Kentucky, Arkansas, and Mississippi), effective Jan. 1, 2025. Responsible for driving market growth for client employers with under 3,000 employees. Has more than 25 years of health plan experience and deep market knowledge. Held several new and existing business leadership roles in the MidSouth region since joining Cigna Healthcare six years ago. Previously served as vice president of sales, Existing Business, for the market's Mid-Market Segment and was involved in new business and retention efforts for Under 500 accounts. Prior to Cigna, held sales roles with Aetna. Resides in Gallatin, TN and is based at Cigna Healthcare's Franklin, Tenn. office.

Debbie McKee

President of Medicare Advantage Plans in Tennessee and Arkansas

Named President of Medicare Advantage (MA) plans in Tennessee and Arkansas in September 2021. Has been with Cigna since 2018, previously serving as regional vice president of MA sales in the southeast region. Brings over a decade of Medicare experience, including working closely with providers. Holds a bachelor's degree from Louisiana State University Medical Center and an executive master's degree in health care leadership from Brown University.

Gregory James Allen

President of Cigna MidSouth Markets

Serves as President of Cigna MidSouth Markets at Cigna Corp. Served as President of Tennessee Market at Cigna Corp. since August 18, 2015. Has leadership responsibility for the products and services Cigna offers in the state of Tennessee. Previously served as the Chief Operating Officer of HealthSpring Inc. Held several leadership positions at Cigna-HealthSpring and previously served as HealthSpring president of the Tennessee market. Before joining Cigna-HealthSpring, was director of finance for North American Medical Management. Also held financial analysis roles with Prudential Health Care and Aetna U.S. HealthCare. Graduate of the Nashville HealthCare Council Fellows program and Vanderbilt University's Larry Van Horn. Earned a Bachelor of Arts degree in accounting from Abilene Christian University and is a licensed certified public accountant in Texas.

John Webb

Market President for West Tennessee/Arkansas/Mississippi

Named Market President for health care and related benefits plans in West Tennessee/Arkansas/Mississippi in September 2020. Also referred to as president of Tri-South at Cigna Healthcare. Has over 30 years of experience in the health care industry. Most recently served as CEO & Managing Partner of Quantum Reach and has held leadership roles at Aetna, Unum and Rockwell International. Former aerospace engineer who worked on the Space Shuttle program for Rockwell International. Earned an MBA from Pepperdine University and a Bachelor of Science degree in Mechanical Engineering from Michigan State University.
