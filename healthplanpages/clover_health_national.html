State Leadership

Andrew Toy

Chief Executive Officer

Andrew is the Chief Executive Officer at Clover Health. As a technology-first Medicare company, Clover focuses on making great healthcare affordable to all of America's seniors. Andrew is a recognized leader in Healthcare innovation and has testified before the Congress Ways and Means subcommittee on Healthcare. Prior to Clover, Andrew was at Google working on the Google Cloud team and was responsible for Developer Platform and Intelligence features. Previously, he led Android for Work, which he joined via the acquisition of his previous startup Divide, where he was a founder. Andrew holds both a B.S. and M.S. in Computer Science from Stanford University, where he has also served as an associate lecturer.

Vivek Garipalli

Co-Founder and Executive Chairperson

Vivek Garipalli is the co-founder, Executive Chairperson, and former Chief Executive Officer of Clover Health. Prior to founding Clover, Vivek founded CarePoint Health in 2008. Vivek also co-founded a healthcare services revenue cycle company called Ensemble Health, and was a founding investor of Flatiron Health. Vivek is active in health tech, serving as a board member of Doctor Evidence and Medically Home and an investor in numerous early-stage health tech companies. Vivek began his career in finance, holding roles at Credit Suisse First Boston, J.P. Morgan Partners and Blackstone Group. He earned a B.B.A. in Entrepreneurship from Emory University.

Peter Kuipers

Chief Financial Officer

Peter Kuipers is the Chief Financial Officer of Clover Health where he is responsible for overseeing all financial aspects of the Company. Kuipers brings over 25 years of technology leadership experience to his role and in 2018 was recognized as the San Francisco Bay Area Public Company CFO of the Year Award for small to medium-sized companies. Kuipers started his career at Ernst & Young. He received a Master's Degree in Economics and Business Administration from the Maastricht University School of Business and Economics, and is a Chartered Accountant in the Netherlands. Prior to Clover, Kuipers served as Executive Vice President and CFO at Omnicell, Inc., as well as divisional CFO and executive FP&A leadership roles at Quantcast, The Weather Company, Yahoo!, Altera Corporation, General Electric Company, and Akzo Nobel.

Brady Priest

CEO of Clover Care Services

Brady Priest has served as Chief Executive Officer of Clover Home Care since July 2022. Previously, from July 2020 to July 2022, Mr. Priest served as Chief Executive Officer of SeekMedicare, a company that Mr. Priest co-founded. Mr. Priest previously held several roles with Clover Health, including Advisor and General Counsel.
