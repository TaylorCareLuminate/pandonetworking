State Leadership

Paul Di Maio

President and CEO

President and CEO of Delta Dental of New Jersey and Delta Dental of Connecticut. Formerly Senior Vice President, General Counsel, and Chief Administrative Officer at Delta Dental. Joined Delta Dental in 2016. Previously held executive roles at Samsung Electronics America and Avaya Inc. Served on the board of Big Brothers Big Sisters of Coastal and Northern New Jersey and as Chair of its Governance Committee. Vice President of the Delta Dental of New Jersey Foundation. Holds a J.D. degree from Seton Hall University School of Law and a B.A. in Psychology from Villanova University.

Dennis G. Wilson

Former President and CEO, Special Advisor

Former President and CEO of Delta Dental of New Jersey and Connecticut, retired effective Dec 31, 2024. Continues as a special advisor through June 30, 2025. Joined DDNJCT as president and CEO in 2013. Led strategic initiatives including focus on individual and small group sales, establishing DDCT as a separate entity, and adding vision insurance. Initiated small business and individual products and the first Special Health Care Needs program.

John Rosero

Senior Vice President and General Counsel

Senior Vice President and General Counsel at Delta Dental of New Jersey and Connecticut. Assumed the role in January 2025. Oversees legal and compliance functions and government affairs operations. Previously held senior leadership positions at Prudential Financial, including Vice President and Chief Compliance Officer for U.S. Businesses, Chief Legal Officer, and Chief Counsel. Holds a Ph.D. in Global Affairs from Rutgers University, an LL.M. in Corporation Law from New York University School of Law, and a J.D. from the State University of New York at Buffalo School of Law. Holds certifications in regulatory compliance and artificial intelligence. Trustee for the Association of the Federal Bar of New Jersey and an advisory council member for OWL.co.

Karim Bennis

Chief Operating Officer and Senior Vice President

Chief Operating Officer and Senior Vice President at Delta Dental of New Jersey and Connecticut. Joined in December 2023. Accomplished healthcare executive with extensive leadership experience across various healthcare sectors. Received MBA at UCLA Anderson School of Management, Master of Public Administration at Harvard Kennedy School of Government, Master of Applied Finance from Pepperdine University, and Bachelor of Business Administration from Institut Supérieur de Gestion in Paris, France.

Stuart 'Stu' Brereton

Vice President and Chief Sales Officer

Vice President and Chief Sales Officer at Delta Dental of New Jersey and Connecticut. Joined in April 2024. Seasoned insurance industry leader in Healthcare, Life, and Disability insurance with extensive sales and business development experience. Most recently served as Regional Vice President at Prudential Financial. Spent many years at The Hartford. Graduated with a bachelor's degree in political science from Gettysburg College.

Randy Stodard

Chief Marketing Officer and Vice President

Chief Marketing Officer and Vice President at Delta Dental of New Jersey and Connecticut. Joined in 2015. Successfully led global sales, marketing, and business development/new product teams at several blue chip companies including Walgreens, Abbott, Pfizer, and Reckitt-Benckiser. Serves on the Boards of Meadowlands Chamber of Commerce, Morris County Chamber of Commerce in New Jersey, and the Stamford Chamber of Commerce in Connecticut. Holds a B.A. in Economics from Vanderbilt University and an M.B.A. from the Fuqua School of Business at Duke University.

Melissa Toll

Vice President of Human Resources

Vice President of Human Resources at Delta Dental of New Jersey and Connecticut. Joined in 2005 as an HR Coordinator and rose through the ranks. Established Delta Dental's Employee Wellness Program.

Barry Petruzzi

Vice President of Underwriting and Actuarial Services

Vice President of Underwriting and Actuarial Services at Delta Dental of New Jersey and Connecticut. Joined in May 2019. Appointed to the Board of Directors of the Commerce and Industry Association of New Jersey (CIANJ) in January 2024.
