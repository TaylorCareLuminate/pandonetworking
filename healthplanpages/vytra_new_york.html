Recent News

2025-05-15
EmblemHealth supports New York State legislation S. 705 (Krueger)/A. 2140 (Jackson), known as the Fair Pricing Act, which aims to cap payments for routine outpatient services performed at hospitals to address healthcare affordability and price variations. The legislation seeks to establish a site-neutral payment policy, limit facility fees, and increase price transparency. EmblemHealth, a non-profit health plan in New York City, endorses the bill for creating a more efficient healthcare system and protecting consumers from unjustified cost inflation.
2025-05-14
EmblemHealth celebrated a healthy spring in New York City by holding and participating in more than a dozen events across the city in April 2025. These events highlighted EmblemHealth's commitment to raising health care awareness and supporting community well-being through partnerships with various community organizations and elected officials. Activities included hosting farmers markets at Neighborhood Care locations, providing fresh produce and health screenings for diabetes, and participating in local community events.
2025-04-16
EmblemHealth announced on April 16, 2025, that GHI and HIP are part of EmblemHealth, clarifying company names for healthcare providers. This update aims to help providers avoid confusion and accurately verify member eligibility, noting that Group Health Incorporated (GHI) is now known as EmblemHealth Plan, Inc., and Health Insurance Plan of Greater New York (HIP) is also part of the EmblemHealth family.
