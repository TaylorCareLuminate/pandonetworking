Jeffrey Horne

Vice President

Came into Local 150 in 2001 as as Apprentice, second-generation member, worked in drilling and traveled throughout the country on drilling and caisson projects, came on staff as an organizer in 2015, worked under Mike Kresge on the Hollywood Crane campaign before taking on South Cook County as Business Agent, where he has overseen large projects including the Ford Manufacturing Plant turnaround and the reconstruction of the I-294 Mile Long Bridge. Took an active role in the negotiations over the District 1-2-3-5 Heavy Highway and Building Agreements in 2021, as well as the first standalone crane agreements for Central and Imperial Crane. Appointed Vice President in September 2021.

Michael Kresge

Recording-Corresponding Secretary

Joined Local 150 as a dozer operator in 1994, worked on mass dirt projects, brought on staff as a member of the Task Force in 1999, promoted to Organizer, instrumental in organizing medium and small paving companies, promoted to Business Representative in 2002, served members in Districts 1, 2 and 4, promoted to Director of Organizing in 2014, oversees more than a dozen Organizers and Task Force members, served as Assistant to the President-Business Manager, appointed Vice President in December 2018, appointed to the office of Recording-Corresponding Secretary following the retirement of Steven Cisco in 2021.

David A. Fagan

Financial Secretary

Responsible for collection of all funds for the union and for maintaining correct financial account of all members, started working as a Local 150 Operating Engineer in 1978, worked in building construction, mass excavation, and the slag industry in northwest Indiana, second-generation member, father worked as a heavy equipment mechanic, came onto the union's staff in 1989 as an organizer.
