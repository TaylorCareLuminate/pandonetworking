Recent News

May 6, 2025
Prime Healthcare's Suburban Community Hospital in East Norriton, PA, is transitioning to a specialty behavioral health care campus, closing its emergency department and other general acute care services at an unspecified date. The facility will be renamed the Suburban Behavioral Health Campus of Roxborough Memorial Hospital. This change is in response to the increased need for mental and behavioral health services in Southeast Pennsylvania, although it involves terminating 109 positions.
May 6, 2025
31 Prime Healthcare hospitals across 13 states, including those in Pennsylvania like Roxborough Memorial Hospital and Lower Bucks Hospital (based on previous search result context, though not explicitly named in this snippet), earned an "A" Hospital Safety Grade from The Leapfrog Group for spring 2025.
May 5, 2025
Capital Health Medical Center – Hopewell will be the first and only hospital in the Mercer and Bucks County region to offer histotripsy, a non-invasive treatment option for liver tumors, with the arrival of the Edison System in the summer of 2025.
March 6, 2025
Capital Health announced that it is the first in Mercer and Bucks Counties to offer Bulkamid, a minimally invasive treatment for stress urinary incontinence. They also announced that accomplished breast surgeons, Dr. Yi Huang and Dr. Jenna Mancinelli, joined their Cancer Center and Center for Comprehensive Breast. Capital Health also earned national recognition for social responsibility in February 2025.
June 6, 2022
Aetna Better Health Kids, a CVS Health company, partnered with The Salvation Army Western Pennsylvania Division to support Camp Allegheny in summer 2022, providing health and wellness items and activities to campers.
June 4, 2025
Capital Health is closing its emergency department and outpatient clinic at Bert and Hamilton avenues in East Trenton, New Jersey. This closure was due to structural issues in the leased space.
June 16, 2025
Pennsylvania lawmakers passed a bill in the House of Representatives that would increase oversight of hospital sales, mergers, and acquisitions involving for-profit and private equity companies, including other healthcare facilities. The bill, which is now in the Senate, would empower the state's attorney general to review and potentially block transactions deemed against the public interest.
January 2025
A survey conducted between November 2024 and January 2025 by The Hospital and Healthsystem Association of Pennsylvania (HAP), LeadingAge PA, and Pennsylvania Health Care Association (PHCA) highlighted persistent healthcare workforce shortages in the state, affecting patient access to care in hospitals and nursing homes.
February 2024
The Change Healthcare cyberattack in February 2024 impacted over 100 million individuals, making it the largest healthcare data breach in the U.S. The attack disrupted IT operations for thousands of pharmacies and healthcare providers, and impacted Change Healthcare applications. Geisinger Healthcare in Danville, PA, immediately disconnected from Change Healthcare's systems following the attack.
February 2024
Aetna Better Health of Pennsylvania has provider notices and newsletters available, including updates from February and March 2024 regarding topics such as date of death recovery, pharmacist billing and enrollment, prior authorization of opioid use disorder, and updates to the PROMISe provider handbook.
April 14, 2025
Onicx Group, a healthcare real estate investment firm, acquired The Merion Building in King of Prussia, Pennsylvania, a medical office building, as part of its expansion into the Philadelphia healthcare market. This is the third medical building acquired by Onicx Group in the past 6 months.
