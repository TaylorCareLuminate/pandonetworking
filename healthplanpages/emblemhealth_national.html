Recent News

2025-05-19
EmblemHealth announced updates to its preauthorization lists effective August 1, 2025, as part of its annual review. The updates include the removal of 303 services and codes, the addition of five new codes, and the expansion of some existing preauthorization requirements to additional members. The announcement specifically mentions updates to the GHI PPO City of New York Preauthorization List.
2025-05-15
EmblemHealth announced its support for New York State legislation S. 705/A. 2140, known as the Fair Pricing Act, which aims to cap payments for routine outpatient services at hospitals to address rising healthcare costs and price variation.
2025-05-08
DC 37 and AdvantageCare Physicians, an EmblemHealth company, celebrated the opening of a new medical office at the DC 37 Health Center in Manhattan to provide direct healthcare services to members.
2025-04-16
EmblemHealth published information clarifying that Group Health Incorporated (GHI) and Health Insurance Plan of Greater New York (HIP) are part of EmblemHealth to help providers and members understand the company names.
2025-04-16
EmblemHealth published an update clarifying that Group Health Incorporated (GHI) and Health Insurance Plan of Greater New York (HIP) are part of EmblemHealth. The article, aimed at providers, explains the historical names and current structure to help avoid confusion when verifying patient insurance and scheduling appointments. It advises providers to use the Check Provider Network Status tool in the Provider Portal.
2025-03-18
EmblemHealth adopted a Prior Authorization Policy for Lutathera (lutetium Lu 177 dotatate), a treatment for certain neuroendocrine tumors, outlining the clinical review criteria for its use.
2025-03-10
EmblemHealth Neighborhood Care has won multiple Stevie® Awards for Innovation in Customer Service in the 19th annual Sales & Customer Service Awards.
