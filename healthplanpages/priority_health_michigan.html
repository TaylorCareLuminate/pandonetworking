Recent News

2025-06-30
Hundreds of Detroit-area families will receive access to fresh, weekly produce this summer at no cost through a grant from the Priority Health Total Health Foundation to the Detroit Pistons Foundation. The funding supports the Pistons Neighbors Program, operated in partnership with the Detroit Parks Coalition.
2025-06-23
The City of Wyandotte, in partnership with Priority Health and National Fitness Campaign (NFC), announced the addition of an outdoor Fitness Court® and digital wellness programming at Pulaski Park. The Fitness Court® was set to open on June 25, 2025, providing free access to high-quality workouts.
2025-06-16
The City of Benton Harbor, in partnership with the Southwest Michigan Regional Chamber, Priority Health, and National Fitness Campaign, unveiled a new Priority Health Fitness Court® at 110 Water Street, aiming to prioritize community health and wellness.
2025-05-27
Priority Health and Virta Health have partnered to offer three new solutions addressing the growing demand for weight management options and diabetes support among employers and employees in Michigan.
2025-02-05
Priority Health has expanded its PriorityGPS navigation services with enhanced offerings, including extended service hours with care navigators, scheduling and pre-enrollment assistance, and live chat support to help members navigate the health care system.
2025-01-22
Priority Health has been awarded a contract by the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services (MDHHS) to offer a new Medicare-Medicaid plan called MI Coordinated Health across all regions in Michigan's Lower Peninsula. This new plan will launch in select counties in January 2026 and expand statewide in 2027, succeeding the MI Health Link program.
