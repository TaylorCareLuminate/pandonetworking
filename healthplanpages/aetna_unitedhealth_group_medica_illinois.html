2025-06-20
Major health insurance companies, including CVS Health's Aetna and Health Care Service Corp. (parent of Blue Cross and Blue Shield in Illinois), are taking steps to streamline and reduce prior authorization requirements. This industry-wide effort aims to address concerns from physicians about delays in patient treatment and administrative burdens. The initiative follows public scrutiny of the health insurance industry.
2025-06-05
A federal judge in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Illinois has ruled that antitrust lawsuits accusing data analytics firm Claritev (formerly MultiPlan) and major health insurers, including UnitedHealth Group and Aetna, of anti-competitive practices to reduce out-of-network reimbursement rates will proceed. Healthcare providers, including the American Medical Association and the Illinois State Medical Society, allege a price-fixing conspiracy that forced physicians to accept lower payments for out-of-network services. The lawsuits claim the collaboration between Claritev and insurers like Aetna and UnitedHealth Group allows for the exchange of sensitive data and collective suppression of payment rates.
2025-06-01
McDonough District Hospital (MDH) in Macomb, Illinois, has signed an insurance contract with Aetna for its HMO and OAP plans, effective June 1, 2025. This agreement allows many state of Illinois employees and others who had Health Alliance insurance to choose the Aetna HMO plan and access MDH services and providers as in-network. MDH is also Tier I in-network for those with Aetna OAP plans and Aetna Medicare Advantage.

2025-05-20
Aetna Better Health of Illinois, a CVS Health company, has distributed $20.4 million in value-based care incentive payments to community health centers across Illinois. This funding aims to support 16 health providers in modernizing operations, extending clinic hours, and implementing population health tools to enhance patient-centered care in under-resourced communities and reduce preventable hospitalizations.
2025-05-19
Aetna Medicaid, part of CVS Health, has launched a pilot program in Illinois, Louisiana, and North Carolina to increase access to home healthcare for older adults and those with disabilities and to support direct care workers. The initiative involves collaboration with the National Alliance for Care at Home and MissionCare Collective to analyze data and identify areas needing targeted action to strengthen the direct care workforce and support providers. Participating Illinois providers will implement a workforce solution aimed at increasing engagement and strengthening support to address turnover.
2025-05-13
Aetna Medicaid, a CVS Health company, announced a collaboration with the National Alliance for Care at Home and MissionCare Collective to increase access to home health care for older adults and those with disabilities and provide more support for direct care workers in Illinois, Louisiana, and North Carolina.
2025-05-04
Health insurer Aetna, owned by CVS Health, will exit the federal Affordable Care Act (ACA) Health Insurance Exchange starting in 2026. This decision will impact around a million Aetna members nationally who will need to find new coverage. The article notes that Aetna is also one of the insurers being sued by the Justice Department over alleged kickbacks related to Medicare Advantage plans, although this is separate from the ACA exchange exit.
2025-04-18
Minnetonka-based health insurer Medica exited the health insurance market in Illinois, Arizona, and Kansas at the start of 2025. This decision came after Medica posted a $176 million operating loss in 2024 due to rising medical costs and lower-than-expected reimbursements from government health plans. The article also mentions that financial results in 2024 were challenging for nonprofit insurers and led to a stock sell-off for UnitedHealth Group.
