Emergency departments at PeaceHealth's RiverBend campus in Springfield, Oregon, have experienced significant increases in patient volume and extended wait times, according to Oregon Health Authority data. 2025-06-10
PeaceHealth announced plans to lay off 1% of its 16,000-person workforce across its facilities in Oregon, Washington, and Alaska, citing industrywide challenges and the need to improve operating performance. The layoffs, affecting approximately 162 employees, were concentrated at PeaceHealth Southwest in Vancouver, Washington, and PeaceHealth Sacred Heart at RiverBend in Springfield, Oregon. The reductions involve eliminating some caregiver roles and closing open positions, with affected workers notified by late May 2025. The union representing some workers protested the layoffs, raising concerns about potential negative impacts on patient care. Despite the layoffs, PeaceHealth reported increased net revenue but noted that expenses were rising at a faster rate. The organization also implemented a hiring freeze for most non-clinical roles in 2025.
2025-05-29
PeaceHealth is implementing staff cuts at its Sacred Heart Medical Center at RiverBend in Springfield, Oregon, as part of a 1% workforce reduction across the nonprofit health system operating in Oregon, Washington, and Alaska. [7, 11] The organization stated that these adjustments are in response to industrywide challenges affecting hospitals nationally and within Oregon. [7] The cuts include eliminating some caregiver positions and not filling certain open roles to improve operational efficiency and ensure the long-term viability of their mission. [7, 11] PeaceHealth is offering transitional support to affected employees and attempting to match qualified individuals with open clinical positions elsewhere in the system. [7, 11] This follows PeaceHealth's closure of its Eugene hospital and emergency department in 2023. [7]
