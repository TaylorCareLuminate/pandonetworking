Jeff Ingrum

CEO, Baylor Scott & White Health Plan

As President and CEO of Baylor Scott & White Health Plan, Jeff Ingrum combines his passion for healthcare and people in serving more than 500,000 members in Texas and leading nearly 800 employees. [4] Since taking the helm in April 2016, Jeff has led Baylor Scott & White Health Plan through several remarkable transformations, including doubling its size through the 2019 acquisition of FirstCare Health Plans. [4] Prior to this role, he served for 18 years as President and CEO of Health Alliance Medical Plans, and 10 years as their Chief Financial Officer. [4] He earned a Bachelor of Accounting from Eastern Illinois University and a Master of Health Administration from the College of St. Francis. [4]
