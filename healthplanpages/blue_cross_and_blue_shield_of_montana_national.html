Recent News

2025-03-04
Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Montana announced that they are accepting applications for their Blue Impact grant program from March 1 through May 1. The grants aim to support local community partners addressing social and economic factors influencing health and wellness in Montana, focusing on areas like economic opportunity, nutrition, neighborhood assets, locally defined health solutions, and optimal health outcomes. Organizations with 501(c)(3) status can apply for up to $50,000 in funding.

State Leadership

Lisa Kelley

President

Lisa Kelley is responsible for developing and executing strategies to accelerate access to affordable health care in Montana. She oversees all Plan operations, including sales and account management, government and public relations, provider network management, and health care management. Lisa previously was General Counsel for the Montana Plan. She also served as corporate brand counsel and supported the company’s national accounts sales team. Earlier, Lisa clerked for Justice Jim Rice on the Montana Supreme Court, and she was an attorney at Hughes, Kellner, Sullivan, & Alke. She joined the BCBSMT legal division in 2010 and has served as general counsel since 2019. She earned an undergraduate degree in business administration from Washington State University and a juris doctor from Gonzaga University School of Law. Kelley currently serves on the Montana Life & Health Insurance Guaranty Association and the Glacier National Park Conservancy boards of directors. She is also an adjunct professor of health law at the Alexander Blewett III School of Law. She replaced Collette Hanson as President, effective January 1, 2024.

Dr. David Lechner

Vice President, Health Care Delivery and Chief Medical Officer

Dr. David Lechner joined Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Montana in 2019 as Chief Medical Officer, serving as the Plan's clinical leader and partnering with the Health Care Management team to advance value-based care in Montana. In 2021, he was promoted to Vice President, Health Care Delivery and CMO, taking on additional responsibility overseeing the network team. Born and raised in Billings as a third-generation Montanan.

Corey Palmer

Vice President, Sales and Account Management

Corey Palmer oversees Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Montana’s sales and retention efforts for all market segments, leading a team responsible for ensuring exceptional service to employer clients, brokers and consultants, and for driving a forward-looking strategy that meets our customers’ evolving needs. Corey holds an MFA in creative writing and a bachelor's in English from the University of Montana.

Collette Hanson

Former President (Retired)

Collette Hanson was named president of Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Montana (BCBSMT) effective in 2020, and retired effective January 1, 2024. She worked at BCBSMT for 39 years in a variety of roles, starting as an entry level claims examiner and working her way up through the organization to serve in several critical leadership roles, including overseeing operations and most recently as the divisional vice president of sales and account management. She is a third-generation Montanan.
