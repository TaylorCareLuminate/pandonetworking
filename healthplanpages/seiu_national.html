Recent News

Undated (updates mention June 27, 2025, and July 1, 2027)
SEIU Local 1000 reached a bargaining agreement with the State that protects raises, pauses the return-to-office order, and allows for future bargaining in 2026. This agreement was reached after the Governor proposed canceling a negotiated 3% raise to address the state's budget deficit, which SEIU Local 1000 opposed. The agreement includes locking in a second 3% raise deferred to July 1, 2027, ensuring future economic security and application to pension and overtime. The agreement also requires departments to revert to telework agreements in place before March 2, 2025.
Undated
SEIU Local 721 news highlights include LA County workers' actions against anti-union efforts, a letter to the editor defending fair pay for LA County workers, behavioral health staff voting to join SEIU 721, reaching a tentative agreement with LA County at the Common Language Table, and joining allies for a 'Summer of Resistance'. The Summer of Resistance involved joining other unions and activists to demand the release of SEIU Leader David Huerta.
June 26, 2025
SEIU Local 73 news includes St. Clair County Housing Authority Workers gaining increased wages and benefits in a new contract on June 26, 2025, Access SMTD Workers securing raises on June 24, 2025, Ottawa Paraprofessionals scoring raises on June 23, 2025, and Hinsdale Custodial and Maintenance Workers getting a wage increase on June 17, 2025. Other news highlights gains for Galesburg FSWs on May 29, 2025, Waukegan Workers winning overtime backpay on May 28, 2025, and UIC Sodexo Workers winning a pathway to $20/HR on May 20, 2025.
June 19, 2025
SEIU Local 221 members overwhelmingly ratified a foundational contract with the County of San Diego. The bargaining team recommended a YES vote on the tentative agreement, with ratification concluding on June 18, 2025. The contract was overwhelmingly approved by June 19, 2025.
July 2, 2025

State Leadership

April Verrett

President

April Verrett is described as a fighter for working people and a visionary leader focused on building an anti-racist labor movement. She was elected the first Black woman president of SEIU on May 20, 2024, after serving two years as secretary-treasurer. She previously served as President of SEIU Local 2015 and Executive Vice President of SEIU Healthcare Illinois and Indiana. Verrett has chaired the SEIU National Home Care Council, co-chaired the National Organizing Committee, and served on the Finance Committee. She is driven by the belief that unions empower workers to fight for more than wages and benefits, and can help eradicate generational poverty, dismantle structural racism, strengthen democracy, and improve lives and communities. [4, 8, 9]

Rocío Sáenz

Secretary-Treasurer

Rocío Sáenz has advocated for immigrant, worker, and racial justice for over three decades. Originally from Mexico, she became a union organizer in 1988 and was a key organizer in the 1990 'Justice for Janitors' strike in Los Angeles. She was first elected SEIU Executive Vice President in 2013, leading the Property Services Division and directing SEIU's work on immigration and the Fight for $15 campaign. She previously led SEIU Local 615 in Boston. Sáenz believes in the power of workers uniting through unions to address structural problems. [5]
