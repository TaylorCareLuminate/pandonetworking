Recent News

2025-04-16
The Change Healthcare cyberattack in February 2024 compromised the protected health information of an estimated 190 million individuals. By October 2024, Change Healthcare confirmed that at least 100 million individuals were affected. Monument Health in South Dakota was informed in December 2024 that data for up to 26,000 of its patients were stolen, indicating ongoing notification of affected entities and individuals.
2025-01-27
A trial commenced in North Dakota on January 27, 2025, challenging the state's ban on gender-affirming medical care for minors. The lawsuit, filed by families of transgender children and a doctor, argues the law violates the state constitution. North Dakota is one of over two dozen states with similar bans facing legal challenges.
2024-07-23
Due to the Change Healthcare cyberattack, North Dakota Medicaid extended timely filing requirements for claims with dates of service on or after September 21, 2023. Claims must be submitted by either July 25, 2024, or the regular timely filing deadline, whichever is later. Providers unable to submit claims electronically can send them on paper or via the MMIS Web Portal.
