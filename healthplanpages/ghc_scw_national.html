Recent News

March 18, 2025
Group Health Cooperative of South Central Wisconsin (GHC-SCW) announced the 2025 Pre-Professional Medical Diversity Scholarship.
June 9, 2025
Group Health Cooperative of South Central Wisconsin (GHC-SCW) provided an update on the unionization process involving direct care employees, stating they respect employees' rights to explore unionization and are working to facilitate a timely, confidential, and in-person election. GHC-SCW requested an expedited investigation by the National Labor Relations Board (NLRB) and proposed new election dates for June 23, 24, and 25, 2025, after previous dates in May were declined by SEIU Wisconsin. They denied accusations of union-busting.
June 10, 2025
Group Health Cooperative of South Central Wisconsin (GHC-SCW) announced that they are offering organ donation registration through GHCMyChart, a first in Wisconsin.
2025-04-18
Group Health Cooperative of South Central Wisconsin (GHC-SCW) announced its new role as the Exclusive Health Care Partner of Run Madtown, Madison's premier running event. This partnership highlights GHC-SCW's commitment to community well-being and healthy lifestyles.
2025-04-08
GHC-SCW celebrated the opening of its expanded behavioral health clinic, the Olin Clinic, located at 208 E. Olin Ave. in Madison. The move to a larger space allows for expanded services, including outpatient therapy, psychiatry, crisis support, and adult inattention/ADHD evaluations, with plans to add Occupational Therapy and Speech-Language Services for their Autism Spectrum Disorder program.
2025
Details regarding GHC-SCW's 2025 network offerings were presented, outlining the continuation of the Dane Choice Network and Neighbors Network based on where members receive Primary Care Services. The presentation also noted major provider network changes and updates regarding the Memorial Hospital of Lafayette County name change and new hospital building.
