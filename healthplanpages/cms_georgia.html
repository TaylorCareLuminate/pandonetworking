2025-06-12
CMS has extended the public health emergency declaration for the State of Georgia in response to Hurricane Helene. The extension was signed on June 12, 2025, and became effective on June 21, 2025, providing continued flexibility to support CMS beneficiaries in Georgia.
2025-05-20
Children's Medical Services (CMS) in Georgia, a statewide program, supports children, youth, and young adults (birth-21) with special healthcare needs and their families by helping them access timely and affordable healthcare services and resources within their communities. The program assists with various medical needs, and eligibility is based on medical condition, annual family income, and residency in Georgia.
2025-04-03
The Georgia Department of Public Health provides CMS Resources, offering services and resources for children with special healthcare needs. These resources include a digital database, information on the CMS Emergency Care Plan Tool, and details about the medical home approach utilized by CMS Care Coordinators.
