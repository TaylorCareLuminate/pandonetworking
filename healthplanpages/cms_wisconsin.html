Recent News

2025-06-25
CMS announced enforcement discretion and clarification of personnel regulations, detailed in QSO-25-21-CLIA, which affects CLIA Laboratories. The Wisconsin Department of Health Services disseminated this bulletin, noting that CMS is exercising enforcement discretion in limited circumstances regarding certain personnel qualification requirements that went into effect on December 28, 2024, due to stakeholder feedback.
2025-04-10
A Consumer Guide for Wisconsin Medicare provides information about the program run by CMS, including details on supplemental insurance (Medigap), Medicare Advantage plans, and prescription drug coverage (Part D). The guide serves as a resource for Wisconsin residents navigating their Medicare options.
2025-04-07
The Wisconsin Department of Health Services outlines the requirements for hospitals regarding State Licensure and Medicare/Medicaid Certification by CMS. Hospitals in Wisconsin must be licensed by the state and CMS to provide certain services, with specific application steps detailed, including submitting the CMS-855 Medicare Enrollment Application.
2025-03-12
The CMS (Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services) published an emergency preparedness rule for health providers participating in Medicare and Medicaid, affecting over 1,200 providers across Wisconsin. This rule aims to establish consistent emergency preparedness policies and procedures to increase patient safety during emergencies and create a better coordinated response to disasters.
2025-01-21
Information on Local Coverage Determinations lists Medicare Administrative Contractors (MACs), including National Government Services, Inc. which serves Wisconsin. MACs are private health care insurers that have been awarded a geographic jurisdiction to process Medicare Part A and Part B medical claims.
