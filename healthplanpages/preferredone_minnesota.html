State Leadership

David Crosby

President & CEO

Seasoned healthcare executive with over 30 years in the health insurance industry. Previously Executive Vice President for Commercial Business at MVP Health Care. Held leadership positions with health plans in Michigan and Pennsylvania. Served as Chief of Value Based Solutions for PreferredOne's parent company, Fairview Health Services. Also served as Chairman of the Minnesota Council of Health Plans. Holds an MBA in Healthcare Management from the State University of New York at Buffalo and an undergraduate degree from Canisius College. [6, 9, 3, 5, 7, 2]

Michael S. Umland

Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer

Started at PreferredOne in June 1994. Previously served as Vice President of Finance and Chief Financial Officer since 1994. Was employed at Fairview Health Services in the corporate treasury department from 1990 to 1994. Held financial positions at a local CPA firm and a local real estate services firm from 1980 to 1990. Received a Bachelor of Arts degree in Accounting and Mathematics from Gustavus Adolphus College and his CPA designation in 1982. Based in the Greater Minneapolis-St. Paul Area. [6, 7]

Paul D. Geiwitz

Chief Marketing Officer

Served in multiple roles for PreferredOne since joining the organization in 1994, including acting as PreferredOne's interim President and Chief Executive Officer in 2015. From 1994 to 2001, he served as Senior Vice President of Marketing and Product Development and Vice President of Strategic Development. Prior to PreferredOne, he was Senior Director of Actuarial Services and Underwriting at HealthPartners (1992-1994) and Manager/Director of Revenue and Reimbursement at Aetna-MedCenters (1984-1992). Holds a Bachelor of Arts degree in Economics from St. Cloud State University. [2, 6]
