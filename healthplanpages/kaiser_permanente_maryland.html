Recent News

May 20, 2025
Enterprise Community Partners, in partnership with Kaiser Permanente Mid-Atlantic States, launched the first Mid-Atlantic Preservation Academy in Columbia, MD. The program selected 12 organizations across DC, Maryland, and Virginia to receive intensive training and $360,000 in grants to preserve affordable homes.
January 30, 2025
Kaiser Permanente has awarded $1,228,000 in grants to 19 community organizations and nonprofits in Washington, D.C., Maryland, and Virginia to advance access to healthcare and promote health equity, addressing social factors of health. Specific grants mentioned include support for initiatives in Charles County and Prince George's County, Maryland, and for low-income seniors in northwest Baltimore.
2024-09-25
Kaiser Permanente Mid-Atlantic's commercial health plan, serving Maryland, Virginia, and Washington, D.C., received a 5 out of 5 stars rating from the National Committee for Quality Assurance (NCQA) 2024 Health Plan Ratings. This is the sixth consecutive year achieving the highest rating, making it the only health plan nationally with this record. Their Medicaid and Medicare plans in the Mid-Atlantic region, including Maryland, also received high ratings of 4.5 out of 5 stars from NCQA.

Israel Rocha

Regional President, Mid-Atlantic region

Named regional president of Kaiser Permanente's Mid-Atlantic region on October 31, 2023, effective December 4. Responsible for the health system's performance and overall success in the region, leading the focus on providing integrated, high-quality health care and coverage in partnership with the executive medical director for the Mid-Atlantic Permanente Medical Group. Has over 20 years of experience leading integrated public health systems. Previously served as CEO of Cook County Health System in Illinois. Held executive leadership roles at NYC Health + Hospitals Elmhurst and Queens hospitals and Doctors Hospital at Renaissance Health System in Texas. Began career working in Washington, DC.

Maria Ansari, MD, FACC

CEO and Executive Director — The Permanente Medical Group, President and CEO — Mid-Atlantic Permanente Medical Group, CEO — Northwest Permanente, Co-CEO — The Permanente Federation

Leads the largest medical group in the nation and is responsible for the health care of nearly five million Kaiser Permanente members in Northern California, Maryland, Virginia and the District of Columbia.

Richard McCarthy, MD

Executive Medical Director, Mid-Atlantic Permanente Medical Group, Kaiser Permanente

Leads care delivery strategy and operations for the Mid-Atlantic Permanente Medical Group in Maryland, Virginia and the District of Columbia. As Associate Executive Director, he has oversight for MAPMG and leads more than 1,800 physicians and staff members who deliver care to nearly 800,000 members in Maryland, Virginia, and the District of Columbia. Served for 9 years as the Physician-in-Chief for the DC and Suburban Maryland service area. Member of Kaiser Permanente's National Health Policy Committee and National Telehealth Sponsors Steering Committee. Joined The Permanente Medical Group in 1997 and was part of the original physician leadership transition team for MAPMG in 2009.
