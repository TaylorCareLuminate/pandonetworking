Recent News

2025-02-13
UnitedHealth Group has agreed to pay at least $20.25 million to settle a U.S. Department of Labor lawsuit filed in the U.S. District Court of Western Wisconsin. The lawsuit alleged that UMR, a division of UnitedHealth Group, improperly denied thousands of claims for emergency room services and urinary drug screenings.
2025-02-12
UnitedHealth Group will pay $20.25 million to resolve a lawsuit with the U.S. Department of Labor. The lawsuit, originally filed in the U.S. District Court for the Western District of Wisconsin, claimed that UMR, a third-party administrator for large employers, wrongly denied thousands of medical claims between 2015 and 2023.
