Recent News

2025-05-14
Meridian Health Plan of Illinois is partnering with farmers markets in southern Illinois throughout 2025 to increase access to fresh produce and combat food insecurity. The initiative offers vouchers for individuals to purchase healthy food and provides health education materials.
2025-04-23
Meridian Health Plan of Illinois's Medicaid managed care contract with Northwestern Medicine ended on December 31, 2024. Patients with this plan needed to choose a new plan starting January 1, 2025, to continue care at Northwestern Medicine, although some qualified for continuity of care until March 31, 2025.
2025-03-17
Meridian Health Plan of Illinois, a Centene subsidiary, was selected by the Illinois Department of Healthcare and Family Services (HFS) to continue providing services for dually eligible Medicare and Medicaid members through a Fully Integrated Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan (D-SNP). The new contract is expected to begin on January 1, 2026.
2025-01-28
Meridian Health Plan of Illinois announced it awarded $528,916 in Health Happens Here Grants™ to 11 organizations in the second half of 2024. These grants support local organizations throughout Illinois to address health-related social needs and close care gaps for Meridian members and the community.
