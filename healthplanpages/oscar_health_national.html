Recent News

2025-05-07
Oscar Health announced strong financial results for the first quarter of 2025, reporting significant year-over-year increases in total revenue and net income. The company reaffirmed its full-year 2025 outlook.
05/07/2025
Oscar Health, Inc. announced strong financial results for the first quarter ended March 31, 2025, reporting approximately $3.0 billion in total revenue, a 42% increase year-over-year. The company also reported significant year-over-year increases in net income and reaffirmed its full year 2025 outlook. Key financial metrics included a medical loss ratio of 75.4% and an SG&A expense ratio of 15.8% for the first quarter of 2025. Mark Bertolini, CEO of Oscar Health, highlighted continued top-line growth and bottom-line performance, expecting meaningful margin expansion this year.

State Leadership

Mark T. Bertolini

Chief Executive Officer

Mark T. Bertolini is the Chief Executive Officer of Oscar Health, a leading insurer dedicated to making a healthier life accessible and affordable for all. [1, 3, 4] Under his leadership, Oscar serves approximately 1M members with Individual & Family, Medicare Advantage, and Small Group plans. [3] Mark is former Co-Chief Executive Officer of Bridgewater Associates. [3, 4] Mark is also a national health care thought leader, and the former Chairman and CEO of Aetna Inc. [3, 4] At Aetna, Mark assumed the role of CEO in November 2010, and of Chairman in April 2011. [3, 4] In November 2018, he stepped down as Chairman and CEO and served as a Director of CVS Health Corporation upon completion of CVS Health's acquisition of Aetna. [3] Throughout Mark's tenure at Aetna, he led the company's transition from a traditional health insurance company to a consumer-oriented health care company. [3] Before joining Aetna, Mark held executive positions at Cigna, NYLCare Health Plans, and SelectCare, Inc., where he was President and Chief Executive Officer. [3, 4] He serves as a director of Verizon Communications Inc., Thrive Global, and the FIDELCO Guide Dog Foundation. [3, 4]

Mario Schlosser

Co-Founder & Chief Technology Officer

Mario Schlosser is the Co-Founder & Chief Technology Officer at Oscar Health. [1, 4, 6, 9] In this role, Mario leads product and engineering, with a focus on building Oscar's technology platform. [4, 6] He is also responsible for continuing to set the strategy for the +Oscar business. [6] Previously, Mario served as CEO of Oscar, leading the company from inception to serving over 1M members. [6] Before co-founding Oscar, Mario also co-founded the largest social gaming company in Latin America, where he led the company's analytics and game design practices. [6] Mario holds a degree in computer science from the University of Hannover in Germany and an MBA from Harvard Business School. [6]

R. Scott Blackley

Chief Financial Officer

Scott Blackley is the Chief Financial Officer, where he oversees treasury, actuarial, financial reporting, capital management and investor relations. [1, 4, 8] Prior to this role, Scott was the Chief Transformation Officer at Oscar from January 2023 through August 2023. [8] Scott was previously the Chief Financial Officer of Capital One, where he served on the Executive Committee and was responsible for leading the overall financial management of the company and advancing its financial and strategic agenda. [8] These responsibilities included accounting, corporate tax, strategic and financial planning, funding and treasury operations, corporate finance, investor relations, and corporate development. [8] Prior to Capital One, Scott held various executive positions at Fannie Mae, most recently as Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of the Capital Markets Group. [8] Scott holds a bachelor's degree in Accounting from the University of Utah. [8] Scott's early career included senior roles in consulting and public accounting, including an appointment to the US Securities and Exchange Commission as a Professional Accounting Fellow and as a Partner with KPMG, LLP. [8]
