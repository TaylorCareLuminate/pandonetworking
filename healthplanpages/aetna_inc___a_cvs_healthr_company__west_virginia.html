Recent News

2025-05-13
Aetna, the National Alliance for Care at Home, and MissionCare Collective have partnered to strengthen the direct care workforce. These collaborative efforts aim to build a scalable framework for long-term workforce stability and serve members in 15 states, including West Virginia. Dr. Chris Jagmin, Aetna's Chief Medical Officer for Medicaid, highlighted the importance of addressing the persistent shortage of direct care workers for older adults and individuals with disabilities.
2025-05-08
AM Best affirmed the Credit Ratings of CVS Health Corporation's Aetna Inc. subsidiaries, including Coventry Health Care of West Virginia, Inc. The ratings reflect the group's strong risk-adjusted capitalization and expected improved operating performance in 2025. The report also noted the impact of elevated financial leverage and goodwill from the parent company, CVS Health.
2025-03-18
Aetna announced that 88 percent of its Medicare Advantage members are enrolled in plans rated 4 stars or higher by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) for 2025. This includes contracts such as H1692 Coventry Health Care of West Virginia, Inc. (HMO). The company highlighted its focus on improving member experience and achieving high-quality performance in its Medicare Advantage offerings.
