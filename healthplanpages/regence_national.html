2025-03-03
Regence BlueShield paid $2.15 billion for health care for its fully insured members in Washington in 2024, averaging $4,495 per member. This amount outpaced the 2024 inflation rate of 2.9%, with an average increase of nearly 14% for various healthcare costs. The rise in claims is attributed primarily to significant increases in prescription drug and medical care costs, including provider contract negotiations. Despite an operating loss, Regence had a net income due to positive investment returns and reported strong capital reserves. The company also highlighted its community investments and employee volunteer hours in Washington.
