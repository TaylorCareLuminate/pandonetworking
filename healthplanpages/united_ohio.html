Recent News

2025-06-02
UnitedHealthcare and Premier Health have reached a multi-year agreement ensuring continued, uninterrupted access for UnitedHealthcare members enrolled in employer-sponsored, individual commercial plans, Medicare Advantage plans (including Group Retiree), and Dual Special Needs Plans (DSNP) to Premier's hospitals and providers in the Dayton region of Southwest Ohio, effective immediately. Letters are being sent to impacted members.
2025-04-17
Starting May 1, 2025, UnitedHealthcare Community Plan of Ohio is implementing new claim denials for ordering, referring, and prescribing (ORP) healthcare professionals. This is due to a state of Ohio requirement that claims include a valid National Provider Identifier (NPI) number for the ORP professional. Claims submitted without a valid NPI will be denied with a Y42 code.

Kurt Lewis

CEO and President for UnitedHealthcare's Employer & Individual operations throughout Ohio

Kurt Lewis was appointed the CEO and President for UnitedHealthcare's Employer & Individual operations throughout Ohio in April of 2016. [2] His current responsibilities encompass health plan strategic initiatives and business operations that include sales, marketing, network contracting, medical programs, community relations, product management and finance. [2] Kurt is a life-long resident of Ohio who grew up in Cuyahoga Falls, a suburb of Akron. [2] He graduated from The Ohio State University with a degree in business administration and began his health insurance career in Columbus more than 30 years ago. [2] Kurt has held various leadership positions within UnitedHealthcare beginning in 1999. [2] Before taking the CEO position in Ohio, Kurt was the Executive Director for UnitedHealthcare in the Southwest Ohio and Northern Kentucky markets focusing on small business, key accounts, sales and account management. [2] He is involved in many community organizations, including The Health Collaborative – Greater Cincinnati, Health Collaborative of Greater Columbus, Ohio Business Roundtable, Ohio Diversity Council, Ohio Association of Health Plans, American Heart Association Men Go Red, Cincinnati Regional Business Committee and First Tee. [2] He also served as CEO for Ohio & Northern Kentucky at UnitedHealth Group Incorporated. [5] As of 2023, he has been CEO of the company's business in three states and previously oversaw operations in Ohio, having been with the company since 1999. [6]
