Recent News

2025-06-13
Intermountain LDS Hospital and Select Health are partnering with the Salt Lake Downtown Farmers Market throughout the summer of 2025 to offer free health screenings and wellness activities. This initiative aims to promote community health and aligns with their mission to help people live healthier lives.
2025-05-07
A May 2025 provider newsletter for the Utah Edition of Select Health discusses improvements to their provider portal and website interface. It also touches on the company's growth in four states and their commitment to working with providers to deliver quality, cost-effective healthcare.
2025-04-22
Select Health published their annual Community Impact Report, "Simple Acts of Caring," on April 22, 2025, highlighting their philanthropic and community relations initiatives and achievements in 2024. The report details partnerships and events aimed at addressing social determinants of health.
2025-01-01
In the first quarter of 2025, First Choice by Select Health of South Carolina highlighted their Individual and Family Qualified Health Plan offered on the South Carolina Health Insurance Marketplace and their efforts to enhance their provider network. The newsletter also mentioned new benefits for 2025, such as doula and lactation services and access to online parenting classes, and updates on covered services effective October 1, 2024.
2024-12-16
A December 2024 Policy Update Bulletin from Select Health details several revised medical policies effective December 2024, including changes related to leadless neuromodulation systems, age requirements for certain procedures, and food allergy testing.
2024-07-01
Select Health of South Carolina announced several updates effective July 1, 2024, including a $30 incentive for correct coding of sports physicals, zero copays for all Medicaid members, and the adoption of SCDHHS's preferred drug list. They also provided updates on Change Healthcare payment recoveries and expanded coverage for continuous glucose monitoring.
