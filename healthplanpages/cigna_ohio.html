Jamie Benedict

President of Medicare Advantage Plans in Ohio

Named president of Cigna's Medicare Advantage (MA) plans in Ohio in May 2021. Has more than a decade of Medicare experience and previously led all major components of an MA plan. Based in Columbus and holds a bachelor's degree in health care administration from Ohio University.

Monica Schmude

Market President for Delaware, Ohio, Pennsylvania & West Virginia

Has been Market President for Delaware, Ohio, Pennsylvania & West Virginia at The Cigna Group since May 2017. Responsibilities include ensuring market strategy, product and network offerings meet customer and client needs, partnering with healthcare professionals, and leading community efforts. Has three decades of experience in healthcare, with previous roles at Aetna and Guardian Insurance Company.

Jeffrey Young

Vice President of Sales for Ohio & West Virginia

Joined Cigna as Vice President of Sales for Ohio & West Virginia in May 2021. Responsible for sales, retention, and customer satisfaction for Cigna clients and business partners in Ohio and West Virginia. Oversees growth and expansion for Cigna products as part of the Liberty Valley leadership team. Has over 20 years of industry experience, having started his career at Cigna and held leadership roles at CVS/Aetna.
