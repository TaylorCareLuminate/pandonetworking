Recent News

2025-06-18
MVP Health Care partnered with ZeOmega, a population health management organization, to transform its care management platform using ZeOmega's Jiva platform. Announced on June 18, 2025, the partnership aims to unify systems, improve data integration, and enhance the member experience through a more connected and personalized approach.
2025-04-09
MVP Health Care, along with Excellus BlueCross BlueShield and CDPHP, called on Congress on April 9, 2025, to pass bipartisan legislation aimed at protecting Medicare Advantage benefits and access to care for members in upstate New York, citing concerns over the financial impact of changes to the Medicare wage index.
2025-03-05
MVP Health Care pursued a custom employee development program called the Leadership Institute with UVM PACE, as highlighted in a news piece on March 5, 2025. This initiative aimed to strengthen leadership depth and build a robust talent pipeline within the organization.
2025-01-01
Effective January 1, 2025, MVP Health Care and the University of Vermont Health Network (UVMHN) will no longer offer their Medicare Advantage plan in Vermont. This update was provided to inform members and providers about the next steps for those impacted.
2025-01-01
MVP Health Care introduced a new Medical Coding section to its Provider Reference Library in January 2025 to accommodate changes in risk adjustment.
2025-01-01
In January 2025, MVP Health Care reviewed and highlighted its procedures and protocols for protecting members who may be at risk or are victims of domestic violence.
