Jatin Dave, MD

Chief Medical Officer, MassHealth

In his dual medical leadership roles, Dr. Dave is the primary clinical liaison between MassHealth (Massachusetts Medicaid) and For Health Consulting at U Mass Chan Medical School. He is a key part of the MassHealth executive team and provides medical, clinical, policy, and strategic oversight of all health care services for MassHealth members. Prior to MassHealth, he served as chief medical officer at New England Quality Care Alliance. He has also held roles at Tufts Medical Center, Tufts Health Plan, and Brigham and Women's Hospital. Dr. Dave completed fellowships in internal medicine at the University of Illinois College of Medicine and geriatric medicine at Harvard University, and holds a master's degree in public health from the Harvard School of Public Health. [2]
