As of March 5, 2025, Peoples Health Network was no longer active for real-time transactions through Optum, and all traffic was to be submitted under United Healthcare (Payer ID: 87726).
2025-02-28
Effective February 28, 2025, Peoples Health Network CPIDs 8455 and 2923 were terminated for remittance transactions through Optum, requiring providers to enroll under UnitedHealthcare CPIDs 3429 and 3550 to avoid interruptions.
2024-12-31
Due to a payer intermediary processing issue on December 27, 2024, some Professional claims transmitted to impacted payers, including Simply Healthcare Plans, Inc., were not processed. A resolution was implemented, and claims were retransmitted on December 30, 2024.
