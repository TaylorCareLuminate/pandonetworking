2025-06-19
As hurricane season begins, Louisiana Healthcare Connections is providing tips for medical preparedness, urging residents to take proactive steps for their health and medical needs during severe weather. Recommendations include preparing medications, safeguarding medical information, ensuring medical device readiness, and registering for medical needs support.
2025-04-14
Louisiana Healthcare Connections has partnered with findhelp to offer a free online Community Resources Directory for members and the public. This tool allows users to search for local services and programs including assistance with child care, food, housing, utilities, health services, job training, and more.
2025-04-14
Louisiana Healthcare Connections highlights six key benefits for its members, including a statewide provider network, unlimited free visits to primary care doctors and urgent care centers, free language interpretation services, free transportation to medical appointments, and a rewards program for completing health check-ups and wellness visits.
