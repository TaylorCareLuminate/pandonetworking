Recent News

2025-06-10
A federal judge has permitted a lawsuit filed by physicians against MultiPlan and several major health insurers, alleging a price-fixing conspiracy that resulted in systematic underpayment for out-of-network services, to advance. The article references a 2024 New York Times investigation and a 2020 study by the Office of the New York State Comptroller that examined MultiPlan's impact on healthcare payments.
2025-03-28
The U.S. Justice Department has filed a statement of interest in the ongoing antitrust litigation against MultiPlan (which recently rebranded to Claritev) and major health insurance companies. The department's filing supports the plaintiffs' argument that alleged coordination among competitors through a third-party like MultiPlan to determine out-of-network rates could violate antitrust laws. The consolidated lawsuits include actions originally filed in New York, Illinois, and California.
2025-02-17
Claritev Corporation, formerly known as MultiPlan, announced a corporate rebrand and will begin trading with a new ticker, CTEV, on the New York Stock Exchange on February 28, 2025. This rebrand marks a significant point in the company's transformation.
2025-02-17
Claritev Corporation, formerly known as MultiPlan, announced its corporate rebranding and commencement of trading under the new ticker symbol CTEV on the New York Stock Exchange. The company, which originated in New York, provides technology, data, and insights focused on healthcare affordability and transparency.
2025-01-08
The American Medical Association is actively challenging alleged price-fixing activities by health insurance companies facilitated by data analytics firms like MultiPlan. The AMA highlights a significant investigation by The New York Times into MultiPlan's business practices, which are claimed to reduce payments to physicians while potentially increasing costs for patients and employers.
