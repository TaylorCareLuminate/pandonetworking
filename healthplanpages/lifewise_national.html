Recent News

2025-01-21
LifeWise Health Plan of Washington announced details regarding their 2025 Individual and Family Plans. Updates include offering plans in 33 counties, adding Cascade Select plans in Grays Harbor and Lincoln counties, and discontinuing the LifeWise Essential Bronze HSA plan. The announcement also provided information for healthcare providers regarding eligibility, benefits, primary care providers, and access to medical policies.
