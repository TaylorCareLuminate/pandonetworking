Recent News

2025-06-17
North Dakota Governor Kelly Armstrong attended the groundbreaking for an expansion of the Altru Behavioral Health Center in Grand Forks. The project, supported by a $12.96 million state grant and requiring a $3.24 million match from Altru, will double the inpatient bed capacity from 24 to 48. The expansion is expected to be finished by fall 2026 and is part of broader state efforts to increase behavioral health services.
2025-05-27
Demolition work has begun on the former Altru Hospital building in Grand Forks, North Dakota, which closed on January 19, 2025, after the new hospital facility opened. The demolition started on May 27, 2025, and is expected to be finished by the end of summer. The site will be redeveloped into a patient and guest parking lot for the new hospital.
2025-01-22
Altru Health System officially opened its new $470 million state-of-the-art hospital in Grand Forks, North Dakota on January 22, 2025. The facility, under construction since 2019, was a collaboration between Altru, PCL Construction, Community Contractors, and JLG Architects. The new hospital features over 550,000 square feet, 12 operating rooms, and 33 monitored patient rooms, and includes private rooms throughout. The former hospital building is planned for demolition to become a parking area and water retention pond.
2025-01-19
Altru Health System completed the transfer of all patients from its old hospital building to the new facility in Grand Forks, North Dakota on January 19, 2025. The move was completed in less than eight hours. The former hospital building is slated for decommissioning and demolition to make space for patient/guest parking and a water retention pond. The Altru 860 building will be repurposed as the Altru Behavioral Health Center.
2024-07-02
Altru Health System is planning to acquire the Devils Lake hospital from CommonSpirit Health. A letter of intent was announced on June 28, 2024. This acquisition aims to create unified healthcare in the Devils Lake region. Altru will purchase the hospital, land, and equipment, subject to a six-month due diligence period. CommonSpirit Health is also undertaking renovations to the hospital's emergency department.
