Recent News

2025-05-30
Sunflower Health Plan is championing health improvement through community engagement in Kansas, investing in programs that address social determinants of health like employment, housing, food access, and education. Highlights from their 2024 Community Impact Report include improved health metrics, member engagement at farmers markets, and initiatives like the My Health Pays rewards program and Shoes for Shots.
2025-05-29
Sunflower Health Plan has partnered with Everlywell to provide at-home diabetic test kits, including HbA1c and Kidney health tests, to eligible members starting in April/May 2025. The program aims to help members screen for and manage health conditions with results shared with both patients and healthcare providers for coordinated care.
2025-01-03
Sunflower Health Plan was selected as one of the three managed care organizations (MCOs) to serve the Kansas Medicaid program (KanCare) starting in 2025, following the departure of Aetna Better Health of Kansas. This contract has a three-year term with two optional one-year extensions.
