Recent News

2025-05-15
MedCost has highlighted key IRS and Department of Health and Human Services updates for the 2026 plan year, relevant for plan sponsors evaluating plan design changes. These updates include the inflation-adjusted contribution limits for Health Savings Accounts (HSAs), as well as the minimum deductible and out-of-pocket maximums for High-Deductible Health Plans (HDHPs), and the Affordable Care Act (ACA) out-of-pocket limits.
2025-04-08
Several employer groups, including Cherokee County, Community Action Opportunities, Van Wingerden International/Valley Chilled Transportation, and Irene Wortham Center, are transitioning their health plan administration to MedCost Benefit Services effective March 1, 2025. This follows a disruption in their previous TPA services. MedCost is working to onboard these groups, which may take 4-6 weeks to fully implement in their system, potentially causing initial delays in issuing ID cards and processing claims.
