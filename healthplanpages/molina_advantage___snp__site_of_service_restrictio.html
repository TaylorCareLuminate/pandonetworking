Recent News

2025-03-26
Molina Healthcare of Texas released an impact report for its doula expansion initiative in partnership with the March of Dimes Houston Collective. This initiative, which launched in 2023 with a $50,000 grant from The MolinaCares Accord, trained eight women of color to become community-based doulas who provided services to under-resourced women in Harris County during 2024. The report was announced in recognition of World Doula Week, March 22-28, 2025.
2025-01-01
Molina Healthcare of Texas Marketplace issued a Provider News Bulletin regarding updates to the Prior Authorization (PA) Code Matrix, effective April 1st, 2025. The bulletin details specific codes being added or removed from prior authorization requirements for Experimental/Investigational services, Imaging & Special Tests, Healthcare Administered Drugs, and Unlisted & Miscellaneous services.
2024-12-20
Molina Healthcare of Texas hosted a movie day at Cinemark Mall Del Norte for residents of Las Alturas Nursing and Transitional Care in Laredo on December 20, 2024. This event was held to recognize the facility for exceeding certain quality and service criteria under the Molina Quality Living Program (MQL).
