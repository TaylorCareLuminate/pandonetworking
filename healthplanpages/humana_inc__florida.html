State Leadership

Caraline Coats

South Florida Medicare President

A 14-year Humana veteran, Coats will lead Humana's South Florida Medicare market and report to Alan Stewart. She is responsible for developing and expanding Humana's Medicare business in the 11-county South Florida region. Coats expanded Humana's Alliance provider relationships and previously served as Vice President of Humana's Bold Goal and Population Health Strategy. She earned an undergraduate degree in biology and a master's in health services administration from the University of Michigan. She has resided in South Florida for the past 12 years and also serves as interim-CEO of The Humana Foundation.

Fernando J. Valverde M.D.

Regional Market President for South and North Florida Medicare Markets

Dr. Valverde has been Regional Market President for South and North Florida Medicare Markets at Humana Inc. since January 6, 2014. He assumes responsibility for continued development and expansion of Humana's Medicare business in the South and North Florida regions and for staff leadership. Prior to joining Humana, Dr. Valverde served as Chief Executive Officer of Florida International University (FIU) Health and Associate Dean for Community and Clinical Affairs at the FIU Herbert Wertheim College of Medicine. He has over 25 years of contributions to the healthcare sector's growth, including founding, operation, and management of several healthcare companies.

Mark Kent

Regional President (Humana-owned, Florida-based primary care practices)

Mark Kent's position was expanded to Regional President with oversight of all Humana-owned, Florida-based primary care practices, including CAC, MetCare and Continucare. Under his leadership, CAC grew to 19 medical centers. Kent reports to Jaewon Ryu. Prior to this role, he spent nearly seven years with Humana in South Florida and Louisville in various strategic, operational and business development roles. He began his career with Humana in Miami in 2004 and was responsible for operational management, strategic planning, clinic startups, and sales and marketing coordination for Humana's affiliated provider centers in South Florida. His experience included designing, planning and implementing one of the first fully electronic medical record systems in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties.

Albert Tzeel, MD, MHSA, CPE, FAAPL

Regional Vice President – Health Services for Senior Products for the Florida Region

Dr. Tzeel is Humana's Regional Vice President – Health Services for Senior Products for the Florida Region. He joined Humana in March, 1999 as the Humana Medical Director for Milwaukee and has also served as Market Medical Officer for Wisconsin and Michigan and National Medical Director for HumanaOne. Albert earned his Medical Degree from the University of Michigan Medical School and his Masters in Health Services Administration from the University of Michigan School of Public Health. He has been active in the American Diabetes Association Community Leadership Board in Florida and is a member of the Board for the Type 1 Diabetes Moonshot from StartUp Health. He is also an author and co-author of multiple papers and books.

George Renaudin

President of Medicare and Medicaid

George Renaudin's expanded role includes Humana's fast-growing Medicaid business which serves more than one million members nationally across several states, including Florida. He was previously Senior Vice President and President of Medicare. Renaudin has 27 years of healthcare industry experience and has been instrumental in leading Humana's Medicare Advantage growth. He is responsible for business operations, clinical operations, product strategy, sales and distribution, and network and provider experience for Medicare and Medicaid members. Renaudin earned a bachelor's degree from Tulane University, a master's degree in Public Policy from Pennsylvania State University, and is a graduate of Loyola University Law School.

Andrew C. Agwunobi, MD, MBA

Segment President for Home Solutions

Dr. Agwunobi leads Humana's Home Solutions segment, which includes operations in Florida. He oversees areas such as care management, in-home nursing, and evaluations. Prior to joining Humana in February 2022, he served as the Secretary of the Florida Agency for Health Care Administration. He has also held CEO positions at other healthcare systems and was Interim President/CEO of UConn Health.
