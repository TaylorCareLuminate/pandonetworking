Recent News

2025-06-06
Tenet Healthcare appointed Lisa Foo as its first Chief Operating Officer, effective May 30, 2025. Foo previously served as the executive vice president of commercial operations.
2025-04-29
Tenet Healthcare announced strong first quarter 2025 results with net operating revenues increasing 20.0% compared to the first quarter of 2024. This growth was attributed to strong net revenue per case growth, facility acquisitions, and expanded service lines. Surgical business same-facility system-wide net patient service revenues also saw a 6.8% increase.
2025-02-14
Tenet Healthcare highlighted strong growth in its ambulatory surgery center (ASC) division, United Surgical Partners International (USPI), and plans for continued expansion in 2025. CEO Saum Sutaria discussed adding nearly 70 ASCs in 2024 and expanding the use of surgical robotics.
2025-02-13
Tenet Healthcare had an "outstanding" 2024 and addressed potential policy changes in Washington, particularly regarding site-neutral payment policies and Medicaid cuts. The company's focus on ASCs is seen as providing some insulation from these changes.
2025-02-12
Tenet reported strong results for the fourth quarter and full year 2024. The company also provided its financial outlook for 2025, expecting Adjusted EBITDA between $3.975 billion and $4.175 billion. Net income available to common shareholders in Q4 2024 was $318 million.
