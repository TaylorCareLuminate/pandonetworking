Recent News

2025-04-11
Governor JB Pritzker and the Illinois Department of Healthcare and Family Services celebrated the 60th anniversary of Illinois Medicaid on April 11, 2025. The program provides healthcare coverage to approximately 3.4 million people in Illinois, about one in four residents, and is the state's largest health insurer. The celebration highlighted the program's evolution and its impact on millions of lives, noting Illinois' leadership in implementing programs to increase quality and accessibility, such as extending postpartum coverage, expanding the All Kids program, launching YouthCare, implementing the PACE program, and expanding coverage for home visits and midwives. Medicaid also has a significant economic impact on the state.
