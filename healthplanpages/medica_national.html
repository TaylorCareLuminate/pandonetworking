State Leadership

Lisa Erickson

President and Chief Executive Officer

Lisa is Medica's President and Chief Executive Officer. She is also a member of the board of directors of Medica Holding Company, which oversees their family of businesses. [1] She has more than 30 years of experience in a wide range of industries, including healthcare, retail, and consumer products. [1, 2] Lisa holds a Bachelor of Business Administration with a major in Finance and Marketing from the University of Wisconsin - Madison and an MBA from the Carlson School of Business at the University of Minnesota. [2] She is a member of the Board of Directors of the Animal Humane Society and previously served on the Board of Trustees for the Academy of Holy Angels. [2]

Thomas R. Lindquist

Chief Strategy & Growth Officer

Tom leads Medica's markets and segments, managing segment strategy, product development, performance, client relationships, network strategy and contracting teams, as well as marketing and customer experience. [1, 4] He is also responsible for developing and executing strategic growth plans. [1, 4] Tom brings extensive healthcare experience including serving as Medicare Region President – Wellcare at Centene, President & Chief Executive Officer – Ascension Comp at Centene, CEO at the Allina Health | Aetna joint venture, President at Molina Healthcare of South Carolina, and with Ochsner Health Plan, UnitedHealth Group, and AccentCare/Texas Home Health. [4] Tom earned his bachelor's degree in management from the University of Texas - Arlington and his MBA in Finance from the University of Houston. [4]

David Webster

Executive

David has more than two decades of care delivery and health plan leadership experience. [1] He leads the strategic direction of Medica's clinical programs and key provider relationships to improve member outcomes. [1] He is also responsible for the clinical leadership of Medica initiatives with Health Systems and providers. [1]

Krista Dusil

Executive

Krista leads fiscal stewardship across the organization, with oversight for all accounting and financial management and reporting functions. [1] She also plays a pivotal role in advising on Medica's strategic plans and developing pricing for products and services. [1] Krista's background includes a broad range of financial management, strategy, and leadership experience that spans more than 20 years. [1] She comes to Medica from NMDP℠, where she served as their CFO. [1] Before that, Krista served as CFO for Sanvello. [1]

Adam Hjerpe

Chief Operating Officer

Adam Hjerpe joins Medica as Chief Operating Officer. [3] In this role, Adam will lead operational initiatives including membership and enrollment, claims, customer service, configuration, and the project management functions. [3] Adam brings more than 30 years of experience in healthcare and financial services. [3] During his 10 years with General Electric and 20 years with UnitedHealth Group, Adam led complex business transformation and implementation as a member of the executive leadership team. [3] Adam earned his bachelor's degree in applied business from the University of Minnesota and holds a Master Black Belt certification. [3] He serves as the board chair for Special Olympics 2026 USA Games coming to Minnesota. [3]
