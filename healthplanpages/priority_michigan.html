Carrie Kincaid

Senior Vice President of Market Development, Senior Vice President of Government Markets

Senior Vice President of Employer Solutions

Joined in 2023, oversees product innovation, wellness, underwriting, sales and client services, sales operations and marketing, and Priority Health's alliance with Cigna, led benefit options for small and large companies in Michigan, has over 15 years of industry experience in product strategy, account management, and large-scale growth initiatives.

Praveen Thadani

President/CEO (through April 2025), Advisor (until June 2025)

Tyson Health Equity Award from the Alliance of Community Health Plans (ACHP) in 2022.

Joan Budden

President and CEO (until Jan 2021)

Served as President and CEO of Priority Health from 2016 until her retirement effective January 1, 2021, began career at Priority Health in 2009 as chief marketing officer, led significant growth including merger with Total Health Care, recognized as a leader for quality, customer service, transparency, and product innovation, championed affordable access to care, recognized as a 2018 Crain's Detroit Business Notable Woman in Health Care and received Michigan Women Forward's 2020 Woman of Achievement and Courage Award.

Nick Gates

Senior Vice President of Finance, Interim President (effective May 2025)

With Priority Health since 2004, served in a variety of finance and leadership roles, oversaw finance strategy, long-range planning, investments, finance operations, accounting, and auditor relationships, has nearly 25 years of finance and accounting experience, provided governance support to the Priority Health finance and audit committee, and was involved in the valuation and due diligence for several acquisitions including Total Health Care and Physicians Health Plan of Northern Indiana.

Alicia Coronas

Vice President of Employer Solutions Products

Appointed in May 2022, focuses on the development and execution of strategy for commercial group markets and innovative products, prior role was divisional vice president, product solutions at Health Care Service Corporation (HCSC) where she led product strategy for large employers for Blue Cross Blue Shield plans in multiple states, has over 20 years of health care experience, held roles at the Blue Cross Blue Shield Association, Accenture, and Hewitt Associates, received a Bachelor of Science from the University of Notre Dame and a Master of Business Administration from the Fuqua School of Business at Duke University, is a current board member of Chicago Run and a certified health insurance executive through AHIP.

Shannon Wilson

Vice President of Population Health & Health Equity

Appointed in April 2022, previously served as vice president of state markets, east, responsible for quality improvement for Priority Health’s Medicare, Medicaid, Commercial and Individual lines of business, oversees the development and execution of Priority Health’s population health and health equity strategy, maintains her position as the executive director of the Total Health Care Foundation, prior roles include executive director of the Grand Rapids African American Health Institute, vice president of the Alliance for Health, scientific program coordinator at the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, and the first health disparities epidemiologist at the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services, received a Bachelor of Science degree in microbiology from Michigan State University and a Master of Public Health degree in hospital and molecular epidemiology from the University of Michigan, is currently pursuing a doctorate in public health leadership from the University of Illinois-Chicago.
