Recent News

2025-07-07
Molina Healthcare, Inc. announced preliminary financial results for the second quarter of 2025 and updated its full year 2025 adjusted earnings per share guidance. The company expects its second quarter 2025 adjusted earnings to be approximately $5.50 per share, modestly below prior expectations, reflecting medical cost pressures. As a result, the full year 2025 adjusted earnings are now expected to be in the range of $21.50 to $22.50 per share. The company attributes the short-term earnings pressure to a temporary dislocation between premium rates and medical cost trends.
