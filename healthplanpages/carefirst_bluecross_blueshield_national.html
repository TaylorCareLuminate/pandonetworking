Recent News

2025-05-22
AbsoluteCare has expanded its partnership with CareFirst BlueCross BlueShield to improve the health and well-being of Dual Special Needs Plan (DSNP) members in Maryland. This collaboration aims to provide comprehensive, coordinated services, including medical, behavioral, and social support, to address the needs of at-risk members who are eligible for both Medicare and Medicaid. The partnership focuses on addressing social drivers of health and improving health outcomes for vulnerable populations.
