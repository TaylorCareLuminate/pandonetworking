March 2025
Arkansas Blue Cross and Blue Shield's March 2025 Providers' News includes a reminder about the laboratory benefit management review program for certain laboratory tests that began on February 1, 2025, for fully insured health plans. The news also mentions the Blue Cross Blue Shield Association's heightened priority on improving FEP (Federal Employee Health Benefit) and PSHB (Postal Service Health Benefit) HEDIS Quality scores in 2025.
April 25, 2025
Blue Cross Blue Shield of North Dakota reported news on April 25, 2025, indicating a change impacting members with an Arkansas Blue Cross and Blue Shield plan. Starting April 1, 2025, Arkansas BCBS began using HealthHelp as a new Utilization Management vendor for high-tech radiology services for Walmart plans and national accounts.
April 2025
Arkansas Blue Cross and Blue Shield's April 2025 Providers' News outlines updates including the Blue Cross Blue Shield Association's simplification of visual indicators on ID cards, phasing out the suitcase icon starting Summer 2025. The news also details changes to the Blueprint Primary Care program effective August 1, 2025, a new electronic credentialing application process starting Summer 2025, and a new payment policy for the injectable fee schedule effective July 1, 2025.

State Leadership

Curtis Barnett

President & CEO

Serves as president and chief executive officer for Arkansas Blue Cross and Blue Shield which serves more than 2 million members nationwide. Prior to his election as president and CEO in 2017, Barnett served as senior vice president of Internal Operations for Arkansas Blue Cross and as president and CEO of its wholly-owned subsidiary company, USAble Corporation. On the national front, Barnett serves on the Blue Cross and Blue Shield Association Board of Directors. He leads as chairman of the Board of Directors for the National Institute for Healthcare Management (NIHCM) and as chairman of the Board of Managers for Blue Health Intelligence, a healthcare data analytics and solutions company.

Christy Giglio

Lead Executive of Customer Service

Joined Arkansas Blue Cross in 1997 as a data analyst. Over the past 25 years, she has served in several leadership roles, including manager of Commercial Pharmacy Operations and director of Pharmacy. Earned her bachelor's degree in marketing from Louisiana Tech University and a master's degree from the University of Arkansas at Little Rock.

Tonya Rogers

Lead Executive of Configuration

Joined Arkansas Blue Cross in 1990 as a developer. She has served in a number of leadership roles, including manager of Claims and Customer Accounts, and Information Systems director of Enrollment and Billing. Rogers earned her bachelor's degree in computer science from Henderson State University.
