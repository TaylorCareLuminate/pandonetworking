Recent News

July 7, 2025
Boston University announced budget cuts averaging 5% across the University for the coming fiscal year (FY2026), resulting in the layoff of approximately 120 staff positions and the elimination of 120 vacant positions. University leaders stated that this decision was a necessary step to address a budget gap and navigate financial uncertainties impacting higher education institutions, including issues related to federal funding, inflation, and shifts in student demographics. While acknowledging the difficulty of these changes, the university indicated that some departments would face larger or smaller reductions than the average 5% cut, and around 20 positions would have modified schedules.
