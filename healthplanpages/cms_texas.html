Recent News

2025-01-17
On January 17, 2025, the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) provided information regarding the Texas Dual Eligible Integrated Care Demonstration Project on its Texas-specific webpage. This project, initially announced in 2014, is a collaboration between the State of Texas and CMS to test an integrated care model for Medicare-Medicaid enrollees, involving contracts with Medicare-Medicaid plans to coordinate and manage covered services.

Shannon Hills

Regional Administrator and Staff Director for CMS Dallas

Shannon Hills leads and coordinates local external engagement efforts with all regional stakeholders and customers in Arkansas, Louisiana, New Mexico, Oklahoma, and Texas. She also provides administrative and operational support to all employees in CMS Dallas, promoting cohesion and collaboration throughout the regional office and with other CMS components. Shannon has over 25 years' experience managing large-scale health and human services programs at the federal government level and in the non-profit sector.
