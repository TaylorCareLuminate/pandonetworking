Rushil Desai

President and Chief Executive Officer, Aetna Better Health of Illinois

Rushil Desai is the President and Chief Executive Officer for Aetna Better Health of Illinois, which is the third largest managed care organization in Illinois. [4] He is responsible for the care of over 400,000 Medicaid members and oversees strategic business operations for one of Aetna's largest Medicaid plans. [4, 5] He began his career at CVS Health in 2020 as Chief Operating Officer for Aetna Better Health of Illinois. [4] Desai has been recognized as one of Crain's Chicago Business 2023 Notable Leaders in Health Care for his transformative leadership. [4, 6] He has implemented initiatives focused on health equity, improving health outcomes, and developing strategic provider partnerships. [4, 5] Under his leadership, Aetna Better Health of Illinois received NCQA Health Equity Accreditation. [5, 8]
