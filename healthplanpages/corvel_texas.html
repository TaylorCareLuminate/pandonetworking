Michael G. Combs

CEO & President, Chairman of the Board

Appointed Chairman of the Board effective November 26, 2024, upon V. Gordon Clemons' retirement. Has been an advisor to Clemons since 2017. CorVel's President and CEO. Located at the principal executive offices in Fort Worth, Texas.

Sarah Scott

Executive Vice President of Product

Promoted to Executive Vice President of Product in February 2025. Has 25 years of experience with CorVel. Previously served as Vice President of Network Solutions, overseeing pharmacy management, network management, ancillary services, independent medical examinations, and professional review. Announcement originated from Fort Worth, Texas.

V. Gordon Clemons

Founder, Retired Chairman of the Board

Founded CorVel Corporation in 1988 and served as Chairman of the Board since 1991. Elected to retire effective November 26, 2024, after 37 years of service. The announcement of his retirement originated from Fort Worth, Texas.
