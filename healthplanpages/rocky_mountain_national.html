State Leadership

Nathan “Nate” C. Olson

President and CEO

Joined Rocky Mountain Health Care Services in November 2017. Has 36 years' experience in finance and healthcare with 28 years serving as President and CEO of numerous healthcare systems. Opened the new Colorado Springs St. Francis Medical Center in 2008 and served as its CEO for five years. Grew up in Woodland Park. Married for 40 years with two daughters and four grandchildren.

Mark Hartman

President

Joined RMHCS in May 2020 after previously serving on the board of directors for two years. Has over 30 years of healthcare experience including clinical and administrative, non-profit and for-profit roles including CEO, CAO and COO. Earned a Bachelor of Arts in Biology from Northwestern College (Iowa), a Master of Physical Therapy from the University of Iowa, and an MBA in Healthcare Administration from Regis University. Has volunteered with local organizations including the Colorado Springs Chamber of Commerce, YMCA of the Pikes Peak Region, Center for Nonprofit Excellence, Audubon Surgical Center and Corporate board at Mt. St. Francis.

Johan Kuitse

Chief Operating Officer

Joined Rocky Mountain Health Care Services in May of 2023. Trained as a physical therapist, served in the not-for-profit hospital sector since 1985 in various clinical and leadership roles. Earned a bachelor's degree in physical therapy from the University of Evansville (IN) and a master's in administration from the University of Notre Dame.

Jay Olson

Chief Risk Officer

Has been with the Rocky Mountain Board of Directors since July 2022. Has 30 years of credit union experience. Oversees risk programs supporting cultural transformation, corporate strategy, and department initiatives. Managed complex IT projects, host system conversions, and customer relationships for technology service providers.

Andi McDonough

Vice President of Rocky Mountain PACE

Joined Rocky Mountain Health Care Services in June 2018. Brings over 20 years of leadership and patient care experience. Graduated from CU Health Sciences Center with a master's degree in physical therapy. Began career at Penrose St. Francis and advanced through various leadership roles to director of rehabilitation and respiratory therapy.

Jacqueline “Jacquie” Schlarb

Vice President of Quality and Compliance

Joined Rocky Mountain Health Care Services in February 2020. Passion for delivering high-quality care. Believes in providing holistic care considering physical, medical, emotional, and social needs. Colorado native. Graduated from the University of Colorado in 2015 with a Bachelor in Nursing Science and has worked as a Registered Nurse in the Colorado Springs community.

Patrick Gordon

Chief Executive Officer

Kevin Fitzgerald
