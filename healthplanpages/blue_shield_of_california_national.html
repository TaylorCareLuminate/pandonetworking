Recent News

2025-02-24
Blue Shield of California members have 24/7 access to U.S. board-certified physicians via Teladoc. Depending on the plan, this service may have $0 extra cost or a lower copay than an office visit. Members can use Teladoc for various conditions like cold and flu symptoms, allergies, bronchitis, respiratory infection, sinus problems, and to get prescriptions. Access is available through the Blue Shield app or by signing into a Blue Shield account. The service includes access to mental health professionals for issues like stress, anxiety, depression, and grief. Teladoc does not replace a primary care physician and does not guarantee prescriptions for certain substances.
