Brent Hunter

General Manager for group employer business in MidAmerica (including Missouri)

Brent Hunter was named General Manager for Cigna Healthcare's group employer business in MidAmerica, which spans Iowa, Kansas, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, and South Dakota. He is responsible for driving market growth for client employers with under 3,000 employees and will be based at Cigna Healthcare's Kansas City office. He has nearly three decades of industry experience, including 25 years with Aetna. He resides in Overland Park, Kansas. [8]
