Executive Leadership Finder — Web Search + AI

Load a list of organizations and this tool finds named executives for the titles you choose. C-level roles (CIO, CISO, CMIO, CTO, CDO, CNIO, CAO) are submitted to Apify as a C-level (seniority 310) employee search — the same submission Prospect Org Admin uses. VP-level titles you enter are submitted as a VP (seniority 300) search; AI then keeps the high-level VPs that fit, and more than one VP per title is allowed. Existing contacts across every BDR and workspace are checked first. A name or LinkedIn URL is only reported when it comes from the database or an Apify/web result.
