HeyReach Campaign Resume

Prevent 'Finished' campaigns from missing new contacts

Why Resume Campaigns?
HeyReach campaigns become 'Finished' when they run out of contacts. If new contacts are added later, they won't be processed unless the campaign is resumed. This tool ensures all Connect and Message campaigns stay active.
