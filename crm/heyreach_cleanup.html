How this works:
  • Campaign Started: Has LinkedIn activity (messages sent/received) - Cannot be moved
  • Assigned (Not Started): Has HeyReach account assigned but no activity yet - Can possibly be moved
  • Ready to Assign: Has intended BDR but not assigned to HeyReach yet - Can be assigned
  • Not Assigned: No BDR assignment at all - Needs to be assigned first
To rebalance misassigned contacts:
  1. Select "Activity Status: Not Started" to find moveable contacts
  2. Use "BDR Intended Assignment" filter to focus on one BDR's contacts
  3. Check contacts where "Currently in HeyReach" doesn't match "Intended Assignment"
  4. Select contacts to move, choose target list, and click "Move Selected"

Note: Filters update dynamically. All filters work together - combine them to find exactly what you need!
