Monitoring Purpose: This tool identifies recipients at risk of receiving too many emails. While there are cases where high frequency is appropriate, this helps ensure intentional communication patterns.

Duplicate Detection: Emails from the same account to the same recipient within 4 minutes are automatically filtered as duplicates (likely double-recorded in the database).
Limited Data Loaded: Due to query limitations, only recent emails were loaded. Results may not include all emails in the selected time window. Consider reviewing the data with this limitation in mind.
