Bulk Decline Cluster Fixer

Automatically detect and restore calls from bulk decline incidents

What Happened?

Issue: Multiple incidents occurred where marking a call as "Spoke Declined" caused bulk declines of many pending calls. This page automatically detects suspicious clusters (>10 calls declined at the same time) and identifies which calls were legitimately declined vs accidentally bulk declined.

How It Works:
• Finds clusters of >10 calls declined at the same time
• Checks campaign_call_tracking to find original legitimate declines (tracking.callId = phone_activities.id)
• For each legitimate decline, gets its company + customerId
• Other calls with matching company + customerId are also legitimate (kept as declined)
• All other calls in the cluster are accidental (restored to scheduled)

Protection Added: phone-calls.html now has a hard limit of 50 calls maximum and shows a confirmation dialog BEFORE any bulk decline operation with exact counts and matching criteria (company AND customerId).

Action Needed: Use this page to restore the incorrectly marked calls back to their pending state. Refresh the page to see the latest incident data.
