Recover Incorrectly Marked Bad Numbers

⚠️ CRITICAL BUG DETECTED: A bug caused the system to mark ALL calls with empty/missing phone numbers as "bad number". This page will help you recover those calls.

What Happened:

When someone marked a call with an empty phone number as "bad", the system incorrectly matched ALL other calls with empty phone numbers and marked them as bad too.

Status: The bug has been FIXED. This won't happen again.
