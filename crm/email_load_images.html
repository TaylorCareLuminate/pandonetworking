Manage images for your email campaigns. Upload and organize images that can be easily inserted into email templates.
How to Use Images in Emails
1. Upload Images: Upload your image files to the /images/crm/ folder and give them descriptive names
2. Copy Token: Click "Copy Token" on any image to get the placeholder code
3. Insert in Emails: Paste the token (like [image:logo]) into your email content
4. Automatic Replacement: The system will automatically replace tokens with actual images when sending
