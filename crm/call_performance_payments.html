Achievement Pool System

What is the Achievement Pool?

The Achievement Pool is a bonus reward system that grows as team members make qualifying calls. When you successfully schedule a meeting with a prospect, you earn the entire current Achievement Pool amount in addition to your base payment!

How the Pool Grows

The Achievement Pool starts at $40.00 for each campaign and grows with every qualifying call made by all team members:

Calls 1-20
+$0.10 each
Calls 21-40
+$0.20 each
Calls 41-60
+$0.30 each
Calls 61-80
+$0.40 each
Calls 81+ (up to $200)
+$0.50 each
Pool above $200
+$0.10 each

Once the pool reaches $200.00, it continues to grow — but at a reduced rate of $0.10 per qualifying call. There is no hard cap; the pool can still climb well above $200 on long campaigns.

Qualifying Call Types

These call outcomes contribute to growing the Achievement Pool:

  • Left Personalized Recorded Message
  • Left Message With Receptionist/Team Member
  • Unable to Leave Message, but Good Number
  • Spoke to Prospect - Asked for Email Follow Up
  • Spoke to Prospect - Scheduled Callback
Example: Achievement Pool Calculation Starting Pool: $40.00 After 15 qualifying calls: $40.00 + (15 × $0.10) = $41.50 After 25 qualifying calls: $40.00 + (20 × $0.10) + (5 × $0.20) = $44.00 After 50 qualifying calls: $40.00 + (20 × $0.10) + (20 × $0.20) + (10 × $0.30) = $49.00 When you schedule a meeting after 50 qualifying calls: Your Payment = $0.50 (base) + $49.00 (pool) = $49.50 total! --- Soft cap example --- After 360 qualifying calls the pool reaches $200.00. Each additional qualifying call then adds $0.10 instead of $0.50. After 400 qualifying calls: $200.00 + (40 × $0.10) = $204.00

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if a scheduled meeting doesn't occur?
If a meeting you scheduled doesn't take place, the Achievement Pool amount will be returned to the pool and you'll only receive the base $0.50 payment for that call.
Can I see the Achievement Pool for different campaigns?
Yes! Each campaign has its own Achievement Pool. The current pool value is displayed in your performance dashboard and updates when you select different campaigns.
Does the pool have a limit?
There is no hard cap on the Achievement Pool. However, once the pool reaches $200.00, the growth rate slows from $0.50 per qualifying call to $0.10 per qualifying call. The pool can still grow well above $200 — it just grows more gradually after that point. This keeps campaigns competitive while keeping the incentive structure sustainable long term.
How often do I get paid?
Payment schedules are determined by your contract terms. All call outcomes and payments are tracked in real-time for accurate reporting.
What if I accidentally mark the wrong outcome?
Contact your supervisor immediately if you mark an incorrect outcome. All call records are tracked with timestamps and can be reviewed and corrected if necessary.
Why don't "Bad Number" calls pay anything?
Bad numbers represent data quality issues that don't provide value to the campaign. However, identifying bad numbers helps improve the overall data quality for future campaigns.

Tips for Maximizing Earnings

Focus on Quality Conversations

The highest-paying outcomes involve actually speaking with prospects. Practice your conversation skills and follow the provided scripts to increase your success rate.

  • Be Professional: Always maintain a professional tone and demeanor
  • Follow Scripts: Use the provided call scripts as your foundation
  • Take Good Notes: Detailed notes help with follow-up and show professionalism
  • Schedule Strategically: Try to call when prospects are most likely to answer
  • Build Rapport: A friendly conversation increases the chance of scheduling meetings
  • Track Achievement Pools: Monitor pool values to see when scheduling meetings is most rewarding
Success Example: - Make 10 qualifying calls (contributing to pool growth) - Successfully schedule 1 meeting when pool is at $45.00 - Total earnings: (10 × $0.50) + $0.50 + $45.00 = $50.50 This shows how contributing to the pool benefits everyone!
