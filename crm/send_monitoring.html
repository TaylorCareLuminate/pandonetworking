OAuth Silent Failure Scanner

After switching from SMTP to OAuth, some emails may have been marked as "sent" in the scheduler queue (scheduledEmails) without a matching delivery record in sentEmailsDatabase. These are ghost sends — they look fine on this page but were never delivered.

This scanner cross-references both collections and flags any discrepancies. Flagged emails are marked status = "failed" so they can be deleted via Campaign Cleanup and rescheduled via Reschedule Campaigns.
