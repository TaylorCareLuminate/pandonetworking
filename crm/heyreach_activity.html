Backfill Missing Events from Campaign Data
Pull lead data from HeyReach campaigns and create synthetic heyreach_activity records for events that were missed (e.g. after an account deletion/recreation). Events are inferred from each lead's current status, progress, and message/connection counts. Existing events are automatically skipped to prevent duplicates.

Inferred events:   CONNECTION_REQUEST_SENT (all leads)  |  CONNECTION_REQUEST_ACCEPTED (if connected)  |  MESSAGE_SENT (if messagesCount > 0)  |  MESSAGE_REPLY_RECEIVED (if status includes "replied")
