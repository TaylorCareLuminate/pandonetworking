Workspace Contact Enrichment

Load known contacts (prospects) from a Connect workspace and enrich each with an email & phone from Seamless.AI. Contacts with a LinkedIn profile URL are enriched directly; others are found by name + company.

~$0.35 per enriched contact
How it works: Select a Connect workspace, load its contacts (from prospect_contacts), then run enrichment. Contacts with a LinkedIn profile URL are enriched directly via Seamless. Those without one are looked up by name + company — Seamless results are name-verified before saving. Contacts already enriched (contact_mining_email set) are skipped automatically.
