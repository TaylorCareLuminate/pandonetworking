# 🚀 Conversation Analysis - Quick Start

## 5-Minute Setup & First Analysis

### Step 1: Open HeyReach Inbox
Navigate to: `crm/heyreach_inbox.html`

### Step 2: Select Customer
1. Click **Customer Filter** dropdown
2. Select a customer (e.g., "Mentavi Health")

### Step 3: Analyze
1. Click the purple **"Analyze Conversations"** button 🧠
2. Wait for completion (watch the logs)

### Step 4: View Results
1. Check the log panel for category breakdown:
   ```
   Category 1 (Outreach No Response): 87
   Category 2 (Incoming No Response): 45
   Category 3 (Conversation Limited): 112
   Category 4 (Conversation Relationship): 116
   ```

### Step 5: Filter & Explore
1. Use **Category Filter** to view specific types
2. Click conversations to see AI analysis details
3. Look for color-coded badges on each card

---

## What Each Category Means

| Category | Meaning | Cost |
|----------|---------|------|
| 🔴 **Category 1** | You reached out, no response | FREE (rule-based) |
| 🟡 **Category 2** | They reached out, you didn't respond | FREE (rule-based) |
| 🔵 **Category 3** | Limited back-and-forth, professional | ~$0.001 (AI) |
| 🟢 **Category 4** | Real relationship, personal | ~$0.001 (AI) |

---

## Tips

✅ **First run takes longest** (5-10 min for 500+ conversations)  
✅ **Subsequent runs are fast** (skips already-analyzed)  
✅ **Category 4 never re-analyzes** (most efficient)  
✅ **Check "This Year" badge** for recent activity  
✅ **Read AI summaries** in conversation modals  

---

## Common Use Cases

### Find Warm Leads
**Filter**: Category 4 (Conversation Relationship) + This Year  
**Purpose**: Identify people you know well who are still engaged

### Follow-Up Opportunities
**Filter**: Category 1 (Outreach No Response)  
**Purpose**: Find people who never responded to your outreach

### Clean Up Spam
**Filter**: Category 2 (Incoming No Response)  
**Purpose**: Identify solicitations and unwanted messages

### Nurture Weak Connections
**Filter**: Category 3 (Conversation Limited) + This Year  
**Purpose**: Find recent but shallow relationships to deepen

## Troubleshooting

**"Please select a customer first"**  
→ Select a customer from the dropdown before clicking Analyze

**No conversations found**  
→ Run "Sync Inbox Now" first to pull LinkedIn messages

**Analysis takes too long**  
→ Normal for first run (20 conversations/batch with 1s delay)  
→ Subsequent runs are much faster
