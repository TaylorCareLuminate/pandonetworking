About the Practice

Ochsner LSU Health is a public-private partnership between Ochsner Health and LSU Health Shreveport, providing healthcare services in over 70 specialties and sub-specialties across North Louisiana. [2, 10, 12] The system includes hospitals, clinics, and health centers, offering a range of care from primary care to advanced services like Level 1 trauma, burn care, cancer care, women's and children's services, and robotic surgery. [1, 8, 9, 11, 12] The organization is dedicated to patient care, advancing medical science through research, and educating future healthcare leaders. [1, 9, 12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Ochsner LSU Health utilizes a patient portal branded as MyOchsner. This patient portal is developed by Epic Systems Corporation. MyOchsner provides patients with secure online access to manage their health information, including scheduling appointments, viewing test results, messaging healthcare providers, and requesting prescription refills. Several search results corroborate the use of Epic as the electronic health record (EHR) system by Ochsner Health, the parent organization in the Ochsner LSU Health partnership. These mentions include information about Epic access for students and instructors, leveraging Epic for population health, and Epic EMR access. The direct link between the MyOchsner portal being developed by Epic and the mentions of Epic within the Ochsner system indicates that Epic is the EHR used by Ochsner LSU Health.
