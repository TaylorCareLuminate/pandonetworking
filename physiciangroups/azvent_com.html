About the Practice

Valley ENT is Arizona's largest ear, nose, and throat physician group. [1] Their providers specialize in otolaryngology, allergy treatments, audiology, and facial plastic surgery. [1] They provide comprehensive ENT care at their locations throughout the state, offering high-quality, clinically proven treatments and technologies. [1]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Valley ENT (azvent.com) utilizes the healow patient portal, which is integrated with the eClinicalWorks electronic health record (EHR) system. The healow platform provides Valley ENT patients with access to various engagement solutions, including online appointment booking (Open Access), Kiosk for check-in, and patient questionnaires. Additionally, patients can use healow Payment Services to make payments through the patient portal, as well as via text or paper statements. This indicates that healow serves as the patient-facing portal branded and used by Valley ENT, and it is part of the broader eClinicalWorks EHR system that the practice uses.
