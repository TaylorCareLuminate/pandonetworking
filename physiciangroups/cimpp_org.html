About the Practice

Coney Island Medical Practice Plan, PC's mission is to be the clinical service provider at Coney Island Hospital. [3, 4] The organization is categorized as a Group Health Practice and Outpatient Health Care Practitioners and Facilities. [2] They contract with Coney Island Hospital to provide physician services, which can include anesthesiologists, radiologists, and pathologists. [6]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Based on the available information, Coney Island Medical Practice Plan, PC (cimpp.org), which serves as the clinical service provider for NYC Health + Hospitals/Coney Island, utilizes the Epic electronic medical record (EHR) system. The patient portal associated with the Epic EHR system is called MyChart. NYC Health + Hospitals/Coney Island successfully implemented the Epic system, including the MyChart patient portal, in 2017. This allows patients to access their medical records, manage appointments, and communicate with their care team online.
