About the Practice

Mount Sinai Queens is a community hospital in Astoria, Queens, offering high-quality outpatient, emergency, and inpatient medical services with a team of nearly 500 physicians across close to 40 medical and surgical specialties and subspecialties. It provides comprehensive care, including a state-of-the-art Emergency Department, imaging services, multispecialty physician practices, advanced operating rooms, interventional radiology, and expanding cancer programs with a new Infusion Center. Mount Sinai Queens is recognized as a primary Stroke Center and has received Magnet® designation for nursing excellence. [9, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Mount Sinai Queens uses MyChart as its patient portal. The MyChart patient portal is licensed from Epic Systems Corporation. This indicates that Mount Sinai Queens is using Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) vendor. Through the MyChart portal, patients can manage appointments, access test results and medical records, request prescription refills, and communicate with their healthcare providers.
