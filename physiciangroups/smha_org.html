About the Practice

St. Mary's Healthcare, located in Amsterdam, NY, is a local, independent healthcare system providing compassionate and comprehensive healthcare services to the people of Montgomery and Fulton counties since 1903. They offer a wide range of services including acute care, primary care, specialty care, behavioral health and addiction services, urgent care, cancer care, women's health, and surgery, delivered across multiple locations including St. Mary's Hospital, the Rao Outpatient Pavilion, and various health centers and clinics.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

St. Mary's Healthcare in Amsterdam, NY (smha.org) uses MyChart as their patient portal. MyChart is the patient portal system that is associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. St. Mary's Healthcare has been in the process of transitioning to a new Health Information System, moving from previous systems, including Meditech, to a single electronic medical record system, Epic. This transition includes the implementation of the MyChart patient portal, which allows patients to manage their health information, view test results, schedule appointments, and communicate with their care team.
