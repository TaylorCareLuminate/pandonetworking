About the Practice

Austin Kidney Associates specializes in nephrology, providing comprehensive care for kidney disorders, including the diagnosis, treatment, and management of conditions such as diabetic and hypertensive kidney disease, kidney failure, electrolyte abnormalities, hypertension, and kidney transplants. [2, 8] They offer a wide range of outpatient services across multiple office locations and provide various dialysis services, including in-center hemodialysis, home peritoneal dialysis, home hemodialysis, and nocturnal dialysis, working in conjunction with dialysis centers like DaVita and Satellite Healthcare. [2, 8] The practice is also involved in kidney transplant programs and offers vascular access management at Austin Access Care. [2, 8] They emphasize preventative medicine, patient education, and strive to provide compassionate and high-quality kidney care. [2, 8]

Methodology & Findings

Austin Kidney Associates (austinkidney.com) utilizes the MyChart patient portal. This patient portal is connected to their electronic medical record (EMR) system, which is called "CKD EHR by Epic." Austin Kidney Associates implemented this Epic-based system on October 1, 2024, in collaboration with DaVita Physician Solutions. They selected CKD EHR by Epic due to its specialization in kidney care. Patients are encouraged to sign up for MyChart to access health information, receive updates, and communicate with the care team. When signing up for MyChart, patients need to search for the organization "DaVita Physician Solutions CKD EHR" and then select Austin Kidney Associates.
