About the Practice

Indiana Nephrology & Internal Medicine, P.C. is a large group of physicians specializing in Nephrology and Internal Medicine, providing comprehensive and up-to-date care for individuals with chronic or acute kidney disease. [2, 3, 9] Their services include the treatment and management of conditions such as acute or chronic kidney disease, prevention of kidney stones, polycystic kidney disease, high blood pressure, dialysis (Kidney Failure), electrolyte disorders, hypertension, and proteinuria. [2, 5, 9] Their care team consists of board-certified physicians and advanced clinical practitioners dedicated to helping patients live well with kidney disease. [2, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Indiana Nephrology And Internal Medicine, P.C. (in-im.com) uses MyChart as their patient portal. They transitioned to MyChart from the Acumen Patient Portal effective November 11, 2019. MyChart allows patients to access their personal health records, send secure messages to their providers, manage appointments, view test results, pay bills online, and request prescription refills. MyChart is a patient portal system provided by Epic Systems, which is a well-known electronic health record (EHR) vendor.
