About the Practice

Capital Cardiology Associates PC is a leading provider of cardiac care in the Capital Region of New York, offering a full range of diagnostic, interventional, surgical, therapeutic, and preventative services for cardiovascular disease. [2, 4, 5] Their team includes board-certified cardiologists and advanced care providers dedicated to providing high-quality, compassionate care. [2, 5, 9] The practice has been serving the community since 1980, with a reputation for reliability and trustworthiness. [2] They also offer services like enhanced cardiac access for same-day evaluations for potential cardiac symptoms. [11]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Capital Cardiology Associates PC utilizes a patient portal branded as the "Capital Cardiology Associates Patient Portal" or "CCA Patient Portal". Their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system is called eScribe. Information from the eScribe website and a related entity, Lille Group (escribeHOST), indicates that the eScribe EHR system includes an integrated patient portal. This suggests that the patient portal used by Capital Cardiology Associates PC is likely the patient portal component of the eScribe EHR system.
