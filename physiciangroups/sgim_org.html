About the Practice

The Society of General Internal Medicine (SGIM) is a professional organization for individuals working in academic general internal medicine. Its members are dedicated to improving health through research, education, and patient care. SGIM focuses on enhancing medical education, improving access to care for all populations, eliminating healthcare disparities, and advancing comprehensive, coordinated, and cost-effective care for adults through innovative education, research, and clinical practice.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Based on the information from sgim.org and related search results, it's important to clarify that the Society of General Internal Medicine (SGIM) is a professional society for academic general internists, not a healthcare provider organization that would use a patient portal or an Electronic Health Record (EHR) system for direct patient care with patients. As a professional society, SGIM's mission is centered on enhancing medical education, improving healthcare access, promoting research, and providing a community for its members who are physicians engaged in the practice and teaching of general internal medicine. While SGIM provides resources and engages in discussions related to EHRs and patient portals, these are in the context of supporting their members in their clinical practices and research, rather than describing SGIM's own internal systems for patient care. SGIM does offer member-focused platforms like GIMLearn (a learning portal) and GIM Connect (a networking platform), but these are distinct from patient portals used for managing personal health information and appointments with healthcare providers. Therefore, SGIM as an organization does not utilize a patient portal or a primary EHR system in the way a hospital or clinic would. The patient portals and EHR systems discussed in the search results are those used by individual general internists and the institutions where they practice.
