About the Practice

North Country Family Health Center (nocofamilyhealth.org) is a Federally Qualified Community Health Center providing accessible, high quality, affordable, patient-centered, and integrated health services to improve the health, wellness, and quality of life for individuals and families in their community. They offer a range of services including adult and pediatric primary medical care, behavioral health, and dental care, regardless of insurance or income, with a sliding fee scale available. The center also provides WIC services, case management, and assistance with insurance enrollment.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

North Country Medical Group, associated with nocofamilyhealth.org, utilizes the athenaOne Electronic Health Record (EHR) system for its medical and behavioral health services. They transitioned to athenaOne on October 20, 2020. While the specific branding or name of the patient portal is not explicitly stated on the readily available patient resources pages of the nocofamilyhealth.org website, it is highly probable that they use the patient portal integrated within the athenaOne system, which is known as athenaPatient. Healthcare providers using the athenaOne platform typically offer the athenaPatient portal to their patients for accessing health information and communicating with their providers.
