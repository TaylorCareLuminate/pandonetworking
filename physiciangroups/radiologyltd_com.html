About the Practice

Radiology Ltd. is a radiology practice based in Tucson, Arizona, providing world-class imaging services to improve the health of Southern Arizonans. They offer compassionate care and a comprehensive range of superior medical imaging services to patients, providers, and healthcare organizations. Their services include diagnostic imaging, interventional radiology procedures, MRI (including wide-bore options), CT, PET, Ultrasound, DEXA, X-Ray, and specialized women's imaging centers. The practice is physician-owned and operated, with radiologists trained at leading academic centers dedicated to quality-focused patient care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Radiology Ltd. (radiologyltd.com) utilizes a patient portal branded as "MyRad". This online portal allows patients to access their medical records, view exam history, obtain educational materials, request results, update information, and manage appointments and billing. The vendor behind the MyRad patient portal and other components of Radiology Ltd.'s workflow management is Royal Solutions Group, also referred to as Royal Health Inc. Radiology Ltd. has implemented "Royal Enterprise Care," a platform from Royal Solutions Group designed to manage the entire radiology workflow, including patient engagement through the portal. Royal Solutions Group is a provider of healthcare IT solutions, including ONC-certified EHR and RIS (Radiology Information System) capabilities. Their platform is described as a complete RIS that can also provide an EMR suite. While the search results confirm Radiology Ltd. uses Royal's patient portal and workflow management solutions, it strongly suggests that Royal Solutions Group is the primary system provider for their radiology information and potentially electronic health records.
