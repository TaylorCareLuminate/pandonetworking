About the Practice

Arizona Pain and Spine Institute, accessible via gotpainarizona.com, specializes in comprehensive and multidisciplinary pain management services. They offer advanced medical treatments, regenerative medicine including amniotic tissue therapy, and minimally invasive procedures to address a wide range of chronic and acute pain conditions. Their care focuses on alleviating pain and improving patients' quality of life, treating conditions such as lower back pain, neck pain, sciatica, musculoskeletal/sports injuries, arthritis, and nerve pain.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The Spine Institute of Arizona (gotpainarizona.com) utilizes a patient portal provided by Prime Clinical Systems Inc. This is indicated on their patient portal login page which states, "© Powered by Prime Clinical Systems" and includes a licensing notice from Prime Clinical Systems Inc.. Prime Clinical Systems Inc. is a company that specializes in providing certified electronic health record (EHR) software and practice management solutions. Their offerings include a fully integrated EHR system.. The patient portal used by the Spine Institute of Arizona is referred to as "Prime Clinical's Patient Portal" or "Patient Chart Manager's PATIENT PORTAL" within the documentation from Prime Clinical Systems.. Based on this information, the patient portal vendor and branding used by Spine Institute of Arizona is Prime Clinical Systems, and their associated EHR vendor is also Prime Clinical Systems.
