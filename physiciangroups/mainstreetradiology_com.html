About the Practice

Main Street Radiology has been a trusted provider of comprehensive diagnostic imaging services in Queens, NY since 1966. [1, 2, 10, 14] They offer a complete spectrum of imaging services including CT scans, MRI, ultrasound, digital mammography, PET/CT, bone density, digital X-rays, interventional radiology, and fluoroscopy, interpreted by American Board of Radiology Certified sub-specialist physicians. [1, 2] Known for their commitment to patient care, they provide services to residents throughout the five boroughs of New York City. [2, 10]
