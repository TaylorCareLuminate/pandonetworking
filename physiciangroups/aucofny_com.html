About the Practice

Advanced Urology Centers of New York (AUCNY) is the largest urology group practice in the United States, providing the highest level of quality medical services across numerous convenient locations in the Bronx, Manhattan, Nassau, Queens, Rockland, Suffolk, and Westchester areas. [1, 6] Committed to clinical excellence and patient experience, AUCNY physicians and staff offer comprehensive, compassionate, and personalized urological care for a wide range of conditions using state-of-the-art technology and evidence-based practices. [2, 3, 4, 9] They treat all urologic diseases, including prostate enlargement, urinary incontinence, kidney stones, and various cancers. [1]
