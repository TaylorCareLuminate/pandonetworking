About the Practice

SMIL Southwest Medical Imaging is a physician-owned radiology practice providing state-of-the-art diagnostic medical imaging and interventional radiology services in the Greater Phoenix Metro area through numerous outpatient centers and in major hospitals. [3, 4, 7, 9] They offer a wide range of imaging modalities including MRI, CT, PET-CT, Mammography, Breast Services, Ultrasound, DEXA, X-ray, and Nuclear Medicine. [3, 4, 7] SMIL is recognized for its over 40 years of experience, advanced technology, independent research, and world-class patient care, focusing on providing high-quality, innovative, and patient-focused services with over 70 expert, fellowship-trained radiologists specializing in various imaging modalities. [3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Southwest Diagnostic Imaging (SMIL) utilizes a patient portal branded as "See My SMIL Exams & Reports". This patient portal is powered by Intelerad. Intelerad is a vendor specializing in medical imaging software, including solutions like PACS and patient portals that allow patients to access their exam results and reports. While Intelerad is the vendor for the patient portal, it is not explicitly stated in the search results that Intelerad is also the vendor for SMIL's broader Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. However, patient portals are typically integrated with or are a component of an organization's EHR infrastructure.
