About the Practice

Renal Associates is a large nephrology and kidney specialist group in South Texas providing comprehensive diagnostic, therapeutic, and preventive care for renal disease. [4, 12] Their services include consultations and treatment for chronic kidney disease, acute kidney failure, severe hypertension and diabetes, fluid and electrolyte disorders, and severe edema states. [4, 6, 12] They offer outpatient and inpatient dialysis supervision, peritoneal dialysis training, home hemodialysis training, kidney transplant evaluation, preparation, and follow-up care, medical management and prevention of kidney stones, nutritional information, and care for high-risk obstetric patients with renal disease. [4, 6, 12] The group is also involved in clinical research and has received certification for following research-based treatment guidelines for complex hypertension. [4, 6, 12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Renal Associates (rapadocs.com) utilizes the MyChart patient portal, which is tied to the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Specifically, they appear to be using an Epic-based EHR known as "Acumen Epic Connect" or "Acumen EHR". According to information found, Renal Associates of Baton Rouge, which is associated with the rapadocs.com domain, mentions "MyChartPortal" and "Acumen EHR" on their staff login page. MyChart is widely recognized as the patient portal system developed by Epic Systems Corporation. Further search results corroborate the use of an Epic-based system in nephrology practices. "Acumen Epic Connect" is described as a nephrology-specific configuration of the Epic EHR, used by many nephrology groups. Acumen Physician Solutions partnered with Epic to create "Acumen 2.0, powered by Epic," highlighting the integration of Acumen's nephrology tools with Epic's robust system. Therefore, the patient portal vendor/branding used by Renal Associates (rapadocs.com) is MyChart (possibly referred to as MyChartPortal), and this is connected to the Acumen Epic Connect EHR system, which is built on the Epic platform.
