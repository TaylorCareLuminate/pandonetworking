About the Practice

Manhattan Pediatrics, associated with manhattanpeds.com, is a group of Board Certified pediatricians providing comprehensive care to infants, children, and adolescents up to age 20. They offer preventive care, treatment for acute and chronic illnesses, and care coordination, aiming to be a medical home for their patients. The practice provides same-day sick appointments and ensures a pediatrician is available 24/7. They are affiliated with New York Presbyterian Hospital (Columbia and Weill Cornell campuses).

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Manhattan Pediatrics (manhattanpeds.com) utilizes the patient portal branded as "My Kid's Chart". This patient portal is provided by PCC (Physician's Computer Company), a company that specializes in electronic health record (EHR) and practice management systems specifically designed for pediatric practices. "My Kid's Chart," as the patient portal for Manhattan Pediatrics, allows patients and families to access various features, including reviewing medical records, viewing test results, managing appointments, and communicating with the practice. PCC is recognized as a provider of pediatric EHR solutions, offering tools that streamline clinical workflows and enhance patient care for pediatric practices. Their focus is exclusively on meeting the needs of independent pediatricians. The integration of the "My Kid's Chart" patient portal with PCC's EHR system provides practices like Manhattan Pediatrics with a comprehensive solution for managing patient information and communication.
