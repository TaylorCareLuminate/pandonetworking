About the Practice

The University of Colorado is a premier public research university system in Colorado with four campuses: Boulder, Colorado Springs, Denver, and the Anschutz Medical Campus. It offers a broad range of undergraduate and graduate programs across various disciplines. The Anschutz Medical Campus is a leading academic medical center renowned for transformative education, groundbreaking research, and comprehensive patient care provided through partners like UCHealth and Children's Hospital Colorado, covering numerous medical specialties and advancing healthcare discoveries.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The University of Colorado (cu.edu), specifically its associated health system UCHealth, utilizes a patient portal branded as "My Health Connection". This portal allows patients to manage appointments, view test results, message providers, and pay bills, among other features. My Health Connection is connected to the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. UCHealth has been using Epic as its EHR system as far back as 2013, and continues to utilize Epic according to more recent information from 2017 and 2024. CU Medicine, which is the region's largest multi-specialty physician group practice and supports University of Colorado School of Medicine physicians, also directs patients to schedule appointments through My Health Connection when seeing providers at hospital partner locations like UCHealth. It is worth noting that the University of Colorado Boulder campus's Wardenburg Health Center appears to use a different patient portal called "MyCUHealth". However, for the broader University of Colorado health system encompassing the hospitals and clinics under UCHealth and CU Medicine, My Health Connection is the patient portal powered by the Epic EHR system.
