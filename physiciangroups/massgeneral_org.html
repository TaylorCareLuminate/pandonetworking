About the Practice

Massachusetts General Hospital offers sophisticated diagnostic and therapeutic care across virtually every medical and surgical specialty and subspecialty. [2] Consistently recognized as one of America's Best Hospitals by U.S. News and World Report, Mass General provides comprehensive primary and specialty care for adults and children. [2, 11, 12, 13] The hospital is the oldest and largest teaching affiliate of Harvard Medical School and conducts the largest hospital-based research program in the United States, bridging innovative science with state-of-the-art clinical medicine to advance patient care, education, and research. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Massachusetts General Hospital (MGH) utilizes a patient portal called Patient Gateway. This portal is part of the Mass General Brigham integrated health system. The Patient Gateway portal is connected with the Epic electronic health record (EHR system). This is indicated by information stating that patients can access health information from other institutions using Patient Gateway if those institutions are also using Epic. Therefore, Massachusetts General Hospital uses the Epic EHR system, with Patient Gateway serving as its patient portal, similar to how Epic uses MyChart at other institutions.
