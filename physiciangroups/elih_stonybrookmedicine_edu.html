About the Practice

Stony Brook Eastern Long Island Hospital (SBELIH) is a 70-bed acute care community hospital located in Greenport, NY. Established in 1905 as the first hospital in Suffolk County, it joined Stony Brook Medicine in 2019. SBELIH provides comprehensive healthcare to the residents of the North Fork and Shelter Island, as well as regional behavioral health programs for the greater Suffolk County area. Key specialties and services include Medical-Surgical, Advanced Ambulatory Care, Behavioral Health, Emergency, Geriatric, Diagnostic Services, Physical Therapy, Gastrointestinal Services, Orthopedics, General Surgery, Wound Care, Interventional Pain Management, and Psychiatry. It also serves as a clinical campus and training site for Stony Brook Medicine.
