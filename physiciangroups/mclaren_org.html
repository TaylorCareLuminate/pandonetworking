About the Practice

McLaren Health Care, headquartered in Grand Blanc, Michigan, is a fully integrated health care delivery system offering quality, evidence-based patient care. The system comprises 12 hospitals, ambulatory surgery centers, imaging centers, a large employed physician network, and health plans. McLaren provides a wide range of services, including primary and specialty care, with key specialties such as cancer care through the Karmanos Cancer Institute (an NCI-designated comprehensive cancer center), cardiovascular services, neurosciences, orthopedics, emergency and trauma care, imaging, and stroke care. They serve communities across Michigan and Indiana with a focus on comprehensive and accessible healthcare.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

McLaren Health Care utilizes the MyMcLarenChart patient portal, which is accessed through the HealtheLife mobile application. This patient portal is provided by Cerner and is integrated directly into the Cerner PowerChart electronic health record (EHR) system. MyMcLarenChart allows patients to access their medical information, view visit summaries, lab and radiology results, send messages to their care team, and access educational information. McLaren recently transitioned to the HealtheLife application for their patient portal, replacing a previous portal called Syntellis. Several other healthcare systems also use the HealtheLife patient portal, and multiple sources indicate that HealtheLife is a patient portal solution offered by Cerner, which integrates with their EHR systems.
