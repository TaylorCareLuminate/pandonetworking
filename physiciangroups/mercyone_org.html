About the Practice

MercyOne is a connected system of healthcare facilities and services dedicated to helping people live their best life. [12] They offer a broad range of medical services focused on treating chronic conditions and complex diseases, as well as acute illness and injury. [2, 4] Services include primary care, specialty care (such as Palliative Care, Pulmonology, Neurology, Kidney Care, Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics, and Cancer Care), urgent care, surgical care, therapy and rehabilitation, and intensive care for critically ill patients. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

MercyOne utilizes several different patient portals across its network, each connecting to a specific electronic health record (EHR) system. The patient portal and associated EHR system in use can vary depending on the specific MercyOne location and whether the services are for hospital or clinic care. The primary patient portal for most of MercyOne is **MyChart**. MyChart is the patient portal developed by **Epic Systems**, and MercyOne's use of MyChart aligns with Trinity Health, of which MercyOne is a member, and which uses Epic as its electronic health record system known as TogetherCare. However, MyChart is explicitly noted as NOT being available for MercyOne Siouxland or MercyOne Genesis. For **MercyOne North Iowa Affiliates** and **MercyOne Elkader Medical Center**, the patient portal used is **HealtheLife**. HealtheLife is a patient portal solution that is commonly associated with the **Cerner** EHR system. Support information for the HealtheLife portal at MercyOne North Iowa also refers to a Cerner Help Line. At **MercyOne Siouxland**, there are different portals for hospital and clinic services. The hospital patient portal is referred to as **My Health Record**, although one source also mentions "MyMercy" in the context of the hospital portal. While "My Health Record" can be a generic term, one search result specifically identifies **Epic** as the EHR used at MercyOne Siouxland Medical Center Hospital Medicine. For clinic services at MercyOne Siouxland, the patient portal is the **NextGen Clinic Patient Portal**. This portal is part of the **NextGen Healthcare** EHR system. **MercyOne Centerville** uses **My OneCare** as its online electronic health record and patient web site. My OneCare appears to be a distinct system, described as an all-in-one, web-based practice management, documentation, and patient communication solution, and is linked to AMS powered by JnJ. It is worth noting that in September 2022, it was reported that MercyOne health system would transition from using Cerner to Epic to align with Trinity Health. While the continued mention of Cerner's HealtheLife and NextGen in more recent search results suggests this transition may be ongoing or specific locations retain different systems, the overall trend for much of the MercyOne network, aligning with Trinity Health, points towards the use of Epic and the MyChart patient portal.
