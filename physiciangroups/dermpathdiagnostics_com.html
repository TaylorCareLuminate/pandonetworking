About the Practice

Dermpath Diagnostics is a full-service laboratory exclusively focused on providing exceptional dermatopathology services. They specialize in diagnosing disorders of the skin, hair, and nails through microscopic examination and advanced diagnostic technologies. Their mission is to provide accurate, clear, and prompt diagnoses, supported by a team of over 75 board-certified dermatopathologists and a comprehensive test menu including special stains, immunofluorescence, and molecular testing. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Dermpath Diagnostics, a dermatopathology service, utilizes the MyQuest patient portal. This patient portal is a product of Quest Diagnostics, which is the parent company of Dermpath Diagnostics. While Quest Diagnostics previously offered an Electronic Health Record (EHR) system known as Quanum EHR (formerly Care360), this system was discontinued as of December 31, 2023. However, Quest Diagnostics continues to provide Quanum Lab Services Manager, a portal specifically designed for physicians to manage laboratory orders and access results. Given that Dermpath Diagnostics is a specialized laboratory, their patient portal (MyQuest) is provided by their parent company, Quest Diagnostics. It is likely that their internal systems for managing laboratory data integrate with MyQuest to provide patients with access to their reports and billing information. While Quest Diagnostics no longer offers a general EHR product (Quanum EHR), their laboratory-focused systems and the MyQuest portal are part of the Quest Diagnostics ecosystem.
