About the Practice

Integrated Medical Professionals, PLLC ("IMP") is a multi-specialty physician group operating in the greater New York Metropolitan area. IMP provides diagnostic and therapeutic services, specializing in urology, radiation oncology, and pathology practices. Their vision is to provide patients with the highest level of care available, measured by clinical outcomes and patient satisfaction, enhancing the quality and efficiency of medical care.
