About the Practice

Eye Associates of New York provides comprehensive eye care services in Midtown Manhattan, including eye exams, contact lenses, LASIK evaluations, and management of conditions like dry eyes, computer vision syndrome, and glaucoma. They offer a full spectrum of personalized eye care for the whole family, utilizing state-of-the-art technology and innovative treatments for various vision and eye health ailments, including myopia management and orthokeratology.
