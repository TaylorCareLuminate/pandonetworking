About the Practice

CRA Medical Imaging, also known as Crouse Radiology Associates, is one of the largest diagnostic imaging practices in Central New York. They offer a full range of imaging services including Ultrasound, X-Ray, MRI, Computed Tomography (CT), PET/CT, Nuclear Medicine, 3D Digital Mammography, and Interventional Radiology. [5] The practice is staffed by experienced, board-certified radiologists and specially-trained technical staff dedicated to providing comprehensive diagnostic and interventional imaging services and patient care. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Crouse Radiology Associates (CRA Medical Imaging) utilizes a patient portal branded as the "CRA Medical Imaging Patient Portal," which is powered by ezAccess. The patient portal can be accessed at https://crouse.myezyaccess.com. Based on information from the athenahealth Marketplace and other sources, the ezAccess patient portal is designed to integrate with athenahealth's EHR systems, including athenaPractice, athenaFlow, and athenaOne. One source also explicitly states that their Patient Portal is provided in partnership with EZAccess, their EHR software vendor and provider. This indicates that Crouse Radiology Associates likely uses an athenahealth EHR system.
