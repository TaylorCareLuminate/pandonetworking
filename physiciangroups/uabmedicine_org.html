About the Practice

UAB Hospital is a 1,207-bed tertiary hospital and academic health science center in Birmingham, Alabama, serving as the flagship property of the UAB Health System and the only ACS verified Level I Trauma Center in Alabama. [1] As the primary teaching hospital for the UAB School of Medicine, it provides a complete range of primary and specialty care services, including nationally recognized programs in oncology, diabetes, neurosciences, heart/vascular, critical care, and organ transplant. [4, 7, 12] It is a major center for clinical research, committed to educating medical professionals and advancing medical science while providing life-changing care. [1, 2, 4]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UAB Hospital (uabmedicine.org) currently uses a patient portal branded as myUABMedicine. This portal provides patients with online access to portions of their electronic medical record, allowing them to manage their health and communicate with healthcare providers. While the University of Alabama System, which includes UAB Health System, has approved a transition to the Epic electronic health record platform with full implementation anticipated within approximately two and a half years from September 2024, the current electronic health record system that the myUABMedicine patient portal connects to appears to be Cerner. Information for developers wishing to connect health management apps to the UAB Medicine electronic health record directs them to resources specifically for the Cerner EHR.
