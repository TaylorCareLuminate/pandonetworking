About the Practice

Orthopaedic & Neurosurgery Specialists (ONS) is an advanced multi-specialty orthopedic and neurosurgery practice serving patients with comprehensive care for musculoskeletal conditions and injuries. They offer a full spectrum of services including sports medicine, minimally invasive orthopedic, spine and brain surgery, joint replacement, trauma care, physical and occupational therapy, MRI, and urgent orthopedic care. [1, 9, 11, 12, 4] ONS is staffed by fellowship-trained physicians and has been recognized nationally for its high quality and patient-centered approach, including being named a Top 5 Orthopedic Center of Excellence. [5, 9]

Methodology & Findings

Orthopaedic & Neurosurgery Specialists PLLC (ONS) uses the Modernizing Medicine (ModMed) Electronic Medical Record (EMR) system, which includes a patient portal. The patient portal is referred to as the ModMed Patient Portal, and it can also be accessed conveniently through the APPatient app. The URL for the patient portal login is ons.ema.md.
