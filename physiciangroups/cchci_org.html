About the Practice

Chiricahua Community Health Centers, Inc. (CCHCI) is a mission-driven Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) in southeastern Arizona. Founded in 1996, it has grown to be the largest primary care organization in the region, serving over 33,000 patients annually in Cochise County. CCHCI provides comprehensive primary healthcare services including medical, dental, behavioral health, pharmacy, radiology, nutritional care, pediatrics, and women's health. They serve diverse communities and special populations, including the homeless, low-income, and migrant farmworkers, regardless of ability to pay or insurance status. Services are delivered through fifteen fixed-site clinics and eight mobile medical/dental units across the rural 6200 square mile region. They also utilize telehealth and community health workers for outreach and home services.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Chiricahua Community Health Centers, Inc. (CCHCI) utilizes the InteliChart Patient Portal for its patients. This patient portal is integrated with their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system, which is NextGen. InteliChart's patient engagement platform, known as Healthy Outcomes, is designed to integrate with a variety of EHR systems, including NextGen. The mention of "NextGen templates" in relation to CCHCI's electronic medical records further supports the use of the NextGen EHR system by the organization. While InteliChart integrates with over 40 different EHR products, the evidence points to NextGen being the specific EHR in use at CCHCI that connects with the InteliChart patient portal.
