About the Practice

Invision Health's Brain and Spine Center provides comprehensive medical services focusing on the diagnosis, evaluation, treatment, and management of disorders of the central, peripheral, and autonomic nervous systems, brain, and spine. [1, 8] Their specialties include Neurosurgery, Neurology, and Interventional Pain Management, offering both surgical and non-surgical treatments such as endoscopic, microsurgery, stereotactic, image-guided surgery, and Gamma Knife Radiosurgery. [1, 3, 8] The practice emphasizes a multidisciplinary approach to provide coordinated, patient-centered care aiming to restore function and improve the quality of life for patients with conditions ranging from acute stroke and traumatic brain injury to spinal disorders and tumors. [2, 3, 7]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Invision Health, including Brain & Spine Medical Services PLLC, utilizes the Siemens Invision system as their electronic health record (EHR). The patient portal provided by Invision Health for its patients is referred to and branded simply as the "Patient Portal" on their website. While the specific name Siemens gives to its patient portal solution that integrates with the Invision EHR is not explicitly mentioned in the context of Invision Health through the search results, Siemens does offer an "eHealth Patient Portal" designed to integrate with their healthcare information systems. Based on the consistent mention of Siemens Invision as the EHR and the generic "Patient Portal" branding on Invision Health's site, it is highly probable that the patient portal used is Siemens' own patient portal solution integrated with their Invision EHR system.
