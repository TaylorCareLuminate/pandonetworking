About the Practice

Stony Brook Children's Hospital is Suffolk County's first and only children's hospital, offering advanced pediatric specialty care in over 30 specialties. [4, 7, 9, 10] It provides a full range of comprehensive medical services for infants, children, and young adults, including leading-edge diagnoses, treatment, and management of childhood conditions, illnesses, and injuries. [4, 5] As an academic medical center, it offers access to clinical trials and serves as a regional referral center for complex cases and trauma, being Suffolk County's only Level I Pediatric Trauma Center. [4, 7, 9] The hospital is committed to patient- and family-centered care, utilizing multidisciplinary teams and advanced technology to provide comprehensive, coordinated, and effective care. [5, 6] They offer specialized care units including a Level III NICU, PICU, and a state-designated Burn Center. [9]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Stony Brook Children's Hospital, as part of Stony Brook Medicine, utilizes a patient portal branded as "MyHealtheLife". This patient portal is powered by Cerner Health. The MyHealtheLife portal provides patients with secure online access to their personal health information, allowing them to review medical records and history, message their doctors, request appointments, and pay bills. This indicates that the underlying Electronic Health Record (EHR) vendor for Stony Brook Children's Hospital is Cerner.
