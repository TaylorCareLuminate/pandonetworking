About the Practice

Summit Healthcare is a not-for-profit healthcare organization and a regional medical center with 101 licensed beds located in the White Mountains of Northeastern Arizona, serving over 90,000 residents. [1] Their mission is to deliver exceptional, compassionate, state-of-the-art healthcare close to home by investing in technology and attracting physicians across various specialties. [1, 10] Services include Comprehensive Healthcare, Bone & Joint Care, Family Medicine, Retail Pharmacy, Urgent Care & Walk In, and OB/GYN. [1, 8, 10, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Summit Healthcare Medical Associates (summithealthcare.net) uses the patient portal branded as Follow My Health. Follow My Health, now known as Veradigm FollowMyHealth, is a cloud-based patient engagement platform. It is designed to integrate with various Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems. While Follow My Health is the patient portal vendor, it is strongly associated with Allscripts Healthcare, LLC, which is identified as the manufacturer in documentation and the provider of the platform that integrates with EHR systems. This suggests that Summit Healthcare Medical Associates likely utilizes an EHR system provided by Allscripts or one that seamlessly integrates with the Follow My Health platform from Veradigm/Allscripts.
