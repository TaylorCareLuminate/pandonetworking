About the Practice

Upstate Cerebral Palsy, now known as Upstate Caring Partners, is a premier provider of innovative programs and services for individuals and their families facing physical, developmental, and mental health challenges in Central New York. [5, 4, 9] They offer a comprehensive range of care including residential and day services, early childhood and school-age education, case management, behavioral and clinical services, health services, and vocational support, focusing on empowering people of all abilities to fulfill life choices.
