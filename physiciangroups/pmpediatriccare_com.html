About the Practice

PM Pediatric Care is the nation's largest provider of specialized pediatric urgent care and a recognized leader in pediatric behavioral health, offering comprehensive medical and mental healthcare for children and young adults from birth through college age. They serve as a convenient alternative to the emergency department for non-life-threatening conditions, including injuries and illnesses, and provide services such as urgent care, telemedicine, and behavioral health teletherapy.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

PM Pediatric Care (pmpediatriccare.com) utilizes a patient portal that can be accessed through their website or via the Healow app. The use of the Healow app and a specific practice code (HAFHBD) indicates that their patient portal is provided by Healow. Healow is the patient portal and telehealth solution offered by eClinicalWorks. This strongly suggests that PM Pediatric Care uses eClinicalWorks as their electronic health record (EHR) vendor.
