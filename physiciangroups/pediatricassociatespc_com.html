About the Practice

Pediatric Associates, P.C. is a pediatric practice serving infants, children, and adolescents in the Richmond metro area. [2] They provide comprehensive pediatric care including well checks, sick visits, vaccinations, school and sports physicals, behavioral health, and speech assessments. [2] The practice offers convenient onsite services such as lab work and has been serving the community for over 40 years. [3]
