About the Practice

Desert Kidney Associates, PLC specializes in renal care, including kidney disease, hypertension, and endocrinology. They are dedicated advocates for patients at risk of or facing chronic kidney disease (CKD) and end-stage renal disease (ESRD). The practice provides a wide spectrum of compassionate and comprehensive kidney care services, including diagnosis, treatment, management of CKD and its complications, kidney transplant management, kidney stone management, dialysis (hemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis), and care for related conditions such as hypertension, diabetic neuropathy, and electrolyte disorders. They aim to meet the varied needs of each patient, provide excellence in healthcare, and improve their quality of life by involving them in their healthcare decisions.

Desert Kidney Associates Utilizes FollowMyHealth for Patient Portal Access

Desert Kidney Associates PLC appears to use the FollowMyHealth platform for its patient portal, providing patients with online access to their health information and communication tools. While the Desert Kidney Associates website generally refers to this service simply as the "Patient Portal," indications from search results, including support information and portal access instructions that align with the FollowMyHealth system, strongly suggest its implementation. FollowMyHealth is a patient engagement platform developed by Jardogs, which was acquired by Allscripts. The platform is designed to integrate with a variety of Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems, offering patients a unified point of access to their medical information from different providers. Based on the connection between FollowMyHealth and Allscripts, it is likely that Desert Kidney Associates utilizes an Allscripts EHR system that integrates with the FollowMyHealth patient portal. FollowMyHealth is known for its ability to synchronize with various EHRs, allowing patients to manage appointments, view test results, request prescription refills, and communicate securely with their healthcare providers. Therefore, Desert Kidney Associates PLC uses the FollowMyHealth patient portal, which is commonly associated with and integrates with Allscripts EHR systems.
