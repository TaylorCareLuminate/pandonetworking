About the Practice

Optum Urgent Care, operating within the broader Optum Care network, offers convenient walk-in medical care for non-life-threatening illnesses and injuries when your primary doctor is unavailable or after hours. [2, 4, 6] These centers are staffed by highly trained doctors, nurse practitioners, and physician assistants. [2, 3] They provide care for a range of conditions including colds, flu, minor infections, sprains, minor cuts, and other urgent but not emergency medical needs. [3, 4] Most locations offer extended hours, and no appointment is necessary. [2, 4] Optum Urgent Care is designed to provide timely care and is not a substitute for emergency room services for life-threatening conditions. [2, 4]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Optum Urgent Care PLLC, as part of the larger Optum healthcare network, utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. This aligns with Optum's transition to the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. MyChart serves as the primary online platform for Optum patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare. Through the MyChart portal, patients can schedule and cancel appointments, request prescription refills, view test results, access medical bills, and securely message their healthcare team. Optum explicitly states that MyChart is the patient portal included with their new Epic EHR system. While some legacy patient portals like FollowMyHealth or NextGen PXP may have been used in the past by certain Optum entities, patients are now encouraged to register for MyChart to maintain access to their health information and connect with their providers. In summary, Optum Urgent Care PLLC uses the MyChart patient portal, which is a product of the Epic EHR system.
