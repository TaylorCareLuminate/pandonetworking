About the Practice

New York Cancer & Blood Specialists (NYCBS) is one of the largest community cancer centers in the United States, dedicated to providing world-class, patient-centered, affordable care to patients with cancer and blood disorders in their own communities. They offer a comprehensive and multidisciplinary approach, utilizing advanced imaging and state-of-the-art therapies including Medical Oncology, Radiation Oncology, and Hematology. NYCBS provides a wide range of supportive services such as nutrition, social work, psychology, physical therapy, and chronic care management, and is involved in cutting-edge clinical trials and personalized treatments.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

New York Cancer & Blood Specialists (NYCBS) utilizes the **Carespace Portal** for its patient portal services. This portal is provided by **Persivia**. The underlying Electronic Health Record (EHR) system used by New York Cancer & Blood Specialists is **Flatiron Health's OncoEMR®**. Multiple sources confirm that NYCBS uses OncoEMR® and that the Carespace patient portal is integrated with this system, offering patients seamless access to their health information. Flatiron Health's OncoEMR® is specifically designed for oncology practices. Therefore, the patient portal vendor/branding used by New York Cancer & Blood Specialists is Carespace (provided by Persivia), and this portal is tied to the Flatiron Health OncoEMR® system as their EHR vendor.
