About the Practice

Weill Cornell Imaging at NewYork-Presbyterian is an outpatient radiology diagnostic center providing superior imaging and interpretation services in nine state-of-the-art facilities located throughout Manhattan and Queens. [4, 7] Drawing on the expertise of world-class, often sub-specialized radiologists who are faculty at Weill Cornell Medicine, the center offers a comprehensive range of diagnostic imaging examinations for patients of all ages, including CT, MRI, PET/CT, PET/MR, bone density, ultrasound, x-ray, EOS, fluoroscopy, mammography, and image-guided breast biopsy. [4, 8, 12] They provide personalized, compassionate care focusing on quality, patient safety, and access, utilizing advanced technology for the early detection and identification of diseases and conditions. [7, 8, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Weill Cornell Imaging at NewYork-Presbyterian, in conjunction with Weill Cornell Medicine and NewYork-Presbyterian, utilizes a patient portal branded as "Connect". This Connect patient portal is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR system used by Weill Cornell Medicine, NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital, and Columbia University Irving Medical Center. The transition to the Epic EHR system and the integrated Connect portal for these institutions was completed on February 1, 2020. Through the Connect portal, patients of Weill Cornell Imaging at NewYork-Presbyterian and affiliated institutions can access various services, including viewing test results, scheduling appointments, paying bills, and communicating with their healthcare providers. This is similar to how Epic's patient portal is widely known as MyChart.
