About the Practice

Camp Lowell Medical Specialists, part of Arizona Community Physicians, is a medical group practice offering comprehensive and compassionate care in Tucson. They specialize in Internal Medicine, Family Medicine, and Dermatology for the whole family. Additional specialties covered by physicians at this location include Midwifery, Geriatric Medicine, Gynecology, Obstetrics & Gynecology, and Public Health & General Preventive Medicine. They provide a patient portal for convenient access to medical records and communication.
