About the Practice

Starling Physicians is a large, physician-owned multispecialty group in Connecticut offering comprehensive and coordinated care across more than two dozen specialties, including primary care, internal medicine, family medicine, pediatrics, and various medical and surgical specialties. [6, 7, 9, 14, 15] With numerous convenient locations, they provide integrated healthcare services ranging from preventive care and treatment coordination to advanced specialty care and on-site services like laboratory testing, physical therapy, and radiology, focusing on the totality of a patient's health throughout their lifetime. [6, 7, 9]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Based on the search results, Starling Physicians appears to be using the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Several sources indicate Starling Physicians selected Epic EHR in 2021 and mention "Patient Epic Updates" [see previous search results 1, 4]. Starling Physicians is also listed in the Epic UserWeb [see previous search result 10]. While the Starling Physicians patient portal page refers to it as "Our innovative patient portal", other results explicitly connect Starling Physicians with the MyChart patient portal. MyChart is the standard patient portal offered by Epic. Therefore, the patient portal vendor/branding used by Starling Physicians is MyChart, which is tied to the Epic EHR system.
