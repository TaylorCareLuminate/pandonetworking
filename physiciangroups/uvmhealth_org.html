About the Practice

Elizabethtown Community Hospital, part of the UVM Health Network, is a non-profit, 25-bed critical access hospital serving Essex County, New York. [4, 6, 9, 10] It provides a range of services including inpatient care (acute and sub-acute), a 24-hour emergency department, access to specialists in areas like Cardiology, gynecology, gastroenterology, oncology, orthopedics, pediatrics, and pulmonary medicine, therapy services, on-site laboratory and pharmacy, all-digital radiology, chemotherapy/infusion treatment, and a cardiac rehabilitation program. [4, 6, 12] The hospital operates two campuses and six community-based primary care health centers, acting as a rural acute care manager and coordinating patient transfers when necessary. [6, 9, 14, 15]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Elizabethtown Community Hospital (uvmhealth.org) uses the **MyChart** patient portal. MyChart is the patient portal specifically associated with the **Epic** electronic health record (EHR) system. The UVM Health Network, of which Elizabethtown Community Hospital is a part, implemented the Epic EHR system across its network. Elizabethtown Community Hospital transitioned to the MyChart portal on April 2, 2022, as part of this network-wide transition to Epic.
