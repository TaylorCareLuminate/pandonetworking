About the Practice

Carewell Medical Associates, PC is dedicated to providing high-quality compassionate primary care. Their services include immunizations, preventive care, chronic care management, lab services such as EKG, allergy testing, and neuro testing, routine checkups including annual physicals and health maintenance testing, and lifestyle counseling for weight loss, smoking, and diet. The practice specializes in primary care, Internal Medicine, Family Practice, and Peripheral Vascular Disease.
