About the Practice

4C Medical Group is a physician-led integrated healthcare delivery network providing primary and specialty care in acute, post-acute, ambulatory, and home-based settings across the Phoenix Metro area. [4, 8, 9] The group focuses on integrated and coordinated care with a patient-centric approach, aiming to improve patient outcomes and experience. [2, 10] They specialize in providing primary care, including care for the Medicare eligible population, across multiple clinic locations, hospitals, skilled nursing facilities, and assisted living facilities. [6, 9, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

4C Medical Group (4cmedicalgroup.com) utilizes Elation Health's Electronic Health Records (EHR) system. The patient portal used by 4C Medical Group is integrated within the Elation Health platform, as it includes patient engagement tools as part of its "Clinical Engagement Platform." While a specific, separate brand name for the patient portal akin to "MyChart" for Epic is not explicitly mentioned in the search results, the portal functionality is provided through their Elation Health EHR system.
