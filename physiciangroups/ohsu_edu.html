About the Practice

Oregon Health & Science University (OHSU) is Oregon's only academic health center and a public research university focusing primarily on health sciences. It provides comprehensive patient care through its hospitals, including OHSU Hospital, a Level I trauma center and general hospital, and Doernbecher Children's Hospital, specializing in pediatric medicine and care for children with long-term illness. OHSU offers a wide range of primary and specialty care services, such as cancer care (Knight Cancer Institute), brain care (OHSU Brain Institute), heart care (Knight Cardiovascular Center), bone and joint care, emergency care, dentistry, nursing, and public health. As a research and education institution, OHSU also trains health care professionals and conducts groundbreaking health research.
