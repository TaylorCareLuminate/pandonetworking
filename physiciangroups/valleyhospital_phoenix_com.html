About the Practice

Valley Hospital is a private psychiatric hospital located in Phoenix, Arizona, specializing in mental health and chemical dependency care. It offers a wide range of inpatient and outpatient services and programs, including crisis stabilization, detox, adult mental health, rehabilitation, partial hospitalization (PHP), and intensive outpatient (IOP). The hospital also provides specialized programs tailored for military/veterans and women, utilizing evidence-based treatment methods to provide supportive and compassionate care.
