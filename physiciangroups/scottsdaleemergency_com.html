About the Practice

Scottsdale Emergency Associates (SEA) is a Multi-Specialty Group specializing in Emergency Medicine, providing emergency medical services and emergency department leadership. They are made up of residency-trained, board-certified emergency physicians committed to providing compassionate, high-quality emergency medical care, including treatment for traumatic injuries.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Scottsdale Emergency Associates, which partners with HonorHealth, appears to utilize the HonorHealth patient portal known as MyChart. MyChart is a patient portal system provided by Epic Systems Corporation. Search results indicate that Scottsdale Emergency Associates staffs emergency departments within the Scottsdale HonorHealth system and is partnered with Honor Health. HonorHealth explicitly brands its patient portal as "HonorHealth MyChart". Information available about the HonorHealth Patient Portal identifies the software used as Epic MyChart. Additionally, a job posting for Physician Assistants at HonorHealth Scottsdale sites mentions the use of "EPIC EMR". MyChart allows patients to access their medical records, view test results, schedule appointments, request prescription refills, and communicate with their healthcare providers. This aligns with the typical functionalities of a patient portal integrated with an Epic EHR system.
