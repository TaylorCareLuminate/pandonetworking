About the Practice

Thunderbird Obstetrics and Gynecology, part of Genesis OBGYN, is a medical group practice specializing in Obstetrics & Gynecology in Glendale, AZ. [14] They strive to deliver compassionate, competent care, offering complete obstetrical and gynecologic services including in-office ultrasounds, procedures, and laboratory services. [2] Genesis OBGYN provides comprehensive women's healthcare across Arizona, covering family planning, pregnancy care (including high-risk pregnancies), gynecological health and surgery, menopause management, and various screenings and treatments. [4, 6, 8, 10]
