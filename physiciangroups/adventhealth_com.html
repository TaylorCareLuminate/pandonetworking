About the Practice

AdventHealth is a faith-based healthcare system providing whole-person care across a network of over 50 hospitals and more than 2,000 care locations in nine states. They offer a wide range of services including primary care, emergency and urgent care, and numerous medical specialties such as digestive care, heart and vascular care, orthopedic care, oncology, neuroscience, and women's health. Their approach focuses on healing the whole person, body, mind, and spirit, utilizing advanced technology and nationally recognized programs.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

AdventHealth uses the AdventHealth patient portal. For visits on or after January 28, 2024, patients should use the AdventHealth patient portal. Prior to this date, the MyCommonSpirit – Mountain portal was used, which was the patient portal before joining AdventHealth. Several sources indicate that AdventHealth has transitioned or is transitioning its patient portal technology to Epic. One source explicitly states that AdventHealth needed to convert their existing patient portal across web, iOS, and Android to Epic, the largest provider of Electronic Health Records (EHR) in the U.S. Another source mentions that AdventHealth uses the MyChart system, which is associated with Epic, for accessing medical records, requesting refills, and messaging healthcare teams. Additionally, a case study notes a migration of their existing patient portal technology stack to Epic from 2020 to 2021 to enhance patient care and engagement. Therefore, AdventHealth uses the AdventHealth patient portal, which is powered by the Epic EHR system. The patient portal allows patients to schedule visits, complete paperwork, view lab results, access health records (including through MyChart), and communicate with their care team.
