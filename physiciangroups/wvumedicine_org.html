About the Practice

WVU Medicine, operating under the West Virginia University Health System, is a non-profit health enterprise affiliated with West Virginia University. It stands as West Virginia's largest health system and private employer, encompassing 25 hospitals and providing healthcare services across West Virginia, Maryland, Ohio, and Pennsylvania. [1, 2, 6] The system offers a wide array of medical and surgical specialties, including but not limited to Primary Care, Pediatrics, Urgent Care, Emergency Medicine, Orthopedics, Neurology, Occupational Medicine, Psychology, Telemedicine, and Podiatry, delivered through its network of hospitals and outpatient clinics, alongside specialized care offered through institutes like the WVU Cancer Institute, WVU Critical Care and Trauma Institute, WVU Eye Institute, WVU Heart and Vascular Institute, and the WVU Rockefeller Neuroscience Institute. [6, 7]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

WVU Medicine utilizes Epic's electronic health record (EHR) system, and its patient portal is branded as MyWVUChart. This portal is Epic's patient-facing application, providing patients with access to their health information. MyWVUChart allows patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare, including scheduling appointments, accessing test results, messaging their care team, requesting prescription renewals, viewing and downloading medical records, and paying bills. The implementation of Epic and the MyWVUChart portal unifies patient records across the WVU Medicine health system, aiming to improve care coordination and patient access to information. Thomas Hospitals, part of WVU Medicine, transitioned to the Epic EHR system and the MyWVUChart portal in May 2024, replacing their previous system. Other WVU Medicine facilities, such as Princeton Community Hospital and United Hospital Center, also use MyWVUChart as their patient portal connected to the Epic system.
