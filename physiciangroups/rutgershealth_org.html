About the Practice

Rutgers Health is New Jersey's largest and most comprehensive academic health system, combining biomedical education, research, and health care to advance health for all in New Jersey and beyond. As an education, research, and health care powerhouse, it provides compassionate care in over 30 specialties, focusing on comprehensive managed care, primary, preventive, and acute care services. Rutgers Health works in affiliation with RWJBarnabas Health.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Rutgers Health primarily utilizes the MyChart patient portal, which is associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Rutgers Health, in conjunction with RWJBarnabas Health, has been implementing the Epic EHR through an initiative called "Epic Together," with the final phases expected to be completed in September 2024. This integrated system includes the MyChart consumer-facing digital tools. While MyChart appears to be the main patient portal for Rutgers Health, particularly for accessing medical records at University Hospital and RWJBarnabas Health locations, it's worth noting that Rutgers Student Health uses a separate online patient portal. Additionally, a specific clinic, the H.O.P.E. Clinic, was transitioning to the athenahealth EHR system. Historically, other EHR systems like GE Centricity EMR and Allscripts Touchworks EHR have also been mentioned in relation to parts of the Rutgers Health network. However, the dominant system being implemented across Rutgers Health in partnership with RWJBarnabas Health is Epic, with MyChart serving as its patient portal.
