About the Practice

Bertrand Chaffee Hospital is a non-profit, community-governed organization providing high quality primary care, outpatient services, emergency, and acute care for rural communities. [1] It is a 24-bed general, short-term, acute care facility that also operates the attached Jennie B. Richmond Nursing Home. [1] Services and specialties include primary care, heart center, rheumatology, surgical services (general, ambulatory, minimally invasive, orthopaedic), imaging (CT, Ultrasound, X-Ray, Bone Density, MRI, Nuclear Medicine, 3D Mammography), lab, physical therapy, and patient and family services. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Bertrand Chaffee Hospital utilizes YourCareCommunity as its online patient portal for accessing inpatient records, lab results, and emergency department care records. They also offer YourCareEverywhere as a related online resource for community members to access health and wellness information. YourCareCommunity has been described as a new electronic medical records system implemented by the hospital. The patient portal platform, YourCareEverywhere, appears to be associated with MEDHOST Cloud Services, Inc. Based on this association and the description of YourCareCommunity as an electronic medical records system, it can be concluded that Bertrand Chaffee Hospital is using an EHR system provided by MEDHOST, branded with the patient portal names YourCareCommunity and YourCareEverywhere.
