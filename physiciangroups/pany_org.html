About the Practice

The Psychoanalytic Association of New York (PANY), formerly the Institute for Psychoanalytic Education (IPE), is an education and treatment center affiliated with NYU Langone Health. [1, 3, 4, 7] Its mission is to promote mental health through advanced clinical training, treatment, and scholarship. [5, 7, 11] PANY provides training and therapy based on psychoanalytic understanding for a wide range of emotional problems and life issues for adults, adolescents, and children. [3, 5, 8, 9] They offer affordable psychoanalysis and psychotherapy and train mental health professionals from various disciplines. [1, 3, 4, 5, 9, 11]

## Psychoanalytic Association of New York Likely Uses NYU Langone Health's MyChart Patient Portal The Institute for Psychoanalytic Education, now known as the Psychoanalytic Association of New York (PANY), is affiliated with NYU Grossman School of Medicine and NYU Langone Health. NYU Langone Health utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is a product of Epic Systems Corporation. While direct confirmation on the PANY website or in the search results explicitly stating that PANY patients use MyChart for their psychoanalytic services was not found, the strong affiliation with NYU Langone Health suggests that patients receiving clinical treatment through PANY would access their health information and manage aspects of their care via the NYU Langone Health MyChart portal. This is the standard patient portal used across the NYU Langone Health system. MyChart, developed by Epic, is a widely used patient portal that allows individuals to access their medical records, schedule appointments, request prescription refills, and communicate with their healthcare providers. It is important to note that PANY also has a member login area on its website, but this appears to be for members of the association rather than for patients accessing clinical services. The information available points to NYU Langone Health's MyChart as the probable patient portal for those receiving care through PANY due to the organizational affiliation and integrated systems within larger health networks.
