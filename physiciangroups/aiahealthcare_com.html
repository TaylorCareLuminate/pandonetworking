About the Practice

Associated Internists, operating as AIA Healthcare, specializes in Internal Medicine, providing comprehensive primary care. They are equipped to manage a wide range of health concerns, from common ailments to severe chronic illnesses and complex conditions. Their services emphasize wellness, disease prevention, and health promotion. The practice also includes hospitalists affiliated with Mercy Gilbert Medical Center and Chandler Regional Medical Center and provides care for patients in skilled nursing facilities in the east valley.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Associated Internists (aiahealthcare.com) utilizes the **FollowMyHealth** patient portal. According to the search results, FollowMyHealth is a patient engagement platform that is now known as Veradigm FollowMyHealth and is associated with Allscripts. The FollowMyHealth portal is designed to integrate with various Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems. While the search results indicate that Associated Internists uses FollowMyHealth, they do not explicitly state the specific EHR system they use in conjunction with it. However, given the association of FollowMyHealth with Veradigm and Allscripts, it is probable that Associated Internists uses an EHR system from one of these vendors.
