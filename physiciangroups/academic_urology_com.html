About the Practice

Academic Urology & Urogynecology of Arizona is a premier urology practice offering compassionate, state-of-the-art care for men and women throughout Arizona. They specialize in the diagnosis, staging, and treatment of a wide range of urologic conditions affecting the prostate, bladder, and kidneys, including complex problems like prostate cancer, enlarged prostate, urinary incontinence, vaginal prolapse, kidney stones, and kidney cancer. [1, 2, 7, 9, 11, 12, 13] Their services encompass medical and surgical treatments, with expertise in minimally invasive techniques such as robotic surgery, laparoscopic surgery, and microsurgery, aiming to improve and restore patient quality of life with shorter recovery periods. [1, 7, 9, 12, 13]
