About the Practice

Oneida Health is an integrated health network serving Madison and western Oneida counties, New York, providing a full spectrum of healthcare services. [3, 13, 20] Founded in 1899, it includes a 101-bed acute care hospital, a 160-bed extended-care and short-term rehab facility, primary care offices, and numerous specialty care practices. [5, 9, 20] Specialties and services offered encompass emergency care, surgical services, cancer care (medical and radiation oncology), cardiology, imaging, laboratory services, orthopedics, neurology, ENT, podiatry, vascular care, sleep studies, OB/GYN, wound care, urology, pediatrics, and outpatient therapy. [2, 3, 4, 7, 14, 17, 20] Oneida Health is dedicated to delivering high-quality, comprehensive, and personalized care conveniently located for the community. [5, 13, 17]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Oneida Health utilizes a patient portal that is branded and accessible via the "MEDITECH MHealth" application. This indicates that MEDITECH is the vendor for their patient portal. While the search results distinguish between an "Office Patient Portal" and a "Hospital Patient Portal," the MEDITECH MHealth app is specifically mentioned in relation to the Hospital Patient Portal. Multiple sources strongly suggest that Oneida Health uses MEDITECH as their Electronic Health Record (EHR) vendor. The President and CEO of Oneida Health, Felissa Koernig, was recognized on a list of Community Hospital CEOs to Know by Becker's Healthcare, a list that spotlights MEDITECH customers. Additionally, there is a reference to experience using "Meditech BAR," which is a component of the MEDITECH Expanse EHR system. Based on the association of the patient portal with MEDITECH and the recognition of Oneida Health's CEO as a MEDITECH customer, the available information strongly suggests that Oneida Health uses MEDITECH as their EHR system, particularly for their hospital services.
