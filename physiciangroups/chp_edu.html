About the Practice

UPMC Children's Hospital of Pittsburgh is the only hospital in Greater Pittsburgh dedicated solely to the care of infants, children, teens, and young adults, affiliated with the University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine. It is a state-verified level 1 pediatric trauma center and provides comprehensive primary, specialty, and urgent care across numerous locations. Nationally ranked in multiple specialties, the hospital is a leader in treating childhood conditions, developing new therapies, and educating future pediatric specialists, offering a wide range of services including critical care medicine, oncology, allergy and immunology, and more.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UPMC Children's Hospital of Pittsburgh (chp.edu) uses a patient portal called MyCHP. MyCHP is a free, secure online portal that allows parents and guardians to access and manage their child's health care information. It provides features such as sending secure messages to the care team, viewing upcoming appointments, requesting appointments, attending video visits, viewing health history, and seeing lab and imaging test results. UPMC Children's Hospital of Pittsburgh utilizes the **Cerner** electronic medical record system. This is distinct from the Epic system used by other UPMC adult services and Children's Community Pediatrics practices. MyCHP is specifically for care received at UPMC Children's Hospital and its neighborhood locations.
