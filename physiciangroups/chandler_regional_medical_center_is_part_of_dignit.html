About the Practice

Chandler Regional Medical Center is a full-service, acute-care, not-for-profit hospital providing comprehensive care to the East Valley of Phoenix. The hospital offers a wide range of specialties including a Level I Trauma Center, open heart surgery program, neurosurgery, orthopedics, high-risk obstetrics and newborn services, advanced diagnostic, surgical, robotics, intensive care services, emergency services, family birth center, gastroenterology, cardiovascular services, neurology, and rehabilitation services.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Chandler Regional Medical Center, which is part of Dignity Health and a member of CommonSpirit Health, utilizes a patient portal branded as "My Portal" or "My Portal by Dignity Health". This patient portal provides patients with secure online access to portions of their electronic health information. Dignity Health has contracted with Cerner Corporation to provide patients access to certain electronic health information through the My Portal platform. This indicates that the underlying Electronic Health Record (EHR) system used by Dignity Health facilities, including Chandler Regional Medical Center, is Cerner. The My Portal allows patients to view summaries of their electronic health record, lab results, medications, immunizations, allergies, and other health information. It also offers features such as secure messaging with healthcare teams and the ability to view upcoming appointments in some locations.
