About the Practice

The Feinstein Institutes for Medical Research is the research arm of Northwell Health, dedicated to producing knowledge to cure disease and translate discoveries to patient care. [2, 3, 5, 7] With 50 research labs and 5,000 researchers and staff, the institutes conduct groundbreaking research across a range of medical fields including molecular medicine, genetics, cancer, neuroscience, behavioral science, bioelectronic medicine, inflammation, autoimmune disorders, Parkinson's disease, Alzheimer's disease, schizophrenia, and health system science. [2, 3, 4, 6, 7] They are home to six leading institutes focusing on behavioral science, bioelectronic medicine, cancer, health system science, molecular medicine, and translational research, aiming to improve lives through medical innovation and education. [3, 4, 6]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Feinstein Institutes for Medical Research, as part of Northwell Health, currently utilizes FollowMyHealth as its patient portal. This portal allows patients to manage their personal health records online, review test results, medications, and communicate with their physicians. Northwell Health is in the process of implementing Epic as its new electronic health record (EHR) system, with the rollout beginning in 2025. This transition aims to unify patient records across all of Northwell Health's facilities. While FollowMyHealth is the current patient portal, Epic's integrated patient portal is typically branded as MyChart. The implementation of Epic suggests a future shift towards using MyChart as the primary patient portal for Northwell Health and its Крайних, including the Feinstein Institutes for Medical Research.
