About the Practice

Tulane Medical Center, accessed via tulanehealthcare.com, is an acclaimed teaching, research, and medical facility located in downtown New Orleans and serving the greater New Orleans area. It serves as the primary teaching hospital for the Tulane University School of Medicine and encompasses centers and clinics covering nearly all major specialties of medicine, providing a range of care from primary to advanced specialty services. [3, 5, 18] It is currently a partnership between LCMC Health and Tulane University. [3, 18] Specialty areas include emergency medicine, cancer care, transplant services, women's health, pediatrics, sports medicine, and comprehensive diabetes care, among many others. [3, 5, 15, 18]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Tulane Medical Center, as part of the LCMC Health system, is transitioning to and primarily utilizes the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Patients of Tulane Medical Center can access their health information through the LCMC Health Patient Portal, which is branded as MyChart. While the "Tulane Doctors Patient Portal" and "MyHealthONE Patient Portal" are also mentioned as access points, the LCMC Health Patient Portal, powered by Epic's MyChart, appears to be the go-forward platform for patients of LCMC Health facilities, including Tulane Medical Center. MyHealthONE is noted in searches as being associated with HCA Healthcare, which is implementing the MEDITECH Expanse EHR system. The explicit mention of partnering with LCMC Health to transition to Epic and offering MyChart as the enhanced patient portal strongly indicates Epic as the main EHR in use or being implemented at Tulane Medical Center within the LCMC Health network.
