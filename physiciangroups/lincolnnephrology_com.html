About the Practice

Lincoln Nephrology & Hypertension is a medical group practice specializing in the diagnosis and treatment of kidney-related disorders and hypertension. [2, 3, 11] They offer a range of services including Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Management, In-Center Hemodialysis, Home Therapies, Vascular Access Management, Post-Kidney Transplant Care, Chronic Immunosuppression, 24 hour Ambulatory Blood Pressure Monitoring, Anemia Management, Electrolyte Disturbances, Nephrolithiasis, Polycystic Kidney Disease, Acute Renal Failure, Clinical Research, Nutrition/Dietitian Services, Glomerular Disease, and Palliative Nephrology. [3] They also manage an inpatient Hemodialysis center and provide collaborative medical consultation and thorough evaluation. [10, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Lincoln Nephrology & Hypertension provides patients with access to their electronic health records and communications through the MyChart patient portal. Patients are instructed to set up a MyChart account specifically for Davita Physician Solutions to receive their records from the clinic. MyChart is the patient portal developed by Epic Systems, a widely used electronic health record (EHR) vendor in the healthcare industry. Davita Physician Solutions, which powers the MyChart portal for Lincoln Nephrology & Hypertension, has partnered with Epic to utilize "CKD EHR by Epic." This platform integrates Epic's technology with Davita's specialized focus on chronic kidney disease (CKD) care. Therefore, Lincoln Nephrology & Hypertension uses the MyChart patient portal, which is a product of the Epic EHR system, facilitated through their affiliation with Davita Physician Solutions.
