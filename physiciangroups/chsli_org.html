About the Practice

St. Francis Hospital & Heart Center, part of the Catholic Health system, is a 449-bed non-profit teaching hospital in Roslyn, NY, and is New York State's only specialty-designated cardiac center. [3, 7, 12] It is a nationally recognized leader in diagnosing, treating, and preventing cardiac disease and offers outstanding programs in other areas including orthopedics, neurology, gastroenterology, cancer, and pulmonary. [3, 4, 9, 11, 12]
