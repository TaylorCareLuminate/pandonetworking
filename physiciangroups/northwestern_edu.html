About the Practice

Northwestern University is a private research university with its main campuses in Evanston and Chicago, Illinois, and an international campus in Doha, Qatar. It is a comprehensive, deeply interdisciplinary institution known for top-tier research, new knowledge creation, creative expression, and practical application. The university offers a rigorous yet empathetic academic environment, emphasizing intellectual curiosity and critical thinking across various fields including communication, journalism, engineering, computer science, mathematics, statistics, biological sciences, physics, chemistry, law, medicine, management, education, and music. Northwestern prepares students to become leaders in their respective fields through a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Northwestern University, through its Northwestern Medicine system, utilizes the MyNM patient portal, which is powered by MyChart. MyChart is the patient portal system commonly associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Northwestern Medicine has been identified as using Epic as their EHR system. The MyNM portal is described as a secure way for patients to manage their healthcare, providing access to medical records, communication with care teams, appointment scheduling, prescription refills, and bill payment. It is explicitly stated that MyNM is powered by MyChart. While Northwestern University Student Health Service also mentions a "NU Personal Health Portal (PnC)" for students to submit immunization records, the main patient portal for the broader Northwestern Medicine health system is MyNM. Therefore, the patient portal vendor/branding used by Northwestern University (specifically Northwestern Medicine) is MyNM, powered by Epic's MyChart.
