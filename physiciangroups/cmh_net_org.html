About the Practice

Columbia Memorial Health, an affiliate of the Albany Med Health System, is a multi-campus health care system serving over 100,000 residents in Columbia, Greene, and Dutchess counties in northeastern New York. It includes a 190-bed acute care hospital and numerous outlying primary and specialty care centers, focusing on primary care, health education, and advanced surgery. The system provides comprehensive care across a wide range of specialties including Bone & Joint (Orthopedics), Cardiology, Colorectal Surgery, Gastroenterology, Nephrology, Pain Management, Psychiatric Care, Rehabilitation, Rheumatology, Sports Medicine, Primary Stroke Care, Surgery, Urogynecology, and Urology.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Columbia Memorial Hospital (cmh-net.org) is transitioning to the MyChart patient portal, which is associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Previously, Columbia Memorial Hospital utilized an online patient portal called myCMH. Support information for myCMH directed users to contact Cerner technical support, suggesting that myCMH was likely associated with a Cerner EHR system. However, as part of the Albany Med Health System, Columbia Memorial Health is adopting a single, unified electronic medical record system and has introduced MyChart as its new patient portal. As of November 2, 2024, access to the previous portal (Healow, used for myCMH) is no longer available, and patients are directed to create a new account in MyChart for seamless access to their health information. MyChart is a patient portal product licensed from Epic Systems Corporation.
