About the Practice

SOMOS Innovation is a preeminent value-based provider organization and physician enablement platform in the New York City market. [1, 5] It focuses on providing culturally competent care to underserved communities, particularly Medicaid and Medicare beneficiaries, in 4 boroughs of New York City and Nassau County. [1, 14] SOMOS empowers independent community physicians by relieving administrative burdens and providing resources to excel in value-based care. [1, 3, 5, 6] Their care includes a focus on preventive care, health access, health equity, and social care, aiming to improve patient health outcomes, enhance quality performance, and improve financial results for practices. [3, 4, 6]

Methodology & Findings

## SOMOS Innovation Likely Leverages The Garage's "Bridge" Platform for Patient Engagement SOMOS Innovation appears to utilize "Bridge," a population health management platform developed by The Garage, for patient engagement and connectivity. While a "SOMOS Portal" exists, search results indicate it is primarily a provider-focused portal, with a copyright held by Evolent Health, a partner that provides various administrative and technology services to SOMOS Innovation. Multiple sources highlight SOMOS Innovation's collaboration with The Garage and the use of the Bridge platform to integrate providers and patients and facilitate population health management initiatives. The Bridge platform is specifically described as encompassing patient engagement functionalities, aiming to connect providers and patients and support a more integrated care experience. Although the term "patient portal" with a distinct brand name (like Epic's MyChart) is not explicitly used in relation to SOMOS Innovation in the search results, the evidence suggests that the patient-facing component is likely provided through The Garage's Bridge platform. This platform is designed to aggregate data from various sources, including EHRs, to enable comprehensive care management and patient interaction. Evolent Health is another key partner for SOMOS Innovation, providing services such as a provider portal. While Evolent Health plays a significant role in supporting SOMOS's operations, the patient engagement platform is more strongly linked to The Garage's Bridge based on the available information. Therefore, it is probable that the patient portal experience for individuals served by SOMOS Innovation and its affiliated providers is facilitated through The Garage's Bridge platform. The branding presented to the patient may be customized or co-branded with SOMOS Innovation, although a specific patient-facing brand name was not identified in the search results. The underlying EHR systems used by individual SOMOS-affiliated practices may vary, as The Garage's Bridge platform is designed to be EHR agnostic and integrate data from different sources.
