About the Practice

North American Partners in Anesthesia (NAPA) is a leading single-specialty anesthesia and perioperative management company that provides anesthesia, pain management, and perioperative care services. [4, 8, 10, 12, 13] As a clinician-led organization, NAPA serves hospitals, ambulatory surgery centers, and physician offices, focusing on delivering high-quality patient care, enhancing operating room performance, and providing comprehensive management services including billing and administrative support. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

North American Partners in Anesthesia (NAPA) utilizes a few different systems to manage its operations and patient interactions, but it does not appear to have a single, branded patient portal for comprehensive clinical record access across all the facilities it serves, similar to how Epic has MyChart. For their Anesthesia Information Management System (AIMS), which is used for clinical documentation and data capture by clinicians, NAPA uses iProcedures. In terms of revenue cycle management (RCM), which handles billing and administrative tasks, NAPA leverages athenaIDX. NAPA does provide an online option for patients to pay their bills through a partnership with Cedar, utilizing the Cedar Pay platform. However, access to clinical patient records for individuals who have received anesthesia services from NAPA is likely provided through the patient portal systems of the individual hospitals and healthcare facilities where the services were rendered. This is because NAPA partners with numerous facilities across several states, and these facilities often utilize various disparate EHR and practice management systems. While a partnership with one medical center mentioned "My Hospital Chart" as a patient portal option, this appears to be specific to that facility rather than a universal NAPA patient portal. Therefore, while NAPA uses specific vendors for anesthesia information management (iProcedures), revenue cycle management (athenaIDX), and online bill payment (Cedar Pay), there is no indication of a unified, NAPA-branded patient portal for accessing clinical information across all their service locations. Patients seeking their clinical records would typically need to access the patient portal provided by the hospital or healthcare facility where they received care.
