About the Practice

MemorialCare is a nonprofit, integrated health system serving patients across Orange and Los Angeles Counties. [3, 11] The system includes four hospitals, two medical groups (MemorialCare Medical Group and MemorialCare Independent Physicians), and a wide network of outpatient facilities such as imaging centers, surgical centers, urgent care centers, and more. [3, 11, 12] MemorialCare provides a broad spectrum of care, from primary care services including family medicine, internal medicine, pediatrics, and urgent care, to various medical and surgical specialties like oncology, cardiology, pulmonology, gastroenterology, and women's care. [9, 10, 12] They are dedicated to providing high-quality, compassionate, and accessible healthcare for a range of needs, from routine check-ups to complex surgical procedures. [3, 12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

MemorialCare utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is powered by Epic's MyChart electronic medical record system. This indicates that MemorialCare's primary EHR vendor for their patient portal and associated electronic medical records is Epic. The myMemorialCare myChart portal provides patients with online access to various health management tools, including scheduling appointments, requesting prescription refills, reviewing health history, accessing test results, and communicating with their doctors. While MemorialCare also has affiliated physicians who may use other systems like NextGen, the main patient portal branded as myMemorialCare and referred to as MyChart is tied to the Epic EHR system. The integration of MyChart with MemorialCare's EMR system is also noted in their telehealth services.
