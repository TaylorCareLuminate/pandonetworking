About the Practice

Associated Retina Consultants is a team of experienced Arizona eye specialists dedicated to the preservation and restoration of vision, focusing on the diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the retina, macula, and vitreous humor, including macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, and retinal detachment, offering personalized care, breakthrough treatments, and clinical trials. Methodology & Findings

Associated Retina Consultants (associatedretinaconsultants.com) utilizes a patient portal branded as myezyaccess.com. While the patient portal vendor is ezAccess, the specific Electronic Health Record (EHR) system that Associated Retina Consultants uses in conjunction with this portal is not explicitly stated in the search results. However, information from other healthcare providers indicates that the ezAccess patient portal is known to integrate with athenahealth EHR products, including athenaPractice, athenaFlow, and athenaClinicals. One medical center also mentioned previously using myEzyAccess and AthenaHealth before transitioning to a different system. Therefore, while Associated Retina Consultants uses the myezyaccess.com patient portal, it is likely that they are using an athenahealth EHR system that integrates with this portal, although this is not definitively confirmed by the provided search results.
