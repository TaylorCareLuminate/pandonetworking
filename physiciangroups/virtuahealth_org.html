About the Practice

Virtua Nephrology is a specialized practice offering comprehensive care and treatment of various types of kidney diseases, individualized hypertension treatment, dialysis management, and post-kidney transplant care. The practice emphasizes a team approach and guides patients through a multi-disciplinary integrative process of medical care, including evaluation and treatment of chronic kidney disease through all stages, kidney stones, reflux nephropathy, medullary sponge kidney, edema, and recurrent urinary tract infection.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Virtua Nephrology, as part of the Virtua Health system, utilizes MyChart as their patient portal. MyChart is a widely used online patient portal that allows individuals to manage various aspects of their healthcare, including scheduling appointments, requesting prescription refills, viewing test results, and communicating with their healthcare providers. Virtua Health implemented the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system in phases, starting in 2017 and going live in acute care settings in 2018. MyChart is the patient portal system that is integrated with and commonly used by healthcare organizations that utilize the Epic EHR. This connection is further supported by a search result mentioning Virtua's participation in an "Epic Community Connect Program," which allows affiliated community healthcare providers to use Virtua's Epic EMR system and offer MyChart. Therefore, the patient portal vendor and branding used by Virtua Nephrology is MyChart, which is tied to the Epic EHR system.
