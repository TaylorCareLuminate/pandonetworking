About the Practice

Yuma Regional Medical Center (YRMC) is a 406-bed non-profit hospital and teaching facility located in Yuma, Arizona, serving the residents and visitors of the surrounding area as the primary medical center. [4], [8] It offers a wide range of advanced medical care and services, including specialties such as Cancer Care, Heart Care, Orthopedics, Primary Care, Pediatric Care, Emergency Care, Behavioral Health, Bariatric Surgery, Cardiology, and Neurology. [2], [9], [15] YRMC is dedicated to providing high-quality, compassionate care with a patient- and family-centered approach. [8] The healthcare system has evolved to encompass 39 ambulatory locations to serve the community's needs. [7]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Based on the information gathered, Yuma Regional Medical Center (yumaregional.org) uses Epic Systems Corporation for its electronic health record (EHR) system. The patient portal vendor/branding used by Yuma Regional Medical Center is referred to as "MyCare". Epic is a widely used EHR vendor, and their patient portal is commonly known as MyChart. While MyCare is the specific branding used by Yuma Regional Medical Center for their patient portal, it is associated with their Epic EHR system.
