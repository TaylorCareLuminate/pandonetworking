About the Practice

Abrazo Health is a healthcare system serving the greater Phoenix area with a network of hospitals, emergency centers, and physician offices. They offer a wide range of services including cardiovascular, stroke and neurosciences, orthopedics, spine, minimally invasive surgery, maternity services, primary care, and specialty care. The system is part of the Arizona Care Network, focusing on patient-centered care.; Dignity Health is a healthcare provider that is part of CommonSpirit Health. They offer a wide range of services across numerous hospitals, ambulatory care sites, and physician clinics. Services include cardiovascular care, orthopedics, emergency medicine, and various other medical services, with a focus on patient-centered care.; One Medical is a membership-based primary care practice aiming to make healthcare more affordable, accessible, and enjoyable through human-centered design, technology, and an exceptional team. They offer comprehensive primary care for adults and children, including same-day appointments, 24/7 virtual care, on-site labs, preventive care, chronic care management, common illness treatment, and mental health services.; Banner Health is a nonprofit health system operating in multiple states. They provide comprehensive care across a wide range of services including primary care, specialty care, urgent care, hospital services, imaging, and various other medical services, with a mission to make healthcare easier.; HonorHealth is one of Arizona's largest nonprofit healthcare systems serving the greater Phoenix area and Florence. They provide a wide range of medical services including acute care, primary care, specialty care (cardiovascular, orthopedics, neurology, gastroenterology, oncology, etc.), urgent care, cancer care, outpatient surgery, clinical research, and rehabilitation services, focusing on collaborative care and approachable expertise.; Valleywise Health is Arizona's largest and longest-serving public teaching hospital and healthcare system, serving as the community safety net for Maricopa County. They provide a range of services including trauma care, burn care, pediatrics, behavioral health, and comprehensive care through their network of community health centers, also serving as a major training center for healthcare professionals.
