About the Practice

CarePoint Health is a healthcare system committed to the health of Hudson County, New Jersey, combining the resources of three area hospitals: Bayonne Medical Center, Christ Hospital in Jersey City, and Hoboken University Medical Center. [3] It is a physician-owned and led multispecialty practice dedicated to providing a high standard of patient-centered care with compassion and innovation. [3, 7] They offer a wide range of services including comprehensive heart care, stroke care, emergency services, behavioral health, wound care, transitional care, radiation oncology, dialysis, sleep center services, and various surgical specialties. [11, 12] Their mission is to improve the health of the communities they serve through coordinated care, smart technology, and a network of experienced medical professionals. [3]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

CarePoint Health utilizes "My Carepoint Health" as its patient portal, providing patients with access to their personal health information from Bayonne Medical Center, Christ Hospital, Hoboken University Medical Center, and their Medical Group practices. CarePoint Health employs different electronic health record (EHR) systems for its various facilities. The health system uses MEDITECH for its in-patient hospital systems. For its 45 medical practices, CarePoint Health selected eClinicalWorks as their comprehensive ambulatory EHR solution. The "My Carepoint Health" patient portal is designed to provide patients with integrated access to their health information originating from these different systems.
