About the Practice

AZ Integrated Neuro Spine & Pain is described as Arizona's premier comprehensive neurology practice, offering patients neurological care, pain management, and surgical/non-surgical treatment options for spine issues. [2, 8] They provide diagnosis, management, and treatment for conditions affecting the central and peripheral nervous systems, including the brain, nerves, and spine. [2, 8] Their specialties include Neurology, Neuropsychology, Pain Management, and Neurorehabilitation, covering conditions such as Alzheimer's, migraines, Multiple Sclerosis, neuromuscular disorders, cerebrovascular disease, and conditions requiring physical medicine and rehabilitation or neuropsychological testing. [2, 4, 5, 7, 9, 11, 14, 15, 16] They aim to provide leading-edge comprehensive care in a patient-centric environment with a team of specialists working together for optimal patient outcomes. [2]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

AZ Integrated Neuro Spine & Pain (azintegrated.com) utilizes the AkosMD platform, which includes a patient portal for patient interactions. While the patient portal does not appear to have a distinct brand name like "MyChart," it is referred to as the "AkosMD portal" or simply the "patient portal" within the context of their telemedicine and healthcare services provided through AkosMD. The AkosMD platform, in addition to telemedicine capabilities, also features a built-in Electronic Medical Record (EMR) module. This indicates that AkosMD is likely the integrated EHR vendor for AZ Integrated Neuro Spine & Pain, providing both the patient portal functionality and the underlying electronic health record system. Through the AkosMD patient portal, patients of AZ Integrated Neuro Spine & Pain can access various features, including the ability to communicate with their providers, request follow-up appointments, view and update their information, access their medication list, and view and pay statements. The platform aims to provide a secure and convenient way for patients to engage with their healthcare team and manage their health information.
