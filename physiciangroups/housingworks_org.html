About the Practice

Housing Works Community Healthcare is a healing community that originated from the AIDS crisis in New York City, fighting for inclusive care, social justice, and an end to homelessness. They provide high-quality integrated medical care and a client-centered approach with a focus on harm reduction and serving low-income New Yorkers living with or affected by HIV/AIDS and/or other chronic health conditions. Specialties include Primary Care, Sexual Health, Adult Gender-Affirming Care, Mental Health, Substance Use Support, Pediatrics, Pain Management, and Telehealth, along with supportive services like housing, job training, and legal assistance.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Housing Works Community Healthcare utilizes the healow platform for its patient portal, which is branded as "healow - Health and Online Wellness". The healow patient portal is a service provided by eClinicalWorks, a company that offers electronic health record (EHR) systems and other healthcare IT solutions. While a direct confirmation from Housing Works Community Healthcare stating they use eClinicalWorks was not found in the search results, eClinicalWorks' integration with the healow portal and their focus on providing services to health centers like Housing Works Community Healthcare strongly suggest that eClinicalWorks is the underlying EHR vendor.
