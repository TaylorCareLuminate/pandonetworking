About the Practice

OhioHealth is a not-for-profit, charitable healthcare system based in Columbus, Ohio, and is an outreach of the United Methodist Church. It serves a multi-county area and includes 15-16 hospitals, 3 joint-venture hospitals, 1 managed hospital, and over 200-300 ambulatory sites, along with hospice, home health, medical equipment, and other health services. [2, 7, 11] The system's mission is to improve the health of those it serves and provide exceptional care, guided by core values including compassion, excellence, inclusion, integrity, and stewardship. [2, 5, 11] OhioHealth offers a wide range of care, encompassing emergency services, primary care, and various specialties such as neuroscience, heart, and cancer care. [7, 10]

Primary Electronic Health Record System

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

OhioHealth utilizes a patient portal called MyChart. This patient portal is powered by Epic, which is also the electronic health record (EHR) system used by OhioHealth. MyChart provides patients with online access to manage various aspects of their healthcare, including scheduling appointments, requesting prescription refills, communicating securely with their physician's office, and accessing their electronic medical record. OhioHealth transitioned to OhioHealth MyChart from a previous portal called myOhioHealth. The implementation of the Epic EHR system and MyChart across OhioHealth facilities, referred to as CareConnect in some instances, aims to create a single, unified patient record and enhance patient access and engagement.
