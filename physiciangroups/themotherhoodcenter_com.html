About the Practice

The Motherhood Center is a comprehensive mental health treatment center specializing in Perinatal Mood and Anxiety Disorders (PMADs) for new and expecting mothers/birthing people and their partners. [1, 8, 14, 15] They provide a range of services including a unique Day Program (Partial Hospitalization Program) for those with severe symptoms, outpatient treatment offering evaluation, therapy, medication management, and trying to conceive consultations, and supportive group therapy. [1, 8, 15, 16] The center offers both virtual and in-person services and takes an interdisciplinary approach to care. [1, 10, 16, 17]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

While The Motherhood Center (themotherhoodcenter.com) provides a patient login area on their website, the specific vendor or branding of their patient portal is not explicitly stated in readily available information. However, search results indicate that InterSystems is a potential technology provider for The Motherhood Center of New York offices. InterSystems offers a patient engagement platform called HealthShare Personal Community. This platform is designed to be configurable and can be white-labeled, which means organizations can brand it with their own name and logo. Given the mention of InterSystems in connection with The Motherhood Center's technology and InterSystems' offering of a white-label patient portal, it is probable that The Motherhood Center utilizes a patient portal powered by InterSystems, potentially branded simply as "The Motherhood Center Patient Portal" or a similar variation, rather than a widely recognized third-party name like "MyChart" (used by Epic). The login page on The Motherhood Center's website does not display any prominent third-party patient portal branding, which aligns with the possibility of a white-labeled solution. While other EHR systems are used by mental health clinics in New York, the specific (though not definitively confirmed for EHR) association with InterSystems makes their HealthShare Personal Community the most likely underlying platform for The Motherhood Center's patient portal based on the available search information.
