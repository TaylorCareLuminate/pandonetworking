About the Practice

Mount St. Mary's Hospital, part of Catholic Health, is a 175-bed licensed acute care facility serving Lewiston and Niagara County, New York. It provides comprehensive acute and lifesaving medical care, including a 24/7 emergency department, advanced cardiac and stroke care, women's services (OB/GYN, maternity), primary care, imaging, lab/pathology, wound care, infusion services, outpatient rehab, and inpatient chemical dependency treatment at the Clearview Treatment Center. The hospital is recognized as a New York State Department of Health Designated Stroke Center and a certified Chest Pain Center.

Methodology & Findings

Mount St. Mary's Hospital, which is part of Catholic Health, uses MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal commonly associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Through the Catholic Health MyChart portal, patients can manage various aspects of their healthcare, including accessing health information, viewing test results, requesting prescription refills, and scheduling appointments. This portal is available online and through a mobile app.
