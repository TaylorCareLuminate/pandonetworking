About the Practice

Arizona Centers for Digestive Health is the state's leading provider for comprehensive gastrointestinal care, specializing in the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of digestive diseases including various cancers, GERD, inflammatory bowel disease, and other conditions. [1, 2, 9, 14] They offer a range of services from general care to advanced endoscopic procedures, provided by a team that includes board-certified gastroenterologists and advanced practitioners focused on personalized patient care. [1, 9, 11] Founded in 2017 by former Mayo Clinic physicians, they serve over 70,000 patients annually across multiple locations in Arizona. [1, 14]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Arizona Centers for Digestive Health (azcdh.com) appears to be using the gMed patient portal, known as gPortal. The footer of the Arizona Centers for Digestive Health website includes a "gMed Login" link, which is a strong indicator of the EHR system they utilize. gMed, now a Modernizing Medicine company, specializes in gastroenterology-specific electronic health record (EHR) systems. Their gGastro suite is a comprehensive EHR and practice management solution that includes an integrated patient portal. This patient portal, gPortal, allows patients to access their healthcare information, schedule appointments, view test results, and communicate with their practice. Based on the presence of the "gMed Login" on their website and gMed's focus on gastroenterology with an integrated patient portal called gPortal, it is highly likely that Arizona Centers for Digestive Health is using the gMed (Modernizing Medicine) EHR system and their associated gPortal for their patient portal.
