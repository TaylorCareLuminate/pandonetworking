About the Practice

Hoag Medical Group (HMG) is a multispecialty physician group closely aligned with Hoag Hospital. HMG offers a comprehensive suite of healthcare services including primary care in Family Medicine, Internal Medicine, and Pediatrics, as well as various specialties such as Allergy & Immunology, Endocrinology, Geriatrics, HIV Medicine, Rheumatology, Sports Medicine, and Travel Medicine. [2, 6, 9, 15] Patients of Hoag Medical Group have access to the entire Hoag healthcare system, including specialists, services, facilities, and urgent care centers. [2, 6, 15] They are committed to providing high-quality, personalized care with convenient access options like same-day appointments and online scheduling. [6, 15]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Hoag Medical Group utilizes a patient portal called Hoag Connect MyChart. This portal provides patients with online access to their health records, allowing them to view test results, communicate with providers, request prescription refills, manage appointments, and pay bills. Hoag Connect MyChart is associated with Epic. Patients can access their Hoag Connect account by downloading the Epic MyChart app and selecting Hoag Connect as their organization. Furthermore, Hoag participates in Health Information Exchange, including "Care Everywhere (Epic)," which facilitates the secure sharing of patient medical information. This connectivity through Epic's platform further indicates that Epic is the EHR vendor used by Hoag Medical Group.
