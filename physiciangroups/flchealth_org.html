About the Practice

Finger Lakes Community Health, associated with the domain flchealth.org, is a comprehensive primary medical and dental service provider with health centers across the Finger Lakes region. [4, 14] They offer full medical, dental, and behavioral health services to everyone in the community, accepting most insurances and offering a generous sliding fee discount. [14] The organization emphasizes wellness and prevention, providing personalized, individualized care in a collaborative, team-based setting. [3, 14] They are experienced in treating a diverse population and are dedicated to providing quality healthcare with an emphasis on underserved and special populations. [8, 14, 16] Services include pediatrics, women's health, men's health, adolescent health, diagnostic lab testing, well child services, immunizations, family planning, preventive screenings, nutrition counseling, tobacco cessation, care coordination, diabetic retinopathy screening, and telehealth specialty access. [15]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Finger Lakes Health (flchealth.org) appears to utilize different patient portals depending on the service location. For Geneva General Hospital and Soldiers & Sailors Memorial Hospital, while a specific portal name is not explicitly stated for the hospital portal itself, access to laboratory test results, clinic reports, and other medical information is available through the UR Medicine MyChart patient portal for Finger Lakes Health patients. MyChart is the patient portal associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. For their primary care and specialist practices, at least one practice, Finger Lakes Internists, uses the MEDENT Patient Portal. The MEDENT Patient Portal is associated with the MEDENT EHR system. Therefore, Finger Lakes Health likely uses the Epic EHR system for its hospitals, with MyChart as the patient portal, and the MEDENT EHR system for at least some of its primary care and specialist practices, with the MEDENT Patient Portal. It's important to note that Finger Lakes Community Health, a separate entity, uses the Healow app, which is the patient portal for eClinicalWorks. The user's request specifically concerned Finger Lakes Health (flchealth.org).
