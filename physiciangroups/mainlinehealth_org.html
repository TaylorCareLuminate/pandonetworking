About the Practice

Founded in 1985, Main Line Health is a not-for-profit health system serving portions of Philadelphia and its western suburbs, committed to delivering advanced medicine and playing a role in prevention and disease management. [1, 3, 8] At its core are four acute care hospitals—Lankenau Medical Center, Bryn Mawr Hospital, Paoli Hospital, and Riddle Hospital—as well as Bryn Mawr Rehabilitation Hospital for rehabilitative medicine, Mirmont Treatment Center for addiction recovery, and Main Line Health HomeCare & Hospice. [1, 8, 10] The system also includes Main Line Health Centers offering outpatient services like primary care, specialists, laboratory, radiology, and rehabilitation, and the Lankenau Institute for Medical Research focusing on cancer, diabetes, and heart disease. [10] With over 150 specialties available, Main Line Health provides comprehensive patient-centered care, education, and research. [10, 15] Specialties include Lung and Thoracic, General Surgery, Senior Health, and Stroke and Neurointervention, among many others. [4]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Main Line Health (mainlinehealth.org) uses Epic as their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. Their patient portal is branded as MyChart. Main Line Health selected Epic in 2016 for a single, integrated EHR system across their continuum of care, with the implementation going live in early 2018. Epic's patient portal functionality was a factor in their selection. The patient portal, Main Line Health MyChart, allows patients to schedule appointments, communicate with their care team, access test results, pay bills, and more. One source explicitly identifies the software for the Main Line Health Patient Portal at my.mainlinehealth.org as Epic MyChart.
