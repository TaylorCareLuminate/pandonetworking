About the Practice

Carolina Nephrology, PA is a medical practice specializing in nephrology and hypertension, providing comprehensive care for kidney conditions. Their services include diagnosis and treatment for acute and chronic kidney disease, end-stage renal disease, dialysis (hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, home dialysis), kidney transplant referral, hypertension, and other related conditions. They are committed to delivering compassionate, quality, and cost-effective healthcare to patients across the Upstate of South Carolina.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Carolina Nephrology PA, located in Greenville, SC, appears to utilize Epic as their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system and MyChart as their patient portal. While direct confirmation of their patient portal name on the Carolina Nephrology PA website (carolinanephrology.com) was not explicitly found in the search results, several pieces of information point to this conclusion. Carolina Nephrology PA is affiliated with Prisma Health.. The Carolina Nephrology website indicates that referring providers who are part of the Prisma network should use EPIC for referrals. This suggests that Epic is integrated within their affiliated network. Further supporting the use of Epic, a job posting for a Medical Assistant at Carolina Nephrology PA lists "Knowledge of Epic is preferred" and mentions the role involves reviewing and entering patient information into their EHR system. Prisma Health, the system Carolina Nephrology PA is affiliated with, explicitly states that they use MyChart as their patient portal. MyChart is widely known as the patient portal system that integrates with Epic's EHR. Although a different practice, North Carolina Nephrology P.A. (not Carolina Nephrology PA) is associated with Interwell Health, which uses "Acumen® Epic Connect," a nephrology-specific Epic EHR. While this doesn't directly apply to Carolina Nephrology PA, it shows a connection between nephrology practices and Epic systems. Considering the affiliation with Prisma Health, the use of Epic for referrals within that network, the preference for Epic knowledge in a job posting for Carolina Nephrology PA, and Prisma Health's use of MyChart, it is highly probable that Carolina Nephrology PA uses Epic as their EHR and MyChart as their patient portal through their connection with Prisma Health.
