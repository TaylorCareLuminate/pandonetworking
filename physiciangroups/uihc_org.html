About the Practice

University of Iowa Physicians is the multispecialty medical and surgical group practice of University of Iowa Health Care, Iowa's comprehensive academic health system. They provide world-class adult and pediatric primary and specialty care to patients across Iowa and beyond, integrating patient care with research and education. Their services range from primary care to highly specialized treatments, delivered at their main hospital campuses in Iowa City and numerous outpatient locations throughout the state.
