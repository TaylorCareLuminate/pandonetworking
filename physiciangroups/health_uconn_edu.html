About the Practice

UConn Health is a vibrant, integrated academic medical center and a branch of the University of Connecticut, dedicated to clinical care, advanced biomedical research, and academic education. It comprises UConn John Dempsey Hospital, the School of Medicine, School of Dental Medicine, and the Graduate School. [2, 4, 6, 8] With over 450 physicians across more than 50 specialties, UConn Health provides a comprehensive range of services including specialized and routine inpatient and outpatient care, dental care, emergency services, and expertise in areas such as cardiology, cancer care, orthopaedics, dermatology, neurosurgery, geriatrics, and maternal fetal medicine. [2, 4, 10, 18] Recognized for innovative, patient-centered care and groundbreaking research, UConn Health is committed to educating the next generation of medical and dental professionals. [3, 9]
