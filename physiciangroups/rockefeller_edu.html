About the Practice

The Rockefeller University is a private biomedical research and graduate-only university in New York City, dedicated to research and graduate education in the biomedical sciences, chemistry, bioinformatics, and physics. [2, 9] It is the oldest biomedical research institute in the United States. [2] With over 80 laboratories, it conducts both clinical and basic research with the mission of improving the understanding of life for the benefit of humanity. [9] The university includes The Rockefeller University Hospital, the first hospital in the US devoted exclusively to clinical research, where patients participate in studies building on basic research findings. [9] The university is known for its interdisciplinary approach and focus on cutting-edge research, providing doctoral and postdoctoral training. [3, 2]
