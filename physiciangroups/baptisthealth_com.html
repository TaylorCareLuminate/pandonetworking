About the Practice

Baptist Health Louisville is a 519-bed hospital providing a comprehensive range of healthcare services. It is recognized for nursing excellence with Magnet designation and is a certified stroke center that performs the most total joint surgeries in Kentucky. [2, 5, 13] Key service areas include emergency care with specialized teams for heart attack and stroke, extensive cancer services, women's health with private maternity rooms and an expanded NICU, orthopedics, neurosurgery, cardiovascular care, bariatric surgery, wound care, behavioral health, occupational health, and in-home care. [13, 9] The hospital is supported by the Baptist Health Medical Group, a network of physicians covering over 85 specialties, as well as urgent care centers and rehabilitation facilities. [12, 2]

Methodology & Findings

Baptist Health Louisville uses MyChart as its patient portal. This patient portal is integrated with or is part of the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system utilized by Baptist Health. Baptist Health completed the implementation of Epic across all its facilities in Kentucky, including physician practices and urgent care locations, in 2017. MyChart provides patients with a free online tool to manage their health, offering access to medical records, appointment scheduling, lab results, financials, and communication with providers.
