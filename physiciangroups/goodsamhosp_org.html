About the Practice

Good Samaritan Hospital, a member of the Westchester Medical Center Health Network, is a non-profit, 286-bed hospital in Suffern, NY. It provides emergency, medical, surgical, obstetrical/gynecological, and acute care services. The hospital offers regional specialty services including a recognized cardiovascular program, comprehensive cancer treatment center, robotic surgery program, Wound and Hyperbaric Institute, maternal/child services, and a dedicated Esophageal and Reflux Center. Specialties for which surgical second opinions are offered include Bariatric Surgery, Breast Cancer, Cardiac Surgery, General Surgery, Gynecology, Interventional Cardiology, Neurosurgery, Ophthalmology, Orthopedic Surgery, Radiation Oncology, and Thoracic Surgery.
