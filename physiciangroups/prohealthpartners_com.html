About the Practice

ProHealth Partners, A Medical Group, Inc. is a comprehensive and well-established network comprising over 160 primary care physicians and specialists across more than 80 locations in the Greater Long Beach, Los Angeles, and Orange County areas. [6] They offer primary care services including Internal Medicine, Family Medicine, and Pediatrics, as well as various specialties such as Gastroenterology, Cardiology, Neurology, Dermatology, and Endocrinology. [7, 10] The group is committed to providing convenient, responsive, and personalized healthcare tailored to the individual needs of patients and their families. [4, 8]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Prohealth Partners (prohealthpartners.com), specifically the medical group located in the Greater Long Beach area, California, appears to utilize the Harris CareTracker system for their electronic health records (EHR) and patient portal. A physician who is part of the ProHealth Partners Medical Group mentions being automated on the Harris CareTracker Physician EMR System and references a Harris CareTracker Patient Portal. Additionally, information regarding patient payments for "PROHEALTH PARTNERS, A MEDICAL GROUP INC" refers to "Caretracker Patients". It is worth noting that a different entity, "ProHealth Physicians" (not associated with prohealthpartners.com), has transitioned to using Epic as their EHR system, which includes the MyChart patient portal. However, based on the web address provided in your query, the Prohealth Partners in California seems to use Harris CareTracker. The patient portal for the ProHealth Partners offering in-home therapy services in Ohio & Michigan, which also uses the prohealthpartners.com domain, was not explicitly identified in the search results.
