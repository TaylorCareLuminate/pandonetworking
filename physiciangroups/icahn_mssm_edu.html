About the Practice

The Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai is a private medical school in New York City and the academic teaching arm of the Mount Sinai Health System. [7] It is an international leader in biomedical education, research, and patient care, renowned for its outstanding programs. [2, 10, 11] The school offers MD, PhD, and Master's degrees with an emphasis on innovative education and translational research across diverse areas including biomedical sciences, public health, genomics/big data, virology, neuroscience, cardiology, geriatrics, and gastrointestinal and liver diseases. [7, 10, 12, 14, 15] It is committed to delivering advanced, compassionate, patient-centric care leveraging the latest discoveries and innovations with an unwavering commitment to health equity. [13, 14]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, as part of the Mount Sinai Health System, utilizes a patient portal branded as MyMountSinai. This patient portal is built on the MyChart platform, which is developed by Epic. MyMountSinai, powered by MyChart, allows patients to access their medical records, communicate with their healthcare team, schedule appointments, and manage other aspects of their health care online. The connection between MyMountSinai, MyChart, and Epic is also evidenced by the presence of the "MyChart is Epic logo" on login pages and descriptions of MyMountSinai being a "MyChart Epic® Diamond Patient Experience recipient". A YouTube video providing a tutorial of the Mount Sinai patient portal explicitly refers to it as "Mount Sinai Patient Portal MyChart" and mentions Epic as the medical record system used.
