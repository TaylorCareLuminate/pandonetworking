About the Practice

Henry Ford Health is a leading academic medical center and a comprehensive integrated health system with over 550 sites across Michigan. It provides a full range of health services including primary, preventative, urgent, complex, and specialty care, health insurance, home health, virtual care, pharmacy, and eye care. [5, 8, 14] Recognized for clinical excellence, Henry Ford Health is also committed to advancing healthcare through innovation, research, clinical trials, and training the next generation of healthcare professionals, while also focusing on community health, addressing disparities, and promoting health equity. [8, 13]
