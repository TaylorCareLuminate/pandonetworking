About the Practice

Flagstaff Medical Center, part of Northern Arizona Healthcare, is a standout regional hospital providing comprehensive healthcare services. [2, 11, 12] It is distinguished by high-quality care and specialty services, including an award-winning spine surgery program, comprehensive inpatient and outpatient behavioral health services, and the only Level 1 Trauma Center north of Phoenix. [2] The medical center also offers services through primary care and specialty physician clinics, outpatient surgical centers, a Heart & Vascular Center, Cancer Centers, EntireCare Rehab & Sports Medicine, Children's Health Center, and Orthopedic & Spine Institute. [11, 12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Flagstaff Medical Center, which is part of Northern Arizona Healthcare (NAH), uses a patient portal branded as MyNAHealthcare. This portal provides patients with secure online access to various aspects of their health information. Northern Arizona Healthcare utilizes an electronic health record (EHR) system from Cerner. Cerner was acquired by Oracle, and some sources now refer to the EHR as Oracle Health EHR. The MyNAHealthcare patient portal offers a view into this Electronic Medical Record (EMR) system. Through MyNAHealthcare, patients can view health information, communicate with their care team, request prescription renewals, schedule appointments, and pay bills for many services.
