About the Practice

Cobre Valley Regional Medical Center (CVRMC) is the premier healthcare provider in southern Gila County and the Copper Corridor area, serving communities including Globe, Miami, Kearny, Superior, Young, and Tonto Basin. Its mission is to develop and maintain a local healthcare delivery system that provides high-quality, efficient, and compassionate care. CVRMC offers a wide range of services from preventative to emergency care, including full-service oncology (chemotherapy and radiation), skin clinic, chronic care management, podiatry, pediatrics, cath lab, infusion therapy, family practice, general surgery, imaging, laboratory, pharmacy, obstetrics and gynecology, physical therapy, cardiology, emergency services, wound center, vein center, sleep center, orthopedics, outpatient surgery, pain management, telemedicine, and weight loss services.
