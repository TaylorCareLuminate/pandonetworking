About the Practice

Associates in Nephrology, S.C. is a specialized healthcare provider with over 50 years of experience in the Chicagoland area, dedicated to providing exceptional and comprehensive care for patients with kidney disease and hypertension. [3, 4, 9] They utilize advanced diagnostic tools and offer personalized, evidence-based approaches to managing all stages of chronic kidney disease, including medication, lifestyle guidance, coordinated care, and various dialysis options such as in-center and home dialysis. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Associates in Nephrology, S.C. (associatesin-nephrology.com), located in Chicago, Illinois, utilizes the MyChart patient portal. This patient portal is integrated with their electronic health record (EHR) system, which is Acumen Epic Connect. Acumen Epic Connect is an EHR system specifically designed for nephrology practices that combines the functionalities of the Epic platform with nephrology expertise. Several sources indicate that the patient portal used with Acumen Epic Connect is MyChart, a widely used patient portal licensed from Epic Systems Corporation. The login page for the Acumen Epic Connect Patient Portal also identifies itself as MyChart. Therefore, Associates in Nephrology, S.C. uses the MyChart patient portal, which is tied to their Acumen Epic Connect EHR system.
