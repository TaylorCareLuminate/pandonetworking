About the Practice

Cardiovascular Consultants, Ltd. (CVC) is a full-service cardiology clinic and the largest provider of cardiovascular medicine in Arizona and the Southwestern U.S., operating since 1986. They offer a comprehensive spectrum of cardiac services, including sophisticated medical consultations, advanced testing like vascular and cardiac ultrasound, surgical diagnostics, nuclear cardiology, PET, and electrophysiology, as well as invasive, vascular, and endovascular surgical services.

Methodology & Findings

Cardiovascular Consultants (cvcheart.com) uses a patient portal provided by athenahealth. According to information on their website, Cardiovascular Consultants transitioned to a new electronic health record and billing system from athenahealth effective November 5, 2024. The patient portal, which allows for online bill pay, scheduling, secure messaging, and access to test results, is accessible via a URL that includes "portal.athenahealth.com," indicating athenahealth as the vendor for the patient portal and the associated EHR system.
