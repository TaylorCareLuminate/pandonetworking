About the Practice

Blythedale Children's Hospital is New York State's only independent, specialty children's hospital dedicated to improving the health and quality of life of children with complex medical illnesses and conditions. [4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 13] It provides comprehensive, individualized, intensive, and innovative medical and therapeutic care, including inpatient and outpatient programs, rehabilitation services (including the largest pediatric therapy department in NYS), specialized feeding programs, and care for conditions like brain injury, pulmonary issues, complex chronic conditions, and post-surgical needs. [4, 5, 6, 7] The hospital also integrates specialized school curricula and family support services to help children achieve their rehabilitative goals and return home. [4, 5, 7]

Methodology & Findings

Blythedale Children's Hospital utilizes a patient portal called the "Family Health Portal". The hospital appears to use the Meditech electronic health record (EHR) system, as they have partnered with Summit Healthcare for their migration to Meditech 6.0. While the Family Health Portal is the branding used by Blythedale, it is likely integrated with their Meditech EHR system.
