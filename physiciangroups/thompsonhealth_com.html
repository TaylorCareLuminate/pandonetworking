About the Practice

F.F. Thompson Hospital, part of UR Medicine Thompson Health, is a 113-bed acute care community hospital in Canandaigua, NY. [2, 9, 10] It serves as the center of a healthcare network providing a full range of services including patient care, emergency medical care, family health and wellness, occupational health, and senior living. [4] The hospital offers various clinical services and programs, utilizing the latest healthcare technology and a compassionate team. [4] Services cover diagnostics, physical therapy, cancer treatment at the Sands Cancer Center, wellness programs, primary care, OB/Gyn, and specialty care, focusing on clinical expertise and personal care for approximately 215,000 residents in the greater Finger Lakes region. [2, 4, 6, 9]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

FF Thompson Hospital (thompsonhealth.com) uses MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is the patient portal system developed by Epic Systems. Through the MyChart portal, patients of FF Thompson Hospital can access their health records and manage aspects of their care. FF Thompson Hospital also partners with PocketHealth, allowing patients to access and review their medical imaging records directly through MyChart. While another system called "My Health eRecord" is mentioned for accessing information from inpatient or observation visits after a specific date, MyChart is consistently presented as the primary patient portal used by Thompson Health.
