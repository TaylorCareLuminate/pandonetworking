About the Practice

The University of Michigan, with a primary campus in Ann Arbor and additional campuses in Dearborn and Flint, is a public research university classified among "R1: Doctoral Universities – Very high research activity". [7, 12] It offers over 250 degree programs across numerous colleges and schools. [7] Michigan Medicine, the university's academic medical center, is a national leader in research and provides a full spectrum of specialty care, including diagnostics, treatment, ongoing care management, and rehabilitation, across three hospitals and more than 125 clinics throughout Southeast Michigan. [16, 19] The university is committed to improving clinical care, value, and health outcomes through a diverse portfolio of high-quality clinical trials. [19] Specialized care is also available, such as comprehensive dental services for patients with special healthcare needs. [21]

Methodology & Findings

The University of Michigan Health (Michigan Medicine) uses a patient portal called MyUofMHealth Patient Portal. This portal is provided by Epic Systems Corporation and is built on the MyChart platform. The underlying electronic health record (EHR) system used by Michigan Medicine is also from Epic Systems Corporation and is branded internally as "MiChart".
