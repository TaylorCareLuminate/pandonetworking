About the Practice

Magnetic Diagnostic Resources of Central New York (MDR of CNY) was a provider of specialized MRI services in Central New York since 1985, accredited by the American College of Radiology. [5, 9, 10] They offered a comprehensive range of routine and specialized MRI scans, including breast MRI, prostate MRI, cardiac MRI, enterography, and fetal MRI, utilizing advanced technology and fellowship-trained radiologists. [4, 8, 13] MDR of CNY focused on providing high-quality imaging in a patient-friendly environment. [8, 9, 10] As of the end of 2024, MDR of CNY closed, and its locations were acquired by Mohawk Glen Radiology and Medical Imaging Center. [5]
