About the Practice

St. Peter's Health Partners Medical Associates (SPHPMA) is one of the Capital Region's largest multi-specialty physician groups, affiliated with St. Peter's Health Partners. With over 950 physicians and advanced practitioners, SPHPMA provides high-quality, compassionate care across more than 20 specialties, including primary care, internal medicine, pediatrics, cardiology, endocrinology, neurology, surgical services, and urgent care. They focus on improving coordination of care among primary care providers, specialists, and hospitals.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

St. Peter's Health Partners (SPHP) Medical Associates utilizes the MyChart patient portal. This transition to "Trinity Health MyChart" allows patients to access a secure online collection of their medical records from within the SPHP network and other facilities within the Trinity Health system. Through the MyChart portal, patients can manage various aspects of their healthcare, including scheduling appointments, viewing health records and test results, requesting prescription refills, and paying bills online. MyChart is a patient portal commonly associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system.
