About the Practice

UCSF Health is internationally renowned for providing highly specialized and innovative care, recognized globally for innovative treatments, advanced technology, and pioneering research applied to patient care. [2, 3] They bring together leading experts in nearly every area of health to drive advancements in treatment and technology. [2] UCSF Health's mission is to positively impact the lives of more patients and practitioners by providing evidence-based, data-driven care that decreases both complications and costs. [5] Their specialties include cancer, neurological disorders, immunological disorders, HIV/AIDS, pediatrics, ear, nose & throat, diabetes & endocrine disorders, urology, gastroenterology & GI surgery, cardiology, heart & vascular surgery, obstetrics & gynecology, ophthalmology, geriatrics, rheumatology, orthopedics, and psychiatry. [3, 7, 9, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UCSF utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. MyChart provides patients with a confidential online platform to manage various aspects of their healthcare, including accessing medical information, sending messages to their care team, requesting prescription refills, scheduling appointments, and viewing test results. UCSF's EHR system, known internally as APeX (Advanced Patient-Centered Excellence), is based on Epic. Patients can access their UCSF electronic health record data through the MyChart online portal or the MyChart mobile app.
