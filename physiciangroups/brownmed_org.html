About the Practice

Brown Medicine is one of the largest nonprofit, academic, multi-specialty medical groups in Rhode Island with practice locations in Providence and the surrounding communities. [2, 3] As a founding member of Brown Physicians, Inc., Brown Medicine has over 200 physicians on staff committed to providing patients with compassionate primary care and advancing medical education and research. [1, 5] The group offers primary care and various subspecialties including Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, Geriatrics & Palliative Medicine, Hematology/ Oncology, Hospital Medicine, Infectious Diseases, Kidney Disease & Hypertension, Podiatry, Pulmonary, Critical Care & Sleep Medicine, Rheumatology, and Travel Medicine. [5] Their mission incorporates patient care, teaching, and research, striving to provide compassionate, accessible, evidence-based, state-of-the-art, quality medical care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Brown Medicine (brownmed.org) utilizes a patient portal that is accessible through their website and is associated with the Healow platform. Patients of Brown Medicine can use the free and secure Healow app to connect to their patient portal account. The Brown Medicine website provides guides specifically for using the Healow application for services like TeleVisits. Healow is a patient engagement platform that is noted to integrate seamlessly with eClinicalWorks EHR systems. eClinicalWorks is identified as a prominent provider of electronic health records, and Healow is described as a patient engagement platform by eClinicalWorks. Although Healow can be used with other EHR systems, its deep integration with and origin from eClinicalWorks strongly suggest that Brown Medicine uses eClinicalWorks as their EHR vendor.
