About the Practice

Valley Perinatal Services is a maternal-fetal medicine practice focused on reducing pre-term delivery and achieving optimal outcomes for high-risk expectant mothers. [2] They provide co-management of pregnant women with high-risk complications alongside their obstetricians. [2] Their services include genetic counseling, in-utero procedures, state-of-the-art obstetric ultrasound and diagnostics, multiple pregnancy care, and comprehensive prenatal screening. [2, 6] The practice is staffed by experienced maternal-fetal medicine specialists, nurse practitioners, and ultrasound sonographers. [2]
