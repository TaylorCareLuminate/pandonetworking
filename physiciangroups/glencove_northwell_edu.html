About the Practice

Glen Cove Hospital, part of Northwell Health, is a community hospital offering a range of inpatient and outpatient services. Specialties and care provided include inpatient and outpatient rehabilitation, cancer care, head and neck surgery, robotic surgery, breast reconstruction, multi-specialty ambulatory surgery, emergency care, acute renal dialysis, and a Traumatic Brain Injury Program. The hospital is recognized for high performance in certain adult specialties and procedures. [3, 6, 8, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Glen Cove Hospital, which is part of the Northwell Health system, utilizes the FollowMyHealth patient portal. This patient portal is a service provided by Veradigm. Northwell Health is in the process of transitioning to a new, unified electronic health record (EHR) system from Epic, with the phased implementation beginning in 2025. This move is intended to consolidate patient records across all Northwell facilities. Before the transition to Epic, Northwell Health used a variety of EHR systems, including Allscripts Sunrise for hospitals and Allscripts Touchworks for outpatient facilities. The FollowMyHealth patient portal is designed to be compatible with various EHR systems, meaning it can integrate with different underlying electronic health record platforms. Therefore, while the patient portal used by Glen Cove Hospital is FollowMyHealth (provided by Veradigm), the specific EHR system it connects to at any given time would be either the previous system or the Epic system, depending on whether that location has completed the transition to Epic.
