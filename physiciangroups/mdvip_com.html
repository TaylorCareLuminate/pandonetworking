About the Practice

MDVIP is an American company operating a national network of concierge physicians focused on personalized preventive primary care. [4, 6] Their model emphasizes comprehensive annual health assessments, customized wellness plans, and enhanced patient-physician relationships, providing member benefits such as same- or next-day appointments, extended visit times, and 24/7 physician availability. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

MDVIP utilizes a patient portal branded as "MDVIP Connect". This portal is designed to facilitate communication between physicians and patients, provide access to medical records, and offer personalized health information. Based on recent information, MDVIP appears to primarily use athenahealth as their preferred Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. MDVIP provides training and support to their affiliated physicians for using the athenahealth EHR, which includes the personalized patient portal and app. While an older article from 2017 mentioned a specific affiliated practice using e-MDs, the more current search results indicate a network-wide emphasis on athenahealth.
