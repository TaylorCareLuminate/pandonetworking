About the Practice

TBHC Physician Services PC is a subordinate organization of The Brooklyn Hospital Center, dedicated to providing high-quality healthcare to the community and supporting the hospital's mission. The organization offers a wide range of inpatient, acute-care, preventive, primary, and specialty services, including but not limited to internal medicine, surgery, pediatrics, family medicine, women's health, cancer care, cardiology, critical care, emergency medicine, dentistry, and dialysis. They provide exemplary healthcare services across various centers and clinics, caring for a diverse population with a focus on comprehensive and accessible care.

Methodology & Findings

TBHC Physician Services PC (part of The Brooklyn Hospital Center) primarily uses MyChart as its patient portal for ambulatory care sites. MyChart is a patient portal that is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. For inpatient, Emergency Department patients, or patients of a clinical service without MyChart, and former patients, TBHC also provides access to health information through the FollowMyHealth portal. Therefore, TBHC Physician Services PC uses the Epic EHR system, with MyChart as the primary patient portal for many services. They also utilize the FollowMyHealth portal for certain patient types.
