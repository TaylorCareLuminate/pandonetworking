About the Practice

Northeast Orthopedics and Sports Medicine (neosmteam.com) provides comprehensive orthopedic care in the Hudson Valley, NY. Their specialties include General Orthopedics, Sports Medicine, Joint Replacement, Pain Management, Hand & Upper Extremity, Foot & Ankle, Spine, and Rheumatology. They offer both surgical and nonsurgical treatments for a wide range of conditions and injuries, such as fractures, arthritis, and ligament tears, and provide on-site physical therapy. The practice is partnered with Montefiore Health System.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Northeast Orthopedics and Sports Medicine (neosmteam.com) likely utilizes the athenahealth electronic health record (EHR) system, and their patient portal is the athenahealth patient portal. While the Northeast Orthopedics and Sports Medicine website refers to an "online portal" and "Patient Portal" without specifying a particular vendor or branding by name on their patient resources or news pages, a news announcement regarding New England Orthopedic Surgeons (NEOS), which shares the same acronym as Northeast Orthopedics and Sports Medicine (NEOSM) and is also an orthopedic practice, stated they were implementing athenaOne, athenahealth's integrated EHR and patient engagement solution, and specifically mentioned using "athenahealth's patient portal". Athenahealth's patient portal is referred to generically as the "athenahealth Patient Portal" and is part of their athenaOne platform. Some practices using athenahealth may brand the portal with their own name, while others use the standard athenahealth interface. Based on the available information, it is highly probable that the patient portal used by Northeast Orthopedics and Sports Medicine is the athenahealth patient portal. This is further supported by another orthopedic practice, Orthopaedic Surgical Associates, explicitly stating they use "Athena Health" for their EMR system and referring to "our Patient Portal" in that context. Patients of Northeast Orthopedics and Sports Medicine are directed to an online portal to complete forms, with a link sent via email after scheduling an appointment. The portal is intended to provide access to various resources. Athenahealth's patient portal allows patients to access medical records, schedule appointments, request prescription refills, and communicate with their care team. Athenahealth also offers a mobile application called athenaPatient™.
