About the Practice

Nascentia Health is a home and community-based healthcare system serving New York State, providing a wide array of services designed to help individuals receive care in their homes. Their specialties include in-home skilled nursing and therapies, home health aides, personal care assistance, and various health insurance plans such as Nascentia Health Plus (a Medicare Dual Advantage Special Needs Plan) and the Nascentia Skilled Nursing Facility Plan. The organization focuses on restoring, maintaining, and promoting the health and independence of those they serve, with a mission to be a premier home and community-based care system.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Nascentia Health Plus, a part of Nascentia Health, offers a patient portal experience for its Medicare Advantage members through a platform referred to as the "DSNP Member Portal". This portal serves as the online access point for members to manage their health information and interact with Nascentia Health Plus services. While Nascentia Health incorporates the use of electronic health records (EHRs) in their operations, and provides a separate online tool for healthcare providers called "Nascentia Online", the specific technology vendor powering the "DSNP Member Portal" is not explicitly mentioned in the search results. Additionally, Nascentia Health has a partnership with HHAeXchange, primarily for Electronic Visit Verification (EVV) and related functionalities for home care providers, which appears to be distinct from the patient-facing member portal. Therefore, based on the available information, the patient portal used by Nascentia Health Plus is referred to as the "DSNP Member Portal," but the underlying EHR vendor or the specific technology platform behind this member portal could not be definitively identified.
