About the Practice

UBMD Internal Medicine is the largest department within the UBMD Physicians' Group, affiliated with the University at Buffalo's Jacobs School of Medicine and Biomedical Sciences. [3, 11] With over 135 doctors, they provide comprehensive primary and specialty care for adults, actively engaged in clinical care, teaching, and research. [3, 8] Their extensive services cover numerous internal medicine subspecialties including Allergy, Behavioral Medicine, Cardiology, Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, Geriatrics, Hematology, Infectious Diseases, Nephrology, Oncology, Primary Care, Pulmonology, Rheumatology, and Sleep Medicine. [3, 4, 6] They manage a wide range of conditions, including complex medical problems, and are allied with major Western New York hospitals. [6, 8]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UBMD Internal Medicine, part of the UBMD Physicians' Group, utilizes the **MyUBMD** patient portal, which is powered by **FollowMyHealth™**. This portal allows patients of UBMD Internal Medicine to manage their health information online. The Electronic Health Record (EHR) system used by UBMD Internal Medicine is **Allscripts**. According to information from 2010, UBMD's Internal Medicine division had been using the Allscripts EHR since 2004, and UBMD subsequently selected Allscripts as the enterprise-wide EHR for their entire group. FollowMyHealth is a patient engagement platform known for its ability to integrate with various EHR systems, including Allscripts. This integration allows for the MyUBMD portal powered by FollowMyHealth to connect with the Allscripts EHR system used by UBMD Internal Medicine, providing patients with access to their health information.
