About the Practice

The Chautauqua Center (TCC Health) is a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) and a Patient Centered Medical Home (PCMH) that provides comprehensive, high-quality patient-centered health and support services in the Chautauqua region. They offer a wide range of preventive and primary care services, including regular checkups, immunizations, women's health services, mental health and substance use counseling, dental care and prevention, and management of chronic diseases. TCC Health provides affordable care on a sliding fee scale based on family size and income, ensuring access to care regardless of ability to pay.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The Chautauqua Center (tcchealth.org) utilizes the Athena Portal for its patient portal services. This portal allows patients to confidentially message their providers, request prescription refills, view lab and test results, and pay medical or behavioral health bills online. The Athena Portal is a component of the healthcare technology solutions provided by athenahealth. Athenahealth is the vendor responsible for the electronic health record (EHR) system that integrates with the Athena Portal. Their integrated platform, known as athenaOne, includes EHR, medical billing, practice management, and patient engagement tools. Therefore, The Chautauqua Center uses athenahealth as its EHR vendor.
