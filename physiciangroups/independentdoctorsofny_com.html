About the Practice

Independent Doctors of New York (IDNY) is an association of independent, experienced, board-certified physicians in New York City. Their members do not participate in managed care plans, allowing them the freedom to practice medicine where the patient comes first. They provide a high level of healthcare uncompromised by outside bureaucratic or financial influences, offer convenient and unhurried appointments, and can perform or recommend any appropriate test, treatment, or surgery, as well as refer patients to any doctor or hospital, regardless of location or affiliation. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

## Independent Doctors of New York: No Single Patient Portal or EHR System Independent Doctors of New York (IDNY), operating under the website independentdoctorsofny.com, functions as an association of independent physicians rather than a single, unified medical practice. As such, there is no single patient portal vendor or electronic health record (EHR) system utilized by the entire group. The independent nature of the member physicians means that each doctor or their individual practice likely selects and uses their own patient portal and EHR system. While the IDNY website features a "Member Login" or "Member Portal," this appears to be for the internal use of the member physicians themselves and not a patient-facing portal for accessing personal health information across the association. Therefore, patients seeking to access a patient portal or understand the EHR system used for their care by an Independent Doctors of New York physician would need to inquire directly with their specific doctor's office. The patient portal branding and the underlying EHR system would be determined by the individual practice, not by the IDNY association as a whole.
