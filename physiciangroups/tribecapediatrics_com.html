About the Practice

Tribeca Pediatrics is a primary care pediatric practice that has been caring for children across New York and New Jersey for over three decades, founded in 1994. They focus on accessible, thoughtful, and family-centered care, providing comprehensive pediatric services including newborn care, well-child visits, same-day sick visits, vaccinations, developmental screenings, and virtual consultations. They emphasize a low-intervention, personalized approach to support a child's development while providing exceptional medical guidance in a welcoming and child-friendly environment. [2, 3, 4, 7, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Based on the information gathered, Tribeca Pediatrics utilizes Office Practicum as their pediatric-specific EHR system. For their patient portal, Tribeca Pediatrics is using a system branded as "Tribeca Pediatrics EMR" which includes both a Practitioner Portal and a Patient Portal. This patient portal is being built on Salesforce Community Cloud to enhance the patient experience, and they are integrating their EHR with Salesforce Health Cloud. Additionally, the login page for the "Tribeca Pediatrics EMR" indicates it was "Made with ❤️ by Beda Software", suggesting Beda Software's involvement in its development. Therefore, while Office Practicum is the core EHR vendor, the patient portal used by Tribeca Pediatrics is a custom-branded solution built on the Salesforce platform, potentially with development assistance from Beda Software, and it integrates with their Office Practicum EHR system.
