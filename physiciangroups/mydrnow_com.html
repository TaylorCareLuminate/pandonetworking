About the Practice

MY DR NOW is a primary care group specializing in family medicine, offering comprehensive healthcare services for patients of all ages. [1, 3, 6, 12, 13] Their services include primary care, pediatrics, women's health, behavioral health, chronic disease management, physicals, immunizations, labs, and in-office procedures. [2, 3, 4, 5, 12, 13] They focus on accessible and convenient care through numerous clinic locations, house calls, and video visits, providing extended hours and accepting walk-ins. [1, 6, 12, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

My Dr Now (mydrnow.com) utilizes MyChart as their patient portal. MyChart is a widely recognized patient portal specifically associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. This indicates that My Dr Now is using Epic for their EHR needs. Through the MyChart portal, patients of My Dr Now can access various features to manage their healthcare, including receiving text message notifications, requesting medical records, scheduling appointments, viewing health overviews and test results, renewing prescriptions, finding health information, and communicating securely with their healthcare team.
