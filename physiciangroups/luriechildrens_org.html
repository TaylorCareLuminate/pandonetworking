About the Practice

Ann & Robert H. Lurie Children's Hospital of Chicago is a nationally ranked pediatric acute care children's hospital providing superior pediatric care in a state-of-the-art setting. [5, 9] It is the largest pediatric provider in the region, offering comprehensive pediatric specialties and subspecialties for patients aged 0-21. [6, 9] The hospital is affiliated with the Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine and is a state-designated Level 1 Pediatric Trauma Center. [8, 9] Care is supported by research conducted through the Stanley Manne Children's Research Institute and spans over 70 pediatric specialties. [2, 5, 6, 7]
