About the Practice

Somers Orthopaedic Surgery & Sports Medicine Group provides expert diagnosis and treatment for musculoskeletal conditions and injuries. Specializing in orthopaedic surgery and sports medicine, they offer comprehensive care for issues affecting the knee, hip, shoulder, elbow, wrist, hand, foot, ankle, and spine. The group provides both surgical treatments, including minimally invasive and robotic-assisted joint replacement, and non-surgical therapies such as PRP, stem cell treatment, injections, and cryoablation. On-site diagnostic facilities like digital X-ray, MRI, and ultrasound are also available for patient convenience. [2, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Somers Orthopaedic Surgery & Sports Medicine Group utilizes a patient portal simply referred to as the "Patient Portal" on their website, somersortho.com. This portal allows patients to request appointments, email their physician's secretary, request medication refills, and access visit summaries. Based on information from 2010, their electronic health record (EHR) system appears to be SRSsoft. A press release from that year noted that Somers Orthopaedic Group upgraded to SurgiMate ESRM, a surgical management solution that seamlessly integrates with SRSsoft. While the patient portal itself is branded generically as "Patient Portal," the underlying EHR system that it integrates with is indicated to be SRSsoft. Somers Orthopaedic Surgery & Sports Medicine Group also uses InstaMed for patient payments, which is accessible through their patient portal. It's worth noting that a listing for their Danbury office, affiliated with Stamford Health, mentions MyChart, which is the patient portal commonly associated with Epic Systems. However, the primary patient portal referred to across the somersortho.com website for the group as a whole is not MyChart.
