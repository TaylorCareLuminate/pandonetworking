About the Practice

Rockland Psychiatric Center is one of 24 state-run psychiatric facilities in New York State, providing treatment, rehabilitation, and support to adults 18 and older with serious mental illness. [4] Services are offered at various levels of care including inpatient/hospital-based care, ambulatory/outpatient clinic care, Assertive Community Treatment (ACT) teams, a mobile mental health team (Sullivan County), and residential programs. [4] The hospital-based services include focused treatment units for deaf adults, geriatrics, and co-occurring substance disorders. [4] The goal is to ensure long-lasting recovery and support in collaboration with local agencies. [4]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Rockland Psychiatric Center, part of the New York State Office of Mental Health (OMH), utilizes the Empire State VistA electronic health record (EHR system). This system is based on the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs' VistA software. While the EHR system for OMH facilities has been identified, information regarding a specific patient portal vendor or branding universally used by all NYS OMH facilities, including Rockland Psychiatric Center, in conjunction with their Empire State VistA system is not readily available in the search results. Although some individual county mental health services licensed by OMH may have their own patient portals, a unified patient portal across the entire NYS OMH system linked to Empire State VistA was not found. The "PSYCKES" system mentioned in the searches is a tool for clinicians and quality improvement, not a patient portal.
