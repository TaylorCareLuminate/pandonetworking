About the Practice

ArchWell Health is a national primary care group dedicated to improving the lives of senior members aged 60 and older. [8, 10] They provide comprehensive senior primary care services, including routine check-ups, preventive screenings, nutrition education, onsite lab work, vaccinations, and care management. [9, 12] Their model emphasizes strong doctor-patient relationships with longer appointment times, offers access to cardiology telehealth and social work referrals, same-day and telehealth appointments, 24/7 phone support, and transportation assistance. [8, 9, 12] ArchWell Health centers also foster a sense of community with activity centers and social events. [8, 10]

Methodology & Findings

ArchWell Health utilizes a patient portal referred to as the "Member Portal" or "Patient Portal". While the branding is specific to ArchWell Health, information from a job description for an EMR/EHR Trainer at the company indicates a strong likelihood that the underlying Electronic Health Record (EHR) system is eClinicalWorks. The job posting specifically mentions the need for the trainer to be an "eCW Super User," a term commonly associated with expertise in the eClinicalWorks system.
