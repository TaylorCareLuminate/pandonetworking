About the Practice

The University at Buffalo Jacobs School of Medicine and Biomedical Sciences, founded in 1846, is a 4-year MD-granting program and part of the SUNY system, offering a wide range of degrees in medical and biomedical sciences including MD, PhD, Master's, undergraduate majors, residency, and postdoctoral programs. [2, 6, 10] Its mission is to advance health and wellness through the education of future leaders, innovative research, and outstanding clinical care, providing training in various healthcare settings and focusing on preparing graduates for competent medical care and research by emphasizing fundamental principles and critical judgment. [2, 4, 10] While it does not own its own hospital, the school affiliates with a consortium of hospitals for clinical training and experiences. [10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UBMD Physicians' Group, the clinical practice of the University at Buffalo Jacobs School of Medicine and Biomedical Sciences (medicine.buffalo.edu), utilizes the MyUBMD patient portal. This patient portal is powered by FollowMyHealth™. FollowMyHealth is a patient engagement platform that is part of Allscripts Healthcare Solutions. While there is a reference to Cerner being used in a collaboration with the Jacobs School of Medicine and the Great Lakes Integrated Medical Record (GLIMR) for medical student training, the patient portal branded as MyUBMD and used by UBMD's clinical practices is consistently identified as being powered by FollowMyHealth, an Allscripts product. Furthermore, records from 2010 indicate that UBMD selected Allscripts as their enterprise EHR solution. An employee resource page for UBMD Internal Medicine also lists Allscripts under medical resources and FollowMyHealth Dashboard under administrative resources, reinforcing the use of Allscripts within the clinical practice. Therefore, the patient portal vendor/branding used by the University at Buffalo Jacobs School of Medicine and Biomedical Sciences' clinical practice (UBMD) is MyUBMD, powered by FollowMyHealth, which is associated with the EHR vendor Allscripts.
