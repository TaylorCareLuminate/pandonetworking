About the Practice

HCA Florida Healthcare is the state's largest network of hospitals and sites of care, comprising over 650 affiliated locations including hospitals, physician practices, freestanding emergency rooms, and urgent care centers. [5, 8] They provide comprehensive, high-quality healthcare to over three million Floridians annually with a mission focused on the care and improvement of human life. [1, 6] The network offers a wide range of advanced healthcare services and specialties, including the state's most comprehensive cardiovascular network, emergency care, orthopedic services, complex GI services, transplantation, stroke care, and robotic surgery. [5, 8, 9, 12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

HCA Florida Healthcare utilizes the MyHealthONE platform as its patient portal. This online portal allows patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare, including scheduling appointments, requesting prescription refills, viewing lab results, and communicating with their doctor's office. HCA Healthcare, the parent company of HCA Florida Healthcare, is in the process of implementing MEDITECH Expanse as its electronic health record (EHR) platform across its facilities. This implementation includes locations in North Florida. While MyHealthONE is the patient-facing portal, MEDITECH Expanse is the underlying EHR system that supports the clinical and administrative functions. PatientKeeper is also mentioned as a physician interface for the EHR within HCA, providing physicians access to patient information. The MyHealthONE portal is presented as a secure online service that partners with HCA Florida Healthcare to provide patients access to their health information.
