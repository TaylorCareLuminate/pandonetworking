About the Practice

White Mountain Regional Medical Center is a not-for-profit, rural critical access hospital with 21 beds, designated as a Level 4 Trauma Center and the first certified pediatric 24-hour emergency department within the critical access designation in Arizona. It provides acute care inpatient, outpatient, and emergency services, offering a wide range of services including medical imaging, laboratory services, surgery, cardiopulmonary services, sleep diagnostics, rehabilitation (swing bed, physical, occupational, speech), a wellness center, chiropractic services, and child care. The Rural Health Clinic provides comprehensive primary care and specialty services such as cardiology, family medicine, interventional pain management, orthopedics, podiatry, and pulmonology, serving the healthcare needs of communities in Apache County, Arizona, and Catron County, New Mexico.
