About the Practice

Horizon Family Medical Group is a multi-specialty medical group serving Orange County, New York, and the Hudson Valley region. [5, 13] They offer comprehensive, compassionate, and patient-centered medical care for patients of all ages, with over 14 specialties including Primary Care, Allergy and Immunology, Behavioral Health, Dermatology, Endocrinology, Endoscopy, Gastroenterology, Nephrology, Nutrition, Ophthalmology, Pulmonology, and Women's Health. [4, 5, 8] They provide a blend of telemedicine and on-site visits, aiming to provide high-quality, coordinated care. [8, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Based on the search results, here's the information about Horizon Family Medical Group's patient portal and EHR system: Horizon Family Medical Group uses a patient portal simply referred to as the "Patient Portal" on their website (horizonfamilymedical.com). This portal allows patients to request appointments, request medication refills, and communicate with staff. Regarding the EHR vendor, Horizon Family Medical Group uses eClinicalWorks (ECW) for their EHR system. They have been using ECW for many years and have integrated it with MedicsPremier for practice management. Therefore, while the patient portal is branded generically as "Patient Portal," it is connected to the eClinicalWorks (ECW) EHR system.
