About the Practice

Union Community Health Center, a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC), has served the Bronx community since 1909, providing comprehensive, culturally sensitive, quality primary care, specialty services, and dental services. [2, 4, 5] They offer a wide range of healthcare services including adult and pediatrics, rapid care, physical and occupational therapy, behavioral health, dental services, and various specialty services. [2, 5] Their mission is to provide comprehensive, culturally competent primary and preventive healthcare services to improve the health status and decrease health disparities of medically underserved populations in the Bronx. [6, 9] Union Community Health Center provides services to over 38,000 unique patients annually. [2, 5]

Methodology & Findings

Union Community Health Center (UCHC) utilizes a patient portal simply referred to as the "Patient Portal". This patient portal is part of the athenahealth system, as UCHC is a partner of athenahealth and uses athenaOne, which includes patient portal functionality. Athenahealth is the electronic health record (EHR) vendor for Union Community Health Center.
