About the Practice

Albany Gastroenterology Consultants specializes in providing comprehensive, high-quality, and compassionate gastroenterology care. [18] They offer a full array of services, procedures, and treatments for various gastrointestinal conditions, including but not limited to colonoscopy, upper endoscopy, and care for conditions like inflammatory bowel disease, liver disease, and GERD. [13, 14, 17] The practice also provides specialized services such as endobariatric weight loss procedures and nutritional resources. [2]

Methodology & Findings

Albany Gastroenterology Consultants, which is part of Allied Digestive Health, utilizes a patient portal for patient communication and access to health information. Allied Digestive Health, a network that Albany Gastroenterology Consultants has joined, selected athenahealth's patient engagement and practice management solutions in December 2021. These solutions typically include a patient portal component. Another practice that joined Allied Digestive Health also refers to a "new Athena portal" for patient payments. Based on Albany Gastroenterology Consultants being part of Allied Digestive Health and Allied Digestive Health's adoption of athenahealth for patient engagement, the patient portal used by Albany Gastroenterology Consultants is likely the athenahealth patient portal.
