About the Practice

Mather Medical Group, PC (MMG), based on Long Island, is a growing group of practices providing a wide scope of medical services including cardiology, general, colorectal, and vascular surgery, endocrinology, internal medicine, rheumatology, pulmonary medicine, gastroenterology, back and neck pain, family medicine, sleep medicine, and interventional radiology. [1, 4] Founded with a mission to provide quality medical care, expanded access, and patient-centered service, MMG is affiliated with Northwell Health and Mather Hospital, leveraging their resources for seamless continuity of care. [1, 4]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Mather Medical Group, affiliated with Mather Hospital and part of the larger Northwell Health system, provides patients with a portal to manage various aspects of their healthcare. While the specific brand name of the patient portal used by Mather Medical Group is not explicitly mentioned in the search results in the same way "MyChart" is associated with Epic, the functionalities offered are consistent with a standard patient portal. These features include updating personal information, secure messaging with their doctor's office, viewing upcoming appointments, accessing test results, and managing billing. Regarding the underlying electronic health record (EHR) system, Mather Hospital, as part of Northwell Health, has been implementing Allscripts EMR. This implementation began around 2010, with continued integration efforts involving Allscripts Acute Care in certain departments as recently as 2021. Mather Hospital's integration into the Northwell Health system in 2017 has facilitated data sharing across the network. Northwell Health also offers a patient portal for accessing medical records. Physicians at Mather Hospital have access to patient electronic medical records through a system called Sunrise. Although one search result noted that organizations using Epic can exchange data with other EHRs like athenahealth and eClinicalWorks, the information specifically about Mather Hospital and its integration within Northwell Health points towards the use of Allscripts. Based on the available information, it is probable that the patient portal used by Mather Medical Group is integrated with an Allscripts EHR system, consistent with the documented EHR usage at Mather Hospital and its affiliation with Northwell Health. The specific branding of this patient portal is not clearly stated in the search results.
