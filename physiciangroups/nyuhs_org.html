About the Practice

UHS Binghamton General Hospital is a 220-bed hospital providing general medical, surgical, and rehabilitative care to the people of Greater Binghamton. [2, 7] It offers 24-hour emergency care and operates major regional programs including orthopedic surgery, bariatric care, wound care, physical therapy and rehabilitation services, chemical dependency treatment service, behavioral health program, sleep center, and a gastroenterology laboratory. [5] Imaging services are also available at this location. [5]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UHS Binghamton General Hospital, part of the United Health Services (UHS) system, utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. This was implemented when UHS transitioned to Epic, a widely-used electronic health record (EHR) and billing system. MyChart is the patient portal specifically associated with the Epic EHR system. Through the UHS MyChart portal, patients can access their health information, schedule and manage appointments, view test results, request prescription refills, and communicate with their healthcare team. This transition to Epic and MyChart aimed to create a single, integrated health record for patients across the UHS network, improving care quality and the patient experience.
