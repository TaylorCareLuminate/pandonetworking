About the Practice

Ironwood Cancer & Research Centers offers comprehensive and personalized cancer care in Arizona through a multidisciplinary approach with a network of over 100 providers. Their services encompass Medical and Radiation Oncology, chemotherapy, immunotherapy, surgery, advanced diagnostic imaging (CT, PET), and access to clinical trials. They provide specialized care across various cancer types via dedicated sub-specialty divisions, including Women's Centers for Breast and Gynecologic Oncology, and programs for Gastrointestinal, Lung, Head and Neck, Myeloma and Lymphoma, Skin & Sarcoma, and Genitourinary Oncology. Additional supportive services like genetic counseling, nutrition, social work, and integrative oncology are available to provide academic-level cancer care within a community setting.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Ironwood Cancer & Research Centers (ironwoodcrc.com) uses the CareSpace patient portal. This patient portal is provided by Flatiron Health and integrates with their OncoEMR® oncology EHR software. The Ironwood Cancer & Research Centers website indicates they are using CareSpace as their new patient portal. While one older result mentioned "See Your Chart" as their electronic medical record system, CareSpace is presented as the current patient portal. CareSpace is described as a platform that allows patients to access their health information, communicate with their care team, and share information with others. Flatiron Health explicitly states that the CareSpace patient portal provides a seamless experience for patients and integrates with their OncoEMR® oncology EHR software.
