About the Practice

The University of Alabama at Birmingham (UAB) is a public research university and academic medical center located in Birmingham, Alabama. [4, 7, 10] It is the largest research institution and single largest employer in the state. [9] UAB is dedicated to its mission of education, research, and world-class patient care, aiming to understand and improve the human experience. [4, 7] It offers comprehensive academic programs and is classified among "R1: Doctoral Universities – Very high research activity". [10] UAB Medicine, the clinical arm, is one of the nation's largest academic medical centers, providing a full spectrum of primary and specialty care, advanced treatments, and opportunities for clinical research across numerous medical fields, including cardiology, oncology, women's and infants care, orthopaedics, and more. [10, 15, 21, 22, 25]

## UAB Medicine Utilizes myUABMedicine Patient Portal, Transitioning to Epic EHR The University of Alabama at Birmingham (UAB) Medicine currently uses a patient portal branded as myUABMedicine. This portal provides patients with online access to portions of their electronic medical record, allowing them to manage their health and communicate with their healthcare providers. The myUABMedicine portal is currently connected to the Cerner electronic health record (EHR) system, which has been in use by UAB Hospital and Health System. However, the University of Alabama System, including UAB Medicine, is in the process of transitioning to the Epic EHR system. This transition was approved in September 2024, with full implementation across all entities expected within two and a half years from that date. While the myUABMedicine portal is currently tied to Cerner, it is anticipated that the patient portal will transition to Epic's patient portal solution, commonly known as MyChart, as the Epic EHR is fully implemented. It's worth noting that some specific areas within UAB, such as the UAB Callahan Eye Hospital Clinic, have also been associated with other portals like nextmd.com, and the Healow platform has been mentioned in relation to a mobile app and potential use in some clinics, which is typically associated with the eClinicalWorks EHR system. However, myUABMedicine is presented as the primary patient portal for UAB Medicine overall.
