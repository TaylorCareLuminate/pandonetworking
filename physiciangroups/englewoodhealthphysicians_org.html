About the Practice

Englewood Health Physician Network, also known as Medical Associates of Englewood PC, is a nonprofit organization and a central component of Englewood Health. It offers primary care and a broad range of specialty services, including bariatric surgery, behavioral health, cancer care, cardiology, and more, to residents of northern New Jersey and beyond. [2, 5, 10] With over 500 providers practicing at more than 100 convenient locations, the network is committed to providing compassionate, high-quality care using state-of-the-art clinical programs, advanced treatments, and diagnostic services, all supported by a unified electronic health record system for seamless patient information integration. [2, 5, 10, 12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Englewood Health Physician Network utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a secure online platform that allows patients to request appointments, view test results, send messages to their doctor's office, and access summaries from their visits. The transition to MyChart occurred as practices joined the Englewood Health Physician Network, and the network emphasizes that being connected on one electronic health record system allows for better-coordinated care among providers within the network and Englewood Hospital. MyChart is the patient portal commonly associated with the Epic electronic health record system.
