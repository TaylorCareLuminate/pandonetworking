About the Practice

Columbia University Irving Medical Center (CUIMC), affiliated with Columbia University, is a 738-bed academic medical center located in northern Manhattan and with locations throughout the New York metropolitan area. [1, 10] It provides comprehensive emergency, primary, and specialty care in virtually every field of medicine, offering services in over 230 specialties and subspecialties. [1, 3, 4] CUIMC is recognized as a leader in medical education, groundbreaking research, and innovative patient care, dedicated to delivering high-quality, compassionate care to patients. [1, 3, 4]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Columbia University Irving Medical Center utilizes the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. The patient portal associated with this EHR is called Connect. Prior to February 2020, Columbia University Medical Center had been using Allscripts Enterprise EHR in their outpatient practices, which included an online personal health record service called iHealth. However, Columbia University Irving Medical Center, NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital, and Weill Cornell Medicine jointly transitioned to the Epic EHR system and the Connect patient portal on February 1, 2020. The Connect portal allows patients to manage appointments, communicate with providers, access test results, request prescription refills, view and pay bills, and more. This is a unified portal for Columbia University Irving Medical Center, NewYork-Presbyterian, and Weill Cornell Medicine. There is also a mobile application available called Columbia Connect App. Patients who previously used the FollowMyHealth myColumbiaDoctors portal can still access their old information, but new health information and communication with providers are now done through Connect.
