About the Practice

Olean General Hospital is a 186-bed acute care community hospital located in Olean, New York, part of the Upper Allegheny Health System and a member of Kaleida Health, providing comprehensive care to southwestern New York and northwestern Pennsylvania. [2, 3, 4, 8, 10, 13] It offers a wide range of services including cancer care (affiliated with Roswell Park Comprehensive Cancer Center), cardiac and vascular care with interventional cardiac catheterization, OB/GYN, general surgery, urology, women's health, internal medicine, pediatrics, sleep medicine, psychiatric care, substance abuse counseling, orthopedic, neurology, 24/7 emergency services, inpatient and outpatient rehabilitation, wound care (as an accredited Center for Wound Healing and Hyperbaric Medicine), dialysis, dental services, nutrition counseling, laboratory and radiology services, physical therapy, pain management, sports medicine, spine care, and maternity services (as a Level 1 Perinatal Center and state-designated Stroke Center). [2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Olean General Hospital (OGH) utilizes a patient portal branded as "YourHealth" for accessing hospital-based medical records. This portal is also used by Bradford Regional Medical Center (BRMC) and Cuba Memorial Hospital, which are part of the Upper Allegheny Health System (UAHS) along with OGH. UAHS is part of the larger Kaleida Health network. Kaleida Health has implemented a new electronic medical record (EHR) system from Cerner. The patient portal used by Kaleida Health is the MyKaleida Patient Portal, which is associated with the HealtheLife mobile app, a patient portal product offered by Cerner. Based on the information available, it is highly probable that the "YourHealth" patient portal used by Olean General Hospital for hospital records is powered by or integrated with Cerner's patient portal technology, likely HealtheLife, as part of the larger Kaleida Health system's EHR implementation. While "YourHealth" is the specific branding for OGH and BRMC hospital access, the underlying EHR vendor for the network is Cerner, and their patient portal offering is HealtheLife.
