About the Practice

Texas Health Resources is a faith-based, nonprofit health system serving North Texas. It provides a comprehensive range of inpatient and outpatient care across its network of facilities, which include Texas Health Presbyterian, Texas Health Arlington Memorial, Texas Health Harris Methodist, and Texas Health Huguley hospitals, as well as the Texas Health Physicians Group and various outpatient centers. Specialties and care provided cover acute care, short-stay care, behavioral health, rehabilitation, transitional care, primary care, numerous medical and surgical specialties, emergency care, imaging, home health, preventive services, and fitness programs, aiming to improve the health of the communities they serve across all stages of life.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Texas Health Resources utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is tied to the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. While one source refers to their electronic health record as "CareConnect One," other information confirms that Epic is their EHR vendor, noting interoperability with other organizations also using Epic. MyChart is the patient portal commonly associated with Epic's EHR system.
