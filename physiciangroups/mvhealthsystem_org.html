About the Practice

Mohawk Valley Health System (MVHS) is a non-profit integrated delivery system serving a multi-county area of central New York, including Oneida, Herkimer, and Madison counties. It is the parent company of the Wynn Hospital, MVHS Rehabilitation and Nursing Center (RNC), and Visiting Nurse Association of Utica and Oneida County, Inc. MVHS provides a comprehensive range of primary and specialty care services, including acute care (with 373 acute care beds at the Wynn Hospital), long-term care (162 beds at MVHS RNC), emergency services, cardiac services, surgical services (general, cardiothoracic, vascular, orthopedic, neurological, plastic and hand), maternity services (including a Level II NICU), pediatrics, diagnostic imaging, laboratory services, oncology, behavioral health, critical care, pulmonary critical care, urology, advanced endoscopy, wound care, bariatric services, comprehensive stroke care (Level III Trauma Center), immune health, and specialty pharmacy services. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Mohawk Valley Health System (MVHS) utilizes the MyChart patient portal, which is a product of Epic Systems. MVHS transitioned to the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system, going live across the enterprise on June 29, 2019. This move integrated their hospitals and physician practices onto a single platform. MyChart provides patients with secure online access to their electronic medical record, allowing them to view health information such as lab results, appointment details, medications, and immunizations. Patients can also use MyChart to communicate with their providers, request prescription renewals, and schedule appointments. The integration of MyChart with Epic allows for enhanced data sharing and care coordination within the MVHS system and with other healthcare organizations that also use MyChart through a feature called Happy Together.
