About the Practice

Franciscan Health is a Catholic healthcare system operating as Franciscan Alliance, Inc., with facilities in Indiana and Illinois. [2, 3, 7] It provides a broad continuum of faith-based, integrated healthcare services, including hospitals, clinics, home health, and physician services. [3, 5, 7] Their care focuses on the whole individual, offering comprehensive medical options for the mind, body, and spirit. [5, 20] Specialties and services include primary care, urgent care, emergency services, behavioral health, and a wide range of specialty care such as cardiology, neurology, cancer care, orthopedics, gynecologic surgery, diabetes care, audiology, ENT, general surgery, urology, nephrology, oncology/hematology, obstetrics, gynecology, and sleep medicine. [3, 8, 16, 20, 21]
