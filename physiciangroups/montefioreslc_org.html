About the Practice

Montefiore St. Luke's Cornwall (MSLC) is a not-for-profit hospital with campuses in Newburgh and Cornwall, NY, dedicated to serving the healthcare needs of the Hudson Valley. [6, 7] As a member of the Montefiore Health System, MSLC provides compassionate and comprehensive healthcare services, including a range of medical specialties such as neonatology, cardiac catheterization, cancer care, rehabilitation services (PT/OT/Speech), primary care, palliative medicine, general surgery, pain management, endocrinology, occupational health, comprehensive weight loss programs, memory disorder assessment and treatment, critical care, and wound healing. [6, 11, 10, 20] The hospital is accredited by The Joint Commission and is recognized for nursing excellence with Magnet designation. [6, 20]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Montefiore St. Luke's Cornwall is currently migrating to MyChart for its patient portal, which is a service typically associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. While a previous "MSLC Patient Portal" is still accessible for past records, the hospital is in the process of transitioning data to MyChart. This indicates that Montefiore St. Luke's Cornwall is adopting or has adopted Epic as their EHR system.
