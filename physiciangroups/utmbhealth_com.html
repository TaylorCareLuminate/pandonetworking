About the Practice

UTMB Health is a major academic health sciences center and a comprehensive health system that has been serving Texas for over 130 years. It includes schools of Medicine, Nursing, Health Professions, Public and Population Health, and Graduate Biomedical Sciences, and operates a world-renowned research enterprise. The health system provides a full range of primary and specialized medical care, including advanced and complex care, through hospitals on four main campuses and an extensive network of over 90 primary and specialty care clinics across Southeast Texas.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UTMB Health utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is a confidential and secure online tool providing patients with access to their medical records, including lab results, immunization history, appointments, and billing information. MyChart is associated with the electronic health record (EHR) vendor Epic. UTMB Health completed the implementation of the Epic EMR system in 2008 and further integrated Epic's Patient Access and Revenue Cycle application modules in 2013. Access to Epic, UTMB's electronic medical record system, is also facilitated through tools like Citrix. Additionally, UTMB Health participates in Care Everywhere, an Epic feature designed for the secure sharing of patient records between healthcare providers. Based on the use of MyChart and explicit mentions of Epic as their EMR system, UTMB Health is using Epic as its EHR vendor.
