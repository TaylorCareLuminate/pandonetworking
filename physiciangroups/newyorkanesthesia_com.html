About the Practice

New York Anesthesia, PC is comprised of a group of board-certified anesthesiologists who provide the highest quality anesthesia care and management in the New York City/Long Island region. Established more than 30 years ago, they provide over 25,000 anesthetic cases annually and have a strong reputation for safety, dependability, and experience. They offer a range of anesthesia services including General Anesthesia, Regional Anesthesia/Nerve Blocks, Conscious Sedation, IV Sedation, Spinal Anesthesia, Epidural Anesthesia, and Monitored Anesthesia Care (MAC) for various medical and dental specialties.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Anesthesia Group, which operates under the name Greater New York Anesthesia Services (GNYAS) at newyorkanesthesia.com, is a strategic partnership between Mount Sinai Health System and US Anesthesia Partners. While the website newyorkanesthesia.com does not directly name its patient portal vendor, its close affiliation with Mount Sinai Health System provides a strong indication. Mount Sinai Health System utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal product developed by Epic Systems. Several search results confirm that Mount Sinai Health System uses Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) system. Given the strategic partnership between GNYAS and Mount Sinai Health System, it is highly probable that GNYAS leverages Mount Sinai's existing patient portal infrastructure. Therefore, Anesthesia Group (newyorkanesthesia.com) is likely using the **MyChart** patient portal, which is integrated with the **Epic** EHR system used by Mount Sinai Health System. While US Anesthesia Partners, the other partner in GNYAS, does utilize Elevated Practice Solutions (EPS) for business support services, including revenue cycle management, and a service powered by Cedar for online bill viewing and payment, the patient-facing portal for accessing medical information and potentially other functionalities is most likely provided through the Mount Sinai MyChart system due to the direct partnership and Mount Sinai's established use of MyChart/Epic.
