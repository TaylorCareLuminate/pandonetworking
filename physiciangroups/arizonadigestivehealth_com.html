About the Practice

Arizona Digestive Health is a gastroenterology practice providing personalized GI care in Arizona. As a partner of GI Alliance, the group offers diagnosis and treatment for diseases and conditions of the digestive system, liver, pancreas, and gallbladder. Services include consultations, endoscopic procedures such as colonoscopy and upper endoscopy, and diagnostic tests like manometry. [1, 3, 10, 13]

Methodology & Findings

## Arizona Digestive Health Leverages Modernizing Medicine's gGastro EHR Arizona Digestive Health, a partner of the national GI Alliance, utilizes Modernizing Medicine Gastroenterology, formerly known as gMed, as its electronic health record (EHR) system. GI Alliance standardized on Modernizing Medicine's gGastro software suite in 2020 to create a unified EHR platform across its affiliated practices. While Arizona Digestive Health refers to its online patient access simply as the "Arizona Digestive Health Patient Portal," the underlying EHR system points to Modernizing Medicine's gGastro. Modernizing Medicine offers a patient portal solution called gPortal, which is likely integrated with the gGastro EHR used by Arizona Digestive Health through its affiliation with GI Alliance. Additionally, Arizona Digestive Health uses the InstaMed Patient Portal specifically for online bill payment services. InstaMed is a payment processing platform that integrates with various EHR and practice management systems, including reportedly with Epic's MyChart, though its integration capabilities extend to other systems as well. This indicates that InstaMed handles the financial transaction aspect of the patient portal experience for Arizona Digestive Health, working in conjunction with their primary patient portal powered by the gGastro EHR. Recent information also highlights GI Alliance's partnership with Ambience Healthcare for AI documentation tools that integrate with major EMRs, with Arizona Digestive Health specifically mentioned as benefiting from this integration. This further supports the conclusion that Arizona Digestive Health is operating on an EMR system that allows for such integrations, consistent with the capabilities of Modernizing Medicine's gGastro.
