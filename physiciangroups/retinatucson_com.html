About the Practice

Retina Associates specializes in diseases and surgery of the retina and vitreous, offering diagnosis and medical and surgical treatment for conditions such as macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, retinal vascular disease, retinal detachment, trauma, and eye tumors. [1, 2, 4, 7, 12] They provide state-of-the-art compassionate care and are actively involved in physician education and clinical research to advance the understanding and treatment of retinal diseases. [1, 13]
