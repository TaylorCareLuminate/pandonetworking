About the Practice

Kaiser Permanente is one of the nation's largest multispecialty medical groups, providing high-quality, affordable health care services to improve the health of its members and communities. [6] With a vast network of physicians and specialists across over 60 specialties, including Cardiology, Oncology, and Mental Health, they offer comprehensive primary and specialty care. [1, 4, 10] They emphasize coordinated, personalized, and evidence-based care, utilizing the latest technologies and techniques to achieve better health outcomes and provide a seamless, patient-centered experience through collaboration among their medical teams. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Kaiser Permanente utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a patient engagement platform licensed from Epic Systems Corporation. This aligns with information indicating that Kaiser Permanente primarily uses Epic Systems as their comprehensive electronic health record (EHR) system. Kaiser Permanente's implementation of an EHR system, particularly Epic Systems, aims to streamline patient care, increase data accessibility, and enhance operational efficiency. Their comprehensive EHR system, known as Kaiser Permanente HealthConnect®, integrates various aspects of care, including pharmacy and lab services, appointments, registration, and billing, and is based on Epic Systems.
