About the Practice

RBK Pediatrics is a physician-owned and managed independent pediatric practice on Long Island that has been providing comprehensive primary and urgent care for children from birth through college years for over 65 years. They offer a range of services including well child visits, sick child visits, urgent care open seven days a week, chronic care management, on-site laboratory procedures, and minor surgical procedures. The practice emphasizes accessible, high-quality care with a focus on building lifelong relationships with families.
