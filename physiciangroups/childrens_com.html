About the Practice

Children's Health is a leading pediatric healthcare system in North Texas, dedicated to making life better for children. Through its affiliation with UT Southwestern, it provides high-quality patient care, innovative treatments, and groundbreaking research for a wide range of pediatric injuries, health issues, and diseases. The system includes Children's Medical Center Dallas, recognized as a top children's hospital nationally and home to the only pediatric Level I Trauma Center in North Texas, as well as Children's Medical Center Plano and numerous specialty centers and primary care practices.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Children's Health (childrens.com) uses **MyChart** as their patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal developed by **Epic Systems**, which is also the vendor for Children's Health's electronic health record (EHR) system. The Children's Health patient portal is described as a secure online tool powered by MyChart, providing 24/7 access to a child's health information. Through MyChart, parents and legal guardians can access features such as viewing upcoming appointments, completing pre-check-in tasks, reviewing test results, managing medical records, requesting prescription renewals, and communicating securely with their child's care team. The integration of MyChart with the Epic EHR system allows for a comprehensive digital dashboard of a child's healthcare needs. Children's Health has been noted for its innovative use of Epic, their electronic health record.
