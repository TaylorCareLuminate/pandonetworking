About the Practice

University of Colorado Medicine (CU Medicine) is the region's largest and most comprehensive multispecialty physician group practice. It provides business operations, revenue cycle, and administrative services to support over 4,000 University of Colorado School of Medicine physicians and advanced practice providers. CU Medicine offers expert, compassionate, and comprehensive healthcare across hundreds of specialties at primary and specialty care clinics and affiliated hospitals, focusing on high-quality, personalized care and medical innovation.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

CU Medicine utilizes a patient portal branded as "My Health Connection". This portal is also used by UCHealth, an affiliated healthcare system. My Health Connection is integrated with and supported by the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Several sources indicate that UCHealth uses the Epic EHR, and that My Health Connection functions as the patient portal for this system. A report on CU Medicine leveraging a third-party platform also mentions integrating with their instance of the Epic EHR, further supporting the use of Epic by CU Medicine. Therefore, similar to how Epic uses MyChart, CU Medicine uses My Health Connection as its patient portal, which is built on the Epic EHR platform.
