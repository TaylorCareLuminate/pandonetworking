About the Practice

ODA Primary Health Care Network is a not-for-profit, federally qualified health center providing comprehensive, patient-centered primary and specialty healthcare services to medically underserved populations in Brooklyn and the Catskills. Founded on the premise that everyone deserves high-quality health care, ODA offers a wide range of services including primary care, specialty care (such as pediatrics, internal medicine, OB/GYN, cardiology, endocrinology, podiatry, dermatology, and more), dental services, behavioral health care, and urgent care. Services are available to all regardless of ability to pay, with sliding scale fees offered based on income and family size. [3, 6, 12, 14, 15]
