About the Practice

Genesis OBGYN is a premier OB/GYN group serving women across Arizona with numerous office locations. They provide a full spectrum of personalized women's healthcare services throughout all stages of life, specializing in obstetrics, gynecology, maternal-fetal medicine, and well-woman care. Services include prenatal care and delivery, high-risk pregnancy expertise, family planning, management of menstrual difficulties and menopause, gynecological surgery including robotic surgery, mammography, ultrasounds, and preventative screenings. They are part of the Unified Women's Health network.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Genesis OBGYN locations affiliated with Privia Health are utilizing the myPrivia patient portal, which is integrated with the athenahealth Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. Several search results confirm that Genesis Obstetrics & Gynecology in locations such as Rockwall, Texas, are part of Privia Health and direct patients to the myPrivia portal. Privia Health, a national physician enablement company, explicitly partners with athenahealth, utilizing athenahealth's medical record, patient engagement, and revenue cycle services. Privia Health also builds its own applications and workflows on the athenahealth platform, indicating a close integration between myPrivia and the athenahealth EHR. Furthermore, third-party integrations with Privia practices, such as Medsien, are noted to seamlessly integrate with Athenahealth. Based on this information, Genesis OBGYN practices that are part of the Privia Health network and use the myPrivia patient portal are using athenahealth as their underlying EHR system.
