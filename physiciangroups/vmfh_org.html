About the Practice

Virginia Mason Medical Center is an integrated hospital, training, and research facility in Seattle, Washington, and is part of Virginia Mason Franciscan Health, one of the largest health care systems in the Seattle-Tacoma area. The system includes 10 hospitals and nearly 300 care sites providing expert, compassionate medical care. Services include primary and specialty care such as cancer care, cardiology, emergency services, orthopedics, sports medicine, and specialized care for conditions like HIV/AIDS through Bailey-Boushay House. The system also incorporates the Benaroya Research Institute and the Virginia Mason Institute, which focuses on training in quality improvement.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Virginia Mason Medical Center (vmfh.org) uses a patient portal called MyVirginiaMason. This portal allows patients to manage appointments, view test results, message care teams, request prescription renewals, and access summaries of their medical information. MyVirginiaMason is accessible via a web browser and also through the HealtheLife mobile application by Cerner Corporation. Additionally, a third-party site that helps patients access their medical records lists the Virginia Mason Medical Center patient portal as using "authorization.cerner.com" for login and specifies "Software: Cerner". This indicates that the electronic health record (EHR) system underpinning the MyVirginiaMason patient portal at Virginia Mason Medical Center is likely provided by Cerner.
