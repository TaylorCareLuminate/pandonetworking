About the Practice

Carondelet St. Joseph's Hospital is a private, for-profit, acute-care hospital in Tucson, Arizona, and is designated as a Level 1 Trauma Center. [3, 4] It offers a wide range of specialized care, including Neurology, Neurosurgery, Neuro Endovascular, Cardiovascular, Orthopedics, Ophthalmology, Maternity care, Urology, Gynecology, Neonatal Intensive Care, Neuro Critical Care, Outpatient Rehabilitation, Emergency Medicine, and Bariatrics. [3, 4, 9] The hospital is part of the Carondelet Health Network.
