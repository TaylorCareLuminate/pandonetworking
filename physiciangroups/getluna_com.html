About the Practice

Luna Physical Therapy is an in-home outpatient physical therapy platform that provides personalized 1:1 care in the patient's home. [1, 4, 15] They offer services for a wide range of conditions and specialties, including orthopedic conditions, sports injuries, post-surgical rehabilitation, geriatric care, neurological rehabilitation, vestibular care, and women's health and pelvic floor therapy. [14, 15] Care is delivered by licensed therapists during 45-55 minute sessions, often supplemented by in-app exercises and communication between appointments. [4, 5, 15, 17] Luna accepts most major commercial insurance plans and Medicare. [4, 5, 15]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Based on the available information, Luna Physical Therapy (getluna.com) utilizes its own branded technology platform, referred to as the "Luna app" and an "intuitive online portal," for patient interactions. This integrated platform serves the function of a patient portal, allowing patients to schedule appointments, communicate with their therapists, monitor their progress, and access care plans and prescribed exercises. Unlike systems such as Epic using "MyChart," Luna's patient portal functionality appears to be a proprietary component of their overall service and technology, rather than a distinct third-party branded portal. While Luna's platform includes features like "Electronic Health Record Integration" and handles the maintenance of an EMR for their therapists, the specific underlying EHR vendor that Luna uses is not explicitly named in the search results.
