About the Practice

Essen Health Care, including Essen Medical Associates PC, is a community-focused healthcare provider with numerous locations across New York City, primarily in the Bronx. [5, 6] They offer a comprehensive range of services including primary care, urgent care, specialty care (covering over 40 specialty areas such as Internal Medicine, Cardiology, Gastroenterology, etc.), pediatric care, house calls, and virtual care. [4, 5, 6, 8] Additionally, they provide care coordination services through their Health Home program, nursing home care, employer health services, and hospitalist services, focusing on delivering high-quality, accessible, and integrated care to the underserved community. [4, 5, 9, 10, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Essen Medical Associates PC, also known as Essen Health Care, utilizes a patient portal branded as "MyEssen". This patient portal allows patients to access their medical records, request appointments and refills, and communicate with their doctor's office. The underlying electronic health record (EHR) system used by Essen Medical Associates is eClinicalWorks. The patient portal technology associated with eClinicalWorks is commonly known as healow (Health Online). Patients can access the portal through the Essen Health Care website or by using the healow app. This connection is similar to how MyChart is the patient portal branding for healthcare providers using the Epic EHR system.
