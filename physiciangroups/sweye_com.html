About the Practice

Established in 1982 by Dr. Lothaire Bluth in Mesa, Arizona, Southwestern Eye Center provides comprehensive optometry and ophthalmology services across Arizona and New Mexico. The experienced team offers a wide range of care including general eye exams, cataract surgery, LASIK, and treatment for conditions such as glaucoma, macular degeneration, cornea and retina diseases, and dry eyes. They also offer subspecialty procedures in areas like vitreoretinal disease, oculoplastics, refractive procedures, low vision aids, and ocular prosthetics, emphasizing high-quality care and advanced technology.
