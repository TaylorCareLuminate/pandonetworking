About the Practice

Ryan Health, formerly The William F. Ryan Community Health Network, is a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) based in Manhattan that has been providing high-quality, comprehensive and affordable primary and specialty care to New York's diverse and underserved communities since 1967. [2, 9] They offer a wide array of services including primary care, pediatrics, women's health, gender-affirming care, dental care, eye care, emotional wellness, HIV/AIDS prevention and care, opioid addiction treatment, sexual and reproductive health, school-based health services, and various specialty care services such as Cardiology, Dermatology, Gastroenterology, Nutrition, and Radiology, serving nearly 50,000 patients annually. [4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Ryan Health utilizes a patient portal branded as MyCare Portal. This portal allows patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare online, including requesting appointments, viewing lab results, and refilling prescriptions. The electronic health record (EHR) vendor that powers Ryan Health's MyCare Portal is eClinicalWorks. A press release from 2009 announced that the William F. Ryan Community Health Network, which is the formal name for Ryan Health, had selected eClinicalWorks' unified electronic medical records and practice management solution. Furthermore, information about Ryan Health's MyCare Portal mentions the availability of the Healow mobile app for accessing health information, and Healow is the patient portal offered by eClinicalWorks. This indicates a direct link between Ryan Health's MyCare Portal and the eClinicalWorks EHR system.
