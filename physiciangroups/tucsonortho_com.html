About the Practice

Tucson Orthopaedic Institute is Southern Arizona's largest and most advanced multi-specialty orthopaedic group, providing comprehensive orthopaedic care since 1994. [3, 6, 14] Their services include surgical and non-surgical treatments, fracture care, imaging, and physical therapy across multiple locations. [3, 10] They offer specialized care for conditions affecting the elbow, hand, shoulder, foot & ankle, spine, and provide services in pediatric orthopaedics, sports medicine, physiatry & EMG, regenerative medicine, and total joint replacement. [3, 6] An After Hours Clinic is also available for acute orthopaedic injuries. [5]

Methodology & Findings

Tucson Orthopaedic Institute utilizes the ModMed Patient Portal for their patients. This patient portal is also referred to as "MyHealth Patient Portal" and accessible through a URL containing "ema.md", which is consistent with ModMed's electronic health record (EHR) system, EMA, designed for orthopaedics. The portal allows patients secure online access to their personal health information, enabling them to schedule non-urgent appointments, access medical records, and request referrals. They can also use the portal for online bill payment.
