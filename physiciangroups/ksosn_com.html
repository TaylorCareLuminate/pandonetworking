About the Practice

Kidney Specialists of Southern Nevada (KSOSN) is a leading nephrology practice providing comprehensive kidney care services across greater Las Vegas, Henderson, and Pahrump. They offer diagnosis, management, and treatment for kidney diseases, including chronic kidney disease support programs, vascular access procedures at their surgical center, and collaboration with dialysis and transplant centers. Their team of physicians, nurse practitioners, nurses, medical assistants, and patient care coordinators are dedicated to patient education and providing resources to slow disease progression and improve quality of life.
