About the Practice

Arizona Mountain Orthopedics offers state-of-the-art comprehensive orthopedic care to Arizona's White Mountain communities, with over 35 years of experience. [3, 12] They specialize in advanced hip, knee, and shoulder surgeries, and provide a variety of diagnostic, surgical, and non-surgical treatments for a wide range of musculoskeletal conditions, including fractures, muscle, tendon, and ligament tears. [2, 3] The practice also includes a Doctor of Podiatric Medicine specializing in foot and ankle injuries. [2, 12] They are committed to patient recovery and education on diagnosis and treatment options. [3, 12]
