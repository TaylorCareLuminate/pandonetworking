About the Practice

Harbor-UCLA Medical Center is a public teaching hospital operated by the Los Angeles County Department of Health Services and affiliated with the David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA. It serves the southwestern Los Angeles County region, providing high-quality, cost-effective, patient-centered care. The hospital is a Level I Trauma Center and offers a comprehensive range of services including primary care, specialty care across numerous disciplines, mental health services, and emergency services, treating a diverse patient population regardless of ability to pay. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Harbor-UCLA Medical Center, as part of the Los Angeles County Department of Health Services (DHS), uses the **LA Health Portal** as its patient portal. This portal was referred to as the "MyWellness patient portal system" when it was initially launched. The LA Health Portal is connected to the county's enterprise-wide electronic health record (EHR) system called **ORCHID**. The ORCHID system, which stands for Online Real-time Centralized Health Information Database, was implemented across all DHS hospitals and outpatient sites. The EHR vendor responsible for creating the ORCHID system is **Cerner**. The LA Health Portal utilizes a Cerner Health account for identity confirmation.
