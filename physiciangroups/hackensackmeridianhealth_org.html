Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Hackensack Meridian Health utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is a product of Epic Systems. Through the MyChart online portal and mobile app, patients can access their medical information, view test results and doctor's notes, manage appointments, request prescription refills, and communicate securely with their healthcare providers. This indicates that Hackensack Meridian Health's electronic health record (EHR) system is provided by Epic Systems.
