About the Practice

Glens Falls Hospital, a member of the Albany Med Health System, is an award-winning not-for-profit community hospital serving patients across five counties in New York's Adirondack Mountains region, covering an area of 6,000 square miles. Its mission is to improve the health of people in the region by providing access to exceptional, affordable, and patient-centered care. The hospital offers a wide array of specialized medical and surgical services, with key centers on its campus including the C.R. Wood Cancer Center, the Joyce Stock Snuggery Childbirth Center, a Wound Healing Center, and the Juckett Family Cardiovascular Center. Other services include an Emergency Department, Behavioral Health Crisis Center, Breast Center, GI Center, Hearing Center, Neurodiagnostics, Orthopedics, Rehabilitation and Wellness Center, Stroke Center, and Outpatient Infusion Center.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Glens Falls Hospital, as part of the Albany Med Health System, utilizes the MyChart patient portal. This transition to MyChart took place on November 2, 2024, replacing previous portals such as My Health Record. MyChart is the patient portal system provided by Epic Systems, indicating that Glens Falls Hospital uses Epic as its electronic health record (EHR) vendor through its affiliation with Albany Med Health System. The MyChart portal allows patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare online, including requesting prescription refills, scheduling appointments, viewing lab results, and accessing medical records.
