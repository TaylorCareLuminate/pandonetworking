About the Practice

Physician Affiliate Group of New York, P.C. (PAGNY) is one of the largest multi-disciplinary physician groups in the country, partnering with NYC Health + Hospitals to provide accountable, responsive, quality, and patient-centered healthcare to diverse and vulnerable populations in New York City. They provide essential services including trauma and emergency medicine, as well as care in numerous specialties such as Allergy, Asthma, and Immunology, Anesthesiology, Emergency Medicine, Family Medicine, Internal Medicine, Obstetrics and Gynecology, Pediatrics, Psychiatry, Radiology, and Surgery. [3, 4, 9, 10] PAGNY is committed to addressing healthcare inequities, promoting provider wellness, and supporting research.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Physician Affiliate Group Of New York PC (PAGNY), through its affiliation with NYC Health + Hospitals, utilizes the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system and its associated patient portal, MyChart. NYC Health + Hospitals completed a system-wide transition to the Epic EMR system, branded as H2O (Health + Hospitals Online). This integrated system connects hospitals, emergency rooms, and outpatient services, providing a unified platform for medical records and financial information. The patient portal used in conjunction with the Epic system at NYC Health + Hospitals is called MyChart. MyChart offers patients secure online access to their health information, allowing them to view test results, send messages to their care team, request prescription refills, and manage appointments. Given PAGNY's role in providing services to NYC Health + Hospitals facilities, the physicians affiliated with PAGNY likely use the Epic EHR system and interact with patients through the MyChart patient portal.
