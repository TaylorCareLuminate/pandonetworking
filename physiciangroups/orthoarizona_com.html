About the Practice

OrthoArizona is a premier orthopedic group in Arizona providing comprehensive musculoskeletal care across numerous locations in the Phoenix metropolitan area. Founded in 1994, the group offers specialized services including orthopedic surgery, sports medicine, spine care, interventional pain management, joint replacement, physical therapy, hand therapy, podiatric surgery, pediatrics, trauma, and workers' compensation. They also provide convenient services such as InstantOrtho walk-in clinics for urgent orthopedic conditions, surgical centers, athletic performance programs, and advanced diagnostic imaging like X-ray and MRI.
