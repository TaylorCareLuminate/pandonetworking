About the Practice

MultiCare Health System is a not-for-profit health care organization based in Tacoma, Washington, serving communities across Washington and Northern Idaho. [14, 18] MultiCare provides a comprehensive range of health care services including hospital care, primary care, urgent care through MultiCare Indigo Health, and a dedicated children's hospital and health network, Mary Bridge Children's Hospital & Health Network. [8, 9, 13, 14, 18] They offer numerous specialty services, such as behavioral health, heart and vascular care via Pulse Heart Institute, cancer care, orthopedics, neurology, digestive health, and more, catering to patients of all ages for needs ranging from routine health screenings to complex illness treatment. [7, 8, 9, 13, 18]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

MultiCare utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is integrated with their electronic health record (EHR) system, Epic. MultiCare's EHR system is referred to as MultiCare Connect, and it is based on an integrated Epic platform. MyChart provides MultiCare patients with secure online access to their personal health information, allowing them to manage appointments, view test results, request prescription refills, and communicate with their care team. MultiCare has been using Epic for its ambulatory care clinics since 1998 and implemented the integrated Epic platform across all outpatient clinics and hospitals. The health system has also integrated other solutions with its Epic system to enhance clinical workflows and decision support. While the majority of MyChart messages with providers remain free, MultiCare implemented a policy effective July 1, 2024, where certain messages requiring medical decision-making and more than five minutes of a provider's time may be billed to the patient or their insurance, similar to practices adopted by other health systems using Epic's MyChart. This change is estimated to affect a small percentage of messages.
