About the Practice

Highland Medical, P.C. offers a comprehensive range of medical specialties and services throughout Rockland County, New York. Specialties include Primary Care, Cardiology, Hematology/Oncology, Surgery, Pulmonology, Neurology, OBS-GYN, Breast Surgery, Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, Nephrology, Psychiatry, Rheumatology, and Skilled Nursing Facility Physicians. [1, 2, 4] They provide patient-centered care focused on early diagnosis, management of chronic conditions, and a team approach to treatment. [2, 4, 8, 13] Highland Medical is part of White Plains Medical Diagnostics PC, affiliated with White Plains Hospital and Montefiore Health System. [4]

Methodology & Findings

Highland Medical, P.C. utilizes MyChart as their patient portal. MyChart is the patient portal system developed by Epic Systems, a widely used electronic health record (EHR) vendor. A practice within Highland Medical PC explicitly directs patients to complete new patient forms on MyChart, further confirming its use. Based on the integration of MyChart, it can be concluded that Highland Medical, P.C. is using Epic as their EHR vendor.
