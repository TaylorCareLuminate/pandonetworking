About the Practice

New York Medical Associates PC (NYMA) was established in 1994 and provides comprehensive diagnostic and management services with a focus on clinical excellence and patient service. [7, 10] Their principal clinical services include Cardiology, Clinical Cardiac Electrophysiology, Internal Medicine, and Preventive Cardiology. [10, 15, 17] They aim to provide the best care in a modern and comfortable environment, emphasizing the internist's role in overall health coordination and utilizing technology like electronic medical records to improve efficiency and safety of care. [10, 15] They are dedicated to the prevention and management of cardiovascular diseases and offer state-of-the-art diagnostic testing. [17]
