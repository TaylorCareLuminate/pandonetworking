About the Practice

Canyon Vista Medical Center is a 100-bed acute care hospital located in Sierra Vista, Arizona, serving southeastern Arizona. [2, 7] It is a Level III Trauma Center, an Arizona Cardiac Receiving Center, and is accredited for Total Hip and Knee Replacement and recognized for Breast Health Excellence. [7] The medical center offers a wide range of services including emergency services, women's services, surgical services, a bone & joint institute, cardiac care, intensive care, behavioral health, diagnostic imaging, laboratory services, rehabilitation, and wound care. [4, 9]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Canyon Vista Medical Center in Sierra Vista, Arizona, utilizes a patient portal branded as "Health Records Online," which is also accessible via the "HealtheLife app." This patient portal is associated with the Cerner electronic health record (EHR) system. While some EHR vendors like Epic use patient portals with specific names such as MyChart, Canyon Vista Medical Center's patient portal points to a Cerner system. The medical center has been noted as using a Cerner Millennium system and has worked with Crossings Healthcare Solutions on implementing and enhancing its clinical solutions. Job postings for Canyon Vista Medical Center have also indicated the use of Cerner.
