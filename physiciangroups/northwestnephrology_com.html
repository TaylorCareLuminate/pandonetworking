About the Practice

Northwest Renal Clinic (NWRC) provides exceptional care for patients with kidney disease at their nine Oregon offices, seven outreach clinics, and local area hospitals. They provide comprehensive diagnostic, therapeutic, and preventive care for renal disease, including consultation for chronic kidney disease (CKD), hypertension treatment, dialysis management, transplantation consultation, vascular access management, and renal education. [3] Northwest Renal Clinic is the largest nephrology practice in Oregon and is dedicated to serving patients in the greater Northwest area. [3]
