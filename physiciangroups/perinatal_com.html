About the Practice

Phoenix Perinatal Associates, associated with perinatal.com and Obstetrix Medical Group, specializes in Maternal-Fetal Medicine (Perinatology) and Nursing, providing comprehensive care for high-risk pregnancies, utilizing advanced technology. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Phoenix Perinatal Associates, which is part of Obstetrix Medical Group and associated with the website perinatal.com, utilizes the NextGen Patient Portal for its patients. The NextGen Patient Portal is a tool that allows patients to communicate with their healthcare team, schedule appointments, access their medical information, make payments, and request medication renewals. Obstetrix Medical Group, the parent organization, indicates that they use the NextGen Patient Portal as one of their patient-facing tools. NextGen Healthcare is a vendor that provides electronic health record (EHR) systems and patient portals.
