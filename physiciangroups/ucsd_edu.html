About the Practice

The University of California San Diego is a public land-grant research university in San Diego, California, recognized for its very high research activity and encompassing institutions like the Scripps Institution of Oceanography. [8, 17] UC San Diego Health, its academic health system, serves as a referral center for complex, specialty care beyond the scope of most community hospitals. [9] Specialties include the region's only burn center, two Comprehensive Stroke Centers, Level I trauma care, pulmonary thromboendarterectomy (PTE), and nationally ranked care in areas such as cancer (as the region's only NCI-designated Comprehensive Cancer Center), heart disease, neurosurgery, and pulmonology. [9, 13, 16, 17, 21] The health system is dedicated to providing outstanding patient care through groundbreaking research and inspired teaching. [9, 20]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UC San Diego Health utilizes MyUCSDChart as its patient portal, which is provided by Epic Systems Corporation. MyUCSDChart allows patients to access their electronic medical records, schedule appointments, view test results, request prescription refills, message their healthcare providers, and pay medical bills. The MyUCSDChart portal is a key component of UC San Diego Health's electronic medical record (EMR) system, which is based on the Epic platform. This integration allows for secure access to patient information stored within UC San Diego Health's Epic data repository. UC San Diego Health also participates in health information exchanges, including Epic's Care Everywhere, to facilitate the secure sharing of patient information with other healthcare providers using Epic platforms. The MyUCSDHealth app provides mobile access to the MyUCSDChart portal features.
