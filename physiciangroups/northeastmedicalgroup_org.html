About the Practice

Northeast Medical Group is a not-for-profit multispecialty medical foundation established in 2010, affiliated with Yale New Haven Health System. It provides high-quality, personalized, and compassionate care through over 130 community practices. The group offers a full range of primary care services, including internal medicine and family medicine, as well as expertise in more than a dozen specialties such as pediatrics, cardiology, gastroenterology, podiatry, allergy and immunology, oncology, maternal fetal medicine, obstetrics and gynecology, neurology, orthopedics, pulmonary medicine, urology, urogynecology, and urgent care. Northeast Medical Group is dedicated to innovation and excellence in patient care, teaching, research, and service, leveraging the resources of the Yale New Haven Health system and its affiliated hospitals.

Methodology & Findings

Northeast Medical Group (northeastmedicalgroup.org) uses MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is associated with the EHR vendor Epic Systems Corporation. Through the MyChart portal, Northeast Medical Group patients can access various features, including viewing their health summary, medications, and test results, scheduling appointments, requesting prescription renewals, and managing billing information. MyChart provides secure online access to portions of a patient's electronic medical record.
