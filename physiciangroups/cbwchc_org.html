About the Practice

The Charles B. Wang Community Health Center (CBWCHC) is a non-profit and Federally Qualified Health Center in NYC, founded in 1971. [7] It offers high-quality, affordable, and culturally responsive comprehensive primary care and social services under one roof, including Internal Medicine, Pediatrics, OB/GYN, Dental Care, Primary Eye Care, and Mental Health. [1], [3] They focus on serving underserved communities, particularly Asian Americans, providing bilingual and bicultural healthcare regardless of language, immigration history, insurance status, or ability to pay. [1], [7]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Charles B. Wang Community Health Center (CBWCHC) utilizes the ezAccess Patient Portal, which is powered by ezAccess. This patient portal is integrated with the athenahealth electronic health record (EHR) system. The ezAccess Patient Portal is described as a comprehensive tool offering features such as appointment scheduling, prescription refill requests, secure communication with providers, and access to health information. It is designed to work with athenaPractice™, athenaFlow™, and athenaOne™, all components of athenahealth's EHR offerings. This indicates that CBWCHC uses athenahealth for their electronic health records.
