Methodology & Findings

Carolina Kidney Associates likely utilizes the MyChart patient portal, which is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. While the Carolina Kidney Associates website refers to a "PATIENT PORTAL" without specifying a vendor name, evidence from multiple sources strongly suggests the use of Epic's Acumen Epic Connect platform within nephrology practices, including those in North Carolina. One report specifically mentions Metrolina Nephrology Associates in North Carolina as a practice utilizing the Epic EHR configuration via Acumen Connect. Given Carolina Kidney Associates' position as a large independent nephrology practice in the North Carolina "triad" region and the noted use of Acumen Epic Connect with MyChart in the state's nephrology field, it is highly probable that Carolina Kidney Associates' patient portal is MyChart, linked to the Acumen Epic Connect EHR system.
