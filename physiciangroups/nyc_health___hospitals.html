About the Practice

NYC Health + Hospitals/Kings County is a municipal hospital located in the East Flatbush neighborhood of Brooklyn, New York City. It is a leading healthcare facility providing a wide range of health services and specialties in all fields of modern medicine, including a world-renowned Level 1 Trauma Center, Designated Stroke Center, Level III Perinatal Center, Designated AIDS Center, Parkinson's Disease Center of Excellence, Diabetes Education Center of Excellence, Behavioral Health Center, and Sexual Assault Forensic Examiner (SAFE) Program Center of Excellence. [2, 7, 9] The hospital offers comprehensive inpatient care, primary care for adults and children, prenatal, labor and delivery services, and a wide array of ambulatory care services through more than 200 clinics. [3, 9, 10] Its mission is to provide care to everyone regardless of their ability to pay. [9, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

NYC Health + Hospitals, including its Kings County location, utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. This aligns with the fact that MyChart is the patient portal solution provided by Epic Systems Corporation. NYC Health + Hospitals has undertaken a system-wide implementation of the Epic electronic medical records (EMR) system, which they refer to as H2O (Health + Hospitals Online). This comprehensive transition to the Epic platform, including the MyChart patient portal, was a major initiative intended to unify their medical records and was largely completed by 2020. NYC Health + Hospitals/Kings County is among the facilities listed as using the MyChart portal. Based on this information, NYC Health + Hospitals Kings County is using the Epic EHR system, with MyChart serving as its patient portal.
