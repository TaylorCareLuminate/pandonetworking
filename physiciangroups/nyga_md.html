About the Practice

New York Gastroenterology Associates (NYGA) is a premier independent gastroenterology practice in New York City with over 25 years of experience, specializing in digestive health. [12, 14] They provide comprehensive care and endoscopic services for a wide range of gastrointestinal conditions, including inflammatory bowel disease (IBD), colon cancer screening, neurogastroenterology, and hepatology. [13, 14] NYGA offers various procedures such as colonoscopy, upper GI endoscopy, and capsule endoscopy, with a focus on quality care, patient well-being, convenience, and active involvement in research to advance treatments. [2, 10, 14, 17]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Gastroenterology Associates LLP (nyga.md) is utilizing the MEDENT system, which functions as an all-in-one Electronic Health Record (EHR), Practice Management, and Patient Portal solution. Their patient portal is branded as the MEDENT Patient Portal. The MEDENT Patient Portal offers patients secure online access to aspects of their medical records and facilitates communication with their doctor's office. This integrated system allows practices to automate various functions and provides features such as accessing patient records, messaging, viewing lab results, and managing appointments. The MEDENT system is available as both a cloud-based service and a server-based system for medical practices.
