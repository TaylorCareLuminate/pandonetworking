About the Practice

Western Nephrology specializes in the diagnosis, treatment, and management of kidney diseases, offering comprehensive nephrology care. [2, 4, 6, 15] Their board-certified physicians treat a variety of conditions including kidney stones, chronic kidney disease (CKD), and end-stage renal disease (ESRD). [1, 4, 15] Care is provided at their clinics, dialysis centers, and rehabilitation centers, encompassing inpatient and outpatient dialysis services, acute dialysis, and hospital inpatient consultations. [1, 11, 14, 15] The practice is committed to providing a high level of patient-focused healthcare. [2]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Western Nephrology (westneph.com) appears to be utilizing Renvio as their Electronic Health Record (EHR) vendor. This is based on a testimonial from Dr. Michael Anger, President & Senior Partner at Western Nephrology, who is quoted in a description of Renvio's services. Renvio, which combines the capabilities of Renesan and Visonex, offers an intuitive EMR system called "Kidney Care Cloud". This platform includes tools designed to engage patients. While the specific branding or name of the patient portal for Western Nephrology is not explicitly stated in the search results in the same way that "MyChart" is associated with Epic, the patient portal functionality is likely integrated within or provided through the Renvio Kidney Care Cloud EMR system.
