About the Practice

Good Samaritan University Hospital, formerly known as Good Samaritan Hospital Medical Center, is a 537-bed non-profit teaching hospital in West Islip, New York, and a member of Catholic Health. It is recognized for providing high-quality, patient-focused care and is the only facility on Long Island to be designated a Comprehensive Stroke Center by both the Joint Commission and NYS Department of Health. The hospital offers a wide range of medical and surgical services, including a verified Level I Adult Trauma Center and Level II Pediatric Trauma Center on the south shore of Long Island, comprehensive cancer care through The Catholic Health Cancer Institute, advanced heart and vascular care via the St. Francis Heart Center, bariatric medicine, and comprehensive maternity services with a Level III NICU. Methodology & Findings

Good Samaritan Hospital Medical Center (goodsamaritan.chsli.org) utilizes the **MyChart** patient portal. MyChart is a widely used patient portal developed by **Epic Systems**. Good Samaritan Hospital is part of the Catholic Health system, and Catholic Health also provides access to MyChart for its patients, allowing for integrated health management across the system. Through the MyChart portal, patients of Good Samaritan Hospital can access their medical records, communicate with their doctors for non-urgent matters, request prescription refills, view test results, and schedule appointments.
