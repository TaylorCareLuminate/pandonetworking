About the Practice

OCLI Vision, which includes Hudson Valley Eye Associates in Hawthorne, is a multi-location ophthalmology practice providing comprehensive eye care services. Their specialties encompass primary vision care, LASIK, cataract surgery, glaucoma, diabetic eye care, corneal disease, retinal disease, oculoplastics, refractive surgery, and neuro-ophthalmology. The group is known for its world-renowned, highly skilled ophthalmologists and optometrists who utilize advanced techniques and technology to address a wide range of eye health concerns and offer various treatments.
