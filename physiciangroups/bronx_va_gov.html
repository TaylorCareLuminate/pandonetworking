About the Practice

The James J. Peters Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center, part of VA Bronx Health Care, is a comprehensive healthcare system and teaching hospital located in the Bronx, NY. It provides primary and specialty health services to Veterans in a three-county area, including amputation care, cardiology, mental health care, pain management, palliative and hospice care, and transplant surgery. The medical center is also recognized as a center for spinal cord injury, PTSD, comprehensive cancer care, Alzheimer's disease, schizophrenia, and alcohol abuse, with accredited research programs and affiliations with major medical schools. Methodology & Findings

The James J. Peters Veterans Affairs Medical Center in the Bronx (bronx.va.gov) primarily uses the My HealtheVet patient portal for veterans to manage their health online. This portal allows veterans to refill and track prescriptions, send secure messages to their health care team, schedule and manage appointments, download medical records, view lab and test results, and order hearing aid batteries and accessories. The EHR system historically used by the VA, including the James J. Peters VA Medical Center, is known as VistA, with CPRS (Computerized Patient Record System) being a key graphical user interface.
