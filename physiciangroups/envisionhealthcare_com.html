About the Practice

Envision Physician Services is a multispecialty physician group and healthcare management team that partners with hospitals and health systems across more than 900 healthcare facilities in 48 states to deliver integrated, patient-centered care. [3, 2] They provide high-quality care across a range of specialties including emergency medicine, hospital medicine, radiology, anesthesiology, women's and children's services, intensivist care, neonatology, pediatrics, primary care, teleradiology, urgent care, and surgical services. [1, 2, 3, 7, 9] More than 19 million patients depend on them annually across the spectrum of specialty care, from operating rooms to emergency departments to birthing suites. [2]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Envision Physician Services, a division of Envision Healthcare, does not operate a single, centralized patient portal or utilize a uniform electronic health record (EHR) system across all of its widespread operations. Instead, the patient portal and underlying EHR system used by a patient will depend on the specific hospital or health system where they received care from an Envision physician. For instance, search results indicate that at locations such as Saint Joseph Health System and Trinity Health Michigan, where Envision Physician Services provides radiology services, the MyChart Patient Portal is utilized. MyChart is the patient portal commonly associated with the Epic EHR system, suggesting that in these affiliated hospitals, Epic is likely the EHR in use. Job postings for Envision Physician Services also mention the use of Epic EMR at specific hospitals like UChicago Ingalls Memorial Hospital and Hackensack Meridian Health Palisades Medical Center. This further supports that Envision physicians utilize the EHR system implemented by their partner institutions. In addition to patient portals for accessing medical information, Envision Physician Services also uses a separate online portal specifically for viewing and paying medical bills. This billing portal is branded as "Welcome to Envision Physician Services" and is powered by Flywire. Therefore, patients seeking to access their medical records or interact with Envision Physician Services regarding their care should identify the hospital or health system where they were treated and access the patient portal provided by that institution. If the partner hospital or health system uses Epic, the patient portal will be MyChart. For billing inquiries, the dedicated Envision Physician Services billing portal is available.
