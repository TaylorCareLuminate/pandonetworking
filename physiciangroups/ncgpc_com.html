About the Practice

Nephrology Consultants of Georgia specializes in kidney care (Nephrology), providing exceptional, updated, and timely care for a wide range of acute and chronic kidney diseases. [6, 7, 9, 12] Services include treatment for Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD), Acute Kidney Injury (AKI), Hypertension (HTN), Proteinuria, Hematuria, Edema, Electrolyte Imbalance, Kidney Stones, Glomerulonephritis, Anemia, and Diabetic Kidney Disease. [7, 8] They offer various treatment options such as Hemodialysis, Peritoneal Dialysis, Home Dialysis, and Transplant Care, with a commitment to comprehensive, personalized care, patient education, and utilizing specialized testing for diagnosis and treatment. [7, 8, 12]

Methodology & Findings

Nephrology Consultants of Georgia PC (ncgpc.com) utilizes a patient portal branded as "My Chart Epic". This patient portal is associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Patients are directed to use "My Chart Epic" to access their health information and communicate with the nursing staff.
