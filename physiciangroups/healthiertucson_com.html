About the Practice

Northwest Medical Center Sahuarita is an 18-bed hospital, a 77,000 square-foot facility, offering a broad range of services including 24/7 emergency care, inpatient medical/surgical care, diagnostic imaging (CT, X-ray, ultrasound, echocardiogram), laboratory services, and primary care and multi-specialty clinics. Specialties include Cardiology (non-interventional), General Surgery, Orthopedics (including total joint replacements), Urogynecology, Gastroenterology, Podiatry, Gynecology, Family Medicine, and Internal Medicine.

Methodology & Findings

Northwest Medical Center Sahuarita, which is part of Northwest Healthcare, uses a patient portal called MyHealthHome. This patient portal allows patients to access their health information, view lab and radiology results, see care summaries, view discharge plans, and more. The MyHealthHome patient portal is associated with Oracle Health. You can access MyHealthHome through the Northwest Healthcare website or by downloading the MyHealthHome – Oracle Health mobile app. The information in the MyHealthHome portal comes from your medical records.
