About the Practice

Interfaith Medical Center is a full-service non-profit community teaching hospital located in Brooklyn, New York. It provides a wide range of primary and specialty care services, including emergency services, behavioral health, dental care, and chemical dependence programs. As a safety-net hospital, it serves the diverse communities of Central Brooklyn and is also involved in graduate medical education.
