About the Practice

Newark Beth Israel Medical Center, an RWJBarnabas Health facility, is a 653-bed quaternary care regional care teaching hospital providing comprehensive health care. It is home to one of the nation's top heart transplant centers, RWJBarnabas Health's Heart Failure Treatment and Transplant Program, New Jersey's only Advanced Lung Disease and Transplant Program, and a Structural Heart and Valve Center. The medical center also houses the Children's Hospital of New Jersey, offering over 30 specialized pediatric services, including the largest Valerie Fund Children's Center for Cancer and Blood Disorders and a Children's Heart Center. Methodology & Findings

Newark Beth Israel Medical Center, part of RWJBarnabas Health, utilizes a patient portal branded as RWJBH MyChart. This patient portal is connected to the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system, as RWJBarnabas Health is transitioning all of its hospitals and medical practices to Epic. MyChart provides patients with online access to their medical records, including test results, medications, and appointments, and allows for communication with their care team.
