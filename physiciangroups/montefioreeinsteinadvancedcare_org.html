About the Practice

Montefiore Einstein Orthopedic Surgery provides high-quality, comprehensive, and advanced musculoskeletal care across locations in the Bronx, Manhattan, Westchester, and Rockland Counties. [2, 6] Their specialized team offers care for all body ligaments, tendons, muscles, bones, and joints, covering areas such as General Orthopedics, Sports Medicine, Joint Replacement, Hand and Upper Extremity, Foot and Ankle, Spine Center, Rheumatology, and Pain Management. [6, 7, 12] They utilize advanced diagnostic testing and provide both non-surgical and surgical treatments, including robotic hip/knee replacement and minimally invasive procedures, aiming to relieve pain and restore function. [6, 7, 9, 15] As part of an academic health system affiliated with Albert Einstein College of Medicine, they are involved in research and education, and are recognized among the top in the nation for Orthopedics. [10, 12, 16]

Methodology & Findings

Montefiore Einstein Orthopedic Surgery, as part of the Montefiore Health System, appears to primarily use MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal licensed from Epic Systems Corporation. While a specific patient portal for "Montefiore Einstein Orthopedic Surgery" or "Montefiore Einstein Advanced Care" was not explicitly stated in every search result, multiple sources indicate that Montefiore Health System facilities, such as Montefiore Nyack Hospital and Montefiore St. Luke's Cornwall, utilize MyChart for patient access to health information. One search result explicitly links MyChart with the Epic logo and states it is licensed from Epic Systems Corporation. Another older document from 2013 mentions a Web portal (MyMontefiore.com) used by a Family Health Center within Montefiore, but more recent results consistently point to MyChart as the prevailing patient portal. An affiliate, Northeast Orthopedics and Sports Medicine, which is associated with Montefiore Einstein Orthopedic Surgery, mentions an online portal for completing patient forms. Based on the widespread use of MyChart across the Montefiore Health System and the direct association of MyChart with Epic, it is highly probable that Montefiore Einstein Orthopedic Surgery also uses MyChart as its patient portal, powered by the Epic EHR system.
