About the Practice

Samaritan Medical Practice PC is a medical group practice and a subordinate organization under Samaritan Medical Center, providing professional physician services to improve healthcare standards for residents of Jefferson County and other areas served by the hospital. The practice offers treatments in various medical specialty areas, including general surgery, vascular surgery, plastic/cosmetic surgery, orthopedic surgery, gastroenterology, otolaryngology-head and neck surgery, anesthesiology, pain medicine, and internal medicine.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Samaritan Medical Practice PC, as part of Samaritan Health, utilizes the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. The patient portal associated with this system is branded as both MySamaritanHealth and MyChart. MySamaritanHealth.com serves as a secure online platform for patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare. Users can log in to view test results, schedule appointments, message their doctors, and more through this portal. It is explicitly stated that as of September 12, 2020, patient records for Samaritan Hospital and Clinics are managed within EPIC's MyChart system. MyChart is described as the electronic patient record, accessible through a partnership with Providence St. Joseph Health. Additionally, Samaritan's use of Epic extends to providing external providers with access to patient information through EpicCare Link.
