About the Practice

Henry J. Carter Specialty Hospital and Nursing Facility, also known as NYC Health + Hospitals/Carter, is a long-term acute care hospital (LTACH) and skilled nursing facility (SNF) located in East Harlem, New York City, with a total of 365 beds. [2, 3, 9] It provides 24/7 specialized clinical support and care, including services from pulmonologists, infectious disease specialists, and cardiologists. [9] The facility is recognized for its expertise in ventilator weaning and offers comprehensive long-term acute care and skilled nursing services for medically complex patients. [3, 7, 9]
