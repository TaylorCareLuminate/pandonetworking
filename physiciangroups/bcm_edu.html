About the Practice

Baylor College of Medicine (BCM) is a private health sciences university located in Houston, Texas, with a branch campus in Temple, Texas. [9, 12] It comprises four schools: the School of Medicine, the Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences, the School of Health Professions, and the National School of Tropical Medicine. [9] BCM is dedicated to improving health through science, scholarship, and innovation, with missions in research, patient care, education, and community service. [8] It provides comprehensive, compassionate, innovative, and evidence-based patient care, including primary care and a wide range of medical and surgical specialties. [10, 11, 14, 16] BCM is a nationally recognized site for cutting-edge biomedical research, translating discoveries into improved patient care. [14]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Baylor College of Medicine (bcm.edu) utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal developed by Epic Systems Corporation. The MyChart portal provides patients with secure online access to their medical records, allowing them to manage their health information, schedule appointments, view test results, request prescription refills, and communicate with their healthcare team. Baylor Medicine also uses EpicCare Link, a web-based application from Epic, to allow external healthcare partners to access patient charts and information within Baylor Medicine's electronic health record system.
