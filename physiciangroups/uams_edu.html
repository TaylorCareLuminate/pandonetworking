About the Practice

The University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) is Arkansas' only health sciences university and academic health center, serving as a statewide health system dedicated to improving the health and well-being of Arkansans through integrated programs in health care, education, and research. It provides high-quality, innovative, patient- and family-centered health care and specialty expertise, including the state's only adult Level 1 Trauma Center, the only adult kidney, liver, and pancreas transplant programs, comprehensive cancer care through the Winthrop P. Rockefeller Cancer Institute and Proton Center of Arkansas, and specialized clinics for conditions like adult sickle cell, spina bifida, cystic fibrosis, and ALS. [3, 5, 6, 7, 11, 13] UAMS is also a key educational institution with colleges covering medicine, nursing, pharmacy, health professions, and public health, alongside a graduate school, training the next generation of healthcare professionals. [4, 9, 11] Through its main campus in Little Rock, a regional campus in Northwest Arkansas, and a network of clinics statewide, UAMS delivers both routine and highly specialized care across numerous medical disciplines. [3, 4, 11, 12, 15, 17, 18]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) utilizes the MyChart patient portal, which is connected to the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. UAMS has been using Epic for its medical center and clinics for several years, and in 2020, converted its regional campuses to the Epic platform as well. MyChart provides patients with online access to their health information, allowing them to view test results, message healthcare providers, schedule appointments, and request prescription refills. The patient portal can be accessed through the login domain mychart.uamshealth.com. This aligns with the common practice of healthcare organizations using Epic's MyChart as their patient portal.
