About the Practice

Nathan Littauer Hospital and Nursing Home is a full-service, 74-bed acute care hospital with an 84-bed skilled nursing home. [2] The network includes the hospital, nursing home, and a network of outpatient primary/specialty care centers, an education and wellness center, and an outpatient surgery center. [6] It offers a wide range of medical and surgical services and specialties, including Adult Medicine, Cardiology, Emergency Care, Family Practice, General Surgery, OB/GYN, Pediatrics, and Quick N Sick urgent care, among many others, and is committed to providing safe, high-quality health and wellness services to its communities. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Nathan Littauer Hospital (nlh.org) utilizes a patient portal branded as "The Nathan Littauer Patient Portal," which is accessed via the MHealth mobile app. This patient portal is provided by MEDITECH, and Nathan Littauer Hospital uses the MEDITECH Expanse electronic health record (EHR) system. The MHealth app is specifically identified as the mobile version of MEDITECH's Patient and Consumer Health Portal. Nathan Littauer Hospital transitioned to the MEDITECH Expanse EHR, and an article from 2020 mentions the hospital adding Interlace Health's Patient Intake Solution as part of this move, which integrates with MEDITECH Expanse. While Hixny, a regional health information exchange, is mentioned in older information related to Nathan Littauer Hospital and operates a patient portal that can integrate with various EHRs, including MEDITECH, the current branding and mobile application used by Nathan Littauer Hospital directly correspond to MEDITECH's patient portal offerings. Therefore, the patient portal vendor and the EHR vendor for Nathan Littauer Hospital are both MEDITECH.
