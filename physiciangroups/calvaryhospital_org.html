About the Practice

Calvary Hospital is the only fully accredited acute care specialty hospital in the U.S. exclusively providing palliative care for adult patients with advanced cancer and other life-limiting illnesses. They offer a continuum of care including inpatient care, home care, hospice, nursing home hospice, wound care, and bereavement support programs. The hospital focuses on addressing the physical, psychological, and spiritual needs of patients and their families.

Methodology & Findings

Based on available information, it is likely that Calvary Hospital in New York (calvaryhospital.org) utilizes a Cerner electronic health record (EHR) system, and their patient portal is likely a Cerner product, potentially branded under a different name. A 2019 tax filing for Calvary Hospital Inc, located in the Bronx, New York, lists "CERNER HEALTH SERVICES" as an IT service provider. This indicates a business relationship with Cerner, a major EHR vendor. While this filing does not explicitly state that Cerner provides their EHR, it suggests a significant level of IT support or services from Cerner, which often includes EHR systems. Cerner offers a patient portal solution known as "HealtheLife". Many hospitals and health systems that use Cerner EHRs brand their patient portals with their own names, such as "MyHealth" used by Blanchard Valley Health System and "MyHealthInfo" used by Munson Healthcare, which is stated to be a Cerner-provided portal. Direct searches for a patient portal link or information about their EHR system on the official Calvary Hospital website (calvaryhospital.org) did not yield specific details about the vendor or the portal's branding. Similarly, searching for a Cerner patient portal specifically for Calvary Hospital in New York did not provide a direct match or confirmation. While some search results mention MyChart (an Epic product) in the context of healthcare providers who partner with Calvary Hospital, such as Brooklyn Health and Greenwich Hospital, this does not confirm that Calvary Hospital itself uses MyChart or Epic as its primary EHR. Given the direct (though not definitively EHR-specific) link to Cerner as an IT service provider in their tax filing, it is the most probable vendor for Calvary Hospital's EHR and associated patient portal. The specific name or branding of the patient portal used by Calvary Hospital is not explicitly stated in the search results.
