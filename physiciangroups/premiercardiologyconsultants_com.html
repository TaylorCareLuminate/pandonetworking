About the Practice

Premier Cardiology Consultants aims to provide comprehensive and personalized care to patients of all ages in New York City and its surrounding counties. The practice's certified cardiologists, electrophysiologists, physician assistants, and support staff specialize in cardiology, cardiac electrophysiology, and interventional cardiology. They offer advanced diagnostics and treatments for various heart conditions, including coronary artery disease, hypertension, arrhythmias, and peripheral vascular disease, and are noted as pioneers in the use of the WATCHMAN™ device.
