About the Practice

Indiana University School of Medicine is a large, multi-campus medical school system in the United States, offering high-quality medical education through its MD program and graduate degrees across nine campuses in Indiana. It provides access to leading medical research and delivers expert primary and specialty care to patients. The school is involved in research across various specialties, including oncology, immunology, substance use, neuroscience, and endocrinology. Its curriculum prepares students for team-based, interdisciplinary healthcare settings, and it also offers programs for other healthcare professionals.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Indiana University School of Medicine (medicine.iu.edu) utilizes the **My IU Health Patient Portal** for patient care, which is part of the larger Indiana University Health system. This patient portal is associated with the electronic health record (EHR) system provided by **Cerner**. While the IU School of Medicine's website mentions a "Health Patient Portal" and "MedConnect" in the context of medical student education and campus health, the primary portal for patients receiving clinical care from IU School of Medicine faculty physicians, who are part of IU Health Physicians, is the My IU Health Patient Portal. A public discussion indicates that Indiana University Health uses Cerner as its EHR vendor and that their patient portal, "My IU Health," serves a similar function to Epic's MyChart but is not the same product.
