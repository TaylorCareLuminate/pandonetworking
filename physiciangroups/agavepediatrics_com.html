About the Practice

Agave Pediatrics is a leading provider of pediatric care in Arizona, offering comprehensive and holistic services for infants, children, and teens with an emphasis on prevention and nurturing healthy futures. Their wide range of services includes well child checks, same day sick visits, osteopathic manual medicine, lactation services, a specialized tongue tie program, allergy testing, various procedures, sports physicals, immunizations, meet & greets, televisits, and after hours triage.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Agave Pediatrics uses the Healow patient portal, which is a patient engagement platform developed by eClinicalWorks. The Healow portal allows patients to access various features, including viewing messages, health records, test results, and upcoming appointments, as well as requesting referrals and prescription refills. Agave Pediatrics also utilizes the Healow app for mobile access to the patient portal. While Agave Pediatrics' billing can be managed through the InstaMed Patient Portal, the primary patient portal for health information and communication appears to be Healow. Healow is designed to integrate with Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems, and is specifically stated to integrate seamlessly with the eClinicalWorks EHR. Several sources confirm that Healow is a product of eClinicalWorks.
