About the Practice

Wyckoff Heights Medical Center is a voluntary, not-for-profit, multi-site teaching and safety net hospital established in 1889, serving the communities of Brooklyn and Queens. [2, 3, 6, 10] It provides comprehensive primary and secondary level inpatient medical surgical, obstetrical/gynecological, and pediatric care services, along with tertiary services such as renal dialysis, oncology, nuclear medicine, podiatry, and dental care. [2] The 324-bed hospital offers extensive outpatient services through its network of community ambulatory care centers, including a 24/7 Emergency Department. [4, 6, 10] Key specialties include cardiology, with services like diagnostic cardiac catheterization and PCI, and it is a designated Primary Stroke Center and Level III Perinatal Center. [2, 3, 8] As a teaching hospital, it supports allopathic and osteopathic programs, committed to training future physicians and providing excellence in care through prevention, education, and treatment. [2, 3, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Wyckoff Heights Medical Center uses the FollowMyHealth patient portal. FollowMyHealth is a patient engagement platform that is designed to integrate with various EHR systems. While FollowMyHealth was historically associated with Allscripts, and some sources still refer to "Allscripts FollowMyHealth," it is now known as Veradigm FollowMyHealth. It's described as EHR-agnostic, meaning it can connect with different EHR systems, not just one specific vendor. Therefore, while Wyckoff Heights Medical Center uses FollowMyHealth as their patient portal, this does not definitively confirm they are using an Allscripts EHR system. It indicates they are using a system that is compatible with FollowMyHealth.
