About the Practice

Pima Heart and Vascular, also known as Pima Heart Physicians, PC, is the largest locally owned and operated cardiovascular practice in Arizona, serving Southern Arizona since 1980. [2, 4, 5, 8, 10] Their team of board-certified and board-eligible physicians specializes in a wide range of cardiovascular fields, including internal medicine, general, interventional, and structural cardiology, electrophysiology, cardiothoracic surgery, and vascular surgery. [2, 4, 5] They offer comprehensive services such as disease prevention, advanced diagnostics, management of cardiac and pulmonary conditions, interventional cardiology, sleep studies, nuclear medicine, and surgical procedures for conditions like high blood pressure, heart disease, and heart failure. [2, 5, 9] Care is provided at numerous clinic locations throughout Southern Arizona, as well as their Vascular Lab, Vein Center, Ambulatory Surgery Center, and at most area hospitals. [2, 4, 5, 6, 9]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Pima Heart Physicians PC (pimaheartandvascular.com) utilizes the InstaMed Patient Portal, particularly for online bill payment services. While InstaMed is a payment processing platform, it integrates with numerous Electronic Health Record (EHR) and practice management systems to provide a more seamless patient experience, including portal functionalities. Information from a case study regarding Pima Heart and Vascular's use of the Luma Health patient engagement platform indicates that Luma Health integrates with the "Nextgen EHR" at Pima Heart. Given that InstaMed is known to integrate with NextGen, this suggests that Pima Heart Physicians PC uses NextGen as their underlying EHR system. Therefore, Pima Heart Physicians PC appears to use a patient portal experience that includes the InstaMed Patient Portal for payments, and their EHR system is likely NextGen. The broader patient portal functionalities beyond payments would be facilitated through the integration between NextGen and potentially other platforms like Luma Health, which is designed to work with EHRs such as NextGen.
