About the Practice

Borg & Ide Imaging, an affiliate of RadNet, provides a comprehensive range of advanced diagnostic imaging services in the Rochester, NY area. They offer state-of-the-art technology and patient-centered care, specializing in modalities such as MRI, CT, PET/CT, Nuclear Medicine, Digital and 3D Mammography, Ultrasound, DEXA for Bone Density, Fluoroscopy, Digital X-ray, Interventional Radiology, and Pain Management. The group is recognized for its commitment to advanced technology, efficient scheduling, rapid report turnaround times, and readily available radiologists for referring physicians, serving Monroe County for over 90 years.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Borg & Ide Imaging, an imaging center affiliated with RadNet, utilizes a patient portal branded as both "Borg and Ide Imaging Patient Portal" and "Borg and Ide Connect". This portal facilitates online appointment scheduling and provides patients with access to their radiology reports and images. The underlying technology for this patient portal is provided by eRAD, which is RadNet's IT division. eRAD specializes in creating integrated, web-based solutions for medical imaging workflows, including portals for both patients and physicians. While RadNet connects with a variety of electronic medical record (EMR) systems, including Epic, the patient portal itself is an eRAD product.
