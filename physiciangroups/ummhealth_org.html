About the Practice

UMass Memorial Health is the largest not-for-profit healthcare system in Central Massachusetts, affiliated with the UMass Chan Medical School. [1, 2] It provides comprehensive health services through a network of hospitals, including an academic medical center, community hospitals, a children's medical center, behavioral health services, and numerous outpatient locations. [2, 4] Key specialties and care areas include Cancer, Cardiology, Orthopedics, Emergency medicine, Surgery, Women's health, and Children's medical services, offering both inpatient and outpatient care, as well as home health, hospice, and urgent care services. [2, 5] The system is committed to excellence in clinical care, service, teaching, and research, leveraging advanced technology and participating in clinical trials to improve community health. [2, 5, 6]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UMass Memorial Health utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart allows patients to manage their health information, access test results, communicate with their care team, schedule appointments, and more, from various devices. MyChart is a patient portal deeply integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. This indicates that UMass Memorial Health is using Epic as their EHR vendor. The connection between UMass Memorial Health and Epic MyChart is also explicitly mentioned in search results that identify the software used for the patient portal.
