About the Practice

At Tulane Doctors, we are dedicated to providing exceptional, patient-centered care through a collaborative approach that advances medicine and tackles global health challenges. [2] They offer a wide range of specialties and services, including cardiology, surgery, breast care, dermatology, endocrinology, oncology, gynecology, orthopedics, and many more, ensuring comprehensive care for individuals and families across Southeast Louisiana in eleven hospitals and over thirty clinics. [11] Tulane Doctors are also involved in innovative research, pioneering new treatments and improving existing ones, and the partnership with LCMC Health expands access to high-quality care throughout Greater New Orleans. [11, 14]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Tulane Doctors utilizes two patient portals: the "Tulane Doctors Patient Portal" and "MyHealthONE Patient Portal". The primary Electronic Health Record (EHR) system used by Tulane University Medical Group (TUMG), which includes Tulane Doctors, is eClinicalWorks. The Tulane Doctors Patient Portal is part of the eClinicalWorks platform. MyHealthONE is a patient portal primarily associated with HCA Healthcare. While the search results indicate that Tulane Doctors provides access to the MyHealthONE portal, the specific scope of services or locations for which MyHealthONE is used by Tulane Doctors is not explicitly detailed. It is possible that MyHealthONE is used for services rendered at affiliated hospitals or facilities that are part of the HCA network. In addition to eClinicalWorks, LCMC Health, with which Tulane Medical Center is affiliated, is in the process of implementing Epic across its facilities and uses EpicCare Link (branded as LCMC Health Link) for external provider access to patient information. However, the search results specifically tie the Tulane University Medical Group's clinic operations to eClinicalWorks.
