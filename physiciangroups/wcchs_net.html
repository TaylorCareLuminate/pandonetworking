About the Practice

Wyoming County Community Health System (WCCHS) is a full-service, county-owned health system serving Wyoming County and the surrounding areas. It comprises a 25-bed critical access hospital, a 10-bed inpatient behavioral health unit, a 138-bed Skilled Nursing Facility, and over 10 primary and specialty care physician clinics. WCCHS provides 24-hour emergency care and a broad range of inpatient and outpatient services, including family and internal medicine, orthopedics, general surgery, podiatry, cardiology, inpatient mental health, neurology, endocrinology, dialysis, breast health services, radiology, and therapy and rehabilitation. Methodology & Findings

Wyoming County Community Health System (WCCHS) utilizes the MyCareCorner patient portal. This patient portal is a service offered by Evident, which is now known as TruBridge, a subsidiary of CPSI. MyCareCorner is integrated with the TruBridge EHR system, also referred to as Evident Thrive EHR. TruBridge provides comprehensive electronic health record solutions specifically designed for rural and community hospitals like WCCHS. Therefore, Wyoming County Community Hospital is using an EHR system provided by TruBridge (formerly Evident), with MyCareCorner serving as its patient portal.
