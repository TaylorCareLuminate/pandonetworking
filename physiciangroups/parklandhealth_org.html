About the Practice

Parkland Health and Hospital System is one of the largest public hospital systems in the country, serving Dallas County. It is the primary teaching hospital for the University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center. Parkland provides a comprehensive range of healthcare services, including a Level I Trauma Center, the first verified burn center in North Texas, a Level III Neonatal Intensive Care Unit, and a network of community-based health centers. These centers offer primary care, women's health, specialty care, urgent care, and various outreach and education programs, caring for a largely uninsured population.
