About the Practice

USF Health is the region's only academic medical center, providing high-quality patient care across more than 30 specialties, treating a wide range of conditions from common illnesses to complex diseases. With over 900 physicians and healthcare professionals, USF Health integrates education, research, and patient care missions, focusing on innovative treatments and training the next generation of healthcare professionals. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

USF Health (health.usf.edu) utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is the patient portal developed by Epic Systems and is an integrated part of the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. MyChart provides patients with secure online access to various aspects of their medical records, allowing them to manage appointments, view test results, request prescription refills, and communicate with their healthcare team. Epic's EHR system, including the MyChart portal, is widely used in healthcare for its comprehensive features and focus on patient engagement and interoperability.
