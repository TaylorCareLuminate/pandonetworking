About the Practice

University Health is a nationally recognized academic health system based in San Antonio and Bexar County, Texas, in partnership with UT Health San Antonio. It provides high-quality, compassionate, patient-centered care for adults and children across a network that includes two hospitals, two ambulatory surgery centers, and over 30 primary, specialty, and preventive health care centers. Key services and specialties include being the region's only Level I trauma center for adults and children, comprehensive diabetes care at the Texas Diabetes Institute, transplant services, heart care, and a wide range of primary and specialty care supported by outcomes-based research and innovative teaching.
