About the Practice

Orlando Health is a private, not-for-profit healthcare organization serving the southeastern United States and Puerto Rico. Founded over 100 years ago, it is a large system comprising numerous hospitals, emergency departments, and more than 375 outpatient facilities. Orlando Health is nationally recognized for its pediatric and adult Level I Trauma programs and features one of the nation's largest neonatal intensive care units. The system offers a wide range of primary and specialty care services, including areas of excellence in orthopedics, heart and vascular, cancer care, neurosciences, surgery, pediatric specialties, neonatology, and women's health. It is also a teaching hospital system.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Orlando Health utilizes **Orlando Health MyChart** as its patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal system developed by Epic Systems. Through the Orlando Health MyChart portal, patients can manage appointments, access test results, request medication refills, pay bills, and communicate securely with their healthcare team. This portal provides secure online access to portions of their medical records. The availability of MyChart strongly indicates that Orlando Health uses **Epic Systems** as their Electronic Health Record (EHR) vendor, as MyChart is the patient-facing component of the Epic EHR system.
