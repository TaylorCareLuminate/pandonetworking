About the Practice

HCA Florida St. Petersburg Hospital is a full-service 215-bed acute care facility providing comprehensive emergency, diagnostic, medical, surgical and women's services. Key specialties include 24/7 emergency care, general and robotic surgery, and comprehensive women's services including prenatal and post-partum care, labor and delivery, and a nursery. Other services include The Orthopedic Center for Joint Replacement and Spine Surgery and New Vision™ Medical Stabilization for Substance Abuse. The hospital is a Joint Commission accredited facility and also holds accreditations and certifications such as Accredited Advanced Primary Stroke Center and Accredited Chest Pain Center.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

HCA Florida Saint Petersburg Hospital utilizes the MyHealthONE patient portal, which is the designated patient portal for HCA Florida Healthcare facilities. This portal allows patients to manage their health information, view recent hospital visits and lab results, schedule appointments, and pay bills. HCA Healthcare, the parent company of HCA Florida Saint Petersburg Hospital, has selected MEDITECH Expanse as its primary electronic health record (EHR) platform. HCA is in the process of implementing MEDITECH Expanse across its hospitals as part of a large-scale digital transformation, transitioning from their previous MEDITECH Magic platform. This indicates that the MyHealthONE patient portal is integrated with the MEDITECH Expanse EHR system used by HCA facilities like HCA Florida Saint Petersburg Hospital.
