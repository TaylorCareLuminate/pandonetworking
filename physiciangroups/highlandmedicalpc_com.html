About the Practice

Clarkstown Medical Associates, part of Highland Medical PC, is an internal medicine and urgent care practice. [9] Their physicians and providers specialize in wellness, acute care, and the management of chronic conditions. [4, 8] They provide comprehensive care for a wide range of ailments, including cardiac conditions, diabetes, infections, and dermatologic issues, and offer services such as physical examinations, EKG's, and preventive care counseling.
