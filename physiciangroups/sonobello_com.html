About the Practice

Sono Bello is an industry leader in providing cosmetic surgery procedures, specializing in laser liposuction, body contouring, and excess skin removal for both men and women. With over 185 board-certified plastic surgeons across more than 100 locations nationwide, Sono Bello emphasizes experience, specialization, and customization to provide personalized total body transformations. They utilize advanced micro-laser technology and a team-based approach to care, with all offices accredited by the AAAHC to maintain a high level of care and attention to detail. Sono Bello offers a variety of procedures including TriSculpt, AbEX, and Venus Legacy treatments, aiming to provide effective results at affordable prices.

Methodology & Findings

**Sono Bello Likely Utilizes MyPatientNow as Patient Portal, Tied to PatientNow EHR** Sono Bello, a provider of laser liposuction and body contouring services, appears to use **MyPatientNow** as their patient portal. This patient portal solution is offered by **PatientNow**, which is also indicated as Sono Bello's Electronic Medical Record (EMR) and practice management software vendor. While a specific, direct statement from Sono Bello confirming their use of MyPatientNow was not found, multiple search results connect Sono Bello to PatientNow for various practice management functions, including photo management integrated within the PatientNow system. PatientNow's own materials describe MyPatientNow as their patient portal, designed to allow patients to manage medical history, view records, and access information securely. This strong association suggests that MyPatientNow is the branded patient portal used by Sono Bello, aligning with PatientNow as their likely underlying EHR and practice management system.
