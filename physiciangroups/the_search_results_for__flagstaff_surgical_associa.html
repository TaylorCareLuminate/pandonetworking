About the Practice

Flagstaff Surgical Associates is a multi-specialty medical and surgical practice located in Flagstaff, Arizona, serving residents of Northern Arizona. They offer comprehensive surgical and specialty healthcare services for men, women, and children. Their specialties include General Surgery, Urology, ENT (Ear, Nose, and Throat, including Allergy and Audiology), and Gastroenterology. The practice provides a wide range of procedures, including minimally invasive and robot-assisted surgeries, to treat various conditions such as obesity, sleep apnea, thyroid disease, kidney and gallbladder disease, cancers, hernias, and gastrointestinal disorders, with a focus on patient-centered care, quality, safety, and satisfaction.

Methodology & Findings

Northern Arizona Surgical Associates, also known as Flagstaff Surgical Associates, utilizes the eClinicalWorks Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. The patient portal vendor and branding associated with their EHR is the eClinicalWorks Patient Portal, which is commonly referred to as and integrated with the healow platform. Flagstaff Surgical Associates' website includes a "Patient Portal" section, directing patients to log in for tasks such as telemedicine appointments and completing medical forms. This aligns with the typical functionalities of the eClinicalWorks Patient Portal, which provides patients with access to records, appointments, and secure messaging. An eClinicalWorks press release specifically mentions Flagstaff Surgical Associates' use of "healow" products, such as healow CHECK-IN and healow Pay, further indicating their integration with the healow platform for patient engagement. The healow brand is a registered trademark of eClinicalWorks, LLC.
