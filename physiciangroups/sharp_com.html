About the Practice

Sharp Community Medical Group is an association of over 1,100 primary care doctors and specialists in San Diego County, offering timely access to over 30 areas of specialized care including cardiology, OBGYN, oncology and orthopedics. [2, 4] They provide exceptional care and convenient access to quality health services through their network of physicians and affiliated urgent care centers and MinuteClinic locations. [2]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Sharp Community Medical Group utilizes a patient portal branded as "Sharp app" and "Sharp account." This patient portal is integrated with or based on Epic's MyChart. Multiple sources indicate that Sharp HealthCare, the larger system that includes Sharp Community Medical Group, has been transitioning to a single Epic platform. The Sharp app was developed during this Epic implementation. The Sharp account patient portal page also features the "MyChart is Epic logo," confirming the underlying technology. Although an older result mentioned Sharp Community Medical Group rolling out "SCMG Connect, an Electronic Health Record and Practice Management System Powered by Allscripts," more recent information strongly points to Epic as the current EHR system supporting the Sharp app and Sharp account patient portal.
