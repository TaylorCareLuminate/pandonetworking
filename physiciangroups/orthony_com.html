About the Practice

OrthoNY is a provider of comprehensive orthopedic care in the Capital/Saratoga Region of Upstate New York, specializing in bone and joint health. They offer diagnosis, management, and recovery support for orthopedic issues, sports injuries, and work-related injuries. Services include treatment for conditions affecting the shoulder, hip, elbow, spine, hand & wrist, knee, foot & ankle, as well as sports medicine, pain management, physical therapy and rehabilitation, diagnostic imaging (MRI), total joint replacement, and orthopedic urgent care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

OrthoNY refers to their patient portal as the "Online Patient Portal" or "Patient Portal". There is no specific vendor branding like "MyChart" mentioned in the search results. Based on the search results, OrthoNY implemented a new Electronic Health Record (EHR) system starting in October 2019 and continuing into December 2019. While the specific vendor of this new EHR system is not explicitly named in the context of the patient portal, one search result from The OrthoForum mentions "About Nextech" and "SRSPro, Nextech's Orthopaedic-specific EHR" in a section related to OrthoNY's surgery center. This suggests that Nextech's SRSPro might be the EHR system used by OrthoNY. Another result indicates that OrthoNY is expanding its partnership with IKS Health to include a patient engagement hub, but this seems to be a separate layer for patient interaction rather than the core EHR system itself. To confirm if Nextech is indeed the EHR vendor for OrthoNY and their patient portal, a more direct confirmation is needed. However, based on the available information, it is likely that OrthoNY uses Nextech's SRSPro as their EHR system, which is connected to their generic "Patient Portal".
