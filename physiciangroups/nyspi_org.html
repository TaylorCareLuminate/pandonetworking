About the Practice

The New York State Psychiatric Institute (NYSPI), established in 1895, is one of the first institutions in the United States to integrate teaching, research, and therapeutic approaches to the care of patients with mental illnesses. Affiliated with Columbia University and NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital, NYSPI provides a state-of-the-art environment for patient care, education, and research, offering inpatient beds, specialized outpatient research clinics, and a range of clinical services for various mental health conditions, including specialized programs like OnTrackNY for early psychosis. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The New York State Psychiatric Institute (nyspi.org) utilizes a patient portal called **Connect**. This portal is shared with its affiliates, Columbia University Irving Medical Center, NewYork-Presbyterian, and Weill Cornell Medicine. The Connect patient portal is tied to the **Epic** electronic health record (EHR) system, which these institutions adopted collectively. Columbia University Irving Medical Center and NewYork-Presbyterian integrated with Weill Cornell Medicine's Epic system and the Connect portal on February 1, 2020.
