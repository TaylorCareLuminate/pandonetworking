About the Practice

Holy Name Medical Center is a comprehensive, 361-bed acute care facility and New Jersey's only independent Catholic health system. It provides high-quality healthcare across a continuum from prevention to wellness, including primary care, emergency services, acute specialty medicine, advanced surgical procedures, minimally invasive interventions, health education, fitness programs, home care, and hospice services. Holy Name offers Centers of Excellence in areas such as surgical services (including robotics), bone and joint care, breast care, cancer care, cardiovascular services, renal dialysis, emergency care, interventional radiology, maternal/child health, multiple sclerosis, sleep medicine, stroke care, and clinical research, and is known for its patient-focused and culturally sensitive care, including an Asian Medical Program. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Holy Name Medical Center utilizes an internally developed electronic health record (EHR) system known as Harmony, which was built with the support of Medicomp Systems for its clinical data engine. This Harmony EHR system includes a comprehensive patient portal. While some affiliated practices may use different patient portal systems, such as the Aprima Patient Portal used by the Women's HealthCare Group of North Jersey, the main patient portal associated with Holy Name Medical Center's Harmony EHR is described as a "Comprehensive Patient Portal" integrated within the Harmony system itself. There is no indication from the search results that this integrated patient portal has a separate, distinct brand name or is provided by a third-party vendor akin to how Epic's patient portal is branded as MyChart. Through this integrated portal, patients can access various features, including requesting appointments, messaging healthcare providers, updating their medical and insurance information, and requesting prescription refills.
