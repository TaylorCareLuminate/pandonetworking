About the Practice

Abrazo Health is a healthcare delivery system in the greater Phoenix area offering a wide range of services through its hospitals, emergency centers, and physician offices. [1, 4] They provide specialized care in areas such as cardiology, orthopedics, spine, neurology, oncology, general surgery, obstetrics, bariatrics, women's health, diagnostic imaging, and emergency medicine, including Level 1 Trauma care. [1, 4] Abrazo Health is committed to providing compassionate, patient-centered, and accessible healthcare to the communities they serve, with a focus on advanced medical treatments and minimally invasive surgical services. [1, 3, 7] They are also involved in graduate medical education and community health education. [1, 2, 14, 22]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Abrazo Health Network utilizes a patient portal branded as **My Health Rec**. Based on available information, it is likely that the electronic health record (EHR) system underpinning the My Health Rec portal at Abrazo Health is **Cerner**. Several search results indicate that Abrazo Health facilities have used and implemented Cerner systems, including a mention of a Cerner EHR system outage at Abrazo Health Hospitals in 2019 and the rollout of Cerner SurgiNet® at Abrazo Health West Valley campus in 2023. While the My Health Rec portal interface itself does not explicitly name the EHR vendor, the documented use of Cerner within the Abrazo Health network suggests a connection between the portal and the Cerner EHR system.
