About the Practice

CHRISTUS Health is an international Catholic nonprofit health system providing faith-based care with a mission to extend the healing ministry of Jesus Christ. [3, 8, 9] It operates over 600 centers, including hospitals, clinics, and urgent cares across Texas, Arkansas, Louisiana, New Mexico, Mexico, Colombia, and Chile. [3, 5, 8, 9] The system offers a comprehensive range of medical services and specialties such as Cancer care, Cardiology, Facial plastic and reconstructive surgery, Family medicine, General surgery, Gynecology, Hematology/oncology, Obstetrics and gynecology (OB/GYN), Orthopedics, Pain Management, Pulmonary services, Radiology and diagnostic imaging, Sleep disorders, Smoking cessation services, Surgery, and Women's services. [11, 12] CHRISTUS Health is committed to providing a complete, individualized healing experience focused on caring for the whole person—mind, body, and spirit—and enhancing care through clinical integration and population health management. [5, 8, 9, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

CHRISTUS Health utilizes patient portals branded as "YourCHRISTUS" and "MyCHRISTUS". The "MyCHRISTUS" portal is a rebranded version of Epic's MyChart, indicating that CHRISTUS Health uses Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) vendor. Initial information identified "YourCHRISTUS" as a patient portal program launched by CHRISTUS Santa Rosa Health System, allowing patients online access to their health records through www.YourCHRISTUS.org. This portal provides access to health information, including lab results, and allows for downloading health records. Further investigation revealed "MyCHRISTUS" as another patient portal and a comprehensive health care app used by CHRISTUS Health for managing health, accessing records, scheduling appointments, and refilling prescriptions. A login page for MyCHRISTUS explicitly states "MyChart by Epic" at the bottom, confirming the underlying EHR vendor for at least this portal. While "YourCHRISTUS" was mentioned in earlier results, "MyCHRISTUS" and its connection to Epic's MyChart are also clearly indicated.
