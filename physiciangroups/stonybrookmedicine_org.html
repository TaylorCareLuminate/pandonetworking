About the Practice

Stony Brook Medicine Radiology, University Faculty Practice Corporation provides comprehensive diagnostic imaging and interventional radiology services. Specialties include Abdominal Imaging, Advanced Cardiac Imaging, Bone Density Scans, Breast Imaging, Emergency Imaging, General Diagnostic Radiology, Interventional Radiology, MRI, Nuclear Medicine, Ultrasound, and Pediatric Radiology. They offer high-quality patient care utilizing a wide range of modalities and advanced technology, emphasizing low-dose scanning protocols. The practice also contributes to medical education by providing clinical instruction and supervision to students and residents.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Stony Brook Radiology, part of Stony Brook Medicine's University Faculty Practice Corporation, utilizes the My HealtheLife Patient Portal. This patient portal is powered by Cerner Health. The My HealtheLife portal provides patients with secure online access to their health records, medical history, lab results, and allows for messaging with their healthcare providers. While a newer portal called "MyStonyBrookMedicine" is being introduced in some practices, My HealtheLife is identified as the patient portal for Stony Brook University Hospital and its ambulatory care sites, which would include the Faculty Practice Corporation and Radiology. This directly links the My HealtheLife patient portal branding to the Cerner EHR system.
