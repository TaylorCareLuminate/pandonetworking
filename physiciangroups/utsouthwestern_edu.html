About the Practice

UT Southwestern Medical Center is a public academic health science center in Dallas, Texas, integrating pioneering biomedical research with exceptional clinical care and education. [9, 12] Its faculty includes Nobel laureates and members of prestigious national academies, responsible for groundbreaking medical advances. [12] UT Southwestern physicians provide care in more than 80 specialties to a large number of hospitalized patients and oversee millions of outpatient visits annually. [9, 12] The center is committed to translating science-driven research quickly to new clinical treatments and provides care across a wide range of medical and surgical disciplines. [12]
