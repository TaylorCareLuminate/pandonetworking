About the Practice

UHS Chenango Memorial Hospital is a 138-bed community hospital in Norwich, NY. It offers a wide range of services including emergency care, inpatient and outpatient surgical care, women's health (maternity, obstetrics, and imaging), intensive care, general surgery, cardiology, diagnostic heart care, orthopedics, diagnostic imaging, laboratory services, a 24-hour physician-staffed emergency department, GI, Nephrology, infusion services, primary care, a Walk-In Center, and physical therapy. The hospital also features a senior living center and a short-term rehabilitation wing.

Methodology & Findings

Chenango Memorial Hospital, as part of the United Health Services (UHS) network, utilizes the MyChart patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal system licensed from Epic Systems Corporation. Therefore, Chenango Memorial Hospital uses the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system.
