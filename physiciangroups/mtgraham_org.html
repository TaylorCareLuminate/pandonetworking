About the Practice

Mt. Graham Regional Medical Center, located in Safford, AZ, is a 25-bed Critical Access Hospital serving Graham and Greenlee Counties. It is a nonprofit, community-owned facility dedicated to improving community health. The medical center provides a wide array of services including medical/surgical care, intensive care, obstetrics, emergency services, comprehensive imaging and laboratory services, and numerous specialty clinics such as oncology, cardiology, and orthopedics.
