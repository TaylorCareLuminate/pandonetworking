About the Practice

Adirondack Radiology Associates, PC is a group of radiology and imaging experts serving Upstate New York. They provide comprehensive radiology and advanced imaging services, including MRI, X-rays, CT scans, Ultrasound, Open MRI, PET/CT, Mammography, DEXA, Pain Management, and Arthrograms. Their team consists of board-certified physicians and experienced technical staff dedicated to providing detailed and accurate scans utilizing the latest technology.
