About the Practice

John Muir Health is a nationally recognized, not-for-profit integrated health system serving Contra Costa County and surrounding communities in the San Francisco Bay Area. It includes two major medical centers in Walnut Creek and Concord, a behavioral health center, and a network of outpatient centers, urgent care centers, and primary care practices. John Muir Health is recognized for specialties including neurosciences, orthopedics, cancer care, cardiovascular care, high-risk obstetrics, behavioral health, general surgery, robotic surgery, weight-loss surgery, rehabilitation, and critical care. They also partner with UCSF Health for cancer care and Stanford Medicine Children's Health for pediatric specialties.
