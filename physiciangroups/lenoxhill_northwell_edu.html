About the Practice

Lenox Hill Hospital is a 450-bed, acute care facility on Manhattan's Upper East Side, part of Northwell Health. [2, 7] It has earned an international reputation for outstanding patient care, offering unparalleled expertise, innovative medical and surgical treatments, and access to the latest clinical trials. [2] The hospital is particularly renowned for excellence in cardiovascular care, neurology, neurosurgery, orthopedics, sports medicine, otolaryngology/head and neck surgery, maternal/child health, and internal medicine. [2, 10] Lenox Hill is also a recognized leader in public health education and community outreach, committed to providing the highest standard of care to a diverse patient population. [2]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Based on your inquiry, it's important to clarify that New York Presbyterian Hospital and Lenox Hill Hospital are two distinct medical institutions operating under different healthcare systems. **New York Presbyterian Hospital:** New York Presbyterian Hospital utilizes a patient portal called **Connect**. This portal is a collaborative effort with Columbia University Irving Medical Center and Weill Cornell Medicine, allowing patients to manage their health information across these affiliated institutions. While older information mentions the use of Eclipsys, more recent reports indicate that New York Presbyterian Hospital has transitioned to and is using **Epic** as its electronic health record (EHR) system. The Connect portal is linked to Epic, with mentions of existing MyChart users being able to log in to Connect. MyChart is the standard patient portal commonly associated with Epic's EHR system. **Lenox Hill Hospital (Part of Northwell Health):** Lenox Hill Hospital is part of the Northwell Health system. Northwell Health's primary patient portal is **FollowMyHealth**. Through the FollowMyHealth portal, patients can access and manage their personal health records, view test results, message physicians, and manage appointments. Northwell Health has announced that it is implementing **Epic** as its new integrated EHR system across its network, with the rollout beginning in 2025. This indicates a transition towards Epic as the central EHR for Lenox Hill Hospital as well. While FollowMyHealth is the current patient portal, it is a patient engagement platform that can integrate with various EHR systems, and its future in relation to Northwell's Epic implementation is likely integration or eventual replacement by Epic's patient portal solution (MyChart).
