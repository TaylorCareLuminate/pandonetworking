About the Practice

Washington University School of Medicine (WashU Medicine) is a leading academic medical center in St. Louis, Missouri, renowned for excellence in patient care, research, and education since 1891. [3, 9, 15] Its clinical practice, Washington University Physicians, comprises nearly 2,000 faculty members providing comprehensive medical and surgical care across more than 75 specialties. [3, 9, 10] Affiliated with nationally ranked hospitals like Barnes-Jewish Hospital and St. Louis Children's Hospital, and the Siteman Cancer Center, WashU Medicine physicians care for millions of patients annually at its main campus and over 130 locations throughout Missouri and Illinois. [3, 9, 10, 15]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Washington University School of Medicine (medicine.washu.edu) utilizes the MyChart patient portal for its patients. MyChart is a widely used patient portal platform developed by Epic Systems, an electronic health record (EHR) vendor. This indicates that Washington University School of Medicine likely uses Epic as their underlying EHR system, as MyChart is the patient-facing component of Epic's EHR.
