About the Practice

St. Luke's Behavioral Health Center, associated with Steward Family Hospital in Phoenix, Arizona, provides diagnosis and care for individuals in need of behavioral health services. Specialties include Adult Inpatient and Outpatient Chemical Dependency programs, the Dr. Tafur Generations Program for Seniors, and Adolescent and Adult Inpatient Mental Health services.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Steward Health Care, of which St. Luke's Medical Center in Phoenix (stewardphoenix.org) is a part, utilizes a patient portal branded as **StewardCONNECT**. Steward Health Care implemented the **MEDITECH Expanse** electronic health record (EHR) system across its network, including its hospitals in Arizona. This suggests that the StewardCONNECT patient portal is likely connected to the MEDITECH Expanse EHR system used by St. Luke's Medical Center in Phoenix. It is worth noting that while some former Steward hospitals in Arizona were acquired by HonorHealth and are transitioning to the Epic EHR system, this information pertains to those specific divested facilities and not necessarily the entire Steward Health Care network or St. Luke's Medical Center under Steward's operation at the time of the MEDITECH implementation. Information about Altera Sunrise EHR was found in relation to a different St. Luke's Medical Center in the Philippines.
