About the Practice

University Physicians Group, a division of Northwell Health Physician Partners, is a multi-specialty medical group providing high quality, accessible healthcare to Staten Island and southern Brooklyn. They offer comprehensive services in primary care, including Internal Medicine and Family Practice, as well as various specialties such as allergy, dermatology, endocrinology, podiatry (including diabetic foot), rheumatology, and pediatric pulmonary critical care, serving patients of all ages.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

University Physicians Group (universityphysiciansgroup.com), a division of Northwell Health Physician Partners, utilizes the **FollowMyHealth** patient portal. This cloud-based portal allows patients to access and manage their health information from Northwell Health Physician Partners office visits and Northwell Health hospital stays. The electronic health record (EHR) system used by Northwell Health, and therefore its physician partners including University Physicians Group, has historically been **Allscripts**, specifically Allscripts Sunrise for hospitals and Allscripts Touchworks for outpatient practices. However, Northwell Health announced an agreement with Epic to implement their EHR system, with a phased rollout beginning in 2025. FollowMyHealth is designed to integrate with various EMR systems, including Allscripts and Epic, providing patients with a single access point to their medical information regardless of the underlying EHR used by their provider.
