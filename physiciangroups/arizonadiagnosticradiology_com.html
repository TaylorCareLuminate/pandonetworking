About the Practice

Arizona Diagnostic Radiology is a network of outpatient imaging centers providing comprehensive diagnostic radiology services across the greater Phoenix area, including the west valley, east valley, and Casa Grande. They offer a broad spectrum of advanced imaging modalities such as MRI, CT, PET, mammography, ultrasound, digital X-ray, and DEXA scans, utilizing state-of-the-art equipment and technology. [1, 3, 6] Their team consists of board-certified radiologists who are subspecialty trained in areas like neuroradiology, musculoskeletal radiology, and women's imaging, dedicated to providing high-quality, cost-effective, and compassionate patient care with a focus on early disease detection. [1, 3, 7] Arizona Diagnostic Radiology is part of the RadNet family of imaging centers. [3, 8, 11]

Methodology & Findings

Arizona Diagnostic Radiology Group LLC (arizonadiagnosticradiology.com) utilizes a patient portal branded as "Connect Patient Portal". This patient portal is provided by eRAD, Inc. as indicated by copyright information on their patient portal selection page and login page. eRAD is a vendor that provides a suite of medical imaging workflow solutions, including Radiology Information Systems (RIS) and Picture Archiving and Communication Systems (PACS). Their RIS is also EHR-certified. The eRAD Patient Portal allows patients to access medical reports, view images, and potentially schedule appointments online.
