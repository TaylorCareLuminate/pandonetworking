About the Practice

Flagstaff Surgical Associates offers specialty health care services to men, women, and children living in Flagstaff, Arizona, with departments in Urology, ENT (ear, nose, and throat, including allergy and audiology), General Surgery, and Gastroenterology. [2, 3] Their board-certified physicians excel in performing minimally invasive procedures and surgeries, including those utilizing the da Vinci® robot-assisted surgical system, to treat a wide variety of conditions. [2, 3, 4] The practice aims to provide patient-centered surgical care that achieves high levels of quality, safety, and patient satisfaction for residents of Northern Arizona. [2, 3]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Flagstaff Surgical Associates, located in Flagstaff, Arizona, utilizes the healow patient portal, which is a product of eClinicalWorks. This indicates that their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system is also provided by eClinicalWorks. According to information from April 2023, Flagstaff Surgical Associates transitioned to eClinicalWorks V12 and uses eClinicalWorks solutions, including healow CHECK-IN and healow Pay, to enhance practice efficiency and patient engagement. The healow platform is designed to work with the eClinicalWorks EHR, providing patients with access to features such as online appointments, bill payment, and communication with the practice.
