About the Practice

Northwell Health-GoHealth Urgent Care centers provide urgent medical care for a wide range of non-life-threatening illnesses and injuries in both adults and pediatric patients as young as one week old. Services offered include diagnosis and treatment for common ailments such as allergies, asthma, cold and flu symptoms, ear infections, skin conditions, sore throats, and urinary tract infections. The centers are equipped with on-site labs and X-ray capabilities, and they also provide flu shots, STD testing and treatment, telemedicine options, and care for workers' compensation cases. Through their partnership with Northwell Health, they offer patients seamless, priority referrals to specialists within the Northwell network.
