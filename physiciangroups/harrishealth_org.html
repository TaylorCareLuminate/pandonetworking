About the Practice

Located in the heart of the Texas Medical Center, Ben Taub Hospital is a public hospital owned and operated by the Harris Health System, serving as a Level I trauma center and acute care facility. [2, 3, 7] Staffed by physician faculty and residents from Baylor College of Medicine, it functions as a teaching hospital providing inpatient services and outpatient care across numerous specialties, including cardiology, neurology, surgery, pediatrics, obstetrics and gynecology, psychiatry, and more, housed partly in the Ben Taub Tower specialty clinics. [2, 4, 9] The hospital is also recognized as a Comprehensive Stroke Center and a Chest Pain Center. [2]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Ben Taub Hospital, which is part of the Harris Health System, utilizes a patient portal branded as MyHealth. This portal appears to be an implementation of the MyChart patient portal. MyChart is a widely used patient portal developed by Epic Systems. Harris Health patients can access MyHealth through the MyChart app by searching for "Harris Health" after downloading the app. The MyHealth portal allows patients to access portions of their medical record, view test results, medications, immunizations, and allergies. Patients can also request prescription renewals and appointments, view summaries of past appointments, and communicate with their healthcare team.
