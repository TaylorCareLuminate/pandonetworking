About the Practice

NextCare Urgent Care is a leading provider of urgent care and occupational medical services, offering a cost-effective and efficient alternative to emergency rooms for non-life-threatening illnesses and injuries. [1, 6, 8, 13] They provide care for conditions such as cold and flu, infections, sprains, minor broken bones, cuts, burns, allergies, and more. [3, 14] Services often include on-site X-ray, lab services, physicals (including sports and pre-employment), vaccinations, and minor surgical procedures. [13, 14] NextCare aims to provide accessible and compassionate healthcare with convenient hours and walk-in or online check-in options. [3, 7, 16]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Nextcare Urgent Care utilizes the NextGen Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. While Nextcare brands its patient-facing technology, including a mobile application, as "NextCare Anywhere," their patient portal functionalities are significantly powered by a partnership with Solv. The "NextCare Anywhere" portal and app allow patients to access medical records, schedule appointments, pay bills, and communicate with their healthcare providers. Nextcare's collaboration with Solv includes integration with their NextGen EHR system, enabling features such as online booking and digital registration to enhance the patient experience. Solv's platform is designed to integrate with various leading EHRs, including NextGen, to streamline patient workflows. Therefore, while "NextCare Anywhere" is the patient portal branding used by Nextcare Urgent Care, the underlying EHR system is NextGen, and the patient portal's capabilities are heavily influenced and facilitated by Nextcare's partnership with Solv.
