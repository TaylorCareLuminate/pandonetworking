About the Practice

Oak Street Health is a healthcare provider specializing in value-based primary care for older adults, particularly those with Medicare. The care model focuses on comprehensive preventive care, including personalized wellness plans, integrated health services, and educational and social activities to support overall health and well-being. They offer both primary and coordinated specialty care, aiming to help patients manage chronic conditions and navigate their healthcare needs, including assistance with Medicare benefits. Oak Street Health utilizes multidisciplinary care teams and aims to provide accessible, convenient, and personalized care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Oak Street Health utilizes a proprietary technology platform called "Canopy" as its in-house medical record software, which manages day-to-day clinical and operational workflows. Canopy is described as an integrated platform that includes electronic medical records and care management. While Canopy serves as their core EHR and includes features supporting their care model and potentially some patient interactions, the search results do not clearly identify a separate, branded patient portal vendor that Oak Street Health universally uses and ties to Canopy, in the same way Epic is tied to MyChart. One search result for "Oak Street Medical," which may be a different entity or a related practice, mentions a patient portal accessible through myhealthrecord.com. However, information specifically about Oak Street Health emphasizes their internally developed Canopy platform. It is also relevant to note that CVS Health, following its acquisition of Oak Street Health, is in the process of implementing a new EHR system for Oak Street Health, though the vendor of this new system has not been publicly disclosed. CVS Health has used Epic for other parts of its business. Therefore, based on the currently available information, Oak Street Health primarily relies on its proprietary Canopy platform for its EHR needs, which appears to incorporate functionalities that might otherwise be found in a separate patient portal. There is no consistent mention of a distinct, branded patient portal vendor used across Oak Street Health that is explicitly linked to their EHR.
