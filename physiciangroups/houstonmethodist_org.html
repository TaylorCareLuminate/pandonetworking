About the Practice

Houston Methodist is a leading academic medical center and hospital system based in Houston, Texas. Established in 1919, it is a not-for-profit, faith-based organization comprising a flagship hospital in the Texas Medical Center and seven community hospitals across the Greater Houston area. Houston Methodist provides highly specialized health care services through its Centers of Excellence, including advanced cancer care, heart care, neurology and neurosurgery, and orthopedics and sports medicine. The system is recognized for its high-quality patient-focused and family-centered care, its commitment to teaching and research, and has been consistently ranked as a top hospital by U.S. News & World Report. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Houston Methodist Hospital utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. This portal allows patients to manage their health information, schedule appointments, view test results, and communicate with hospital staff. The electronic health record (EHR) system that powers MyChart at Houston Methodist Hospital is Epic. Houston Methodist has implemented Epic across its facilities. Medical Clinic of Houston, for instance, leases Epic's electronic medical record and MyChart patient portal from Houston Methodist.
