About the Practice

Balboa Nephrology Medical Group is a comprehensive nephrology team specializing in kidney care, serving communities in San Diego, Orange County, and Imperial Valley. They provide superior and consistent care for patients with kidney disease, including dialysis, advanced kidney care, and transplant services. The group is recognized for quality and outcomes-oriented care and is involved in kidney research and value-based care initiatives.
