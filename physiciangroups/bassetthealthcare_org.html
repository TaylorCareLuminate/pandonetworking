About the Practice

O'Connor Hospital, part of the Bassett Healthcare Network, is a Critical Access Hospital located in Delhi, NY. It is licensed for 23 acute care medical/surgical beds, including extended care "swing" beds for patient recovery and rehabilitation. The hospital offers a comprehensive range of outpatient specialty care clinics such as Cardiology, Orthopedics, General Surgery, OB-GYN, and Rehabilitation services. Nationally recognized for outstanding emergency care, O'Connor Hospital is known for having among the shortest ER wait times.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

O'Connor Hospital in Delhi, New York, which is part of the Bassett Healthcare Network, uses a patient portal called MyBassett Health Connection. This patient portal is powered by Epic's MyChart. Bassett Healthcare Network selected Epic as their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system in 2018. The MyBassett Health Connection portal provides patients with online access to their medical records, allowing them to view test results, message their providers, request prescription renewals, and manage appointments. Access to MyBassett is available through the MyChart mobile app, where users select Bassett Healthcare Network as their provider. This direct connection between MyBassett Health Connection and the MyChart platform indicates that Epic is the underlying EHR vendor for O'Connor Hospital (part of Bassett Healthcare Network).
