About the Practice

CenterWell Senior Primary Care specializes in providing personalized and proactive primary care services tailored for seniors. Their team-based approach includes doctors, care coaches, behavioral health specialists, and pharmacists who work together to address the physical, emotional, and social wellness of patients. Services offered encompass disease management programs, hospital care coordination, in-house lab tests, wellness services, social services, transportation, and TeleVisits, with an emphasis on longer one-on-one time with the care team and integrated home health and pharmacy services.

Methodology & Findings

CenterWell Senior Primary Care is currently utilizing a patient portal branded as the "Health Portal" which is powered by eClinicalWeb, LLC, and is accessible through the Healow App. According to information from CenterWell, they are in the process of transitioning to a new patient portal. This new portal, branded as the "CenterWell Patient Portal," is powered by athenahealth and is scheduled to begin rolling out in mid-June 2025. Therefore, CenterWell Senior Primary Care is currently using the eClinicalWorks EHR system, as Healow is the patient portal solution provided by eClinicalWeb, LLC (eClinicalWorks). They are transitioning to an EHR system provided by athenahealth, which will power their new patient portal.
