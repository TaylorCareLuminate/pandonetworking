About the Practice

St. Joseph's Health is a regional non-profit health care system based in Syracuse, NY, which includes St. Joseph's Physicians. It offers a full range of general and specialty care, inpatient and outpatient services, primary care, and home care. Services and specialties include primary care for adults, children, and newborns, cardiology, cardiac surgery, surgical services, behavioral health, imaging, wound care, respiratory therapy, and various other medical and surgical specialties aimed at advancing the well-being of the communities served through a comprehensive range of services and a focus on health maintenance and prevention. Methodology & Findings

St. Joseph's Physicians in Syracuse, NY, part of St. Joseph's Health, uses the Trinity Health MyChart patient portal. This portal is branded as MyChart, which is a patient portal developed by Epic Systems Corporation. St. Joseph's Health transitioned to the new Trinity Health MyChart portal from their previous My St. Joseph's portal starting March 18, 2023. This transition to a MyChart branded portal indicates that St. Joseph's Health, and by extension St. Joseph's Physicians, is using Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) vendor.
