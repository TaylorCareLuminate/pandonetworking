About the Practice

The University of Mississippi Medical Center (UMMC) is the state's only academic medical center, integrating education, research, and patient care to improve the health of Mississippians and eliminate health disparities. UMMC provides a comprehensive range of primary care, specialty care, and advanced subspecialty services, including Mississippi's only Level I Trauma Center, Level IV Neonatal Intensive Care Unit, a dedicated Children's Hospital, and various organ transplant programs. Care is delivered through its hospitals, numerous clinics across the state, and an extensive telehealth network.
