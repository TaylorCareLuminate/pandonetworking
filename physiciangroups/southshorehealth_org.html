About the Practice

South Shore Health is a comprehensive health system anchored by South Shore Hospital, a 396-bed acute care facility in Weymouth, Massachusetts. [10, 18] It is the only community hospital in the state with a Level III neonatal intensive care unit and one of two in Massachusetts with a Level II trauma center designation for adults. [10, 18] The system provides a full range of services including primary care, emergency medicine, cardiovascular care (including a cardiac catheterization lab), diagnostic services, outpatient surgery, physical therapy, senior care, behavioral health services, and various specialties like orthopedics, gastroenterology, and women's care. [1, 4, 18] South Shore Health is committed to meeting the primary healthcare needs of the community with both hospital-based and numerous satellite locations and clinics. [1, 8]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

South Shore Hospital, part of South Shore Health, utilizes the patient portal branded as South Shore MyChart. This online tool allows patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare, including accessing appointment information, viewing health summaries and test results, and managing payments. MyChart is a patient portal developed and powered by Epic Systems. Epic is a widely used electronic health record (EHR) system by healthcare organizations. Based on South Shore Health's use of the MyChart patient portal, it can be concluded that South Shore Health uses Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) vendor.
