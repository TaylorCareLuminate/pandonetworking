About the Practice

Huntington Hospital is a 371-bed, non-profit community and teaching acute care hospital that is part of Northwell Health and affiliated with the Donald and Barbara Zucker School of Medicine at Hofstra/Northwell. [2, 3, 5] It provides a wide range of services and has been recognized for high performance and excellence in various specialties including neurosurgery, orthopedics, cancer care through the Cancer Institute at Huntington, cardiology, emergency medicine (Level III trauma center), thoracic surgery, obstetrics and gynecology (Level II Perinatal Center), colorectal surgery, spine surgery, hernia surgery, and hip and knee replacement. [3, 4, 5, 7, 11] The hospital holds a 5-star rating from CMS and multiple Magnet designations for nursing excellence. [3, 4, 5]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Huntington Hospital, part of Northwell Health, is currently utilizing the FollowMyHealth patient portal. This portal is a third-party application that integrates with various electronic health record (EHR) systems. Northwell Health has announced a transition to Epic as their next-generation EHR system, with the implementation rolling out in phases starting in 2025. Prior to this transition, information from 2020 suggests that Northwell Health had an extended contract with Allscripts for their Sunrise platform, and Allscripts' FollowMyHealth was used for services like video visits. Based on the available information, it is likely that Huntington Hospital is currently using an Allscripts EHR system that integrates with the FollowMyHealth patient portal, while preparing for the system-wide transition to Epic.
