About the Practice

Northwest Allied Physicians, affiliated with Northwest Healthcare, is a medical group offering a wide range of specialties including Internal Medicine, Family Medicine, Orthopedic Surgery, Cardiothoracic Surgery, Gastroenterology, General Surgery, Gynecology, GYN Surgery, Neurology, Sleep Medicine, Geriatrics, Sports Medicine, Breast Surgery, Podiatry, Bariatrics, Infectious Disease, and Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation. They provide coordinated care with other Northwest Healthcare services such as Outpatient Imaging, The Women's Center, The Wound Care Center, Outpatient Therapy, and Inpatient Rehab, serving communities across Tucson, Oro Valley, Marana, Green Valley, and Vail.
