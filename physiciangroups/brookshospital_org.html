About the Practice

Brooks-TLC Hospital System is a healthcare provider serving Northern Chautauqua, Cattaraugus, and Southern Erie counties. It includes Brooks Memorial Hospital, a 65-bed general medical and surgical hospital, Gowanda Urgent Care and Medical Center, and outpatient substance abuse disorder clinics. The system offers a comprehensive range of inpatient and outpatient services, including emergency care, surgical services (general, orthopedic, spine, minimally invasive hip and hand), physical therapy and rehabilitation, laboratory services, maternity/labor/delivery, endoscopic GI procedures, urgent care, and diagnostic imaging such as mammography, CT, ultrasound, and MRI. They are dedicated to providing quality, compassionate healthcare close to home.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Brooks-TLC Hospital System utilizes a patient portal branded as "Your Health Hospital Patient Portal". This portal is directly connected to the hospital's electronic health record (EHR) system, allowing patients access to medical information such as lab results, radiology reports, and health summaries. While the specific vendor of the "Your Health Hospital Patient Portal" is not explicitly stated in the search results, information regarding system integration at Brooks-TLC Hospital System indicates that they integrate with multiple EHR systems, including Cerner, Meditech CS, and Meditech Magic. Furthermore, Meditech C/S version 5.6.7 is specifically noted as being used for the Radiology Information System (RIS) at Brooks-TLC, alongside other entities. This suggests that Meditech is at least one of the EHR systems in use by Brooks-TLC Hospital System, and it integrates with their patient portal.
