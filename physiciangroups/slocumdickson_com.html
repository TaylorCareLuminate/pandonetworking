About the Practice

Slocum-Dickson Medical Group is a physician-owned and directed multi-specialty group practice in Central New York, providing comprehensive medical care since 1938. They offer over 25 medical departments covering a wide range of primary care, specialty care, and urgent care services. The group is accredited by the Accreditation Association for Ambulatory Health Care (AAAHC) and is focused on patient-centered, physician-directed, quality care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Slocum-Dickson Medical Group utilizes MyChart as their patient portal. MyChart is the patient portal developed and primarily associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Recent news from March 2025 confirms that Slocum-Dickson Medical Group is integrating into the Mohawk Valley Health System's Epic Community Connect platform. This integration provides Slocum-Dickson Medical Group with access to the Epic EHR system, allowing for enhanced patient care coordination and access to medical information through the shared platform. This partnership allows providers from both organizations to share patient charts, facilitate orders and referrals, and receive real-time updates on shared patients.
