About the Practice

Mercy Health — St. Vincent Medical Center, located in Toledo, OH, is the city's first hospital and a Level I Trauma Center for children and adults. It offers a wide range of services including a Burn Center, Birthing Center, Heart and Vascular Institute, Neuroscience Institute, and Life Flight network. As a teaching hospital, it provides comprehensive care across various specialties such as cardiovascular medicine, cancer medicine, maternal and fetal services, advanced surgical services, psychiatric services, orthopedics, and advanced radiological services, aiming to provide high-quality, compassionate healthcare.

Methodology & Findings

Saint Vincent Catholic Medical Center, which is part of Mercy Health and associated with the mercy.com domain, uses MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal commonly associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Through the MyChart patient portal, patients of Mercy Health, including likely those of Saint Vincent Catholic Medical Center in Toledo, Ohio, can access their health information, view test results, request prescription refills, schedule appointments, and communicate with their healthcare team.
