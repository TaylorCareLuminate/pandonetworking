About the Practice

Cedars-Sinai Medical Center is a non-profit, tertiary, 915-bed teaching hospital and multi-specialty academic health science center located in Los Angeles, California. [6, 12] It is one of the largest in the U.S. [12, 13] The center provides a wide range of clinical programs, from primary care to specialized treatments for complex illnesses, and is a leader in clinical care and research in areas such as heart disease, cancer, and brain disorders. [12] Cedars-Sinai is also a Level I trauma center and offers extensive critical care and emergency services. [11] It is recognized for its excellence in patient care, research, teaching, and community service. [12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Cedars-Sinai Medical Center utilizes a patient portal branded as "My CS-Link". This portal allows patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare online, including communication with doctors, appointment scheduling, viewing lab results, and accessing medical records. My CS-Link is connected to and accessible through the Epic MyChart mobile app, indicating that My CS-Link is the customized patient portal provided by Epic for Cedars-Sinai. Several sources confirm Cedars-Sinai is a customer of Epic, an electronic health record (EHR) vendor. This connection between My CS-Link, the Epic MyChart app, and Cedars-Sinai being an Epic customer confirms that Cedars-Sinai Medical Center is using Epic as their EHR system.
