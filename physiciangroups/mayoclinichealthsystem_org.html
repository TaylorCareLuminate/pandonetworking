About the Practice

Mayo Clinic Health System is a community-based medical facility system owned by Mayo Clinic, focused on providing medical care in rural communities across Minnesota, Iowa, and Wisconsin. It offers a full spectrum of healthcare options, with more than 100 medical services and specialties available, linking the expertise of Mayo Clinic with healthcare providers in local communities. Facilities include hospitals and multispecialty clinics.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Mayo Clinic Health System utilizes a patient portal simply branded as "Patient Portal" to provide patients with online access to their health information and connect with their care team. This patient portal is powered by the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Multiple sources confirm that Mayo Clinic has implemented Epic as its single, integrated EHR system across all its locations, including Mayo Clinic Health System sites. The implementation of Epic at Mayo Clinic Health System locations began in 2017. Epic's patient portal is widely known as MyChart, and reports from 2018 indicate that Mayo Clinic was working with Epic to offer an enhanced version of MyChart. Therefore, while the branding used by Mayo Clinic Health System is "Patient Portal," the underlying technology and platform is Epic's MyChart.
