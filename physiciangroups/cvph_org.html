About the Practice

The University of Vermont Health Network - Champlain Valley Physicians Hospital (CVPH) is a private, not-for-profit hospital in Plattsburgh, NY, part of a six-hospital network serving northern New York and Vermont. Established in 1967, CVPH offers a full spectrum of health care services, including a 24-Hour Emergency Care Center, comprehensive cardiovascular services, FitzPatrick Cancer Center, Joint Care Center, Progressive Women & Children's Center, Integrative Therapy, and Family Medicine Center. [3, 5] It has 300 inpatient beds and a 34-bed Skilled Nursing Facility and is recognized for patient-centered care, rated high performing in heart attacks, heart failure, and COPD by U.S. News & World Report. [2, 3, 12] CVPH is committed to providing high quality, cost-effective, and compassionate care close to home, utilizing its connection to the University of Vermont for advanced medical treatments. [2, 8]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Champlain Valley Physicians Hospital (CVPH) utilizes two patient portals: MyChart and the Hixny Patient Portal. MyChart is the primary patient portal and is part of the UVM Health Network, to which CVPH belongs. The UVM Health Network implemented Epic, and MyChart is the patient portal associated with this EHR system. CVPH transitioned to using MyChart for hospital patient access. The Hixny Patient Portal is still accessible for patients who had existing accounts, but MyChart appears to be the go-to portal for current and future health information. Therefore, CVPH primarily uses MyChart, which is the patient portal for the Epic electronic health record system used by the UVM Health Network.
