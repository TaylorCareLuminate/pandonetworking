About the Practice

Arizona Kidney Disease & Hypertension Centers (AKDHC) is one of the nation's largest groups of physicians specializing in the care of patients with renal disease, including chronic kidney disease (CKD), vascular access management, dialysis, transplantation, and clinical research. [1, 3, 10] Since 1976, AKDHC has provided services throughout Arizona, aiming to deliver quality medical care with advanced treatments and state-of-the-art technologies with compassion and caring. [1, 3, 10, 16] Their mission is to enhance the well-being of their patients by practicing high-level, research-based, comprehensive, state-of-the-art nephrology and transplant medicine. [1, 3]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Arizona Kidney Disease and Hypertension Center (AKDHC) utilizes a patient portal branded as "DocTalk". This patient portal is connected to AKDHC's electronic health record (EHR) system. According to search results, the DocTalk EHR system and patient portal were originally developed by AKDHC's Management Information Systems (MIS) department. This indicates that DocTalk is not a patient portal provided by a large, external EHR vendor but rather a system that was developed internally or specifically for AKDHC's use. The DocTalk system is described as an EHR platform used by providers to record patient activities and aims to increase efficiency and improve patient care.
