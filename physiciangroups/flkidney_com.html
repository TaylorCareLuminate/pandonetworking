About the Practice

Florida Kidney Physicians specializes in Nephrology, providing expert care for people with kidney disease and hypertension. [1, 6] Their mission is to improve lives by offering high-quality care through compassion, empowerment, and innovation, covering services such as diagnosis, treatment, and management of acute and chronic kidney disease, high blood pressure, dialysis care, kidney transplant, and kidney stones. [1, 12] They also offer specialized programs like a Glomerulonephritis Center of Excellence, telehealth services, and clinical research opportunities, with a focus on patient education to empower individuals in managing their health. [1, 12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Florida Kidney Physicians utilizes MyChart as their patient portal, which is powered by the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. According to search results, Florida Kidney Care, which appears to be connected with Florida Kidney Physicians, explicitly states that they use "Acumen 2.0, Powered by EPIC. My Chart" for their patient portal. Further supporting this is the presence of a MyChart profile page for Dr. Rhodes Kriete, a physician listed with Florida Kidney Physicians. MyChart is widely recognized as the patient portal integrated with the Epic EHR system.
