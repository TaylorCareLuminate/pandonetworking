About the Practice

Sage Medical, associated with Scottsdale Physicians Group PLC, offers mobile medical services primarily for seniors in Maricopa County. Their services include geriatric care, primary care, coordination with healthcare networks, and mobile diagnostic imaging such as X-rays, EKGs, Ultrasounds, and Phlebotomy Lab Testing. The practice focuses on providing high-quality care in the patient's home to support independence and enhance quality of life.
