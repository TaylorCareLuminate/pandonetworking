About the Practice

Sun Health is a long-standing Arizona nonprofit organization dedicated to championing healthy living and superior health care in the West Valley through philanthropy, inspired living, and wellness programs. They support local healthcare and research and operate three Life Plan Communities: Grandview Terrace, La Loma Village, and The Colonnade, offering various levels of care including independent living, assisted living, memory support, skilled nursing, and rehabilitation. Sun Health also provides the Sun Health At Home program for in-home care and offers community-based health and wellness programs focusing on managing common health conditions like diabetes, Alzheimer's, and dementia, as well as general fitness and rehabilitative services.
