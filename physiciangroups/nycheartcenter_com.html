About the Practice

New York Comprehensive Cardiology, also referred to as The Heart Center, provides comprehensive cardiovascular disease prevention, diagnostic testing, and treatment services. [2, 3] Their mission is to offer a superior standard of cardiovascular care in a patient-focused setting, utilizing advanced diagnostics, expert treatment, and lifestyle support to treat existing conditions and prevent future problems. [3] Services include cardiovascular assessment, coronary angiography and stent placement, cardiac testing, primary care, nutrition services, and weight management. [2, 5, 12, 15, 16]
