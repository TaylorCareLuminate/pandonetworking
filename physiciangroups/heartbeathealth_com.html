About the Practice

Heartbeat Health is a virtual-first cardiovascular care company focused on identifying, preventing, and managing cardiovascular diseases. They offer comprehensive heart care through telemedicine, including virtual visits, remote monitoring, and coordination of in-person diagnostic testing. Heartbeat Health serves patients, providers, payers, and at-risk organizations across the United States, aiming to revolutionize cardiovascular care delivery and improve outcomes.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Heartbeat Health (heartbeathealth.com) utilizes Athenahealth as its Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. Their platform features "Seamless Athena EHR Integration for Centralized Patient Data" and has "Integrated Athena EMR to streamline access to patient records and support real-time care management and reporting". While Heartbeat Health provides a patient portal for its users to manage aspects of their healthcare such as viewing medical history, sending messages, and scheduling appointments, the specific branding of this portal is consistent with Athenahealth's patient portal, which is known as "athenaPatient". This suggests that the patient portal used by Heartbeat Health is likely the athenaPatient portal provided by their EHR vendor, Athenahealth.
