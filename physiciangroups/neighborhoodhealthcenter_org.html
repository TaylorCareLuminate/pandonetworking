About the Practice

Neighborhood Health Center is a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) providing high-quality, patient-centered primary healthcare and integrated care to all Western New Yorkers, regardless of insurance status or ability to pay. [4, 7] They offer comprehensive health and social services including pediatrics, internal medicine/family medicine, OB/GYN, pediatric and adult dental services, podiatry, behavioral health, social work, nutritional counseling, and pharmacy services. [4, 9] They also provide onsite lab, ultrasound, various testing services (STD, COVID-19), vaccines, health education, and support services, with a special focus on the medically under-insured, uninsured, and those experiencing homelessness. [4, 5] The center aims to improve community health through health education and promotion of healthy lifestyles to achieve health equity. [5]

Methodology & Findings

Neighborhood Health Center of WNY (neighborhoodhealthcenter.org) utilizes MyChart as their patient portal, which is a product of the electronic health record (EHR) vendor Epic. The MyChart patient portal offers patients various features, including the ability to communicate with their provider, access test results, request prescription refills, pay bills, and manage appointments.
