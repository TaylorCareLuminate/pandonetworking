About the Practice

Morris Heights Health Center (MHHC) is a Federally Qualified Health Center serving the Bronx and New York City. It provides comprehensive primary care, dental, behavioral health, and a wide range of specialty services including ophthalmology, pulmonology, gastroenterology, podiatry, optometry, cardiology, ENT, physical therapy, dermatology, and neurology. MHHC also offers HIV counseling, testing, and treatment, care coordination for chronic conditions, nutrition counseling, pharmacy services, and social support. [2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 10] Known as "The Caring Place," MHHC operates across 30 sites, including 19 school-based centers, serving over 50,000 patients annually, with a focus on providing quality, affordable, and accessible care to medically, socially, and economically disadvantaged communities. [4, 5, 7]

Methodology & Findings

Morris Heights Health Center (MHHC) uses the MyChart patient portal. This is indicated by a MyChart login page specifically branded "NYC H+H MyChart" appearing in search results related to MHHC's patient portal, and the page stating that MyChart is licensed from Epic Systems Corporation. MyChart is a patient portal developed by Epic Systems. Through the MyChart portal, patients can typically access health information, schedule appointments, view test results, and communicate with their healthcare providers. Based on this, the EHR vendor used by Morris Heights Health Center is likely Epic Systems, as MyChart is Epic's patient portal.
