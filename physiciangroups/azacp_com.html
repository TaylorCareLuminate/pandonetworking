About the Practice

Founded in 1994, Arizona Community Physicians is the largest physician-owned, predominantly primary care medical group practice in Arizona, with offices in Tucson, Oro Valley, and Green Valley. [1, 2, 5] The group is patient-centered and provides comprehensive outpatient services across various specialties including family medicine, internal medicine, geriatrics, pediatrics, endocrinology, rheumatology, dermatology, and gynecology. [1, 5, 7] In addition to physician services, they own and operate a full-service laboratory, two radiology facilities, and a testing center, aiming to be a full-service integrated healthcare provider. [5, 7] They also formed an accountable care organization, Abacus Health, and emphasize developing personal, long-term relationships between patients and their providers. [1, 5, 7]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Arizona Community Physicians (azacp.com) utilizes MyChart as their patient portal, which is a system powered by Epic. The Arizona Community Physicians website features a dedicated section for "ACP TeleHealth and Portal" where MyChart is prominently displayed with a support link. Although "FollowMyHealth Support" is also mentioned, the specific "ACP MyChart Support" email address and the inclusion of "EpicMobile Tablet | EpicMobile Phone" on their portal page strongly suggest that Epic is the underlying electronic health record (EHR) system in use by Arizona Community Physicians.
