About the Practice

Kidney Care Center specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of all kidney-related conditions, offering services in general nephrology, hypertension, kidney stones, and end-stage renal disease (ESRD). [3, 5] They provide advanced treatment therapies including in-center dialysis, peritoneal dialysis, home hemodialysis, and transplant care. [3, 5] The center is committed to providing optimal care and improving outcomes for patients with chronic kidney disease through comprehensive care coordination, particularly for Medicare beneficiaries with CKD stages 4 and 5 and ESRD, and participates in programs aimed at slowing CKD progression and promoting home dialysis and transplantation. [3, 5, 11]
