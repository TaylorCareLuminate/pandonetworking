About the Practice

Alice Hyde Medical Center, part of The University of Vermont Health Network, is a healthcare facility located in Malone, NY, providing compassionate, community-focused health care to over 55,000 residents in the North Country region of New York. It offers a wide range of inpatient and outpatient services, including 24-hour emergency care, long-term care, skilled nursing, assisted living, cancer treatment, hemodialysis, cardiac rehabilitation, orthopedic and rehabilitation services, primary care through multiple family health centers, urgent care, dental care, heart health, obstetrics, midwifery, gynecology, surgical services, emergency medicine, endocrinology, gastroenterology, hematology, hospitalist services, laboratory services, medical imaging, medical nutrition therapy, nephrology, ophthalmology, orthopedic and sports medicine, otolaryngology, pain management, palliative care, pathology, pediatrics, podiatry, pulmonology, and various rehabilitation therapies.

Methodology & Findings

Alice Hyde Medical Center, as part of the UVM Health Network, uses the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Their patient portal is called MyChart. Alice Hyde Medical Center transitioned to MyChart on April 2, 2022. This aligns with the common practice of healthcare systems using Epic's MyChart as their patient portal.
