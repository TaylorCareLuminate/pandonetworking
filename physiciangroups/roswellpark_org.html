About the Practice

Roswell Park Comprehensive Cancer Center, based in Buffalo, NY, is the first institution in the United States founded specifically for cancer research and is a National Cancer Institute-designated comprehensive cancer center. [10, 2, 16] Specializing in oncology, Roswell Park provides advanced, multidisciplinary treatment for all forms of adult and pediatric cancer, integrating research and clinical applications through an aggressive clinical trials program. [10, 2] They offer a range of specialized services, including critical care, clinical genetics, pain management, palliative care, and supportive services, aiming to provide comprehensive care and improve patient outcomes and quality of life. [8, 15, 16, 14]
