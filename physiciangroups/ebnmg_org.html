About the Practice

East Bay Nephrology Medical Group (EBNMG) is a professional medical corporation established in 1972, consisting of nephrologists, mid-level providers, and support staff, serving as one of the largest groups of kidney physicians in Northern California. [3, 6] Specializing in Nephrology, the group offers comprehensive care for acute and chronic kidney conditions, including in-office consultations, hospital-based and emergency care, management of renal dialysis patients at various centers, and collaboration with major Kidney Transplant Programs. [6, 10] Their services encompass disease management, dialysis access care, and patient education, provided by board-certified specialists dedicated to medical excellence and compassionate care. [6, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

East Bay Nephrology Medical Group (ebnmg.org) utilizes the Athenahealth platform for its electronic health records (EHR) and patient portal. The patient portal used by East Bay Nephrology Medical Group is referred to as the "Athena Patient Portal" or simply "Patient Portal" on their website. This indicates that Athenahealth is the vendor providing the patient portal and likely the underlying EHR system for the group. While a search result for a physician associated with East Bay Nephrology Medical Group at a separate location mentioned MyChart, which is Epic's patient portal, this appears to be related to a different affiliation and not the primary patient portal directly integrated with EBNMG's main practice. Another patient portal mentioned, associated with "East Bay Community Action Program," uses a different login and is not related to East Bay Nephrology Medical Group.
