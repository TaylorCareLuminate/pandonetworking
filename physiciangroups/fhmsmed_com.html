About the Practice

Family Health Medical Services (FHMS) is a family medicine practice offering comprehensive, compassionate, and patient-centered primary care across Western New York for patients of all ages, from newborns to seniors. Their services encompass routine adult and well-child care, sick and urgent visits, physicals, immunizations, chronic disease management, and women's health services. [2, 8, 9, 10] FHMS integrates biological, clinical, and behavioral sciences into their medical services and operates as a Certified Patient-Centered Medical Home, focusing on coordinated and continuous care. [2, 6, 8]
