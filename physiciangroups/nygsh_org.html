About the Practice

Gracie Square Hospital is a psychiatric hospital affiliated with NewYork-Presbyterian Healthcare System, providing advanced, patient-centered inpatient behavioral health services for adults aged 18 and older. [5, 6, 12] It offers comprehensive and personalized care with specialized programs for young adults, older adults, and those with psychotic disorders, mood disorders, and co-occurring substance abuse challenges. [11] Treatment is short-term, individualized, and delivered in a caring and safe environment by a multidisciplinary team, incorporating medications, various therapies (individual, group, milieu), and therapeutic activities. [4, 11]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Gracie Square Hospital, through its affiliation with NewYork-Presbyterian and Weill Cornell Medicine, utilizes a patient portal called "Connect". This Connect portal is powered by the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. NewYork-Presbyterian, Columbia University Irving Medical Center, and Weill Cornell Medicine transitioned to the Epic EHR system and the Connect patient portal on February 1, 2020. Existing MyChart users can log in to the Connect portal.
