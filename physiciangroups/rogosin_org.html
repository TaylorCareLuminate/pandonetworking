About the Practice

The Rogosin Institute is a leading independent, not-for-profit medical care and research organization affiliated with NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital and Weill Cornell Medical College, specializing in the diagnosis, treatment, and management of chronic and end-stage kidney disease. [6, 7, 8] They provide comprehensive, high-quality, and compassionate care through a multifaceted integrative approach that includes in-center hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, and is a leader in home dialysis programs. [8, 12] Rogosin also focuses on kidney transplantation, offering programs to make it more accessible and successful. [6] Beyond kidney disease, the institute treats patients with hypertension and lipid disorders and conducts research in these areas, as well as in diabetes and cancer, aiming to bring innovative treatments to patients. [6, 7, 12, 14] Their mission emphasizes patient education and empowerment for managing their health. [8, 12, 15]
