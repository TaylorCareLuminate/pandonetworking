About the Practice

True North Neurology is a full-service Neurology, Headache Medicine, and Sleep Medicine practice located in Port Jefferson Station, Commack, and Shirley, New York. They offer comprehensive, compassionate care with highly specialized providers treating neurological disorders for children and adults, including Migraines, Multiple Sclerosis, Epilepsy, and Seizures. The practice utilizes advanced neurodiagnostic technologies and cutting-edge treatments, integrating personalized methods within their state-of-the-art facilities to improve patient quality of life and support their journey to better neurological health.
