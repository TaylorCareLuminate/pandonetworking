About the Practice

WMCHealth Physicians Orthopedics, part of WMCHealth Advanced Physician Services, provides comprehensive orthopedic care for musculoskeletal disorders in the Hudson Valley. They offer both surgical and nonsurgical treatments, including specialized care in sports medicine, hand and upper extremity surgery, joint replacement, spine surgery, pediatric orthopedics, orthopedic trauma, and foot and ankle surgery. [1, 3, 5] They are an academic practice utilizing the latest techniques and technology, including minimally invasive procedures like arthroscopy, and are recognized for treating a wide range of conditions from routine sprains to complex procedures like hand replantations. [3, 5]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

WMCHealth Physicians Orthopedics, as part of the Westchester Medical Center Health Network (WMCHealth), primarily uses the **myCare** patient portal. This transition to the myCare portal began on October 17, 2020. The myCare portal serves as a secure online platform offering patients access to their personal health information, including medical history, medication lists, discharge instructions, test results, and educational materials. WMCHealth has a strategic alliance with **Cerner** for its integrated electronic health record (EHR) system, which was being rolled out across the network starting in 2019. This indicates that the EHR system underpinning the myCare patient portal at WMCHealth, and consequently for WMCHealth Physicians Orthopedics, is Cerner.
