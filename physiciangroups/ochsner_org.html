About the Practice

Ochsner Health is a leading nonprofit healthcare provider in the Gulf South, delivering expert care across a vast network of hospitals and more than 370 health and urgent care centers in Louisiana and Mississippi. They offer comprehensive primary care and a wide range of medical specialties, including Heart & Vascular, Cancer Care, Neuroscience, Orthopedics, Transplant, Digital Medicine, Dermatology, Digestive Disease, and various surgical services, among many others. [2, 3, 7, 8, 12, 13] Ochsner is also an academic medical center involved in research and education, focused on providing the best patient care through coordinated services and advanced technology like their electronic medical record system and patient portal, MyOchsner. [3, 12, 13, 14]

Methodology & Findings

Ochsner Health uses MyOchsner as its patient portal, which is integrated with their electronic health record (EHR) system provided by Epic Systems. Ochsner Health has been utilizing Epic as their EHR provider since 2012. The MyOchsner patient portal is part of the integrated software package offered by Epic.
