About the Practice

Rhode Island Hospital is a private, 719-bed, not-for-profit acute care hospital and the largest hospital in the state, serving as the principal teaching and research hospital for The Warren Alpert Medical School of Brown University. [6, 9, 13] It is the state's only Level I Trauma Center and verified burn center, providing comprehensive diagnostic and treatment services with particular expertise in cardiology, oncology, neurosciences, orthopedics, organ transplantation, pediatrics through Hasbro Children's Hospital, psychiatry, and diabetes. [6, 9, 11, 13] The hospital offers numerous specialized programs and centers, including the Cardiovascular Institute, Comprehensive Stroke Center, and Cancer Institute. [3] Hasbro Children's Hospital, its pediatric division, is the state's premier pediatric facility with specialized units and services for children. [6, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Rhode Island Hospital, which is part of Brown University Health, utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. This patient portal is integrated with Brown University Health's electronic health record (EHR) system, known as LifeChart. LifeChart is built on the Epic EHR platform, confirming that Epic is the vendor for the EHR system used by Rhode Island Hospital. MyChart allows patients to access their medical records, view test results, schedule appointments, and communicate with their healthcare team.
