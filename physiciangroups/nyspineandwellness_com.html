About the Practice

New York Spine & Wellness Center specializes in comprehensive pain management, particularly for spine and back pain, offering minimally-invasive treatments and a full range of state-of-the-art procedures to reduce pain and improve quality of life. [2, 5] Their approach is multidisciplinary, combining interventional pain management with physical therapy, chiropractic care, behavioral health therapy, and other modalities, delivered by a team of board-certified medical pain specialists, physician assistants, nurse practitioners, chiropractors, and behavioral health specialists, aiming to address the physical, mental, and emotional aspects of pain and enhance overall wellness. [2, 5] They are described as the largest and most experienced pain management center in New York State, treating all types of acute and chronic pain with the goal of diminishing pain, promoting functionality, and helping patients live better lives. [3, 5]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

New York Spine & Wellness Center (nyspineandwellness.com) utilizes the Follow My Health platform as their patient portal. Follow My Health is a patient engagement tool developed by Allscripts, which is now part of Veradigm. This platform is designed to integrate with various electronic health record (EHR) systems, providing patients with a single point of access to their health information regardless of the specific EHR system used by their healthcare provider. Healthcare providers within a connected organization access patient medical information through their own EMR systems.
