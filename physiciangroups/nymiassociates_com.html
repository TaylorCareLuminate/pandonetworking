About the Practice

New York Medical Imaging Associates, located on the Upper East Side of Manhattan, is a community-based diagnostic radiology and imaging practice. They are committed to providing high-quality diagnostic imaging to patients and physicians using state-of-the-art equipment. Their highly trained, board-certified radiologists specialize in subspecialties including neuroradiology, musculoskeletal radiology, women's imaging, interventional radiology, and gastro-intestinal radiology, offering a wide range of services such as MRI, PET/CT, CT, Digital Mammography, Ultrasound, and X-Ray. They are ACR accredited and a Breast Imaging Center of Excellence, focused on providing effective and accountable patient care with customized personal attention.

Methodology & Findings

Based on the information gathered, Radiology Associates (nymiassociates.com), also known as New York Medical Imaging Associates, utilizes a patient portal branded as **Clearpath**. The patient portal is accessible through the domain **myclearpath.com**. Clearpath is a platform that specializes in the digital sharing of medical images and records, rather than being a full electronic health record (EHR) system itself. It integrates with various health information systems, including EHRs and PACS (Picture Archiving and Communication Systems), to facilitate the electronic delivery of medical records and images to patients and providers. While Clearpath is known to integrate with several major EHR platforms such as Epic, Cerner, Allscripts, Athena Health, and NextGen, the search results do not specify which particular EHR vendor New York Medical Imaging Associates uses in conjunction with their Clearpath portal.
