About the Practice

UBMD Primary Care, formerly UBMD Family Medicine, provides patient-centered primary care for individuals and families of all ages, from pediatrics through geriatrics. Their board-certified physicians, who are also faculty at the University at Buffalo's Jacobs School of Medicine and Biomedical Sciences, offer a comprehensive range of services including physicals, sick visits, and mental health care. The practice also includes a specialized Addiction Medicine division and is part of the larger UBMD Physicians' Group.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UBMD Family Medicine Inc. utilizes a patient portal branded as MyUBMD. This portal is powered by FollowMyHealth™. MyUBMD, powered by FollowMyHealth, allows patients to manage their medical information online, including making or rescheduling appointments, securely sending and receiving healthcare information, receiving email reminders, viewing lab results, tracking health metrics like blood pressure and weight, and requesting prescription refills. Access is available 24/7 from various devices. To tie this to an EHR vendor, further investigation is needed to determine which EHR systems integrate with or are directly associated with FollowMyHealth.
