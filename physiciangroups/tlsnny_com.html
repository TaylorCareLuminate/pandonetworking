About the Practice

Transitional Living Services of Northern New York (TLS) is a private not-for-profit agency providing psychiatric and rehabilitative support services to children, adolescents, and adults in need of assistance to live independently in the community. [2] Their Behavioral Health and Wellness Center offers comprehensive outpatient mental health services, including individual, group, and family therapy utilizing various methods such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), medication management, and specialized programs for individuals with co-occurring disorders and trauma. [3, 4, 10, 12] The agency's primary goal is to help individuals live in the least restrictive setting possible through varying levels of support. [2]
