About the Practice

Central Texas Nephrology Associates provides comprehensive care for patients with kidney disease and difficult to manage hypertension cases. [4] They offer comprehensive inpatient and outpatient services, including kidney transplant after-care, in-center dialysis, nocturnal dialysis, and Home Therapy options, covering diagnosis and treatment of chronic kidney disease, dialysis, nocturnal dialysis, home therapy, self-care, vascular access, and transplant. [4] The practice also provides education to empower patients for all kidney disease processes. [4]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Central Texas Nephrology Associates (ctna1.com) utilizes the MyChart patient portal, which is provided through a partnership with Acumen Physician Solutions. MyChart is the patient portal branding used by Epic Systems Corporation. Central Texas Nephrology Associates offers patients access to MyChart for various functions, including receiving reminders, statements, patient education materials, and lab results electronically. Patients can also use the portal to message their doctor's office, view appointments, access office notes and treatment plans, check lab results, update demographic information, view and pay bills. To access the MyChart mobile app for Central Texas Nephrology Associates, users are directed to choose "Acumen Physician Solutions" under "My Organizations." Acumen Physician Solutions has a product called "Acumen Epic Connect," which integrates Epic's EHR and practice management system with Acumen's specialized knowledge in nephrology. This collaboration allows Acumen Physician Solutions to offer a nephrology-specific EHR solution powered by Epic. Therefore, based on the use of the MyChart patient portal and the information regarding Acumen Physician Solutions' partnership with Epic through "Acumen Epic Connect," the EHR vendor used by Central Texas Nephrology Associates is Epic, facilitated by Acumen Physician Solutions.
