About the Practice

The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center is one of the world's most respected centers devoted exclusively to cancer patient care, research, education, and prevention. [5, 6] Located primarily in Houston, Texas, it is a comprehensive cancer center providing outstanding programs that integrate patient care, research, and prevention. [4, 5] MD Anderson offers a wide range of care, including surgical procedures, advanced radiation like proton therapy, diagnostic services, specialized clinics for conditions like fatigue and kidney problems in cancer patients, and the world's largest cancer clinical trials program. [3, 6, 13, 15] It is consistently ranked as a top hospital for cancer care and research. [4, 6]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center utilizes Epic as its electronic health record (EHR) system. Their patient portal is branded as MyChart. While MD Anderson previously had a homegrown patient portal called myMDAnderson, which was later connected to their Epic EHR, the current information indicates MyChart is presented as the patient portal for accessing medical records and other services. There is also a portal for physicians called myMDAnderson for Physicians, which is powered by EpicCare Link.
