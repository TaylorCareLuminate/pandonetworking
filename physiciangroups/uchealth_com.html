About the Practice

UC Health is the only adult academic health system in the Cincinnati Tri-State region, committed to advancing medicine and improving health through groundbreaking medical research, education, and the delivery of outstanding primary and specialty care services. [6, 8] It brings together the region's top clinicians and researchers to provide world-class, comprehensive care across a wide range of medical and surgical specialties. [8, 14] As the region's largest group of board-certified physicians, UC Health, including its flagship University of Cincinnati Medical Center and West Chester Hospital, provides access to the most advanced and complex care. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UC Health (uchealth.com) utilizes a patient portal branded as "My UC Health," which is also known as MyChart. This patient portal is associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. My UC Health, powered by MyChart, offers patients secure online access to their personal health information. Through the portal, patients can manage various aspects of their healthcare, including requesting appointments, refilling prescriptions, viewing test results, and communicating with their healthcare providers. The MyChart app is also available for mobile devices, allowing convenient access to health information anytime. Notably, one source indicates that UCHealth's implementation of Epic was chosen to provide seamless access to clinical charts for patients who receive care at both Estes Park Health and UCHealth, further confirming the use of Epic as the underlying EHR system.
