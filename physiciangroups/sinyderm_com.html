About the Practice

SINY Dermatology provides comprehensive medical, cosmetic, and surgical dermatology services across New York City, Long Island, and South Florida. Their board-certified physicians and medical team offer diagnosis and treatment for various skin conditions, including eczema, psoriasis, acne, skin allergies, rosacea, and skin cancer. They also specialize in cosmetic procedures such as laser treatments, fillers, non-invasive fat loss, and body contouring, focusing on enhancing natural beauty with personalized care plans.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

SINY Dermatology appears to utilize a patient portal provided by Tebra, a company that offers an integrated electronic health record (EHR) and practice management platform. Evidence suggests this connection through the patient portal login URL and copyright information on SINY Dermatology's website. A patient portal instructional video for SINY Dermatology mentions the URL "sinyderm.mma.md" for accessing the portal. Furthermore, the footer of SINY Dermatology's website includes the copyright "© Copyright 2025 Tebra Inc.", indicating a relationship with Tebra. Tebra, formed by the merger of Kareo and PatientPop, provides a comprehensive platform for independent healthcare practices, which includes a certified EHR system and an integrated patient portal. This patient portal allows patients to schedule appointments, communicate with the practice, and access their health records. While a specific, distinct branding name for the patient portal (akin to Epic's "MyChart") is not explicitly mentioned in the search results, the portal used by SINY Dermatology is part of the Tebra platform. Therefore, the patient portal vendor is Tebra, and the portal is integrated with Tebra's EHR system.
