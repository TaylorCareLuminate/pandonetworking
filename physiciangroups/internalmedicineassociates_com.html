Methodology & Findings

Internal Medicine Associates, likely the practice at internalmedicineassociates.com, appears to use a patient portal powered by Bizmatics, Inc.. Bizmatics is commonly associated with the PrognoCIS EHR system. The patient portal provides patients with online access to their medical health records, allowing them to check appointment statuses, review lab results, request prescription refills, complete medical history forms, and view messages from the clinic. The portal is available 24/7 via a secure username and password. It is worth noting that other Internal Medicine Associates practices in different locations may use different patient portal and EHR systems. For example, Internal Medicine Associates of Johns Creek & Crabapple uses eClinicalWorks for their patient portal, and Internal Medicine Associates West Columbia uses LMC MyChart, which is associated with Epic EHR. However, the information directly linked to a patient portal login under "Internal Medicine Associates, LTD", with a Bizmatics copyright, points to Bizmatics as the vendor for the practice likely associated with internalmedicineassociates.com.
