About the Practice

Niagara Falls Memorial Medical Center (NFMMC) is a full-service, 171-bed regional medical center offering extensive inpatient and outpatient services across the Greater Niagara region, Western New York, and Ontario. [2, 7, 11] NFMMC provides comprehensive care including cardiovascular services, women's health, surgical procedures, primary care, bariatric weight loss, rehabilitation, laboratory and imaging, emergency care, oncology, wound care, stroke care, behavioral health, orthopedics & sports medicine, infectious diseases, and gastroenterology. [2, 7, 10, 11, 12, 15] Additionally, it encompasses the Schoellkopf Health Center, a 120-bed skilled nursing facility focused on short-term rehabilitation and elder care, and serves as a vital safety net hospital for the medically underserved. [7, 11]
