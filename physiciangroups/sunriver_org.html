About the Practice

Sun River Health is a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) that provides high-quality, complete health care to individuals of all ages across Long Island, the Hudson River Valley, and New York City, regardless of their ability to pay or insurance status. [3, 4, 14] Their comprehensive services include primary care, family medicine, pediatrics, women's health (OB-GYN), dental care, behavioral and mental health services (including substance use treatment), podiatry, optometry, nutrition services, urgent care, sexual health, HIV care and prevention, and Hepatitis C treatment. [4, 3, 7] They operate with a patient-centered approach, emphasizing coordinated care and ensuring patients receive the care and respect they deserve. [4, 5, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Sun River Health utilizes the healow patient portal, which is accessible online and through the healow mobile app for both iOS and Android devices. The healow platform is developed by eClinicalWorks, and it integrates with the eClinicalWorks Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. The healow patient portal allows patients to manage their health information securely online. Through the portal and app, patients can access their health records, view test results, request prescription refills, schedule appointments, and communicate securely with their healthcare team. The integration of the healow portal with the eClinicalWorks EHR system facilitates the secure exchange of health information and allows healthcare providers to access patient data for better care coordination.
