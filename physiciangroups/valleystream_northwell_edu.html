About the Practice

Long Island Jewish Valley Stream is a 284-bed, full-service community hospital providing care for patients in Nassau County and southeastern Queens. [3] It offers advanced treatments in various medical specialties, including orthopedics, acute care, gastroenterology, general surgery, wound care, and vascular surgery, and features a state-of-the-art emergency department. [3] The hospital houses a dedicated orthopedic hospital and is a designated primary stroke center. [3, 12] It is affiliated with the Donald and Barbara Zucker School of Medicine at Hofstra/Northwell and provides rehabilitation and support services. [3]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Long Island Jewish Valley Stream (valleystream.northwell.edu) utilizes the **FollowMyHealth** patient portal. FollowMyHealth is a patient engagement platform that allows individuals to access and manage their health information online. Northwell Health, the parent organization of Long Island Jewish Valley Stream, uses the **Allscripts Sunrise** platform as its electronic health record (EHR) system. FollowMyHealth is a patient engagement business that is part of Allscripts, and it is designed to integrate with various EHR systems, including Allscripts' own platforms. Therefore, the FollowMyHealth portal used by Long Island Jewish Valley Stream is tied to the Allscripts EHR system used by Northwell Health.
