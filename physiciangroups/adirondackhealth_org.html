About the Practice

Adirondack Medical Center in Saranac Lake is part of Adirondack Health, a full-service, independent healthcare provider serving the Adirondack region. [6, 17] The Saranac Lake campus offers a broad range of inpatient and outpatient medical and surgical services, as well as 24-hour emergency care. [6] Adirondack Health provides care across more than 25 specialties, including primary care, surgical services, emergency care, oncology, cardiology, labor and delivery, physical, occupational, and speech therapy, and orthopaedics. [11, 12, 17, 19] It is described as a key community institution dedicated to providing compassionate and sophisticated healthcare. [4, 14]
