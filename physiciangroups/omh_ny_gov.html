About the Practice

South Beach Psychiatric Center, part of the New York State Office of Mental Health, is a state-run psychiatric facility providing personalized treatment and rehabilitation services for adolescents and adults. [2] It offers intermediate level inpatient services, including a specialized unit for monolingual Chinese individuals and a unit for young people aged 12-18. [2] Outpatient treatment is available through a network of community mental health centers and includes services such as pharmacotherapy, individual and group therapy, co-occurring substance abuse services, and family support. [2, 5] The center emphasizes wellness and recovery through skill building, cognitive remediation, and self-help.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The search results indicate that South Beach Psychiatric Center is a New York State Office of Mental Health (OMH) facility. Several OMH-related systems are mentioned, including a PCS restricted portal for agencies and a "WEB SALUTE Login" for accessing secure OMH applications like PSYCKES, which requires an OMH user ID and RSA SecurID. However, these appear to be for internal or provider use rather than a patient-facing portal. One result mentions "MyCHOIS" in a list of OMH resources related to PSYCKES, and another mentions a "Patient Portal" in the context of Greene County Mental Health Center, which is also under OMH oversight, but doesn't name the vendor. There is no clear indication of a specific patient portal vendor or branding used by South Beach Psychiatric Center or the OMH as a whole for patient access to their health information, nor is there information directly linking any of these systems to an EHR vendor used by South Beach Psychiatric Center.
