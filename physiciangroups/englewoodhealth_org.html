About the Practice

Englewood Health is one of New Jersey's leading hospitals and healthcare networks, delivering nationally recognized inpatient and outpatient care through Englewood Hospital, the Englewood Health Physician Network, urgent care centers, and imaging centers across multiple counties in northern New Jersey. It is a community teaching hospital offering a broad range of advanced clinical programs, treatments, and diagnostic services with areas of excellence including cardiac surgery and care, cancer care, orthopedic surgery, spine surgery, vascular surgery, women's health, diagnostic and breast imaging, bloodless medicine and surgery, and robotic surgery. The network provides comprehensive primary and specialty care, behavioral health services, and is committed to health equity and population health.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Englewood Health utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which provides patients with secure online access to their health information, including managing appointments, viewing test results, communicating with their doctor's office, requesting prescription refills, and paying bills. This patient portal is connected to their electronic health record (EHR) system. Englewood Health uses the Epic EHR system. Englewood Hospital and Medical Center selected Epic EHR in 2016. The Epic system provides a single electronic health record for patients across the Englewood Health network, including the hospital, physician practices, and urgent care centers, facilitating coordinated care. The use of MyChart is consistent with Epic as MyChart is the patient portal commonly associated with the Epic EHR system.
