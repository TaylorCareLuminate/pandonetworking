About the Practice

Premier Medical Group of the Hudson Valley, PC is an all-specialty medical practice providing comprehensive, integrated, in-depth, and compassionate care across numerous medical and surgical fields. They offer services in urology, gastroenterology, cardiology, rheumatology, neurology, podiatry, dermatology & plastic surgery, gynecology, and primary care, which includes pulmonology, endocrinology, nephrology, and family practice. The group utilizes state-of-the-art procedures, advanced technology, and provides the convenience of in-house pathology lab and radiology department services to patients across the Hudson Valley region, including Dutchess, Ulster, and Orange Counties.
