About the Practice

The Atria Health Institute is a membership-based health care organization focused on longevity science and precision medicine, delivering proactive, preventive health care. [2] They offer comprehensive, continuous care through meticulous screening, advanced therapeutics, lifestyle optimization, and compassionate ongoing care, aiming to prevent disease and extend healthy life. [2, 8] With a team of over 50 doctors across more than 15 specialties, including pediatrics, they provide tailored care for the whole family and conduct research to translate scientific breakthroughs into clinical practice.
