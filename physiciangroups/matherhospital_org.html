About the Practice

Mather Hospital, a 248-bed community teaching hospital and member of Northwell Health, is recognized for providing high-quality patient care and clinical excellence. The hospital offers a wide spectrum of services including bariatric surgery, orthopedic surgery, neurosurgery, pulmonology, robotic surgery, breast health, mental health, emergency services, wound care, hyperbaric oxygen therapy, as well as specialized care in gastroenterology, geriatrics, neurology, orthopedics, and pulmonology. [3, 4, 7, 10] Mather Hospital has received numerous accolades for patient safety and nursing excellence, including multiple 'A' grades from The Leapfrog Group and Magnet® recognition. [2, 4, 7, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Mather Hospital, which is part of Northwell Health, primarily utilizes the **FollowMyHealth** patient portal for patients to access and manage their health information. Northwell Health also mentions **PrimePATIENT** as another available patient portal. Northwell Health has entered into an agreement with **Epic** to implement their next-generation electronic health record (EHR) system across the health system, with the rollout beginning in 2025. This indicates a move towards Epic's integrated EHR system from their previous vendor, which included Allscripts. While FollowMyHealth is currently the main patient portal for Northwell Health and its member hospitals like Mather, the adoption of Epic suggests a future transition to Epic's corresponding patient portal, typically branded as MyChart, as the Epic EHR is implemented throughout the Northwell Health network.
