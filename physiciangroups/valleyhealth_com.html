About the Practice

Valley Medical Group, an integral part of Valley Health System, is a physician-led and professionally-managed group practice dedicated to improving community health. It features more than 300 physicians and nearly 200 advanced practice providers in 60 adult and pediatric specialties, offering comprehensive medical services including primary, walk-in, and specialty care across numerous locations in northern New Jersey and southern New York State.

## Valley Medical Group (valleyhealth.com) Utilizes MyChart Patient Portal Powered by Epic EHR Valley Medical Group, part of Valley Health System, primarily uses the MyChart patient portal, which is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. This transition to Epic and the associated MyChart portal was announced by Valley Health System, highlighting enhanced patient access to health information and improved communication with healthcare providers. MyChart serves as a central online platform for patients of Valley Medical Group and other Valley Health System entities to access various health services. Through MyChart, patients can manage appointments, view test results, request prescription renewals, review medical history and immunizations, and securely message their doctors' offices. The implementation of Epic and MyChart by Valley Health aims to provide more efficient care documentation and improve the patient experience. While MyChart is the primary patient portal for Valley Medical Group as part of Valley Health System, some search results also indicated a patient portal associated with athenahealth for Valley Medical Group. This could suggest the possibility of different systems in use for specific practices within the larger group or a legacy system. However, the overarching system adopted by Valley Health System for its medical group practices, as widely reported, is Epic with the MyChart portal. Epic is a widely recognized EHR vendor, and MyChart is their patient portal solution used by numerous healthcare organizations.
