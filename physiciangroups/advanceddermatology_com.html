About the Practice

Advanced Dermatology and Cosmetic Surgery is a premier dermatology and cosmetic surgery practice with over 150 offices across 14 states. They offer a comprehensive range of medical, surgical, and cosmetic dermatology services for patients of all ages, treating common to complex conditions of the skin, hair, and nails, including advanced skin cancer treatments. Their services encompass general dermatology, Mohs surgery, and cosmetic procedures such as Botox, dermal fillers, chemical peels, microdermabrasion, medical facials, laser treatments, hair removal, and permanent makeup, utilizing leading-edge technology and a patient-centric approach to care.
