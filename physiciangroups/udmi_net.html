About the Practice

University Diagnostic Medical Imaging (UDMI) is a full-service diagnostic radiology facility based in New York City, recognized as a premier center for a wide range of imaging services. [4, 12] Specializing in modalities such as MRI, CT, PET/CT, Ultrasound, Mammography, X-RAY, Nuclear Medicine, and Fluoroscopy, UDMI also offers specialized care in areas like Women's Imaging, Interventional Radiology (including biopsies and pain management), Musculoskeletal Radiology, Neuroradiology, and Oncologic Molecular Imaging. [8, 12] Founded in 1986, the center emphasizes utilizing advanced technology and providing exceptional patient care with a highly trained, multi-cultural staff dedicated to delivering definitive diagnoses. [3, 4, 8, 12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

University Diagnostic Medical Imaging (udmi.net) utilizes a patient portal simply branded as "Patient Portal". This patient portal is provided by MedicsPremier, which is an Electronic Health Record (EHR) and practice management software developed by Advanced Data Systems (ADS). While some EHR systems have distinctly branded patient portals, such as Epic's MyChart, University Diagnostic Medical Imaging's patient portal carries the same name as the vendor's patient portal offering, MedicsPremier Patient Portal. MedicsPremier is described as a comprehensive EHR and practice management solution that includes a patient portal feature. This portal allows patients to access health records, schedule appointments, and manage billing.
