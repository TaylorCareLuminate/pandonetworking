About the Practice

Premier Health Group is a physician-owned, multidisciplinary medical group providing comprehensive, patient-centered healthcare with a holistic approach in the NJ & NY Metro Area. They offer state-of-the-art treatment and outpatient surgical care, with a team specializing in various fields including Vein & Pain management, minimally invasive abdominal and hernia surgery, plastic surgery, and pain management.
