About the Practice

Evernorth Care Group, formerly Cigna Medical Group, is a premier multi-specialty group practice providing high-quality, personalized primary, specialty, virtual, and urgent care. [1, 6] They offer a comprehensive range of services including behavioral health, dermatology, family medicine, geriatric medicine, internal medicine, along with onsite lab services, pharmacies, imaging services, and vision services at many of their health centers across the Phoenix metropolitan area. [1, 12, 5]

Methodology & Findings

Cigna Medical Group, an Evernorth company, utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal system developed by Epic Systems. Cigna Medical Group uses Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) system. This aligns with the common practice of healthcare providers using MyChart as the patient-facing component of their Epic EHR system.
