About the Practice

Brookdale Hospital Medical Center is a voluntary nonprofit teaching hospital and a regional tertiary care center. It is a Level II trauma center, Thrombectomy-Capable Stroke Center, Level III Perinatal Center, and AIDS center. The hospital provides 24-hour emergency services, long-term specialty care, and outpatient programs, and includes specialties such as rehabilitative medicine and long-term care. [3, 4]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Brookdale Hospital Medical Center, part of One Brooklyn Health, utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is branded as MyOneBrooklynHealth. MyChart is a patient portal system developed by Epic Systems. One Brooklyn Health System has been noted in the context of implementing an Epic EMR Telehealth Solution. This aligns with the common association of the MyChart patient portal with the Epic electronic health record system, similar to the example provided where Epic uses MyChart.
