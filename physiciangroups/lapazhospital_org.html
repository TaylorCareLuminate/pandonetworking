About the Practice

La Paz Regional Hospital is a not-for-profit, 25-bed Critical Access Hospital located in Parker, Arizona, serving La Paz County and surrounding areas. [2, 8] It offers a broad range of inpatient and outpatient services, including acute care, emergency care, intensive care, and outpatient surgery. [8, 12] The hospital operates rural health clinics and specialty clinics, providing care in areas such as General and Vascular Surgery, Orthopedics, Rheumatology, ENT, Nephrology, Cardiac Rehab, Chemotherapy/Infusion Services, Imaging, Laboratory, Physical Therapy, and Urgent Care. [2, 3, 7, 12] They also utilize telemedicine through the Mayo Clinic TeleStroke and TeleNeurology programs. [12] Its commitment is to provide quality, accessible, personalized care with the motto "Where Community and Compassion Come Together". [2]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

La Paz Regional Hospital in Parker, Arizona, uses MEDITECH Expanse as their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. The hospital's website features links for a "Hospital Patient Portal" and a "Clinic Patient Portal". While the La Paz Regional Hospital website indicates "@2025 FastHealth Corporation" at the bottom of the patient portal page, suggesting FastHealth's involvement with the website or related services, the patient portal directly integrated with the MEDITECH Expanse EHR is likely MEDITECH's offering. MEDITECH's patient portal is referred to as "MyHealth patient portal" and integrates with "Expanse Patient Connect" for features like secure messaging. MEDITECH also offers an "MHealth app" in conjunction with the Expanse patient portal. Therefore, La Paz Regional Hospital is likely using MEDITECH's patient portal solution, potentially branded as MyHealth and utilizing Expanse Patient Connect and the MHealth app, which is integrated with their MEDITECH Expanse EHR system.
