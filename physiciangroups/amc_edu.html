About the Practice

Albany Medical Center South Clinical Campus provides a range of inpatient and outpatient services, including outpatient operating room services for plastic surgery, urological, and ophthalmological procedures, as well as diagnostic and testing services like Cat Scan, Ultrasound, X-ray, and Mammography. [2, 4] It also offers specialized nursing services [2] and houses the Communication Disorders Center for speech and swallowing therapy. [7] The campus transitioned to primarily an outpatient surgical center in November 2010 [3] and is described as a Short Term Acute Care facility with multi-specialty ambulatory surgery services. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Albany Medical Center, including its South Clinical Campus, uses the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Their patient portal is called MyChart. Albany Medical Center transitioned to the Epic EMR system in March 2024, replacing previous systems such as MEDENT, MEDITECH, Allscripts, Soarian, and Cerner. This transition included the launch of the MyChart patient portal. MyChart provides patients with secure online access to their health records, allowing them to manage appointments, view test results, request prescription refills, pay bills, and message their doctors. This change is part of a larger effort to unify all hospitals within the Albany Med Health System under a single platform.
