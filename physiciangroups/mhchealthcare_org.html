About the Practice

MHC Healthcare is a community health center dedicated to providing compassionate, quality, and accessible whole-person healthcare to the community since 1957. [4, 6] They offer affordable and comprehensive services including Family Practice, Pediatrics, Internal Medicine, Obstetrics and Gynecology, Behavioral Health, Dental Care, Pharmacy, Laboratory & Radiology, Physical Therapy, and WIC Program. [3, 4, 5, 6] They focus on wellness, disease prevention, chronic disease management, and patient-centered care in a comfortable and safe environment. [3, 6] They serve over 55,000 patients annually. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Marana Health Center (MHC Healthcare) is currently transitioning its electronic health record (EHR) system and patient portal. Prior to a change set to begin on July 8th, 2025, MHC Healthcare was utilizing a patient portal branded as "ezAccess". This ezAccess patient portal is associated with athenahealth EHR systems, including athenaPractice, athenaFlow, and athenaOne. Starting July 8th, 2025, Marana Health, formerly known as MHC Healthcare, is implementing athenaOne as its new unified EHR solution. The athenaOne platform includes integrated patient engagement tools, which would encompass their new patient portal. This move to athenaOne aims to consolidate their clinical, administrative, and operational processes.
