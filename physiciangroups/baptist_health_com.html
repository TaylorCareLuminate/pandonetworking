About the Practice

As the central hub of the Baptist Health system, Baptist Medical Center Jacksonville provides the highest level of medical and surgical care using the latest technologies. [5] The system offers a broad spectrum of medical services and specialties including Behavioral Health, Cancer Care, Emergency Care, Geriatric Care, Heart & Vascular Care, Home Health Care, Maternal & Newborn Care, Imaging, Neurology and Neurosurgery, Rehabilitation Services, Women's Care, and Lab Services. [5] Other key facilities include Baptist Medical Center Beaches, Clay, Nassau, and South, along with specialty hospitals like Baptist Heart Hospital and Wolfson Children's Hospital. [2]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Baptist Medical Center, associated with baptist-health.com, primarily uses MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is the patient portal system provided by Epic Systems. MyChart allows patients to combine their Baptist Health medical records in one location, schedule appointments, review lab results, manage financials, and more. While MyChart appears to be the predominant patient portal for Baptist Health, other portals like FollowMyHealth and My Health Rec are also mentioned in relation to other specific Baptist Health entities or locations. However, the information strongly suggests that MyChart is the patient portal integrated with Baptist Health's electronic health record system.
