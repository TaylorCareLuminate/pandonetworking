About the Practice

University of Missouri Health Care, part of the University of Missouri System, is an academic health system based in Columbia, Missouri, providing a full spectrum of care from primary to highly specialized treatment for complex conditions. It comprises five hospitals in Columbia, including dedicated centers for cancer, orthopaedics, women's and children's health, and psychiatry, along with numerous clinics across mid-Missouri and an affiliation with Capital Region Medical Center in Jefferson City. As an academic health center, it is involved in groundbreaking research, training future health professionals, and offering advanced treatments across a wide range of specialties including cardiology, neurology, trauma, burn care, and various surgical fields.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The University of Missouri Health Care (MU Health Care) utilizes a patient portal branded as HEALTHConnect. This portal provides patients with online access to their health information and allows them to perform various tasks such as managing appointments, viewing lab results and clinical notes, requesting prescription renewals, and communicating with their healthcare team. MU Health Care's electronic health record (EHR) system is provided by Oracle Health. They have a partnership with Oracle Health through the Tiger Institute for Health Innovation and are involved in the design of the Oracle Health EHR system. MU Health Care also uses Oracle Health Seamless Exchange to integrate patient data from multiple sources into a comprehensive record. Therefore, the HEALTHConnect patient portal is connected to the Oracle Health EHR system used by MU Health Care.
