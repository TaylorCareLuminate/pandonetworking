About the Practice

St. John's Episcopal Hospital, part of Episcopal Health Services (EHS), is a teaching hospital located on the Rockaway Peninsula in Queens, New York. It provides comprehensive preventive, diagnostic, treatment, and rehabilitative services, including a wide range of specialties such as Emergency Medicine, Obstetrics/Gynecology, Pediatrics, and Behavioral Health, to the diverse and medically underserved communities of the Rockaways and Five Towns. The hospital is committed to delivering high-quality, value-based care with cultural sensitivity, regardless of a patient's ability to pay, and serves as an academic setting for training future healthcare providers.
