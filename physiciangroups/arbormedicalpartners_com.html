About the Practice

Arbor Medical Partners is a community pediatric practice providing comprehensive medical care for children from birth through young adulthood in the Phoenix metropolitan area. [3, 4, 10] Their team of highly skilled, board-certified physicians and professional staff are committed to offering state-of-the-art pediatric care, including well visits, sick visits, urgent care, prenatal consultations, and lactation support. [3, 4, 5, 6, 7] They focus on preventive care, managing acute and chronic illnesses, and supporting optimal growth and development, aiming to be a caring medical home for each family. [3, 4, 6]
