About the Practice

HealthAlliance Hospital Mary's Avenue Campus, part of the Westchester Medical Center Health Network (WMCHealth), is a hospital in Kingston, NY, offering comprehensive inpatient care and a range of specialty services. Key services include an emergency care center (with emergency behavioral health), critical care unit, family birthing center, advanced imaging, cardiac catheterization, inpatient psychiatry, ambulatory surgery, infusion therapy, endoscopy, comprehensive breast care, cancer services, addiction and substance abuse services, and dialysis.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

HealthAlliance Hospital Marys Avenue (hahv.org), which is part of the Westchester Medical Center Health Network (WMCHealth), utilizes the FollowMyHealth patient portal for its patients. The activation of this patient portal is linked to emails originating from "@HealthAlliance.direct.relayhealth.com", and users are directed to contact RelayHealth Customer Support for assistance, indicating that the FollowMyHealth platform at HealthAlliance is powered by RelayHealth. The WMCHealth Network, including HealthAlliance hospitals, has entered into a strategic alliance with Cerner to implement their integrated electronic health record (EHR) system across the network. Based on this information, the FollowMyHealth patient portal used by HealthAlliance Hospital Marys Avenue is tied to the Cerner EHR system used by the WMCHealth Network. While there is mention of WMCHealth transitioning to a new patient portal called myCare, the information specifically for HealthAlliance Hospital continues to list FollowMyHealth as the patient portal for patients of HealthAlliance Hospital and Margaretville Hospital. RelayHealth, a company that provides health information exchange and patient engagement tools, is now part of CoverMyMeds, which is a McKesson company. This suggests that FollowMyHealth, provided by RelayHealth, integrates with the Cerner EHR system in use at HealthAlliance Hospital through the WMCHealth network.
