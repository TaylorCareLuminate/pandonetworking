About the Practice

Georgetown University Nephrology, part of the Division of Nephrology and Hypertension, specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of kidney and urinary disorders in adults. They provide comprehensive care including inpatient and outpatient services, various dialysis modalities such as hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, and continuous renal replacement therapy, as well as a "Home Only" dialysis program. Their expertise covers chronic kidney disease, acute kidney injury, hypertension, glomerular diseases, acid-base and electrolyte disorders, pregnancy-related renal disease, onco-nephrology, and kidney transplantation. The division is actively involved in clinical care within the hospital's intensive care units, intermediate care units, solid organ transplantation ward, and oncology ward, and is also engaged in research and fellowship training.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Georgetown University Nephrology, as part of MedStar Health, utilizes the **MedStar Health Patient Portal** (also referred to as myMedStar Patient Portal) for patient access to their health information. This patient portal is integrated with the **Cerner Millennium** electronic health record (EHR) system, which is used throughout the MedStar Health system. Patients using the MedStar Health Patient Portal can access features such as prescriptions, communication with their provider, medical records, test results, and appointments. MedStar Health has been noted to operate on a single installation of the Cerner Millennium EHR. Access to medical records through their digital platform is facilitated by integration with the Cerner EHR. MedStar Health also leverages the Cerner electronic health record and a data platform for research purposes.
