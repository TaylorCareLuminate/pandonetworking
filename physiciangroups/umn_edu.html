About the Practice

The University of Minnesota System is a public land-grant research university system with five main campuses across the state: Twin Cities (Minneapolis and Saint Paul), Crookston, Duluth, Morris, and Rochester. As an R1: Doctoral University with very high research activity, it is a member of the Association of American Universities and ranks highly in research expenditures. The university offers a wide range of undergraduate, graduate, and professional programs across numerous disciplines including engineering, medicine, law, agriculture, social sciences, and health sciences. It also operates public service programs such as medical, dental, and veterinary clinics, fulfilling its land-grant mission of education, groundbreaking research, and community-engaged outreach.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

University of Minnesota (umn.edu), specifically for University of Minnesota Physicians and the M Health Fairview partnership, utilizes the MyChart patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal licensed from Epic Systems Corporation. While MyChart appears to be the primary patient portal for physician and hospital services, other specific departments within the University of Minnesota use different patient portals. These include a "Secure Medical Patient Portal" for UMN Duluth Health Services, "PatientAccess" for the Dental Clinics, and "MyVMC" for the Veterinary Medical Center. However, for connecting to their physicians and accessing health records for services under University of Minnesota Physicians and M Health Fairview, MyChart is the portal used.
