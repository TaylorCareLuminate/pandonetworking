About the Practice

The Laser & Skin Surgery Center of New York, associated with laserskinsurgery.com, is a renowned dermatology and plastic surgery practice offering comprehensive medical and cosmetic skin care services. [4, 7] Specializing in laser surgery, Mohs surgery, dermatologic surgery, and cosmetic procedures, the center treats a wide range of conditions including skin cancer, acne scarring, tattoo removal, vascular birthmarks, leg veins, cellulite, excessive sweating, hair removal, wrinkles, sun damage, and stretch marks. [3] They are known for utilizing advanced technologies, including over 100 lasers and devices, and providing care performed by board-certified dermatologists with sub-specialties in dermatologic surgery and a board-certified plastic surgeon. [4, 7, 10]
