About the Practice

The Johns Hopkins Hospital, located in Baltimore, Maryland, is a world-renowned academic medical center and the teaching hospital and biomedical research facility for the Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine. [4, 17] Dedicated to advancing patient care, education, and research, it provides a comprehensive range of state-of-the-art tertiary and quaternary care. [2, 17] The hospital is recognized for excellence in numerous medical specialties, including neurology and neurosurgery, otolaryngology, psychiatry, rheumatology, and pediatric care, and offers a wide array of inpatient, outpatient, primary, and emergency care services. [3, 10] It is consistently ranked among the top hospitals nationally and includes the Johns Hopkins Children's Center and the Johns Hopkins Kimmel Cancer Center. [4, 7, 17]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The Johns Hopkins Hospital (hopkinsmedicine.org) uses the MyChart patient portal. This patient portal is provided by the EHR vendor Epic. MyChart allows patients to access their medical information, schedule appointments, view test results, pay medical bills, and communicate with their healthcare team. While Johns Hopkins University Student Health and Well-Being uses a separate system called MyHealth Portal, MyChart is the designated patient portal for The Johns Hopkins Hospital and the broader Johns Hopkins Medicine system, and it is associated with Epic.
