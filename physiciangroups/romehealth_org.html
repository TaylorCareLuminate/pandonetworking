About the Practice

Rome Health, formerly Rome Memorial Hospital, is a non-profit healthcare system based in Rome, N.Y., providing comprehensive and compassionate medical care across various stages of life to the Central New York community. [9, 26] The system offers a wide range of services including 24-hour emergency care, surgical services, maternity care, behavioral health, cardiology, medical imaging, orthopedics, and various outpatient and rehabilitation services. [9, 15, 24, 25] Rome Health also operates an 80-bed skilled nursing facility and multiple outpatient centers and primary care practices in Rome, Boonville, and Camden, and is an affiliate of St. Joseph's Health. [9, 16, 26]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Rome Health (romehealth.org) utilizes the **Medent** patient portal for their affiliated primary care and pediatric physician practices. This portal allows patients to schedule appointments, request prescription refills, and send and receive non-urgent messages. For their hospital services, Rome Health provides a separate patient portal. This hospital portal enables patients to view and download portions of their medical records, access lab and medical imaging test results, and pay bills online. While Rome Health is affiliated with St. Joseph's Health, which uses MyChart, the search results indicate that Rome Health maintains its own distinct patient portals, with Medent being the named vendor for their primary care and pediatric practices. Rome Health also mentions the option for patients to use MyLinks® to retrieve medical records from various healthcare providers into a personal health record.
