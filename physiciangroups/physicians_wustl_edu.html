About the Practice

WashU Medicine physicians are the primary care and specialist physicians who are members of the full-time faculty at Washington University School of Medicine in St. Louis. [14] This is a highly active clinical practice group of more than 1,900 physicians and clinicians, representing more than 78 specialties and subspecialties in medicine and surgery. [14] Patients receive comprehensive medical outpatient and inpatient care at more than 130 locations. [14] They are associated with Barnes-Jewish Hospital and St. Louis Children's Hospital, providing a wide range of board-certified specialists and advanced technological resources. [14] WashU Medicine physicians are committed to providing compassionate care of the highest quality. [14]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Washington University Physicians utilize the MyChart patient portal, which is branded and provided by Epic Systems Corporation. MyChart serves as a secure online tool offering patients access to manage their healthcare information. Through the MyChart portal, patients can handle various tasks including scheduling appointments, viewing test results and doctor's comments, communicating with their care team, requesting prescription refills, and paying bills online. This integrated system allows for a single medical record to follow a patient across the Washington University Physicians and BJC HealthCare network, aiming to improve coordination and continuity of care. Washington University School of Medicine and BJC HealthCare selected Epic as their integrated electronic health record (EHR) system. MyChart is the patient portal component of the Epic EHR system.
