About the Practice

UBMD Surgery is Western New York's largest and most respected multispecialty surgical practice, part of the UBMD Physicians' Group. They provide excellent patient care and outcomes for patients of all ages across a broad spectrum of surgical conditions while also teaching the next generation of surgeons. Their certified and fellowship-trained surgeons specialize in areas including Bariatric Surgery, Breast Surgery, Burn Surgery, Colorectal Surgery, Endovascular Surgery, General Surgery, Hepato-Pancreato-Biliary Surgery, Pediatric Surgery, Surgical Critical Care, Surgical Oncology, Thoracic Surgery, Transplant Surgery, Trauma Surgery, Vascular Surgery, and Wound Care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UBMD Surgery, part of the UBMD Physicians' Group, utilizes the **MyUBMD** patient portal. This patient portal is powered by **FollowMyHealth™**. The electronic health record (EHR) system used by UBMD Physicians' Group, which includes UBMD Surgery, is **Allscripts**. UBMD selected Allscripts to create a unified patient record across their various practices. An employee resources page for UBMD Internal Medicine also lists both Allscripts and FollowMyHealth, indicating their integrated use within the UBMD group.
