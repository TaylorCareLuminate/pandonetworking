About the Practice

Community Medical and Dental Care (CMADC), also known as the Monsey Family Medical Center, is a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC Look-Alike) and a Patient Centered Medical Home Level III. [4, 2] Established in 1993, CMADC provides comprehensive and preventive medical, dental, and behavioral health services. [2, 4, 7] Their services include Internal Medicine, Pediatrics, Dentistry and Oral Surgery, Women's Health (Obstetrics and Gynecology), Behavioral Health, Dermatology, Urology, Ophthalmology, Allergy and Immunology, Speech and Language Pathology, and Podiatry. [2, 4, 7] They aim to provide high-quality care regardless of the client's ability to pay and are sensitive to the social and cultural needs of their diverse patient population, offering services in multiple languages. [2, 7]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Community Medical Center (cmadc.com) provides a patient portal called MyCommunity Health. This portal allows patients to access a portion of their personalized health information online, including electronic communication with their care team, requesting appointments and medication refills, and viewing immunization records, medication lists, lab results, and radiology results. While the search results confirm the patient portal name used by Community Medical Center (cmadc.com) is MyCommunity Health, they do not explicitly state the underlying EHR vendor for this specific medical center. Another organization with a similar name, Community Medical Centers (fresno.ucsfchildrens.org), uses a patient portal called MyHealthMate, which is a MyChart product licensed from Epic Systems Corporation. However, this does not confirm that the Community Medical Center using cmadc.com also uses Epic or MyChart for their MyCommunity Health portal. Community Medical Center (cmadc.com) did implement a new computerized medical record system in 2011 across their inpatient care centers, outpatient clinics, labs, and imaging centers.
