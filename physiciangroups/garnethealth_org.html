About the Practice

Garnet Health Medical Center, part of the non-profit Garnet Health system, is a 383-bed hospital located in Middletown, NY. [3, 8] Formed by a merger, it moved to a new facility in 2011 and is staffed by over 2,400 healthcare professionals and more than 600 doctors. [3] Along with other Garnet Health facilities, it provides a broad spectrum of care and specialty services to the mid-Hudson Valley and Catskill region, including emergency medicine, surgical services, cardiology, oncology, orthopedic services, birthing centers, behavioral health, diagnostic imaging, and primary and specialty care. [8, 12, 13] Its mission is to improve community health through exceptional healthcare. [3, 8, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Garnet Health Medical Center utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a secure electronic health records portal that allows patients to communicate with their doctor, access test results, request prescription refills, make appointments, and review visit notes. The Garnet Health app also integrates with Epic MyChart. Based on MyChart being the patient portal, the underlying electronic health record (EHR) vendor used by Garnet Health Medical Center is Epic.
