About the Practice

NYC Psychiatric Associates specializes in Psychiatry, offering diagnosis and treatment for a range of mental and emotional health conditions including schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, anxiety, substance use disorder, and treatment-resistant depression utilizing methods like Spravato and TMS. [2, 5] They provide comprehensive care for women's mental health, addressing trauma and postpartum depression, and offer child psychiatry services for patients aged four and older for conditions such as ADHD, PTSD, insomnia, and sleep disorders. [2, 5] Services are available through in-office and telemedicine appointments and include psychiatric evaluations, medication management, psychotherapy, and holistic services, emphasizing evidence-based treatments and self-care strategies. [2, 4, 5, 7, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

NYC Psychiatric Associates utilizes a patient portal branded as Tebra, which was formerly known as Kareo. The practice's website indicates that payments can be made through the "Tebra PortaL". Tebra provides a platform that includes both patient portal functionalities and electronic health record (EHR) systems for healthcare practices. Therefore, it is likely that Tebra serves as both the patient portal vendor and the EHR vendor for NYC Psychiatric Associates.
