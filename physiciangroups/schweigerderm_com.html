About the Practice

Schweiger Dermatology Group is a leading dermatology practice offering comprehensive medical, cosmetic, and surgical dermatology services, as well as allergy care. [2, 8, 12, 13, 14] Founded with the goal of eliminating long wait times for high-quality dermatologists, the group is committed to providing an Ultimate Patient Experience characterized by expert, fast, compassionate, and seamless care. [2, 8, 14] They treat a wide range of conditions including acne, eczema, psoriasis, and skin cancer, utilizing advanced treatments and technology in modern offices. [2, 7, 9, 10, 12, 13]

Methodology & Findings

Schweiger Dermatology Group utilizes the EMA patient portal, which is part of the EMA electronic health record (EHR) system developed by ModMed (Modernizing Medicine). The patient portal can be accessed via a URL containing "ema.md". EMA is a cloud-based, specialty-specific EHR system used in various medical fields, including dermatology. The integrated patient portal allows patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare, such as updating medical records, scheduling appointments, accessing lab results, communicating with the practice, and paying bills.
