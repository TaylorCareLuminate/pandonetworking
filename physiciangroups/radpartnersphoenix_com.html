About the Practice

Radiology Partners Phoenix (formerly EVDI) has been providing radiology services in Arizona since 1965, operating via a hospital-based practice and in outpatient imaging centers located in the East Valley area of metropolitan Phoenix since 1989. [2, 4] They are committed to providing quality services and excellent patient care in both inpatient and outpatient settings, offering convenient and comprehensive services in their imaging centers. [2, 4] Their team consists of over 45 fellowship-trained, board-certified radiologists specializing in areas including Body Imaging, Breast & Women's Imaging, General Radiology, Musculoskeletal, Neuroradiology, Pediatric Radiology, and Vascular & Interventional Radiology. [8, 10, 12] Radiology Partners Phoenix is a division of Sonoran Radiology, Ltd. and partners with the Banner Hospital System. [4, 9, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Based on the available information regarding Radiology Partners Phoenix (radpartnersphoenix.com), it is challenging to definitively name a single patient portal vendor used for all their patient interactions due to the organization's history of mergers and affiliations with other imaging centers and hospital systems in the Phoenix area, including EVDI, Sonoran Radiology, SMIL, Southwest Diagnostic Imaging, and Banner Imaging. While the radpartnersphoenix.com website provides contact information for billing and other requests, it does not feature a prominent link to a patient portal for general patient access to records or scheduling. However, the search results indicate a strong connection to SMIL Southwest Medical Imaging. SMIL utilizes a patient portal referred to as the "SMIL Patient Portal" or "See My SMIL Exams & Reports (Patient Portal)". Patients who have had appointments at SMIL locations are invited to enroll in this portal. It is likely that patients of Radiology Partners Phoenix who received services at a SMIL facility would use this portal. The specific EHR vendor powering the SMIL Patient Portal is not mentioned in the search results. Another relevant point is the mention of ChartSwap for requesting reports, with "Southwest Diagnostic Imaging" listed as the provider. ChartSwap is typically a platform used for the exchange of medical records between healthcare providers and requesters, rather than a direct patient portal for accessing images and reports. A search result also showed a login page for "NextGen Patient Portal," suggesting it might be used by an affiliated practice. However, a direct link establishing that Radiology Partners Phoenix universally uses NextGen for their patient portal is not clearly present. In conclusion, while it appears likely that patients of Radiology Partners Phoenix who were seen at SMIL locations would use the branded "SMIL Patient Portal," a single, unified patient portal vendor directly linked to radpartnersphoenix.com for all patients, along with its associated EHR vendor, cannot be definitively identified based on the provided search results. To determine the exact patient portal for a specific patient, it might be necessary to refer to the information provided during their visit or contact Radiology Partners Phoenix directly.
