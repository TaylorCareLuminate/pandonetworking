About the Practice

The Center for Advanced Medicine & Surgery (CAMS) at White Plains Hospital is a large outpatient facility providing highly specialized, advanced care. It houses state-of-the-art operating rooms, endoscopy and procedure suites, advanced imaging including PET/MRI and women's imaging, wound care with hyperbaric chambers, and non-oncologic infusion. Specialty practices cover areas such as surgical specialties (Bariatric, Colorectal, ENT, Endocrine, Plastics), Wound Care, Hyperbaric Medicine, Heart and Vascular Program (Cardiology, Cardiac Electrophysiology, Vascular Surgery, Cardiac Surgery), Orthopedic Surgery, Spine Surgery, Interventional Pain Management, Neurosurgery, Neurosciences, Urology, and Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation Pain Medicine.

Methodology & Findings

White Plains Hospital Center For Advanced Medicine & Surgery utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. This patient portal is tied to the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. White Plains Hospital transitioned to the Epic EHR system on May 1, 2021, providing patients with access to their health information through the MyChart website and mobile app.
