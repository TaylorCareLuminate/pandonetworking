About the Practice

Terence Cardinal Cooke Health Care Center (TCC), located in East Harlem, is a continuing-care facility providing long-term skilled nursing care for frail elders, subacute rehabilitation after hospitalization or surgery, cognitive care for adults with dementia, and specialized care for underserved populations, including pioneering care for individuals with Huntington's Disease, ALS, and other neurodegenerative disorders. [2, 3, 5, 6] It offers a multitude of special care units and two large outpatient clinics, focusing on providing holistic and compassionate care to vulnerable individuals. [2, 5]
