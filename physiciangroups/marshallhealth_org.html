About the Practice

University Physicians and Surgeons, Inc. is doing business as Marshall Health, which is the faculty practice plan of the Marshall University Joan C. Edwards School of Medicine. [5, 11, 13, 16, 19] As the region's only academic health system, Marshall Health is the largest, most comprehensive healthcare provider group in the region, offering primary and advanced care in over 75 specialty areas. [6, 8, 11, 13, 17] They provide a wide range of inpatient and outpatient services across more than 40 locations throughout West Virginia, eastern Kentucky, and southern Ohio, and are committed to clinical research and the training of future healthcare professionals. [2, 5, 6, 7, 8, 11, 13, 14]
