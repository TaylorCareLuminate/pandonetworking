About the Practice

Arizona Arthritis & Rheumatology Associates specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of arthritis and rheumatologic conditions. [8] They offer care for a range of diseases including Ankylosing Spondylitis, Gout, Osteoporosis, Psoriatic Arthritis, Rheumatoid Arthritis, and Systemic Lupus Erythematosus. [8] The practice provides services such as Bone Densitometry and MRI for extremities. [6] They have multiple care teams across various locations in Arizona. [8]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Arizona Arthritis & Rheumatology Associates (azarthritis.com) utilizes a patient portal powered by InteliChart. While the specific branding or name for their InteliChart portal isn't explicitly stated as something like "MyInteliChart," the technology provider is confirmed as InteliChart. Arizona Arthritis & Rheumatology Associates appears to use NextGen as their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. A 2018 announcement detailed a partnership between Arizona Arthritis & Rheumatology Associates and TSI Healthcare for NextGen Practice Management and EHR solutions. Furthermore, AARA Clinical Research, which originated from Arizona Arthritis & Rheumatology Associates, states that they utilize NextGen EMR at all their locations. InteliChart, the patient portal provider, is also known to integrate with NextGen and other EHR systems. In addition to the InteliChart patient portal and NextGen EHR, Arizona Arthritis & Rheumatology Associates also mentions using InstaMed for online bill payment and Luma for scheduling appointments for established patients. They have also introduced an app called Rheumera for patient care designed for Rheumatology.
