About the Practice

Cook Children's Physician Network is the largest pediatric multi-specialty physician group in North Texas, part of the integrated Cook Children's Health Care System. It comprises approximately 550 physicians and surgeons, including pediatric specialists and primary care pediatricians, and 370 advanced practice providers. The network covers over 30 specialties and subspecialties, providing comprehensive pediatric care through various settings such as the medical center, specialty clinics, neighborhood clinics, primary care offices, and outpatient settings, managing over one million patient encounters annually. [2, 3, 4, 6] Its mission is to improve the health of every child in the region through the prevention and treatment of illness, disease, and injury. [2, 6]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Cook Children's Physician Network uses a patient portal called MyCookChildren's. This portal is a branded version of Epic's MyChart patient portal. MyCookChildren's provides patients and families with secure online access to manage their child's healthcare. Features of the portal include viewing test results, messaging the care team, scheduling and viewing appointments, accessing immunization records, requesting prescription refills, and paying bills online. Cook Children's has also updated the MyCookChildren's/MyChart portal to allow for automatic release of many lab, imaging, and procedure results. The MyCookChildren's portal is accessible via a website and a mobile app.
