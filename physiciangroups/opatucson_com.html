About the Practice

Old Pueblo Anesthesia (OPA) is a physician-only private practice anesthesia group that has served the Tucson community for many years, providing peri-operative care of the highest quality with a total commitment to patient safety and well-being. [8] They employ board-certified or board-eligible anesthesiologists who provide anesthesia services for various procedures including surgery, labor & delivery, pain management, and diagnostic procedures such as gastroenterology, MRI/CT scans, and interventional radiology. [4, 7, 8] OPA is the exclusive anesthesia provider at Tucson Medical Center and also services numerous surgery centers in the Great Tucson area. [4, 6, 7] They offer dedicated expertise in pediatric anesthesia and labor & delivery anesthesia. [4, 8]
