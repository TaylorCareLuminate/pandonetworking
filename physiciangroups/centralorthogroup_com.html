About the Practice

Central Orthopedic Group provides comprehensive, specialized orthopedic care on Long Island and in Westchester County. Their specialties include Orthopedic Surgery, Orthopedic Foot & Ankle Surgery, Sports Medicine, Physical Therapy, and Pain Management, covering a full spectrum of musculoskeletal conditions.
