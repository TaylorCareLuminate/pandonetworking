About the Practice

Hospice of the Valley (hov.org) is a leading not-for-profit hospice and supportive care provider in Arizona, primarily serving Maricopa and northern Pinal counties and the Tucson area. [5, 8, 13, 19] Founded in 1977, their mission is to bring comfort, dignity, and compassionate care to the community, ensuring no one is turned away regardless of insurance or financial resources. [3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 13] They provide specialized care for people facing life-limiting illnesses, addressing their physical, emotional, social, and spiritual needs through in-home care, inpatient care homes, and specialized programs including palliative care, dementia care, pulmonary care, pediatric and perinatal care, and grief support. [3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 13, 15, 17]
