About the Practice

Valley Anesthesiology Consultants is a medical group practice headquartered in Phoenix, AZ, specializing in Anesthesiology and Pain Medicine. [2, 10] The company provides a wide range of anesthesia care services, including cardiovascular and thoracic, obstetric, neurosurgical, pediatric, and transplant anesthesia, to healthcare providers and institutions like hospitals, ambulatory surgery centers, and medical offices throughout the greater Phoenix area.
