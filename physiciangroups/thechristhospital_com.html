About the Practice

The Christ Hospital Health Network is a not-for-profit acute care facility and an extensive network serving the Greater Cincinnati region with over 100 locations. [2, 11, 14] It provides exceptional, compassionate care with specialties including cardiovascular care (recognized as Greater Cincinnati's Heart Hospital), orthopedic and spine treatment (a Center of Excellence), women's health (including a birthing center), major surgery, cancer care, behavioral medicine, emergency medicine, labor & delivery, and kidney transplant. [2, 9, 14] The network offers a comprehensive range of services including inpatient care, emergency medicine, inpatient surgery, and various outpatient services such as primary care and specialist physician practices, imaging, lab services, and therapy. [2, 8] They are recognized nationally for quality, outcomes, and patient satisfaction. [9]
