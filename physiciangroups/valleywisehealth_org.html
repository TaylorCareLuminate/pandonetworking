About the Practice

Valleywise Health is a public hospital and healthcare system located throughout Maricopa County, Arizona. [2, 12] It includes the Valleywise Health Medical Center, the nationally verified Arizona Burn Center, a Level 1 Trauma Center, multiple behavioral health centers, and a network of community health centers. [2, 12] As the Phoenix area's only public teaching health system, Valleywise Health provides comprehensive and specialized care across numerous disciplines including burn care, trauma, emergency medicine, behavioral health, maternity, pediatrics, and various medical and surgical specialties, serving a diverse patient population with a commitment to providing exceptional care. [3, 10, 11, 12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Valleywise Health Medical Center utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. This patient portal is branded as MyChart on the Valleywise Health website and allows patients to self-schedule appointments, communicate with their doctors, request prescription refills, access test results, and pay bills. The MyChart patient portal is exclusively used by healthcare systems that have implemented the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Therefore, based on their use of MyChart, Valleywise Health Medical Center is using Epic as their EHR vendor.
