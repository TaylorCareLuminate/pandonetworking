About the Practice

Boro Park OB/GYN is a practice in Brooklyn, New York, providing comprehensive obstetrics and gynecological services for over 30 years. Their highly skilled medical team includes twelve doctors and six board-certified midwives who offer cutting-edge treatment and technologies for women's health. They specialize in diagnosing and treating conditions such as fibroids, abnormal bleeding, STDs, and infertility, and provide prenatal care, support during childbirth, gynecological exams, postpartum appointments, and annual wellness checks. On-site services include ultrasounds, a certified laboratory, and office-based surgical procedures. [2, 4]
