About the Practice

2nd Chance Treatment Center provides comprehensive outpatient treatment for a wide range of mental health conditions and substance use disorders, including mood disorders, anxiety disorders, ADHD, and opioid and alcohol addiction. [2, 12] Utilizing an evidence-based approach, their care includes medication management, psychotherapy, and patient and family education to optimize patient well-being and support sustained recovery. [2, 12] They specialize in addiction medicine, psychiatry, and mental health services, including medication-assisted treatment. [1, 2, 12]

Based on available information, it appears that 2nd Chance Treatment Center (2ndchancetreatment.com) likely utilizes TherapyPortal as their patient portal.
