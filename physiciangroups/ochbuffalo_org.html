About the Practice

John R. Oishei Children's Hospital (OCH) is a women's and children's hospital in Buffalo, New York, serving as the regional center for comprehensive pediatric trauma, surgical, and medical care, including neonatal, perinatal, and obstetrical services. [5, 6, 9] It is one of the only freestanding children's hospitals in New York State, providing pediatric specialties and subspecialties to infants, children, teens, and young adults up to age 21, and maternity services for expectant mothers. [9] OCH is a teaching hospital affiliated with the University at Buffalo and is affiliated with the Kaleida Health System. [9] It holds designations as a Level I Regional Pediatric Trauma Center and a Level IV Neonatal Intensive Care Unit. [8, 9, 11]

Methodology & Findings

John R. Oishei Children's Hospital (ochbuffalo.org), part of Kaleida Health, utilizes the MyKaleida Patient Portal. This patient portal is accessible via the HealtheLife mobile app. The HealtheLife patient portal is associated with the EHR vendor Cerner. This connection is further supported by information indicating that connecting external applications to the patient portal requires an established connection with Cerner, the EHR vendor. Technical support for the HealtheLife patient portal also directs users to contact Cerner Portal Support. Therefore, John R. Oishei Children's Hospital uses the Cerner EHR system.
