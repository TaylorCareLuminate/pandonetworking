About the Practice

Refuah Health Center, a non-profit organization, provides high-quality, comprehensive medical, dental, and supportive services to all patients, regardless of their ability to pay. [2, 3] This multi-specialty community health center offers a wide range of care including family medicine, pediatrics, women's health (OB/GYN, midwifery, birthing center), various specialty medicine fields (such as podiatry, rheumatology, and pediatric specialties), behavioral health, dentistry, urgent care, optometry, and rehabilitation therapies. Methodology & Findings

Refuah Health Center (refuahhealthcenter.com) provides a patient portal for its patients. While the specific vendor or branding name of this patient portal is not clearly identified in the available information, Refuah Health Center's website directs patients to a "Patient Portal" for accessing services and information. Regarding their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system, Refuah Health Center utilizes Cognos for analyzing EHR data and generating reports, indicating a system capable of detailed data management. The organization has also been involved in initiatives for targeted EHR development and data sharing through platforms like RHIO and SHIN-NY, suggesting an integrated approach to health information. Although a patient portal is available and they use systems that interact with EHR data, the exact name of their patient portal and its direct connection to a specific EHR vendor, similar to how Epic is tied to MyChart, are not explicitly stated in the provided search results.
