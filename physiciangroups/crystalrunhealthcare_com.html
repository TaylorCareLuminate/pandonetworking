About the Practice

Crystal Run Healthcare is a premier multi-specialty group medical practice serving the healthcare needs of the Hudson Valley and lower Catskill region. With over 400 providers and more than 50 medical and surgical specialties, the practice offers seamlessly integrated Primary Care and Urgent Care, as well as a wide range of medical and surgical specialties. They also provide advanced diagnostic imaging services and lab testing, emphasizing a unique model of coordinated care focused on improving health outcomes and providing access to quality care to residents.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Crystal Run Healthcare currently uses MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal system provided by Epic. It's worth noting that effective March 1, 2025, Crystal Run Healthcare, powered by Optum, transitioned to the Optum Medical Care MyChart portal. Previously, they also utilized a NextGen patient portal, which was available in read-only mode until June 1, 2025. Crystal Run Healthcare has been recognized for its use of electronic health records and was an early adopter of EHR systems. Crystal Run Healthcare and Optum are both subsidiaries of UnitedHealth Group.
