About the Practice

St. Luke's Health Texas Health Network is a physician-led Clinically Integrated Network (CIN) operating within the larger St. Luke's Health system, a member of CommonSpirit Health, serving the Greater Houston, Lufkin/Livingston, and Bryan/College Station areas. [1, 4, 8, 10] Established with the mission of reducing medical expenses while improving the quality of care and increasing patient satisfaction, the network provides highly coordinated healthcare services and aims for a patient-centric delivery model. [1, 4] They offer comprehensive care for the whole person, with recognized specialties including cardiovascular care, sports medicine, orthopedic/spine surgery, cancer care, home health & hospice, pediatrics, imaging, lab services, and primary care across their hospitals, medical group locations, and outpatient centers. [8, 9, 10, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

St. Luke's Texas Health Network, which is part of CommonSpirit Health, has been utilizing MyChart as its patient portal for several of its Texas facilities. Starting June 28, 2025, St. Luke's Health is transitioning to a new CommonSpirit Health patient portal and app, and existing MyChart users will be able to use their current login credentials to access this new portal. New MyChart users can also receive assistance with activating their accounts during the check-in process. MyChart is the patient portal system developed by Epic Systems. The integration and continued use of MyChart credentials for the new unified portal strongly suggest that Epic is the primary Electronic Health Record (EHR) vendor for a significant portion of St. Luke's Texas Health Network facilities, particularly those transitioning to the new CommonSpirit Health portal. It is worth noting that one facility, St. Luke's Health – Patients Medical Center, is mentioned as using a MEDITECH patient portal, indicating that not all hospitals within the network may be on the same EHR system. However, the widespread adoption and transition from MyChart for several key Texas locations point towards Epic as a major EHR provider for St. Luke's Texas Health Network.
