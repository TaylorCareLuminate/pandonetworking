About the Practice

St. James Hospital, an affiliate of the University of Rochester Medical Center (UR Medicine), is a regional, full-service hospital located in Hornell, NY. [3, 4, 8] It provides a range of inpatient and outpatient services, including a 24-hour emergency department, general surgery, orthopaedics and rehab services, imaging and laboratory services, and Urgent Care. [3, 4, 8] The hospital also offers a host of outpatient clinics such as cardiology, ENT, neurosurgery, urology, and pain management. [3, 4, 8]

Methodology & Findings

St James Hospital, as an affiliate of UR Medicine, utilizes the MyChart patient portal. This patient portal is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system, which UR Medicine refers to as eRecord. St. James Hospital transitioned to the Epic EHR system on March 5. Through the MyChart portal, patients can access various services online, including scheduling appointments, viewing test results, utilizing eCheck-in, and reading notes from their healthcare providers. Patients can also access and share medical imaging records through MyChart via a partnership with PocketHealth.
