About the Practice

M Health Fairview is a healthcare brand in Minnesota that represents a partnership between the University of Minnesota, University of Minnesota Physicians, and Fairview Health Services. This collaboration combines the university's clinical innovation and training with Fairview's community medicine roots to provide a comprehensive range of health services. They offer primary care, specialty care across more than 60 areas including pediatrics, cancer care, heart care, neuroscience, and mental health, as well as hospital care and pharmacy services through a network of over 10 hospitals and 60 clinics located throughout Minnesota.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

M Health Fairview utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is a product of Epic Systems Corporation. MyChart provides patients with access to their health information and allows them to communicate with their care team. M Health Fairview uses the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. The MyChart patient portal is explicitly licensed from Epic Systems Corporation. While "Fairview Connect" is also mentioned as their electronic health record system, the prominent presence of MyChart and its direct licensing from Epic indicates that Epic is the underlying EHR vendor.
