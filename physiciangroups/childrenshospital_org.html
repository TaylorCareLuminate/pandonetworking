About the Practice

Boston Children's Health Physicians (BCHP) is a large multi-specialty group comprising over 300 clinicians dedicated to providing comprehensive primary and specialty care for newborns, children, and adolescents. [4, 6] They offer services in more than 60 locations throughout New York's Metropolitan Area, the Hudson Valley, Connecticut, and Eastern Massachusetts. [4, 6] BCHP covers over 20 areas of medical expertise, including cardiology, oncology, pulmonology, gastroenterology, adolescent medicine, critical care, developmental-behavioral pediatrics, endocrinology, hematology, infectious diseases, mental health and wellness, neonatology, neurology, nutrition, ophthalmology, nephrology, psychology, rheumatology, and speech-language pathology. [4, 11] Their care encompasses a full range of services, from routine wellness visits to the diagnosis and management of complex conditions, and they have an integrated behavioral health program. [4]

Methodology & Findings

Boston Children's Health Physicians (childrenshospital.org) uses a patient portal called "MyChildren's Patient Portal". This patient portal is a rebranded instance of Epic's MyChart. Therefore, the EHR vendor utilized by Boston Children's Health Physicians is Epic.
