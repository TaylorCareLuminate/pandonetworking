About the Practice

The Children's Medical Group provides comprehensive, evidence-based pediatric care for patients from birth through college age in Dutchess, Orange, and Ulster counties. Services include well-care and same-day sick visits, with evening, weekend, and holiday availability, and in-office lab services. Their team of Board Certified Pediatricians and experienced Certified Pediatric Nurse Practitioners are committed to providing high-quality, compassionate care and the group is recognized by the NCQA.
