About the Practice

A.T. Still University (ATSU) is the founding institution of osteopathic healthcare, established in 1892, and is a leading health sciences university. [4, 11] It is dedicated to preparing highly competent professionals through innovative academic programs with a commitment to its osteopathic heritage and focus on whole person healthcare, scholarship, community health, interprofessional education, diversity, and underserved populations. [1, 10, 11] ATSU offers graduate degrees in fields such as osteopathic medicine, dental medicine, athletic training, audiology, physical therapy, occupational therapy, and physician assistant studies. [11] The university also provides patient care through various centers and clinics, offering services like osteopathic manipulative medicine, comprehensive dental care, occupational and physical therapy, and balance and hearing care. [7, 9, 12]

Methodology & Findings

Based on the search results, here's what I could find regarding A.T. Still University's (ATSU) patient portal and EHR systems: ATSU appears to utilize different systems for educational purposes and potentially across its various clinics or affiliated health centers. For educational purposes, ATSU uses: * **Centricity EMR:** Piloted for student training in the Virtual Community Health Center (VCHC) at ATSU-SOMA. * **Envision Community Health Center:** The branding for their virtual patient encounter platform which incorporates an EHR. * **EHR Go:** Listed as an educational tool in the Physician Assistant program curriculum. * **CORE-AT Electronic Medical Record (EMR):** Used by students in athletic training programs. For patient care in their clinics or affiliated centers, the information is less consolidated: * **Hearing Health Portal:** Used specifically by the AFA Balance & Hearing Institute at ATSU for patient paperwork. * **Patient Portal:** Mentioned by Beaufort-Jasper-Hampton Comprehensive Health Services, Inc. and The Wright Center, both of which have partnerships with ATSU. * **ezAccess Patient Portal:** Used by North Country HealthCare, which is listed in connection with A.T. Still University. * **St. Louis Dental Center:** This center, associated with ATSU's Missouri School of Dentistry and Oral Health, also mentions a "Patient Portal". Different clinics or partner organizations seem to utilize different patient portals, some of which are named (Hearing Health Portal, ezAccess Patient Portal), while others are generically referred to as "Patient Portal". The underlying EHR systems for these patient portals are not consistently identified in the search results, although Centricity EMR was mentioned in the context of a past student training pilot program.
