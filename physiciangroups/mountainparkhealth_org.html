About the Practice

Mountain Park Health Center is a nonprofit community health center providing affordable, high-quality, whole-patient care for the entire family in the Phoenix metro area. They operate as a Patient Centered Medical Home, offering a wide range of services including pediatrics, women's health care/pregnancy, family/internal medicine, behavioral health, nutrition services, and on-site pharmacy and lab services, aiming to provide comprehensive care under one roof.

Methodology & Findings

Mountain Park Health Center utilizes a patient portal that is integrated with their eClinicalWorks Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. The patient portal allows patients to communicate with their care team, access medical records, schedule appointments, and view lab results. Mountain Park Health Center has actively worked with eClinicalWorks to enhance the patient portal experience, including simplifying the login process. Additionally, there is a mobile application associated with the patient portal called "healow".
