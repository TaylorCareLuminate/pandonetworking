About the Practice

U.S. Dermatology Partners is one of the largest physician-owned dermatology practices in the country, with over 100 locations. They provide comprehensive medical, surgical, and cosmetic dermatological care for the skin, hair, and nails for the entire family. Services include skin cancer diagnosis and removal, Mohs surgery, acne treatments, cosmetic procedures such as Botox, dermal fillers, microneedling, facials, Kybella, Latisse, and laser therapies, as well as annual skin examinations and treatment for conditions like psoriasis, eczema, and hives. The practice focuses on patient-first care, combining the personal touch of private practices with the benefits of a large network of physicians and subspecialty thought leaders.

Primary Electronic Health Record System

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

U.S. Dermatology Partners utilizes the EMA electronic health record (EHR) system developed by Modernizing Medicine (now known as ModMed). The patient portal associated with this EHR system is referred to as the "Patient Portal" within the ModMed EMA platform. While U.S. Dermatology Partners also uses the InstaMed Patient Portal specifically for processing bill payments, the broader patient portal functionalities, including accessing medical records and sending messages to providers, are integrated within the ModMed EMA system. Documentation from U.S. Dermatology Partners explicitly mentions the availability of "electronic (EMA) visit notes" within their patient portal, confirming the connection to the EMA system. ModMed's EMA is highlighted as an AI-powered, specialty-specific EHR platform with a range of features, including a patient portal for streamlined patient interaction.
