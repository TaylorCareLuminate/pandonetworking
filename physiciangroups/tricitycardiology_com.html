About the Practice

Tri-City Cardiology Consultants PC provides comprehensive heart and vascular care in the Phoenix Metro area. They specialize in medical cardiology, interventional cardiology, electrophysiology, and vascular medicine, offering prevention, diagnosis, intervention, and treatment for a full spectrum of cardiac conditions. Services include diagnostic testing, disease management for conditions like heart failure and hypertension, vein and vascular treatments, and various procedures such as cardiac catheterizations, angioplasty, stenting, pacemaker implantation, and ablation.
