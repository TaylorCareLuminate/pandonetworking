About the Practice

Upstate Orthopedics is a comprehensive orthopedic surgery practice affiliated with the Department of Orthopedic Surgery at SUNY Upstate Medical University, serving Central New York. [2, 5] They provide top-of-the-line specialized orthopedic care for patients of all ages, offering consultations, evaluations, and treatment for a full range of orthopedic conditions and problems, including sports medicine, pediatric orthopedics, hand and wrist microsurgery, upper extremity surgery, orthopedic oncology, adult and pediatric spine surgery, foot and ankle surgery, joint replacement/reconstruction, and orthopedic trauma. [2, 3] Their goal is to be a one-stop destination for all orthopedic-related problems, delivering patient-focused musculoskeletal care to facilitate patients' return to an active and healthy lifestyle. [2]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Upstate Orthopedics (upstateorthopedics.com) utilizes the Upstate MyChart patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal developed by Epic Systems. This indicates that Upstate Orthopedics is using Epic as their Electronic Health Record (EHR) vendor. The Upstate MyChart portal provides patients with secure online access to various health management tools. Patients can use the portal to review lab test results, request or cancel appointments, refill medications, send messages to their healthcare providers, and access their immunization records. The MyChart application is accessible online and via a mobile app for both Apple and Android devices.
