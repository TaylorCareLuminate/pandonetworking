About the Practice

UCLA Health is a leading academic medical center and healthcare system based in Southern California, consistently ranked among America's top hospitals. [2, 21] Its mission encompasses providing state-of-the-art patient care, generating research discoveries for new treatments and diagnoses, and training future healthcare professionals. [3] UCLA Health offers a comprehensive and advanced range of services through its five medical centers and hospitals and more than 280 clinics and locations across Southern California and the Central Coast. [3] They provide care in virtually every field of medicine, including primary care, immediate care, and numerous specialties such as organ transplantation, cardiac surgery, neurosurgery, cancer treatment, pediatrics, internal medicine, family medicine, allergy & immunology, cardiology, dermatology, digestive diseases, endocrinology, nephrology, pain management, pulmonology, sleep medicine, bariatric surgery, rheumatology, and integrative medicine approaches like East-West medicine. [2, 3, 7, 13, 15, 16, 18, 21] As a Level 1 trauma center, Ronald Reagan UCLA Medical Center provides critical and cutting-edge services with specialized floors for various medical disciplines. [21]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UCLA Health uses Epic as its electronic health record (EHR) system, which they refer to as CareConnect. The patient portal used by UCLA Health is called myUCLAhealth, and it is the MyChart application, which is the patient portal developed by Epic. This aligns with the common practice of healthcare systems using Epic to offer a patient portal branded as MyChart.
