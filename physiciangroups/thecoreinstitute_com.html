About the Practice

The CORE Institute is a nationally recognized leader in orthopedic and neurological care, providing a wide range of services including Orthopedics, Spine, Neurology, Fractures & Trauma, Pain Management, Physical Therapy, and more across multiple locations in Arizona and Michigan. [2, 4, 5, 17, 19] Built on a foundation of pioneering research, academics, and community service, the institute is dedicated to delivering excellent patient outcomes through evidence-based medicine, standardized protocols, and a patient-centered approach, aiming to help patients return to the activities they love and "Keep Life in Motion". [2, 3, 4, 9, 12, 19] They also operate The CORE Institute Specialty Hospital in Phoenix for state-of-the-art orthopedic surgical care and offer an Urgent Ortho program for same-day injury treatment. [6, 13, 14]

### The CORE Institute Utilizes Multiple Patient Technologies, Including Portals by MedBridge and One Medical Passport The CORE Institute provides its patients with access to a "Patient Portal" for managing aspects of their care such as bill payment and prescription refills. While the branding on the portal is generic, the login page indicates that the portal is powered by **MedBridge Inc.**. In addition to the general Patient Portal, The CORE Institute also employs **One Medical Passport®** for patients to submit and access their medical history information, particularly in preparation for surgical procedures. One Medical Passport, now a part of **Mnet Health**, is described as a specialized electronic health record (EHR) solution focused on streamlining the pre-admissions process and is designed to integrate with existing EHR systems used by healthcare providers. Furthermore, The CORE Institute utilizes a proprietary patient engagement mobile application called **myrecovery**. This app supports patients throughout their orthopedic and spine care journey by providing educational content, enabling progress tracking, and facilitating communication with their care team. The myrecovery platform is known to integrate with a variety of EHR systems. While these resources provide insight into the patient-facing technologies used by The CORE Institute and their respective vendors, the available information does not explicitly name the primary, comprehensive EHR system that The CORE Institute uses for its internal clinical operations, with which these patient portals and applications likely integrate.
