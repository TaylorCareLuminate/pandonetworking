About the Practice

The Emergency Department at Long Island Jewish Forest Hills, part of Northwell Health, is a 911 receiving hospital that sees approximately 60,000 patients annually, treating all types of acute conditions and traumatic injuries. It is staffed by residency-trained, board-certified physicians and registered nurses certified in emergency and trauma care. The department offers state-of-the-art services including New York state-certified Stroke services, an AHA-certified heart station, a separate fast-track unit for minor illnesses and injuries, bedside registration, and advanced technology like ultrasound, telestroke, and telepsychiatry services. For complex cardiac, neurosurgical, or pediatric cases, they stabilize and transfer patients to tertiary facilities within Northwell Health. The department is also a New York state-designated Stroke Center and a certified heart station. [2, 3, 12, 18]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Long Island Jewish Valley Stream (LIJVS) - Department of Emergency Medicine, as part of Northwell Health, currently utilizes the **FollowMyHealth** patient portal. Northwell Health is in the process of transitioning its electronic health record (EHR) system to **Epic**. This transition is being rolled out in phases, with the initial launch expected in 2025. While the current patient portal is FollowMyHealth, Epic's patient portal is widely known as MyChart. The implementation of Epic suggests that Northwell Health's patient portal will likely transition to MyChart in the future to align with the new EHR system.
