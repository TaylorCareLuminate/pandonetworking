About the Practice

Select Specialty Hospitals in Phoenix are critical illness recovery hospitals specializing in treating patients with medically complex conditions, trauma, or intensive care needs requiring extended stays. Their physician-led, interdisciplinary teams provide specialized care and advanced treatment to help patients recover and regain independence in abilities such as breathing, speaking, eating, walking, and thinking. They offer programs and care for conditions including pulmonary/ventilator liberation, brain injury, cardiac conditions, infectious disease, medically complex care, neurologic conditions, renal disorders, and wound care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Select Specialty Hospital - Phoenix Inc., which is part of the Select Medical health network, utilizes MyChart as their patient portal. MyChart is a secure online platform that provides patients with access to their medical records. Evidence suggests that the electronic health record (EHR) vendor behind the MyChart patient portal for Select Medical facilities is Epic. This is further supported by the observation that MyChart is a patient portal commonly associated with Epic Systems Corporation in other hospital settings as well.
