About the Practice

Stamford Health is a not-for-profit health system in Fairfield County, CT, centered around Stamford Hospital, a 305-bed community teaching hospital. [4, 14, 16] It includes the Stamford Health Medical Group with over 150 providers across 40 locations and the Stamford Hospital Foundation. [4, 11] Stamford Health provides a wide range of services from primary care to advanced specialty care, including partnerships for cancer care (with Dana-Farber Brigham Cancer Center), cardiac and neuro care (with Columbia University Irving Medical Center), and orthopedics (with HSS). [4, 5, 11] They are a teaching affiliate of Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians & Surgeons and are committed to high-quality, patient-centered care, holding a Planetree designation. [4, 14, 16]
