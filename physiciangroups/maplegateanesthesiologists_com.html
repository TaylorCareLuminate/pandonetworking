About the Practice

Maple-Gate Anesthesiologists, P.C., established in 1983, is the largest anesthesia group in Western New York, affiliated with the University of Buffalo Department of Anesthesiology. [1, 7] They provide comprehensive anesthesia care including general, regional, specialized, bariatric, cardiothoracic, obstetrical, ambulatory, perioperative pain management, neurosurgical, and vascular anesthesia at affiliated hospitals and surgery centers, demonstrating dedication to high standards of patient care. [1, 7]
