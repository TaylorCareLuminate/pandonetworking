About the Practice

Fresenius Medical Care is the world's leading provider of products and services for individuals with renal diseases, specifically chronic kidney failure and end-stage renal disease. The company offers comprehensive kidney care along the entire dialysis value chain and the Renal Care Continuum, providing dialysis services through a vast network of outpatient dialysis centers and supplying a full range of dialysis products, including machines, dialyzers, and related disposables. Their services encompass in-center hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, home hemodialysis, vascular access care, renal pharmacy, laboratory and diagnostic services, critical care, and patient education and support to help individuals manage their condition and improve their quality of life.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Fresenius Medical Care North America utilizes a patient portal branded as PatientHub. This portal allows patients to manage various aspects of their kidney care, including viewing lab results, tracking supply orders, and communicating with their care team. Fresenius Medical Care North America selected EPIC EHR as their electronic health record system in 2020. This indicates that the PatientHub portal used by Fresenius Medical Care is likely integrated with or powered by the Epic EHR system, similar to how Epic uses MyChart as its patient portal. While the search results confirm the use of PatientHub by Fresenius and the selection of Epic as their EHR, they do not explicitly state that PatientHub is the direct equivalent of MyChart for Fresenius or that it is a branded version of Epic's patient portal. However, the selection of Epic strongly suggests a connection between the EHR and the patient portal.
