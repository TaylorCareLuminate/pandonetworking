About the Practice

Sun Life Health is a non-profit Federally-Qualified Community Health Center providing accessible, high-quality preventative health care services for over 45 years. [2] Serving over 50,000 patients, including a significant percentage of children, they offer a wide range of services such as Primary Care, Ob/Gyn, Pediatrics, Pharmacy, General and Pediatric Dentistry, Orthodontics, and Integrated Behavioral health care. [2, 4, 7, 11, 13] Accredited by The Joint Commission, Sun Life Health is committed to the well-being of their patients, viewing healthcare as more than just medicine but as human care focused on overall health and wellness. [2, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Sun Life Family Health Center (sunlifehealth.org) utilizes a patient portal that is accessible via a web portal and promotes the use of the "Healow App" for mobile access. The Healow App is a patient engagement platform developed by eClinicalWorks. It is designed to integrate with the eClinicalWorks Electronic Health Record (EHR) system, allowing patients to access their medical records, schedule appointments, view lab results, and communicate with their healthcare providers. While Healow is described in some instances as being potentially EHR-agnostic for certain solutions like Telehealth, its primary and most comprehensive integration is with the eClinicalWorks EHR. Based on Sun Life Family Health Center's use of the Healow App and patient portal, it is highly likely that they are using the eClinicalWorks EHR system.
