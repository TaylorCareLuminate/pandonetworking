About the Practice

Mass General Brigham is a not-for-profit, integrated academic health care system founded by Brigham and Women's Hospital and Massachusetts General Hospital, which are teaching affiliates of Harvard Medical School and leading academic medical centers. The system connects a full continuum of care across academic medical centers, community and specialty hospitals, a health insurance plan, physician networks, community health centers, home care, and long-term care services. [2, 4, 10] It is committed to patient care, is the largest hospital-based research enterprise in the United States with an annual budget exceeding $2 billion, and focuses on teaching and service to the community. [2, 4, 7] Mass General Brigham offers a full range of care and includes several nationally recognized hospitals. [7, 8]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Mass General Brigham uses an online patient portal called Patient Gateway. This portal is a rebranded version of MyChart, which is an application that extends the Epic electronic health record system. According to information from Mass General Brigham and related hospitals, Patient Gateway provides patients with a secure online connection to their healthcare providers, allowing them to communicate with doctors, schedule appointments, request prescription renewals, view test results and after-visit summaries, access health record information, and manage billing. The underlying EHR system that powers Patient Gateway at Mass General Brigham is Epic. This is further supported by the mention that patients might access health information from external providers if they use the same EHR vendor, noted as Epic. The Patient Gateway login page also displays the "MyChart is Epic logo," confirming the connection to Epic Systems Corporation.
