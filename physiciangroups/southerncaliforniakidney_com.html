About the Practice

Southern California Kidney Consultants, also referred to as Southern California Kidney Associates, is a nephrology practice providing high-quality and compassionate care for patients with kidney disease. They specialize in the treatment of acute and chronic kidney disease, diabetic kidney disease, difficult-to-control hypertension, electrolyte abnormalities, glomerulonephritis, home dialysis, hypertensive kidney disease, kidney stones, kidney transplantation management, and outpatient dialysis. However, the direct link of "Southern California Kidney Consultants" at a specific location (Garden Grove) to the MyChart portal points towards the use of Epic at that facility.
