About the Practice

Hematology-Oncology Associates of Central New York (HOACNY) is a private, comprehensive cancer center established in 1982, dedicated to providing high-quality care to patients dealing with cancer and blood disorders. [2, 3, 6] They offer a range of services including medical oncology, radiation oncology, hematology services, diagnostic imaging, access to clinical research trials, and various support services in a healing environment for the mind, body, and spirit. Methodology & Findings

Hematology-Oncology Associates of CNY uses a patient portal called CareSpace. CareSpace is a digital health platform developed by Persivia. Persivia's CareSpace platform is designed to integrate with various EHR and Practice Management systems, and is specifically mentioned as working in conjunction with systems like Cerner and other ambulatory EHRs. One search result also directly links CareSpace to Flatiron Health's OncoEMR® as a user-friendly patient portal option that integrates with their oncology EHR software. Based on this information, Hematology-Oncology Associates of CNY is using the CareSpace patient portal, which is a platform developed by Persivia that can integrate with different EHR systems.
