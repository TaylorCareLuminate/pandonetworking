About the Practice

Inova is a leading nonprofit healthcare provider in Northern Virginia and the Washington, D.C. metropolitan area, offering world-class healthcare through an expansive network of hospitals, primary and specialty care practices, emergency and urgent care centers, and outpatient services. Inova provides comprehensive care for more than one million unique patients annually, covering a broad range of specialties. It is home to Northern Virginia's only Level 1 Trauma Center and Level 4 Neonatal Intensive Care Unit and is recognized for excellence in areas such as heart and vascular, cancer, neurosciences, pediatrics, women's services, and behavioral health, providing high-quality healthcare to each person in every community it serves. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Inova, the not-for-profit healthcare system based in Northern Virginia, utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a secure online platform that allows patients to access and manage various aspects of their health information, including upcoming appointments, medications, test results, visit summaries, and billing statements. This patient portal is a product of Epic, a leading provider of electronic health records (EHR) systems. Through MyChart, Inova patients can also schedule appointments, communicate with their healthcare providers, request prescription renewals, and manage their bills. Inova also offers a mobile app that provides access to the MyChart portal, enhancing convenience for patients.
