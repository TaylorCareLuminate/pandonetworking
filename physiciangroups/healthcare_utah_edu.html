About the Practice

University of Utah Health is the Mountain West's only academic health care system, providing patient care, education, and research. [11, 12] It serves Utah, Idaho, Wyoming, Montana, western Colorado, and much of Nevada. [11] The system includes five hospitals, 12 community health centers, and several specialty centers, offering a wide range of primary and specialty care, including oncology, cardiology, diabetes treatment, genetics, ophthalmology, orthopedics, neuroscience, psychiatry, and women's health. [11, 12, 16]

Methodology & Findings

University of Utah Health utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal system provided by Epic. Through the MyChart portal, patients of University of Utah Health can access various services, including communicating with their healthcare team, getting test results, managing prescriptions and refills, paying bills, and managing appointments.
