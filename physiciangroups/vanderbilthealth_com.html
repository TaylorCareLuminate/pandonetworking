About the Practice

Vanderbilt University Medical Center (VUMC) is a prominent academic health system located in Nashville, Tennessee, with numerous hospitals, clinics, and facilities extending throughout Middle Tennessee. As an independent non-profit organization academically affiliated with Vanderbilt University, VUMC is a leading provider of patient care, biomedical research, and professional health education in the Southeast. It offers comprehensive care across a wide array of specialties, including Level 1 Trauma, Level 4 Neonatal Intensive Care, Level 3 Burn, organ transplantation, cancer, cardiology, neurology, pediatrics, psychiatry, rehabilitation, and various surgical and medical disciplines. [2, 5, 6, 9, 13] Driven by the vision of being the world leader in advancing personalized health, VUMC is committed to personalizing the patient experience through a caring spirit and distinctive capabilities. Methodology & Findings

Vanderbilt University Medical Center (VUMC) utilizes "My Health at Vanderbilt" as its secure online patient portal. This portal allows patients to manage medical details, schedule appointments, and more. VUMC's electronic health record (EHR) system is Epic. The "My Health at Vanderbilt" patient portal is connected to this Epic system, providing a unified medical record view across hospital, clinic, and home care settings.
