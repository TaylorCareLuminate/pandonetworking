About the Practice

Nationwide Children's Hospital is one of the largest and most comprehensive pediatric hospitals and research institutes in the United States, serving as an internationally prominent academic health care system. The hospital provides compassionate care, advanced diagnostics, treatments, and research for a wide range of pediatric conditions, with over 1.7 million patient visits annually from across the US and internationally. Recognized among U.S. News & World Report's Best Children's Hospitals Honor Roll, it houses a top-funded pediatric research institute and the largest pediatric behavioral and mental health treatment and research pavilion of its kind. Nationwide Children's offers a vast array of pediatric specialties, including but not limited to cardiology, oncology, behavioral health, surgery, critical care, and various other medical and surgical subspecialties.

Methodology & Findings

Nationwide Children's Hospital utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal product developed by Epic. Nationwide Children's Hospital selected Epic Care Everywhere for their Electronic Health Record system in 2019. The hospital also uses Epic EHR and analytics tools. While eClinicalWorks is used as a community-wide solution for continuity of care across affiliated practices, the main hospital's EHR system is Epic, which aligns with their use of the MyChart patient portal.
