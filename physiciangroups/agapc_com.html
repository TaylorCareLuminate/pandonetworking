About the Practice

The Anesthesia Group of Albany, PC is the largest anesthesia group in the Albany-Saratoga region of Upstate New York, providing comprehensive and compassionate anesthesia care for over 40 years. They specialize in general and regional anesthesia for a variety of procedures including endoscopic, cardiac, orthopedic, pediatric, obstetric, cosmetic, and general surgical procedures, and also serve ambulatory surgery centers and an endoscopy center.
