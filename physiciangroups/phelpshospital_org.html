About the Practice

Phelps Hospital, part of Northwell Health, is a full-service acute care community hospital located in Sleepy Hollow, NY. It is a 218-bed facility offering a broad range of inpatient and outpatient services across more than 60 medical specialties. The hospital is recognized for excellence in various areas including cancer care, stroke care, surgical care, gastroenterology, and hyperbaric medicine, and is dedicated to improving community health regardless of a patient's ability to pay.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Phelps Memorial Hospital Association, associated with the domain phelpshospital.org and operating as part of Northwell Health, utilizes the FollowMyHealth patient portal. Northwell Health, the parent system of Phelps Hospital, has selected and is in the process of implementing Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) system. This implementation is expected to be rolled out in phases, beginning in 2025. The FollowMyHealth patient portal allows patients to access their health information, communicate with providers, manage appointments, and handle prescription refills. While FollowMyHealth can integrate with various EHR systems, Northwell Health's adoption of Epic indicates the EHR system that will be underlying the patient portal experience for Phelps Hospital patients as the transition progresses. Prior to the full Epic implementation across Northwell Health, the specific EHR system used by Phelps Hospital in conjunction with FollowMyHealth would have been dependent on the systems in place within the Northwell Health network before the transition. However, the stated strategic direction is towards a unified Epic EHR across the health system.
