About the Practice

Associated Medical Professionals of NY (A.M.P.) is a leading multi-specialty medical practice serving the Central New York region. [10, 11] Established in 2008, A.M.P. offers a robust suite of services including advanced urological care, men's health, women's health, pediatric urology, cancer care, radiation oncology, interventional radiology, robotic surgery, pathology, lithotripsy, imaging, and clinical research, utilizing innovative treatments and advanced technology. Methodology & Findings

Associated Medical Professionals of NY PLLC (ampofny.com) utilizes a patient portal branded as MEDENT. This portal offers patients various functionalities, including the ability to request appointments, exchange messages with healthcare providers, request prescription refills, and access their medical records. While the search results clearly identify MEDENT as the patient portal used by Associated Medical Professionals of NY, they do not explicitly state that MEDENT is also the underlying Electronic Health Record (EHR) vendor. However, patient portals are typically integrated with or provided by the same company that supplies the EHR system to a healthcare practice. Therefore, Associated Medical Professionals of NY PLLC uses the MEDENT patient portal. It is strongly suggested that MEDENT is also their EHR vendor, although this direct link is not definitively confirmed by the search results.
