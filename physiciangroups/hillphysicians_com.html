Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Hill Physicians Medical Group utilizes a patient portal branded as MyHillChart. This portal is licensed from Epic Systems Corporation, indicating that Epic is the Electronic Health Record (EHR) system used by Hill Physicians Medical Group, similar to how MyChart is the patient portal for Epic. MyHillChart allows members to manage their health information and interact with their care team securely online. Features of the MyHillChart portal include accessing test results, communicating with doctors, requesting prescription refills, and managing appointments. It is accessible via computer, tablet, or mobile phone. While MyHillChart is available at some practices within Hill Physicians Medical Group, it may not be available at all locations.
