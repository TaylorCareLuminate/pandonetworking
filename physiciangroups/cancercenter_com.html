About the Practice

City of Hope Phoenix, formerly Cancer Treatment Centers of America Phoenix, is a comprehensive cancer hospital and outpatient care network with locations in Goodyear, Gilbert, North Phoenix, and Scottsdale, Arizona. [12, 2, 4] They provide personalized, comprehensive cancer treatment for the whole patient, addressing mind, body, and soul. [2, 4] Specializing in areas such as blood cancer care, bone marrow transplantation (BMT), and CAR T cell therapy, they offer advanced treatments including genomic testing, precision cancer treatment, surgery, radiation, immunotherapy, chemotherapy, and supportive care services. [2, 4, 12, 13] The center also engages in groundbreaking clinical trials and collaborates with the Translational Genomics Research Institute (TGen). [2, 4]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

City of Hope Phoenix, formerly Cancer Treatment Centers of America, utilizes a patient portal branded as MyCityofHope. This patient portal is powered by Epic's MyChart technology. City of Hope has been implementing the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system across its entire network, including the Phoenix location, with the implementation expected to enhance collaboration and standardize protocols. The MyCityofHope patient portal provides patients with secure online access to their health information, allowing them to view test results, communicate with their care team, request appointments, and manage their medical records, consistent with the functionalities of Epic's MyChart. The login page for MyCityofHope explicitly mentions "MyChart by Epic" and that MyChart is licensed from Epic Systems Corporation, confirming the connection between the patient portal and the EHR vendor.
