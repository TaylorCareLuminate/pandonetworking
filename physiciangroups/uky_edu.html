About the Practice

The University of Kentucky, located in Lexington, Kentucky, is a public land-grant research university founded in 1865 with a mission to educate, innovate, heal, and serve the Commonwealth. [3, 9, 17] It is a comprehensive institution with 16 academic colleges, a graduate school, and over 200 academic programs across various disciplines. [9, 17] UK HealthCare, its clinical enterprise, is consistently ranked as the No. 1 hospital in Kentucky and is recognized nationally for numerous adult and pediatric specialties including cancer care, ear, nose & throat, OB/GYN, gastroenterology, GI surgery, geriatrics, neurology & neurosurgery, orthopedics, pulmonology & lung surgery, and urology. [2, 10, 16, 20] As an academic health system, UK HealthCare provides advanced, personalized specialty care and access to the latest treatments, driven by its research and highly trained medical staff. [20]
