About the Practice

Westfield Memorial Hospital, part of Allegheny Health Network (AHN), provides quality care in western New York. [3] The hospital offers a range of primary services including cardiac rehabilitation, physical therapy, outpatient surgery, radiology services (including mammography, CT, ultrasound, and bone density scans), laboratory testing, primary care, wound care, infusion care, and 24/7 emergency services. [2, 7] Specialty care is available across various disciplines such as orthopedics, cardiovascular medicine, general surgery, gastroenterology, neuroscience, endocrinology, and more. [3, 4] The hospital is committed to meeting the health needs of the community with compassion and excellence. [12, 15]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Westfield Memorial Hospital, as part of the Allegheny Health Network (AHN), utilizes the MyChart patient portal. AHN has been using Epic's electronic health record (EHR) system, including the MyChart patient portal, since 2013. Therefore, the patient portal vendor/branding used by Westfield Memorial Hospital is MyChart, which is the patient-facing portal for the Epic EHR system.
