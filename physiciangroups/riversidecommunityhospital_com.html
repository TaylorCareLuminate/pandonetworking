About the Practice

Riverside Community Hospital, part of HCA Healthcare, is a 542-licensed bed acute care hospital serving the Inland Empire region since 1901. [1] It is recognized as a Level I Trauma Center, Comprehensive Stroke Center, and a STEMI (heart attack) Receiving Center. [1, 15] The hospital provides a wide range of medical services and specialties, including trauma, cancer care, neurosurgery, orthopedics, general surgery, cardiology, stroke intervention, robotic surgery, heart care, and Level III Neonatal Intensive Care. [1, 9, 15, 19] They are committed to providing high-quality, patient-centered care combining skilled caregivers with advanced technology. [1]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Riverside Health System (riversidecommunityhospital.com) utilizes the MyHealthONE patient portal. This patient portal is associated with HCA Healthcare, which operates Riverside Community Hospital. HCA Healthcare has selected MEDITECH Expanse as their primary go-forward Electronic Health Record (EHR) platform for implementation across their facilities. This indicates that the MyHealthONE patient portal used by Riverside Community Hospital is the patient-facing interface for the MEDITECH Expanse EHR system.
