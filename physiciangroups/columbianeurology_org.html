About the Practice

The CUIMC/Neurological Institute of New York, located on the campus of Columbia University Irving Medical Center, is a premier center for world-class patient care, education, and research in neurological diseases. Established in 1909 as the nation's first specialty hospital for nervous system disorders, it offers comprehensive care across a wide range of specialties including Aging and Dementia, ALS, Child Neurology, Epilepsy, Movement Disorders, Stroke, and more, provided by subspecialty-trained neurologists. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Columbia University Irving Medical Center (CUIMC) and the Neurological Institute of New York utilize a patient portal called "Connect". This portal serves as a unified access point for patients across CUIMC, NewYork-Presbyterian, and Weill Cornell Medicine. The Connect patient portal is integrated with the Epic electronic medical record (EHR system, which is used by these institutions. CUIMC and NewYork-Presbyterian fully transitioned to the Epic system and the Connect portal on February 1, 2020.
