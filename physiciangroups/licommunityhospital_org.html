About the Practice

Long Island Community Hospital, formerly Brookhaven Memorial Hospital Medical Center, is a 306-bed community hospital located in Patchogue, NY, serving Suffolk County. It offers a range of healthcare services including a certified Level II Trauma Center, cardiac catheterization laboratory, a New York State Department of Health-designated Stroke Center, primary and specialty care, radiology, laboratory, sports and physical therapy, dialysis, cardio-pulmonary rehabilitation, wellness, HIV/AIDS care, and inpatient psychiatric services. The hospital is committed to providing leading-edge health care solutions and personalized comfort to the community.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Brookhaven Memorial Hospital Medical Center, now known as NYU Langone Hospital—Suffolk, utilizes the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. The patient portal used by NYU Langone Health, including NYU Langone Hospital—Suffolk, is called NYU Langone Health MyChart. This aligns with the common practice of healthcare systems using Epic to brand their patient portal as MyChart.
