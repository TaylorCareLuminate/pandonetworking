About the Practice

Sound Physicians is a physician-founded and led multispecialty medical group providing services across the acute episode of care in over 400 hospitals across 45 states. Specializing in hospital medicine, intensivist/critical care, emergency medicine, anesthesia, telehealth, and physician advisor services, they focus on improving clinical quality, efficiency, patient satisfaction, and financial performance for their hospital partners. They leverage a proprietary technology platform and data-driven insights to optimize clinical performance and deliver high-quality, patient-centered care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Sound Physicians utilizes different patient portals depending on the nature of the patient's interaction and the healthcare facility where they received care. For billing and payment management, Sound Physicians directs patients to portals branded as MyDocBill.com. These portals, such as mydocbill.com/sndp, mydocbill.com/snda, and mydocbill.com/sned, are used for hospital medicine, anesthesia, and emergency medicine services, respectively. MyDocBill.com is a service provided by Zotec Partners, a company specializing in revenue cycle management and patient engagement solutions. Through the MyDocBill portal, patients can manage their accounts, view billing statements, and make payments. In situations where Sound Physicians clinicians provide care within partner hospitals or health systems, patients may utilize the patient portal provided by that specific institution to access their health records. For example, Sound Physicians is mentioned in conjunction with Mount Carmel Health System and Trinity Health Mid-Atlantic, both of which utilize the MyChart patient portal. MyChart is the patient portal commonly associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. This suggests that if a patient was treated by a Sound Physicians clinician at a hospital using Epic and MyChart, they would access their clinical information through MyChart. While Sound Physicians integrates with various technologies for clinical workflows and telemedicine, such as AmplifyMD, the search results do not indicate that Sound Physicians uses a single,统一 EHR system across all its service lines and locations that directly links to the MyDocBill.com portal for comprehensive medical record access in the way MyChart is integrated with Epic for patient records. MyDocBill.com appears to be primarily focused on the financial aspects of patient encounters with Sound Physicians.
