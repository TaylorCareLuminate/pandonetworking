About the Practice

New York Bone & Joint Specialists is a top-rated, full-service orthopedic surgery and sports medicine practice providing comprehensive care for nearly all orthopedic and sports medicine injuries or conditions, including the Shoulder, Knee, Hip, Back, Spine, Neck, Foot, Ankle, Elbow, and Hand & Wrist. [3, 6, 10] Their care philosophy emphasizes preservation, prioritizing non-surgical treatments and utilizing advanced non-surgical options whenever possible, successfully treating over 90% of cases without surgery. [6, 7, 8, 11, 13] When surgery is necessary, their expert surgeons perform complex procedures with a focus on preserving important structures for long-term joint health. [6, 7, 8] They offer a full spectrum of care from traditional physical therapy and regenerative medical injections to state-of-the-art surgical repairs and reconstructions. [6, 7, 8, 12]
