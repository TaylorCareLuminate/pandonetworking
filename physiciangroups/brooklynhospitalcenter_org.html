About the Practice

The Brooklyn Hospital Center is a 464-licensed-bed, full-service community teaching hospital located in Downtown Brooklyn, New York City. [4] It is affiliated with the Mount Sinai Health System. [4, 6] The hospital provides a wide range of inpatient, acute care, outpatient, primary, preventive, and specialty care services. [2, 4, 7, 9] Key service areas and specialties include Emergency Medicine, Cancer Care, Heart & Vascular Care, Imaging, Men's Health, Neurology & Neurosurgery, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Primary Care, Surgical Services, Women's Health, and various specialized services like stroke care, wound care, dialysis, and HIV primary care through its PATH Center. [4, 5, 6, 7]
