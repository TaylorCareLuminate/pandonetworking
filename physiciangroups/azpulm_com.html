About the Practice

Arizona Pulmonary Specialists is a multi-specialty private practice located in Phoenix and Scottsdale, Arizona, offering comprehensive care for patients with lung conditions, infectious diseases, and rheumatologic disorders. [2, 4] Their services encompass pulmonary medicine, critical care, infectious diseases, sleep apnea, and are recognized as a PHA-accredited Pulmonary Hypertension Care Center. [3, 4, 6, 8] The practice is dedicated to providing exceptional, compassionate, and personalized care, emphasizing patient education, advanced procedures, and clinical research to improve patient outcomes and help them breathe better. [2, 4, 7]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Arizona Pulmonary Specialists (azpulm.com) utilizes a patient portal branded as MyHealthRecord.com. This patient portal is operated and hosted by Greenway Health, LLC. MyHealthRecord.com allows patients of Arizona Pulmonary Specialists to access their health records, view doctor's notes, message their care team, request prescription renewals, and review current medications online. While the name "My Health Record" is also associated with an Australian digital health record platform, the domain name used by Arizona Pulmonary Specialists (MyHealthRecord.com) indicates it is the service provided by Greenway Health. Therefore, the EHR vendor likely used by Arizona Pulmonary Specialists is Greenway Health.
