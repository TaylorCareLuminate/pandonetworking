About the Practice

Cayuga Medical Center at Ithaca, part of the Cayuga Health System, is a not-for-profit acute-care hospital located in Ithaca, New York, providing comprehensive diagnostic and treatment services to residents of Tompkins and surrounding counties. The hospital offers a wide range of inpatient and outpatient care, including adult and pediatric services, maternal-child health, a Level II Neonatal Nursery, general and specialized surgery (such as neurosurgery, orthopedics, and vascular surgery), adult and adolescent inpatient psychiatry, medical rehabilitation, physical therapy, sports medicine, emergency care, cardiology, oncology services, and behavioral health.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Cayuga Medical Center at Ithaca, part of Cayuga Health, is transitioning to using Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) system. With this transition, their new patient portal is MyChart. Previously, Cayuga Health utilized "Cayuga Health Medent Mobile and MyCayugaHealth patient portal," but these portals were no longer available as of March 22, 2025, as the system moved to Epic and the MyChart patient portal. MyChart is noted as offering a streamlined experience for various patient tasks, including scheduling appointments, communicating with providers, accessing medical records, and paying bills. Information from previous portals is being securely transferred to MyChart.
