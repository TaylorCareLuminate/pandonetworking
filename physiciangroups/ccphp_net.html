About the Practice

Castle Connolly Private Health Partners (CCPHP) is a leading concierge (membership-model) medicine organization. [6, 8, 11] They collaborate with exceptional physicians across the nation to provide a personalized and comprehensive healthcare experience focused on enhancing the physician-patient relationship. [8, 11, 15] Their services include enhanced access to physicians with amenities such as 24/7 communication, same- or next-day appointments, expedited referrals to specialists, and a robust wellness program (SENS Solution®) covering sleep, exercise, nutrition, and stress management. [7, 8, 11, 14] CCPHP aims to empower physicians to practice with fewer administrative burdens and more time for patient care, while offering members a more convenient and attentive approach to health and wellbeing. [6, 10, 13, 15]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

While Castle Connolly Private Health Partners (CCPHP) collaborates with a network of concierge physicians, it appears that the specific Electronic Health Record (EHR) system and associated patient portal used may vary among the affiliated practices. There is no single EHR or patient portal that is stated to be universally mandated or used by all CCPHP affiliated physicians. However, search results indicate that at least one practice collaborating with CCPHP, **Medical Associates of the Hudson Valley**, utilizes the **Medent Patient Portal**. This portal allows their patients to communicate securely with their provider, access medical records and test results, request prescription refills, and manage appointments and billing. This suggests that Medent is one of the patient portal systems in use within the CCPHP network. CCPHP itself emphasizes providing members with benefits such as a "HIPAA compliant patient portal" and a "telehealth and messaging app" for 24/7 communication with their doctor and access to medical records. They also offer a separate AI-powered platform called **Engagement RX (ERX)**, which serves as a wellness hub for members, focusing on tracking progress, accessing health content, and connecting with health coaches, distinct from a traditional patient portal for medical care. Based on the available information, it is most likely that CCPHP-affiliated practices maintain their own EHR systems and patient portals, with Medent being one example used by at least one practice in the network. CCPHP appears to focus on providing overarching support and technology that integrates with the existing systems of their affiliated physicians, rather than requiring a specific platform.
