About the Practice

Wellstar is a not-for-profit integrated healthcare system based in Georgia. It is one of the largest healthcare systems in the state, providing compassionate and world-class care across a wide range of medical services and specialties. These include primary care, heart care, cancer treatment, neurological disorders, women's health, pediatrics, bone & joint care, emergency care, and various therapies. Care is delivered through a comprehensive network of hospitals, medical office locations, urgent care centers, health parks, and specialized facilities.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Wellstar Health System utilizes the patient portal branded as Wellstar MyChart. This portal allows patients secure online access to their health records, including viewing test results, scheduling appointments, and messaging their care team. Wellstar MyChart is provided by Epic. Epic is a well-known vendor of electronic health record (EHR) systems, and "MyChart" is the patient portal component of their EHR suite. Some information also indicates a transition from a previous portal, VIP Portal, to Wellstar MyChart in late 2024.
