About the Practice

Midwestern University Clinics provide comprehensive, patient-centered healthcare services across multiple specialties, including dental, eye, family medicine, physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech-language pathology, osteopathic manipulative medicine, podiatry, clinical psychology, and veterinary medicine. [3, 4, 5, 8, 13, 15] Operating in state-of-the-art facilities, the clinics serve as a key training ground where advanced graduate-level students gain practical experience under the direct supervision of experienced faculty practitioners, ensuring high-quality care at affordable prices. [3, 4, 8, 12, 15] The scope of services allows for treating a wide range of health issues and offers convenient inter-specialty referrals.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Midwestern University Clinics utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal platform that is used in conjunction with electronic health record (EHR) systems, and Midwestern University Clinics uses Epic as their EHR system. Patients of Midwestern University Clinics can use MyChart to access their health records, manage appointments including scheduling, rescheduling, and canceling, complete pre-appointment check-in, securely message the healthcare team, view test results, request prescription refills, manage medications, and make payments. MyChart also offers features for families to manage multiple patient accounts through proxy access.
