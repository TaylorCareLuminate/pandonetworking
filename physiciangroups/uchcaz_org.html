About the Practice

United Community Health Center (UCHC) is a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) and a medical home providing accessible, comprehensive, innovative, compassionate, and culturally sensitive primary and preventive healthcare to southern Arizona communities, open to all regardless of ability to pay. [4, 6, 13] They offer a range of services including primary care for all ages covering illness, injuries, chronic conditions, immunizations, and health maintenance, as well as pediatrics, behavioral health services, and family dental care. [7, 8] Additional patient support services include transportation, lab draws, enrollment assistance for programs, and a discount pharmacy program. [8, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

United Community Health Center (UCHC) in Arizona utilizes the **HealthCare Support Portal** as their patient portal. This portal facilitates communication between patients and providers, offering features such as secure messaging and access from various devices. While UCHC's website refers to their patient portal specifically as "HealthCare Support Portal," evidence suggests that their Electronic Health Record (EHR) vendor is likely **NextGen Healthcare**. Several search results indicate that NextGen Healthcare provides a patient portal solution that integrates with their EHR system. NextGen Healthcare's patient portal is sometimes referred to as "NextGen Patient Portal" and has also been associated with the name "YourHealthFile Patient Portal" for co-branding purposes. Notably, NextGen Healthcare serves Community Health Centers (CHCs) and Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs), which aligns with UCHC's designation as an FQHC. Although a direct statement confirming "HealthCare Support Portal" is the specific branding for NextGen's patient portal at UCHC was not found, the consistent use of "HealthCare Support Portal" by UCHC and the strong association of NextGen Healthcare with integrated patient portals for FQHCs make it highly probable that UCHC is using a NextGen Healthcare EHR system with their patient portal branded as HealthCare Support Portal.
