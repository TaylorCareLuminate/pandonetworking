About the Practice

University Medical Imaging (UMI) is a diagnostic imaging center in Rochester, NY, offering a comprehensive range of radiology services including MRI, CT scans, ultrasound, X-ray, and bone density scans. [4] They are known for providing patient-centered, compassionate care using state-of-the-art technology, including 3T MRI and Low Dose CT scanners. [3, 6, 11] Their medical staff consists exclusively of fellowship-trained radiologists, many of whom are also faculty at the University of Rochester School of Medicine and Dentistry, bringing a high level of sub-specialized expertise to diagnoses.
