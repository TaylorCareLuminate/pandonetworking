About the Practice

Sierra Tucson is a premier residential and outpatient treatment center in Tucson, Arizona, with over 40 years of experience providing integrative, evidence-based care for adults with addiction, mental health disorders, trauma, eating disorders, and chronic pain on a 160-acre campus. Utilizing a multidisciplinary approach and the Sierra Tucson Model, they offer comprehensive treatment focused on identifying and resolving underlying issues for holistic healing and lasting recovery.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Sierra Tucson utilizes a platform called **Connect365** as a continuing care portal for its participants. This platform includes a HIPAA-compliant web portal and a mobile app designed to support individuals in their recovery following residential treatment. The Connect365 portal facilitates ongoing connection between participants, their recovery coaches, and their approved support teams. Key features of Connect365 include the ability for participants to log in daily to share their progress, secure messaging with their recovery coach and support team, progress monitoring by recovery coaches, and access to resources for long-term recovery. It aims to provide consistent support and accountability during the crucial period after leaving residential care. While Connect365 serves as a patient portal specifically for continuing care and recovery management, the search results did not explicitly identify the primary Electronic Health Record (EHR) system that Sierra Tucson uses for comprehensive patient medical records, appointments, and other general healthcare management functions. One search result mentioned Sierra Tucson utilizing "electronic health record (EHR) systems" in a job description, but the specific vendor or system name was not provided. Therefore, while Connect365 is a branded patient portal used by Sierra Tucson, its direct tie to their core EHR vendor, in the same way MyChart is tied to Epic, could not be definitively established from the search results.
