About the Practice

Vassar Brothers Medical Center (VBMC) is a 349-bed facility in Poughkeepsie, NY, part of the Nuvance Health network. It is a regional medical center serving the Mid-Hudson Valley, providing comprehensive care with expertise in cardiac services, cancer care, women and children's health, stroke, and trauma. VBMC is home to the region's first cardiothoracic surgery program, the only Level III neonatal intensive care unit (NICU), and is certified as a Thrombectomy-Capable Stroke Center and designated as a Robotic Center of Excellence. They offer a wide range of services including robotic orthopedic surgery, liver and pancreatic surgery, interventional neuroradiology, surgical oncology, TAVR, behavioral and mental health, surgical services, primary care, imaging, and wound care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Vassar Brothers Medical Center, part of the Nuvance Health network, utilizes the MyNuvanceHealth patient portal for patient access to their health information. There are two versions of this portal, MyNuvanceHealth/Blue and MyNuvanceHealth/Green. Based on available information, the MyNuvanceHealth portal appears to be connected with the Cerner electronic health record (EHR) system. A privacy policy for "Cerner Patient Portal" is hosted on the Nuvance Health website, and a description for the MyNuvanceHealth/Blue app mentions it uses the "Same Cerner health system". Therefore, Vassar Brothers Medical Center likely uses the Cerner EHR system, accessed by patients through the MyNuvanceHealth portals (MyNuvanceHealth/Blue and MyNuvanceHealth/Green).
