About the Practice

The University of California, Irvine (UCI) is a public land-grant research university and one of the ten campuses of the UC system, founded in 1965 in Irvine, California. [8, 9] Classified among "R1: Doctoral Universities – Very high research activity" and a member of the Association of American Universities, UCI is known for academic achievement, premier research, and innovation. [8, 10] UCI includes the UC Irvine Medical Center, a large teaching hospital in Orange, offering a full scope of acute- and general-care services with over 400 specialty and primary care physicians. [8, 16] The Medical Center provides care in numerous specialties, including cancer care through the NCI-designated Chao Family Comprehensive Cancer Center, digestive diseases, heart health, neurology, neurosurgery, orthopedic surgery, primary care, surgery, women's health, and is home to the county's only Level I trauma center and multiple-organ transplant center. [16, 18]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

University of California, Irvine (UCI) Health uses the MyChart patient portal for patients of UCI Medical Center and its related locations. MyChart is the patient portal system provided by Epic Systems. UCI Health utilizes Epic as its electronic health record (EHR) system. This includes a shared instance of Epic with UC San Diego Health. While UCI Health also uses "My Health Rec" for community patients from recently acquired hospitals that previously used a different EHR system (Cerner), the plan is to transition these facilities to the Epic system as well.
