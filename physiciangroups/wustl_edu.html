About the Practice

Washington University in St. Louis (WashU) is a private research university located in St. Louis, Missouri, with campuses including the 355-acre Danforth Campus and the Medical Campus. [7] It comprises nine schools and offers numerous degree programs. [7] The Medical Campus is home to the School of Medicine and partners like Barnes-Jewish Hospital and St. Louis Children's Hospital. [7] WashU Medicine physicians provide patient care across more than 130 locations, offering expertise in a wide range of medical specialties, including acute and critical care surgery, allergy and immunology, cancer, cardiology, dermatology, and pediatrics. [12, 15, 10] They are committed to patient care rooted in research, offering access to clinical trials. [15]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Washington University (WashU) Medicine utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart provides patients with secure online access to manage aspects of their healthcare, including scheduling appointments, viewing test results, communicating with their care team, and requesting prescription refills. The underlying electronic health record (EHR) system used by Washington University School of Medicine and BJC HealthCare is Epic. The implementation of Epic aimed to create a single, integrated system across their healthcare organizations to improve coordination and continuity of patient care, with MyChart being the patient-facing component of this system. Previously, Washington University used a patient portal called FollowMyHealth, associated with the Allscripts system, but they transitioned to Epic and MyChart. The Student Health Center at WashU also has a Student Health Portal, which allows students to manage appointments and communicate with providers. However, for Washington University Medicine physicians, MyChart is the primary patient portal.
