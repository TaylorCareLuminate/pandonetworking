About the Practice

Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist is a nationally recognized, fully integrated academic medical center and health system based in Winston-Salem, North Carolina, and part of Advocate Health, the third-largest nonprofit health system in the United States. [5, 7] It comprises an integrated clinical system anchored by the 885-bed tertiary-care Medical Center, Brenner Children's Hospital, five community hospitals, over 300 primary and specialty care locations, and more than 2700 physicians. [7] The system's academic core is the Wake Forest School of Medicine, a leader in medical education and research. [7] Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist offers highly specialized, patient- and family-centered care across more than 100 areas of medicine, with key specialties including Neurology and Neurosurgery, Pediatrics, Primary Care, Comprehensive Cancer, Heart and Vascular, and Orthopaedics, driven by a mission to improve health through excellence in patient care, research, and education. [5, 6, 7, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist utilizes the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Their patient portal, which provides patients access to their health information, is branded as MyAtriumHealth. Previously, Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist used a patient portal called myWakeHealth, but they transitioned to MyAtriumHealth on March 2, 2024. Patients can log in to MyAtriumHealth using their existing myWakeHealth usernames and passwords. The connection between MyAtriumHealth and the Epic EHR is further supported by several factors. The Informatics and Analytics team at Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist includes a physician with multiple Epic certifications whose work focuses on improving EHR workflows. Additionally, information regarding the merger of Atrium Health and Wake Forest Baptist Health mentions that Lyft integrated its services with Epic, described as one of the largest EHR providers, to facilitate requesting rides for patients through the EHR. Finally, patients who prefer using the MyChart app can access their MyAtriumHealth account by adding Atrium Health as an organization within the MyChart application, indicating that MyAtriumHealth is built on the Epic MyChart platform. Through the MyAtriumHealth portal, patients can access their electronic health records, view test results, schedule appointments, message their healthcare team, and request prescription renewals.
