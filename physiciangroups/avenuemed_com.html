About the Practice

Avenue Medical Associates, PC is a private Manhattan Internal Medicine practice offering comprehensive, personalized, and forward-thinking healthcare. They specialize in Internal Medicine and Travel Medicine, providing services such as cutting-edge cancer and disease screening, including cancer predisposition gene testing, body MRI imaging, and coronary CT. The practice focuses on managing common medical issues, referring patients to specialists when necessary, and coordinating care.
