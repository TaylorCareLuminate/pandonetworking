About the Practice

Boston Children's Hospital is a nationally ranked, freestanding acute care children's hospital in Boston, Massachusetts, and the primary pediatric teaching hospital for Harvard Medical School. [2, 7, 8] As one of the largest pediatric medical centers in the United States, it offers a complete range of health care services for children from birth through 21 years of age, and in some cases, provides fetal interventions and treats adults. [6, 7, 8] It is home to the world's largest pediatric research enterprise and is the leading recipient of pediatric research funding from the National Institutes of Health (NIH). [2, 7, 8] The hospital provides comprehensive pediatric specialties and subspecialties through over 40 clinical departments and 258 specialized clinical programs and has been ranked as the #1 pediatric hospital multiple times by U.S. News & World Report and Newsweek. [2, 3, 7]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Boston Children's Hospital utilizes a patient portal called MyChildren's Patient Portal. This portal is linked to the Epic EHR system. According to information from September and October 2023, Boston Children's Hospital was in the process of transitioning to a single Epic EHR system, having previously used a combination of Cerner and Epic. This transition was expected to be completed by the summer of 2024. The move to Epic was aimed at enhancing interoperability and improving efficiencies. The MyChildren's Patient Portal allows patients and families to manage health records, review test results, schedule appointments, and communicate with care teams, and it is accessible via a web version or through the MyChart app.
