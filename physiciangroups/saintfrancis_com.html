About the Practice

Saint Francis Health System is a Catholic, not-for-profit health system based in Tulsa, Oklahoma, and is the largest private employer in Eastern Oklahoma. [8, 3] Its mission is to extend the presence and healing ministry of Christ. [3, 11] Anchored by the 1,112-bed Saint Francis Hospital, the system includes hospitals in south Tulsa, Muskogee, and Vinita, northeastern Oklahoma's only children's hospital, a heart hospital, a psychiatric hospital, and over 110 Warren Clinic locations. [8, 3] They provide comprehensive healthcare services including heart care, cancer care, primary care, pediatrics, neurosciences, behavioral and mental health, orthopedic care, women's health, and more. [5]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Saint Francis Health System utilizes MyChart as their patient portal. MyChart is the patient portal provided by Epic Systems, indicating that Saint Francis Health System is an Epic user for their electronic health record (EHR) system. MyChart allows patients of Saint Francis Health System to securely access their health information, including lab results, appointment details, medications, and immunizations. It also provides features for communicating with providers, requesting prescription refills, managing appointments, and accessing virtual care options. This aligns with the typical functionalities of a patient portal integrated with a comprehensive EHR system like Epic.
