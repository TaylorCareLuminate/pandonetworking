About the Practice

Rush University is a private health sciences university in Chicago, Illinois, founded in 1972 as the academic arm of Rush University Medical Center. [12] It comprises Rush Medical College, the College of Nursing, the College of Health Sciences, and the Graduate College. [12] Rush University Medical Center is an academic medical center and the primary teaching hospital for the Rush University System for Health, offering tertiary care and over 70 residency and fellowship programs in a wide range of medical and surgical specialties and subspecialties. [2, 12] The system provides comprehensive care including highly ranked specialties such as Neurology and neurosurgery, Orthopedics, Cardiology, Cancer, and more, as well as programs like the Road Home Program for veteran mental health and Rush Connect for virtual specialty care. [2, 16] Rush also includes facilities like Rush Specialty Hospital for physical rehabilitation and numerous outpatient clinic locations. [15, 14]

Methodology & Findings

Rush University Medical Center utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. This patient portal is licensed from Epic Systems Corporation. MyChart provides patients with access to their health records, the ability to communicate with their care team, view test results, manage appointments, and pay medical bills. Rush also offers a mobile application, My Rush App, which provides similar access to health records and additional features.
