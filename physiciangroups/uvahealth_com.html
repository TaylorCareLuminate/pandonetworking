About the Practice

UVA Health is an integrated health system based in Virginia, encompassing a world-class academic medical center that includes a Level 1 trauma center, a Level IV NICU, the state's first NCI-designated Comprehensive Cancer Center, and the #1 pediatric hospital in Virginia. [7] The system also comprises community hospitals, a specialty rehabilitation hospital, and a network of primary and specialty care clinics across Virginia, providing high-quality care for a wide range of conditions, from routine health needs to complex diseases. [7, 9, 11] UVA Health offers expertise in numerous specialties including cancer care, pediatric care, heart and vascular care, transplant services, neurosciences, behavioral health, surgical services utilizing advanced techniques, infusion therapy, and various medical and surgical subspecialties like endocrinology, nephrology, and pulmonary care. [3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 11] Through its commitment to teaching and research, UVA Health focuses on advancing medicine and innovating excellence in patient care. [7]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UVA Health utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. MyChart provides patients with a secure online tool to manage various aspects of their healthcare, including requesting appointments, communicating with their doctors, renewing prescriptions, viewing test results and medical records, and paying bills. UVA Health has been working to integrate recently acquired community medical centers onto their Epic system, further consolidating their use of this EHR. Healthcare providers at UVA Health can also access patient records through EpicCare, a HIPAA-compliant tool that allows for reviewing test results, appointments, and clinical notes. Students also primarily use the UVA Health portal which is linked to EPIC for accessing electronic medical records.
