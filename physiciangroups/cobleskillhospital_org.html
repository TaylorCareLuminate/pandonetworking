About the Practice

Cobleskill Regional Hospital is a 40-bed not-for-profit community hospital affiliated with Bassett Healthcare Network. It is Schoharie County's only provider of acute inpatient medical care, emergency care available 24/7, short-stay inpatient rehabilitation, and a broad range of diagnostic and therapeutic services. The hospital offers over 20 outpatient specialty services including cancer care, general surgery, heart care, medical imaging, neurology, orthopedics, physical therapy, urology, laboratory services, sleep disorders care, OB-GYN, lung and respiratory health, diabetes care, and ENT.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Cobleskill Regional Hospital, which is part of the Bassett Healthcare Network, utilizes a patient portal branded as MyBassett Health Connection. This portal appears to be built on the Epic MyChart platform, as indicated by a reference to the "mychart menu" within the MyBassett Health Connection login page. Therefore, the patient portal vendor/branding is MyBassett, and it is associated with the Epic EHR system.
