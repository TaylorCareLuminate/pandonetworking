About the Practice

Primary Care of Western New York, LLP provides quality primary healthcare for the entire family, caring for patients of all ages from infancy through adulthood. They operate as a Patient Centered Medical Home utilizing a team-based care model and are affiliated with Sisters of Charity Hospital for in-patient care, with connections to community specialists.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Western New York Medical Practice PC (primarycarewny.com) utilizes the MEDENT Patient Portal, which is part of an all-in-one Electronic Health Record (EHR), Patient Portal, and Practice Management system provided by MEDENT. MEDENT is a software development and services company that offers this integrated system to medical practices. The MEDENT Patient Portal provides patients with secure online access to portions of their medical records and a convenient way to communicate with the practice. This indicates that the patient portal is directly tied to the MEDENT EHR system used by Western New York Medical Practice PC.
