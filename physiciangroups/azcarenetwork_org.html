About the Practice

Arizona Care Network (ACN) is a physician-led accountable care organization (ACO) and clinically integrated network (CIN) comprising over 5,000 healthcare providers. As an ACO and CIN, ACN focuses on improving the quality and reducing the cost of healthcare by actively managing and coordinating care for their patients, particularly those with complex or chronic illnesses. They provide high-quality, coordinated care to patients across various insurance plans, including Medicare, Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, and commercial contracts, aiming to prevent health problems, treat conditions early, and guide patients through care transitions while reducing duplication of services and administrative inefficiencies.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Arizona Care Network (AZ Care Network), an accountable care organization, operates as a network of various healthcare providers rather than a single entity utilizing one universal electronic health record (EHR) system and patient portal for all members. As such, the specific patient portal a patient uses is dependent on the individual provider or healthcare system they receive care from within the AZ Care Network. However, a significant partner within the AZ Care Network is Dignity Health. According to search results, Dignity Health utilizes a patient portal branded as "My Portal" or "My Care". Information regarding Dignity Health's EHR system indicates that they have expanded their use of Cerner's EHR, including Cerner Millennium® for hospitals and PowerChart Ambulatory™ for clinics. This expansion aimed to create a unified patient record and a single patient portal experience across Dignity Health's facilities. Therefore, while AZ Care Network itself does not have a single patient portal or a single underlying EHR system for its entire network, patients receiving care from Dignity Health providers within the AZ Care Network would likely use the "My Portal" or "My Care" patient portal, which is tied to the Cerner EHR system used by Dignity Health. It is important for patients to confirm with their specific provider within the AZ Care Network which patient portal they use.
