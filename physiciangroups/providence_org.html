About the Practice

Providence Affiliated Physicians is a network of private practice physicians across Southern California, including Los Angeles, Orange County, and the High Desert, offering personalized primary and specialty care. The network includes over 350 primary care doctors and more than 3,200 specialists, providing a wide range of services from routine preventive care, treatment, and health education to advanced therapies and surgical procedures. Care is delivered through a network of hospitals, urgent care centers, and specialty clinics, focusing on comprehensive medical care for the whole person using advanced technology and state-of-the-art medical techniques.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Providence Affiliated Physicians (providence.org) utilizes MyChart as their patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal developed by Epic Systems. Through the MyChart portal, patients of Providence can manage various aspects of their healthcare online, including accessing test results, scheduling appointments, requesting prescription refills, and communicating with their doctors. This is a secure and convenient way for patients to engage with their personal health information. Providence also offers the Providence App, which provides additional ways to access care and medical records.
