About the Practice

St. Anthony Community Hospital, a 60-bed hospital in Warwick, NY and a member of the Westchester Medical Center Health Network, serves residents of Orange County, NY, and Sussex and Passaic counties, NJ. [3, 8, 10] It is a primary resource for emergency, medical, surgical, obstetrical/gynecological, and acute-care services, with comprehensive surgical programs including joint replacement and plastic surgery, as well as specialties like radiology with 3D mammography, a birthing center, outpatient infusion, and sleep disorder services. [3, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16] Schervier Pavilion, a skilled nursing facility, is also located on the campus. [3, 8]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

St. Anthony Community Hospital, which is part of the Westchester Medical Center Health Network (WMCHealth), utilizes the myCare Patient Portal for its patients. WMCHealth has been implementing Cerner's integrated electronic health record (EHR) system and services across its network. This indicates that the myCare patient portal used by St. Anthony Community Hospital is connected to the Cerner EHR system.
