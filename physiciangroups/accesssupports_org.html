About the Practice

Access: Supports for Living is a non-profit organization dedicated to helping people live the healthiest and fullest lives possible, primarily supporting individuals and families facing challenges associated with developmental disabilities and behavioral health issues. They serve adults and children with a range of needs, including those with significant medical or behavioral issues and others living independently with minimal help. Their services encompass behavioral health, intellectual and developmental disabilities support, coordinated care, urgent care, residential services, employment support, and various therapies like individual, family, and group therapy, aiming to tailor support to individual goals and promote independence and community integration.
