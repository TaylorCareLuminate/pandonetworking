About the Practice

Yavapai Regional Medical Center (YRMC), affiliated with Dignity Health, is a not-for-profit health care organization serving Yavapai County and northern Arizona with two acute-care hospitals in Prescott and Prescott Valley, and associated outpatient diagnosis and treatment centers. [2, 13] They provide comprehensive medical and surgical treatments and specialty care, including cardiac, digestive health, emergency, geriatrics, lymphedema, maternity, neuro, obstetrics, orthopedic, vein and wound care, urologic treatments, cancer care, imaging, lung disease and transplant, primary care, rehabilitation services, sleep center services, neurology and neurosurgery, and pediatrics. [2, 8] YRMC is focused on providing quality and compassionate medical care, adopting innovative technologies like Virtual Integrated Care and robotic-assisted surgery to enhance patient care close to home. [2, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Yavapai Regional Medical Center (YRMC) utilizes a patient portal branded as "YRMC CareConnect". This portal allows patients to securely access their medical information, including health records, clinical summaries, lab results, and imaging results. YRMC CareConnect is powered by Cerner Health, which is an Electronic Medical Record (EMR) system. Patients can access their medical record information through the Healthe Life Patient Portal app, which is compatible with YRMC CareConnect, as well as the Apple Health app. This indicates that Yavapai Regional Medical Center is using Cerner as their EHR vendor.
