About the Practice

Square Care Medical Group is a physician-owned and led, multi-specialty group focused on women's healthcare across Long Island and the New York Metropolitan area. [2, 4, 6, 14, 16] They provide a range of services including obstetrics, gynecology, maternal fetal medicine, and primary care, aiming to offer comprehensive and personalized care to women throughout their lives. [2, 4, 13, 14, 16] The group has a large network of over 60 providers and multiple locations, stated as 16 locations and expanding to 72 providers across 20 locations as of late 2022. [2, 4, 6, 14] They are affiliated with Unified Women's Healthcare. [14, 16]
