About the Practice

Northwest Louisiana Nephrology, LLC specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of diseases of the kidney, including dialysis and transplantation. The practice is committed to delivering compassionate care while focusing on effective and up-to-date treatment options for patients with all stages of kidney disease, as well as those on in-center and home dialysis. They provide both outpatient and inpatient services.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Northwest Louisiana Nephrology, LLC provides a patient portal for accessing medical information. While their website simply refers to it as "Patient Portal," it is connected to the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Northwest Louisiana Nephrology is among the practices that have integrated into Interwell Health's nephrology-focused Epic EHR system, known as Acumen Epic Connect. This indicates that the patient portal used by Northwest Louisiana Nephrology is part of the Acumen Epic Connect platform, which is built on the Epic infrastructure. While Epic's widely recognized patient portal is called MyChart, the search results do not specifically state that Northwest Louisiana Nephrology's portal is branded as MyChart. However, its integration with the Epic system via Acumen Epic Connect confirms the underlying EHR vendor.
