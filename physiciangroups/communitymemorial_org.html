About the Practice

Community Memorial Hospital, located in Hamilton, NY, is a 25-bed critical-access hospital and a cornerstone of patient-centered healthcare for the region since 1952, primarily serving Madison County and parts of surrounding counties. [2, 3, 6, 10, 11] It provides a wide range of services including 24/7 emergency room care, urgent, primary, and specialty care, inpatient care, and surgical procedures. [2, 7] Specialties offered include cardiology, surgery, pulmonology, orthopedics, and women's health, providing high-quality healthcare close to home. [3, 4, 6, 7] Community Memorial Hospital was rated as a 5-star hospital by CMS in 2020. [6]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Community Memorial Health System (communitymemorial.org) is utilizing MyChart as their patient portal. This patient portal allows patients to manage their health information, communicate with providers, schedule appointments, view test results, and pay bills. The use of MyChart strongly indicates that Community Memorial Health System is using Epic as their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. MyChart is the patient portal developed by Epic Systems, and the two are integrated. Furthermore, the FAQ section of Community Memorial Health System's Internal Medicine Residency Program explicitly states that their hospitals and academic medicine health centers use Epic. While searches for "Community Memorial Hospital patient portal" also yielded results for "My CMH Health Portal" and "MyHealthChart" for other similarly named hospitals, the information specifically tied to communitymemorial.org and Community Memorial Health System in Ventura, CA, points to MyChart as their current patient portal and Epic as their EHR system.
