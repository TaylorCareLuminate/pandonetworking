About the Practice

Accountable Health Partners (AHP) is a Health Care Network focused on creating stronger relationships and better outcomes for medical providers, benefits professionals, and health plan members. AHP assists medical providers with contract negotiations that return significant dollars to their practice, helps benefits professionals reduce unnecessary expenses to healthcare plans, and connects health plan members with needed providers to enable them to get better care. ## Patient Portal and EHR Use Within Accountable Health Partners Accountable Health Partners (AHP), a clinically integrated network based in Rochester, New York, does not operate a single, centralized patient portal or mandate a specific Electronic Health Record (EHR) system for all of its member healthcare providers. Instead, the patient portal and EHR system utilized by a patient are determined by the individual medical practice or hospital they visit within the AHP network. However, there is a strong indication that Epic's patient portal, MyChart, is prevalent among AHP members, particularly those affiliated with UR Medicine. An "EPIC Connect Opportunity" has been extended to community practices within the AHP network, facilitating integration with UR Medicine's Epic system and providing patients with access to MyChart. Several member practices of AHP also explicitly state that they use MyChart for patient interactions. While AHP focuses on clinical integration, care coordination, and value-based arrangements among its diverse network of providers, its technology infrastructure appears geared towards data aggregation and analytics for population health management and reporting through partnerships with companies like Health Catalyst. This further supports the model where individual members utilize their own EHR systems and associated patient portals. Therefore, if you are a patient of an Accountable Health Partners member, the specific patient portal you will use, and the underlying EHR system, will depend on your healthcare provider. Based on available information, MyChart, tied to the Epic EHR system, is a commonly used patient portal within the AHP network, especially among providers connected to UR Medicine.
