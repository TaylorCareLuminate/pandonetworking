About the Practice

Kingsbrook Jewish Medical Center, now part of One Brooklyn Health, was a 303-bed full-service community teaching hospital that has transitioned into a Medical Village. It provides a range of outpatient, emergency, and nursing home care, including ambulatory surgery, cardiology, critical care medicine, emergency/urgent care, gastroenterology, pulmonary services, a ventilator dependent unit, wound care, diagnostic imaging, and specialized centers such as The Bone & Joint Center, The Neurosciences Institute, and the Kingsbrook Rehabilitation Institute, which features Brooklyn's only New York State approved Brain Injury & Coma Recovery Unit. Services also encompass medical and surgical treatment, rehabilitation medicine, geriatric psychiatric medicine, and long-term care through Rutland Nursing Home.

Methodology & Findings

Kingsbrook Jewish Medical Center, as part of One Brooklyn Health (OBH), utilizes the MyChart patient portal. MyChart is a widely used patient portal solution that is commonly integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Although the search results directly state that One Brooklyn Health uses MyChart, they do not explicitly state that Epic is the underlying EHR system for Kingsbrook Jewish Medical Center. However, the strong association between MyChart and Epic suggests that Epic is the likely EHR vendor.
