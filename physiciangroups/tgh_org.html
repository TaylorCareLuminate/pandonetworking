About the Practice

Tampa General Hospital is a private, not-for-profit academic medical center and one of the largest hospitals in Florida, licensed for 1,040 beds. [11, 15] It is the primary teaching hospital for the USF Health Morsani College of Medicine and the region's only center for Level I trauma and comprehensive burn care. [13, 14, 15] TGH is recognized for world-class care and is one of the nation's busiest adult solid organ transplant centers, a nationally accredited comprehensive stroke center, and home to the Muma Children's Hospital at TGH. [12, 13, 14] It offers a wide range of specialty care and is consistently ranked among the nation's best in multiple specialties by U.S. News & World Report. [11, 12, 14, 15]
