About the Practice

Ramapo Anesthesia, based in Suffern, N.Y., is a leading, award-winning, and nationally recognized group of physicians specializing in anesthesiology services. With a team of over 100 physicians and 30 CRNAs, they provide a wide range of anesthesia care, including general, local, regional, epidural, spinal, peripheral nerve block, surgical anesthesia, obstetrics anesthesia, sedation, monitored anesthesia care, pain management, cardiovascular, neurosurgical, and thoracic anesthesia, as well as outpatient anesthesia care. They serve numerous hospitals and over 20 outpatient facilities throughout New York and New Jersey, with a strong commitment to delivering quality, safe, and compassionate patient-centered care.
