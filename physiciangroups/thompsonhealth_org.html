About the Practice

Thompson Health, part of UR Medicine, is an integrated health care delivery system in the Finger Lakes region. It includes F.F. Thompson Hospital, a 113-bed acute care community hospital offering a full range of health care services across 48 specialties, including acute care, primary care, surgical services, urgent and emergency care, diagnostic services, and long-term care through the M.M. Ewing Continuing Care Center. The system also includes F.F.T. Senior Communities. Thompson Health is recognized for excellence in nursing and various surgical and medical specialties, aiming to improve the health of residents in the Finger Lakes and surrounding region.
