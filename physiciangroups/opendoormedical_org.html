About the Practice

Open Door Family Medical Center is a Federally Qualified Health Center providing affordable, quality, and accessible healthcare in Westchester and Putnam counties, regardless of ability to pay. [3, 10, 12] They serve as a "medical home," offering a range of services including primary medical care (Family Medicine, Adults, Pediatrics), dental care, behavioral health care (Psychiatry, Clinical Social Work, Counseling), women's health care (OB/GYN, Family Planning), and specialty services such as Podiatry and Vision Services. [2, 7, 8] Open Door also emphasizes wellness, nutrition, chronic disease management, and provides support services like Case Management and Patient Advocacy. [2, 7, 8, 11] They are committed to serving diverse populations, including low-income families and those without insurance. [10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Open Door Family Medical Center (opendoormedical.org) utilizes a patient portal branded as the "Open Door Patient Portal". This portal allows patients to manage their healthcare online, including accessing medical records, requesting appointments and medication refills, and messaging their care team. The electronic health record (EHR) vendor used by Open Door Family Medical Center is eClinicalWorks. Open Door implemented eClinicalWorks for their electronic health records system as early as 2007 and has since used their comprehensive EMR/PM solution. The patient portal is integrated with their eClinicalWorks system.
