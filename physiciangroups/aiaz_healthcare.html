About the Practice

AIA Hospitalists LLC specializes in Internal Medicine, providing long-term, comprehensive care for adolescents, adults, and the elderly in both office and hospital settings. Their services include managing common and complex illnesses, as well as primary care focused on disease prevention and wellness. Internists are trained in the diagnosis and treatment of a wide range of conditions affecting various bodily systems.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Aia Hospitalists LLC, which is associated with Dignity Health, utilizes a patient portal called "My Portal". This patient portal is connected to Dignity Health's Electronic Health Record (EHR) system provided by Cerner. Dignity Health has integrated Cerner's PowerChart Ambulatory EHR across its clinics and uses Cerner Millennium EHR in its acute care facilities. The My Portal patient portal allows patients to access their medical information, including summaries, lab results, and imaging results, which are provided through their medical health record in the Cerner system. While AIA Healthcare (which appears related to AIA Hospitalists) also mentions FollowMyHealth and PayMyDoctor in the context of patient services like bill payment, the primary patient portal explicitly named and linked to AIA Hospitalists through their Dignity Health affiliation is My Portal. FollowMyHealth is described as a patient engagement platform that can integrate with various EHR systems, including Allscripts, and PayMyDoctor is an online billing portal associated with Veradigm. However, the direct link for AIA Hospitalists and Dignity Health's patient portal is My Portal, powered by Cerner.
