Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Stony Brook Internists University Faculty Practice Corporation, as part of the Stony Brook Medicine network, utilizes the "My HealtheLife" patient portal. This patient portal is powered by Cerner Health, indicating that Cerner is the Electronic Health Record (EHR) vendor used by Stony Brook Medicine. The My HealtheLife portal provides patients with secure online access to their personal health information, allowing them to review health records, medical history, lab results, and exchange messages with their doctors.
