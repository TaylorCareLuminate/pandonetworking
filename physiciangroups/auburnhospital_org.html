About the Practice

Auburn Community Hospital (ACH) is a not-for-profit, 99-bed acute care facility serving a population of approximately 80,000 in Cayuga County and the surrounding areas of the Finger Lakes region in Central New York. [4, 6] It is the sole provider of acute and general hospital services in its service area. [4, 6] With over 95% of its physicians board certified in at least one specialty, ACH is dedicated to providing compassionate, quality care and improving the health of the community. [4, 6] The hospital offers a range of services including acute care, long-term care, rehabilitation, urgent care, primary care, and various specialty care services and medical departments. [7, 8]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Auburn Community Hospital (auburnhospital.org) utilizes the FollowMyHealth patient portal. This portal provides patients with secure online access to their personal health information from hospital visits and tests. According to information from Auburn Community Hospital, the hospital launched the "Horizon Patient Folder (HPF) and Electronic Health Record (EHR) system" on March 31, 2010, and refers to HPF as the legal medical record. This indicates that the core EHR system used by Auburn Community Hospital is Horizon Patient Folder (HPF).
