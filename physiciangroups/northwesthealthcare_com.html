About the Practice

Northwest Healthcare is an integrated healthcare network in Tucson and surrounding communities, committed to providing quality, accessible healthcare ranging from routine primary care to highly specialized cardiac and surgical services. [9, 10] Northwest Medical Center, a key part of this network, is a 287-bed (or 300-bed) hospital offering a full spectrum of services including 24/7 emergency care, intensive care units, women's services (birthing center, NICU, imaging), urgent care centers, diagnostic imaging, therapy services, ambulatory surgery centers, and robotic-assisted surgery. [7, 16, 19] The network and medical center provide care across numerous specialties such as advanced cardiology and cardiothoracic surgery, bariatric and foregut surgery, gastroenterology, general surgery, orthopedics, OB/GYN, podiatry, pulmonology, urogynecology, and urology. [7]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Northwest Medical Center (northwesthealthcare.com) utilizes the MyHealthHome patient portal, which is provided by Oracle Health. Oracle Health, formerly known as Cerner, is a vendor that provides electronic health record (EHR) systems, and MyHealthHome is their patient portal offering. The MyHealthHome portal allows patients of Northwest Healthcare to securely access and manage their personal health information online. This includes viewing recent lab results, obtaining radiology reports, reviewing medications and allergies, and accessing immunization records and clinical summaries of their care. The transition to the MyHealthHome portal at Northwest Healthcare occurred in December 2021, replacing a previous system. Patients can access the portal through the Northwest Healthcare website or via the MyHealthHome mobile app powered by Oracle Health.
