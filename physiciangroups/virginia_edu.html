About the Practice

The University of Virginia (UVA) is a public research university located in Charlottesville, Virginia, founded in 1819 by Thomas Jefferson. It is recognized as a top public university known for academic excellence and value, serving the Commonwealth, nation, and world by advancing knowledge and developing citizen leaders. UVA encompasses undergraduate and professional schools, including the School of Law, the Darden School of Business, and the School of Medicine, and is home to the UNESCO World Heritage site, the Academical Village. The university also includes UVA Health, which provides comprehensive patient care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UVA Health, part of the University of Virginia, utilizes the MyChart patient portal for its patients. This portal is a product of Epic. MyChart provides patients with secure online access to various aspects of their medical records. Through MyChart, patients can request and view appointments, communicate with their healthcare providers, request prescription renewals, view test results, access health information resources, and pay bills. For UVA students specifically, there is also a "HealthyHoos Patient Portal" which allows them to schedule appointments and access personal health information using their Netbadge ID. However, the main patient portal for UVA Health is MyChart, which is associated with the Epic EHR system. Supporting this, information for referring providers mentions a transition to "UVA Health EpicCare Link" for accessing patient records.
