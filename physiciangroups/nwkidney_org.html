About the Practice

Northwest Kidney Centers is a regional, not-for-profit community-based provider specializing in kidney dialysis, public health education, and research into the causes and treatments of chronic kidney disease. Established in Seattle in 1962 as the world's first out-of-hospital dialysis provider, it offers dialysis services throughout the greater Seattle area in numerous free-standing clinics, affiliated hospitals, and through a home dialysis program, aiming to promote the optimal health, quality of life, and independence of people with kidney disease.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Northwest Kidney Centers (nwkidney.org) utilizes Clarity EMR as its Electronic Health Record system. The patient portal used by Northwest Kidney Centers is branded as "My Northwest Kidney Centers". Clarity EMR is an EHR solution specifically designed for dialysis clinics and is developed and marketed by Visonex. While search results indicate that Northwest Kidney Centers uses Clarity EMR and refers to their patient portal as "My Northwest Kidney Centers", there is no explicit confirmation in the search results that "My Northwest Kidney Centers" is a specific patient portal product name offered by Visonex. However, given that Clarity EMR is listed as a system accessed by staff and "My Northwest Kidney Centers" is the patient-facing portal, it is likely that this portal is integrated with or is the patient interface for their Clarity EMR system.
