About the Practice

Samaritan Hospital (Samaritan Memorial Hospital) in Macon, MO, is a Critical Access Hospital providing a wide range of inpatient, outpatient, and emergency healthcare services. [6] The hospital offers general medical and surgical care, including orthopedics, gynecology, ENT, gastroenterology, podiatry, and pain management procedures. [8] Specialized care is available through the Center for Specialty Care, featuring multiple providers across various specialties. [14] Primary care services, including adult and pediatric care, preventative care, and treatment for acute and chronic illnesses, are provided through Total Family Health Care, a division of the hospital. [10, 12] Additional services include cardiopulmonary and physical rehabilitation, dialysis, home health, laboratory and radiology services, respiratory therapy, sleep studies, skilled nursing care, and social services. [2, 3, 4, 15]

Methodology & Findings

Samaritan Medical Center (smhmo.org) utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is the patient portal system provided by Epic, and Samaritan Medical Center has been using Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) system. Patient records for Samaritan Hospital and Clinics have been managed within Epic's MyChart system since September 12, 2020.
