About the Practice

Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons (VP&S) is the medical school of Columbia University, recognized as one of the oldest and most celebrated academic medical institutions in the United States. VP&S is dedicated to transforming human health through discovery, advancing patient care, and educating future leaders in medicine and biomedical science. It offers comprehensive medical education, conducts research across various disciplines, and provides patient care through its affiliation with NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital and other sites, serving diverse populations locally and globally.

Methodology & Findings

Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons, as part of the Columbia University Irving Medical Center, utilizes a patient portal branded as "Connect". This patient portal is shared with NewYork-Presbyterian and Weill Cornell Medicine. Columbia University Irving Medical Center, NewYork-Presbyterian, and Weill Cornell Medicine transitioned to the Epic electronic medical record system and the Connect patient portal on February 1, 2020. Therefore, the Connect patient portal is tied to the Epic EHR system.
