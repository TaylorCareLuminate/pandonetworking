About the Practice

Cigna Healthcare of Arizona Inc. is a health service company that provides a range of insurance products including health, pharmacy, dental, supplemental, and Medicare plans for individuals, families, and businesses. [1, 2] As the parent entity for Evernorth Care Group (formerly Cigna Medical Group), they also offer direct patient care through multiple health care centers across the Phoenix metropolitan area, providing comprehensive primary and specialty care, behavioral health, dermatology, laboratory, imaging, and pharmacy services with a focus on integrated and accessible treatment. [7, 15, 16] They emphasize high-quality care, offering virtual care options and specialized programs for managing various health conditions and specialty medications. [2, 3, 11, 12, 15]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Cigna Healthcare of Arizona Inc., working in conjunction with Evernorth Care Group, utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a widely recognized patient portal provided by Epic Systems. This suggests that Epic is the underlying electronic health record (EHR) system used by Cigna Healthcare of Arizona Inc. through its affiliation with Evernorth Care Group. The MyChart portal allows patients to access their secure health information, including test results and medication lists, manage appointments, contact providers, access medical records, and pay bills online. The association with Evernorth Care Group is explicitly stated in the context of the MyChart portal for Cigna HealthCare of Arizona, Inc.
