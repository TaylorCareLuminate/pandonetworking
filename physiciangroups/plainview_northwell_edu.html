About the Practice

Plainview Hospital, part of Northwell Health, is a 219-bed acute care community hospital providing a wide range of exceptional medical and surgical care. [3, 4] Key services include surgical specialties such as gynecologic, ENT, urologic, colorectal, endocrine, vascular, bariatric, and robotic procedures, as well as cardiology with an interventional cath lab, wound care and hyperbaric medicine, and 24/7 emergency services. [2, 4, 5, 8] The hospital also offers care in gastroenterology, orthopedics, pediatric urology, and vascular surgery. [9] It is recognized as a New York State Department of Health Primary Stroke Center and has received awards for excellence in heart attack, heart failure, pneumonia, and surgical care. [4, 9]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Plainview Hospital, which is part of Northwell Health, utilizes the **FollowMyHealth** patient portal. This patient portal is provided by **Allscripts**. FollowMyHealth is designed to integrate with various Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems, and is explicitly stated to integrate with Allscripts' own EHRs. Through the FollowMyHealth portal, patients of Plainview Hospital and other Northwell Health facilities can access their medical records, view test results, manage appointments, and communicate with their healthcare providers. While other patient portals like PrimePATIENT and ezAccess might be used by specific physician practices within the Northwell Health system, FollowMyHealth is the prominent portal associated with Northwell Health hospitals like Plainview.
