About the Practice

Neurology Associates of Stony Brook, a location of Stony Brook Medicine's Neuroscience Institute, provides comprehensive neurological care for adults and children. They offer services including general neurology, clinical neurophysiology (EEG, NCV/EMG), therapeutic injections (Botox, Trigger Point), and Vagus Nerve Stimulator management. They diagnose and treat a wide range of conditions affecting the brain, spine, nerves, and muscles, such as migraines, epilepsy, Parkinson's and other movement disorders, dementia, and neuromuscular disorders. They are also involved in specialized care for stroke, multiple sclerosis, ALS, Alzheimer's, sleep disorders, chronic pain, brain tumors, cerebral palsy, and autism as part of the larger institute.
