Methodology & Findings

District Medical Group Inc. (dmgaz.org) utilizes a patient portal branded as the "DMG Patient Portal". Evidence suggests that the vendor for this patient portal is NextGen, as indicated by the "NextGen Patient Portal" branding and references to "NextMD.com" in the portal's login information. While a partner organization, Valleywise Health, mentions using MyChart (associated with Epic) for self-scheduling with District Medical Group physicians, the primary patient portal directly linked with dmgaz.org appears to be a NextGen product.
