About the Practice

Integrated Medical Services (IMS) is Arizona's largest independent multi-specialty provider group, serving communities across the Phoenix metro area and beyond, including northern Arizona. [2, 3, 5] It is a physician-owned and led enterprise offering a comprehensive range of medical services with over 70 physicians and providers across numerous specialties, including Primary Care, Cardiology, Gastroenterology, Pain Management, Sports Medicine, Podiatry, Endocrinology, Neurology, Orthopedic Surgery, and Behavioral Health. [2, 3, 5] IMS provides personalized, expert, and coordinated care, focusing on clinical excellence and patient-focused service, with options for in-person and virtual appointments. [2, 3, 5, 6]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Based on the information from the Google search results, Integrated Medical Services (imsaz.com) utilizes a patient portal that is integrated with their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. While the specific branding or name of the patient portal is not explicitly mentioned as a distinct product name like "MyChart," the search results indicate that IMS has been using athenahealth's integrated suite of solutions, known as athenaOne, since January 2023. AthenaOne includes patient engagement tools. Previously, in 2009, Integrated Medical Services had selected Allscripts for their EHR and Practice Management solution. However, a more recent article from March 2024 highlights IMS's growth and success with athenahealth, stating they use athenahealth's cloud-based suite of integrated EHR, revenue cycle management, and patient engagement solutions – athenaOne®. This indicates a transition from Allscripts to athenahealth. Therefore, Integrated Medical Services (imsaz.com) is currently using a patient portal that is part of the athenaOne suite provided by the EHR vendor athenahealth. While the portal is consistently referred to as the "Patient Portal" on the IMS website, it is integrated with their athenahealth EHR system.
