About the Practice

University of Pittsburgh Physicians are the clinical faculty of the University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine, practicing within the UPMC health system. They provide patient care across a wide range of medical and surgical specialties, integrating clinical service with medical education and research. Their practice is conducted at various UPMC hospitals and outpatient facilities throughout the Pittsburgh region, offering comprehensive healthcare services.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

University of Pittsburgh Physicians, a part of the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center (UPMC), is currently transitioning to a unified Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. While UPMC previously utilized a mix of EHRs, including Oracle Cerner for inpatient care and Epic for outpatient services, the health system is consolidating to a single Epic platform with an expected completion date of mid-2026. The patient portal primarily used by UPMC, and which will have expanded functions with the move to the unified Epic system, is called MyUPMC. This aligns with the common practice of healthcare systems using Epic, where the patient portal is often branded as MyChart or a variation of it, although UPMC uses the MyUPMC branding. Other patient portals mentioned in relation to UPMC facilities include UPMC Central PA Portal, MyCareCorner, MyLinks, and MyCHP (for Children's Hospital of Pittsburgh).
