About the Practice

St. John's Riverside Hospital is a comprehensive network of healthcare services serving the communities of Yonkers and the Rivertowns, including Hastings-on-Hudson, Ardsley, Dobbs Ferry, and Irvington. It provides a wide range of inpatient and outpatient services, including emergency care, surgical services with a focus on minimally-invasive and robotic procedures, cardiology, cancer care, diagnostic services, physical therapy, sleep services, wound healing, women's health, and extensive behavioral health services, being a leader in chemical dependency treatment in New York State since 1967. The hospital is committed to providing technologically advanced and compassionate care close to home.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

St. John's Riverside Hospital in Yonkers, NY (riversidehealth.org) utilizes a patient portal branded as "MyStJohnsHealth". For convenient access via mobile devices, the hospital recommends using the "MHealth mobile app". The MHealth app is explicitly identified as the mobile version of MEDITECH's Patient and Consumer Health Portal and is powered by MEDITECH. This strong connection between the patient portal used by St. John's Riverside Hospital and MEDITECH suggests that MEDITECH is the likely vendor for the hospital's electronic health record (EHR) system that underpins the MyStJohnsHealth portal.
