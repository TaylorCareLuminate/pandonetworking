About the Practice

The MetroHealth System is a non-profit, public health care system located in Cleveland, Ohio, founded in 1837 to serve the residents of the city and Cuyahoga County. [2, 3] It operates four hospitals, four emergency departments, and more than 20 health centers and 40 additional sites throughout the county, providing care to everyone regardless of ability to pay. [5, 8, 10, 13] As an academic medical center affiliated with Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine, MetroHealth is committed to teaching and research. [4, 5, 8, 10] It is home to Cuyahoga County's only verified Level I Adult Trauma Center and Level II Pediatric Trauma Center, and Ohio's only adult and pediatric trauma and burn center. [4, 5, 10] MetroHealth provides a wide range of services from primary care to complex services and is focused on improving community health and well-being. [5, 8, 13]
