About the Practice

Rockland Pulmonary Medical Associates is part of Bon Secours Medical Group, a member of the Westchester Medical Center Health Network. They specialize in Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine, providing consultations and care for respiratory disorders such as COPD, asthma, lung cancer, interstitial lung diseases, and pulmonary hypertension. Diagnostic and therapeutic procedures offered include pulmonary function testing, bronchoscopy, and critical care services. [6, 7]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Rockland Pulmonary Medical Associates, as part of the Westchester Medical Center Health Network (WMCHealth), utilizes the myCare patient portal. This transition to the myCare portal by WMCHealth began in October. WMCHealth has a strategic alliance with Cerner for an integrated electronic health record (EHR) system. This suggests that the myCare patient portal is likely connected to the Cerner EHR system used by the WMCHealth network.
