About the Practice

Sunnyview Rehabilitation Hospital is a 115-bed hospital in Schenectady, NY, specializing in physical rehabilitation, offering both inpatient and outpatient care. It provides comprehensive, personalized, and state-of-the-art treatment for a range of conditions, including stroke, brain injury, spinal cord injury, lower extremity amputation, cardio-pulmonary issues, and post-surgical recovery. [2, 3, 4, 12] Recognized as a leader in neurological patient care, Sunnyview is the only stand-alone hospital in upstate New York solely dedicated to rehabilitation and recovery, supported by technology and research. [6, 11, 12]
