Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

HCA Houston Healthcare Medical Center, as part of the larger HCA Healthcare network, utilizes the **MyHealthONE** patient portal. This portal serves as a central online platform for patients to access their medical records, view test results, schedule appointments, and manage their healthcare information. The electronic health record (EHR) system that underpins the MyHealthONE portal at HCA Healthcare facilities, including HCA Houston Healthcare Medical Center, is primarily **MEDITECH Expanse**. HCA Healthcare has been in the process of implementing MEDITECH Expanse as its main EHR platform, transitioning from an earlier version of MEDITECH. Information from EHR environments, including MEDITECH 5.6.7 and Expanse, provides data to the MyHealthONE patient portal for inpatient facilities. While MyHealthONE is the prevalent patient portal branding, one mention was found of a "Patient Portal powered by Baritrax360" in relation to HCA Houston Healthcare Medical Center, specifically for bariatric surgery services. This suggests that while MyHealthONE is the primary and overarching patient portal for HCA Healthcare, specialized portals might be in use for specific departments or services that could also tie into the main EHR system. However, MyHealthONE is the widely used and promoted patient portal across the HCA network and is directly linked to the MEDITECH EHR system.
