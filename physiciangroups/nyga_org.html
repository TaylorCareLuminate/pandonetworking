About the Practice

The Endoscopy Center of New York (ECNY) is a state-of-the-art ambulatory surgery center affiliated with New York Gastroenterology Associates (NYGA). [2, 7] It specializes in providing exceptional endoscopic and gastroenterology procedures, including colonoscopy, upper endoscopy, flexible sigmoidoscopy, esophageal dilation, and HemWell, in a comfortable, patient-centric environment. [2, 7, 8] Located on the Upper East Side of Manhattan, ECNY is equipped with the latest medical technology for accurate diagnoses and effective treatments. [2, 7]
