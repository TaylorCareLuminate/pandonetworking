About the Practice

Saratoga Ophthalmology is a comprehensive eye care group providing leading-edge care for a full range of vision and eye conditions. They specialize in Vitreo-Retinal, Oculo Plastic, and Neuro Ophthalmic conditions, offering medical, surgical, and laser treatment for the retina and vitreous, oculoplastic surgery, treatment for neuro-ophthalmological conditions, as well as care for glaucoma and cataracts. They also provide comprehensive eye exams and other eye surgeries.
