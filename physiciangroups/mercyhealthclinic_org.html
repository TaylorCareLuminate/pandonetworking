About the Practice

Mercy Health Clinic is a not-for-profit, non-sectarian, community-based primary health care provider serving medically underserved, low-income residents of Montgomery County, Maryland. [1, 2, 4, 8] The clinic provides high-quality medical care, health education support, and prescription medications free of charge to eligible patients. [4, 6] Services include primary medical care such as wellness visits, chronic condition management, and age-appropriate screenings, as well as specialty care in areas including gynecology, dermatology, ophthalmology, endocrinology, urology, and pulmonology. [2]
