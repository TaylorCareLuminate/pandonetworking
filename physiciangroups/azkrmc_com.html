About the Practice

Kingman Regional Medical Center (KRMC) is the largest healthcare provider in northwest Arizona and the only remaining non-profit hospital in Mohave County. [2, 8] It is a 235-bed multi-campus healthcare system offering a full range of medical services, including essential primary and preventive care, medical imaging, diagnostic capabilities, surgical technologies, and advanced treatments in cardiac care, cancer care, rehabilitation, and pain management therapies. [2, 5] KRMC is also Arizona's first rural teaching hospital, with residency programs in family medicine and emergency medicine. [2, 5]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Kingman Regional Medical Center (KRMC) in Kingman, Arizona, utilizes the "My KRMC Health Portal" as its patient portal. This portal is also accessible through the "MHealth" mobile application. The MHealth app is identified as the mobile version of MEDITECH's Patient and Consumer Health Portal. MEDITECH is a healthcare software company known for providing electronic health record (EHR) systems. This connection indicates that Kingman Regional Medical Center is using MEDITECH as their EHR vendor. The My KRMC Health Portal allows patients to manage aspects of their health records, view appointments, health records, medications, and test results. KRMC launched the new My KRMC Health Portal on October 21, 2019, coinciding with an update to their electronic health record system.
