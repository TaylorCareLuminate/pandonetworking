About the Practice

Cohen Children's Medical Center, part of Northwell Health, is a pediatric acute care children's teaching hospital located in New Hyde Park, New York. [1] It is the largest provider of pediatric health services in New York state, offering comprehensive pediatric specialties and subspecialties for patients aged 0-21. [1, 2, 6, 7] Ranked among the nation's best children's hospitals in multiple specialties by U.S. News & World Report, the center also serves as a Level I Pediatric Trauma Center. [1, 2, 7, 13] They are dedicated to providing specialized medical, surgical, and dental care in both inpatient and outpatient settings. [7]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Cohen Children's Medical Center, as part of the Northwell Health system, currently utilizes **FollowMyHealth** as its primary patient portal. This portal serves as the platform for patients and their authorized representatives to access medical information, manage appointments, and facilitate communication with healthcare teams. Northwell Health has announced plans to transition to **Epic** as its new electronic health record (EHR) system. This comprehensive change is being implemented in phases, with the initial rollout commencing in 2025. As part of this transition to Epic, it is widely anticipated that Northwell Health will adopt **MyChart**, Epic's integrated patient portal, which is commonly used by healthcare systems utilizing the Epic EHR. While FollowMyHealth is currently in use and integrates with the existing EHR systems at Northwell Health, the organization is in the process of consolidating its various medical record systems under the single Epic platform. This move aims to create a unified patient record across all Northwell facilities, including Cohen Children's Medical Center. Therefore, while FollowMyHealth is the current patient portal vendor for Cohen Children's Medical Center, the future system will involve Epic as the EHR and likely MyChart as the patient portal as the transition progresses.
