About the Practice

BryLin Hospital is a leading behavioral health care provider offering short-term, crisis care inpatient mental health treatment for children, adolescents, and adults in Western New York. They provide comprehensive assessment, crisis stabilization, and individualized care for a range of conditions including depression, bipolar disorder, anxiety, schizophrenia, and PTSD, with specialized programs and a focus on evidence-based, person-centered, trauma-sensitive, and multidisciplinary approaches. BryLin also has a Center of Excellence for Electroconvulsive Therapy (ECT). The Williamsville location offers outpatient mental health and substance use disorder treatment for individuals of all ages.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

## Brylin Hospital Likely Utilizes MyKaleida Patient Portal, Powered by Cerner Brylin Hospital appears to use the MyKaleida Patient Portal, which is a patient portal provided by Kaleida Health and is part of the Cerner electronic health record (EHR) system. While Brylin Hospital's own website mentions a patient portal for existing patients, providing a username and password for access, it does not specify the vendor or branding of this portal. However, a connection to Kaleida Health's patient portal is strongly suggested through shared services and employee eligibility. Information indicates that individuals, including employees of Brylin Hospital, receive test results through the Kaleida Health Patient Portal. Furthermore, employees of Brylin Hospital are eligible to join the Kaleida Health Federal Credit Union, another indicator of a close affiliation. Kaleida Health has been undergoing a significant implementation and upgrade of the Cerner EHR system since 2019. The patient portal used by Kaleida Health is named MyKaleida Patient Portal and is accessible via the HealtheLife mobile app. HealtheLife is a patient portal platform offered by Cerner. Based on these connections, it is highly likely that Brylin Hospital leverages the MyKaleida Patient Portal, which is integrated with Kaleida Health's Cerner EHR system, for at least some, if not all, of its patient portal services.
