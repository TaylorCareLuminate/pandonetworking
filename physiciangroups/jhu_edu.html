About the Practice

Johns Hopkins University, founded in 1876, is a private research university headquartered in Baltimore, Maryland, and recognized as the first research university in the United States. Comprising 10 academic divisions spread across nine campuses on three continents, JHU is a global leader in education, innovative discovery, and patient care. Through its renowned School of Medicine, School of Nursing, and Bloomberg School of Public Health, and its affiliation with Johns Hopkins Medicine and The Johns Hopkins Hospital, the university provides a wide range of specialty medical care and is consistently ranked among the nation's top hospitals for various specialties.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Johns Hopkins Medicine, which is affiliated with Johns Hopkins University (jhu.edu), utilizes the MyChart patient portal. This patient portal is a product of the electronic health record (EHR) vendor Epic. It is worth noting that Johns Hopkins University Student Health and Well-Being uses a separate system called the MyHealth Portal specifically for student health services, which is distinct from the MyChart system used by Johns Hopkins Medicine. However, the primary patient portal and associated EHR for the broader Johns Hopkins Medicine system is MyChart by Epic.
