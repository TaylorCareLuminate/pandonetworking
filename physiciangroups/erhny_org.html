About the Practice

Ellenville Regional Hospital, with web domain erhny.org, is a non-profit, community-focused general acute care hospital located in Ellenville, NY. It provides a range of medical services including emergency services, rehabilitation, inpatient and outpatient care, diagnostic imaging, and laboratory services. The hospital offers specialized care in areas such as Cardiology, Orthopedics, Gastroenterology, General Surgery, Neurology, Podiatry, Wound Care, Urology, Hematology, Oncology, and Pain Management. [2, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 15, 16] It is recognized as a critical access hospital and serves as a training site for various clinical departments. [13, 17]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Ellenville Regional Hospital (erhny.org) uses the athenaOne electronic health record (EHR) system, which they implemented in February 2017. The athenaOne system includes a patient engagement platform that features a patient portal. While the specific branding name used by Ellenville Regional Hospital for their patient portal on their website is consistently referred to as "Patient Portal", athenahealth's integrated patient portal solution is often referred to as athenaPatient and has a与之配套的移动应用程序. Older information suggests Ellenville Regional Hospital had a connection with EpicCare Link in 2011 for sharing patient information with the Institute for Family Health, which utilized Epic's system. However, the more recent information indicates athenaOne is their current comprehensive EHR system. Another result from 2012 mentioned Healthcare Management Systems as their EHR vendor, but this appears to be outdated given the 2017 implementation of athenaOne. Therefore, Ellenville Regional Hospital's patient portal is powered by the athenaOne EHR system, and the patient portal branding is likely associated with athenahealth's patient portal offering, potentially using the athenaPatient platform.
