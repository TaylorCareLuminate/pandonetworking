About the Practice

New York Ophthalmology is a full-service eye care clinic providing families with exceptional and high-quality vision care across Bronx, Brooklyn, Manhattan & Queens. [4] They offer a comprehensive suite of vision correction services, including LASIK, PRK, and EVO ICL. [4] The clinic provides treatment for a wide array of eye care concerns, from comprehensive eye exams to eye surgery and disease management, including Glaucoma Care, Retina Care, Diabetic Eye Care, Cataract Surgery, Corneal Surgery, and Dry Eye Care. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Based on the search results, it appears that Greater New York Ophthalmology Eye Care PLLC, which is associated with New York Ophthalmology, uses a patient portal provided by **Crystal Practice Management**. Crystal Practice Management is also an Electronic Health Record (EHR) vendor. Their software is designed for optometric practices and includes features such as-based medical records, billing, patient scheduling, and a patient portal. The patient portal offered by Crystal Practice Management allows patients to access their medical information, manage appointments, and communicate with their healthcare providers.
