About the Practice

The University of Arizona Cancer Center is the only NCI-Designated Comprehensive Cancer Center headquartered in Arizona, dedicated to alleviating the burden of cancer, particularly in underserved populations. It serves as a regional resource and national model for overcoming cancer risks, improving treatments through transdisciplinary discoveries, training scientists and providers, and engaging communities. The center focuses on patient care, research, prevention, education, outreach, and training, providing multidisciplinary care. Specialties include women's cancers (breast, ovarian), men's cancers (prostate), gastrointestinal cancers (colon, pancreas, liver), lymphoma, and skin cancers, with a significant Cancer Prevention and Control Program.

Methodology & Findings

The University of Arizona Cancer Center, which is a part of Banner - University Medical Center Tucson, uses the Banner Health patient portal. This portal is branded as "Patient Account," having previously been known as the MyBanner patient portal. Patients can access their health information through the Patient Account online or via the Banner Health App. The electronic health record (EHR) vendor associated with the Banner Health Patient Portal is Cerner.
