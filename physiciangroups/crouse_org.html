About the Practice

Crouse Hospital is a private, not-for-profit acute care hospital located in Syracuse, New York, serving a multi-county area in Central and Northern New York. Licensed for over 500 beds, its mission is to provide the best in patient care and promote community health through advanced technology and compassionate service. Crouse offers a comprehensive range of general and specialty services, including emergency care, extensive cardiac services (adult and pediatric), a Neuroscience Institute, orthopedic care, stroke care (designated Comprehensive Stroke Center), oncology, surgical services with robotic capabilities, women's services, bariatric surgery, and chemical dependency treatment. It is also recognized as a Regional Perinatal Center with a notable NICU.
