About the Practice

Manhattan Cardiology is a premier facility in New York specializing in top-tier heart and vascular care, with a strong emphasis on cardiac testing and preventive treatment through early detection. [1, 7, 9] Their team of board-certified cardiologists provides comprehensive and personalized care for a range of heart health issues, including elevated blood pressure, high cholesterol, heart arrhythmia, heart failure, and chest pain. [3, 9, 12] Services offered include heart screening, exercise stress tests, ECG monitoring, cholesterol tests, and coronary calcium scoring, focusing on preventive and interventional cardiology. [3, 4, 9, 12]
