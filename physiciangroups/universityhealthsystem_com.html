About the Practice

University Health System is a nationally recognized teaching hospital and network of outpatient healthcare centers in San Antonio, Texas, owned by Bexar County. In partnership with UT Medicine San Antonio, it is known for advanced treatments, new technologies, and clinical research. It is a premier Level I trauma center for South Texas, including a pediatric trauma center and specialized burn care. The system offers comprehensive care across numerous specialties, including kidney disease, gynecology, diabetes, organ transplantation (kidney, liver, lung), heart and vascular care, neurology, ENT, behavioral health, obstetrics, pediatrics, primary care, and urgent care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

University Health System, accessible through universityhealthsystem.com, utilizes the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Their patient portal is called MyChart. MyChart is the patient portal specifically associated with the Epic EHR system, similar to the example provided in the user's request. This portal allows patients to access their health information, schedule appointments, view test results, and communicate with their healthcare team. University Health System adopted the Epic system after a thorough review, transitioning from a previous EMR system by Allscripts. The implementation of Epic was expected to be completed by July 2020, with the aim of creating a more seamless experience for patients and improving the sharing of medical history with partners like UT Health San Antonio, who also use Epic.
