About the Practice

BJC HealthCare is one of the largest nonprofit health care integrated delivery organizations in the United States, serving urban, suburban, and rural communities across Missouri and southern Illinois. [9, 14] They provide a wide range of clinical care programs, from routine care to the most advanced treatments, covering various health needs. [2, 8, 14] BJC offers specialized care in areas including Cancer, Heart & Vascular, Neurosciences, Obstetrics & Gynecology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Primary Care, and Transplant. [8] They are committed to improving community health and well-being through leadership, education, innovation, and excellence in medicine. [14]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

BJC HealthCare utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. Several sources confirm that BJC HealthCare, in conjunction with Washington University Physicians, uses MyChart for patients to manage their health information online. MyChart is a widely recognized patient portal developed by Epic Systems. Therefore, based on BJC HealthCare's use of the MyChart patient portal, their electronic health record (EHR) vendor is Epic.
