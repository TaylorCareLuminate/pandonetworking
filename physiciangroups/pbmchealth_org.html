About the Practice

Peconic Bay Medical Center is a non-profit 144-bed community hospital in Riverhead, NY, serving as the eastern hub of Northwell Health. It provides comprehensive and compassionate care to the communities of eastern Suffolk County and Long Island's North Fork, with services including orthopedic surgery (Center of Excellence for Hip and Knee Replacement), robotic surgery (Center of Excellence), emergency care (Advanced Primary Stroke Center, Level III Trauma Center), cardiac care (interventional cardiac catheterization laboratory suite, ranked in the top 100 nationally for coronary intervention), women's health, comprehensive inpatient medical care, palliative care, and extensive outpatient services like primary care, gastroenterology, cancer treatment, labs, and imaging. Methodology & Findings

Peconic Bay Medical Center (pbmchealth.org) utilizes the FollowMyHealth patient portal. This patient portal is a product of Allscripts, which is now known as Veradigm. FollowMyHealth is designed to integrate with various electronic health record (EHR) systems, including those offered by Allscripts. Peconic Bay Medical Center is part of Northwell Health, and the FollowMyHealth portal is associated with Northwell Health's patient information system. Based on the patient portal vendor, it is likely that Peconic Bay Medical Center uses an Allscripts (Veradigm) EHR system.
