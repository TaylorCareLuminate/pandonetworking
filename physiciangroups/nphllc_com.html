About the Practice

Metropolitan Anesthesia Consultants is a dynamic group comprised of more than 100 physician Anesthesiologists providing excellence in anesthesia care at many hospitals and surgery centers throughout the Dallas/Fort Worth Metroplex. [2, 8] They specialize in all aspects and specialties of anesthesia, offering a full suite of general and obstetrical anesthesia services and providing comprehensive care with an emphasis on safety, reliability, professionalism, and compassionate care. [8, 12] Their physicians also provide postoperative pain control utilizing a multi-modal approach through regional anesthesia techniques. [2]

Methodology & Findings

The search results indicate that Metropolitan Anesthesia Consultants (nphllc.com) uses the InstaMed Patient Portal, at least for online bill payment. While InstaMed is a payment platform, it integrates with various EHR and practice management systems. One search result specifically mentions a testimonial from Metropolitan Anesthesia Consultants about using MedEvolve for revenue cycle management. MedEvolve provides revenue cycle management technology and services. Another result mentions that National Partners in Healthcare (NPH), which appears related to Metro Anesthesia Consultants, partners with physicians and health systems and focuses on revenue cycle management. While InstaMed is identified as the patient portal for payments, and MedEvolve is used for revenue cycle management, the specific comprehensive EHR system used by Metro Anesthesia Consultants for managing patient records, appointments, etc., is not explicitly stated in the search results. InstaMed's integration capabilities suggest they would connect with an existing EHR system, and MedEvolve's focus on revenue cycle management also implies interaction with an EHR. However, the name of that core EHR vendor remains unknown.
