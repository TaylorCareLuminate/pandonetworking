About the Practice

Claxton-Hepburn Medical Center is a private, not-for-profit, 127-bed community hospital and regional referral center located in Ogdensburg, NY, serving St. Lawrence County and surrounding communities. [3, 12] It offers primary care and a comprehensive range of regional and countywide services and specialties, including acute care, intensive care, birthing services, behavioral health for adults, children, and adolescents, cancer care (radiation and medical oncology), dialysis, wound healing, advanced diagnostic imaging, surgical services (including robotic procedures), laboratory services, convenient care, orthopedics, pain management, pulmonology, radiology, renal care, sleep disorder center, urology, weight center, wellness programs, and women's health. [3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12, 14]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Claxton-Hepburn Medical Center utilizes a patient portal accessible via their website and recommends the use of the "MHealth mobile app" for a more optimized experience. The MHealth mobile app is the mobile version of MEDITECH's Patient and Consumer Health Portal. This indicates a direct link between the patient portal used by Claxton-Hepburn Medical Center and the MEDITECH Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. Further supporting this connection, multiple sources, including recent job postings and a 2017 article, indicate that Claxton-Hepburn Medical Center uses the MEDITECH Hospital Information System. Job listings for positions at Claxton-Hepburn have listed experience with the Meditech system as desired or required.
