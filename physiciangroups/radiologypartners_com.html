About the Practice

Empire State Radiology, an affiliate of Radiology Partners, is a private radiology practice group dedicated to providing a full range of high quality imaging and professional radiology services through diagnostic excellence in imaging. [2] They offer innovative diagnostic procedures and therapeutic interventions, utilizing state-of-the-art imaging technology. [3, 7, 9] Their radiologists are board certified in diagnostic radiology and fellowship trained in various subspecialties, including Body Imaging, Breast Imaging, Interventional and Vascular Radiology, Musculoskeletal imaging, Neuroradiology/Head & Neck Imaging, Nuclear Medicine, and Pediatric Imaging. [2] They provide tailored reports, fast turnaround, and 24/7 coverage, serving facilities throughout the New York and New Jersey region with onsite, remote, and hybrid options. [2, 4, 6, 7, 9, 12, 13]
