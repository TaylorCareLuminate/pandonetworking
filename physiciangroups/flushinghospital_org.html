About the Practice

Flushing Hospital Medical Center is a 293-bed, not-for-profit teaching hospital located in Queens, New York, and part of the MediSys Health System. Established in 1884, it provides acute care hospital services, outpatient clinic services, and emergency services. Key specialties and care include Adult and Pediatric Emergency Departments, a Chemical Dependency Unit for medically supervised detox, and designations as a New York State Designated Stroke Center and a Level 3 Perinatal Center. The hospital offers a range of services including ambulatory surgery, mental health services, dental care, maternity services, medical and primary care, and renal dialysis.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Flushing Hospital Medical Center, which is part of the MediSys Health Network, uses a patient portal called **MediSys Connect**. This patient portal is licensed from **Epic Systems Corporation**, indicating that Epic is the electronic health record (EHR) system utilized by Flushing Hospital Medical Center. MediSys Connect provides patients with secure online access to their medical records and allows them to manage their health information. Through the portal, patients can schedule appointments, view health information such as medications, allergies, and test results, request medication refills, access health information resources, and message their care team. The availability of MediSys Connect on both the App Store and Google Play further enhances patient access to their health information via mobile devices. An additional search result also corroborates the use of Epic as the EHR system at Flushing Hospital Medical Center.
