About the Practice

Thompson Health, part of UR Medicine, is an award-winning, non-profit healthcare system serving the greater Finger Lakes region of upstate New York. It provides a full range of healthcare services including acute care at F.F. Thompson Hospital, skilled nursing care at the M.M. Ewing Continuing Care Center, independent and enriched senior living, primary care, and various specialty services such as OB/Gyn, cancer care through the Sands Cancer Center (part of UR Medicine's Wilmot Cancer Institute), rehabilitative services, diagnostic imaging, orthopedic surgery, urgent care, and wellness programs to residents in Ontario, Livingston, and Wayne counties.
