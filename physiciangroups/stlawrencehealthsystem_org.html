About the Practice

Massena Hospital is a 25-bed acute care facility affiliated with St. Lawrence Health System. It provides a range of inpatient services including medical, surgical, labor and delivery, and pediatrics, as well as emergency care. The hospital also offers comprehensive ancillary services such as a 24/7 laboratory, advanced imaging (MRI, CT, bone densitometry, ultrasound, mammography, fluoroscopy), nutritional counseling, and various therapies (physical, occupational, speech, respiratory). Additionally, through outreach centers, it provides primary and specialty care in areas like cardiology, infusion, nephrology, neurology, OB/GYN, women's health, pediatrics, surgery, and walk-in services. Behavioral Health services are located in a separate building.
