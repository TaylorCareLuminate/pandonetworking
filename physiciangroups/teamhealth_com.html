About the Practice

TeamHealth is a physician practice founded in 1979 and based in Knoxville, Tennessee, focused on medical outsourcing. The company provides physicians and advanced practice clinicians in numerous specialties, including emergency medicine, hospital medicine, anesthesiology, critical care, obstetrics, orthopedic surgery, general surgery, ambulatory care, post-acute care, and medical call centers. TeamHealth partners with hospitals and healthcare facilities across the United States to provide staffing and improve healthcare delivery and patient outcomes.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

TeamHealth, a large physician practice that provides staffing and administrative services to a wide network of hospitals and healthcare facilities across the United States, does not utilize a single, universal patient portal or electronic health record (EHR) system for all the patients they serve. Instead, the patient portal and underlying EHR system used by a patient who received care from a TeamHealth-affiliated provider are determined by the specific hospital or healthcare facility where the patient was treated. TeamHealth partners with approximately 2,900 acute and post-acute facilities nationwide. As part of these partnerships, TeamHealth clinicians integrate with the existing systems of these facilities. For instance, AccessNurse, a company under TeamHealth, interfaces with more than 300 different EMRs. A partnership between TeamHealth and Array Behavioral Care also highlighted the integration with existing EHR systems of the partner facilities. While specific patient portals like "MyRecord" are mentioned in conjunction with TeamHealth at certain locations like Med Center Health, this portal is specific to that health system. Similarly, "MyChart" might be used by a hospital system that partners with TeamHealth, such as Trinity Health Mid-Atlantic. TeamHealth does offer a portal for patients to view and pay their bills online, but this is distinct from a comprehensive patient health portal for accessing medical records. Therefore, if you received care from a TeamHealth provider, the patient portal you would use is the one provided by the hospital or healthcare facility where you were seen. To find the specific patient portal and its associated EHR vendor, you would need to identify the hospital or facility and then inquire about their patient portal access.
