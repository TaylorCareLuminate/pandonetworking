About the Practice

NYC Health + Hospitals/Kings County is a leading healthcare facility providing comprehensive care to all New Yorkers, regardless of their ability to pay. As part of the largest municipal healthcare system in the US, it offers a wide range of inpatient, outpatient, and specialty services across all fields of modern medicine, including a world-renowned Level 1 Trauma Center. With over 627 beds and more than 200 clinics, the hospital provides primary care, prenatal services, labor and delivery, and specialized treatment for various conditions, utilizing state-of-the-art equipment and technology.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

NYC Health + Hospitals/Kings County utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal system licensed from Epic Systems Corporation. Through the MyChart portal, patients of NYC Health + Hospitals can access various features online or via a mobile app, including viewing test results, managing appointments, messaging their doctor's office, requesting prescription refills, and paying bills. This indicates that the underlying electronic health record (EHR) system in use by NYC Health + Hospitals, including the Kings County location, is Epic.
