About the Practice

Summit Health is a physician-driven, patient-centric network formed by the 2019 merger of Summit Medical Group and CityMD, now part of Walgreens-owned VillageMD. It is a multi-specialty medical practice offering a full spectrum of high-quality primary, specialty, and urgent care services. [8, 9, 11, 15] They aim to simplify healthcare and provide a more connected, comprehensive, and responsive care experience, serving millions of patients across multiple states. [14, 15]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Summit Health utilizes different patient portal systems depending on the user's location and the history of the specific medical group they access. For patients in Oregon, Summit Health uses the MyChart patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal commonly associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Notably, Summit Health has been recognized as a top user of Epic Systems. For other Summit Health locations, the patient portal is generally referred to as the "Patient Portal" on their website. While the branding is generic, information suggests this portal is integrated with athenahealth solutions in some areas. Summit Health's website also indicates ongoing system integration following mergers with entities like Westmed and New Jersey Urology, which may mean different portal experiences depending on the legacy system of the practice. In summary, Summit Health uses MyChart (Epic) for its Oregon locations and a different patient portal, potentially integrated with athenahealth, for other areas.
