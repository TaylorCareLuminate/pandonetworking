About the Practice

Trinity Health is one of the largest not-for-profit, faith-based healthcare systems in the United States, serving diverse communities across 26-27 states. The system includes numerous hospitals, outpatient facilities, senior communities, and home-based care programs. Trinity Health provides comprehensive healthcare services with over 40 specialties, focusing on delivering advanced, personal, and compassionate care, including a significant focus on senior care, home health, and hospice services.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Trinity Health primarily uses the MyChart patient portal, which is tied to the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Multiple locations within the Trinity Health system, including those in Michigan, Mid-Atlantic, and New England, utilize Trinity Health MyChart. Trinity Health has announced a significant upgrade to its EHR system, selecting Epic as the new technology partner for a nationwide implementation.. This transition to Epic includes the Epic platform's patient-facing application, MyChart.. The health system is in the process of implementing Epic, with a projected go-live date of November 1, 2026, for all Trinity Health-owned locations.. Trinity Health Michigan's "Trinity Health Connect" program, which offers access to their EHR and practice management system to independent providers, is also powered by Epic and provides access to MyChart.. While Trinity Health in Minot, North Dakota, previously used a patient portal called "My Patient Portal" which utilized Cerner Health, more recent information indicates that Trinity Health based in Minot has also selected Epic as its new EHR provider with a planned transition to Epic and MyChart by November 2026. Earlier reports from 2014 mentioned Trinity Medical Center in Minot using Cerner products for clinical solutions despite a settlement regarding patient accounting software.. In summary, the main patient portal vendor and branding used by Trinity Health is MyChart, which is connected to the Epic EHR system. The organization is in the process of implementing Epic and MyChart across its entire system, transitioning away from other systems, such as Cerner in some locations.
