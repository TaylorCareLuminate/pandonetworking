About the Practice

TMC Health is Southern Arizona’s only locally governed nonprofit health system, serving the community for over 80 years with a mission to provide exceptional health care with compassion. The system includes Tucson Medical Center, TMC Rincon, TMCOne primary and specialty care practices, Benson Hospital, and Northern Cochise Community Hospital. TMC Health offers a wide range of services including award-winning acute and emergency care, pediatric care (including Tucson's first Pediatric Emergency Department), women's services, maternity care, cardiac care, orthopaedic care, neuroscience, hospice, surgical services, and vascular care. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

TMC Health utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is a product licensed from Epic Systems Corporation. This directly links the patient portal branding to the underlying EHR vendor. Through the MyChart portal, patients of TMC Health can access various features to manage their healthcare, including communicating with their doctors, viewing test results, requesting prescription refills, completing eCheck-in, managing appointments, and utilizing telehealth tools. The MyChart portal serves the entire TMC Health network, including TMCOne, Tucson Medical Center Rincon Campus, and TMC Health Cancer Center.
