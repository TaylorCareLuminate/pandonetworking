About the Practice

DENT Neurologic Institute is the largest outpatient neurology practice in the United States, specializing in neurologic care and state-of-the-art neurodiagnostics. [4, 8] They provide comprehensive and compassionate patient care across 26 sub-specialty clinics, offering advanced diagnostic services, clinical research, and education for a wide range of neurological disorders. [8, 9, 11] Their objective is to provide the highest quality care by staying on the forefront of neurological advances, utilizing the latest technology, and maintaining partnerships with other specialized clinicians. [4, 8]

Methodology & Findings

Dent Neurologic Institute utilizes a patient portal with the branding "myDENTChart," and their patient guide also references the "Healow App." The Healow App is the patient portal and personal health record platform developed by eClinicalWorks. Dent Neurologic Institute uses eClinicalWorks as their electronic health record (EHR) system.
