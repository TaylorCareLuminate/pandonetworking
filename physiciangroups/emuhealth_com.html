About the Practice

EMU Health is a premier dual-licensed Article 28 Diagnostic & Treatment Center and AAAHC-accredited Ambulatory Surgical Center in Queens, NY, founded in 2016. It is a trusted multi-specialty outpatient medical facility providing comprehensive, high-quality, evidence-based care under one roof. Services include Primary Care, Orthopedic Surgery, Pain Management, Gynecology, General Surgery, Urology, Vascular Surgery, Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery, Breast Surgery, Sports Medicine, Podiatry, Rheumatology, Physical Therapy, Women's Health, Interventional Radiology & Cardiology, and specialized pediatric dental care under general anesthesia. The center is committed to accessible and compassionate care for the community.
