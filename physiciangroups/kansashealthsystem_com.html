About the Practice

The University of Kansas Health System is a nonprofit, academic medical center affiliated with the University of Kansas Medical Center. It serves as a destination for complex care and diagnosis, leveraging expertise in medical research and education with physicians representing over 200 specialties. [3, 11, 12, 15] It is the region's only nationally verified Level I Trauma Center and includes a National Cancer Institute-designated comprehensive cancer center. [3, 7, 11, 16] The health system provides a wide range of care, from primary care to highly specialized clinical services, guided by a mission to lead the nation in caring, healing, teaching, and discovering. [10, 13, 16, 17, 18]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The University of Kansas Health System (kansashealthsystem.com) uses MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is the patient portal commonly used by healthcare organizations that utilize the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. MyChart provides patients with secure online access to their medical information. Through MyChart, patients can manage appointments, view test results, request prescription refills, pay medical bills, and communicate with their healthcare team. Several sources indicate that MyChart is an integrated feature of the Epic EHR system, allowing patients to access information stored within Epic.
