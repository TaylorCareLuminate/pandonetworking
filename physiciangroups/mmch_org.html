About the Practice

Maimonides Midwood Community Hospital (MMCH), formerly New York Community Hospital, is a voluntary, non-profit community hospital in Brooklyn, NY, providing inpatient, ancillary, and outpatient care regardless of a patient's ability to pay. [2] As part of Maimonides Health, it offers comprehensive services including Medical-Surgical, ICU, Rehabilitation, Emergency Services, Diagnostic Imaging, and Outpatient Services. [9] Recent enhancements include a new infusion center, a new MRI suite, an advanced cardiac suite, and a cardiac catheterization lab. [2, 7, 18] MMCH has also been designated a Primary Stroke Center. [18]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

New York Community Hospital Of Brooklyn, Inc., which is now part of Maimonides Health and referred to in some contexts as Maimonides Midwood Community Hospital, utilizes the FollowMyHealth patient portal. FollowMyHealth is a patient engagement platform provided by Veradigm, formerly part of Allscripts. FollowMyHealth is designed to be EHR-agnostic, meaning it can integrate with various electronic health record systems. However, information available suggests a strong integration and association with Allscripts EHR platforms, including Allscripts Practice Management. Veradigm, the provider of FollowMyHealth, also offers other healthcare software solutions, and FollowMyHealth is noted to integrate seamlessly with Allscripts systems. Based on the patient portal used and the known integrations of FollowMyHealth, the EHR system likely in use by New York Community Hospital Of Brooklyn, Inc. (Maimonides Health) is a product of Allscripts.
