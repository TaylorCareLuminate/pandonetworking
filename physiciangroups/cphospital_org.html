About the Practice

Canton-Potsdam Hospital is a not-for-profit community hospital offering a full range of general and specialty care, inpatient and outpatient services, and community health education programs. [2, 4, 10, 15] Core programs include emergency medicine, acute care, hospitalist medicine, and critical care, supplemented by outpatient services across multiple locations in St. Lawrence County. [4, 15] The hospital provides specialty care in over 40 fields, featuring a robust orthopedic surgery and sports medicine program, a Center for Cancer Care, and is designated as a Level III Trauma Center. [4, 9, 15] Additional services include physical, occupational, and speech therapy, surgical services, diagnostic imaging, primary care, and inpatient drug and alcohol rehabilitation. [3, 5, 8, 11, 12] Its mission is to provide skilled, compassionate, cost-effective care that promotes wellness and meets community needs, aiming to improve health, expand access, and lower costs through service coordination. [13]
