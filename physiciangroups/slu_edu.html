About the Practice

Saint Louis University School of Medicine, established in 1836, is a private, Jesuit medical school known for training physicians and biomedical scientists, conducting medical research, and providing health services on a local, national and international level. [2] The school is a pioneer and conducts research in various specialties including geriatric medicine, organ transplantation, chronic disease prevention, cardiovascular disease, neurosciences, vaccine research, cancer, infectious disease, liver disease, aging/neurological disorders, and heart/lung disease. [2, 4, 10] Committed to compassionate, patient-centered, and humanistic medicine grounded in Jesuit values, it cultivates practitioners who are shaping the future of healthcare and works for health equity and the well-being of the community. [3, 6]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Saint Louis University School of Medicine (SLU.edu), specifically its clinical practice known as SLUCare, uses the MyChart patient portal. This portal is part of the broader SSM Health system. SSM Health, the parent organization of SLUCare, selected Epic Systems as its electronic health record (EHR) vendor in 2020. MyChart is the patient portal system developed by Epic. Therefore, the patient portal vendor/branding/name used by Saint Louis University School of Medicine (SLUCare) is MyChart, and the underlying EHR vendor is Epic.
