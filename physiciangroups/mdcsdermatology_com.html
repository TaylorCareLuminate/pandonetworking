About the Practice

MDCS Dermatology: Medical Dermatology and Cosmetic Surgery is a premier practice with locations in New York City, Long Island, and New Jersey, offering expertise in dermatologic medicine, surgery, and aesthetics for patients of all ages. [1, 2] They provide advanced skin treatments utilizing state-of-the-art equipment and have a staff of board-certified dermatologists, plastic surgeons, physician assistants, and estheticians. [1, 3] Services range from treating skin cancer with Mohs surgery to customized skin rejuvenation programs, covering virtually every type of skin issue: medical, surgical, and cosmetic. [1, 3]
