About the Practice

NYU Grossman School of Medicine, part of NYU Langone Health, is a private academic medical center founded in 1841. Its core mission encompasses the education and training of physicians and scientists, pioneering medical research, and providing comprehensive patient care across a wide range of specialties. The school offers innovative medical degree programs, including an accelerated three-year MD pathway, and provides graduate medical training in over 70 specialties. It is recognized nationally for its high quality of care, patient safety, and significant contributions to medical science.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

NYU School of Medicine, which is part of NYU Langone Health, uses MyChart as its patient portal. This patient portal is provided by Epic Systems, the vendor for NYU Langone Health's electronic health record (EHR) system. The NYU Langone Health app allows patients to log in with their MyChart account to access their health records, schedule appointments, view test results, and message their care team. MyChart serves as a secure online platform for patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare conveniently.
