About the Practice

Reproductive Medicine Associates of New York (RMA of New York) is a world-renowned fertility center specializing in reproductive medicine. [4, 10] They provide comprehensive fertility testing and a wide range of treatments including IVF, IUI, PGT, egg freezing, sperm banking, and embryo cryopreservation. [5, 10] Their team of expert reproductive endocrinologists, nurses, and embryologists utilize an evidence-based, data-driven approach to deliver compassionate, individualized care and achieve high IVF success rates. [4, 7, 10, 11] RMA of New York also serves as the Division of Reproductive Endocrinology and Infertility for the Mount Sinai Health System. [4, 7, 10]
