About the Practice

Hudson River Health Care (HRHCare) is a non-profit Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) providing comprehensive primary, preventive, behavioral, and oral health care and care coordination services to underserved and vulnerable populations regardless of insurance status or ability to pay. Services include adult, adolescent and pediatric primary care, obstetrical/GYN care, family planning, HIV services, mental health, podiatry, nutrition counseling, health education, translation, transportation, benefits counseling, nurse midwifery, dentistry, optometry, and substance abuse treatment. The organization focuses on integrated care, including addressing childhood trauma, and operates a network of health centers in southern New York, the Hudson Valley, Long Island (including Suffolk County), and New York City.

Methodology & Findings

Based on the search results, HRHCare, which has recently rebranded as Sun River Health, utilizes a patient portal called "My HRHCare Patient Login". The patient portal is provided by eClinicalWorks, as indicated by the login URL "https://health.eclinicalworks.com/HRHCare" and a mention of the "eClinicalWorks Consent form". Therefore, HRHCare (now Sun River Health) uses the eClinicalWorks EHR system, and their patient portal is branded as My HRHCare Patient Login, powered by eClinicalWorks.
