About the Practice

Lahey Hospital & Medical Center, part of Beth Israel Lahey Health, is a physician-led nonprofit teaching hospital based in Burlington, Massachusetts, and is a regional medical campus of UMass Chan Medical School. It offers a wide range of specialty and primary care services, including featured services such as Bariatric Surgery, Cancer Care, Cardiovascular Care, Orthopedics, and Urology, for which it has been nationally recognized. The center provides high-quality, accessible, and personal healthcare through its academic medical center, community hospitals, and various outpatient centers, offering emergency services and comprehensive medical and surgical services for diverse conditions.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Lahey Hospital and Medical Center, as part of Beth Israel Lahey Health, is utilizing a patient portal called MyBILH Chart. This patient portal is associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Beth Israel Lahey Health has been in the process of rolling out MyBILH Chart and the Epic EHR system across its different entities to consolidate health records and provide patients with a single portal to manage their care. This transition began on June 1, 2024. MyBILH Chart allows patients to message their doctor's office, review test results, view notes and visit summaries, access health information, manage appointments, and request prescription refills. Community providers can also use a related portal, Beth Israel Lahey Health Link (BILH Link), to view patient information stored in the Epic EHR. An agreement between Lahey Health and Epic to implement a new EHR system was initially announced in 2013.
