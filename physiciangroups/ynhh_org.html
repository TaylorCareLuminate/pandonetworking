About the Practice

Northeast Medical Group Pediatrics, formerly known in some locations as Pediatric Associates, provides comprehensive medical care for children from newborns to young adults up to age 21. Services include well-child visits, immunizations, sick appointments, sports physicals, management of chronic illnesses, minor trauma care, and behavioral health support. They offer both in-person and telehealth visits, as well as prenatal consultations, and provide referrals to specialists at Yale New Haven Children's Hospital.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Northeast Medical Group, Pediatric Associates, a part of Yale New Haven Health, utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart provides patients with secure online access to portions of their electronic medical record, including health summaries, medications, and test results. This patient portal is widely recognized as being associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Yale New Haven Health System, which includes Northeast Medical Group, uses an electronic medical records system that MyChart accesses.
