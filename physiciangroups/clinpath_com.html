About the Practice

Clin-Path Associates, PLC is Arizona's largest private pathology practice with over 40 years of experience, providing professional and laboratory services to healthcare facilities in the Phoenix metro area and statewide. [2, 3, 4, 8] They offer hospital-based pathology, outpatient diagnostics, and specialized services in areas such as Dermatopathology, Hematopathology, Breast Pathology, Cytopathology, GI & Liver Pathology, and Pediatric Pathology, focusing on accurate and timely diagnoses for patient care.
