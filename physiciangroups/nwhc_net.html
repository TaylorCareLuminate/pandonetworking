About the Practice

Northern Westchester Hospital (NWH) is a not-for-profit, 245-bed facility in Mount Kisco, New York, part of Northwell Health. [8, 11, 18] It provides quality, patient-centered care with state-of-the-art technology. [3, 9, 10, 15, 18] Specialties include Bariatric Surgery, Cancer Treatment and Wellness, Cardiovascular Disease, Gamma Knife, Robotic Surgery, Orthopedic and Spine, Breast Care, and Maternity services. [3, 8, 10, 11, 12] NWH is recognized for its patient-centered care and nursing excellence. [9, 11, 16]

Methodology & Findings

Northern Westchester Hospital (NWHC), which is a member of Northwell Health, utilizes the FollowMyHealth patient portal. FollowMyHealth is a patient engagement platform developed by Allscripts Healthcare, LLC. While FollowMyHealth is designed to integrate seamlessly with various electronic health record (EHR) systems, it is an Allscripts product. Therefore, based on the patient portal used, Northern Westchester Hospital likely uses an EHR system that integrates with or is provided by Allscripts.
