About the Practice

SMIL Southwest Medical Imaging is a physician-owned and physician-led provider of outpatient diagnostic medical imaging and interventional radiology services in the Phoenix Metro area. With over 40 years of experience, they offer a comprehensive range of services using state-of-the-art technology, including PET, Nuclear Medicine, MRI, CT, Mammography, Ultrasound, DEXA, and X-ray. The organization focuses on providing high-quality, patient-centered care with a commitment to accuracy and a positive patient experience through their multiple locations and fellowship-trained radiologists.
