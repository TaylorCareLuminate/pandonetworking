About the Practice

Urban Health Plan (UHP) is a large federally qualified health center system serving communities in the Bronx, Corona, Queens, and Central Harlem since 1974. [1, 2, 13] Their mission is to improve community health and quality of life by providing affordable, comprehensive, quality, primary and specialty health care. [1, 13] Services include primary care (Adult Medicine, Pediatrics, Teen/Adolescent Health, Geriatrics, Women's Health/OB-GYN), specialty care (Allergy, Behavioral Health, Cardiology, Dermatology, Endocrinology, Family Planning, Reproductive Services, Podiatry, Optometry, Gastroenterology, Rheumatology, Nephrology, Infectious Disease, Immunology, Neurology, Psychiatry), dental care, diagnostic and ancillary services, and support services such as Nutrition, Social Services, Health Education, and WIC. [3, 6, 9, 15, 16, 17] They are committed to addressing social determinants of health and are also a teaching health center. [6]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Urban Health Plan, an healthcare provider with locations in the Bronx, Central Harlem, and Corona, Queens, utilizes eClinicalWorks as their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. Their patient portal is branded as healow, which is a product offered by eClinicalWorks. Patients of Urban Health Plan can access the healow patient portal and app to schedule appointments, including virtual visits through the Virtual Care Center, and manage other healthcare needs. The integration of the eClinicalWorks EHR and the healow platform is also used by Urban Health Plan to improve patient engagement and operational efficiency, such as utilizing features like the healow no-show prediction AI model.
