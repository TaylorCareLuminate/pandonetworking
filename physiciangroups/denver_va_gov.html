About the Practice

The VA Eastern Colorado Health Care System, which includes the Rocky Mountain Regional VA Medical Center and multiple clinics, is a 1a-High Complexity facility providing comprehensive health care to Veterans across a large region including Colorado and parts of surrounding states. The system offers a broad spectrum of services, including primary care, specialty medical and surgical care (such as pulmonary, critical care, oncology, and cardiac services), mental health care (including psychiatry, psychology, and addiction treatment), physical medicine and rehabilitation, and emergency services. It is also a teaching and research facility affiliated with academic institutions. [2, 3, 13, 16, 17]
