About the Practice

Buffalo Medical Group is one of the oldest and largest physician-directed group practices in New York State, offering comprehensive and coordinated patient care through a multidisciplinary team of experienced physicians, surgeons, and other healthcare professionals. [7] They provide a wide range of primary and specialty care services, including Allergy & Immunology, Cardiology, Dermatology, Endocrinology, Gastroenterology, General Surgery, Internal Medicine, Neurology, Obstetrics & Gynecology, Oncology, Orthopedic Surgery, Pediatrics, Pulmonary & Critical Care Medicine, Radiology, Rheumatology, Urology, and Vascular Surgery. [5, 9] Complementary services such as diagnostic imaging, laboratory services, physical therapy, pharmacy services, behavioral health, and sleep centers are also available. [3, 5]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Buffalo Medical Group utilizes a patient portal branded as MyBMGChart. This patient portal is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Buffalo Medical Group adopted the Epic EHR system in 1999 and subsequently launched their patient portal, MyBMGChart, in 2007. MyBMGChart allows patients to access their personal health records, request prescription refills, and communicate with their care team. The login domain for the Buffalo Medical Group patient portal is www.mybmgchart.com, and it is identified as Epic MyChart software.
