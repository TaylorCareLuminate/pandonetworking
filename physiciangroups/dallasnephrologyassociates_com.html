About the Practice

Dallas Nephrology Associates (DNA) is a national leader in nephrology, providing comprehensive kidney care for patients with kidney disease, hypertension, transplants, and complicated electrolyte disorders. [1, 9, 13] They offer the entire spectrum of nephrology care, including office management of CKD, 24/7 hospital coverage, dialysis clinics, management of dialysis accesses, and serve as Medical Director for transplant programs. [1, 7] With a focus on innovation and patient choice, DNA emphasizes person-centered, value-based care and has one of the largest teams of kidney specialists in the United States. [1, 2, 4]

Methodology & Findings

Dallas Nephrology Associates utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. This is the same patient portal branding used by healthcare providers who have implemented Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) system. The Dallas Nephrology Associates website provides information on how patients can sign up for and utilize their MyChart account to manage appointments, access test results, communicate with their care team, and more.
