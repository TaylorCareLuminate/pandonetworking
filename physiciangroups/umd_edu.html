About the Practice

The University of Maryland, College Park (UMD) is a top-ranked public land-grant research university and the flagship institution of the University System of Maryland. Located in College Park, MD, just outside Washington, D.C., UMD leverages its prime location to offer extensive connections to national institutions, government, and cultural resources. The university is dedicated to advancing knowledge, providing outstanding and innovative instruction, and cultivating intellectual growth across a wide array of academic disciplines and interdisciplinary fields, including notable programs in engineering, business, computer science, public policy, biological sciences, and psychology.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The University of Maryland Medical System (UMMS) uses MyChart as its patient portal. Previously known as MyPortfolio, the portal was rebranded to MyChart as of August 15, 2024, to offer a more consistent experience for patients who use MyChart with different healthcare providers. MyChart is a patient portal that connects you to your health care information, allowing you to schedule appointments, pay bills, view test results, request prescription refills, and communicate with your healthcare team. MyChart is the patient portal commonly associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. While the search results explicitly state that UMMS uses MyChart, they do not directly name Epic as the EHR vendor within the provided snippets. However, given the strong and widely recognized association between MyChart and Epic, it is highly probable that the University of Maryland Medical System utilizes Epic as its EHR system.
