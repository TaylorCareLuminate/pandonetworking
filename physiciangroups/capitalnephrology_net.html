About the Practice

Capital Nephrology Medical Group specializes in Nephrology and provides comprehensive care for kidney-related diseases, including chronic kidney disease, acute kidney failure, high blood pressure, dialysis, transplantation, and vascular access management. The group offers state-of-the-art and evidence-based medicine, operating an outpatient surgery center for access procedures, and serves patients across multiple convenient locations in the Greater Sacramento and surrounding areas.
