About the Practice

Eastern Nephrology Associates (ENA) is a premier nephrology practice serving eastern North Carolina and northeastern South Carolina, formed by a merger of Eastern Nephrology Associates and Southeastern Nephrology Associates. They specialize in comprehensive kidney care, offering services for Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD), End Stage Kidney Disease (ESKD), kidney dialysis, kidney transplant, hypertension, and vascular access. The practice provides patient-centered, state-of-the-art medical care delivered by a team of board-certified nephrologists, interventional and transplant nephrologists, advanced practice providers, and support staff, and includes in-house laboratory services, vascular access centers, a transplant care team, and a clinical research program focused on improving patient outcomes and quality of life.
