About the Practice

UChicago Medicine is a nationally ranked academic medical center affiliated with the University of Chicago, encompassing the University of Chicago Health System, the Biological Sciences Division, and the Pritzker School of Medicine. Its mission is centered on patient care, research, and education. UChicago Medicine provides a full array of medical and surgical specialties for adults and children, including notable programs in cancer, digestive diseases, endocrinology, kidney disease, respiratory disorders, heart and vascular, urology, geriatrics, gynecology, neurology and neurosurgery, ear, nose, and throat, orthopaedics, and transplantation. It operates inpatient hospitals and numerous outpatient clinics and physician practices across Chicago, its suburbs, and Northwest Indiana, and is recognized for its groundbreaking research and comprehensive care.

Methodology & Findings

UChicago Medicine utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is a product of Epic. The MyChart patient portal provides patients with secure online access to manage various aspects of their healthcare, including scheduling appointments, viewing test results, renewing prescriptions, messaging care teams, and paying bills. This indicates a partnership between UChicago Medicine and Epic, with MyChart serving as the patient-facing component of Epic's electronic health record (EHR) system.
