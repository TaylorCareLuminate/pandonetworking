About the Practice

AdvantageCare Physicians (ACPNY) is a primary and specialty care practice serving patients across all five New York City boroughs and Long Island. [3, 5] They offer a "Whole You Care" approach that includes essential family care like annual checkups, vaccinations, and women's health, as well as management of chronic conditions such as diabetes and asthma. [3, 6] ACPNY provides expanding specialty services, including cardiology, gastroenterology, behavioral health, and more. [3, 9] Many locations also provide convenient access to on-site radiology and lab services. [6, 9]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

AdvantageCare Physicians (ACPNY) utilizes the myACPNY patient portal, which is a branded version of MyChart, the patient portal developed by Epic Systems Corporation. This indicates that AdvantageCare Physicians uses the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. The myACPNY portal allows patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare online, including scheduling appointments, requesting prescription refills, viewing test results and medical records, and communicating with their care team. The integration with the Epic EHR system provides patients with a centralized platform for accessing their health information.
