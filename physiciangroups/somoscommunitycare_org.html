About the Practice

SOMOS Community Care is a non-profit, physician-led network comprising over 2,500 healthcare providers dedicated to serving more than 1 million Medicaid and Medicare patients, primarily within New York City's underserved immigrant communities across the Bronx, Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens, and Nassau County. The organization focuses on providing high-quality, culturally competent, and community-based healthcare, including primary care, specialty care, behavioral health services, and addressing social determinants of health. SOMOS operates a large network of medical offices and clinics, emphasizing a value-based care model aimed at improving health outcomes, reducing hospitalizations, and providing comprehensive, integrated support tailored to patients' language and cultural backgrounds.
