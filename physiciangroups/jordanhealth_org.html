About the Practice

Anthony L. Jordan Health Center, a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC), was established to provide comprehensive primary preventive medical and dental care with a special commitment to low-income and underserved populations. It offers a wide range of services including family medicine, urgent care, dentistry, OB/GYN, pediatrics, behavioral health, optometry, podiatry, pharmacy, lab services, and WIC supplemental nutrition. The center strives to deliver high-quality care regardless of age, income, or insurance coverage and aims to be a comprehensive healthcare provider for the community.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Anthony L Jordan Health Center (jordanhealth.org) utilizes the Healow patient portal. Healow is a patient engagement platform developed by eClinicalWorks and is designed to integrate with the eClinicalWorks Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. The Healow portal provides patients with online access to various aspects of their healthcare, including the ability to request or schedule appointments, access health records, view test results, request prescription refills, and communicate securely with their healthcare team. This portal is accessible via a mobile app or a web portal. Multiple sources indicate a strong connection and integration between the Healow platform and the eClinicalWorks EHR. For instance, one search result explicitly states that the patient portal/Healow app is provided in partnership with eClinicalWorks.
