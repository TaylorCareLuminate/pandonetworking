About the Practice

Phoenician Medical Center Inc., which includes Phoenician Primary Care, is a multi-specialty medical group serving the Phoenix Metropolitan Area with a comprehensive integrated care network. They offer primary care and subspecialties such as Neurology & Sleep Medicine, Pain Management, Cardiology, Anesthesiology, Vein & Vascular, ENT, and Ophthalmology. Ancillary services provided include Imaging and Radiology, Laboratory, Outpatient Surgery Centers, Sleep Centers, Physical Therapy, Neuro Muscular Therapy, and Home Health, focusing on a whole-person approach to healthcare.
