About the Practice

Associated Valley Radiologists Ltd is a radiology group practice that provides comprehensive diagnostic and interventional radiology services. Their specialties encompass Diagnostic Radiology, Neuroradiology, Pediatric Radiology, Vascular & Interventional Radiology, as well as subspecialties like cardiac imaging, musculoskeletal radiology, nuclear medicine, oncologic radiology, and women's imaging. [1, 12] They employ various advanced imaging techniques including x-ray, radionuclides, ultrasound, electromagnetic radiation, MRI, CT, CTA, mammography, bone density, body composition, and nuclear medicine to accurately diagnose and contribute to the treatment of medical conditions. [2, 5]

Methodology & Findings

Associated Valley Radiologists Ltd, which is managed by Banner Imaging, appears to utilize a patient portal provided by MedInformatix. While Banner Health, the managing entity of Banner Imaging, primarily uses Cerner and eClinicalWorks as their preferred electronic health record (EHR) platforms, evidence suggests that a MedInformatix system is in use for radiology services. A patient portal directly associated with Associated Valley Radiologists includes a copyright by MedInformatix Inc., indicating their role as the vendor for this portal. This portal allows patients to request appointments, view and manage medical records, exchange secure messages, and access forms. MedInformatix is a company that provides Radiology Information Systems (RIS) and EHR software specifically designed for radiology practices, which includes a patient portal as a key feature. The capabilities of the MedInformatix patient portal align with the features mentioned for the portal linked to Associated Valley Radiologists. Furthermore, the "Banner Imaging Patient Portal," to which Associated Valley Radiologists refers patients, allows access to imaging reports and facilitates online scheduling for certain exams. This aligns with the functionalities of a radiology-specific patient portal like that offered by MedInformatix. Based on the available information, the patient portal vendor/branding used by Associated Valley Radiologists is a MedInformatix patient portal, likely integrated with or branded under Banner Imaging. The underlying EHR system specifically for their radiology operations is also indicated to be MedInformatix, given its specialization in radiology information systems and EHRs.
