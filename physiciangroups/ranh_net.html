About the Practice

Radiology Associates of New Hartford is a well-established private imaging group in Central New York providing professional radiology services across multiple hospitals and affiliated imaging centers. [3, 4] They specialize in a wide range of imaging technologies and services including Diagnostic Radiology, Cardiovascular Imaging, Interventional Radiology, Neuroradiology, Body Imaging, Nuclear Medicine, Women's Imaging, Musculoskeletal Radiology, X-Ray, Ultrasound, Computed Tomography (CT), and Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI). Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Radiology Associates of New Hartford (ranh.net) utilizes a patient portal developed by MedInformatix Inc.. The patient portal is branded as the "Patient Portal" and provides patients with electronic access to view and manage their medical records, view upcoming appointments, exchange secure messages with staff, and view images and studies. The copyright information on the patient portal page indicates that it is a product of MedInformatix Inc., suggesting that MedInformatix is likely the vendor for their electronic health record (EHR) system as well.
