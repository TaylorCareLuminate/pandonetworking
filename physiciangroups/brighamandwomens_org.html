About the Practice

Brigham and Women's Hospital is a world-renowned academic medical center and a major teaching hospital of Harvard Medical School based in Boston, Massachusetts. It offers comprehensive, world-class patient care across virtually every area of adult medicine and is nationally recognized in numerous specialties, including cancer, cardiology, obstetrics and gynecology, diabetes & endocrinology, and rheumatology. The hospital is also a leader in biomedical research and medical education, and is a founding member of the Mass General Brigham integrated healthcare system.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Brigham and Women's Hospital, as part of Mass General Brigham, utilizes a patient portal branded as "Patient Gateway". This online tool allows patients to manage appointments, request prescription renewals, view test results, communicate with their doctors, and access their health records. The underlying electronic health record (EHR) vendor for the Patient Gateway system used by Mass General Brigham is Epic. This is indicated by information stating that patients might be able to access health information from external providers if they use the same EHR vendor, "known as Epic". This is similar to how Epic's patient portal is often branded as MyChart by other healthcare organizations.
