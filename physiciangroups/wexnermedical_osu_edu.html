About the Practice

The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center is a multidisciplinary academic medical center located in Columbus, Ohio, on the main campus of Ohio State University, recognized as a leader in central Ohio for healthcare and medical research. [3, 15] Its mission is to improve people's lives through innovation in research, education, and patient care, providing world-class care and access to advanced treatments across virtually every medical specialty and subspecialty through its seven hospitals and network of outpatient centers. [3, 5, 15]
