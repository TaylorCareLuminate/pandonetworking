About the Practice

New York Institute of Technology College of Osteopathic Medicine (NYITCOM) is a private medical school that trains osteopathic physicians for a lifetime of learning and practice, integrating evidence-based knowledge, critical thinking, and osteopathic principles and practice. [3, 6, 13] Offering a Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine (D.O.) degree, NYITCOM focuses on providing progressive, patient-centered, population-based osteopathic healthcare through education and research. [3, 4, 12] The college prepares physicians for diverse healthcare careers, including service in urban and rural communities, and provides clinical training through affiliations with hospitals and its own primary care centers. [3, 8, 9, 12, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

New York Institute of Technology College of Osteopathic Medicine (NYITCOM) utilizes the Healow patient portal. The Healow patient portal is a platform developed by eClinicalWorks. Patients of NYITCOM can access the Healow patient portal through the Healow app or via the clinic's website. This portal allows patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare, including accessing health information, scheduling appointments, requesting prescription refills, and communicating securely with their healthcare providers. The integration of the Healow portal with the eClinicalWorks EHR system facilitates the secure exchange of health information.
