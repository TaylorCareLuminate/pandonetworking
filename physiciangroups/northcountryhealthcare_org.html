About the Practice

North Country HealthCare is a federally qualified community health center serving communities across northern Arizona. [2, 4, 6, 13] It provides affordable, accessible, quality primary care and a wide range of comprehensive, integrated medical services to patients of all ages. [2, 4, 6, 9] Specialties include Primary Care & Family Medicine, Pediatrics, Obstetrics & Gynecology, Dental Care, Behavioral Health, Pharmacy, Physical Therapy, and Telemedicine, among others. [2, 9, 12] The organization focuses on the health and well-being of patients and the community, offering services to all, including the uninsured and underinsured, with a sliding fee scale. [2, 4, 6, 9]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

North Country HealthCare (northcountryhealthcare.org) utilizes a patient portal referred to as the NCH Patient Portal or North Country Healthcare patient portal. This portal is integrated with their electronic health record (EHR) system, which is MEDITECH Expanse. Patients can access the portal online and also through the MEDITECH MHealth app for mobile devices. North Country Healthcare implemented a shared MEDITECH Expanse EHR across their facilities to consolidate patient information.
