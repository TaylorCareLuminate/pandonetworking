About the Practice

Arizona Sports Medicine Center (azsportsmedicine.com) is a recognized leader in sports medicine and orthopedic surgery in the Southwest, with over 20 years of experience. [2, 3, 5, 7] They specialize in diagnosing, treating, rehabilitating, and preventing sports-related injuries and orthopedic conditions, including bone, joint, and muscle injuries and disorders. [1, 4] They treat patients ranging from weekend warriors to elite professionals, utilizing advanced technology and minimally invasive approaches. [1, 2, 3, 4] Their board-certified physicians are committed to providing comprehensive and personalized care to help patients return to their optimal performance level. [1, 2, 3] They are also the team physicians for several professional sports teams. [2, 3, 5, 6, 10]
