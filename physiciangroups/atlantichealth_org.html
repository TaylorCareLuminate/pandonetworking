About the Practice

Atlantic Health System is a non-profit healthcare network primarily serving New Jersey. They are dedicated to designing and delivering high-quality, innovative, and personalized healthcare to build healthier communities and improve lives. The system includes seven award-winning hospitals and offers a full continuum of care across more than 550 sites, covering a wide range of specialties such as cardiology and heart surgery, orthopedics, cancer care, neuroscience, women's health, pediatrics, and providing services like urgent care, emergency care, rehabilitation, diagnostic imaging, and laboratory services.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Atlantic Health System utilizes the MyChart patient portal, which is a product of Epic Systems. This indicates that Atlantic Health likely uses Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) vendor. MyChart provides patients with secure online access to their health information. Through the MyChart portal, patients can message their providers, schedule appointments, request prescription refills, view medical records, and pay hospital bills. The portal also offers options for instant activation via text message or standard activation through a third-party verification system. Patients can also use MyChart to access medical records electronically and manage proxy access for family members. Assistance for MyChart is available through a dedicated support center.
