About the Practice

TJH Medical Services, PC is a large multi-specialty physician group network established in 1993, affiliated with Jamaica Hospital Medical Center and Flushing Hospital Medical Center. It is one of New York City's largest physician networks and provides care across numerous specialties including Cardiology, Critical Care, Bariatric and General Surgery, OBGYN, Vascular Surgery, Pulmonary, Gastroenterology, Orthopedic Surgery, Internal Medicine, Family Medicine, and more. [5, 10, 12, 2, 3, 7] The network primarily serves predominately low-income communities in Queens and East New York. [10]
