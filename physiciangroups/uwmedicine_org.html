About the Practice

The University of Washington School of Medicine, part of UW Medicine, is a five-state medical school serving the WWAMI region (Washington, Wyoming, Alaska, Montana, and Idaho). It is nationally recognized for excellence in biomedical education, research, and healthcare, particularly in primary care, family medicine, and rural medicine training. The school advances medical knowledge through scientific research and provides patient care across a wide range of medical specialties through its affiliated hospitals and clinics, including UW Medical Center (Montlake and Northwest), Harborview Medical Center, Seattle Children's Hospital, and others throughout the region. It also offers extensive graduate medical education and PhD programs.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UW Medicine, which includes the University of Washington School of Medicine, utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a widely used online portal that allows patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare, including accessing medical information, scheduling appointments, messaging care teams, and viewing test results. The electronic health record (EHR) system that powers UW Medicine's MyChart patient portal is Epic. UW Medicine explicitly states that they use Epic as their electronic health record system. The relationship between Epic and MyChart is well-established, with MyChart being the patient portal solution developed by Epic Systems.
