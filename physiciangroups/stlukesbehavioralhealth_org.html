About the Practice

St. Luke's Behavioral Health Center is a 127-bed facility located in Phoenix, Arizona, offering a full spectrum of inpatient and outpatient services for seniors, adults, adolescents, and children requiring psychiatric and chemical dependency care. [2, 6] The center specializes in mental and behavioral illness and drug and alcohol addiction treatment. [2, 6, 9] They provide individualized treatment plans developed by a multidisciplinary team of specialists including psychiatrists, therapists, and nurses, utilizing a holistic approach that addresses each patient's physiological, psychological, spiritual, and social needs. [2, 6, 8, 9]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

St. Luke's Behavioral Health Center, which is part of Steward Health Care, utilizes the **StewardCONNECT** patient portal. This patient portal is a unified platform for patients of Steward Health Care hospitals and outpatient practices, designed to provide access to health information, appointment scheduling, and other features. Steward Health Care has been implementing **MEDITECH Expanse** as its electronic health record (EHR) system across its network of hospitals, including those in Arizona. This initiative is part of Steward Health Care's strategy to consolidate its facilities under a single, integrated EHR platform. While some former Steward Medical Group practices may have previously used an AthenaHealth patient portal, the current information indicates that Steward Health Care's unified patient portal is StewardCONNECT, which is likely integrated with their MEDITECH Expanse EHR system.
