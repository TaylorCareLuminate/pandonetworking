About the Practice

NewYork-Presbyterian is a nonprofit academic medical center and one of the nation's most comprehensive, integrated academic healthcare systems, serving as the primary teaching hospital for Weill Cornell Medicine and Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons. [2, 7, 10, 12] It provides high-quality, compassionate care across a vast array of medical and surgical specialties, including top-ranked programs in cardiology and heart surgery, neurology and neurosurgery, cancer care, pediatrics, and many others. [2, 7, 9, 10] The system encompasses multiple hospital campuses, numerous primary and specialty care clinics and medical groups, and offers telemedicine services to provide comprehensive patient care, engage in groundbreaking research, and offer extensive medical education. [3, 7, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital (nyp.org) currently uses a patient portal called "Connect". This patient portal is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system, which NewYork-Presbyterian, in collaboration with Columbia University Irving Medical Center and Weill Cornell Medicine, adopted starting in February 2020. Previously, NewYork-Presbyterian had a patient portal known as "myNYP.org" which utilized Microsoft technologies. Another patient portal, "MyHealth," is mentioned in relation to the New York Presbyterian Medical Group. However, the "Connect" portal tied to the Epic EHR is the system currently in widespread use across NewYork-Presbyterian, Columbia University Irving Medical Center, and Weill Cornell Medicine.
