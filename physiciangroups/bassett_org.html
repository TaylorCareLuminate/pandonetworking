About the Practice

Bassett Healthcare Network is a health care system serving eight counties in Central New York. It includes six hospitals and over 30 regional health centers, providing a comprehensive range of care from primary and preventive services to advanced medical and surgical specialties. The network's hub, Bassett Medical Center in Cooperstown, is a nationally recognized tertiary care hospital and teaching facility offering specialized services like open-heart surgery, cancer treatment, neurosurgery, and trauma care. [4, 11, 12, 14, 16] Bassett Healthcare Network is committed to providing high-quality, accessible care and also engages in teaching and research through affiliations and its research institute. [11, 14]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Bassett Healthcare Network utilizes a patient portal called MyBassett Health Connection. This patient portal is a branded version of Epic's MyChart. Bassett Healthcare Network selected Epic as their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system in 2018. The MyBassett Health Connection portal is seamlessly connected to Bassett Healthcare Network's electronic health record system, allowing patients access to their personal health information, including test results, appointments, and the ability to message their doctor and request prescription renewals. The MyBassett Health Connection login page displays the "MyChart is Epic logo" and indicates that "MyChart® licensed from Epic Systems Corporation". Patients can access MyBassett Health Connection online or through the MyChart app, selecting Bassett Healthcare Network as their provider.
