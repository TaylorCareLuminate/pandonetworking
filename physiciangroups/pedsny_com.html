About the Practice

Pediatric & Adolescent Medicine, LLP, also known as PedsNY, is a pediatric practice established in 1979 that provides comprehensive medical care for newborns, toddlers, children, and teens. They offer a range of services including well child care, sick child care, newborn/infant care, adolescent care, international travel consults, and development and behavioral support. The practice is community-based with strong academic connections, dedicated to personally caring for every patient and family.

Methodology & Findings

Pediatric & Adolescent Medicine, LLP (pedsny.com) utilizes a patient portal that can be accessed online and through the Healow app. The practice's unique code for the Healow app is FJACCA. Healow is a product of eClinicalWorks, which is a widely used electronic health record (EHR) system vendor in the United States. The Healow patient portal is designed to integrate with the eClinicalWorks EHR, allowing patients to access their medical information, schedule appointments, request refills, and communicate securely with their healthcare providers. Based on the use of the Healow portal, it is highly likely that Pediatric & Adolescent Medicine, LLP is using the eClinicalWorks EHR system.
