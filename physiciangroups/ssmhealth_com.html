About the Practice

SSM Health is a Catholic, non-profit health care system operating in Illinois, Missouri, Oklahoma, and Wisconsin. With nearly 39,000 employees and 11,000 providers, they offer a wide range of services including 23 hospitals, pediatric medical centers, outpatient centers, clinics, surgery centers, nursing homes, assisted living, physician offices, emergency centers, rehabilitation facilities, urgent care, home care, and hospice. [3, 6] They provide comprehensive care for families, encompassing primary care, specialty services like obstetrics, gynecology, orthopedics, and physical therapy, specialized rehabilitation for conditions such as stroke, brain/spinal cord injury, and cancer, and care for autism, behavior, and communication disorders, as well as in-home hospital quality care. [8, 12, 5, 6, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

SSM Health utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. This patient portal is provided by Epic Systems Corporation, which is also the vendor for SSM Health's electronic health record (EHR) system. SSM Health has been an Epic customer since 2005 and has undertaken initiatives to consolidate and upgrade its Epic EHR system across its facilities. The MyChart portal offers patients access to their digital health records, the ability to communicate with their care team, request prescription refills, view test results, and manage appointments.
