About the Practice

Guthrie Lourdes Hospital, formerly Our Lady of Lourdes Memorial Hospital, is a 197-bed acute care community hospital in Binghamton, NY, which joined the Guthrie healthcare system in February 2024. [2, 4, 12] It provides comprehensive and personalized care, including a surgery center, cancer center, birthing center, infusion services, laboratory services, imaging and radiology, mental health services, hospice, and home care. [2] The hospital offers a wide range of specialties such as surgical care, joint replacement, orthopedic surgery, comprehensive cancer care (medical oncology, radiation oncology, breast care), diabetes care, 24/7 emergency care (designated Primary Stroke Center), labor and delivery, rehabilitation services, urology, and wound care. [2, 5, 6]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Our Lady of Lourdes Memorial Hospital, now part of the Guthrie healthcare network, utilizes the eGuthrie patient portal. This patient portal is branded as eGuthrie but is powered by Epic MyChart. Guthrie has adopted Epic as their new Electronic Health Record (EHR) system across their network, integrating patient health information into one system. The eGuthrie portal allows patients to access their medical records, contact providers, view test results, schedule appointments, and manage billing online. The former Lourdes Patient Portal was scheduled to be discontinued as of April 30, 2025, with patients encouraged to register for eGuthrie. This aligns Our Lady of Lourdes Memorial Hospital with Guthrie's integrated Epic EHR system and MyChart-based patient portal.
