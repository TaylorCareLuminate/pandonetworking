About the Practice

Baylor Scott & White Health is the largest not-for-profit healthcare system in Texas, formed from the 2013 merger of Scott & White Health and Baylor Healthcare System. [11, 13] It is an integrated care delivery network providing a full continuum of care across over 50 hospitals and more than 800 patient care sites. [11, 13] The system offers a wide range of primary and award-winning specialty care, including Cancer Care, Heart and Vascular Care, Orthopedics, Neuroscience, Emergency Care, Bariatric Surgery, Dermatology, Gastroenterology, and various surgical services, delivered through in-person and virtual touchpoints. [6, 9, 10, 13, 14, 15]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Baylor Scott & White Health utilizes the MyBSWHealth patient portal for its patients. This portal allows patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare, including accessing lab results, scheduling appointments, requesting prescriptions, and viewing medical records. While the patient portal is branded as MyBSWHealth, internal resources at Baylor Scott & White Health list "Epic" under their EHR Applications. This indicates that the MyBSWHealth patient portal is powered by the Epic electronic health record system, similar to how other health systems use Epic's MyChart portal.
