About the Practice

Children's Clinics in Southern Arizona is a comprehensive medical home providing family-centered, outpatient medical and therapy services for children with special healthcare needs, from birth to young adulthood. They offer over 25 medical and dental specialties, primary care, behavioral health, and rehabilitative therapies including physical, occupational, speech, audiology, and nutrition. Services are delivered in a coordinated manner through partnerships with community practices, Banner University Medical Center, and Tucson Medical Center.
