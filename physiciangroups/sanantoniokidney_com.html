About the Practice

San Antonio Kidney Disease Center Physicians Group specializes in nephrology, providing a full spectrum of care for kidney disease and hypertension. Established in 1978, they offer services including hospital treatments, kidney transplant procedures, outpatient dialysis, and vascular access management. They have multiple convenient locations throughout San Antonio and surrounding areas, and also travel to numerous dialysis units. The group is committed to patient care, education, and research to provide advanced treatment.

Methodology & Findings

San Antonio Kidney Disease Center Physicians Group (sanantoniokidney.com) utilizes the FollowMyHealth patient portal. This patient portal allows patients to communicate online with their doctors, view lab results, and manage their health information securely. FollowMyHealth is described as a cloud-based, EHR-agnostic patient engagement platform that can integrate with various Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems. While there are mentions of FollowMyHealth integrating with Allscripts and being referred to as "Allscripts FollowMyHealth" in the context of its capabilities and use with the Allscripts EHR, the search results do not definitively state that San Antonio Kidney Disease Center Physicians Group specifically uses Allscripts or any other particular EHR system in conjunction with their FollowMyHealth portal.
