About the Practice

Millennium Medical Imaging is a physician-directed medical practice providing comprehensive radiology services. Their specialties include General Radiology/Ultrasound, MRI/CT, Pain Management, Women's Imaging, Interventional Radiology, PET Imaging, Vascular Imaging, and Musculoskeletal/Neurological Imaging. They offer accurate, timely, physician-driven services and provide 24/7 hospital-based professional imaging services, utilizing interventional techniques for pain management.
