About the Practice

Orlin & Cohen Orthopedic Group is recognized as Long Island's leading and largest private multispecialty orthopedic practice. They provide comprehensive orthopedic care delivered by board-certified, fellowship-trained subspecialists. Their services cover a broad spectrum of orthopedic needs including sports medicine, foot and ankle, hand and upper extremities, joint replacement, spine, neck and back, neurology, pain management, radiology, orthopedic surgery, orthopedic trauma, diagnostic testing, physical rehabilitation, and arthroscopic surgery. The group also offers in-house advanced diagnostic imaging, neuromuscular testing, pain management, physical rehabilitation, and occupational therapy.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Orlin & Cohen Orthopedic Group, as part of Northwell Health, currently utilizes the **FollowMyHealth** patient portal. This patient portal is provided by a third party and allows patients to access and manage their health information online, including viewing test results, medications, and appointments, as well as messaging their physician. While Northwell Health has announced plans to implement Epic as their new electronic health record (EHR) system with a phased rollout beginning in 2025, and Epic's patient portal is known as MyChart, Orlin & Cohen is currently using FollowMyHealth as their patient portal interface through Northwell Health's existing systems. Prior to the transition to Epic, Northwell Health utilized Allscripts as their EHR system.
