About the Practice

Wickenburg Community Hospital & Clinics is a non-profit Critical Access Hospital and rural health clinic system established in 1926, serving a large rural area in Arizona. The hospital is designated as a Level IV Trauma Center and offers 24/7 Emergency Medicine, inpatient care, surgical services, diagnostic imaging, laboratory, and a wide range of specialty and primary care services. These include General Surgery, Hand Surgery, Podiatric Care, Interventional Pain Management, Urology, Orthopedic & Sports Medicine, as well as visiting specialists in Cardiology, Oncology, and Dermatology. Their mission is to prioritize the patient, family, and community by providing quality health and wellness services to all, regardless of background, and accepting various insurance plans including Medicare and AHCCCS.
