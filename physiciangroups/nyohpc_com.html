About the Practice

New York Oncology Hematology (NYOH) is the Capital Region's largest provider of community-based cancer care and blood disorder services, with a team of over 30 board-certified physicians and over 300 cancer care specialists across multiple state-of-the-art centers. They offer comprehensive, patient-centered treatment including medical oncology, hematology, radiation oncology, neuro-oncology, and gynecologic oncology & surgery. Services provided include chemotherapy, radiation therapy, stem cell transplant, immunotherapy, targeted therapies, and access to clinical trials, focusing on personalized treatment plans and supportive care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

New York Oncology Hematology (NYOH) utilizes **Navigating Care** as their patient portal. This platform serves as a digital tool for patients to access health information, communicate with their care team, and manage appointments. Navigating Care is a patient relationship management (PRM) platform specifically designed for oncology practices. While Navigating Care is built to integrate with various Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems, it has a notable collaboration with McKesson. A 2019 announcement highlighted that Navigating Care's PRM complements McKesson's existing solutions, including the oncology-specific **iKnowMed** EHR, for community-based oncologists. This close relationship suggests that iKnowMed is a likely EHR system used by practices that have implemented the Navigating Care portal, such as New York Oncology Hematology.
