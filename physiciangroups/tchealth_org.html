About the Practice

Tuba City Regional Health Care Corporation (TCRHCC) is a Joint Commission accredited health center operating a hospital, clinics, and mobile clinics to provide safe, accessible, quality, and culturally sensitive healthcare primarily to Native American tribes including the Navajo, Hopi, and Southern Paiute in Northern Arizona. [2, 3, 6, 11] Services offered are comprehensive and include inpatient and outpatient emergency care, dental and ophthalmology services, orthopedics, OB/GYN, oral surgery, eye surgery, urological procedures, various medical specialties, mental health services, and public health programs. [2, 3, 4, 5, 11] Established in 1975 and becoming independent from the Indian Health Service in 2002, TCRHCC serves a large rural area and functions as a regional referral center. [2, 3, 11]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Tuba City Regional Health Care Corporation (tchealth.org) utilizes a patient portal that connects to the Sunrise electronic health record (EHR) system. While the patient portal is branded simply as "Patient Portal" on the Tuba City Regional Health Care website, internal references within the portal's settings point to a "Sunrise connection source". The Sunrise EHR system was originally developed by Allscripts and is now part of Altera Digital Health. This indicates that Tuba City Regional Health Care Corporation is using the Sunrise EHR platform from Altera Digital Health. The Sunrise system is described as a single-platform solution for integrated care, and it includes a patient portal component.
