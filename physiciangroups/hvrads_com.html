About the Practice

Hudson Valley Radiologists, P.C. (HVR) is a medical group practice providing comprehensive diagnostic and interventional radiology services in the Hudson Valley. [6, 9] Their board-certified radiologists offer a wide range of imaging exams including CT scans, Diagnostic X-Rays, Interventional Radiology, MRI, Nuclear Medicine, PET/CT, Ultrasound, and Women's Imaging. [5, 7, 9] HVR is dedicated to delivering professional excellence and a high level of patient care, partnering with local hospitals and referring physicians. [5, 11]
