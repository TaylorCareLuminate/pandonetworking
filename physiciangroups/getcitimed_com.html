About the Practice

CitiMed at getcitimed.com provides comprehensive medical care and support for accident victims in South Florida, serving Miami-Dade and Broward Counties. They offer a multidisciplinary approach with services including Diagnostic Imaging, Rehabilitation Medicine, Neurosurgery, Orthopaedic Care, and Spine Care to help patients recover from injuries sustained in accidents. The facilities are staffed with board-certified physicians and licensed medical staff dedicated to restoring well-being.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

While CitiMed Comprehensive TBI Center in New York, accessible via getcitimed.com, provides a patient portal for its patients, the specific vendor or branding name of this portal is not publicly disclosed in the search results. Similarly, the Electronic Health Record (EHR) system that the patient portal is tied to is not identified in the available information. Searches for "CitiMed patient portal," "getcitimed.com patient portal," "getcitimed.com patient portal vendor," "CitiMed Comprehensive TBI Center patient portal system," and "what patient portal does getcitimed.com use" indicate the presence of a patient portal with a login interface, but do not name the technology provider. It is important to note that a separate "Citimed|CitiLAW Attorney Patient Portal" also exists, but this is specifically for attorneys and distinct from a patient portal for medical record access. Unlike some healthcare providers that use widely recognized patient portals such as MyChart (Epic), Manage My Health, or Athena Health, the branding of the patient portal used by CitiMed Comprehensive TBI Center is not readily apparent from the conducted searches. Therefore, while CitiMed at getcitimed.com does utilize a patient portal, the vendor/branding name and the associated EHR vendor could not be determined from the available public information.
