About the Practice

Stanford Health Care, part of the adult health care delivery system of Stanford Medicine, combines clinical care, research, and education to advance the understanding and practice of medicine. They provide compassionate, coordinated, and personalized care, focusing on precision health, preventing disease before it strikes and treating it decisively when it does. As a Level 1 trauma center, Stanford Health Care offers general acute care services and tertiary medical care, with renowned clinical specialties including organ transplantation, cancer diagnosis and treatment, cardiovascular medicine and surgery, and neurosciences. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Stanford Health Care uses a patient portal branded as "MyHealth". This patient portal is connected to and powered by the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Multiple sources confirm that Stanford Health Care utilizes Epic as their EHR system. The MyHealth portal allows patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare, including scheduling appointments, viewing test results, communicating with their care team, and accessing their health information, with data integrated from the Epic EHR.
