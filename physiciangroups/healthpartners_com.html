About the Practice

HealthPartners and Park Nicollet is an integrated, non-profit healthcare provider and health insurance company based in Bloomington, Minnesota, and the largest care group in Minnesota. [4, 6] They offer a wide range of care, including primary care and expertise in over 50 medical and surgical specialties, treating more than 65,000 conditions. [3, 4, 6] Services include everyday care like routine physicals, urgent care, and women's and kids' health, as well as extensive specialty care such as cardiology, oncology, orthopedics, dermatology, surgery, and more, provided through numerous clinic locations and hospitals across the Twin Cities and Western Wisconsin. [4, 6, 7, 10, 12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

HealthPartners and Park Nicollet utilize a patient portal branded as MyChart. This patient portal is integrated into their online account system, allowing patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare, including viewing health records, messaging their care team, scheduling appointments, and requesting prescription refills. The patient portal MyChart is a product of the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. HealthPartners and Park Nicollet use Epic MyChart, indicating that Epic is the EHR vendor for these healthcare providers.
