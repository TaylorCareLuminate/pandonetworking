About the Practice

Island Musculoskeletal Care (IMC Bone Doc) is a leading orthopedic practice serving the Bronx, Queens, Brooklyn, and Long Island, specializing in injuries and illnesses affecting the bones, muscles, tendons, and ligaments. [4, 6, 7] They provide comprehensive medical care including orthopedic surgery, pain management, and physical therapy, using both surgical and non-surgical techniques. [4, 7] The practice treats a wide range of orthopedic injuries and medical conditions, including those resulting from car accidents and work injuries, and assists with navigating no-fault insurance and workers' compensation. [4, 6, 7] Their team consists of experienced orthopedic surgeons, physiatrists, sports medicine doctors, and physical therapists with over 100 years of combined medical experience. [4, 7] IMC Bone Doc aims to offer convenient care, often providing diagnosis, surgery, and physical therapy at one location. [4]
