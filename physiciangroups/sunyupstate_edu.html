About the Practice

University OB/GYN Associates, affiliated with SUNY Upstate Medical University, provides comprehensive women's healthcare in Syracuse, NY. Their services encompass a wide range of gynecological conditions and obstetric care, including general gynecology and obstetrics, surgical procedures, on-site services, gynecologic oncology, family planning, maternal fetal medicine, lactation consulting, and breast care health. They have multiple locations to serve patients depending on the type of care needed.

Methodology & Findings

University OB/GYN Associates, which is affiliated with SUNY Upstate Medical University, utilizes the MyChart patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal system provided by Epic. Therefore, University OB/GYN Associates uses the MyChart patient portal, which is part of the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system used by SUNY Upstate Medical University.
