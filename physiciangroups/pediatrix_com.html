About the Practice

Pediatrix® Medical Group is a leading provider of specialized health care for women, babies, and children across the United States, partnering with hospitals, health systems, and other facilities as well as operating office-based practices. [2, 3, 5] Since 1979, Pediatrix-affiliated clinicians have delivered compassionate care across various settings, with expertise spanning obstetrics and gynecology, maternal-fetal medicine, neonatology, and over 20 pediatric subspecialties, alongside primary and urgent care clinics. [3, 9] The organization emphasizes high-quality, evidence-based care, investing in research, education, and safety initiatives to improve patient outcomes. [3, 5]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Pediatrix (pediatrix.com) utilizes the **NextGen Patient Portal** as their patient portal, which is part of the **NextGen EHR** system. The patient portal is accessible online and also has a mobile application known as **NextGen Mobile Solutions**. The NextGen Patient Portal allows patients to email their physician, review test results, request prescription refills, ask billing questions, and download their health records. It serves as a secure online tool to simplify communication between patients and their healthcare providers. The integration between the NextGen Patient Portal and the NextGen EHR system allows for features such as importing patient information directly into the EHR, facilitating electronic communication and clinical data exchange. NextGen also offers specific EHR solutions tailored for pediatrics, which include features relevant to the care of children.
