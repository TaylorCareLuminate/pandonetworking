About the Practice

The Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC) is a public medical school and the state's only comprehensive academic health system located in Charleston, South Carolina. Established in 1824, its mission is to preserve and optimize human life in South Carolina through education, research, and patient care. MUSC provides a wide range of specialty care, educates over 3,100 students in six colleges, and trains over 950 residents and fellows across more than 80 residency and fellowship programs in primary care and various specialties and sub-specialties. It is also a center for research and operates a public hospital.
