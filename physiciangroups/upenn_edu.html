About the Practice

Penn Medicine is a leading academic medical center comprising the University of Pennsylvania Health System and the Perelman School of Medicine, the oldest medical school in the United States. [4, 13, 14] Dedicated to medical education, biomedical research, and excellence in patient care and community service, Penn Medicine provides life-saving care and develops the next generation of healthcare. [4, 9, 22] Specialties and care provided include primary care, outpatient surgeries, cancer care, orthopaedics, heart and vascular care, neurology, women's health, rehabilitation, and behavioral health, with a focus on patient safety, access to quality healthcare, and translating discoveries into new treatments. [4, 9, 30]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

University of Pennsylvania (责upoenn.edu), specifically Penn Medicine, utilizes MyChart by myPennMedicine as their patient portal, which is powered by the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Penn Medicine's EHR system is known internally as PennChart. MyChart by myPennMedicine serves as the patient-facing portal that allows patients to access portions of their medical record, schedule appointments, communicate with their care team, and manage their health information online. Student Health and Counseling services at the University of Pennsylvania also transitioned to PennChart, with access for students directed through their MyChart by myPennMedicine accounts. The integration of MyChart by myPennMedicine with the PennChart (Epic) system allows for a unified health record and improved coordination of care for patients. MyChart Bedside is also mentioned as a distinct tool used for engaging inpatients, providing them with access to their hospital stay information and acting as an extension of the EHR.
