About the Practice

Atrium Health, part of Advocate Health, is a nationally recognized leader in shaping health outcomes through innovative research, education, and compassionate patient care, with Wake Forest University School of Medicine as its academic core. [2, 3] They provide a wide range of primary and specialty healthcare services across more than 1400 care locations and 40 hospitals. [3] Key specialties include Primary Care, Behavioral Health, Pediatrics (Levine Children's), Cancer Care (Levine Cancer), Musculoskeletal Institute, Neurosciences Institute, Heart & Vascular (Sanger Heart & Vascular Institute), Women's Care, and Rehabilitation (Carolinas Rehabilitation). [1, 3] They offer care in various settings including hospitals, clinics, urgent cares, and doctor's offices. [12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Atrium Health utilizes the Epic Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. Their patient portal is branded as MyAtriumHealth. MyAtriumHealth serves as the patient portal for Atrium Health, including locations like Atrium Health Floyd and Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist. This portal allows patients to access their health information and manage billing with a single login. The use of Epic as the underlying EHR system by Atrium Health aligns with MyAtriumHealth likely being their customized or branded version of Epic's standard patient portal, commonly known as MyChart.
