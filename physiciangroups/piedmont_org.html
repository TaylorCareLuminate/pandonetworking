About the Practice

Piedmont Healthcare is a not-for-profit community health system based in Georgia, recognized as the largest healthcare system in the state. [3, 4] It is dedicated to providing high-quality, compassionate care across a vast network of over 2,100 locations, including 26 hospitals, urgent care centers, QuickCare locations, and numerous physician practices. [4, 11, 17] Piedmont offers a wide range of medical services and specialties, including primary care, oncology, heart and vascular care, neurosciences, transplant services, maternity care, and various forms of immediate and virtual care. [1, 4] The system focuses on making a positive difference in the lives of patients and communities it serves, covering 85 percent of Georgia's population. [4, 11, 17]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Piedmont Healthcare utilizes Piedmont MyChart as its patient portal, which is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Piedmont MyChart provides patients with secure online access to various aspects of their healthcare. Through the portal, patients can manage appointments, view health summaries, access test results, request prescription renewals, and communicate with their medical care team. The transition to Epic and the implementation of Piedmont MyChart have been highlighted as a way to offer patients more access and control over their healthcare information, consolidating medical history into a single electronic record. MyChart is a widely used patient portal developed by Epic Systems Corporation.
