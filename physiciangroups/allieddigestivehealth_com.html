About the Practice

Allied Digestive Health is an integrated network of gastroenterology care centers in New Jersey and New York, comprising over 200 gastroenterologists, pathologists, anesthesiologists, nurse practitioners, and physician assistants. The group is dedicated to providing compassionate, high-quality, and comprehensive care for gastrointestinal disorders, offering a full spectrum of services including diagnosis, treatment, preventative screening, nutrition counseling, and chronic care management through innovative healthcare delivery models and advanced technology.
