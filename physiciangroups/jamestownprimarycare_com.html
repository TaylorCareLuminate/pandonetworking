About the Practice

Jamestown Primary Care, part of Great Lakes Physician Practice, is a family medicine practice based in Jamestown, NY, offering comprehensive primary care services including preventive care and chronic disease management. The team comprises family medicine physicians and advanced practice providers, providing both in-person and telehealth appointments.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Great Lakes Physician Practice PC (jamestownprimarycare.com) utilizes MyUPMC as their patient portal. This patient portal is associated with UPMC, which is currently transitioning to a unified Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. UPMC is in the process of transferring patient medical records from their previous Oracle Cerner system to a single Epic EHR, with the transition expected to be completed by mid-2026. As part of this transition, UPMC plans to introduce new features to the Epic-based MyUPMC patient portal. Therefore, Great Lakes Physician Practice PC, using the MyUPMC portal provided through UPMC, is connected to the Epic EHR system.
