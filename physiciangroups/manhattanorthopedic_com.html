About the Practice

Manhattan Orthopedics is a world-class comprehensive orthopedic surgery practice. They provide care for a wide range of conditions including knee, hip, neck, shoulder, and back pain, as well as arthritis, sports injuries, scoliosis, spinal deformities, spinal injuries, and herniated discs. The practice offers both surgical (including minimally invasive) and non-surgical treatment options, with highly experienced physicians specializing in various orthopedic subspecialties to provide personalized care.

While "NextGen Patient Portal" was noted as a patient portal vendor in general search results, and another medical group was mentioned as transitioning away from a NextGen portal, there is no direct evidence linking Manhattan Orthopedic & Sports Medicine Group P.C. to NextGen or any other specific comprehensive patient portal or EHR vendor.
