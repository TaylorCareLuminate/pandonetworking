About the Practice

Arizona Internal Medicine (AZIM) is an office-based Internal Medicine practice specializing in the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of adult diseases, with over 15 years of experience in the community. They provide personal care, emphasizing disease prevention, health maintenance, patient education, and treatment of acute and chronic medical conditions in a family-friendly atmosphere.
