About the Practice

General Physician, PC is a large multi-disciplinary medical practice serving Western New York and Northern Pennsylvania. [2, 5] They offer comprehensive, patient-centered care across 18 to 20 different specialties, including primary care, women's health, advanced cardiovascular care, cancer care, and various surgical specialties. [2, 3, 5] Their integrated care model focuses on collaborative and convenient healthcare for their patients. [5, 6]
