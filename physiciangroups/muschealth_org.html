About the Practice

MUSC Health is the clinical enterprise of the Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC), a comprehensive academic health system founded in 1824. [4, 8, 15] It is dedicated to preserving and optimizing human life in South Carolina through education, research, and patient care. [4] MUSC Health is nationally recognized for its innovation, patient and family-centered care, and quality outcomes. [8, 15] The system provides high-quality, comprehensive primary and specialty care through a network including a downtown Charleston campus, regional medical centers, outreach locations, and a robust telehealth network. [8, 9, 15, 16] Services cover a wide range of specialties, and MUSC Health University Medical Center in Charleston has been ranked the No. 1 hospital in South Carolina by U.S. News & World Report for multiple consecutive years. [16, 17]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

MUSC Health utilizes the MyChart patient portal. MyChart is the patient portal developed by Epic, which is the electronic health record (EHR) platform used by MUSC Health. Through the MyChart portal, patients of MUSC Health can access a range of services online, including scheduling medical appointments, viewing health information such as medications, allergies, and test results, requesting medication refills, and messaging their care team. The MyChart app is also available for mobile devices. Recent information from June 2025 further confirms that Epic is MUSC Health's electronic medical record and MyChart is its patient portal, as mentioned in relation to a partnership to expand virtual care services. MUSC Health also has a separate patient portal specifically for MUSC Health - Orangeburg, referred to as "My Patient Record," where patients can access portions of their health record. Additionally, a "MUHA Vendor Portal" exists for vendors, which is distinct from patient portals. However, the primary and widely referenced patient portal for MUSC Health is MyChart.
