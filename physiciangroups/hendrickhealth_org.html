About the Practice

Hendrick Health, a non-profit healthcare system founded in 1924 and headquartered in Abilene, Texas, provides a wide range of comprehensive healthcare services to West Central Texas, serving 24 counties. [2, 4, 10, 18] Their services and specialties include cardiovascular care, neurology and neurosurgery, neonatal and pediatric intensive care, orthopedics, women's health, cancer services, and rehabilitation. [2, 10, 18] The system comprises Hendrick Medical Center, Hendrick Medical Center South, and Hendrick Medical Center Brownwood, along with Hendrick Clinic, a multispecialty medical group, operating with the mission to deliver high quality healthcare emphasizing excellence and compassion consistent with the healing ministry of Jesus Christ. [2, 4, 9, 16, 18]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Hendrick Health utilizes two distinct patient portals, each connected to a different electronic health record (EHR) system depending on the location of care. For services provided through the Hendrick Health System, the patient portal used is **FollowMyHealth**. This portal is integrated with the **Allscripts Sunrise** EHR system, which is the primary EHR platform for the Hendrick Health System. Hendrick Health System has been noted as a client of Allscripts, implementing their Sunrise EHR and the FollowMyHealth application for patient access. For connecting with providers at Hendrick Clinic locations, the patient portal used is **Athenahealth**. Athenahealth is also an EHR vendor, and their patient portal is part of their services offered to clinics. In summary, Hendrick Health's EHR system for its hospital and health system services is **Allscripts Sunrise**, accessed via the **FollowMyHealth** portal, while their clinics use the **Athenahealth** patient portal, which is part of the Athenahealth EHR ecosystem.
