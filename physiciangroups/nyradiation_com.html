Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

University Radiation Oncology Associates (nyradiation.com) utilizes a patient portal provided by MedInformatix Inc.. This patient portal is part of the comprehensive MedInformatix Electronic Health Record (EHR) and Enterprise Practice Management (EPM) solution. The patient portal, referred to as "Patient Portal" within the MedInformatix system, allows patients to access various services. These services typically include requesting appointments, submitting demographic information, reviewing medical records, reports, and billing statements, and exchanging secure messages with staff. MedInformatix is confirmed as an EHR vendor, with their software certified as a Complete EHR. The patient portal is seamlessly integrated with their EHR and practice management modules, facilitating the flow of patient data for administrative and clinical functions.
