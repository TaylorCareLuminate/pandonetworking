About the Practice

Creighton University is a private research university in Omaha, Nebraska, founded in 1878 by the Society of Jesus, rooted in the Jesuit, Catholic tradition. Accredited by the Higher Learning Commission, it offers a wide range of undergraduate, graduate, and professional programs with a focus on academic excellence, service, and educating the whole person. Specialties include Arts and Sciences, Business, Dentistry, Law, Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy and Health Professions. Creighton also operates the Creighton University Medical Center, providing advanced healthcare services and emphasizing compassionate patient care, particularly in areas like trauma, cardiac care, cancer care, specialized surgery, transfusion-free medicine, and high-risk obstetrics.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Creighton University's main patient portal is MyChart, which is the patient portal commonly associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. This is further supported by the mention of "Epic Care Link" in relation to CHI Health, which includes CHI Health Creighton University Medical Center. While the Creighton School of Dentistry uses a different patient portal called PatientAccess by Exan, the broader health services affiliated with Creighton University utilize MyChart.
