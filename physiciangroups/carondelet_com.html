About the Practice

Carondelet Medical Group - Urology provides compassionate and comprehensive care, diagnosing and treating a variety of urological conditions. They offer some of the latest procedures, including minimally invasive treatment options. The specialty focuses on the medical and surgical treatment of conditions related to the male and female urinary tract (kidneys, ureter, bladder, and urethra) and the male reproductive system, including but not limited to prostate cancer, enlarged prostate, kidney stones, impotence/erectile dysfunction, incontinence, and urinary infections. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Carondelet Medical Group refers to their patient portal as "My Health Rec". Based on the search results, "My Health Rec" is a patient portal used by several healthcare providers, including those that were previously part of Tenet and now part of UCI Health community locations. While the searches confirm the name of the patient portal used by Carondelet, they do not explicitly state which EHR system "My Health Rec" is connected to for Carondelet Medical Group-Urology East. One search result mentions that UCI Health uses MyChart for their main medical center and My Health Rec for community locations, implying they might be different systems or instances. Another result mentions contacting "patientportalsupport@syntellis.com" for difficulties with My Health Record patient portal at Memorial Medical Center, suggesting a possible vendor or related service provider in some contexts where My Health Rec is used. However, this is for a different medical center and cannot be definitively tied to Carondelet. To determine the specific EHR vendor for Carondelet Medical Group-Urology East's "My Health Rec" portal, more targeted information is needed.
