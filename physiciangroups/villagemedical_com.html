About the Practice

Village Medical provides high-quality, accessible healthcare services with a coordinated and value-based approach, primarily focusing on primary care. They offer comprehensive services including annual check-ups, lab work, illness and injury treatment, specialist referrals, and medication management, with tailored care for adults over 65 and chronic condition management. Services are delivered in traditional clinic settings, in patients' homes, and through virtual visits, often co-located with Walgreens pharmacies. Specialties and care areas mentioned include internal medicine, cardiovascular medicine, skin cancer, emergency medicine, diabetes care, mental health management, acupuncture, obstetrics & gynecology, family care, pediatrics, sports medicine, and urgent care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Village Medical refers to its patient portal simply as the "Village Medical patient portal," accessible through their website and a dedicated "Village Medical™️ App". While Village Medical's parent company, VillageMD, has developed a proprietary system called "docOS™," which is described as a digital healthcare system designed to enhance patient and clinician engagement and integrate with existing EHRs, recent information suggests that Village Medical utilizes Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) system. An article from 2023 discussing Epic's integration of AI mentioned that Village Medical physicians would need to use Epic's mobile application for the service to function, indicating the use of Epic. Therefore, the patient portal branding is "Village Medical patient portal" and "Village Medical™️ App," and the underlying EHR system appears to be Epic.
