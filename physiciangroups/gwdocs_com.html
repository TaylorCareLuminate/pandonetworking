About the Practice

The GW Medical Faculty Associates (GW MFA) is the largest independent academic physician practice in the Washington, D.C. metro area, providing comprehensive, personalized, innovative, equitable, and high-quality care. With more than 750 providers and 51 clinical specialty areas, GW MFA offers a wide range of medical and surgical services using cutting-edge approaches at multiple locations across Washington, D.C., Maryland, and Virginia. The organization is dedicated to patient care, clinical research, teaching the next generation of physician leaders, and addressing community health needs. [9, 11]

Methodology & Findings

GW Medical Faculty Associates (gwdocs.com) utilizes MyChart as their patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal commonly associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Several sources indicate that GW Medical Faculty Associates uses Epic as their EHR. While an older article from 2007 mentioned the use of Allscripts, more recent information points to Epic being the current EHR system in place at GW Medical Faculty Associates.
