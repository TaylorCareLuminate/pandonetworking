About the Practice

North Scottsdale Pediatrics, part of Arbor Medical Partners, is a leading pediatric medicine practice in Scottsdale, Arizona, established in 1988. They provide comprehensive, quality, and compassionate healthcare services based on evidence-based medicine for patients ranging from infancy to young adulthood, with a strong focus on customer service.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

North Scottsdale Pediatrics, a member of Arbor Medical Partners, utilizes athenahealth for its electronic health record (EHR) system. The patient portal provided by North Scottsdale Pediatrics is branded as the athenahealth patient portal. Athenahealth also offers a mobile application for patient access called athenaPatient. Patients of North Scottsdale Pediatrics can use the athenahealth patient portal for various functions, including requesting refills, referrals, asking providers questions, and scheduling appointments online. This integration of the patient portal with the athenahealth EHR system allows for managing health information and communication with the practice. The practice highlights the convenience and ease of access the athenahealth patient portal provides to their patients.
