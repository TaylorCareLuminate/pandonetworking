About the Practice

ECU Health Medical Center is the flagship teaching hospital for East Carolina University's Brody School of Medicine and the ECU Health system, located in Greenville, North Carolina. It serves as a Level I Trauma Center and a Comprehensive Stroke Center, providing acute, intermediate, rehabilitation, and outpatient health services to a vast region of eastern North Carolina. The medical center includes specialized facilities such as the East Carolina Heart Institute and the James and Connie Maynard Children's Hospital, offering a wide range of primary and specialty care services.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

ECU Health Medical Center utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is described as ECU Health's free online patient portal, providing a secure way for patients to access health records, schedule appointments, communicate with healthcare providers, view test results, and manage their health information. The patient portal MyChart is a product of Epic Systems. While the search results explicitly state that ECU Health uses MyChart, one search result also indicates a connection to Epic by mentioning "CareLink is a web-based portal that connects to Epic, allowing referring providers and their staff to access patient information – like outpatient and inpatient notes, scheduled appointments and medications – and review radiology images and follow the progress of care delivered at ECU Health facilities". Furthermore, ECU Health is listed among the organizations that use the Epic UserWeb, a portal for Epic users. This strongly suggests that Epic is the electronic health record (EHR) system used by ECU Health Medical Center, aligning with MyChart being their patient portal.
