About the Practice

Mercy is a health system founded by the Sisters of Mercy, offering compassionate care and exceptional service through a healing ministry. It includes physician practices, outpatient facilities, urgent care, acute care locations, and hospitals across multiple states including Arkansas, Kansas, Missouri, Oklahoma, Ohio, Kentucky, and Maryland. Mercy is committed to addressing health disparities and utilizing advanced electronic health records to improve care. They provide comprehensive services covering primary care, specialty care, emergency services, and more.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Mercy, as part of its electronic health record system, utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. This patient portal is connected to the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system used by Mercy. MyChart provides patients with secure online access to their health information. Through the MyChart portal and app, patients can manage appointments, view test results, request prescription refills, and access their health records. Mercy has been recognized by Epic for its extensive use of the Epic EHR system. In 2024, Mercy Hospital Southeast transitioned to the Epic system, further integrating MyMercy patient portal access for patients in that area.
