About the Practice

Adelante Healthcare is a private nonprofit Federally Qualified Health Center operating in Central Arizona, primarily Maricopa County. They provide comprehensive primary and multi-specialty healthcare services to diverse communities, focusing on accessible, affordable, and quality care. Services offered include Adult and Family Medicine, Pediatrics, Women's Health (OB/GYN), Dental, Behavioral Health, Lab, Pharmacy, and WIC Nutrition. [2, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Adelante Healthcare utilizes a patient portal simply branded as the "Patient Portal". This portal allows patients to access medical records, view test results and prescriptions, schedule appointments, and communicate with their providers. The underlying electronic health record (EHR) system that the Adelante Healthcare Patient Portal is connected to is NextGen Healthcare. Several sources indicate that Adelante Healthcare uses NextGen as their EHR provider. A case study by Providertech mentions Adelante Healthcare exploring options for add-on applications for their "electronic health record (EHR) provider, NextGen" and later notes Providertech's integration with their "NextGen EHR". An interview with Adelante Healthcare's CIO from 2016 also discusses their transition to "Nextgen". Furthermore, a page regarding online appointment scheduling for Adelante Healthcare by Medfusion explicitly references "NextGen Healthcare" in relation to the patient portal and its services. While the portal is called "Patient Portal" on the Adelante Healthcare website, its functionality and integration point to it being the patient portal solution offered by NextGen Healthcare. InstaMed is also mentioned in connection with an Adelante Healthcare patient portal for bill payment.
