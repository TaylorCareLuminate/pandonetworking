About the Practice

BronxCare Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Health Center is a free-standing ambulatory care facility and service network providing comprehensive primary and ambulatory healthcare services to economically deprived populations in the South and Central Bronx and surrounding communities. [8, 10] They offer a broad range of primary and specialty services including medical, dental, pediatrics, OB/GYN, ophthalmology, optometry, allergy treatment, nephrology, podiatry, gastroenterology, and dermatology, as well as health education and nutrition counseling. [2, 4] The center aims to improve health outcomes and promote wellness, striving to be a premier community health center in the Bronx and New York City. [8, 10]

Methodology & Findings

Portal Our "my BronxCare" Portal is offering patients the opportunity to have confidential access to their health information. Patients can contact their physician, request appointments, review lab results, and request prescription refills. myBronxCare is described as a user-friendly online service that provides patients with safe, secure, and confidential access to their health information. Patients can use it to contact their physician, request and verify appointments, view medical information such as diagnoses and lab results, and review prescriptions and request refills. Patients can register for myBronxCare at mybronxcare.followmyhealth.com. Based on the registration URL, the patient portal vendor/branding used by BronxCare Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Health Center is **myBronxCare**, and it is powered by **FollowMyHealth**. To determine the EHR vendor associated with FollowMyHealth, a search for "FollowMyHealth EHR vendor" was conducted. The search results indicate that FollowMyHealth is a patient engagement platform developed by Allscripts. While FollowMyHealth is a separate platform, it is designed to integrate with various EHR systems, including those from Allscripts and other vendors. Therefore, while the patient portal is FollowMyHealth, the underlying EHR system used by BronxCare would likely be an Allscripts product or another EHR system that integrates with FollowMyHealth.
