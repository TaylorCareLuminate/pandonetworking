About the Practice

Nephrology Associates of Montgomery County is a dedicated nephrology practice with two experienced physicians in Rockville, Maryland, specializing in kidney care. [3, 8] As an internal medicine practice, they focus on the function and diseases of the kidneys, leveraging their expertise to diagnose and manage various forms of kidney disease, from acute to chronic conditions, and are committed to empowering patients through education. [3] They do not perform surgeries. [3]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The refined search using "site:namc-maryland.com patient portal" and "site:namc-maryland.com EHR vendor" did not directly identify a patient portal name or EHR vendor on the namc-maryland.com website itself. The search results still included information about other medical groups and general information about patient portals and EHR vendors. Given the lack of explicit information on their website through these searches, it's likely that the patient portal access is provided through a separate login page or a link that isn't prominently labeled with the vendor name in a way that the search engine easily picked up. Therefore, the next step should be to specifically look for terms like "login," "patient record," or "secure access" on their website, as these might lead to the patient portal login page where the vendor branding might be present.
