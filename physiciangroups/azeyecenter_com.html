About the Practice

Arizona Eye Center, with web domain azeyecenter.com, specializes in comprehensive eye care with a focus on cataract surgery, utilizing Dr. Michael J. Depenbusch's patented low-impact technique. They provide a range of services including routine eye exams, treatment for glaucoma and diabetic eye disease, clear lens extraction, intraocular contact lenses, and laser surgeries.
