About the Practice

SightMD is a comprehensive ophthalmology practice that was founded by combining established practices with the mission to provide state-of-the-art eye care with a personalized approach, prioritizing patients. [1, 2] They offer a full spectrum of medical and surgical eye care services for all ages, including routine eye exams, contact lens fittings, LASIK and laser vision correction, cataract surgery, cornea and external disease treatment, glaucoma management, retinal care, neuro-ophthalmology, pediatric ophthalmology, oculoplastic surgery, and audiology. [3, 5, 6, 10, 12, 14] SightMD is committed to delivering clinically excellent and compassionate care across their locations in New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, and Connecticut. [2, 5, 10, 13]

Methodology & Findings

SightMD utilizes the NextGen Patient Portal for its patients to access health information and communicate with their doctors. This secure online platform allows patients to update their information, view and download medical records, and is accessible 24/7 on internet-connected devices. The Electronic Health Record (EHR) system used by SightMD is myCare iMedicWare EHR. This cloud-based system is designed for ophthalmology practices and handles various functions from practice management to revenue cycle.
