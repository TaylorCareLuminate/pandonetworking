About the Practice

Community Medical Center is a leading healthcare system in Western Montana, offering personalized adult and pediatric treatments and technology. They provide a wide range of medical specialties and certifications, including extensive pediatric services through Community Children's, featuring a Level IIIb NICU. As a teaching hospital, they partner with other organizations for transport and medical education, upholding a longstanding tradition of care since 1922 and recognized for patient safety.
