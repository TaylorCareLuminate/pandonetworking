About the Practice

OCLI Vision is a leading ophthalmology practice with numerous locations throughout the Northeast, including Connecticut, New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, and West Virginia. They offer comprehensive eye care services and specialize in a wide range of treatments, including primary vision care, LASIK, cataract surgery, glaucoma, diabetic eye care, corneal disease, retinal disease, oculoplastics, refractive surgery, and neuro-ophthalmology, utilizing advanced technology and a team of highly skilled ophthalmologists and optometrists.

## OCLI Vision Leverages NextGen Healthcare for Patient Portal and EHR OCLI Vision, a prominent ophthalmology group, appears to utilize **NextGen Healthcare** for its electronic health record (EHR) system and patient portal. While the specific branding of OCLI Vision's patient portal may vary by location (e.g., "OCLI Vision – NJ", "OCLI Vision – NY"), the underlying technology is strongly linked to NextGen. Several factors support this conclusion. Job descriptions for positions at OCLI Vision and its management services partner, Spectrum Vision Partners, explicitly mention experience with or the need to learn **NextGen Healthcare** and **Legacy NextGen EHR** and **NextGen Practice Management System**. Furthermore, Spectrum Vision Partners, which supports OCLI Vision's operations, utilizes a payment system that integrates with **NextGen**. NextGen Healthcare offers a patient portal solution known as the **NextGen® Patient Portal**. This portal, which can be co-branded with a practice's logo, is often referred to by the branding **YourHealthFile** in its standard form. The NextGen Patient Portal provides patients with access to various features, including viewing medical records, secure messaging with providers, appointment scheduling, requesting prescription refills, and paying bills online. Although one search result for a specific OCLI Vision location mentioned "Patient Portal MDI", this reference is likely either a regional variation in naming or related to a specific integration, rather than indicating a different primary patient portal vendor for the entire OCLI Vision network. Another similarly named entity, OCL Vision (oclevision.com), appears to use a different patient portal branded as APPatient™. Based on the available information, OCLI Vision's patient portal is powered by **NextGen Healthcare**, which also serves as their EHR vendor. The patient portal likely operates under a co-branded OCLI Vision name, potentially utilizing the core functionalities of the **YourHealthFile** platform provided by NextGen.
