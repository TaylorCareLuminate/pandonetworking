About the Practice

Centurion Anesthesia, associated with Sedation Vacation Perioperative Medicine Pllc, specializes in providing comprehensive anesthesia services, including general, regional, local, and sedation, to ambulatory surgery centers, office-based surgery locations, hospitals, and dental practices. [5, 6, 7] They serve the Greater New York City area, Long Island, and states across the US, focusing on patient safety, comfort, and pain management during perioperative care. [2, 5, 8] Their team includes board-certified physician anesthesiologists with specialized training in areas like pediatrics, cardiac anesthesia, and pain management. [2, 8]
