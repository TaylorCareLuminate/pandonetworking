About the Practice

St. Peter's Health Partners Medical Associates, P.C. (SPHPMA) is one of the Capital Region's largest multi-specialty physician groups, with more than 950 physicians and advanced practitioners across more than 130 locations. [1, 9] Affiliated with St. Peter's Health Partners, the group offers high-quality, compassionate care and advanced treatment options, providing improved coordination of care among primary care providers, specialists, hospitals, and other health care providers. [1, 11] Headquartered in Albany, NY, the group represents over 20 specialties, including primary care, internal medicine, pediatrics, cardiology, endocrinology, OB/GYN, oncology, neurology, neurosurgery, pulmonary and critical care, urology, and various surgical specialties. [9, 11]

Methodology & Findings

St. Peter's Health Partners Medical Associates uses the Trinity Health MyChart patient portal. This transition to MyChart occurred around May 8, 2021, for new information from doctor's office and hospital visits. For services received after June 10, 2023, a MyChart account is needed for online medical record access, replacing a previous Patient Portal. MyChart is a patient portal product provided by Epic. St. Peter's Health offers MyChart through an Epic Community Connect partnership. Therefore, St. Peter's Health Partners Medical Associates uses Epic as their EHR vendor.
