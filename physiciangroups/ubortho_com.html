About the Practice

UBMD Orthopaedics & Sports Medicine is the largest orthopaedics group in Western New York, offering complete care for all orthopaedic and musculoskeletal conditions for patients of all ages. [4, 10] Their team comprises board-certified, fellowship-trained surgeons and sports medicine specialists, physical therapists, athletic trainers, sports performance coaches, and other providers. [3, 4] They provide comprehensive services including imaging, diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation, and injury prevention, specializing in concussions, sports injuries, broken bones, joint care, muscle damage, and conditions affecting the back, spine, extremities, hip, knee, elbow, shoulder, and neck. [1, 3, 4] Additional services include cartilage restoration, joint reconstruction and replacement, orthopaedic trauma treatment, pediatric orthopaedics, urgent orthopaedic care (UB OrthoCare), orthopaedic oncology, medical massage, and regenerative medicine. [3, 4] UBMD Orthopaedics & Sports Medicine physicians serve as official team doctors for professional and collegiate sports teams, and the group is affiliated with major local hospital systems. [1, 3, 4, 10] Their physicians also hold academic positions at the University at Buffalo, contributing to research and medical education. [1, 2, 12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UBMD Orthopaedics & Sports Medicine (ubortho.com) utilizes a patient portal branded as MyUBMD, which is powered by FollowMyHealth. FollowMyHealth is a patient engagement platform that is now known as Veradigm FollowMyHealth and is closely associated with the EHR vendor Allscripts. The MyUBMD patient portal, supported by FollowMyHealth, allows patients to manage their medical information online. This includes requesting prescription refills, sending messages to physicians, updating medical history, and requesting appointments. FollowMyHealth is designed to integrate with various Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems, and multiple sources indicate a strong connection to Allscripts, referring to the platform as the "Allscripts FollowMyHealth portal". This suggests that UBMD Orthopaedics & Sports Medicine likely uses an Allscripts or Veradigm EHR system, as FollowMyHealth is their patient engagement solution.
