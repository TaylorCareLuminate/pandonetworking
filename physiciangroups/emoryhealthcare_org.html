About the Practice

Emory Healthcare is a premier academic health system in Atlanta, Georgia, and the most extensive physician group practice in the state, with nearly 500 provider locations and 11 hospitals. [4, 12, 15] Affiliated with the Emory University School of Medicine, it integrates education, discovery, and health care to provide outstanding, compassionate, and innovative patient care fueled by research. [4, 12, 15] Emory Healthcare offers a wide range of specialized care, including nationally ranked programs in cancer, cardiology and heart surgery, neurology and neurosurgery, orthopedics, geriatric services, diabetes and endocrinology, transplant care, and primary care services. [4, 8, 12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Emory Healthcare utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Emory Healthcare transitioned to using Epic for its electronic health records on October 1, 2022, and MyChart is included as part of this system. MyChart provides patients with a secure online portal to access their health information, manage appointments, communicate with their care team, and handle billing matters. Prior to this transition, Emory Healthcare used several different patient portals, including Blue, Gold, HealtheLife, Eye Center, and Emory Decatur, all of which were consolidated into the single MyChart system.
