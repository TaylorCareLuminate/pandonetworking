About the Practice

Carondelet Medical Group is a trusted network of primary care and specialty providers serving Tucson and Southern Arizona, part of the Carondelet Health Network. They offer comprehensive, compassionate, and high-quality patient-centered care across more than a dozen clinic locations. Specialties include Cardiology, Orthopedics, Neurology/Neurosurgery, Urology, General Surgery, Bariatric Surgery, Family Medicine, and Pediatrics, focusing on acute, chronic, traumatic, and overuse injuries and various medical conditions.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Carondelet Medical Group utilizes the Athenahealth platform for its patient portal and Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. The patient portal, which is part of the Athenahealth suite of services, provides patients with online access to schedule appointments, request prescription refills, view test results, manage personal health information, and handle billing and payments. The patient portal used by Carondelet Medical Group is branded as an Athenahealth patient portal, with login pages specifically associated with Carondelet Medical Group powered by Athenahealth. Athenahealth is a vendor that offers comprehensive healthcare technology solutions, including EHR, practice management, and patient engagement tools. Carondelet Medical Group has indicated the implementation of a new EHR system and a corresponding user-friendly patient portal. Information suggests that Athenahealth's athenaOne is the integrated EHR system that includes the patient portal functionality used by the medical group. While Carondelet Health Network hospitals use a patient portal called "My Health Rec," the medical group's online presence and login points clearly indicate the use of the Athenahealth patient portal for their clinic operations.
