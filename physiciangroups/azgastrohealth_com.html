About the Practice

Arizona Gastrointestinal Associates PLC specializes in comprehensive gastrointestinal care. They provide expert diagnosis, treatment, and management for a wide range of digestive disorders, utilizing state-of-the-art technology for procedures such as Colonoscopy, Endoscopy, Anorectal Manometry, Capsule Endoscopy, Endoscopic Ultrasonography, Upper Endoscopy, PEG tube placements, hemorrhoidal treatment, and push enteroscopy. The practice focuses on delivering high-quality, patient-centered care for conditions including Irritable bowel syndrome, Acid reflux, Inflammatory bowel disease, Diverticulitis, Hepatitis, Fatty liver disease, Cirrhosis, Celiac disease, and Pancreatitis.
