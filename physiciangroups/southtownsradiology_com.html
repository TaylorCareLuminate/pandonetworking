About the Practice

Southtowns Radiology is a leading medical imaging and diagnostic radiology center serving Western New York, with nationally accredited outpatient imaging centers in Hamburg and Orchard Park. They provide a comprehensive range of services including CT scans, MRI, Ultrasound, Breast Imaging (including 3D mammography and are a Breast Center of Excellence), Bone Density Screening, and X-Ray. The practice is staffed by a team of over 20 board-certified radiologists with various subspecialties, emphasizing compassionate care and utilizing state-of-the-art equipment to provide quick, efficient, and cost-effective diagnostic solutions.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Southtowns Radiology, located in the Buffalo and Western New York area, appears to utilize the MyKaleida patient portal, which is part of the HealtheLife platform, for its patient portal services. This is strongly suggested by Southtowns Radiology's affiliation with Catholic Health and Niagara Falls Memorial Medical Center, both of which are part of the Kaleida Health network that uses the MyKaleida portal. The MyKaleida patient portal, powered by HealtheLife, allows patients to access various health information online, including test results (such as radiology reports), manage appointments, and communicate with their healthcare providers. Multiple sources indicate that the HealtheLife patient portal is commonly associated with the Cerner electronic health record (EHR) system. Therefore, it is likely that Southtowns Radiology, through its connection with the Kaleida Health network and the use of the MyKaleida/HealtheLife portal, is integrated with a Cerner EHR system. While another patient portal and EHR vendor, MEDENT, is used by some other practices in the "Southtowns" area, such as Southtowns Gastroenterology and Southtowns Pediatrics, the direct partnership and location affiliations of Southtowns Radiology with entities within the Kaleida Health network point towards the use of the MyKaleida/HealtheLife (Cerner) system for patient portal functionalities.
