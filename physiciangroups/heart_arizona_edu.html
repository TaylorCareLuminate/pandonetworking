About the Practice

The University of Arizona Sarver Heart Center, founded in 1986, is dedicated to preventing and curing cardiovascular disease through research, education, and patient care. It comprises over 160 physicians and scientists who collaborate to advance cardiovascular medicine and patient care. The center provides a full spectrum of care for various cardiovascular conditions, including advanced heart disease, specializing in areas such as electrophysiology, cardiac transplant, adult congenital cardiology, and structural heart disease. Their services encompass personalized medication management, catheterization procedures, mechanical circulatory support, total artificial heart, and heart transplantation. The center is also recognized for developing and promoting Chest-Compression Only CPR.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Sarver Heart Center, as part of the University of Arizona and in partnership with Banner Health for clinical care, utilizes the Banner Health patient portal. This portal, now referred to as Patient Account and formerly known as the MyBanner patient portal, is the digital interface for patients to access their health information. The electronic health record (EHR) system that underpins the Banner Health patient portal is Cerner. This means that the patient data accessed through the Banner Health Patient Account is managed within the Cerner EHR system used by Banner Health facilities, including those where Sarver Heart Centerülü affiliated physicians provide care. Patients of Sarver Heart Center who receive care through the Banner Health system can access their medical records, schedule appointments, and communicate with their providers via the Banner Health Patient Account, available through the Banner Health website (account.bannerhealth.com) or their mobile app. While the University of Arizona's Campus Health utilizes a patient portal called PatientLink, this appears to be separate and primarily for student health services, not the specialized care provided by Sarver Heart Center through its partnership with Banner Health.
