About the Practice

PIH Health is a nonprofit, regional healthcare network serving approximately 3 million residents in the Los Angeles County, Orange County, and San Gabriel Valley region. [16, 17] The integrated network includes three hospitals (PIH Health Downey Hospital, PIH Health Good Samaritan Hospital, and PIH Health Whittier Hospital), numerous outpatient medical office buildings, a multispecialty medical group, home healthcare services, and hospice care. [16, 17] They provide a wide range of specialties and care, including primary care, urgent care, emergency services, heart and vascular care, cancer, digestive health, orthopedics, women's health, bariatric surgery, gastroenterology, gynecology, allergy care, dermatology, and ear, nose, and throat (ENT) doctors. [16, 17, 8, 7, 13] PIH Health focuses on personalized attention, specialized care using advanced medical technology, and a team approach.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Based on the search results, PIH Health currently utilizes a patient portal powered by **FollowMyHealth**. This portal is described as an all-in-one personal health record that allows patients to access medical information, schedule appointments, request prescription renewals, and communicate with their physician's office. While FollowMyHealth is the current patient portal vendor, there are strong indications that PIH Health is also involved with **Epic**. Several search results mention job postings for "Epic Application Analyst" positions at PIH Health, with a focus on supporting Epic applications, including the **MyChart** patient portal. An event titled "PIH Health: Epic Demo - MyChart Patient Engagement" was also found. This suggests that PIH Health may be implementing or transitioning to Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) system, with MyChart being the associated patient portal, similar to how Epic is tied to MyChart in other healthcare systems. FollowMyHealth is an EHR-agnostic platform that can integrate with various EHR systems. One search result specifically mentions that Allscripts' electronic health record (EHR) offers the ability to conduct telehealth visits through FollowMyHealth. However, the search results do not explicitly state which EHR system is currently integrated with FollowMyHealth at PIH Health. The presence of Epic-related job postings focusing on MyChart at PIH Health points towards a likely future or current use of Epic as their primary EHR.
