About the Practice

Founded in 1892, Deaconess Health System is a premier provider of high-quality, compassionate healthcare serving over 1.5 million people in southwestern Indiana, western Kentucky, and southeastern Illinois. The system includes 20 owned, joint-ventured, sponsored, or affiliated hospital locations and over 150 care locations throughout the Tri-State area. Deaconess offers a wide range of services through its multispecialty Deaconess Clinic, featuring primary care and top specialty doctors, a freestanding Cancer Center, Urgent Care facilities, and specialty hospitals like The Women's Hospital, The Heart Hospital, and The Orthopedic and Neuroscience Hospital. They are committed to progressive, patient-centered care and have been recognized as a leading hospital in the region.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Deaconess Hospital utilizes the MyChart patient portal, which is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. MyChart provides patients with a secure online platform to access various aspects of their medical record, including viewing test results, scheduling appointments, requesting prescription refills, and communicating with their healthcare providers. Deaconess Health System refers to their Epic EHR implementation as "Deaconess CareConnect." Several hospitals affiliated with Deaconess Health System have transitioned to the Epic EHR through the CareConnect program. This aligns with the common practice of healthcare systems using the Epic EHR and offering the MyChart patient portal to their patients.
