About the Practice

Loma Linda University Health (LLUH) is a faith-based academic medical center and nonprofit religious corporation in Loma Linda, California, dedicated to the Seventh-day Adventist mission "to make man whole" physically, intellectually, emotionally, and spiritually through healthcare delivery, education, and research. [3, 13] As the umbrella organization for a university, medical center, six hospitals, and numerous clinics, LLUH serves as the tertiary medical facility and the only Level 1 trauma center for inland Southern California, providing comprehensive primary care and a broad spectrum of subspecialties including cardiology, neurological care, orthopaedics, neonatology, behavioral health, cancer, heart, perinatal service, rehabilitation, and transplants. [3, 10, 11, 12, 13] It treats over 1.5 million outpatients and over 40,000 inpatients annually, operates remote clinics in the western U.S., and has global campuses, furthering its mission worldwide and providing extensive training opportunities for future healthcare professionals. [12, 13, 15, 20]
