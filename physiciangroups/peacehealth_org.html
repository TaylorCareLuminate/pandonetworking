About the Practice

PeaceHealth St. Joseph Medical Center is a hospital operated by PeaceHealth in Bellingham, Washington. [2, 5] It is the sole community hospital in Whatcom County and a Level II trauma center. [5, 7] The medical center provides comprehensive specialty care and centers of excellence in cardiovascular, cancer, spine and joint replacements, as well as general medical care. [2, 5, 6, 7] It was the first hospital founded by the Sisters of St. Joseph of Peace in 1891. [2, 5, 7]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

St. Joseph Medical Center, which is part of PeaceHealth, utilizes a patient portal branded as MyPeaceHealth. This patient portal is also referred to as MyChart. MyPeaceHealth provides patients with secure online access to their medical records, allowing them to manage appointments, view test results, request prescription renewals, and communicate with their care team. MyChart is a widely used patient portal developed by Epic Systems. The association of MyPeaceHealth with MyChart indicates that PeaceHealth likely uses Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) vendor.
