About the Practice

Established in August 2016, Fort Worth Renal Group is a patient-centered nephrology practice dedicated to providing compassionate and competent care to those managing the challenges of chronic kidney disease. [3] They offer comprehensive renal care and are a leading dialysis and kidney care practice in DFW, providing advanced diagnostics, personalized treatment plans, and a holistic approach to kidney health. [10, 9] Specialties include general nephrology, interventional nephrology, and kidney transplantation. [9] They treat conditions such as acute and chronic kidney diseases, glomerulonephritis, hypertension, nephrolithiasis, electrolytes and fluid imbalance, hemodialysis, and peritoneal dialysis. [9] The group believes in a physician-patient partnership and serves communities including Downtown Fort Worth, Keller, and Southwest Fort Worth. [6, 11]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Fort Worth Renal Group (dfwrenal.com) utilizes the MyChart patient portal, which is part of their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system, Acumen 2.0, powered by Epic. Fort Worth Renal Group partnered with Acumen Physician Solutions to implement Acumen 2.0. This system is built on the Epic platform, and its patient portal is branded as MyChart. MyChart provides patients with features such as secure messaging with their healthcare providers, access to test results, online bill payment, and the ability to manage appointments. This integrated approach leverages Epic's robust EHR capabilities, optimized by Acumen for the specific needs of nephrology practices.
