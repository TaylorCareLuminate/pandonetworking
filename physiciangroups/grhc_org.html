About the Practice

Gila River Health Care (GRHC) is a healthcare entity of the Gila River Indian Community and a Tribal-638 organization, dedicated to providing high-quality, culturally sensitive care to the Gila River Indian Community, the Ak-Chin Indian Community, and other federally recognized tribes. [4, 10, 14] GRHC operates Hu Hu Kam Memorial Hospital, offering general medical and surgical inpatient, outpatient, and emergency services, along with several clinics and a skilled nursing facility called The Caring House. [4, 6, 11, 17] They offer comprehensive services across numerous specialties, including obstetrics, gynecology, pediatrics, diabetes, optometry, dental, podiatry, cardiology, orthopedics, surgery, behavioral health, medical imaging, pharmacy, and dialysis. [4, 6, 11, 19] GRHC is certified by The Joint Commission and CMS, demonstrating their commitment to excellence in patient care. [7, 10, 13, 14]

Methodology & Findings

Gila River Health Care (GRHC) uses a patient portal provided by Intelichart. This "New Patient Portal" was launched on September 25, 2023. Based on information available, the Gila River Health Care Patient Portal allows data access through other applications using the NextGen API. This indicates that the underlying Electronic Health Record (EHR) system used by GRHC is likely NextGen.
