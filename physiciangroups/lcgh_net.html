About the Practice

Lewis County General Hospital is a 25-bed Critical Access Hospital and part of the Lewis County Health System, located in Lowville, NY. It operates a 160-bed Residential Health Care Facility and provides a wide range of inpatient and outpatient services. These include primary and acute care, ambulatory surgery, emergency services (Level II Emergency Department), diagnostic and rehabilitative services, hospice, and home health care. The health system also encompasses specialty centers for surgery, orthopedics, and women's health, as well as various health centers and clinics in surrounding areas, offering primary and specialized medical care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Lewis County General Hospital (lcgh.net) appears to use the MEDITECH Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. This is indicated by their use of a patient portal that recommends the "MHealth mobile app" for optimal access. The MHealth application is the mobile interface for MEDITECH's Patient and Consumer Health Portal. MEDITECH is a healthcare software company known for providing EHR systems to healthcare providers. While Lewis County Health System also uses InstaMed for patient bill payment, this is a separate portal specifically for financial transactions and not the primary patient portal for accessing health information.
