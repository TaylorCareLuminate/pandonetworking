Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Valley View Medical Center (valleyviewmedicalcenter.net) utilizes a patient portal branded as "My HealthPoint". The registration process for this portal may involve a system called "YourCareUniverse". Further investigation reveals that YourCareUniverse is a healthcare consumer engagement platform, including a patient portal, developed by a subsidiary of MEDHOST. While YourCareUniverse is designed to integrate with various certified EHR systems, MEDHOST also provides its own Electronic Health Record (EHR) system called Enterprise. MEDHOST actively promotes YourCareUniverse in conjunction with its Enterprise EHR as part of a patient engagement solution. Based on the connection between Valley View Medical Center's patient portal ("My HealthPoint" potentially powered by YourCareUniverse) and YourCareUniverse's relationship with MEDHOST, it is strongly indicated that Valley View Medical Center is using MEDHOST as their EHR vendor. Although the search results do not definitively confirm the specific MEDHOST EHR system in use by Valley View Medical Center, the patient portal technology points towards a MEDHOST affiliation.
