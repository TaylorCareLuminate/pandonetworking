About the Practice

Oswego Health is a non-profit healthcare system based in Oswego County, New York, providing accessible, quality care to residents. The system includes Oswego Hospital, a community hospital offering acute medical, emergency, surgical, maternity, pediatric, and behavioral health services. In addition to the hospital, Oswego Health operates multiple locations and services including urgent care centers, a skilled nursing facility, retirement living options, specialty medical practices (such as cardiology, orthopedics, ENT, gastroenterology, and more), lab and medical imaging sites, physical therapy, and a hospital-based certified home health care agency.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Oswego Health, including Oswego Hospital, uses the "Oswego Health Patient and Consumer Health Portal" for patient access to their health information. For services specifically at Oswego Hospital and its associated specialty departments, the patient portal is branded as "MyHealth". The Electronic Health Record (EHR) system utilized by Oswego Health is MEDITECH Expanse. Evidence suggests integration between third-party services and the MEDITECH EHR at Oswego Health, further supporting its use. In summary, Oswego Hospital (Oswego Health) uses the "Oswego Health Patient and Consumer Health Portal," also referred to as "MyHealth" for hospital and specialty services, which is connected to the MEDITECH EHR system.
