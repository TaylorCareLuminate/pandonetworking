About the Practice

HCA HealthONE is one of the largest and most comprehensive healthcare systems in Metro Denver and the Rocky Mountain region, offering more than 170 care sites. They provide a wide range of accessible, exceptional healthcare services across a cohesive network of hospitals, doctors, and colleagues. Their services cover a full range of service lines and care sites, including complex orthopedics, leading-edge cancer care, specialized pediatrics, advanced neurological care, cardiovascular services, oncology, orthopedics and spine care, robotic surgery, bariatric surgery, labor and delivery, primary care, internal medicine, chronic disease management, immunizations, and women's health services, all committed to high-quality, patient-centered care and the improvement of human life. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

HCA HealthONE, part of the HCA Healthcare system, uses a patient portal called MyHealthONE. This portal is the secure online platform for patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare. HCA Healthcare has a long-standing partnership with MEDITECH and is in the process of implementing MEDITECH Expanse as its primary electronic health record (EHR) platform across its facilities. The MyHealthONE portal serves as the patient interface for the HCA Healthcare network. While a direct statement confirming MyHealthONE is exclusively "powered by" MEDITECH was not found, the widespread implementation of MEDITECH as the go-forward EHR for HCA Healthcare, and MyHealthONE being the unified patient portal for the system, indicates a strong integration between MyHealthONE and the MEDITECH EHR.
