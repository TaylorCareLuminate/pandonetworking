About the Practice

El Rio Health is a community health center dedicated to improving community health through comprehensive, accessible, affordable, quality, and compassionate care. They provide a wide range of services including primary, specialized, and dental care for all community members. Key service areas include Adult Medicine, Pediatrics, OB/GYN, midwifery, family medicine, physical therapy, and behavioral health. Most locations offer integrated services such as pharmacy, radiology, laboratory, and health and wellness programs. El Rio Health serves over 125,000 people in Tucson and operates the largest non-profit dental program in Arizona.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

El Rio Health utilizes the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system, and their patient portal is called El Rio MyChart. MyChart is a patient portal developed by Epic Systems, allowing patients to access their health information, schedule appointments, and communicate with their healthcare providers. According to information from El Rio Health, they transitioned to the Epic system, describing it as a comprehensive electronic medical record used by leading healthcare providers. A report also indicated El Rio's plan for an organization-wide migration to the Epic EHR by November 2021. The El Rio MyChart portal provides patients with a secure way to view their health information, manage appointments, access test results, and request prescription refills.
