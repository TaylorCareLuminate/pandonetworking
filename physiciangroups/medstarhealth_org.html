Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

MedStar Health uses "The MedStar Health Patient Portal" as its patient portal. This portal is integrated with the Cerner Millennium electronic health record (EHR) system, which MedStar Health utilizes. MedStar Health launched a new digital platform for patients that includes access to their medical records through this integration with Cerner's EHR. While the patient portal is branded as "The MedStar Health Patient Portal", the underlying EHR vendor is Cerner.
