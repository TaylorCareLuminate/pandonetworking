About the Practice

Excelsior Orthopaedics is an Orthopaedic Center of Excellence providing world-class, progressive musculoskeletal care in Western New York. [4, 9] They offer comprehensive care delivered by a broad spectrum of specialists including surgeons, sports medicine physicians, athletic trainers, physical therapists, physician assistants, and nurses. [1, 4, 5] Services include orthopaedic urgent care, in-house physical therapy, imaging, sports performance, nutrition counseling, and outpatient surgery, aiming to restore function and enhance quality of life for their patients. [4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Excelsior Orthopaedics utilizes a patient portal simply branded as "Patient Portal". This portal allows existing patients secure communication with their healthcare team, access to patient files, the ability to request prescription refills, and the option to request and cancel appointments. Based on available information, the patient portal is connected to the MEDENT EMR/EHR system. A search result explicitly refers to "medent.excelsiorortho.com" in the context of "Medent Mobile," describing it as a web-based mobile extension of the MEDENT EMR/EHR system. Other results also mention Excelsior Orthopaedics working within an EMR system and participating in Electronic Health Record Technology.
