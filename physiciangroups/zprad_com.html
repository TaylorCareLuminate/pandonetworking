About the Practice

Zwanger-Pesiri Radiology is a radiology group with over 70 years of diagnostic excellence, providing patient-centered care in New York, primarily on Long Island (Nassau and Suffolk counties), Queens, and Brooklyn. [4, 8, 10, 11, 12] They offer a wide range of advanced medical imaging services including MRI (including 3T, 1.5T, and open-sided units, as well as IV sedation and cardiac MRI), CT (including low-dose and PET/CT), Nuclear Medicine, Ultrasound, X-Ray (including dynamic digital radiography), 3D Mammography, and DEXA bone densitometry. [4, 5, 7, 8, 11, 12] The practice employs a team of Board Certified radiologists with specialized training and subspecialties in areas such as Neuroradiology, Musculoskeletal Radiology, Body Imaging, and Breast Imaging, focusing on providing high-quality care and fast turnaround times for results. [3, 4, 7, 10]
