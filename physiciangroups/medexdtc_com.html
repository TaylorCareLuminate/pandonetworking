About the Practice

Medex Diagnostic & Treatment Center is a multispecialty clinic located in the Forest Hills neighborhood of Queens, NY, offering comprehensive care for patients of all ages. They provide 24-hour urgent care, primary care, and access to over 30 board-certified specialists across a wide range of fields including cardiology, dermatology, ENT, gastroenterology, internal medicine, podiatry, psychiatry, urology, allergies, gynecology, ophthalmology, and orthopedic surgery. The center emphasizes providing high-quality, individualized, and preventive care, diagnostic testing, and is an approved location for USCIS civil service medical exams, with various departments working together under one roof for seamless referrals.

Medex Diagnostic & Treatment Center (medexdtc.com) utilizes a Patient Portal for various patient functions, including the ability to schedule and reschedule appointments. In addition to their general Patient Portal, Medex Diagnostic & Treatment Center also uses a platform called ChartRequest for the specific purpose of medical record requests and health information exchange. ChartRequest provides a secure online platform for patients and other requestors to facilitate the exchange of health information. While ChartRequest has features related to patient records, the references on the Medex website to logging into a Patient Portal for scheduling suggest a broader functional portal may also be in use. Based on the available information from the searches, the specific vendor or branding of the primary Patient Portal used by Medex Diagnostic & Treatment Center for scheduling and other typical patient portal features (beyond medical record requests handled by ChartRequest) is not explicitly stated. Therefore, it is not possible to definitively tie their Patient Portal to a specific EHR vendor like Epic's MyChart, as the vendor of their core Patient Portal has not been identified in the search results.
