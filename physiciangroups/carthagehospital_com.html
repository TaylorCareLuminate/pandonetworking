About the Practice

Carthage Area Hospital is a not-for-profit 25-bed Critical Access Hospital established in 1965, serving approximately 83,000 residents in Jefferson, northern Lewis, and southern St. Lawrence Counties, including military personnel and their families at Fort Drum. The hospital is accredited by The Joint Commission and is committed to providing compassionate, personalized, high-quality healthcare. Services include a Level III Emergency Department, OB/GYN, Primary Care, as well as a variety of specialty services such as Neurosurgery, Urology, Orthopedics, Podiatry, Behavioral Health, and School-Based Health Care.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Carthage Area Hospital (carthagehospital.com) utilizes the MyCareCorner patient portal for patient access to their health information. This patient portal is linked to the hospital's Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. Multiple sources indicate a strong association between the MyCareCorner patient portal and the Evident Thrive EHR system developed by CPSI. While the hospital's website confirms the use of MyCareCorner, the connection to a specific EHR vendor is made through the common pairing of MyCareCorner with the Evident Thrive system in healthcare IT contexts.
