About the Practice

Community Care Physicians (CCP) is the largest independent multispecialty medical group in the Capital Region of New York, providing primary care, over 30 medical specialties, urgent care, and wellness services for patients of all ages. [1, 3, 4, 6] It is a physician-led organization founded in 1984, focused on patient-centered care and utilizing a shared electronic health record system across its practices. [1, 7]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Community Care Physicians (communitycare.com) utilizes a patient portal called mycareDOT™. This portal is powered by FollowMyHealth®, a platform associated with the electronic health record (EHR) vendor, Allscripts. The mycareDOT™ portal, as identified on Community Care Physicians' website and related pages, allows patients to manage their health information and communicate securely with their doctors' offices. FollowMyHealth® is explicitly mentioned as the technology powering this portal. Further investigation reveals that FollowMyHealth is a patient engagement platform provided by Allscripts. Multiple sources indicate this connection, with some noting that Allscripts is now known as Veradigm. FollowMyHealth is described as a cloud-based platform that integrates with EHR systems, including those offered by Allscripts, to provide patients with access to their medical information and facilitate communication with healthcare providers. Based on this information, Community Care Physicians is using the mycareDOT™ patient portal, which is branded by Community Care Physicians and powered by FollowMyHealth®, an online platform associated with the EHR vendor Allscripts (now Veradigm).
