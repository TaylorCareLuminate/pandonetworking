About the Practice

Horizon Family Medical Group serves Orange County, New York, with a commitment to providing compassionate, personalized, and high-quality medical care tailored to individual needs. As the fastest growing organization of its kind in the region, they offer comprehensive health services through a patient-centered philosophy, combining proactive disease management with an empathetic care environment. [5, 9] They have over 120 providers covering 14+ specialties, spanning from allergy to women's health. [4, 6] Horizon Family Medical Group is designated as a patient-centered medical home (PCMH), Level 3, emphasizing coordinated and patient-centered care, and they also offer telehealth services. Methodology & Findings

Horizon Medical Group (horizonfamilymedicalgroup.com) utilizes a patient portal that is part of the MedicsPremier system from Advanced Data Systems (ADS). This system integrates the group's various office locations and manages functions including their patient portal. The patient portal offers patients convenient 24-hour access to request appointments, medication refills, and communicate with staff. It also allows patients to view health information such as visit summaries, lab results, and upcoming appointments.
