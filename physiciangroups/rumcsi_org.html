About the Practice

Richmond University Medical Center (RUMC) is an award-winning 470+ bed healthcare facility and teaching institution serving Staten Island and the metropolitan area as a leading provider of healthcare services for acute, chronic, and emergency conditions. [9, 10] It provides a full spectrum of emergent, acute, primary, behavioral health and educational services in an environment that promotes the highest satisfaction among patients, families, physicians and staff. [8] Specialties and care provided include emergency/trauma care (Level I Adult and Level II Pediatric Trauma Center), minimally invasive laparoscopic and robotic surgery, gastroenterology, cardiology, pediatrics, oncology (cancer), neonatal intensive care (Level III NICU), maternal health, behavioral health (including inpatient psychiatric services for adolescents), endocrinology, urology, orthopedics, and primary care services, among many others. [9, 10, 11, 12]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Richmond University Medical Center (RUMC) appears to utilize a dual Electronic Health Record (EHR) system environment, with different systems in place for the main hospital and its affiliated network of clinics. The patient portal used for Richmond University Medical Center is branded as the **RUMC Patient Portal**. Access to this portal is recommended through the **MHealth** mobile app. MHealth is identified as the mobile version of **MEDITECH's** Patient and Consumer Health Portal, indicating that the main hospital likely uses MEDITECH as its EHR vendor. Supporting this, information from RUMC indicates they are transitioning to a fully integrated electronic medical recording system with Meditech in use for various hospital services. For the affiliated Richmond Health Network, an **Athena Patient Portal** is used. This suggests that the clinics within the Richmond Health Network utilize **Athenahealth** as their EHR system. A job posting for a Medical Assistant at Richmond University Medical Center also listed "Athena Health preferred" under computer skills, further supporting the use of Athenahealth in their clinical settings. Therefore, Richmond University Medical Center (rumcsi.org) uses the RUMC Patient Portal, powered by MEDITECH, for its hospital services, while its affiliate, Richmond Health Network, uses the Athena Patient Portal, which is tied to the Athenahealth EHR system.
