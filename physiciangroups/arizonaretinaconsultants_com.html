About the Practice

Retinal Consultants of Arizona is the largest and most experienced retina practice in Arizona, specializing in preserving and restoring sight through world-class retinal care. [2, 9] They offer innovative, compassionate, and comprehensive care focusing on the diagnosis and treatment of diseases affecting the retina, macula, and vitreous, including macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, retinal tears and detachments, and other retinal conditions. [1, 2, 4, 9] The practice is also involved in clinical research and trials to advance treatment options. [1, 2, 9]
