About the Practice

Benson Hospital is a Critical Access Hospital serving the San Pedro Valley community since 1970. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8] It provides a full spectrum of healthcare services including 24/7 emergency care, inpatient and skilled nursing services, outpatient services, primary care, and specialty clinics. [2, 3, 7, 8, 10] Specialties include emergency care, acute care, skilled nursing, primary care, and various specialty services such as cardiology, podiatry, mammography, rehabilitation, pulmonology, neurology, surgical services, laboratory, and radiology. [2, 3, 7, 8, 10] Benson Hospital is affiliated with TMC Health. [2, 3, 4, 5, 7]
