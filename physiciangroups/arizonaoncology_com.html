About the Practice

Arizona Oncology is the largest cancer care provider in Arizona and an affiliate of The US Oncology Network, delivering world-class, compassionate, and evidence-based cancer care and breakthrough research close to where patients live and work. [1, 3] They specialize in Medical Oncology, Radiation Oncology, Breast Surgical Oncology, Hematology, Urology, Gynecologic Oncology, and Stem Cell Transplant. [1, 2, 3, 13] The group offers comprehensive patient care, advanced technology, access to clinical trials, and utilizes an integrated care team approach for individualized treatment. [1, 2, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Arizona Oncology, part of the US Oncology Network, appears to utilize a variety of patient portals, with a prominent one being My Care Plus, which is associated with the iKnowMed EHR system used by the US Oncology Network. However, Arizona Oncology explicitly states that each physician uses a different portal, which means patients might not be able to access all their records in a single location. The My Care Plus portal, tied to the iKnowMed EHR, offers patients secure online access to their clinical information, including their personal health record. In addition to My Care Plus, Arizona Oncology had plans to deploy the Navigating Cancer platform to streamline patient relationship management across its practices. While not solely a patient portal in the traditional sense, this platform aims to enhance communication and patient engagement. Therefore, while My Care Plus seems to be a significant patient portal in use due to the affiliation with the US Oncology Network and its iKnowMed EHR, the specific patient portal a patient will use at Arizona Oncology can vary depending on their individual physician. The Navigating Cancer platform may also play a role in patient communication and engagement efforts.
