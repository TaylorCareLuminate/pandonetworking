About the Practice

BronxCare Health System is the largest voluntary, not-for-profit health and teaching hospital system in the South and Central Bronx, with 859 beds and 4500 employees. It provides a wide range of accessible services including comprehensive psychiatric and chemical dependency programs, long-term care, and extensive outpatient services. Key specialties and care areas include emergency medicine, comprehensive cancer care, neurosurgery, primary care, various surgical disciplines, orthopaedics, ophthalmology, family medicine, pediatrics, dentistry, pharmacy, women's health, numerous medical specialties, cardiology, and stroke care. As a teaching hospital, BronxCare is affiliated with the Mount Sinai School of Medicine and is accredited by the Joint Commission, committed to delivering high quality, comprehensive, accessible, compassionate, and equitable care with a focus on patient safety and satisfaction.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

BronxCare Health System utilizes the "myBronxCare" patient portal, which is provided by FollowMyHealth, now known as Veradigm FollowMyHealth. This patient engagement platform is integrated with BronxCare's electronic health record (EHR) system. According to Allscripts' Q2 2021 earnings calls, BronxCare Health System extended their agreement with Allscripts and selected FollowMyHealth for mobile patient engagement as part of their transition to an integrated electronic health record system using the Allscripts Sunrise platform. This indicates that BronxCare Health System is using the Allscripts Sunrise EHR system, with FollowMyHealth serving as the patient portal.
