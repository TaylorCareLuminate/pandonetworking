About the Practice

Albert Einstein College of Medicine is a private, research-intensive medical school located in the Bronx, New York City. Founded in 1953, it operates as an independent degree-granting institution within the Montefiore Einstein Health System. Einstein offers MD, PhD, and master's programs, emphasizing the integration of scientific research with patient-centered clinical care and education. The college is home to several NIH-designated research centers focusing on areas such as aging, cancer, diabetes, and AIDS, contributing to significant medical advancements. Through its affiliation with Montefiore Health System, students receive clinical training across a wide range of specialties and settings, with a strong commitment to addressing healthcare needs and promoting social justice in its community.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Albert Einstein College of Medicine (einsteinmed.edu), as the university hospital system for Montefiore Health System, utilizes the MyChart patient portal. MyChart is a widely used patient portal developed and licensed by Epic Systems Corporation. While Albert Einstein College of Medicine also has an affiliation with Jefferson Health in the Greater Philadelphia region, where the MyEinsteinHealth patient portal powered by Cerner is used for services in that area, the "einsteinmed.edu" domain is primarily associated with the New York-based Montefiore Health System. Studies conducted at Albert Einstein College of Medicine and Montefiore Medical Center have also indicated the use of the Epic EHR system. Therefore, patients associated with Albert Einstein College of Medicine through its main affiliation with Montefiore Health System will access their health information through the MyChart portal, which is connected to the Epic EHR system.
