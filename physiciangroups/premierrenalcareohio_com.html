About the Practice

Premier Renal Care Associates, operating under premierrenalcareohio.com, specializes in Nephrology, providing comprehensive diagnosis and treatment for kidney disorders and hypertension. Their services include chronic kidney disease management, various dialysis options (hemodialysis and home-based therapies), kidney stone management, fluid and electrolyte management, pre-transplant evaluations, transplant follow-up care, and preventive health services. They are committed to delivering personalized, educational, and preventative care to achieve long-term health solutions for their patients.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Based on the information gathered, it appears there are two distinct entities referenced in your query: RenalCare Associates, S.C. (located in Illinois) and Premier Renal Care (located in Ohio, operating via premierrenalcareohio.com). The patient portal and EHR systems used by each are different. For **Premier Renal Care (premierrenalcareohio.com)** in Ohio: Premier Renal Care utilizes a patient portal that is referred to on their website as "Patients Portal" or "Premier's Patient Portal". This portal allows patients to request appointments, refill prescriptions, view health information, and pay bills online. While the specific vendor or branding of this portal beyond "Patient Portal" is not explicitly mentioned in the search results, an employment opportunity listing for Premier Renal Care indicates that experience with eClinicalWorks EMR software is a plus, suggesting this may be their underlying EHR system. Premier Renal Care was previously a division of Unity Health Network but separated in September 2022. For **RenalCare Associates, S.C.** (located in Illinois), which seems to be a separate entity from Premier Renal Care: RenalCare Associates, S.C. uses the **Epic MyChart** patient portal. Their electronic health record (EHR) system is **Acumen 2.0**, which is powered by Epic. This integration allows for features such as accessing lab results, health records, and communicating with their care team through the MyChart portal.
