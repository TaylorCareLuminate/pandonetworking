About the Practice

BayCare is a leading, not-for-profit, community-based academic health care system in West Central Florida, connecting individuals and families to a wide range of services at hundreds of locations, including 16 hospitals. [9, 11] It offers comprehensive care with numerous specialties including cardiac, behavioral health, pediatrics, primary care, bariatrics, cancer, dermatology, diabetes, gastroenterology, heart and vascular, neurosciences, orthopedics, and many more. [1, 10] Services range from emergency surgery and prenatal counseling to outpatient imaging, laboratory services, urgent care, and home health care, aiming to provide high-quality, compassionate care for diverse health needs throughout a lifetime. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

BayCare utilizes a patient portal branded as myBayCare. This portal provides patients with online access to their medical records, allowing them to view test results, request prescription refills, and communicate with their healthcare providers. BayCare's electronic health record (EHR) system is primarily from Cerner, which is now part of Oracle Health. Information from 2011 indicated a partnership with eClinicalWorks for ambulatory practices, with an interface to the inpatient Cerner system. More recent results from 2018, 2019, 2021, and 2023 consistently mention the use of Cerner and Oracle Health for their EHR and related solutions.
