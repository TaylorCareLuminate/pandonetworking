About the Practice

Onvida Health, formerly Yuma Regional Medical Center, is a not-for-profit healthcare organization serving the residents and visitors of Yuma and surrounding areas with a patient- and family-centered approach. [3, 10] They offer nationally recognized medical care and treatment options across a range of specialties, including primary care, pediatrics, cancer care, heart care, orthopedics, behavioral health, endocrinology and diabetes care, emergency services, and rehabilitative services. [2, 3, 6, 8, 9, 11, 15] Committed to high-quality and compassionate care, Onvida Health utilizes the latest in medical technology to provide comprehensive services close to home. [3]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Onvida Health utilizes the MyCare Patient Portal as their online platform for patients to access medical records, manage appointments, and handle billing. While the name "MyCare Patient Portal" is used by other healthcare organizations with different Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems, evidence suggests that Onvida Health's patient portal is connected to the Epic EHR system. Reports indicate that Onvida Health clinicians are integrating AI applications from Ambience Healthcare directly within Epic's EHR. Furthermore, a job posting for an "Epic Analyst" at Onvida Health explicitly requires Epic certification, strongly indicating the use of Epic as their EHR system. Therefore, the MyCare Patient Portal is the patient-facing interface for Onvida Health's Epic EHR system, similar to how Epic's patient portal is commonly branded as MyChart by many healthcare providers.
