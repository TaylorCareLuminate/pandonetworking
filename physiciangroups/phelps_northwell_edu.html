About the Practice

Phelps Hospital, a member of Northwell Health, is a full-service acute care community hospital located in Sleepy Hollow, NY. It provides inpatient and outpatient services with 218 beds and offers highly specialized care in over 65 medical specialties, including recognized excellence in cancer care, stroke care, surgical care, gastroenterology, hyperbaric medicine, cardiovascular diagnostics, cardiac rehabilitation, and behavioral and mental health. The hospital serves the residents of Westchester, Rockland, and Putnam Counties.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Phelps Hospital, part of Northwell Health, currently utilizes the FollowMyHealth patient portal. This platform allows patients to manage their personal health records online, access test results, medications, and communicate with their physicians. Northwell Health is in the process of transitioning to Epic as its electronic health record (EHR) system, with implementation phasing in starting in 2025. Epic is a widely used EHR vendor known for its integrated solutions and patient portal, MyChart. While the search results confirm Northwell's move to Epic, they do not explicitly state that MyChart will be the patient portal used by Northwell Health, including Phelps Hospital, after the Epic transition is complete. However, MyChart is the standard patient portal connected with the Epic EHR system.
