About the Practice

The Renaissance School of Medicine at Stony Brook University is a public medical school founded in 1971, part of the Stony Brook Medicine healthcare system. Its mission encompasses training future physicians and investigators, advancing the understanding of health and disease through research, and providing world-class, compassionate healthcare. [10, 14] The school includes basic science and clinical departments and offers graduate, post-graduate, and continuing education programs, with a broad range of specialties available through its residency and fellowship programs. [10, 12, 14] Patient care is delivered through its primary training site, Stony Brook University Hospital, and other affiliated hospitals and numerous outpatient locations. [10, 13, 14]
