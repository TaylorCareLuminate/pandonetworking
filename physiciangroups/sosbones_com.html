About the Practice

Syracuse Orthopedic Specialists (SOS) provides comprehensive, total orthopedic care for a wide range of joint and muscle injuries, chronic musculoskeletal conditions, and pain management. [3, 5, 13] Their team of fellowship-trained, board-certified physicians and support staff offer expertise across numerous specialties including Foot & Ankle, General Orthopedics & Trauma, Hand & Wrist, Hip, Joint Replacement, Knee, Neck & Back, Podiatry, Robotics, Shoulder, and Sports Medicine. [5, 12] SOS is committed to providing individualized, high-quality care from diagnosis and treatment through recovery and injury prevention, utilizing advanced technologies and procedures. [3, 14]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Syracuse Orthopedic Specialists (SOS) utilizes the FollowMyHealth patient portal, which is branded as the "SOS Secure Patient Portal". FollowMyHealth is a patient engagement platform known to integrate with various Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems. While FollowMyHealth can connect with different EHRs, information indicates a strong relationship between Syracuse Orthopedic Specialists and Veradigm. SOS is currently using several Veradigm solutions, including Veradigm Practice Management, Veradigm Revenue Analytics, and Veradigm Payerpath. Veradigm's services are designed to integrate with a healthcare practice's EHR system. Given that FollowMyHealth is also referred to as "Veradigm FollowMyHealth" and integrates with EHRs, the underlying EHR system used by Syracuse Orthopedic Specialists is likely a Veradigm product.
