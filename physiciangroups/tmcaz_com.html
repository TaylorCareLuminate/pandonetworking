About the Practice

Tucson Medical Center (TMC) is a locally governed, nonprofit health system based in Southern Arizona, serving the community since 1944. [2] TMC is a leading provider of emergency care and pediatric care, including intensive care units for adults, children, and newborns. [2] The system offers comprehensive programs and services across numerous medical and surgical specialties and sub-specialties, such as cardiovascular, neuroscience, orthopedics, pain management, gastroenterology, cancer care, and specialized care for women and seniors. [2, 12] TMC provides a full spectrum of care including preventative, ongoing, urgent, and emergency services, and operates TMCOne locations for primary and specialty care throughout Tucson and Southern Arizona. [5, 10, 11]

Methodology & Findings

Tucson Medical Center (tmcaz.com) uses MyChart as its patient portal. This patient portal is tied to the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. MyChart allows patients to manage various aspects of their healthcare online, including communicating with their doctor, accessing test results, requesting prescription refills, completing eCheck-in for appointments, and scheduling or viewing appointments. You can access the MyChart portal through the tmcaz.com website. TMC Health, which includes Tucson Medical Center and TMCOne, utilizes MyChart for their network.
