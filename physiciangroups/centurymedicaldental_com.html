About the Practice

Century Medical & Dental Center is a multi-specialty medical and dental clinic with multiple locations in NYC (Manhattan, Brooklyn, and Bronx) providing high-quality, individualized, comprehensive acute and chronic care under one roof. They offer a wide range of medical and dental specialties including Primary Care, Internal Medicine, Family Practice, Pediatrics, Gynecology, Cardiology, Dermatology, Dentistry, and many others, with a focus on providing care for the whole family. Their staff consists of board-certified physicians and experienced practitioners utilizing state-of-the-art equipment. [1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 12, 13]
