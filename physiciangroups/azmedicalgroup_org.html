About the Practice

District Medical Group (DMG), also known as AZ Medical Group, offers comprehensive pediatric surgical care for children ranging from infants to young adults. Their team addresses a wide array of surgical needs for congenital and acquired conditions and diseases. Services include diagnostic procedures, operative care utilizing both traditional and minimally invasive techniques, and postoperative management for a broad spectrum of surgical problems.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Pediatric Surgeons of Phoenix, which is part of District Medical Group (DMG), utilizes a patient portal branded as the "DMG Patient Portal". District Medical Group transitioned to the Cerner electronic medical record (EMR) system across all of their corporate clinics, including DMG Children's Rehabilitative Services, in 2021. This indicates that the "DMG Patient Portal" is powered by the Cerner EHR system. While an older result from 2020 mentioned "new forms within the Athena EHR" at DMG IT, the more recent information confirms the transition and consolidation to Cerner for their clinic locations.
