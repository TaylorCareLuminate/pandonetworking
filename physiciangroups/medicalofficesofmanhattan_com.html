About the Practice

Medical Offices of Manhattan is a multi-specialty medical practice in NYC providing comprehensive primary care and a range of specialized services including Gastroenterology, Nutrition, Psychiatry, Cardiology, Dermatology, Podiatry, and Endocrinology. They offer same-day appointments and welcome walk-ins to address urgent healthcare needs promptly. The practice focuses on providing invaluable diagnostic and treatment recommendations with a mission centered on early detection and prevention to ensure patients receive the right care.

Methodology & Findings

Medical Offices of Manhattan utilizes several patient portal and application platforms, including a dedicated lab portal, a general patient portal, and a mobile app. The specific vendors and their likely associated EHR system are as follows: For accessing lab and imaging reports, Medical Offices of Manhattan uses a "Patient Lab Portal powered by LabFinder, LLC". LabFinder is a platform that facilitates the connection between patients, physicians, and diagnostic centers and can integrate with various EHR systems. Medical Offices of Manhattan also provides a general "Portal Login" for functionalities such as requesting appointments, updating demographic information, viewing statements, and making payments. The vendor for this specific web-based portal is not explicitly named on their website in the search results. Additionally, Medical Offices of Manhattan offers a mobile application called "MOM Health App". This app is developed by Medical Office Technologies, Inc. Medical Office Technologies, Inc. also offers a patient portal called "ezAccess Patient Portal". The ezAccess portal is designed to work with and relies on athenahealth's products, specifically mentioning integration with "athenaPractice (athenaFlow)" and "athenaOne" for data exchange and enhanced workflows. This suggests that Medical Offices of Manhattan likely uses an athenahealth EHR/PM system, and the MOM Health App and the general patient portal functionalities (other than the LabFinder-powered lab results) are provided through Medical Office Technologies, Inc.'s ezAccess portal, which integrates with their athenahealth system.
