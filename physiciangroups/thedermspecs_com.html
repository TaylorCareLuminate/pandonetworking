About the Practice

The Dermatology Specialists is a full-service dermatology practice with over 50 locations, offering comprehensive medical, surgical, and cosmetic care for skin, hair, and nails. They treat a wide range of conditions including acne, eczema, psoriasis, vitiligo, hair loss, and perform skin cancer screenings. The practice also provides cosmetic services such as Botox, fillers, lasers, chemical peels, microneedling, and medical facials, with a mission to provide high-quality dermatology to everyone.

Methodology & Findings

The Dermatology Specialists (thedermspecs.com) appears to use eClinicalWorks as their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. They also partner with NexHealth for their digital patient experience, which likely includes their patient portal interface and functionalities like online scheduling, reminders, and forms. While the patient portal may be branded or accessed through NexHealth's platform, the underlying EHR system managing patient records and clinical data is likely eClinicalWorks. This is similar to how Epic is the EHR system, and MyChart is the patient portal interface provided by Epic. In this case, eClinicalWorks is the likely EHR, and NexHealth appears to be the platform providing the patient portal experience and other digital patient engagement tools.
