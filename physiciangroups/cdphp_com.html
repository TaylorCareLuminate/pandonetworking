About the Practice

Capital District Physicians' Health Plan (CDPHP) is a physician-founded, member-focused, and community-based not-for-profit health plan established in 1984. [4, 10, 13] Its mission is to provide quality health care at a reasonable cost for subscribers and operate as a model for the delivery, financing, and administration of health care services. [5, 6, 7] CDPHP offers high-quality, affordable health insurance plans, including commercial, self-funded, Medicare, Medicaid, Child Health Plus, and Essential Plan products, serving members in 29 counties throughout New York with a network of over 10,000 providers. [7, 10, 13, 19] The organization is recognized for its superior customer service, community engagement, and commitment to improving the health care experience and quality of care. [4, 13]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Capital District Physician Health Plan (CDPHP) primarily offers a member account portal for its members to manage health plan benefits, view claims, track spending, and access wellness resources. This member portal is distinct from a patient portal used for accessing medical records and communicating directly with healthcare providers. However, several physician practices affiliated with CDPHP, such as Community Care Physicians (CCP) and Capital Region Family Medicine, utilize a patient portal branded as mycareDOT™. This mycareDOT™ portal is powered by FollowMyHealth®, a patient engagement platform. Through mycareDOT™, patients of these practices can access their medical records, view test results, request appointments and prescription refills, and message their doctors. FollowMyHealth is designed to integrate with various Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems. While Community Care Physicians states they use an integrated EHR system linked to mycareDOT™, the specific EHR vendor used by all CDPHP-affiliated practices utilizing FollowMyHealth is not uniformly identified in the search results. Some information suggests integration capabilities with Allscripts, as FollowMyHealth is also known as Veradigm FollowMyHealth (Veradigm is part of Allscripts). An older article from 2010 mentioned Community Care Physicians integrating their mycareDOT™ portal (then developed by RelayHealth, which was later acquired by Allscripts) with their EHR system at the time. CDPHP also facilitates members' access to their clinical and claim information through third-party applications via a collaboration with 1upHealth, which connects to the CDPHP member account. This allows members to access and share their health data with chosen apps. In the broader Capital Region, other health systems like Albany Med Health System use Epic as their EHR and MyChart as their patient portal. This highlights that different healthcare providers and systems in the area may use different EHR and patient portal solutions. In summary, while CDPHP provides a member portal for benefits management, patients receiving care from affiliated practices like Community Care Physicians and Capital Region Family Medicine will likely use the mycareDOT™ patient portal, powered by FollowMyHealth, to access their medical records and interact with their providers. This patient portal integrates with the respective EHR systems used by those practices, which may include or integrate with Allscripts solutions.
