About the Practice

Corewell Health is a not-for-profit integrated health system in Michigan providing healthcare and coverage, formed by the integration of Beaumont Health and Spectrum Health. [2, 4, 5, 11] It operates 21 hospitals, over 300 outpatient locations, and post-acute facilities, serving millions of Michigan residents. [2, 4, 5] The system offers a wide range of medical, surgical, and specialty care services, including cardiovascular, pulmonary, neurology, infectious disease, rehabilitation, home care, geriatrics, long-term care, palliative care, hospice, trauma, burn care, cancer, obstetrics and gynecology, orthopedics, and neurosciences. [3, 7, 8, 12, 13, 14] Corewell Health also includes Priority Health, a provider-sponsored health plan. [2, 4, 5]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Corewell Health utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is provided by Epic Systems Corporation. This indicates that Corewell Health uses Epic as its electronic health record (EHR) vendor. MyChart offers patients secure online and mobile access to their Corewell Health electronic health record, allowing them to manage appointments, view test results, request prescription renewals, and communicate with their healthcare team. Since early 2024, Corewell Health consolidated patient accounts into a new Corewell Health MyChart experience. The MyChart platform is designed to enhance patient engagement and care management.
