About the Practice

San Carlos Apache Healthcare Corporation (SCAHC), also known as Izeeˊ Baa Gowah, is a tribal corporation serving the San Carlos Apache Tribe in Southeastern Arizona. It provides comprehensive healthcare services across a five-building campus, including primary care, specialty clinics, emergency services, inpatient care, dental services, optometry, pharmacy, podiatry/wound care, public health, and behavioral health. SCAHC offers preventative care, testing, treatment, and support services, aiming to heal, serve, and empower the Apache people while honoring their traditions through compassionate, patient-centered care.
