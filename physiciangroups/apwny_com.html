About the Practice

Associated Physicians of Western New York, P.C. (APWNY) is a primary care team providing patient-centered, timely, advanced quality care. They operate as a Patient Centered Medical Home with NCQA accreditation, offering Internal Medicine and Family Practice specialties. Their providers also attend hospital patients at Sisters of Charity Hospital.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Associated Physicians Of Western New York PC (APWNY) appears to utilize a patient portal branded as **Healow**, which is commonly associated with the **eClinicalWorks** Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. While the APWNY website refers generally to a "Patient Portal" and provides tutorials for it, it specifically mentions using the **Healow App** for telehealth visits. This aligns with information from other sources indicating that Healow is a patient engagement platform developed by eClinicalWorks and serves as their patient portal solution, offering features such as accessing health records, scheduling appointments, and secure messaging. Based on the promotion of the Healow App by APWNY and the strong integration between Healow and the eClinicalWorks EHR, it is highly likely that APWNY is using the Healow patient portal which is powered by the eClinicalWorks platform.
