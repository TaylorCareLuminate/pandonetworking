About the Practice

Penn Medicine is one of the world's leading academic medical centers, dedicated to the related missions of medical education, biomedical research, excellence in patient care, and community service. [3] It consists of the University of Pennsylvania Health System and Penn's Raymond and Ruth Perelman School of Medicine. [3] Known for pioneering discoveries like CAR T cell therapy and mRNA technology, Penn Medicine offers a wide range of specialized health care services including cancer care, orthopaedics, heart and vascular care, primary care, neurology, and women's health, combining advanced science with personalized, patient-centered care across its hospitals and numerous outpatient locations. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Penn Medicine utilizes a patient portal branded as "myPennMedicine," which is a customized version of MyChart. This patient portal is tied to the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. MyPennMedicine, powered by Epic's MyChart, provides patients with secure online access to manage various aspects of their healthcare. Through the portal, patients can access their medical records, view test results, request prescription renewals, schedule appointments, communicate with their care team, and pay bills online. Some search results also mention "PennChart," which appears to be related to the Epic system used internally by Penn Medicine, and accessing it directs users to MyChart through myPennMedicine for patient-facing functions.
