About the Practice

St. John's Medical Group is the physician network of St. John's Riverside Hospital, serving Yonkers and Southern Westchester with locations also in Dobbs Ferry and Hartsdale. [1, 3] They offer patient-centered, comprehensive care across a wide variety of specialties, including Endocrinology, Family Medicine, Gastroenterology, Geriatrics, Internal Medicine, Interventional Pain Management, Medical Weight Loss Management, Nephrology, Orthopedic Surgery, Orthopedics, Outpatient Dialysis, Plastic Surgery, Podiatry, Vascular, and Wound Healing. [2, 3] Their physicians provide non-rushed appointments and connect patients to leading specialists, focusing on delivering quality healthcare in a compassionate and efficient environment. [1, 2, 3]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

St. John's Medical Group (stjohnsmedgroup.com), which is affiliated with St. John's Riverside Hospital, uses a patient portal called MyStJohnsHealth. This portal provides patients with secure online access to their health information, including laboratory and radiology results, medications, allergies, and visit history. The information available through the MyStJohnsHealth portal is drawn from St. John's Riverside Hospital's Electronic Health Record (EHR). While the specific vendor of the MyStJohnsHealth portal is not explicitly named, a job posting for an EHR System Analyst at St. John's Medical Group mentions collaborating with the "Medgen Support Team" to troubleshoot issues and support the EHR system. This indicates that the likely EHR vendor utilized by St. John's Medical Group and St. John's Riverside Hospital is Medgen.
