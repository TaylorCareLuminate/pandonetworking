About the Practice

Jefferson Health is reimagining healthcare with a mission to improve lives through a service-minded community of providers and specialists. They provide quality, compassionate care and are dedicated to improving community health. Jefferson Health serves the Delaware Valley with a robust network of 32 hospitals and physician practices, offering a broad family of primary care physicians and specialists. They are home to nationally ranked experts and the Sidney Kimmel Comprehensive Cancer Center – Jefferson Health, an NCI-designated Comprehensive Cancer Center, providing comprehensive outpatient cancer services. Partnering with Thomas Jefferson University, they also focus on educating future health professionals and discovering new knowledge, with recognized excellence in numerous specialties including Cancer, Diabetes and Endocrinology, Ear, Nose & Throat, Gastroenterology & GI Surgery, Neurology & Neurosurgery, Ophthalmology, Orthopedics, Pulmonology, and Urology. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Jefferson Health uses a patient portal called MyJeffersonHealth. This portal is branded as MyChart, which is a patient portal developed by Epic Systems. Multiple search results explicitly connect MyJeffersonHealth to MyChart and the Epic logo is displayed. One result also states that MyChart offers access to an electronic health record (EHR) called Epic. This indicates that Jefferson Health is using Epic as their EHR vendor.
