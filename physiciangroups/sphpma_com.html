About the Practice

Cardiology Associates of Schenectady is a medical group practice specializing in cardiovascular care and is part of St. Peter's Health Partners Medical Associates (SPHPMA) since January 1, 2021. [8, 9] They offer a full range of comprehensive and compassionate cardiovascular testing, diagnostic, and treatment services for patients in Schenectady, Montgomery, Fulton, and southern Saratoga counties. [4, 8] The practice is composed of cardiologists and physician assistants dedicated to providing excellent heart health outcomes. [4, 8]

Methodology & Findings

Cardiology Associates of Schenectady, which is part of St. Peter's Health Partners Medical Associates (SPHPMA), utilizes the MyChart patient portal. St. Peter's Health Partners transitioned to the Trinity Health MyChart portal for patient access to health information from visits on or after May 8, 2021. This portal provides access to electronic health records and billing information. MyChart is the patient portal commonly associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system.
