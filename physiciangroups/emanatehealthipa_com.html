About the Practice

Emanate Health Independent Physician Association (IPA) is a physician network located in the San Gabriel Valley, comprising over 200 primary care doctors and specialists dedicated to providing quality, compassionate healthcare. [1, 2, 10, 11, 13] As a physician-led organization, Emanate Health IPA offers patients direct access to Emanate Health hospitals and a wide range of services including lab work, radiology, and imaging. [1, 3, 8] Specialties and care provided cover primary care, specialty care such as cardiac care, orthopedics, sports medicine, rehabilitation, cancer care, maternity services, and diabetes education, along with urgent care and after-hours services. [1, 6, 8, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Emanate Health Independent Physician Association (emanatehealthipa.com) appears to utilize the MEDITECH Expanse Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. This is indicated by several factors gleaned from recent web searches. The patient portal used by Emanate Health and promoted for use by patients is referred to as the "Patient Health Portal" and is accessible via the "MHealth mobile app". Crucially, the "MHealth app" is identified as the mobile version of MEDITECH's Patient and Consumer Health Portal and is directly associated with the MEDITECH Expanse EHR system. Multiple sources confirm that the MEDITECH Expanse patient portal and MHealth app provide patients with access to their health information, including test results, appointments, and secure messaging with their care team. While Emanate Health IPA is a separate entity from the main Emanate Health hospitals, they are affiliated and share resources, with MedPoint Management providing management services to the IPA. The Emanate Health IPA provider manual mentions "Expanse access" for clinics to view laboratory results, further supporting the presence of the Expanse EHR within their affiliated network. Additionally, the manual refers to the "MPM Provider Web Portal," managed by MedPoint Management, for submitting authorization requests, which likely integrates with the underlying EHR system. Based on the association of the "Patient Health Portal" and "MHealth app" with MEDITECH's Expanse EHR, and the indication of "Expanse access" within the IPA's resources, it can be concluded that Emanate Health Independent Physician Association is using the MEDITECH Expanse EHR system.
