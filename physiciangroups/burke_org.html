About the Practice

Burke Rehabilitation Hospital is a nationally recognized, accredited not-for-profit hospital specializing solely in physical rehabilitation, research, and graduate medical education. [3, 4, 11] As a member of the Montefiore Health System, Burke offers a full spectrum of inpatient and outpatient services for patients recovering from a wide range of conditions including stroke, brain and spinal cord injuries, neurological disorders, orthopedic injuries, and cardiac and pulmonary conditions. [2, 4, 5] It is the only hospital in Westchester County entirely dedicated to rehabilitation medicine. [2, 11]

Methodology & Findings

Burke Rehabilitation Hospital uses MyChart as its patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal developed by Epic Systems. Therefore, Burke Rehabilitation Hospital is using Epic as their EHR vendor. The MyChart patient portal for Burke Rehabilitation Hospital allows patients to manage their health information, view medical records and test results, manage appointments, pay bills online, and communicate with their healthcare providers. Burke Rehabilitation Hospital is part of the Montefiore Health System network, and the MyChart support line is also referred to as Montefiore MyChart Help and Technical Support.
