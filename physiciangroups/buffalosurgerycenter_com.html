About the Practice

Buffalo Surgery Center is a leading multi-specialty ambulatory surgery center in Western New York dedicated to the surgical and procedural treatment of patients. [2, 4] Utilizing world-class technology and state-of-the-art equipment, the center specializes in Orthopaedics, including total joint replacement and minimally invasive procedures, and Gastroenterology/Colorectal procedures like endoscopy and diagnostic testing. [2, 4, 12, 15] They focus on providing superior patient care with respect, dignity, and compassion in a pleasant and relaxing atmosphere. [2, 4, 7]
