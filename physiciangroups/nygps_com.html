About the Practice

New York Group for Plastic Surgery (NYGPS) is the oldest and largest private academic plastic surgery practice in the United States, founded in 1948. [7] They offer a wide range of aesthetic and reconstructive procedures with a focus on personalized care. [3, 6] Key specialties include breast reconstruction, microsurgery, cosmetic surgery of the face, breast, and body, burn and complex wound management, hand surgery, non-invasive procedures, pediatric plastic and craniofacial surgery, post-weight loss surgery, and skin care. [5, 7, 13] Their team of board-certified plastic surgeons provides expert and compassionate care. [4, 6]

Methodology & Findings

New York Plastic Surgical Group (nygps.com) utilizes myTouchMD for their patient portal. This patient portal is a platform that allows patients to access medical information, communicate with staff, and view information related to their procedures. myTouchMD is part of TouchMD, which was acquired by NexTech Systems, an EHR and practice management software provider, in 2010. Based on this acquisition, the EHR vendor associated with myTouchMD and likely used by New York Plastic Surgical Group is NexTech Systems.
