About the Practice

Jewish Family & Children's Service (JFCS) is a non-profit organization providing integrated behavioral health, healthcare, and social services. The JFCS West Valley Healthcare Center offers comprehensive care including behavioral health treatment such as psychiatric care, substance abuse treatment, individual and group therapy, and play therapy, alongside primary care services for adults including treatment for common illnesses, immunizations, and physical exams. They adopt a "whole person" approach to care, serving individuals of all ages, faiths, and backgrounds, with a focus on providing access to underserved communities.
