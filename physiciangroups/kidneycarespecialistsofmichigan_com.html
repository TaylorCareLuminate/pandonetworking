About the Practice

Michigan Kidney Consultants, P.C. is Michigan's Largest Board Certified Nephrology Group. [1] They are dedicated to providing the most advanced diagnostics and treatments available for kidney disease and end-stage renal failure. [1] Their physicians are board certified in the sub-speciality of nephrology as well as internal medicine. [1] Patient services include acute and chronic inpatient/outpatient hemodialysis and CAPD/CCPD, kidney transplantation evaluation and follow-up, kidney biopsy, dialysis access, clinical studies, and the diagnosis and treatment of hypertension. [1]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Michigan Kidney Consultants, P.C. (kidneycarespecialistsofmichigan.com) utilizes MyChart as their patient portal, which is integrated with the Acumen nEHR system. This allows patients to access various health information online, including upcoming appointments, medications, allergies, lab results, and vital signs. While MyChart is a widely recognized patient portal brand also used by healthcare systems on the Epic EHR platform, Michigan Kidney Consultants, P.C.'s website explicitly states their MyChart portal is part of the Acumen nEHR.
