About the Practice

UBMD Emergency Medicine, founded in 2007, is the non-profit faculty practice plan of the University at Buffalo Jacobs School of Medicine and Biomedical Sciences. They provide a full spectrum of high quality, compassionate, evidence-based emergency medical care 24/7 to patients across Western New York by staffing the Emergency Departments at numerous leading hospitals. Patient care is supervised by University faculty, board certified Emergency Physicians, supported by over 150 physicians and advanced practice providers. Their core mission includes educating residents and they have expertise in divisional specialties such as Emergency Medicine Research, Sports Medicine, Emergency Medicine Ultrasound, and Telemedicine, and are involved in community programs like NY MATTERS and the Emergency Physician Response Program.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The University at Buffalo Department of Emergency Medicine (ubmdemergency.com), as part of UBMD Physicians' Group, utilizes a patient portal branded as the **UBMD Patient Portal**, which is powered by **FollowMyHealth™**. FollowMyHealth is a patient engagement platform that is designed to integrate with various Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems. UBMD Physicians' Group uses the **Cerner** EMR system. Although not specifically stated for the Emergency Medicine department, the UBMD-wide use of FollowMyHealth and the known integration capabilities of FollowMyHealth with Cerner indicate that the UBMD Patient Portal (FollowMyHealth) is the patient portal used in conjunction with their Cerner EHR.
