About the Practice

ENT and Allergy Associates, LLP (ENTA) is the largest ear, nose, throat, allergy, and audiology practice in the country, with over 450 clinicians across 60+ office locations in New York, New Jersey, and Pennsylvania. [1, 5, 11] They offer comprehensive and subspecialty medical and surgical care for a wide range of conditions including general ENT, allergy, audiology, sinus and skull base surgery, voice and swallowing disorders, facial plastics, disorders of the inner ear, asthma, clinical immunology, and diagnostic audiology. [1, 5] ENTA is dedicated to providing personalized, evidence-based treatments with a focus on clinical excellence and a superb patient experience, utilizing the latest technology. [1, 5]
