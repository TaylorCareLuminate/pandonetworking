About the Practice

Auburn Memorial Medical Services PC, associated with imaamed.com and affiliated with Auburn Community Hospital, provides comprehensive office-based primary care and specialty services to the Auburn and surrounding areas. Specialties include Internal Medicine, Gastroenterology, Pulmonology and Sleep Medicine, Hospital Medicine, Diabetic Care and Education, Wellness and Preventive Care, as well as Orthopedic, Cardiology, Endocrinology, ENT, Surgical Services, OB/GYN, Podiatry, Nephrology, Urology, Neurology, Pediatric, and Urgent Care. [2, 10, 18] They are committed to providing high quality patient care coordination and treating the whole person. [12, 28]

Methodology & Findings

Auburn Memorial Medical Services PC (imaamed.com) utilizes the Medent patient portal. Medent is identified as an "All-In-One" system that encompasses EMR/EHR, Practice Management, and Patient Portal software. This indicates that Medent serves as both the patient portal vendor and the Electronic Health Record (EHR) vendor for Auburn Memorial Medical Services PC.
