About the Practice

White Plains Hospital Physician Associates (WPHPA) is a division of White Plains Hospital, a leading healthcare provider in Westchester County and a member of the Montefiore Health System. WPHPA aligns local practicing physicians with Hospital services to deliver high-quality, patient-centered care. They offer a wide range of primary, specialty, and sub-specialty care services including Allergy & Immunology, Bariatric Surgery, Cardiology, Dermatology, Endocrinology, ENT/Otolaryngology, Family Medicine, Gastroenterology, General Surgery, Geriatrics, Headache Medicine, Hepatology, Infectious Disease, Internal Medicine & Primary Care, Interventional Oncology, Maternal-Fetal Medicine, Medical Oncology & Hematology, Nephrology, Neurology, Neurosurgery, Nutrition, Obstetrics & Gynecology, Orthopedics, Pain Management, Pediatrics, Physical & Occupational/Hand Therapy, Plastic Surgery, Podiatry, Pulmonology, Radiation Oncology, Rheumatology, Sleep Medicine, Spine Surgery, Surgical Oncology, and Urgent Care.

Methodology & Findings

White Plains Hospital Physician Associates uses the MyChart patient portal. White Plains Hospital, with which White Plains Hospital Physician Associates is affiliated, transitioned to Epic, a comprehensive electronic health record system, on May 1, 2021, and utilizes MyChart as its patient portal. Patients can access their health information, schedule appointments, view test results, and manage their care through the MyChart website or mobile app. The MyChart application is also used for virtual health visits with White Plains Hospital Physician Associates providers.
