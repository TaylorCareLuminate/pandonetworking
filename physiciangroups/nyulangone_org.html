About the Practice

NYU Langone Health is a world-class integrated academic health system based in New York City, recognized for excellence in patient care, education, and research. It is one of the largest health systems in the Northeast, with a mission to care, teach, and discover, aiming for the best patient outcomes. NYU Langone offers comprehensive inpatient and outpatient services across a wide range of medical and surgical specialties, including cardiology, oncology (Perlmutter Cancer Center), neurology and neurosurgery, orthopedics (NYU Langone Orthopedic Hospital), and physical medicine and rehabilitation (Rusk Rehabilitation). The system includes multiple inpatient facilities and numerous ambulatory locations, providing advanced care for diverse conditions across all stages of life and is highly ranked nationally for quality and safety.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

NYU Langone Health utilizes "NYU Langone Health MyChart" as its patient portal. This patient portal is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system, which is used by NYU Langone Health. MyChart is a secure online portal that allows patients to manage their healthcare, including accessing medical records, scheduling appointments, requesting prescription refills, and communicating with their doctors. NYU Langone Health adopted the Epic system in 2009, and it serves as a comprehensive database for coordinating various aspects of patient care. The MyChart portal provides patients with convenient access to their health information within the Epic system. The NYU Langone Health app also allows patients to access their MyChart account on mobile devices.
