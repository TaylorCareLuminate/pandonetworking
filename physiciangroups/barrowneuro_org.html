About the Practice

Barrow Neurological Institute at Dignity Health St. Joseph's Hospital and Medical Center in Phoenix, Arizona, is an international leader in the treatment, research, and education of brain and spinal diseases, conditions, and injuries. It is one of the busiest centers for neurology and neurosurgery in the United States, treating a wide range of neurological disorders and housing specialized centers like the Ivy Brain Tumor Center and the Muhammad Ali Parkinson Center. Barrow is highly regarded for neuroscience education and research, contributing to breakthroughs in neurosurgery and neurology.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Barrow Neurological Institute (barrowneuro.org) utilizes the "My Portal (Patient Portal)" for its patients to access their health information. This patient portal is a component of the larger Dignity Health system. Dignity Health, the parent organization, uses the Cerner electronic health record (EHR) system across its facilities. This includes the implementation of Cerner's PowerChart Ambulatory EHR in their clinic locations and Cerner Millennium EHR in their hospitals. The "My Portal" patient portal, which Barrow Neurological Institute uses, is powered by Cerner, in collaboration with Okta for identity management. Based on this information, it can be concluded that Barrow Neurological Institute likely uses the Cerner EHR system, with patients accessing their health information and services through the Dignity Health "My Portal (Patient Portal)".
