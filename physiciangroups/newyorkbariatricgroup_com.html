Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

New York Bariatric Group appears to utilize MyChart as their patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal system developed by Epic Systems. According to information from Stamford Health, which mentions New York Bariatric Group, MyChart is used as a patient portal to manage health information, schedule appointments, find providers, and handle billing. This is also indicated in relation to New York Bariatric Group locations in both Stamford, CT and Roslyn Heights, NY. The New York Bariatric Group's own website also refers to a "Patient Portal" where patients can track progress and request appointments. While the vendor name is not explicitly stated on the New York Bariatric Group's portal pages found, the external references strongly suggest the use of MyChart. Given that MyChart is the patient portal product of Epic, it indicates that New York Bariatric Group likely uses Epic as their electronic health record (EHR) system.
