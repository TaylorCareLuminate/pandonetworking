About the Practice

UR Medicine Noyes Health, including Nicholas H. Noyes Memorial Hospital, is a 67-bed acute care community hospital serving Livingston and surrounding counties, affiliated with the University of Rochester Medical Center. It provides comprehensive, compassionate, community-based care including medical, surgical, and emergency inpatient and outpatient services. Specialties and services offered encompass acute care, birthing, cardiology, diagnostic imaging, emergency care, infusion services, hospice care, hospitalist services, kidney and dialysis, laboratory, mental health services, prenatal care, rehabilitation therapy, respiratory therapy, surgical services, sleep studies, and family medicine.

Methodology & Findings

Noyes Memorial Hospital, which is part of UR Medicine, utilizes the MyChart patient portal for its patients. MyChart is a widely recognized patient portal solution that is commonly associated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. Through the MyChart portal, patients of Noyes Health can access various features to manage their healthcare, including viewing test results, requesting prescription renewals, scheduling appointments, and communicating with their healthcare team.
