About the Practice

Northside Hospital is a leading not-for-profit healthcare system in the Atlanta metropolitan area, Georgia, comprising five acute-care hospitals and more than 250 outpatient locations. [8, 11] It is recognized as a top provider for services including cancer care, newborn deliveries (leading the U.S.), surgical services with advanced robotic technology, cardiovascular care, orthopedic and sports medicine, neurology, women's health, and comprehensive rehabilitation services. [1, 5, 8, 14] The system is committed to providing quality healthcare across a wide range of specialties.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Northside Hospital primarily utilizes the MyOneChart patient portal for many of its services, including Orthopedic Institute-Sports Medicine, Family Medicine and Urgent Care, and the Cardiovascular Institute. Another patient portal, FollowMyHealth, is used by the Northside Hospital Diagnostic Clinic. While MyOneChart is described in some contexts as a personal health portal and even an electronic health record (EHR) system, it functions as a patient engagement platform that integrates with an underlying EHR system. According to multiple sources, Northside Hospital uses Cerner as its electronic health record (EHR) system. This indicates that the MyOneChart and potentially the FollowMyHealth portals used by Northside Hospital are integrated with the Cerner EHR system to provide patients access to their health information.
