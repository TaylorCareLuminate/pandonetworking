About the Practice

The University of Washington, founded in 1861, is a public research university with its main campus in Seattle and additional campuses in Bothell and Tacoma. It is consistently ranked among the top public universities globally and is a leading institution in research, receiving significant federal funding. [3, 5, 7] The university is particularly renowned for its strengths in medical, engineering, and scientific research, and it includes a world-class medical center, UW Medicine. [3, 4, 6, 9] Offering a wide array of undergraduate, graduate, and professional programs across numerous colleges and schools, the UW serves a large and diverse student body dedicated to learning, innovation, and community building. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

University of Washington (washington.edu), specifically UW Medicine, utilizes the MyChart patient portal. This patient portal is tied to the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. MyChart allows patients to manage their healthcare online, including accessing medical information, booking appointments, paying bills, messaging care teams, and viewing test results. The UW Health medical record is maintained on the Epic platform, with MyChart being a primary application for patient access.
