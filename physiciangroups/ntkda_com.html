About the Practice

North Texas Kidney Disease Associates (NTKDA) specializes in Nephrology, providing comprehensive and compassionate care for patients with kidney disease and hypertension. They offer expert diagnosis, treatment, and prevention for conditions including acute and chronic kidney disease, end-stage renal disease, hypertension, and electrolyte disorders. NTKDA also manages patients requiring kidney transplants and various dialysis modalities, such as Hemodialysis, Peritoneal Dialysis, Home hemodialysis, and Nocturnal Dialysis. Their focus is on delivering high-quality, individualized medical care and patient education to the community. [5, 9, 10, 11, 12]

North Texas Kidney Disease Associates (ntkda.com) utilizes a patient portal provided by Athenahealth. This indicates that Athenahealth is the vendor for their patient portal and likely their Electronic Health Record (EHR) system as well.
