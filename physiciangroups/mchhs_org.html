About the Practice

Memorial Community Hospital & Health System, with its main campus in Blair, Nebraska, is an independent, not-for-profit hospital system serving Burt and Washington Counties. They provide comprehensive inpatient and outpatient healthcare services, including a specialty clinic offering care in over 15 areas such as Cardiology, Neurosurgery, Orthopedics, Pulmonology, and Urology. Their mission is to promote community health by identifying and meeting local needs through quality care in a compassionate environment.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Memorial Community Hospital & Health System (mchhs.org) utilizes "MCHHS My Chart" as its patient portal. This branding strongly suggests that the underlying electronic health record (EHR) system is Epic. MyChart is a patient portal developed by Epic Systems, a widely used EHR vendor in the United States. Healthcare organizations that implement Epic typically offer MyChart to their patients, providing them with online access to their medical records, appointment scheduling, communication with providers, and other health management tools. While direct confirmation explicitly stating that Memorial Community Hospital & Health System in Blair, Nebraska, uses Epic was not found in every search result, the consistent use of the "My Chart" branding for their patient portal aligns with Epic's ecosystem. Other Memorial Health systems and hospitals mentioning MyChart explicitly state their use of the Epic EHR system. The presence of "MCHHS My Chart" as the patient portal name for Memorial Community Hospital & Health System in Blair, Nebraska, indicates a very high probability that Epic is their EHR vendor.
