About the Practice

Columbia University's Department of Surgery, a vital part of the Columbia University Irving Medical Center and partner of NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital, delivers comprehensive surgical care, education, and research. They offer a full spectrum of specialized and subspecialized surgical services for complex conditions, consistently recognized among the nation's top hospitals. Specialties include abdominal organ transplant, cancer surgery, colorectal surgery, general surgery, trauma surgery, critical care, hernia repair, plastic & reconstructive surgery, cardiothoracic surgery, vascular surgery, liver surgery, thoracic surgery, endocrine surgery, and pediatric surgery, utilizing the latest technology and research for expert, personalized care. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The patient portal used by Columbia University Department of Surgery, which is part of Columbia University Irving Medical Center, is called **Connect**. Connect is a unified patient portal for Columbia University Irving Medical Center, NewYork-Presbyterian, and Weill Cornell Medicine. This portal is integrated with the **Epic** electronic health record (EHR) system. Columbia University Irving Medical Center and NewYork-Presbyterian fully adopted the Epic EHR system and the Connect patient portal on February 1, 2020. Therefore, similar to how Epic uses MyChart, Columbia University Department of Surgery uses the Connect patient portal, which is tied to the Epic EHR system.
