About the Practice

University of Maryland Medical Center Midtown Campus, formerly Maryland General Hospital, is a community teaching hospital located in Baltimore's cultural center. Since its founding over 130 years ago, it has provided a full range of medical and surgical care as one of two locations of the University of Maryland Medical Center. UMMC Midtown Campus operates 144 inpatient beds and offers care in more than 30 medical specialties, with a wide array of outpatient centers serving over 120,000 patients annually. [2, 13] The hospital is staffed by University of Maryland School of Medicine clinical faculty physicians and team members, committed to improving community health through care and health education. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The University of Maryland Medical Center Midtown Campus, as part of the University of Maryland Medical System (UMMS), utilizes the MyChart patient portal. This portal was previously known as MyPortfolio, and the name was changed to MyChart on August 15, 2024, to streamline the experience for patients who use MyChart with various healthcare providers. MyChart is the patient portal system provided by Epic Systems. Therefore, the University of Maryland Medical Center Midtown Campus uses Epic as its electronic health record (EHR) vendor.
