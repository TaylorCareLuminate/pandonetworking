About the Practice

ProHEALTH Care Associates, LLP is a multi-specialty medical group practice providing comprehensive healthcare services including urgent care, primary care, and a wide range of specialty care. Specialties offered encompass Pediatrics, Gastroenterology, Surgical Assistance, Urology, OBYGN, Orthopedics, Pain Management, Podiatry, Cardiology, General Pediatrics, Psychology/Psychiatry, Neurology, Eye Care, Geriatric Medicine, Internal Medicine, and Family Medicine, catering to the health and wellness needs of patients of all ages.
