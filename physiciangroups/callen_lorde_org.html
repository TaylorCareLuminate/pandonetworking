About the Practice

Callen-Lorde Community Health Center is a global leader in LGBTQ+ healthcare, dedicated to providing sensitive, quality, comprehensive care and related services to New York's lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, and queer communities in all their diversity, regardless of ability to pay. Their services encompass primary care, sexual health, behavioral health, women's health, transgender health, HIV treatment and prevention, dental care, and case management, aiming to transform lives and drive positive change through healthcare that is considered a human right. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Callen-Lorde Community Health Center is utilizing MyChart as their patient portal, which is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system. The health center has been transitioning to the comprehensive Epic EHR system to enhance patient experience, improve care coordination, and streamline services. MyChart serves as their new patient portal, allowing patients to manage contact information, message providers, pay bills, download medical records, and view lab results, among other features. The implementation of Epic also enables the use of features like Care Everywhere, which allows for the secure electronic exchange of patient medical information with other healthcare organizations using Epic.
