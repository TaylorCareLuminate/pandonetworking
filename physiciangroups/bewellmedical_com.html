About the Practice

Be Well Primary Health Care Center is a Multi-Specialty Diagnostic and Treatment Center located in Flatbush, Brooklyn, NY. As an Article 28 Diagnostic and Treatment Center established in 2004, it serves as a one-stop-shop offering comprehensive primary care with a focus on preventative services for patients of all ages. The center provides an extensive range of specialty services including internal medicine, dentistry, ophthalmology, optometry, physical therapy, allergy, dermatology, gastroenterology, orthopedics, otolaryngology, pain management, podiatry, urology, cardiology, endocrinology, general surgery, hematology, neurology, OB GYN, pediatrics, sonography, surgery, and vascular surgery. Recognized as a Patient-Centered Medical Home, Be Well is committed to coordinating care and delivering optimal patient outcomes.
