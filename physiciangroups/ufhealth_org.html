About the Practice

UF Health is the academic health center of the University of Florida, dedicated to promoting health through outstanding patient care, education in health professions and biomedical sciences, and research. [1] The network includes 12 hospitals and hundreds of physician practices and outpatient locations across Florida, including Gainesville, Jacksonville, and Central Florida. [1, 4, 7] UF Health provides high-quality, patient-centered care with numerous nationally ranked adult and pediatric specialties such as neurology, cancer, heart care, pediatrics, ENT, urology, geriatrics, orthopedics, and pulmonology. [1, 14] Services range from primary care and specialty care to emergency and urgent care, rehabilitation, dental services, and home health, serving as Florida's premier hospital system and a vital resource for the community with a mission to heal, comfort, educate, and discover. [1, 7, 8, 11, 14]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UF Health utilizes the MyUFHealth patient portal, which is powered by Epic. Patients can access MyUFHealth through the MyChart mobile app provided by Epic Systems. This indicates that UF Health's electronic health record (EHR) system is Epic. MyUFHealth allows patients to access portions of their medical record, review test results, communicate securely with providers, manage appointments, and more. The implementation of Epic across UF Health locations aims to connect records for seamless access to health information. UF Health St. Johns, for example, recently completed its transition to the Epic system, integrating patient records and scheduling into the unified platform used throughout the UF Health system. UF Health also uses EpicCare MyLink as a community physician portal for external physicians to access patient information.
