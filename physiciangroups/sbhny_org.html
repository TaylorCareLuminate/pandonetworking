About the Practice

SBH Health System, including St. Barnabas Hospital, is a non-profit teaching hospital in the Bronx, New York City, providing compassionate, high-quality acute care and operating as a Level II Trauma Center, a State-designated Stroke Center, and a State-designated AIDS Center. [19, 26] Committed to the health and wellness of the community, SBH goes beyond treating illness to address social determinants of health and offers medical education programs training residents. [16, 19, 26]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

St. Barnabas Hospital (SBH Health System) uses the MyChart patient portal. MyChart is a patient portal system developed by Epic. Reports indicate that SBH Health System successfully implemented the Epic EHR system, which includes the MyChart portal. This implementation was part of an infrastructure overhaul that also included an Oracle ERP system. MyChart replaced a previous portal that had significantly lower patient engagement. The adoption of MyChart is seen as central to helping the community access care.
