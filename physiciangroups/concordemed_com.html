About the Practice

Concorde Medical Group is a multi-specialty practice located in Manhattan, NYC, providing comprehensive and compassionate healthcare for over 25 years. As a member of Northwell Health, the group offers a broad range of medical services anchored by general internists and enhanced by specialists across numerous disciplines including Cardiology, Dermatology, Endocrinology & Diabetes, Infectious Diseases, Allergy, Asthma, & Clinical Immunology, Gastroenterology & Liver Diseases, Gynecology & Urogynecology, Podiatry, Neurology, Physiatry, and Rheumatology. They are committed to delivering personal, individualized care utilizing state-of-the-art technology and are affiliated with NYU School of Medicine and NYU Langone Medical Center.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Concorde Medical Group utilizes the ezAccess platform as its patient portal, providing patients with access to their health records and the ability to communicate with their providers. The ezAccess patient portal is designed to integrate with athenahealth's electronic health record (EHR) systems, specifically athenaPractice, athenaFlow, and athenaOne. Concorde Medical Group is also identified as a member of Northwell Health Physician Partners. While Northwell Health has announced plans to implement Epic as their system-wide EHR in the future, with the first phase beginning in 2025, the current patient portal in use by Concorde Medical Group, ezAccess, is directly linked to integration with athenahealth products. This indicates that Concorde Medical Group currently uses an athenahealth EHR system that is integrated with the ezAccess patient portal.
