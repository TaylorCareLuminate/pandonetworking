About the Practice

Essentia Health is an integrated health system serving patients across Minnesota, Wisconsin, North Dakota, and Idaho through a network of hospitals, clinics, long-term care facilities, and more. [2, 10] Guided by values of quality, hospitality, respect, justice, stewardship, and teamwork, Essentia Health is called to make a healthy difference in people's lives by providing a wide range of primary and specialty care services, including Cardiology, Oncology, Orthopedics, Neurology, Behavioral Health, and many others. [2, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 14] The organization is accredited as an Accountable Care Organization by the National Committee for Quality Assurance. [2, 10]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Essentia Health utilizes MyChart as their patient portal. This patient portal is provided by Epic, which is also the vendor for Essentia Health's electronic health record (EHR) system, EpicCare. Essentia Health transitioned to using Epic for their EHR system in 2019 and implemented MyChart as part of this change. MyChart allows patients secure online access to portions of their medical records, the ability to communicate with their care team, schedule appointments, request prescription refills, view test results, and more. While some search results mention "Essentia EMR" by Netsmart, this appears to be a separate system potentially used for behavioral health, and the primary patient portal and wider EHR system for Essentia Health are MyChart and Epic, respectively.
