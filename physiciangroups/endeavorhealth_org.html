About the Practice

Endeavor Health Medical Group is the premier medical group of Endeavor Health, formed from the merger of previous medical groups. It provides comprehensive care for the entire family across more than 50 specialties, including primary care such as Family Medicine, Internal Medicine, and Pediatrics, as well as various specialties like Behavioral Health, Bariatric & Obesity Medicine, Midwifery, Neurology, OB/GYN, Orthopedics, Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation, Cancer, and Cardiovascular care. [1, 2, 6] They emphasize convenient access, online scheduling, and a team-based approach to deliver personalized, high-quality care for everything from annual wellness visits to chronic and complex medical conditions. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Endeavor Health Medical Group utilizes multiple patient portals depending on the care location, including NorthShoreConnect, Edward-Elmhurst Health MyChart, and NCH MyChart, as they work towards a single unified patient portal. The electronic health record (EHR) system used by Endeavor Health, which integrates with these patient portals, is Epic. The MyChart patient portal branding, used by Edward-Elmhurst Health and NCH, is associated with the Epic EHR platform, allowing patients to access their medical records and manage their healthcare. NorthShoreConnect also provides access to patient results stored in the new Epic EHR system. This indicates that while Endeavor Health uses different patient portal names for different parts of its system, the underlying EHR for these portals is Epic.
