About the Practice

Great Lakes Medical Imaging is the premier diagnostic imaging services provider in Western New York, dedicated to providing the highest quality imaging for patients and their families. [5, 6, 8, 13] They offer a full spectrum of diagnostic imaging services with expertise in nearly every type of imaging, including specialty areas such as Neuroradiology, Musculoskeletal imaging, Breast imaging, Abdominal imaging, Pediatric imaging, and Interventional Radiology. [5, 6, 7, 8, 11] Utilizing advanced technology, they aim to provide accurate and timely results in a comfortable setting, emphasizing compassionate care. [5, 7, 9] Great Lakes Medical Imaging is recognized for its excellence, holding the American College of Radiology® Diagnostic Imaging Center of Excellence award. [5, 7]
