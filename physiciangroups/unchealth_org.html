About the Practice

UNC Health is a not-for-profit integrated academic health care system owned by the state of North Carolina and based in Chapel Hill. [4] Its mission is to improve the health and well-being of North Carolinians and others served by providing leadership and excellence in patient care, education, and research. [2, 4] The system includes UNC Hospitals, the clinical programs of the UNC School of Medicine, and 16 hospitals across 20 campuses across the state. [4] UNC Health offers compassionate clinical care, leading-edge research, and a statewide network for care closer to home. [8] Specialized patient care services cover a wide range of areas including cancer care through the Lineberger Comprehensive Cancer Center, women's and children's services, cardiovascular care, diabetes care, lung conditions, rehabilitation, spine care, wound management, and transplant services, alongside expertise in numerous other medical and surgical specialties. [12] Urgent care and virtual care options are also available. [9]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

UNC Health utilizes a patient portal branded as My UNC Chart. This patient portal is connected with the Epic electronic medical record (EHR system), which UNC Health refers to as EPIC@UNC. My UNC Chart allows patients to access portions of their electronic medical record, view test results, manage appointments, request prescription refills, and communicate with their providers. The MyChart mobile app is also available for access. While most UNC Health patients use My UNC Chart, one location, Onslow Memorial, uses a different patient portal.
