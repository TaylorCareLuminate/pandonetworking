About the Practice

Greenberg Cosmetic Surgery & Dermatology is a leading cosmetic surgery group specializing in a wide range of plastic surgery and dermatological procedures for the face, body, and breasts, including both surgical and non-surgical options. [12, 13, 9] Founded by Dr. Stephen T. Greenberg, the practice is known for combining advanced surgical techniques with cutting-edge technologies and offering rapid recovery programs. [10, 12, 11] They provide personalized care and aim to help patients achieve natural-looking results and improved self-confidence. [11, 12]
