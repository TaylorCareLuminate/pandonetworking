About the Practice

UT Physicians is the clinical practice of McGovern Medical School at UTHealth Houston, providing comprehensive primary and specialty care for patients of all ages. With more than 2,000 clinicians certified in over 80 medical specialties and subspecialties, they offer integrated, personalized care ranging from routine wellness visits to urgent, highly specialized, and complex treatments, incorporating the latest medical treatments and technology.

Methodology & Findings

UT Physicians utilizes a patient portal called MyUTHealth Houston, which is a branded version of the MyChart medical app. This patient portal is integrated with the Epic electronic health record (EHR) system used by UTHealth Houston, the parent institution of UT Physicians. UTHealth Houston, including UT Physicians, transitioned to the Epic EHR system on May 8, 2021. The MyUTHealth Houston portal provides patients with secure online access to their medical records, allowing them to request appointments, view health summaries, access test results, request prescription renewals, communicate with their healthcare team, and pay invoices. The MyUTHealth app replaced a previous patient portal called MyUTP. MyChart is a registered trademark of Epic Systems Corporation.
