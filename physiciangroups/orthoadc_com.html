About the Practice

Orthopedic Associates of Dutchess County (OADC) is a provider of advanced, accessible, and comprehensive orthopedic and physical therapy services in the Mid-Hudson Valley community, with over 45 years of experience. [2, 4, 6, 7] They offer surgical and non-surgical care for a wide range of orthopedic needs, including Total Joint Replacement, Adult and Pediatric Fracture Care, Sports Medicine, Concussion Management, Orthopedic Oncology, Regenerative Medicine, Spine, Foot, Ankle, and Hand Surgery, as well as Pain Management. [2, 3, 6] OADC has five convenient office locations, several with OrthoExpress Walk-In Centers, and provides on-site Imaging, Bracing, and Physical and Occupational Therapy. [2, 5, 7] They also have a state-of-the-art ambulatory surgery center, The Surgery Center at Orthopedic Associates (SCOA), attached to their Poughkeepsie office, offering Hospital-Free Outpatient Joint Replacement. [2]

Methodology & Findings

Orthopedic Associates of Dutchess County (orthoadc.com) utilizes the Modernizing Medicine (ModMed) Electronic Medical Record (EMR) system. Their patient portal is part of the Modernizing Medicine system and can be accessed via the APPatient app on smartphones or through a patient portal website with the URL ons.ema.md. The branding of the patient portal is directly associated with Modernizing Medicine.
