About the Practice

Texas Children's Hospital is an internationally recognized acute care women's and children's hospital with its main campus located in the Texas Medical Center in Houston. [3, 8] It is the primary pediatric teaching hospital affiliated with Baylor College of Medicine and is the largest children's hospital in the United States with 973 beds. [3] Texas Children's provides comprehensive pediatric specialty and subspecialty care to patients from age 0-21 across more than 40 pediatric subspecialties, including an ACS verified level I pediatric trauma center, regional pediatric and neonatal intensive care units, and specialized programs for areas like pediatric cancer and adult congenital heart disease. [3, 8] The hospital also houses the Texas Children's Hospital - Pavilion for Women, offering gynecological and maternity care. [3, 8]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Texas Children's Hospital utilizes MyChart as its patient portal, which is a system commonly associated with Epic Systems. MyChart provides patients and families with online access to various health management tools, including communicating with doctors, accessing test results, requesting prescription refills, managing appointments, and viewing medical history and immunizations. It is available through a web browser or a mobile app. Multiple sources confirm that Texas Children's Hospital uses Epic Systems for their electronic health record (EHR) system. This aligns with the use of MyChart, as MyChart is the patient portal developed by Epic Systems.
