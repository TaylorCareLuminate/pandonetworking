About the Practice

East Side Medical Practice specializes in Internal Medicine, providing comprehensive primary care for adults. Their services encompass diagnosis, treatment of chronic illnesses, health promotion, and disease prevention across a broad spectrum of adult health issues. The practice is equipped to handle complex medical cases and offers services including wellness exams, management of various conditions like diabetes and hypertension, and immigration medical exams. Some associated physicians cover additional specialties such as Podiatry and Dermatology.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

East Side Medical Practice (eastsidemedicalpractice.com) appears to utilize the **Manage My Health** platform for its patient portal. A testimonial from a Practice Manager at "East Side Medical" specifically references features of Manage My Health, indicating its use by the practice. Manage My Health functions as a patient portal that facilitates secure communication and access to health information between patients and their healthcare providers. The platform also offers a Shared Electronic Health Record (SEHR) and is designed to integrate with various practice management systems, including Medtech32 and Medtech Evolution, which could be the underlying EHR systems used by practices leveraging Manage My Health.
