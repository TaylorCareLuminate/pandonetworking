About the Practice

Jackson Health System is a non-profit academic medical system located in Miami, Florida, governed by the Public Health Trust. [2, 8] It is one of the nation's largest public healthcare networks and has a flagship academic partnership with the University of Miami. [2] Jackson Health System is committed to providing a single, high standard of quality care for all residents of Miami-Dade County, regardless of their ability to pay. [2, 8] The system offers a comprehensive network of services and a wide range of specialties, including bariatrics, cardiac care, surgical services, endocrinology, gastroenterology, maternal-fetal medicine, obstetrics and gynecology, orthopedics, pediatrics, primary care, and urology. [4, 11] It includes hospitals, urgent care centers, specialty care centers, and primary care clinics. [3, 4, 8]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Jackson Health System uses the patient portal branded as myJacksonHealth. This patient portal is provided by Oracle Cerner. Based on the patient portal vendor, Jackson Health System utilizes an Oracle Cerner electronic health record (EHR) system. Multiple sources confirm Jackson Health System's use of a Cerner EHR. Jackson Memorial Hospital, which is part of the Jackson Health System, is noted as a large hospital using an Oracle Cerner EHR.
