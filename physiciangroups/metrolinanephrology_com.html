About the Practice

Metrolina Nephrology Associates is the region's most recognized and experienced nephrology group, specializing in the treatment and management of kidney disease for over 40 years. Composed of numerous board-certified nephrologists and advanced practice providers, the practice offers comprehensive nephrology services, including the management of chronic kidney disease, dialysis, vascular access management through Metrolina Vascular Care, and kidney and kidney/pancreas transplantation services via The Metrolina Transplant Clinic.
