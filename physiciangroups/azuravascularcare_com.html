About the Practice

Azura Vascular Care specializes in minimally invasive outpatient procedures for the treatment and management of a wide range of vascular conditions. [4, 5, 6] They operate outpatient and ambulatory surgery centers across the United States, focusing on conditions including dialysis access management, peripheral arterial disease, uterine fibroids, varicose veins, and varicoceles. [2, 4, 5, 6]
