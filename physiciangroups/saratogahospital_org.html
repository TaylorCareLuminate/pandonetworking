About the Practice

Saratoga Hospital, part of the Albany Med Health System, is a 171-bed independent, locally governed community hospital and the leading healthcare provider in the Saratoga region. With over 450 physicians and medical professionals across more than 20 locations, it offers comprehensive care in a broad range of medical specialties, including inpatient services, urgent and primary care, diagnostic imaging, surgical services like bariatric surgery, women's health, cardiology, neurology, oncology, and rehabilitation. Recognized for high-quality, personalized, and compassionate patient care, Saratoga Hospital holds Magnet designation for nursing excellence.

Methodology & Findings

Saratoga Hospital, which is part of the Albany Med Health System, utilizes the MyChart patient portal. This transition to MyChart occurred on November 2, 2024, replacing their previous Medent patient portal. MyChart is the patient portal solution provided by Epic Systems, indicating that Saratoga Hospital uses Epic as its Electronic Health Record (EHR) system.
