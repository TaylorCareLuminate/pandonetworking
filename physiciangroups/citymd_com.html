About the Practice

CityMD, which includes City Medical of the Upper East Side, PLLC, is a healthcare company primarily focused on providing urgent care services. They treat a range of illnesses and injuries, including sore throats, flu, COVID-19, allergies, sprains, cuts, and minor burns. Services offered include X-rays, lab tests and screenings, vaccinations, physical exams, pediatric care, women's health services, and occupational medicine. They provide walk-in care as an alternative to emergency rooms for non-life-threatening conditions.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

City Medical of Upper East Side PLLC, which is part of Summit Health and CityMD, appears to utilize eClinicalWorks as its electronic health record (EHR) vendor. A report from 2016 indicated that CityMD went live with eClinicalWorks, specifically mentioning the incorporation of the eClinicalWorks Patient Portal into their system. While the branding for patient access has evolved, the underlying technology seems to be connected to eClinicalWorks. Current information points to a "Summit + CityMD app" which offers features typical of a patient portal, including accessing test results, managing prescriptions, and scheduling appointments. There is also mention of a "My Summit Health App" that serves a similar purpose for managing healthcare information. Although the "Summit Health+CityMD" sign-in process is noted as being separate from the traditional patient portal account creation, the functionalities provided through these platforms align with the capabilities of the eClinicalWorks Patient Portal. Therefore, based on the available information, the patient portal vendor/branding used by City Medical of Upper East Side PLLC (CityMD) is likely an iteration or integration of the eClinicalWorks Patient Portal, presented to patients through platforms like the Summit + CityMD app and potentially the My Summit Health App, tied to the eClinicalWorks EHR system.
