About the Practice

Hawaii Health Partners is the state's first physician-led Accountable Care Organization (ACO) dedicated to transforming healthcare in Hawai'i by focusing on value-based care. [3, 10] Their goal is to improve healthcare quality, efficiency, enhance patient experience, and reduce the overall cost of care. [3] A subsidiary of Hawaii Pacific Health, HHP integrates a high-performing network of physicians, facilities, and hospitals to deliver high-quality, appropriate, team-based, integrated care. [3, 10] They provide services focusing on population health management, complex care coordination for high-risk patients, and managed care services, including referral management. [15] Hawaii Health Partners supports its physician members in practicing value-based care through collaboration, technology access, and programs for efficient care. [3]
