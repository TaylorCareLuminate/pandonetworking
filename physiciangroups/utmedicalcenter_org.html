About the Practice

The University of Tennessee Medical Center (UTMC) is the East Tennessee region's only academic medical center, serving as a referral center for East Tennessee, Southeast Kentucky, and Western North Carolina with a mission of healing, education, and discovery. It is a Level I Trauma Center and a Comprehensive Stroke Center, offering a wide range of specialized care through its Centers of Excellence including the Heart Hospital, Cancer Institute, Brain and Spine Institute, Center for Women & Infants, Emergency and Trauma Center, and Heart Lung Vascular Institute. UTMC provides both inpatient and outpatient services and is involved in medical education through the UT Graduate School of Medicine and research.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The University of Tennessee Medical Center (UTMC), which is associated with utmedicalcenter.org, utilizes the HealtheLife patient portal. The HealtheLife patient portal is linked to the Cerner electronic health record (EHR) system. Information from the University of Tennessee Medical Center also indicates the use of the Cerner System for their electronic medical records (EMR). Therefore, based on the patient portal vendor and branding, the University of Tennessee Medical Center (utmedicalcenter.org) is using the Cerner EHR system.
