About the Practice

Windsong Radiology Group is a diagnostic radiology center in Western New York offering comprehensive outpatient imaging services, including X-ray, CT, MRI, PET/CT, ultrasound, bone density (DEXA), and nuclear medicine, utilizing advanced technology and interpreted by board-certified, subspecialized radiologists. [3, 4, 5, 6] Their specialties include breast care (mammography, ultrasound, MRI, breast surgery), interventional and vascular services (minimally invasive treatments), and advanced prostate procedures. [3, 4, 6, 9, 10]
