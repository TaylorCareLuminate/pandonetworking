About the Practice

Flagstaff Emergency Physicians (FEP) is an independent group of board-certified and residency-trained Emergency Medicine physicians with over 49 years of experience providing care in Northern Arizona. They staff the Emergency Departments at Flagstaff Medical Center and Verde Valley Medical Center, offering comprehensive care for all emergency medical needs and trauma.

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Flagstaff Emergency Physicians (flagstaffemergencyphysicians.com) utilizes the MyNAHealthcare patient portal, which is the patient portal platform used by Northern Arizona Healthcare (NAH). Northern Arizona Healthcare has been noted to use the Cerner electronic health record (EHR) system. More recent information indicates NAH's integration with the Oracle Health EHR, which is a result of Oracle acquiring Cerner. Therefore, based on Flagstaff Emergency Physicians directing patients to the MyNAHealthcare portal and MyNAHealthcare being the patient portal for Northern Arizona Healthcare's Cerner (now Oracle Health) EHR system, it can be concluded that Flagstaff Emergency Physicians is using an EHR system associated with Oracle Health (formerly Cerner). The MyNAHealthcare portal provides patients with access to their electronic medical record information within this system.
