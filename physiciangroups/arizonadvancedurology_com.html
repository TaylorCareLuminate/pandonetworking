About the Practice

Arizona Urology Specialists is Arizona's premier urology practice, serving the greater Phoenix and Scottsdale regions, with over 20 physicians across multiple medical offices. [5, 8] Their experienced team includes board-certified Urologists, a Radiation Oncologist, Physician Assistants, and Nurse Practitioners. [5, 8] They provide a comprehensive array of urologic services and take an integrated approach to care, offering patients access to experienced specialists, innovative diagnostic tools, and advanced treatments and therapies, including minimally invasive surgery, urologic oncology, urethral syndrome, urinary incontinence, robotic surgery, radiation oncology, and advanced cancer therapeutics. [3, 5, 8]
