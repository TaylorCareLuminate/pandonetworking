About the Practice

Stony Brook Southampton Hospital, a 125-bed hospital accredited by the Joint Commission and a campus of Stony Brook University Hospital, is staffed by approximately 250 physicians, dentists, and allied health professionals representing more than 50 medical specialties. [5, 9] It offers a diverse array of clinical services, ranging from primary medical care to specialized surgical procedures, including orthopedics and bariatrics. [3, 5] As the sole provider of emergency care on Long Island's South Fork, it is a provisional Level III adult Trauma Center. [5, 9] The hospital includes specialized centers such as the Audrey and Martin Gruss Heart & Stroke Center, The Ellen Hermanson Breast Center, and The Center for Advanced Wound Healing, and provides care through 32 satellite locations across the South Fork. [3, 5, 15]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Stony Brook Southampton Hospital utilizes the MyHealtheLife patient portal, which is powered by Cerner Health. This portal allows patients to access their medical records from December 3, 2024, forward. For medical records dating before December 3, 2024, patients can still access them through a former Stony Brook Southampton Hospital patient portal until its permanent closure on March 31, 2025. The MyHealtheLife portal, part of the broader Stony Brook Medicine patient portal system, enables patients to view health records and communicate with their doctors.
