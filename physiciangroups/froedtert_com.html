About the Practice

The Froedtert & the Medical College of Wisconsin regional health network is a partnership supporting patient care, innovation, medical research, and education. It includes eastern Wisconsin's only academic medical center and adult Level I Trauma Center at Froedtert Hospital in Milwaukee. [13] They provide comprehensive adult health care, from routine check-ups to advanced care for complex conditions like cancer and heart disease, and are recognized for specialty care including neuroscience and stroke care. [7, 10, 11] The network includes multiple hospitals, health centers, and clinics across southeastern Wisconsin. [13]
