About the Practice

The Phoenix Indian Medical Center (PIMC) is a Joint Commission accredited 127-bed hospital providing comprehensive healthcare services to eligible American Indians and Alaska Natives. [2, 3, 5] Located in Phoenix, Arizona, PIMC offers a wide range of primary, tertiary, and specialty care services, including medical, surgical, dental, behavioral health, and various specialized clinics such as diabetes, eye care, and women's health. [2, 3, 6] It serves a large patient population from the greater Phoenix area and surrounding tribes, and also provides specialty care support to rural facilities in Arizona, Nevada, and Utah. [2, 3]

Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Phoenix Indian Medical Center (PIMC), which is part of the Indian Health Service (IHS), uses a patient portal known as the Personal Health Record (PHR). This patient portal allows individuals to access their health records online, track appointments, medications, and test results, and communicate securely with their healthcare providers. The Electronic Health Record (EHR) system that the Personal Health Record (PHR) is connected to at PIMC is the Resource and Patient Management System Electronic Health Record (RPMS-EHR). PIMC successfully implemented the RPMS-EHR in its Ambulatory Care departments.
