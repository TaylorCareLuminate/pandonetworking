About the Practice

Amazon One Medical is a membership-based and technology-powered primary care organization offering in-person and 24/7 virtual care services. They provide on-site labs and programs for preventive care, chronic care management, common illnesses, and mental health concerns. [1, 7, 8, 9] The service aims to make healthcare easier to access and more enjoyable through a human-centered, technology-forward approach, covering everything from physicals to chronic care. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

Amazon One Medical utilizes a patient portal that is integrated within their proprietary "One Medical app" and accessible through their website, Onemedical.com. This platform allows patients to manage appointments, message their care team, access health records, and handle prescriptions. The underlying electronic health record (EHR) system used by Amazon One Medical is a custom-built, proprietary system referred to as "1Life". This indicates that One Medical does not use a third-party EHR vendor like Epic (which uses MyChart as its patient portal), but rather a system developed in-house. While "One Medical Passport" and "Chirp for Patients" were mentioned in search results, "One Medical Passport" appears to be a separate system used by a different healthcare provider for pre-surgical assessments, and "Chirp for Patients" is the application for "One Medical Seniors," which may be a related but distinct entity. Therefore, the patient portal branding is directly tied to Amazon One Medical's own "One Medical app" and their proprietary "1Life" EHR system.
