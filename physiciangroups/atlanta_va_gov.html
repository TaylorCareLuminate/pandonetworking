About the Practice

The VA Atlanta Healthcare System provides outstanding health care to Veterans across a 50-county area in northeast Georgia, operating the Joseph Maxwell Cleland Atlanta VA Medical Center in Decatur and 21 community-based outpatient clinics. The main campus offers primary care and a wide range of specialty health services, including mental health, audiology and speech, dental services, hospice and palliative care, nutrition counseling, and prescriptions. As a teaching hospital affiliated with institutions like Emory University and Morehouse College, it provides professional training in most major medical and surgical specialties and subspecialties and conducts significant medical research in areas such as PTSD, memory and cognition disorders, hypertension, oncology, gastroenterology, and pulmonary medicine. Research Analysis
Methodology & Findings

The Atlanta VA Medical Center, as part of the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) health care system, utilizes the patient portal known as My HealtheVet. The VA is currently in the process of transitioning to a new electronic health record (EHR) system. As part of this modernization effort, a new patient portal called My VA Health is being rolled out. This new My VA Health portal is associated with the EHR system provided by Cerner, which is now part of Oracle Health. While the transition is underway, Veterans may still use the My HealtheVet portal, particularly for services or at facilities not yet fully integrated into the new EHR system. The My VA Health portal is intended to provide a single point for managing healthcare needs alongside other VA benefits and services on VA.gov.
