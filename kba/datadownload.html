About Data Downloads

  • Merged Data: Combines contact information with CRM event history, analytics, and organization status
  • Contacts Only: Export contact information from the hotsheet database including organization status
  • CRM Only: Export just the CRM timeline events and outreach history
  • Organization Status: Includes status values like "normal", "engaged", "not-target", and "meeting-failure"
  • Bad Email Tracking: Includes a column indicating if an email has been marked as bad
  • All downloads are in CSV format and can be opened in Excel or Google Sheets
