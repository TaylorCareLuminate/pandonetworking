Paste in a complete RFP with all questions. AI will extract questions AND generate answers for all of them automatically. This runs as a background job, so you can close this window and check the Jobs tab later for results.

How it works:
1. Click "Start Background Job" to begin processing
2. Your job will be created and start running in the background
3. You can close this window or navigate away
4. Check the "Jobs" tab anytime to see progress and results
5. When complete, view and download your responses
